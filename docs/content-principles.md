# Content Principles

This site should make Jamie legible for technical operations, product operations,
implementation, civic technology, and bounded consulting opportunities.

Every public-facing section should answer:

1. What did Jamie do?
2. Toward what end?
3. What became usable?
4. Why is it relevant to technical operations / implementation?
5. What remains protected?

Prefer action verbs.
Translate insider language.
Use collective-work language where appropriate.
Do not publish private materials.
Do not overclaim.
