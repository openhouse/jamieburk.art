# OTI Technical Operations Message

I am excited by this role because it names the kind of work I have repeatedly
done across civic, cultural, small-business, and public-facing technical
environments: building the operating backbone that lets teams stay oriented and
keep work moving.

For 14+ years, I have translated ambiguous stakeholder needs into requirements,
workflows, decision logs, action trackers, public-facing tools, launch plans,
adoption materials, and durable handoffs. My strongest work sits between
technical and nontechnical people: clarifying scope, coordinating
implementation, documenting what matters, and leaving teams with operating
materials they can keep using.

The OTI description's emphasis on delivery coordination, onboarding, status
reporting, runbooks, retros, and honest communication about what is and is not
working is especially resonant. In my work with THICK ARTS, Harry J. Epstein
Company, CallNYC, FairRentNYC / Commercial Rent Stabilization, WOWList, and 196
Artists Residency / Sunday Dinner, I have built systems that help people
re-enter complex work, understand decisions, coordinate next steps, and move
from unclear context to useful action.

I would bring OTI a calm, documentation-centered operating practice: the ability
to surface risks early, coordinate across stakeholders, write clearly, support
adoption, and improve working systems over time.
