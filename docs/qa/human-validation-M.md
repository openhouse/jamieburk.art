# Human Validation Protocol M

Status: `pending-human-review`

This protocol creates a bounded way to learn from people. It does not record a
review that has not happened. The exact candidate means the reviewed commit,
candidate content hash, date, staging URL, and robots state are recorded before
the session begins.

## Unfamiliar Hiring Readers

Use at least three unfamiliar readers:

1. A hiring manager in technical project management, product operations, or
   implementation.
2. A recruiter or experienced referrer.
3. A civic, public-interest, or public-sector delivery leader.

After 30 seconds, ask without prompting:

1. What role is Jamie seeking?
2. What does Jamie help a team do?
3. What would you click next?

After two minutes, ask:

1. Which three proofs do you remember?
2. What did Jamie personally do?
3. What became usable, changed, or easier to continue?
4. What feels private, overstated, broken, confusing, or hard to trust?
5. Would you interview, refer, continue, or stop?

Record only the reader class, date, exact candidate, answers, observed task
completion, and approved non-identifying notes. Do not infer approval from
silence. Do not record an AI role-play, Codex judgment, or Jamie's predicted
answer as an unfamiliar-reader result.

## Collaborator Corroboration

Record project existence, Jamie's contribution, collective accomplishment,
observed use, observed outcome, limitation, and permission to paraphrase or
attribute as separate fields. A collaborator may corroborate one proposition
without corroborating another. Silence is not approval. Consequential claims
without a reply remain held or retain their existing public boundary.

## Evaluator Independence

An authoring agent is not an independent reviewer. An optimizer may propose a
revision; a deterministic checker may establish structural consistency; a
read-only model grader may apply a frozen rubric; only a real human session may
establish human comprehension, consent, firsthand corroboration, or release
approval.
