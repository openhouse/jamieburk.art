# Chad Lens independent review

## Criterion

`CHAD-001` asks whether a hiring or public-sector reader can identify, in one
pass:

1. Jamie as the actor;
2. the concrete intervention;
3. what became usable;
4. toward what end;
5. where credit or outcomes remain collective.

The passing threshold is `0.8`. The criterion is semantic and requires two
independent graders.

## Baseline result

| Grader | Score | Result |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Independent Chad grader A | 0.8 | Pass |
| Independent Chad grader B | 0.8 | Pass |

Both graders found that the homepage, Technical Operations page, resume, and
all three priority cases make Jamie, the intervention, a usable result, and its
purpose legible in one pass. HJE and FairRentNYC preserve contribution and
collective-credit boundaries, satisfying the criterion's explicit boundary
requirement.

## Hill-climb decision

No public-copy change was accepted. The criterion met its threshold on the
first independent run, and the protocol rejects changes made only to improve
grader-facing wording. Stopping here preserves the smaller-change objective.

The graders identified non-blocking opportunities toward a score of `1.0`:

- reduce repeated role-level lists across the homepage, Technical Operations,
  and resume;
- make the HJE opening a complete first-person sentence rather than relying on
  page context for the subject;
- clarify how CallNYC's independent-build language relates to the later
  collaborator credit;
- make the Contact introduction more specific.

Those ideas require their own bounded hypotheses and must retain HJE
contribution language, FairRentNYC collective credit, CallNYC's independent and
unofficial boundaries, and all artifact and source limitations.

## Limits

The graders did not infer production deployment, public-use approval, image
rights, collaborator consent, or final resume approval. One grader also flagged
the stronger HJE verb in the resume PDF for future reconciliation with the
careful public knowledge-bank projection; this review does not resolve that
separate factual-governance question.
