## Mission-relevant discoveries

The account functioned as shared neighborhood-operating infrastructure rather
than only a broadcast feed. The population documents several recurring uses:

- inviting residents to shape a proposed neighborhood resource and cultural
  center;
- publishing intake and scheduling information for free household tire pickup;
- circulating neighborhood cleanup, transit, voter, public-health, housing,
  and food-access resources;
- preserving public exchanges with municipal and community stakeholders;
- sustaining a project identity that collaborators could continue using.

The URL inventory contains 133 external-link occurrences across 118 records and
31 distinct short URLs. Nine routed destinations were selected for deeper
mission relevance:

- Kansas City Star reporting on Leon's Thriftway;
- Northeast News reporting on local affordable-housing policy;
- Curbed reporting on a proposed renter tax credit;
- the RideKC Next system-redesign page;
- a KCUR Missouri primary-election guide;
- the Missouri Secretary of State voter-outreach lookup;
- Kansas City Area Transportation Authority Ozone Alert information;
- a KC Town Hall COVID-19 resource Q&A;
- a KC Town Hall cleanup-method video.

These source records support a claim about civic-resource curation. They do not
establish that KC Town Hall authored the linked material, formally partnered
with every publisher, caused use of the resources, or produced policy or service
outcomes. The curation claim remains held from the public case study.
