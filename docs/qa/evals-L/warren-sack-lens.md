# Warren Sack Lens

## Purpose

This lens tests whether the portfolio recognizes Jamie as a builder and
investigator of social information systems: someone who moves recursively among
relationships, research, software, image analysis, interface, physical space,
and collective experience.

## Source basis

- protected, unofficial UCSC narrative evaluations for Introduction to Digital
  Media, Spring 2004, and Social Information Spaces, Winter 2006;
- Laura Mattingly's public 2006 Good Times account of Open House and Jamie's
  social-software interpretation.

## Criterion

Passing requires 100/100 across five independent dimensions:

1. **Source integrity - 20:** the academic source is protected, attributed, and
   bounded as an unofficial copy.
2. **Recursive relational thinking - 20:** the record preserves the social
   network, structural-equivalence, and physical/online analysis.
3. **Prototype range - 20:** the record preserves movement among software,
   image analysis, interface design, sensors, projection, and physical space.
4. **Collective and causal discipline - 20:** class work is not presented as
   production deployment, historical priority, or sole-authored group work.
5. **Present continuity - 20:** the public portfolio states that people inhabit,
   understand, enter, reshape, and carry forward systems rather than merely
   receiving administrative outputs.

The eval is a critical regression gate, not a claim that Warren Sack reviewed
the current portfolio.
