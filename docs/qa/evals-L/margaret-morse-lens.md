# Margaret Morse Lens

## Purpose

This lens tests whether the portfolio recognizes Jamie's embodied, artistic,
performative, and media-archaeological intelligence as part of his present
systems practice. It prevents the hiring argument from reducing every valuable
activity to organizational utility.

## Source basis

- protected, unofficial UCSC narrative evaluations for Visual Culture and
  Technology and New Media Theory Seminar, both Spring 2006;
- a held screenshot of Margaret Morse's 2014 recommendation;
- Laura Mattingly's public 2006 Good Times account of Open House.

Private correspondence, student identifiers, contact details, protected paths,
raw evaluation text, and the recommendation image stay outside the repository.

## Criterion

Passing requires 100/100 across five independent dimensions:

1. **Source integrity and privacy - 20:** source posture, rights, and exclusions
   are explicit.
2. **Embodied intelligence - 20:** the bank preserves media archaeology,
   embodied theory, performance, attention, and Time is Long without conflating
   records.
3. **Experiment as practice - 20:** the public self-description values
   participation, attention, atmosphere, place, memory, and inhabitation.
4. **Connected threshold - 20:** the artistic, civic, technical, and social
   practices remain visibly connected.
5. **Bounded praise - 20:** academic praise is not converted into sole
   authorship, universal later outcomes, or cleared reproduction rights.

The eval is a critical regression gate, not a claim that Margaret Morse reviewed
the current portfolio.
