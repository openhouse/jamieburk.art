# Five-Minute Hiring Comprehension Review

This is an external, no-coaching usability protocol for the portfolio. It is
not replaced by an LLM reading, an automated eval, or Jamie explaining the work.

## Participants

Recruit five people across these reader roles: recruiter, hiring manager,
technical or operations peer, public-interest or civic-tech leader, and trusted
referrer. A single person may not fill multiple slots in the minimum set.

## Task

Give each participant the homepage URL with no introduction. After five
minutes, close the site and ask:

1. What roles is Jamie seeking?
2. What are three concrete things Jamie has delivered?
3. Which result or proof felt strongest?
4. What remained confusing, doubtful, or hard to verify?
5. What would you do next: contact, resume, another case study, or leave?

Do not correct answers during the session. Record paraphrases rather than
private identifying details. Ask permission before retaining a quotation.

## Criterion

The external criterion is met only when at least four of five participants can
name a target role family, at least three can name three concrete deliveries,
and no more than one mistakes an archived or collective project for an official
government product or sole-causality claim.

Until sessions exist, the automated eval may pass only as `governed-open`: the
protocol is ready, the zero-session state is honest, and no external validation
is claimed.
