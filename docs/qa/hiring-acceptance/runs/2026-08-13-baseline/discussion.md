## Shared strengths

- The target professional category and operating-structure promise are clear.
- FairRentNYC, CallNYC, WOW List, Harry J. Epstein, Sunday Dinner / 196, and KC
  Town Hall make stakeholder translation, public-facing implementation,
  documentation, continuity, and collective credit credible.
- Privacy, uncertainty, source lineage, and public/private boundaries read as
  product and operating judgment rather than compliance decoration.
- The site supplies a clear resume and contact path.

## Shared blockers

- **ACLU:** budget, procurement, and invoice ownership remain unproved, and no
  single case exposes a complete scope, schedule, risk, metric, communication,
  and outcome record.
- **CodePath AI Operations:** the site does not show a personally built and
  professionally shipped LLM or agent workflow with real users, production
  evals, reusable handoff assets, changed behavior, and measured time or quality
  gains.
- **CodePath Engineering:** the site does not show a real issue moving through
  reproduction, stack-trace interpretation, prioritization, test authorship,
  retest, and verified closure.
- **OTI Senior Product:** the strongest civic-product evidence does not yet form
  one complete discovery, prototype, pilot, launch, instrumentation,
  accessibility, conformance, and learning dossier. Civil-service eligibility
  remains an employer decision.
- **OTI Technical Operations:** the role is expired, and formal technology
  hiring, employee onboarding, team-health metrics, procurement, and portfolio
  authority remain unproved.

## Responsible hill-climb boundary

The next UI iteration may reduce reader burden and make the relationship between
field work and public systems more explicit. It may not manufacture a budget,
professional AI deployment, test trace, accessibility conformance record,
civil-service determination, or open job. Those failures remain visible until
new source-backed evidence or employer review changes them.

These are fictionalized analytical lenses, not quotations, endorsements,
participation claims, predictions, or actual hiring decisions.
