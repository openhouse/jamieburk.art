# NTER CHNG Wayback Hill Climb

Date: 2026-07-15

## Recovery

The pass began with the supplied January 28, 2011 Wayback capture of
`nterchng.com`. The archived homepage identifies NTER CHNG as an interactive
texting installation by Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier and
places its January 2010 presentation at the Arts Incubator Cocoon Gallery in
Kansas City.

Research then recovered:

- a 2013 homepage capture that retains the three-person credit and an embedded
  project-video identifier;
- a January 2010 Pitch listing describing the installation's software,
  architectural, and real-time participant-message form;
- contemporaneous KC Studio coverage identifying the official Kansas City
  America: Now and Here site and the May 2011 launch context;
- the official archived exhibition artist index, which lists Bolton, Burkart,
  and Fuselier as visual artists and links them to one shared artist page;
- the official archived shared artist page, used through a protected,
  metadata-only source record because its raw payload includes historical
  personal phone numbers; and
- the Nerman Museum exhibition page, retained only as broader program context.

The homepage-linked press release was not recovered. The record does not turn
that non-recovery into a claim that the release never existed.

## Claims And Boundaries

Two public-safe, dormant claims were developed:

1. Jamie co-created NTER CHNG with Drew Bolton and Garrett Fuselier as an
   interactive installation combining software, a two-sided digital wall, and
   participant text messages in a shared real-time dialog.
2. The official archived America: Now and Here Kansas City site listed the
   three collaborators as visual artists and presented NTER CHNG on their
   shared artist page during the 2011 Kansas City launch.

The graph does not assign programming, spatial design, motion graphics,
installation, or production ownership to one collaborator. It does not place
NTER CHNG at the Nerman Museum, imply a direct collaboration with Barbara
Kruger, claim a solo exhibition, or infer audience impact. Video playability,
media rights, participation measures, individual division of work, and the
missing press release remain an explicit research task.
