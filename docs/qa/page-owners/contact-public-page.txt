Scoped work

Begin at the level that fits the decision in front of you. Each option is separately scoped; none creates an automatic next engagement.

OPTION 1

Focused working session
The question
Is there one important decision, knot, or working artifact that would benefit from an hour of structured attention?
A useful outcome
A clarified question, decision map, or agreed next step appropriate to the session.
The boundary
This is paid, defined work. A separate agreement is required before the session begins or continues beyond its agreed scope.

OPTION 2

Knowledge operations diagnostic
The question
Does a specific workflow, evidence base, or team-memory problem need diagnosis before implementation?
A useful outcome
A scoped map of what is known, open, protected, risky, and worth doing next.
The boundary
The diagnostic is separately scoped. A separate agreement is required for any implementation that follows.

OPTION 3

Implementation or fractional operations
The question
Is there enough shared understanding to scope delivery, adoption, documentation, and handoff?
A useful outcome
A separately agreed engagement with explicit scope, authority, cadence, and exit conditions.
The boundary
A separate agreement is required for the engagement and for any extension or renewal.
Start with a conversation

Share the decision, knot, or artifact involved; why it matters now; who needs to use the result; and any real deadline or access constraint.

A conversation is not an engagement. Scope, timing, fees, and any paid work are confirmed in writing before work begins.
