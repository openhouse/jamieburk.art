# Application Outcomes Protocol M

Status: `no-observations-yet`

Application and referral outcomes are private, aggregate learning signals. Do
not publish employer names, individual reviewer identities, correspondence, or
private job-search records. Do not attribute an interview, referral, rejection,
or offer to the portfolio without evidence that supports that causal claim.

## Real Job Review

Status: `pending-real-job-review`

Evaluate at least five dated real job descriptions. Archetypes and
agent-authored sample postings do not count. Store the underlying postings in
the private job-search workspace; the public repository may contain only an
approved, non-identifying aggregate.

For each posting, record privately:

- role and organization;
- posting date and capture date;
- required capabilities in the employer's own framing;
- strongest matching portfolio proof;
- material proof gap;
- whether the gap blocks application; and
- the next bounded action.

Do not rewrite employer needs to fit existing proof. Do not use source volume as
a proxy for professional importance. Compare what the role asks for with what
the portfolio can defensibly demonstrate.

## Outcome Ledger

Keep dated aggregate counts for applications sent, referrals, screens,
interviews, offers, declines, and unknown outcomes. Preserve `unknown` instead
of guessing. Review patterns only after a meaningful denominator exists.

## Countability And Archive Bias

Run a countability bias review quarterly. Enumerable web, database, social, and
press traces must not crowd out offline, relational, maintenance, embodied, or
implementation work. Candidate depth should respond to hiring relevance,
evidence quality, and Jamie's actual practice, not the number of surviving
artifacts.
