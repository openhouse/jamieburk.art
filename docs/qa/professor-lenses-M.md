# Professor Lenses M

These two evals preserve dimensions of Jamie's practice that a hiring-focused
portfolio can otherwise compress away. They are source-bounded editorial
controls, not simulated testimonials.

The names identify perspectives derived from records Jamie supplied. The
criteria and pass decisions are written by Codex. A pass does not represent a
fresh review, endorsement, or approval by Prof. Margaret Morse or Prof. Warren
Sack.

## Source boundary

- The public 2006 *Open House* profile is the public evidence bridge. It
  documents participation, communal responsibility, tending, distributed
  documentation, art/life inquiry, and Jamie's explicit connection between the
  inhabited gallery and social software.
- Protected UCSC narrative evaluations informed the questions but are not
  copied into this public repository. Student identifiers, grades, unofficial
  transcript text, and private correspondence are outside the public-use
  boundary.
- A user-supplied screenshot of a public 2014 recommendation informed the
  Margaret Morse lens. The screenshot is not committed and the eval does not
  convert it into a new testimonial surface.

## Prof. Margaret Morse lens

**Question:** Does the portfolio preserve one clear threshold where Jamie's
artistic, civic, technical, and social practices remain connected through
participation, attention, hospitality, memory, place, and the ways people
inhabit structures?

**Pass condition:** `/about` keeps Jamie legible for technical project and
implementation roles while naming the embodied, participatory lineage that
distinguishes how he makes structure usable.

**Failure condition:** The portfolio reduces Jamie to organizational utility
and removes experimentation, atmosphere, artistic inquiry, or care for how
people inhabit a system.

## Prof. Warren Sack lens

**Question:** Does the portfolio make Jamie visible as an original
social-technical thinker who finds patterns connecting people, information,
and place, then tests them through interfaces, workflows, prototypes, and
inhabited systems?

**Pass condition:** `/about` shows technical imagination and prototyping as
part of the current practice without making unsupported claims about historical
coursework or sole authorship.

**Failure condition:** The portfolio presents Jamie only as an implementer of
settled requirements and obscures his ability to frame questions, recognize
social patterns, and prototype systems before their form is settled.

## Runtime

Run:

```sh
npm run check:professor-lenses
```

The deterministic checker confirms the register, source boundary, public
source reading, About-page signals, hiring clarity, and integration with the
recursive eval protocol. It does not establish factual truth by itself.
