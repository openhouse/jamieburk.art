# Content Safety

This repo is public-safe by default. When in doubt, omit the detail or mark it as approval-pending in staging.

## Do Not Publish

- Home address, private phone number, birth date, identity-document details, or credentials
- Therapy, medical, medication, insurance, or clinical details
- Private financial, trust, tax, debt, banking, property-conflict, or revenue details
- Private client details, private dashboards, private analytics, or admin screenshots
- Raw Sunday Dinner guest data, residency records, attendance data, or contact lists
- Private coalition notes, legal-review materials, stakeholder lists, strategy materials, private emails, or messages
- Unapproved quotes, unapproved photos, or non-public source materials
- Anything implying official government service where the work was a civic/prototype project
- Private, proprietary, or unlicensed font files

## Safe Language

Prefer:

- Archived prototype
- Public-facing tool
- Co-built
- Contributed to
- Supported
- Helped structure
- Built and stewarded
- Public-safe summary
- Redacted example
- Representative diagram
- Collective work
- Not an official current service
- Approval required
- Private material intentionally omitted

## Claim Discipline

- Use "contributed to" for revenue growth unless causation has been explicitly approved.
- Use "supported" rather than "managed" unless the scope is exact.
- Use "co-built" or "helped structure" for collective work.
- Use "public-safe summary" for civic, coalition, and community work.
- Hide or soften any metric that has not been approved.

## Approval Pending

Staging may show careful pending language such as:

```text
Public email pending confirmation.
Approved screenshot pending.
Private material intentionally omitted.
```

Production should not contain visible review TODOs, private material, dead-end contact flows, or placeholder files presented as final assets.

## Source Trails

Source trails should name public-safe categories, not private source contents. Good examples:

- Public website
- Public-safe summary
- Approved screenshot pending
- Public data source
- Private material intentionally omitted
- Collaborator review required
