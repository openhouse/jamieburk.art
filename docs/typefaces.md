# Typefaces

The V1 type direction is public-safe and font-file-free.

## Current Policy

- Karla is the body, UI, prose, card, and long-reading font.
- Archivo Narrow is the label, metadata, section-eyebrow, and compact
  civic-emphasis font.
- League Spartan and Oswald are not required for the shipped interface in this
  branch.
- No private, proprietary, or unapproved font files should be committed or
  served from this repo.

Use `next/font/google` rather than committing font binaries.

Trade Gothic, Verlag, Gotham Rounded, FondFont RISQUE, and Maria's handwriting
font are historical design references only. They are not shipped unless
licensing and consent are explicitly confirmed.
