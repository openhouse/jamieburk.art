# Typefaces

The site ships public web fonts through `next/font/google`.

## Shipped Fonts

- Karla for body, UI, and prose.
- Archivo Narrow for display headings, page titles, proof labels, and compact
  public-grotesque emphasis.

## Not Shipped

- Trade Gothic, Verlag, Gotham Rounded, FondFont RISQUE, and Maria are not
  shipped unless Jamie explicitly confirms web licensing or permission.
- No private or proprietary font files belong in the repo.

Do not commit `.ttf`, `.otf`, `.woff`, or `.woff2` files unless licensing and
permission are explicit and documented.
