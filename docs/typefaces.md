# Typefaces

The site ships public web fonts through `next/font/google`.

## Shipped Fonts

- Karla for body, UI, and prose.
- Archivo Narrow for display headings, eyebrows, proof labels, and compact
  public-grotesque emphasis.

## Not Shipped

- Trade Gothic, Verlag, and Gotham Rounded are not shipped unless licensing is
  confirmed.
- The Maria handwriting font is not shipped unless Jamie explicitly confirms
  permission.
- No private or proprietary font files belong in the repo.

Do not commit `.ttf`, `.otf`, `.woff`, or `.woff2` files unless Jamie explicitly
confirms licensing and permission.
