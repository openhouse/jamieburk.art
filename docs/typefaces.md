# Typeface Policy

V1 uses two safe Google Fonts through `next/font/google`.

- **Karla:** body text, UI, long prose, buttons, navigation, and readable
  summaries.
- **Archivo Narrow:** display headings, section eyebrows, proof labels, compact
  metadata, and OpenGraph-style display text where practical.

League Spartan and Oswald are not part of V1. Do not commit private,
proprietary, custom, or unlicensed font files.
