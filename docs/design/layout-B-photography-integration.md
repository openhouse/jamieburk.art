# Layout B Photography Integration

## Decision

`features/layout-B` is a public working branch for evaluating a photographic
front-end candidate. Jamie authorized the six metadata-stripped derivatives in
`apps/www/public/images/field-notes/` for public pull-request review on
2026-07-26.

This authorization does not complete production publication review. Jamie's
final selection, captions, photographer credits, rights, consent, and route
placement remain open human gates.

## Held Field

Recognizable collaborators, residents, event participants, artists, private
interiors, guest histories, and campaign gatherings remain held. They may be
stronger photographs, but this candidate does not use them until represented
people, photographer, credit, context, and collective-work questions receive
specific review.

## Visual Argument

The composition proceeds from person to practice to consequence:

1. Jamie appears in the first viewport.
2. Fast paths and proof bands reduce hiring-reader burden.
3. Work rows join role, years, evidence, and available public media.
4. A field sequence shows building, neighborhood operation, and handwork.
5. Case-study photography appears only where it changes understanding.
6. The colophon shows that the interface itself is made and maintained.

The design uses the Human Index palette supplied by Jamie as a starting point.
Work-jacket blue structures action, graphite carries authority, green marks
stewardship, and red pencil is reserved for focus and correction.

## Acceptance Boundary

Automated evaluation may verify registration, metadata removal, stable
dimensions, route placement, responsive behavior, alt text, captions, and
absence of private locators. It may not approve production publication,
photographer credit, rights, consent, collective attribution, or the reader's
human response.
