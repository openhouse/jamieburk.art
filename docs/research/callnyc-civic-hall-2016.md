# CallNYC / Civic Hall Research Memo - 2016

**Status:** Public-safe research memo

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-11

**Purpose:** Support the CallNYC case-study pilot without exposing private
research material

## Recovered Evidence

1. Civic Hall announced `1/30 1-3pm` for a New York City Council hackathon
   focused on constituent services:
   <https://x.com/CivicHall/status/693124020917522433>
2. The attached Council-branded graphic reads `New York City Council Hackathon`
   and displays `labs.council.nyc`:
   <https://pbs.twimg.com/media/CZ5m-mAWwAA42td.png:large>
3. The New York City Council posted from Civic Hall on January 30 and identified
   the gathering as its first `#CouncilStat` hackathon:
   <https://x.com/NYCCouncil/status/693509031768506368>
4. A January 31 Wayback capture preserves both posts inside Civic Hall's
   embedded social feed:
   <https://web.archive.org/web/20160131004601/http://civichall.org/events/page/2/>
5. Miranda Neubauer's March 14, 2016 Politico New York reporting connects Jamie
   Burkart to the January event, pre-release material, the subsequent full data
   release, and his independent development and iteration of CallNYC:
   <https://callnyc.org/data/media/Politico-Website-provides-new-information-about-council-members-focus.pdf>
6. The public repository documents the surviving implementation:
   <https://github.com/openhouse/CallNYC>
7. A private participant photograph shows a breakout placard reading `Digital
   District - Help improve City Council District office operations` and carries
   an approximate 2:10 p.m. image timestamp. The photograph has no public link
   and is not reproduced here.

## Not Recovered

No recovered source establishes:

- a dedicated Civic Hall calendar listing or event-detail page;
- complete registration-form contents;
- a full agenda;
- a complete participant or breakout roster;
- the facilitator of Digital District;
- the other breakout tracks;
- formal winners or a published project roster.

The Wayback page is evidence from an embedded social feed. It must not be
described as a recovered Civic Hall calendar listing.

## Safe Wording

> A New York City Council constituent-services hackathon at Civic Hall on
> January 30, 2016.

With citation:

> Civic Hall announced the gathering for 1-3 p.m.; the Council described it on
> the event day as its first CouncilStat hackathon.

For event branding:

> A recovered promotional graphic branded the gathering "New York City Council
> Hackathon."

Treat the graphic as event branding, not proof of a longer formal registration
title.

For the breakout:

> One documented breakout was titled "Digital District - Help improve City
> Council District office operations."

The photograph supports a breakout-table title. It does not establish the
overall event title. Its approximate timestamp does not establish the event
hours or start time.

For the project relationship:

> After the complete CouncilStat data was released, Jamie independently built
> CallNYC as a resident-facing translation layer.

The hackathon supplies institutional context. CallNYC is the independent
follow-on documented by reporting and the surviving repository.

## Anti-Claims

- Do not say Jamie organized, led, or officially represented the hackathon.
- Do not say Digital District was the event's official title.
- Do not say 2:10 p.m. was the event's start time.
- Do not say Jamie authored all participant recommendations.
- Do not say Jamie caused the CouncilStat release.
- Do not describe CallNYC as an official Council product.
- Do not describe CallNYC as commissioned, a formal hackathon submission, or a
  winning entry.
- Do not say Speaker Melissa Mark-Viverito attended.
- Do not treat CouncilStat totals as simple measures of office quality,
  effectiveness, specialization, or resident need.
- Do not claim measured improvements in case resolution, district-office
  efficiency, or constituent satisfaction.

## Distinctions To Preserve

- **Embedded feed / calendar:** the archive preserves social posts embedded on
  a Civic Hall page; it is not a recovered calendar listing.
- **Brand / formal title:** the graphic documents visible event branding; it
  does not recover a longer registration title.
- **Image timestamp / event hours:** the private image's approximate timestamp
  describes the image record; Civic Hall's public post supplies the announced
  event hours.
- **Hackathon / independent follow-on:** the event gave Jamie institutional and
  data context; reporting and code support CallNYC as his subsequent independent
  prototype.

## Publication Boundary

The public page may describe the private participant photograph at the bounded
level above. It may not reproduce the photograph, expose private metadata,
identify people from it, or invent a public URL. Public source links may be
shown normally and should remain subject to periodic manual link review.
