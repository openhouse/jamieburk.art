# Publishing Governance

This document translates the repo operating doctrine into public-safe claim
governance for the proofs bank and the website.

## Mission

Build and maintain a focused, public-safe portfolio that makes Jamie legible as a
Technical Project Manager - Product Operations & Implementation lead who builds
operating structure for ambiguous public-facing technical work.

The proofs bank is the source-of-truth layer for that public surface. The website
should project selected claims from the bank for clarity, audience, and purpose.
It should not become a raw archive, private-source browser, or complete
bibliography.

## Publishing Standard

Every claim should be safe to defend in a public repository. Use the newspaper
test: if a sentence, screenshot, name, or source note would create avoidable
harm or confusion if quoted publicly, do not publish it.

Use public-safe summaries, redacted examples, representative diagrams, approved
artifacts, and careful collective-work language.

When uncertain, write: `TODO: Jamie approval required.`

## Protected Material

Do not publish private emails, raw transcripts, private coalition notes,
legal-review materials, health or financial details, private correspondence,
unapproved photos, private fonts, credentials, private stakeholder lists,
internal analytics, client-private materials, or raw community records.

Protected material may inform a public-safe aggregate or bounded summary only
when the resulting claim is defensible without exposing the underlying source.

## Projection Rules

1. Add or revise claims in `professional-accomplishments.md` before projecting
   them into website copy.
2. Use the safest website wording, not the strongest private wording.
3. Preserve collective-work boundaries for civic, cultural, and coalition work.
4. Mark source status, confidence, website projection, and "Do not say"
   boundaries for durable claims.
5. Keep the website focused on proof, role fit, and usable context.

## V1 Scope

For V1, do not add a CMS, database, auth, search, analytics, AI chatbot, archive
browser, private document browser, full photo archive, heavy animation, or major
framework change without explicit approval.

This constraint is part of the public-safety model. A focused static surface is
easier to review, stage, and approve than a system that exposes more raw context.

## Deployment Boundary

Staging comes first at `staging.jamieburk.art` and should remain noindex.
Production at `jamieburk.art` becomes indexable only after Jamie approves the
content and metadata.

## Voice

Precise, warm, modular, ethically grounded.

Documentation is care. Do not overclaim.
