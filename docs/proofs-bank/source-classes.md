# Source Classes

Use these source classes in `claims.md` instead of exposing private evidence.

## public source

Public websites, public project pages, public journalism, public records, or
public repositories.

Do not infer private operations, private authorship, or campaign ownership from
a public page unless another approved source supports that claim.

## public-safe summary

A summary of private or local material that removes private names, raw records,
source paths, sensitive context, and unapproved artifacts.

The summary may support a public claim. The underlying material stays out of
the repo and website.

## approved resume

A claim supported by Jamie-approved resume/profile material.

Resume support does not automatically approve screenshots, private metrics,
contact details, or collaborator names.

## approved artifact

A public artifact, screenshot, PDF, image, quote, or document Jamie has
explicitly approved for publication.

## representative diagram

A redrawn or generalized artifact that explains the work without exposing
private source material.

## private material intentionally omitted

Private evidence exists, but the repo carries only a public-safe aggregate,
pattern, or bounded summary.

## collaborator review required

The claim may affect collaborators, coalition partners, clients, employers,
community members, or named participants. Do not project it publicly until
review is complete.

## metric needs confirmation

The metric may be directionally supported, but exact public wording, placement,
or source status needs Jamie approval.

## protected / do not publish

Never publish the source, claim, quote, screenshot, record, name, path, or
detail.
