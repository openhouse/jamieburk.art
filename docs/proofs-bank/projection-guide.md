# Projection Guide

The website is a projection of the public-safe proofs bank, not a dump of the
evidence.

## Projection Rules

- Do not strengthen a claim beyond `claims.md`, `sources.md`, or
  `apps/www/src/data/proofs.ts`.
- Use public-safe summaries, redacted examples, representative artifacts, and
  collective-work language.
- Keep proof-bank internals out of public pages. The homepage should be clearer
  and narrower than the bank.
- There is no public `/proofs` page.
- Approximate counts are allowed when exact counts would imply false precision.
- Source-Backed Team Memory remains Lab / method / proof-of-practice, not a SaaS
  claim.

## Page Rules

Homepage:

- Use only the strongest, safest claims.
- Use readable proof-strip wording.
- Keep `I turn under-structured work into usable systems` as the anchor, then
  clarify that the work often begins with emerging goals and unclear handoffs.

Work cards:

- Answer what was unclear and what became usable.
- Use `Representative artifacts`, not `Artifact gallery`, unless real artifacts
  are approved.

Case studies:

- Preserve Known / Open / Protected on sensitive work.
- Put public-safety notes before artifacts.
- Use Chad Lens for purpose, agency, and reader burden.

Technical Operations:

- Align to institutional language: delivery coordination, risks, handbooks,
  runbooks, onboarding, decision records, status rhythms, retros, and handoffs.

Lab:

- Keep `AI drafts. Humans review.`
- Do not imply broad ingestion, surveillance, chatbot behavior, or AI replacing
  judgment.
- Keep the Lab page out of the production sitemap until Jamie approves it as an
  indexable public method page.

## Never Project

- Raw transcripts.
- Private correspondence.
- Private coalition notes.
- Legal review materials.
- Client-private materials.
- Private stakeholder lists.
- Internal analytics.
- Unapproved photos, screenshots, or quotes.
- Private/proprietary fonts.
- Credentials, keys, tokens, or secret configuration.
- Raw community records, guest lists, attendance records, or contact sheets.
