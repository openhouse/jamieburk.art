# Knowledge Wiki A-E Family Closure

## Decision

`feature/wiki-D` starts from the exact reviewed head of
`feature/knowledge-wiki-D` and targets `develop`. The A-E family is frozen at
the full SHAs recorded in the Knowledge Wiki foundation suite and closure
ledger. Source branches are read-only inputs, not a merge queue.

The integration retains one authority model:

- `docs/knowledge-bank/` is the canonical Markdown root;
- `scripts/lib/knowledge-wiki.mjs` compiles the graph;
- the portfolio remains a selective projection;
- `apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/records.ts` remains the canonical citation
  registry;
- private originals remain outside the public repository.

   whose source records are absent from D's canonical registry.
6. Regenerate the graph, run deterministic and adversarial checks, obtain two

## Stop Conditions

Automation may stop only when:

- every frozen branch has explicit dispositions;
- every adopted or adapted destination exists in the canonical graph;
- no competing Wiki tree or public Wiki route exists;
- stable links, typed relations, canonical references, and generated artifacts
  pass;
- campaign outcomes retain collective and institutional causality boundaries;
- human-authored voice, collaborator attribution, rights, consent, editorial
  selection, deployment, and production observation remain human gates;
- two independent judgments bind to the same candidate and contract; and
- the exact unchanged candidate passes twice.

Automated closure means the branch is ready for Jamie's final review. It does
not mean the website, a photograph, an application, or production deployment
has been approved.
