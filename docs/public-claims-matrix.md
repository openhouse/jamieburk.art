# Public Claims Matrix

This document records public-safe claim decisions for the V1 portfolio. It does
not publish private source files, private records, raw transcripts, contact
lists, screenshots, or internal materials.

## Claim Standard

- Known: supported by the current public resume and at least one project-source
  record or public-facing artifact.
- Open: plausible and source-backed, but approval-gated because the claim names
  sensitive details, needs a public citation link, or could expose private
  context.
- Protected: intentionally omitted because privacy, consent, client trust, legal
  review, civic sensitivity, or community safety requires it.
