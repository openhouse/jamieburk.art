# Public projection

## What this is

Public projection is the separate decision to compose an eligible claim for a
specific reader, purpose, and surface.

## Contract

Evidence readiness does not cause publication. An explicit publish decision
must match the exact claim and surface. Defer, retire, and disallow decisions
remain visible. The website receives composed public wording, not unrestricted
access to the knowledge universe.

## Evidence and limits

Atlas is a private component. It creates no public wiki, archive browser,
knowledge-bank route, or automatic claim publication. Human approval,
collaborator permission, and media rights remain external gates.
