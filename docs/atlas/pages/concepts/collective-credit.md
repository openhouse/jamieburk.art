# Collective credit

## What this is

Collective credit lets Jamie remain visible as an actor while preserving the
people and institutions whose work made a project possible.

## Contract

Each mature claim distinguishes direct role, collective role, project context,
and outcome context. Public wording names collaborators or collective scope
when the evidence requires it and does not convert amplified work into Jamie's
authorship.

## Evidence and limits

Attribution is not solved once for an entire project. It belongs to the exact
claim, source, and public surface. Community authority and consent may still
limit whether a true relationship should be represented publicly.
