# Citational care

## What this is

Citational care treats evidence, credit, qualification, and correction as part
of the public sentence rather than administrative material left behind it.

## Contract

A source says what it supports, what it does not establish, how it was
preserved, and whether it may be exposed. Claims point to proposition-level
support. Public wording remains bounded to its source position and correction
history.

## Evidence and limits

A citation does not confer ownership, permission, causation, or completeness.
Protected evidence may support a reviewed claim while its locator and content
remain outside the public repository.
