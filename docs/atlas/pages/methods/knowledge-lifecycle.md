# Knowledge lifecycle

## What this is

The knowledge lifecycle is the route from a potentially meaningful fragment to
a defensible, bounded, and optionally selected public claim.

## Contract

Every fragment is accessioned or explicitly held. Sources are closely read into
atomic propositions and limitations. Claims mature only through qualifying
evidence, boundaries, anti-claims, review, and a separate publication decision.
Rejected, corrected, superseded, deferred, and unrecovered knowledge remains
inspectable.

## Evidence and limits

Atlas compiles complete project slices from the typed bank. It does not copy raw
private material into git, authenticate human approval, or interpret a missing
record as proof that an event did not occur.
