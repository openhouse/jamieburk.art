# Public project identity systems

## What this is

This page preserves a cross-project hypothesis about establishing durable
public identities. It is not an approved role claim.

## Human situation

Collective projects needed stable names and routes through which different
people could explain work, invite participation, and document activity.

## What Jamie did

Jamie remembers contributing to this pattern, but account creation,
administration, naming, and individual post authorship require project-specific
records and collaborator testimony.

## What became usable

Surviving identities provide research leads across CallNYC, NYC Artist
Coalition, WOWList, KC Town Hall, and KC Spaces Fund.

## Evidence and limits

Continuity of an identity does not establish who created, administered, or
authored each part of it.

## Open questions

Creation records, administrator histories, design files, and collaborator proof
notes remain to be recovered.

## Public projection

Do not publish the account-establishment claim until its human and documentary
gates are satisfied.
