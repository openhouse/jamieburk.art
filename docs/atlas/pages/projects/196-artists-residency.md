# 196 Artists Residency

## What this is

This is the public-safe record of a residency onboarding and access workflow.

## Human situation

An invited artist and collaborator needed a welcoming, legible path from
proposal acceptance to independent use of the space.

## What Jamie did

Jamie evaluated a proposal, extended an invitation, planned a configuration
check-in, and designed a self-service key workflow.

## What became usable

The participant received a dated invitation and practical route into the
residency without making ordinary access depend on constant staff mediation.

## Evidence and limits

One acceptance record supports this workflow, not the entire residency,
participant endorsement, or artistic outcomes.

## Open questions

Participant identity, creative work, and testimony remain private without
permission.

## Public projection

Describe Jamie's selection and access role without identifying the participant
or absorbing the artist's work.
