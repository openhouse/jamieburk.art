# WOWList

## What this is

WOWList is an archived community event-sharing platform represented by a
public application, source records, social corpora, and operating evidence.

## Human situation

Local cultural activity needed a shared discovery and participation surface,
while the people doing support and publishing work needed a system they could
actually operate.

## What Jamie did

The bank records Jamie's product, technical, publishing, and support roles with
the distinctions required to preserve collective work.

## What became usable

An event-sharing product and its support practices became available to a
distributed cultural network. Atlas makes the scale, source roles, and limits
inspectable without putting the database on the public website.

## Evidence and limits

Recovered application, database, post, reply, and archive records support
bounded claims. Richard Kim and other product collaborators retain their
credit. Recovered surfaces do not establish a complete lifetime export,
unobserved deletion history, or sole authorship of amplified work.

## Open questions

Historical adoption, account authorship, and platform-wide impact remain
bounded to what the recovered evidence can establish.

## Public projection

Only selected claims appear publicly. Deeper operational evidence remains in
the bank and Atlas for future briefs, evaluation, and correction.
