# FairRentNYC

## What this is

FairRentNYC is represented here as collective civic work with public-facing
reference and campaign infrastructure.

## Human situation

Artists, small businesses, and cultural spaces needed commercial-rent policy
to become legible in the lived terms of continuity, planning, and keeping a
front door.

## What Jamie did

The canonical bank separates Jamie's documented technical, editorial, and
organizing contributions from the work of the wider coalition and from later
legislative outcomes.

## What became usable

The work produced public reference surfaces and shared campaign language that
could support participation without claiming a mandate larger than the record.

## Evidence and limits

Campaign-owned sources can establish public activity and wording. They do not
independently prove adoption, sole authorship, or causal responsibility for a
later policy outcome.

## Open questions

Some contribution and outcome chains remain appropriate subjects for
corroboration rather than stronger public copy.

## Public projection

Atlas preserves eligible knowledge without requiring it to appear. Collective
credit and explicit publication decisions remain mandatory.
