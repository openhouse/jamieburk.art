# Open House

## What this is

Open House was a communal and participatory program at UC Santa Cruz whose
operating form was itself part of the work.

## Human situation

Participants needed a setting in which making, living, responsibility, public
activity, and learning could be practiced together rather than administered as
separate programs.

## What Jamie did

The canonical record supports describing Jamie as an instigator and sustaining
organizer who tended the conditions for collective decisions and public work.

## What became usable

A functioning communal residence, open Shop Shows, shared decision practices,
and documentation made participation concrete.

## Evidence and limits

The evidence does not support sole leadership or authorship of participant work.

## Open questions

Participant perspectives and the fuller program chronology remain appropriate
subjects for consent-aware recovery.

## Public projection

Public wording should describe the setting Jamie helped sustain while retaining
participant agency and collective ownership.
