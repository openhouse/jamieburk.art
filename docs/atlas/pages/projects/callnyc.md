# CallNYC

## What this is

CallNYC is an archived independent civic-data prototype. This page is an entry
point into the complete public-safe project slice retained by the typed
knowledge bank; it is not a replacement for that evidence.

## Human situation

Constituent-services data existed, but a resident still needed a legible path
from a citywide record to an issue, a district context, and a possible next
step.

## What Jamie did

The canonical record describes Jamie's independent product and translation
work while keeping the Council hackathon, the later data release, and the
prototype's development in their corrected chronology.

## What became usable

The work made a resident-facing interpretation available and now supplies a
bounded portfolio example of turning institutional data into a public product.

## Evidence and limits

The project slice retains public announcements, official and journalistic
sources, repository evidence, recovery research, corrections, anti-claims, and
the explicit boundary that CallNYC was not an official Council product or a
documented winner.

## Open questions

Unrecovered event pages, incomplete social slots, and participant-media rights
remain research or review questions. Non-recovery is not evidence of
nonexistence.

## Public projection

Only approved wording selected through the canonical publication decisions may
appear on the portfolio. Atlas adds navigation and explanation; it does not
publish the deeper bank automatically.
