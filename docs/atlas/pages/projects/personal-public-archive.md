# Jamie Burkart personal public archive

## What this is

This is a protected research layer for routing public traces toward stronger
professional sources, not a resume, chronology, or public personal archive.

## Human situation

Useful project leads can survive in personal accounts alongside ordinary life,
relationships, locations, and contexts that do not belong in professional
projection.

## What Jamie did

Jamie authorized bounded review of his public traces while retaining privacy,
authorship, rights, and relevance gates.

## What became usable

Aggregate accounting and selected public records can point researchers toward
project sources without exposing the underlying personal population.

## Evidence and limits

Complete disposition means coverage of the current control, not a lifetime
export. Missing interaction data is not zero, and reposts retain source
authorship.

## Open questions

Unreviewed media, unresolved slots, rights, consent, and professional relevance
remain protected review questions.

## Public projection

Keep raw records, exact identifiers, personal context, and protected locators
outside public Atlas output.
