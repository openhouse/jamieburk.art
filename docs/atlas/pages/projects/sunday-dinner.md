# Sunday Dinner

## What this is

Sunday Dinner is a participatory cultural format whose meaning lives in both
the gathering and the operating structure that allowed people to join it.

## Human situation

A room of artists and neighbors needed more than an audience arrangement. It
needed an invitation, a shared object, and enough structure for participation
to become possible.

## What Jamie did

The knowledge bank preserves Jamie's contribution to the format and its
infrastructure while keeping collaborator and participant work visible.

## What became usable

The project offered a repeatable way for a cultural room to become a temporary
participatory body rather than a passive event.

## Evidence and limits

Public-safe records may establish format, participation, and operating method.
They do not authorize participant identification, private records, or inflated
claims about downstream outcomes.

## Open questions

Any participant-level history or private documentation remains outside Atlas
unless consent, source position, and public purpose are established.

## Public projection

The portfolio should compose the clearest bounded example, not expose the
underlying community record.
