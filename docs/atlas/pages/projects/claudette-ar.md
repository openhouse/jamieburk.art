# Claudette's Theatre on Wheels AR

## What this is

This is the bounded record of a collaborative augmented-reality and video work
for Make Use Visible in Munich.

## Human situation

A mobile cultural project needed a digital layer that could make its material
encounterable in place without replacing the physical and collaborative work.

## What Jamie did

The canonical record preserves Jamie's documented creative-technology role
inside an augmented-reality collaboration with Michael Rees.
Anne Dufy Burkart, Julia Fredenburg, Jamie, and Claudette share the documented
source-video production context.

## What became usable

An augmented-reality and video experience connected the project to a public
program context.
