# KC Spaces Fund

## What this is

KC Spaces Fund was an emergency relief effort with public application,
donation, partnership, grant-recognition, and campaign surfaces.

## Human situation

Grassroots cultural spaces needed an understandable and actionable route to
emergency support during 2020.

## What Jamie did

The bank supports Jamie's behind-the-scenes web and operational infrastructure
role while keeping organizing, fundraising, allocation, and social publishing
with their named owners.

## What became usable

A consistent project identity connected public routes for applying, donating,
joining, fundraising, and recognizing recipient spaces.

## Evidence and limits

The records do not show that Jamie raised or allocated funds, authored campaign
posts, created every account, or caused donations.
Caitlin Horsmon, Jordan Carr, Kendell Harbin, and Megan Pobywajlo retain the
public organizer credit stated by the fundraiser; Allied Media Projects retains
the stated fiscal-sponsor role.
