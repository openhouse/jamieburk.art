# Horse Lords - Truthers video

## What this is

This is the bounded joint-maker record for the official 2016 music video.

## Human situation

The music called for a visual system capable of carrying repetition, variation,
text, material simplicity, and color into another medium.

## What Jamie did

Jamie Burkart and M.C. Schmidt co-created the video.

## What became usable

A completed official video was published and featured by NPR Music.

## Evidence and limits

The sources establish joint credit and the feature, not the collaborators'
division of labor, commissioning terms, commercial results, or reach. NPR's
description of method is attributed to Horse Lords saxophonist Andrew Bernstein.

## Open questions

Division of labor and any additional production history remain unclaimed.

## Public projection

Always credit Jamie Burkart and M.C. Schmidt together.
