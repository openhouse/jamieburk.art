# NTER CHNG

## What this is

NTER CHNG joined software, phones, projection, a constructed wall, and a gallery
setting so individual messages could enter a shared public dialogue.

## Human situation

Gallery visitors needed a low-barrier way to contribute through devices they
already carried while seeing private one-to-one exchange become many-to-many.

## What Jamie did

Jamie co-designed the work with Drew Bolton and Garrett Fuselier and
participated in its software, implementation, and installation planning.

## What became usable

A working participatory interface connected visitor input to a collective
architectural display and later entered America: Now and Here's Kansas City
program.

## Evidence and limits

Credit Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier as co-makers. Preserve
Mary Nichols's wall-engineering and construction support and Megan Mantia and
Elisha Stetson's actor credits. Planning records do not prove completion of
every listed task or assign the final labor split.
