# Commercial vacancy data

## What this is

This is a bounded pilot design for public commercial-vacancy and lease-cost
indicators, not a released government dataset.

## Human situation

Communities and public agencies needed a fuller baseline while confidential
filings and tenant information still required protection.

## What Jamie did

Jamie organized the source corpus and designed a smallest-serious pilot with
geographic aggregation, coverage reporting, suppression, methods, and required
fields.

## What became usable

The proposal creates a concrete implementation and decision path for testing
more informative indicators without exposing protected records.

## Evidence and limits

The artifact is a proposal. It does not establish agency adoption, a released
dataset, validated analysis, or policy outcome.

## Open questions

Institutional ownership, data agreements, field feasibility, and review of
privacy thresholds remain open.

## Public projection

Describe the work as pilot and implementation design, retaining every privacy,
coverage, suppression, and methodological boundary.
