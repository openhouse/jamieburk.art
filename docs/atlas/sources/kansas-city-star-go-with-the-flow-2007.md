## Atomic observations

The dossier contains 50 atomic observations. They preserve distinct source
statements instead of compressing the article into one narrative claim.

Publication and identity:

- A1 lead placement and A4 continuation
- Darryl Levings’s byline and the November 15, 2007 publication date
- the article’s printed `James Burkart` identity, normalized to Jamie only in
  present-day composition
- Libby Hendon, James Burkart, and Laura Mattingly as the named traveling crew
- Laura Mattingly’s steering credit in the front-page photograph caption
- Joshua Corban and Brian Loden photograph-credit context

Material and operating system:

- three-week construction period
- discarded building remnants, civic refuse, and reused syrup drums
- approximate 12-by-13-foot dimensions
- two bicycles linked to a paddlewheel
- floating as the ordinary mode and applying power around hazards
- reported two-to-four-mile-per-hour pace
- source-attributed spontaneous-navigation and technology positions

Route and participation:

- July 21 departure from Kansas City’s West Bottoms with friends aboard
- more than 1,000 miles traveled by publication
- a publication-time position south of Baton Rouge
- friends joining around St. Louis, Memphis, and Vicksburg
- food, water, and interaction stops
- Jamie’s reported practice of inviting people encountered aboard
- separately attributed Chain of Rocks accounts from Laura Mattingly and Libby
  Hendon

Interruption and recovery:

- 14 reported prior Coast Guard encounters, kept distinct from separately
  named contacts with other authorities
- boarding north of Vicksburg, the reported design assessment, and beaching
- a 51-day interruption
- hospitality, legal assistance, raft retrieval, temporary work, and a
  community sendoff
- repair and adaptation through a lifted bow, drag cones, oars, and a marine
  radio

Endpoint and interpretation boundaries:

- Natchez and fog-delay snapshots
- expected salt-water geography below New Orleans
- uncertainty about when salt would be reached
- no expectation of seeing the Gulf from the raft at publication time
- Jamie’s attributed descriptions of the river as invisible, polluted,
  associated with violence, divisive, historical, and industrial space
- Jamie’s attributed hope that the river might awaken cultural connection
  between Kansas City’s West Bottoms and Delta towns

## Claims

The dossier contains 3 claims. None is currently projected onto the website.

1. `CLM-WATERWAYS-KCSTAR-COLLECTIVE-EXPEDITION`: the article attributes the
   idea to Jamie and documents Jamie, Libby Hendon, and Laura Mattingly on a
   collective reclaimed-material, bicycle-powered expedition traveling more
   than 1,000 miles from Kansas City into Louisiana.
2. `CLM-WATERWAYS-KCSTAR-ADAPTIVE-OPERATIONS`: the article documents an
   improvised operating system adapting after a 51-day interruption through
   community, legal, material, labor, repair, and communications support.
3. `CLM-WATERWAYS-KCSTAR-PARTICIPATORY-METHOD`: the article supports a bounded
   interpretation of the project as lived river inquiry organized around
   encounters, invitation, and possible cultural connection.

## Anti-claims

Atlas explicitly rejects using this dossier to say that:

- Jamie alone designed, built, steered, operated, or completed the work;
- the article proves arrival at salt water or the Gulf of Mexico;
- the article supplies a complete route, participant roster, or contribution
  ledger;
- encounters establish measured impact;
- the reporting constitutes engineering certification or blanket regulatory
  approval;
- possession of the PDF grants republication rights;
- front-page prominence establishes endorsement, reach, or impact; or
- 14 is a combined count across every authority named in the article.

## Source limitations

- The article is a mid-voyage source, not final-outcome evidence.
- Its route, roster, and contribution accounts are incomplete.
- Jamie’s closing statements are attributed perspective, not community
  consensus.
- No measured outcome study is present.
- Source and image rights remain uncleared.
- Artifact metadata must not replace publication metadata.
- Text extraction cannot substitute for page-image review.
- Contemporary sources disagree on whether the consequential Coast Guard stop
  occurred on September 15 or September 18; the dossier does not silently
  select a date.
