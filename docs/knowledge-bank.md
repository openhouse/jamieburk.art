## Claim Strength Rules

- Use the strongest accurate claim, but keep the verb honest.
- Prefer public artifacts, aggregate counts, approved summaries, and
  contribution-framed language.
- Mark claims as approved, public-safe summary, needs approval, evidence needed,
  omit from production, or protected.
- If a claim depends on private records, summarize the evidence class without
  exposing the records.
- Keep collective work collective: co-built, contributed to, helped structure,
  supported, stewarded.
- Do not claim sole authorship, official authority, legal analysis, policy
  ownership, or causality unless the evidence supports it.
