# Knowledge Bank

This document is the public-safe editorial source of truth for jamieburk.art.

The Knowledge Bank answers:

- What can the site say?
- What evidence supports it?
- Where may the claim appear?
- What wording is safe?
- What is too strong?
- What is open?
- What is protected?
- What blocks production?

## Rules

- The website projects from this bank; it does not invent claims in components.
- Claims must be public-safe, evidence-backed, or clearly marked as open.
- Private source material may be named by class but not exposed.
- Collective work must use collective-credit language.
- Exact metrics require explicit approval in `docs/proofs-bank.md` or safe wording must be used.
- Public TODOs do not belong in public UI.
- Missing or protected evidence is not a defect when exposure would violate privacy, consent, client trust, law, civic sensitivity, or community safety.

## Knowledge Bank States

### Known

Public-safe and evidence-backed enough to say.

### Open

Needs approval, citation, screenshot, stronger evidence, or revised wording.

### Protected

Intentionally omitted because privacy, consent, law, civic sensitivity, client trust, or community safety requires it.

## Relationship To The Site

The website is a projection of this bank. It should be shorter, clearer, and shaped for hiring managers, public-sector readers, referrers, collaborators, and non-specialists.

The bank may hold stronger claims, limits, support levels, and approval states. The public site should use the smallest accurate claim that makes Jamie's role legible.

## Relationship To Production

Production may only become indexable after all production blockers are resolved and Jamie approves the exact reviewed staging commit.

Production indexing is never inferred from `APP_ENV=production`. It requires `NEXT_PUBLIC_ROBOTS_POLICY=index`, production URLs, public contact approval, public-safety checks, production preflight checks, and an explicit go/no-go.
