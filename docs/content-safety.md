# Content Safety

Do not publish private emails, raw transcripts, private coalition notes,
legal-review materials, health or financial details, private correspondence,
unapproved photos, private fonts, credentials, stakeholder lists, internal
analytics, client-private material, or raw community records.

Use public-safe summaries, representative diagrams, careful collective-work
language, and explicit protected-boundary notes.

Protected material may be named as intentionally omitted. The site should make
boundaries legible without exposing the underlying private source material.
