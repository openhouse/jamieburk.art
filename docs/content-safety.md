# Content Safety

This repo is public. The website is a projection of public-safe knowledge, not
an archive browser.

## General Rule

Use public-safe summaries, approved artifacts, redacted examples,
representative diagrams, public links, aggregate counts, and careful
collective-work language.

Protected absence is allowed. It is acceptable to say that private material is
intentionally omitted; it is not acceptable to publish the private material.

## Never Publish

- private emails
- raw transcripts
- private coalition notes
- legal-review materials
- health or financial details
- private correspondence
- private stakeholder lists
- internal analytics
- credentials
- client-private materials
- raw community records
- unreviewed photos, screenshots, names, quotes, or collaborator details
- private, proprietary, or unlicensed font files

## Project Rules

credentials, vendor terms, or internal operations documents.

legal-review materials, stakeholder lists, raw strategy context, private emails,
or quotes not reviewed for public use. Not legal advice.

Private coalition records, admin details, source files, contact lists, strategy
notes, field notes, and unreviewed collaborator details stay out of the repo.

service.

records.

stories, or unreviewed photos.

details.

production SaaS, AI replacement for judgment, legal advice, medical advice, or
