# Content Safety

`jamieburk.art` is a public proof surface, not a public dump of source material.

## Publishable

- Public contact links: email, LinkedIn, GitHub, location.
- Resume PDF with authorized job-search contact information.
- Public-safe summaries of professional work.
- Public archives, public GitHub repositories, public press links, and approved
  public artifacts.
- Defensible metrics that match the Knowledge Bank and approved resume.

## Do Not Publish

- Raw transcripts, private calls, private coalition notes, legal-review
  materials, private correspondence, private stakeholder lists, private
  community records, health or financial details, private analytics, credentials,
  internal dashboards, client-private materials, or unapproved photos and quotes.
- Private local paths or references to personal source folders.
- Phone number in HTML.
- Private pilots or relationship context for Source-Backed Team Memory.

## Review Rule

If a claim is useful but not yet supported by the Knowledge Bank, leave it out
of the public app and add a review note outside public-facing copy.
