# Content safety

This site is a public-safe portfolio, not a private archive. It should publish
summaries, representative patterns, approved public artifacts, redacted examples,
and careful collective-work language.

## Do not publish

- Private emails or private correspondence
- Raw transcripts
- Private coalition notes
- Legal-review materials
- Private stakeholder lists
- Health, financial, family, or therapy details
- Unapproved photos, quotes, names, or collaborator credits
- Client-private analytics, customer data, vendor data, or operating details
- Private or proprietary fonts
- Credentials, private keys, tokens, or `.env` files
- Raw community records, attendance lists, addresses, or guest data

## Publish instead

- Public-safe summaries
- Redacted examples
- Representative diagrams
- Representative artifact types
- Approved public links
- Careful collective-work language: helped, supported, contributed to,
  co-built, helped structure, helped build and steward
- Known / Open / Protected notes on sensitive work

## Production gate

Production pages must not render unresolved approval TODOs, placeholder resume
language, private-source markers, or copy that implies a claim is approved when
it still needs review. The safety checks are a backstop; they do not replace
Jamie's final content and public-safety approval.
