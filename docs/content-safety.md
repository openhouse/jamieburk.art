# Content Safety

The portfolio is public. Treat every committed sentence as something that could
be read by a hiring manager, collaborator, funder, journalist, or community
member.

## Do Not Publish

- private emails or private correspondence
- raw meeting exports or private transcripts
- private coalition notes or legal-review materials
- stakeholder lists, signup lists, phone numbers, or private contact records
- health, financial, credential, or client-private details
- internal analytics dashboards or exact revenue breakdowns
- unapproved collaborator names, quotes, photos, screenshots, or artifacts
- private/proprietary font files
- source paths from Jamie's local machines or personal cloud storage

## Use Instead

- public-safe summaries
- approved artifacts
- redacted examples
- representative diagrams
- aggregate metrics with careful boundaries
- collective-work language where the work was collective
- Known / Open / Protected notes

When a claim is useful but not ready, keep it in `docs/knowledge-bank/` with an
approval state. Do not project it into public copy until the wording, surface,
and boundaries are clear.
