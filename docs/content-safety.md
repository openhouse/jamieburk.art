# Content Safety

This site uses selected public-safe summaries. Private notes, raw transcripts, legal-review materials, client-sensitive data, community records, unapproved photos, private correspondence, credentials, raw analytics, and private contact lists are intentionally omitted.

`docs/knowledge-bank.md`, `docs/proofs-bank.md`, and `docs/public-claims-inventory.md` are the public-safe knowledge layer for claims in this repo. They may include strong professional claims, aggregate metrics, source classes, relationships, boundaries, and approval state. They must not include raw private source material.

## Default Rules

- Use collective-work language.
- Do not publish private materials.
- Do not publish unapproved names, quotes, screenshots, metrics, or photos.
- Do not expose private analytics, revenue details, dashboards, credentials, private contact lists, or user-level records.
- Move uncertain items into launch blockers, claims inventory, or review notes, not public UI.
- Use Known / Open / Protected.

## Known / Open / Protected

Known is public-safe and evidence-backed enough to say.

Open needs approval, citation, screenshot, stronger evidence, or revised wording.

Protected is intentionally omitted because privacy, consent, client trust, law, civic sensitivity, or community safety requires it.

## Representative Artifacts

Use "Representative artifacts," not "Artifact gallery," when artifacts are redacted, recreated, summarized, or illustrative rather than literal screenshots.

### 196 / Sunday Dinner

No guest lists, attendance records, addresses, private stories, phone numbers, private scores, or unapproved photos.

### KC Town Hall

No private financial, legal, property, banking, partner, or stakeholder details.

### Source-Backed Team Memory

Early method / research / consulting practice. Not a production SaaS, AI replacement for judgment, legal/medical advice system, surveillance system, or private archive browser.

## Scanner Policy

Safety scripts should be specific, not brittle.

They should scan production-facing app content strictly, scan docs for real secrets and private paths, and allow safety docs to mention risk terms in policy context.

Do not block every occurrence of words like private, transcript, legal, secret, Trade Gothic, Verlag, Gotham Rounded, Maria, or collaborator when those words appear inside safety guidance.
