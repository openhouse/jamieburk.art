# Content Safety

This site should stay public-safe, referral-ready, and careful about claims.

## Do Not Publish

- Private emails or private correspondence.
- Raw transcripts.
- Private coalition notes.
- Legal-review materials.
- Private stakeholder lists.
- Health, therapy, medication, insurance, financial, trust, banking, tax, or debt details.
- Private client details.
- Private analytics dashboards.
- Customer data.
- Raw Sunday Dinner guest data.
- Residency records.
- Attendance records.
- Unapproved photos.
- Private fonts.
- Credentials, passwords, admin screenshots, or secrets.
- Anything that implies official government service where the project was an independent civic prototype.

## Use These Labels

- public-safe summary
- redacted example
- archived prototype
- not an official current service
- selected public-safe materials available on request
- TODO: Jamie approval required before publishing this detail.

## Use These Verbs

- co-built
- contributed to
- helped structure
- stewarded
- supported
- translated

## Preserve

Keep Known / Open / Protected sections wherever they already exist. They are a strong pattern for making unfinished or sensitive work legible without overclaiming.
