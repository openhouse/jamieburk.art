# Composite release status

## Agent-verifiable

The composite requires deterministic lifecycle, citation, corpus, public-safety,
route, visual-evidence, build, staging, and production-mode preflight gates. It
also requires two stable independent semantic scorecards on one exact candidate.
Current results are recorded in the feature-knowledge-k QA run history and PR.

## Human or external

Collaborator corroboration, unfamiliar-reader sessions, rights and consent,
market response, production observation, and Jamie's exact-candidate approval
remain separate. The machine-readable human-status file keeps empty evidence
explicit and rejects false closure.

## Production authority

An agent-verifiable pass may support `framework-ready` or `application-ready`.
It does not authorize deployment, DNS or indexing changes, or a
`production-ready` claim. Production authority remains with Jamie after review
of the exact commit and postdeploy plan.
