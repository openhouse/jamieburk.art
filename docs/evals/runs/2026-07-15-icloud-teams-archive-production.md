## Archive method

The three directories are large working archives and may contain iCloud
placeholders. The review used their overview and orientation files first,
then selected a small number of project anchors for hydration and close
reading. It did not equate a folder listing, filename, or search hit with a
verified accomplishment.

Every selected item was routed through the knowledge lifecycle:

1. orient from the archive overview;
2. hydrate and close-read the underlying artifact;
3. separate direct support, context, non-support, and private boundaries;
4. compare the result to current proof claims and open research;
5. create or strengthen a canonical claim only when the evidence supported it;
6. decide website projection separately from claim maturity;
7. run schema, graph, privacy, claim-discipline, build, and preflight checks.
