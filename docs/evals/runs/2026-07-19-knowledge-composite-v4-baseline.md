## Reason For Version Four

Two independent version-three holdouts rejected the first NYC Artist Coalition
shared-folder candidate. Both reproduced its Git and content fingerprints, but
found that the fingerprint omitted most of the new evidence layer and that the
candidate-bound mosaic review was stale. They also found that the new public
claim projection was outside composition-budget accounting, the recovered
archive population had not entered the survivorship register, and aggregate
semantic checks could allow a required statement to survive in the wrong
artifact.

Version four treats those findings as instrument and candidate defects rather
than averaging them away. It:

- fingerprints the canonical archive module, graph records, public census,
  Wiki records, public case-study projection, and focused checks;
- records the NYCAC shared folder in the archival survivorship exact set;
- budgets the new claim projection for the NYCAC case-study instance and binds
  its rendered source;
- validates file-specific access, authorship, collective-credit, application,
  human-gate, and public-boundary semantics; and
- requires a fresh candidate-bound mosaic review and two new independent
  holdouts.

## Preserved Invariants

- The lineage baseline, donor A-N decisions, canonical lifecycle, frozen 27
  portfolio evals, protected custody, and human gates remain unchanged.
- Accessible-population closure is not represented as historical completeness,
  authorship, publication permission, rights clearance, or individual credit.
- The private item-level manifest remains outside Git; only aggregate public-safe
  accounting and opaque custody metadata enter the repository.
- Earlier holdout receipts are historical evidence only and cannot satisfy this
  run.
- Code-review readiness remains separate from application sharing, production
  approval, rights, consent, hands-on QA, and indexing.

## Starting Evidence

Before version four was frozen, the first NYCAC candidate passed its seven
focused criteria at `100/100`, the canonical graph and citation checks, Node 26
typecheck and lint, production build, noindex runtime smoke, responsive browser
checks, and private-locator leakage checks. The holdouts nevertheless returned
`iterate`, demonstrating that machine closure did not override structural
evaluation defects.

## Decision

Repair the candidate and evaluator together, rerun the full deterministic
suite, freeze a new exact implementation commit, bind the mosaic review to its
candidate fingerprint, and request two fresh read-only independent holdouts.
