## PR-026: Margaret Morse Lens

This lens asks whether the portfolio preserves Jamie's artistic method rather
than translating his entire life into organizational utility.

A passing candidate shows:

- one compact threshold where artistic, civic, technical, and social work
  remain visibly connected;
- an inhabitable situation that Jamie made and tended, not merely an event he
  administered;
- attention to atmosphere, hospitality, participation, place, documentation,
  and shared authorship; and
- a present-tense explanation of why those qualities matter to Jamie's
  operating practice now.

The lens does not require a detached art-history section. It asks the current
hiring composition to preserve the source of Jamie's distinct way of building
systems.

## PR-027: Warren Sack Lens

This lens asks whether Jamie's recursive social-systems thinking is concrete
enough to inspect.

A passing candidate makes this sequence legible across at least three projects:

1. observe relationships around a system;
2. model or prototype those relationships;
3. build an interface or inhabitable structure;
4. expose it to use; and
5. learn from response and revise.

Technical nouns count only when they explain what became possible. The lens
also requires explicit boundaries between an original application and
invention of a field-wide concept, a classroom prototype and a deployed
product, and Jamie's contribution and collaborative authorship.

frozen eval, public candidate, public *Open House* source, and public-safe
source summaries. It does not receive raw academic records, this patch's
