## Proof-Note Request

Ask one bounded question at a time:

> I am building a source-backed record of our shared work. From your own
> memory, what did you directly observe me create, coordinate, decide, or carry
> forward? What was collective, what should I correct, and what should remain
> private? Please say separately whether I may keep your response as private
> background, paraphrase it publicly, name you, or quote you. No response is
> completely fine and will not be interpreted as agreement or disagreement.

AI-assisted archival synthesis must be labeled as synthesis. It cannot stand
in for first-hand collaborator testimony.

Jamie owns outreach unless a named research steward is recorded later. A
fallback source may narrow a claim or preserve project-level evidence; it may
not be treated as a substitute for first-hand role corroboration. Consent to
retain a response, permission to paraphrase, permission to name, and permission
to quote are separate states and remain ungranted until explicitly recorded.
