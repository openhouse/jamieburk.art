# External Evidence Protocols

These protocols prepare work that agents cannot complete. Empty evidence is an
honest state, not a prompt to generate a substitute.

## Unprimed Hiring Review

Use five people: recruiter, hiring manager, technical or operations peer,
public-interest technology leader, and trusted referrer. Give each only the
exact candidate URL and SHA.

After 30 seconds ask:

1. What role is Jamie seeking?
2. What does he help a team make usable?
3. What would you click next?

After five minutes ask for three concrete proofs, Jamie's action in each, the
bounded result, the strongest evidence, and anything confusing or hard to
trust. Record coded aggregates, not names or raw private notes. Four of five
must identify the role family; three must name three accurate deliveries; no
more than one may infer official government ownership or solo policy causality.

## Collaborator Review

Send the exact public wording, source position, contribution boundary, and
proposed use. Ask whether the collaborator confirms, complicates, corrects,
declines, or did not observe the claim. Permission to quote, name, show media,
and paraphrase are separate decisions. An AI proof note is never collaborator
testimony.

## Visual Rights And Consent

For every selected image or artifact record subject, bounded date, context,
creator, rights holder, permission, depicted-person consent, public-safety
review, alt text, caption, claim relationship, and what the image does not
establish. Custody, public availability, and inclusion in an archive do not
equal permission.

## Application Response

Maintain only bounded non-identifying aggregates by date window and role family:
applications, referrals, screens, interviews, offers, recurring confusion, and
the resulting content decision. Small samples, selection effects, employer
behavior, and labor-market conditions prevent causal attribution to the site.

## Runtime And Release

After Jamie approves the exact SHA, capture deployed SHA, prior release,
rollback command, DNS, TLS, apex and `www` behavior, preserved subdomains,
health, canonical URLs, robots, sitemap, resume HTML, resume PDF noindex, and
key-route smoke. Local build evidence cannot satisfy this protocol.

## Minimized Receipt

Every public-safe receipt includes:

- protocol ID;
- exact candidate SHA;
- date;
- coded observer role;
- observed tasks and disposition;
- aggregate finding;
- permission scope;
- next decision;
- no names, contact details, correspondence, authenticated state, or raw notes.
