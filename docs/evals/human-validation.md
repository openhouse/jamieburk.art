# Human Validation and Launch Handoff

The machine system may identify what needs review. It may not perform or invent
human review.

## Hiring-reader protocol

Use the exact candidate digest. Give a hiring reader no archive context and ask:

1. What work does Jamie do?
2. When would you bring him into a project?
3. What did he personally do in the proof you remember?
4. What became usable?
5. What would you inspect or do next?

Retain only a dated public-safe aggregate: candidate digest, reader category,
answers correct/unclear, strongest confusion, and resulting content decision.
Do not commit names, contact details, private feedback, or recruiting strategy.

## Collaborator corroboration

Ask about one bounded action or role claim at a time. Offer confirm, revise,
decline, and keep-private options. Preserve collective credit and do not treat
silence as approval. Store only the public-safe disposition and claim IDs.

## Media rights and consent

Factual relevance, representation, copyright, privacy, and participant consent
are separate checks. Existing approved media may remain. New participant images
stay offline until the exact use is cleared.

## Exact candidate approval

Approval must name:

- full candidate revision;
- candidate digest;
- approved action: share for applications, deploy staging, deploy production,
  or enable production indexing;
- any exclusions or expiring conditions;
- reviewer and date.

Approval of a proposal or earlier version does not transfer to a changed
candidate.

## Production cutover

Before deployment, record reviewed SHA, candidate digest, image tag, rollback
target, robots policy, and owner. After deployment, verify health, primary
routes, resume, contact, citations, robots, sitemap, console, and displayed
revision where available.

The post-deploy state cannot pass before an approved deployment.
