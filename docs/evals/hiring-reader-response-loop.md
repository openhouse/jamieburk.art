# Hiring Reader Response Loop

This protocol measures whether the portfolio creates accurate understanding and
a useful next action for the people Jamie needs to reach. Preparing this file
does not satisfy `PR-019`; only independent human review of an exact candidate
commit can do that.

## Review Population

Use at least three independent readers across these perspectives:

- a hiring manager for technical project management, product operations, or
  implementation;
- a recruiter or experienced referrer familiar with those roles;
- a civic technology, public-interest technology, or public-sector delivery
  leader.

Do not brief readers on the intended answer. Do not publish their names,
employers, correspondence, or application details without separate permission.

## Tasks

### After 30 Seconds

1. What kind of role is Jamie seeking?
2. What does Jamie help a team do?
3. What would you click next?

### After Two Minutes

1. Name three projects or proof points you remember.
2. For each, what did Jamie personally do?
3. What became usable, changed, or easier to continue?
4. What, if anything, feels private, overstated, broken, confusing, or hard to
   trust?
5. Would you interview, refer, continue reading, or stop? Why?

## Decision Rule

`PR-019` remains `pending-human-review` until three dated scorecards name the
same exact commit. Findings must be dispositioned as accepted, deferred,
rejected, or requiring research. Silence, rejection, or an application outcome
must never be attributed to the site without evidence.
