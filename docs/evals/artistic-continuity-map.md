# Artistic Continuity Map

This map is evidence for `PR-026`. It checks that Jamie Burkart's artistic,
civic, technical, and social practices remain connected on a primary public
surface without displacing the portfolio's hiring purpose.

| Dimension | Public evidence | Present operating significance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artistic | *Open House* was a 10-day communal gallery experiment Jamie initiated and tended. | The work begins by making an inhabitable situation, not by treating people as inputs to an administrative process. |
| Social | Housemates shared responsibility and decisions; participants made and distributed documentation. | Participation and shared authorship are part of the operating model. |
| Civic | The group met with Porter staff, cleaned the space, and marked a new boundary around technical equipment. | Public and institutional response becomes material for collective repair and revision. |
| Technical | Jamie described the project through social software and representations of relationships; the About page connects this lineage to later interfaces and systems. | Technical structure is used to make relationships legible and actionable, not as an isolated tool display. |

The About page states the differentiating concerns directly: invitation,
place, atmosphere, shared authorship, and careful attention. It cites the
independently published *Good Times* feature rather than rendering protected
faculty records.
