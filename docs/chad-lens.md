# Chad Lens

Chad's lens is the public-copy standard for this portfolio. It is a practical
test for whether Jamie is visible, the work is legible, and the claim can be
defended without overexposure.

Before public copy ships, ask:

1. Is Jamie visible as the actor?
2. Does the sentence answer "toward what end?"
3. Is the language legible to a hiring manager, HR reader, public-sector
   reviewer, or warm referrer?
4. Are specialized terms translated once?
5. Are acronyms spelled out or avoided?
6. Are arrows and symbols avoided where words are clearer?
7. Are unfamiliar entities defined?
8. Does the copy protect collective credit and avoid overclaiming?
9. Does it avoid reducing stewardship, facilitation, convening, coordination, or
   analysis to mere tracking?
10. Does it make the work impressive without making the reader decode it?

## Preferred Action Language

Use verbs that make Jamie visible without claiming sole ownership where the work
was collective:

- built
- structured
- translated
- coordinated
- stewarded
- facilitated
- mapped
- documented
- synthesized
- supported
- co-built
- contributed to
- helped make usable

## Translate Once

Some words are useful but need plain-language support.

- under-structured: loosely defined work where requirements, ownership,
  documentation, or handoffs are not yet clear
- data: open-data workflows, dataset documentation, data visualization, or
  data-product requirements
- maintainers: local calendar organizers, editors, or community event stewards
- lightweight: simple, repeatable, low-overhead
- MVP: first working version

## Public Copy Rule

The website is not the proof bank. The website is a composed public surface that
projects from the proof bank for a reader with limited time and a real decision
to make.
