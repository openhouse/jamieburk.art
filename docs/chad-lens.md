# Chad Lens

Chad's lens is the public-copy standard for this portfolio. It is a practical
test for whether Jamie is visible, the work is legible, and the claim can be
defended without overexposure.

Before public copy ships, ask:

1. Is Jamie visible as the actor?
2. Does the sentence answer "toward what end?"
3. Is the language legible to a hiring manager, HR reader, public-sector
   reviewer, technical-operations lead, or warm referrer?
4. Are specialized terms translated once?
5. Are acronyms spelled out or avoided?
6. Are symbols and arrows avoided where words are clearer?
7. Are unfamiliar entities defined?
8. Does the copy protect collective credit and avoid overclaiming?
9. Does it avoid reducing stewardship, facilitation, convening, coordination, or
   analysis to mere "tracking"?
10. Does it make the work impressive without making the reader decode it?

## Preferred Verbs

Use verbs that make Jamie visible without claiming sole ownership where the work
was collective:

- built
- co-built
- created
- stewarded
- synthesized
- structured
- translated
- coordinated
- documented
- maintained
- supported
- contributed to
- helped teams use

## Translate Once

Some terms are useful but need plain-language support the first time they appear.

- under-structured: work where the need is real, but requirements, workflows,
  ownership, documentation, and handoffs are not yet clear
- tracking meetings: synthesizing meetings into decision records, action
  trackers, open questions, and stakeholder next steps
- data: public datasets, source context, dataset documentation, data-product
  requirements, or analysis outputs
- source-backed: tied to inspectable source material and reviewable decisions
- lightweight: simple, repeatable, low-overhead
- maintainer: the person or team responsible for keeping a system usable after
  launch

## Public Copy Rule

The website is not the knowledge bank. The website is a composed public surface
that projects from the knowledge bank for a reader with limited time and a real
decision to make.
