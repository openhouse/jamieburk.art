# Chad Lens

This is the public-copy standard for the portfolio, resume, referral copy, and
case-study language.

Jamie creates operating structure for complex public-facing teams, turning
emerging or under-structured work into requirements, workflows, documentation,
decision trails, launch support, onboarding materials, and durable handoffs.

The warmer truth underneath that sentence is: structure grows out of the
material. Jamie often finds a nascent cultural, organizational, conceptual, or
technical pattern that already exists and gives it resilient public-safe form.
The professional copy should preserve that dignity without making a hiring
reader decode it.

## Test Each Public Sentence

1. Is Jamie visible as an actor?
2. Does the sentence answer "toward what end?"
3. Is the language legible to a hiring manager, referral partner, or civic-tech
   reviewer?
4. Are project names and acronyms defined where needed?
5. Does the copy protect collective credit and avoid overclaiming?
6. Does it avoid reducing stewardship, facilitation, convening, coordination, or
   analysis to mere tracking?
7. Does it make the work impressive without publishing private evidence?

## Preferred Verbs

- created
- built
- co-built
- structured
- stewarded
- translated
- documented
- coordinated
- facilitated
- supported
- contributed to
- helped make usable

## Avoid

- tracked, when the work was coordination, stewardship, analysis, or operating
  structure
- owned, when responsibility was collective
- led the movement
- caused, when the record supports contribution rather than sole causality
- official, unless the source is official
- guaranteed
- automated trust
- AI-powered truth
- private archive browser
