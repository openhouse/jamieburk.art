## Claim Tiers

**Public-ready:** Supported by the current resume, public or public-safe source packages, and/or non-sensitive archive analysis. Safe for site copy if phrased carefully.

**Public-ready with approval:** Factually supported but needs Jamie or collaborator approval before appearing on public pages because it touches consent, attribution, screenshots, or active civic work.

**Resume-only for now:** Appropriate in the supplied production resume, but the site should soften the verb or omit details until public proof is approved.

**Internal only:** Useful for confidence or future source work, but do not publish.

**Reject / avoid:** Unsupported, overbroad, or likely to create public-safety risk.
