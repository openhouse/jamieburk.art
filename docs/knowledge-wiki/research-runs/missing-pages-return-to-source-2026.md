# Missing Pages Return-To-Source Run, 2026

## Research Question

What do we see when we reopen representative original sources before composing
the Wiki pages we most wish existed, and which parts of the account remain
open, protected, or dependent on human review?

## Coverage Declaration

This is a bounded source return for ten named pages. It is not a claim to have
read every record in Jamie's archive. It deliberately samples different source
classes and project periods, records what was directly inspected, and keeps a
librarian and collaborator question open where the evidence remains thin.

| Source class | Access position | Fresh finding | Boundary carried forward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WOW List production dump dated July 22, 2017 | Protected, aggregate-only | Archive metadata and protected counts reproduced 1,846 user rows and 16,142 post or event rows. | No person-level row, contact field, follow, star, or geolocation record entered the repository. |
| Sunday Dinner workbook | Protected, aggregate-only | Sheet structure and an event-by-guest matrix confirmed that invitations and attendance were maintained as operational data over time. | This pass was not a new full-workbook census; names, contact fields, and row-level attendance remain private. |
| KC Town Hall municipal proposal packet | Public government record | Visual review of the Finance pages confirmed the "completed 2019" Phase One label, $189,629 total, and the separate later phase. | The packet does not independently assign Jamie the general-contractor title or prove completion of later redevelopment. |
| Commercial Rent Stabilization running minutes | Protected collaboration record with public-safe metadata | The record defines readable campaign memory and explicit consent limits for sensitive tenant, business, and landlord information. | Meeting notes do not automatically authorize publication or establish policy outcomes. |
| Commercial Rent Stabilization provenance redline | Protected working document with public-safe metadata | The document distinguishes legislative source layers from individual drafting authorship and labels itself unofficial discussion material, not legal advice. | It does not prove authorship of every provision or adoption of the compared language. |

| 2011 NTER CHNG archived site | Public web archive | The recovered page identifies an interactive texting installation by Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier. | The page alone does not establish every implementation detail, visitor outcome, or later exhibition history. |

## Present-Tense Reading

The reread strengthens a cross-medium account of Jamie as someone who notices
how people enter a situation and then builds operational forms around that
entry. It also makes the danger of compression clearer. A hiring narrative can
erase artistic inquiry, collective labor, failed work, and private
circumstances if the Wiki does not preserve them as explicit boundaries and
questions.

## What Changed

- The KC Town Hall page now leads with the packet's completed cold-shell scope
  while keeping the general-contractor title under inquiry.
- The WOW List page uses reproduced aggregate counts and explicitly withholds
  all person-level records.
- The civic-interface page uses the Council transcript's actual institutional
  framing rather than inferred motive.
- The participatory-practice page omits vulnerable third-party details that do
  not help establish Jamie's role.
- The chronology distinguishes selected evidence from exhaustive life history.
- Handoff and adoption remain a governed-open capability rather than a polished
  portfolio-wide claim.

## What The Sources Do Not Establish

The inspected sources do not establish sole authorship of collective work,
complete causal accounts of legislation or institutional change, successful
handoff for every project, permission to republish protected artifacts, or a
complete inventory of Jamie's practice.

## Librarian Questions

The automated checks cannot answer these. Jamie and collaborators remain the
reviewers for them:

1. Which recipient can best describe a system Jamie handed off and what they
   could operate without him?
2. Which contractor, architect, engineer, or collaborator can corroborate
   Jamie's KC Town Hall coordination role?
3. Which failed or abandoned project most usefully complicates the current
   participation lineage?
4. Which rights-cleared visual artifacts best show the work without exposing a
   participant or private record?
5. What source does Jamie remember that would materially revise one of these
   pages?

## Re-Entry Rule

New material enters through the existing source, claim, inquiry, correction,
and projection lifecycle. Full access authorization permits research; it does
not by itself make a source public-safe, rights-cleared, consented, or suitable
for the portfolio.
