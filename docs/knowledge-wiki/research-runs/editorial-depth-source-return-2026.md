# Editorial Depth Source Return

## Coverage Declaration

This was a bounded source return for four pages identified after the first ten
missing pages were completed. It did not reopen every source for every project.
It returned to the source classes most capable of correcting each proposed
page and preserved private material outside Git.

| Page | Source position reopened | Present finding | Boundary carried forward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identity Systems as Shared Infrastructure | Canonical social-account and campaign records | Identity establishment and attributable collaborator use are distinct from post authorship and project ownership. | Administrator history, credentials, private design files, and unlicensed media remain protected. |
| Learning Through Making | Protected UCSC narrative record and independent Open House reporting | The record joins embodied inquiry, strong analytical work, prototypes, participation, and recursive revision. | No grades, identifiers, raw evaluations, blanket endorsements, or deployment inflation. |
| Places Where the Work Became Real | Public and protected place-bearing project records | Galleries, waterways, tables, cultural spaces, neighborhood routes, and construction sites changed what could be learned and built. | Place does not authorize access, identify participants, or convert people into evidence. |
| What Is at Stake for Me | Canonical practice authorities plus Jamie's private voice guide as drafting context | A first-person interpretation can connect documented practices while remaining visibly pending Jamie's authorship review. | The private voice corpus is not quoted, linked, published, or treated as independent accomplishment evidence. |

## Present-Tense Reading

The first ten missing pages established the Wiki's structural range. This pass
adds four forms of depth that should not be collapsed into project summaries:
shared identity as an operating layer, learning through embodied making, place
as a condition of knowledge, and Jamie's unresolved first-person account of why
the work matters.

## What Changed

- Identity work is separated into establishment, design, administration,
  artifact authorship, and collective ownership.
- The artistic and educational lineage now includes both writing strength and
  the documented tension between embodied work and assigned essay forms.
- Place is treated as a specific condition of work rather than atmosphere.
- First-person stakes have a governed drafting state instead of being inferred
  from career evidence or silently written in Jamie's voice.

## What The Sources Do Not Establish

- that Jamie authored every visual artifact or shared-account message;
- that every classroom prototype became a deployed system;
- that present-day access or safety follows from a historical place record;
- that participants consent to identification because an event was public;
- that an AI-assisted first-person draft is Jamie-approved; or
- that private source access is publication permission.

## Protected Handling

The pass used protected academic and voice material only for bounded review.
No raw evaluation text, grade, student identifier, private voice passage,
source path, private address, credential, participant row, or unapproved image
was copied into the Wiki. Public-safe summaries and canonical stable IDs carry
the provenance boundary.

## Librarian Questions

- Which identity artifacts have clear artifact-level authorship and licenses?
- Which learning projects have surviving public documentation or collaborator
  accounts?
- Which places can be represented without exposing private addresses or
  participants?
- Which sentences in the first-person draft does Jamie recognize, reject, or
  want to rewrite?

## Re-Entry Rule

New evidence returns through source, claim, inquiry, correction, rights,
consent, and projection review. A first-person revision also requires explicit
Jamie authorship review; no machine command may resolve that gate. Continue
through the [Editorial Depth Evaluation](../evaluations/editorial-depth.md).
