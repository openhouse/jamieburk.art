# Knowledge Wiki Family Closure Research Run

## Question

How can five independently developed Knowledge Wiki branches become one
reviewable candidate without treating their archive observations as identical
or their strongest ideas as mutually exclusive?

## Five Observations

| Branch | Snapshot and method as recorded by branch | Population observation | Content depth |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| A | Authenticated recursive census with selected document review | 2,365 accessible descendants | 16 selected documents close-read |
| B | Authenticated recursive inventory with stable identifiers, classification, disposition, and protected manifest digest | 2,408 unique accessible items across 258 folders | nine priority documents received metadata review; two readable bodies were close-read |
| C | Authenticated pass over its defined accessible snapshot | 2,078 records | 36 priority Google Docs close-read |
| D | Authenticated recursive census counting 257 nested folders and 1,935 files | 2,192 reachable descendants | 18 records close-read |
| E | Authenticated recursive traversal from 61 root items | 2,405 accessible items across 257 captured folders | 63 priority documents close-read |

These observations are **not directly comparable**. They were produced by
different traversals, snapshots, inclusion rules, item representations, and
close-reading strategies. A larger population or close-reading count is not by
itself evidence of greater truth, quality, or publication readiness.

## Current Canonical Operational Receipt

The B receipt governs this candidate's current operations: 2,408 inventoried =
2,408 classified = 2,408 dispositioned. Its private manifest digest and public
aggregate remain the reproducibility boundary. This selection preserves one
operable contract while the other four observations remain provenance and
future reconciliation evidence.

Complete accounting does not establish that every file body was read, that
every item is a unique intellectual work, that every item is relevant, that
folder metadata proves authorship, or that any protected record is cleared for
publication.

## What The Family Contributed

- A contributed editorial story units, scenes, visual review, and a precise
  continuity ladder.
- B contributed the authority architecture, source-return discipline,
  candidate binding, and human-gate model.
- C contributed clearer professional positioning and campaign-level narrative.
- D contributed operational evidence semantics, maintenance, and review
  packetization.
- E contributed pressure-first interpretation, decision records, translation,
  and accessibility attention.

## Protected Boundary

No private manifest, item identifier, folder path, filename, owner display,
source body, participant data, or unapproved media entered this reconciliation.
The public table is a method comparison, not a map back into the archive.

## Re-Entry

A future census must record its snapshot, root, traversal method, inclusion and
deduplication rules, folder accounting, errors, disposition taxonomy, manifest
digest, and content-review depth before it may supersede the current receipt.

Review the [family closure evaluation](../evaluations/family-closure.md) or the
[current NYC Artist Coalition archive run](nycac-shared-folder-2026.md).
