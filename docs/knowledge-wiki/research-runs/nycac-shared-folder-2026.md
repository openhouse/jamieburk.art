# NYC Artist Coalition Shared-Folder Archival Production

## Completion Contract

One hundred percent means every accessible item was inventoried, classified,
and dispositioned. It does not mean every file body was read, every right was
cleared, or every item was selected for a claim.

```text
2,408 inventoried = 2,408 classified = 2,408 dispositioned
```

All 258 folders reached a terminal state. The exact manifest is protected
outside git; the [public-safe census](../sources/nycac-shared-folder-census-2026.md)
contains only aggregates and boundaries.

## Priority Reading Result

Nine priority documents received title and metadata review. Readable body text
was recovered from two. Those readings strengthen research directions around
Cabaret Law testimony and M.A.R.C.H. public-action infrastructure, but they do
not establish nearby-file authorship or public outcomes.

## Next Queue

1. Match public artifacts to campaign sites, repositories, official records,
   legislation, and independent reporting.
2. Distinguish creator, editor, implementer, coordinator, maintainer, approver,
   host, speaker, participant, and subject roles.
3. Request collaborator review for consequential individual-role claims.
4. Route visual leads through creator, rights, subject, consent, and Jamie
   review before any public proposal.
5. Preserve unsupported stronger wording as held hypotheses.

## Human Gates

Automated closure does not approve authorship, collaborator credit, media
rights, participant consent, application wording, website publication,
indexing, deployment, or production readiness.
