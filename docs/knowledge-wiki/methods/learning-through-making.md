# Learning Through Making

## Orientation

Jamie's technical practice did not appear suddenly as administration. It grew
through art, media archaeology, social-software experiments, installations,
writing, and the repeated act of making an idea inhabitable enough to test.

## Present Reading

The public and protected educational records show a method with several forms:

- bring a historical media object into the present and learn from its behavior;
- model relationships in software and build an interface people can enter;
- translate an online structure into physical movement and space;
- make a participatory situation, observe what happens, and revise its rules;
- write closely enough to distinguish an arresting intuition from a supported
  argument.

The record contains both strong analytical writing and moments when embodied
projects outpaced the assigned essay form. That tension belongs in the account.
Learning through making is not an excuse to avoid writing; writing, diagrams,
source trails, and documentation are among the things Jamie makes.

## What Became Usable

- a recursive model-build-observe-revise practice;
- comfort moving between physical, social, and computational systems;
- prototypes and installations that made abstract relations inspectable; and
- an understanding that documentation must help someone who was not present
  enter the work later.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 editorial-depth source return](../research-runs/editorial-depth-source-return-2026.md)
revisited the protected UCSC narrative record and the independently published
Open House feature. The first documents historical faculty observations; the
second shows one public participatory system changing in response to use and
institutional feedback.

**Establishes:** an early artistic and technical lineage joining embodied
inquiry, media systems, prototyping, relational analysis, participation, and
revision.

**Does not establish:** production deployment of classroom prototypes,
field-wide invention of structural equivalence, sole authorship of collaborative
installations, or current blanket faculty endorsement.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** bounded historical examples of media archaeology, software and
  interface prototyping, installation, analytical writing, and iterative
  participatory governance.
- **Open:** surviving project documentation, collaborator accounts, what failed,
  and which present practices Jamie would now describe differently.
- **Protected:** grades, student identifiers, raw narrative evaluations, private
  correspondence, recommendation imagery, and protected source locations.

## Next Research

Recover public project documentation where possible, credit collaborators at
artifact level, and test the lineage against projects that did not produce a
successful or durable system. Continue through [Participatory Public Practice](../projects/participatory-public-practice.md).
