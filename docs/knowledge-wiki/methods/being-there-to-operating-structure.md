# From Being There To Operating Structure

## Orientation

"Being there changes everything" was not only a WOW List motto. It names a
working method: enter the situation, pay attention to what people are already
doing, notice where participation breaks down, make the next action easier,
and preserve enough memory for the work to continue.

## Present Reading

The method moves recursively rather than linearly:

1. **Be present.** Listen in the place where the work and friction occur.
2. **Believe the signal.** Treat lived experience as evidence worthy of
   investigation, while distinguishing testimony from external verification.
3. **Make an entry point.** Build the invitation, form, calendar, meeting,
   map, website, checklist, or interface that lets people act.
4. **Observe use.** Learn from questions, workarounds, refusals, and handoffs.
5. **Make structure.** Convert repeated needs into clearer roles, workflows,
   records, and public routes.
6. **Return to the source.** Reopen the original record and revise the account
   from the needs and ethics of the present.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 return-to-source run](../research-runs/missing-pages-return-to-source-2026.md)
revisited artistic, event, product, campaign, construction, legislative, and
database sources. That mixed corpus resists a single medium-specific story and
supports the method as a recurring pattern across different kinds of work.

**Establishes:** repeated evidence of Jamie combining situated attention with
invitations, workflows, interfaces, coordination, and documentation.

**Does not establish:** that every project followed the same sequence, that
presence guarantees understanding, or that a framework owns the contributions
of participants and collaborators.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** the recurring pattern can be traced across public and protected
  sources without exposing the underlying people-level records.
- **Open:** exceptions, failed experiments, collaborator language for the
  method, and where current professional framing makes the practice too neat.
- **Protected:** raw participant histories, private communications, and
  sensitive context whose publication is unnecessary to explain the method.
