# Source-Backed Team Memory

Source-Backed Team Memory helps a team move from scattered documents,
conversations, decisions, and unresolved questions into a shared record another
person can inspect and continue.

The method preserves:

- what is known and why;
- what remains uncertain;
- who contributed and where credit is collective;
- what changed and what was corrected;
- what remains protected;
- what action should happen next.

The Knowledge Wiki uses this method without treating AI-generated relations as
facts. Authored records remain reviewable in Git, governed evidence remains in
its canonical registries, and human decisions stay human.
