# Identity Systems As Shared Infrastructure

## Orientation

A public identity can do operating work. A name, domain, account, visual
language, handbill, map, or recurring campaign pattern can help people recognize
a shared undertaking, know where to enter, and continue speaking through it.
Jamie repeatedly worked on this layer across products, campaigns, public events,
and neighborhood projects.

## Present Reading

The strongest evidence is not that Jamie made every artifact or authored every
message. It is that he helped establish recognizable systems other people could
use. NYC Artist Coalition's shared identity supported four named campaign
systems, and attributable public posts show Olympia Kazi using the coalition
identity across multiple years. WOW List joined product identity to organizer
support. CallNYC used a distinct public identity to translate civic data into
resident-facing routes.

Keep the contribution types separate:

- identity and account establishment;
- naming, visual design, and campaign architecture;
- account administration over a bounded period;
- authorship of a particular post or artifact; and
- ownership of the collective project or its outcomes.

Evidence for one does not automatically establish the others.

## What Became Usable

- recognizable entry points across web, social, print, and in-person work;
- campaign families that could share a public language without becoming one
  person's voice;
- repeatable patterns for invitations, calls to action, maps, and public
  information; and
- continuity when another collaborator needed to speak or organize through an
  established project identity.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 editorial-depth source return](../research-runs/editorial-depth-source-return-2026.md)
reopened the canonical social-account record, the CallNYC branding record, and
the coalition participation system. The record supports identity establishment
and attributable collaborator use while leaving administrator history and
undifferentiated shared-account authorship unresolved.

**Establishes:** a recurring practice of building public identity as an
operating and continuity layer, with a particularly strong bounded coalition
example.

**Does not establish:** that Jamie authored every shared-account post, designed
every artifact, controlled each account throughout its life, or owned collective
campaign outcomes.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** selected identity-establishment actions, durable public accounts,
  named campaign relationships, and attributable collaborator use.
- **Open:** contribution splits for many graphics and campaign artifacts,
  administrator history, recipient accounts of reuse, and maintenance cost.
- **Protected:** credentials, account access, private design files, unapproved
  collaborator statements, participant data, and internal strategy.

## Media And Typeface Boundary

No logo, screenshot, photograph, font file, or type specimen becomes reusable
because it is evidence. Publication requires separate authorship, license,
privacy, consent, context, and display decisions. Proprietary or private font
files remain outside the repository.
