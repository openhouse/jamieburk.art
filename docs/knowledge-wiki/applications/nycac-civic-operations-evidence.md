# NYC Artist Coalition Civic Operations Evidence

Begin with the [NYC Artist Coalition project record](../projects/nyc-artist-coalition.md),
then compare the evidence with [Technical Operations](../capabilities/technical-operations.md)
and the current [OTI opportunity](../opportunities/oti-technical-operations.md).

## Referral Sentence

Jamie builds operating structure for ambiguous public-facing work. In NYC
Artist Coalition, he helped connect recurring participation, public identity,
practical safety work, campaign websites, testimony, and coalition memory so
distributed cultural-space knowledge could become usable public action.

## What This Demonstrates

- coordination across public participants, collaborators, venues, agencies,
  elected offices, campaigns, and technical surfaces;
- translation of lived experience into recommendations, testimony, guides,
  calls to action, and follow-through;
- implementation across meetings, websites, identity, public communications,
  and operating memory;
- risk judgment around privacy, safety, attribution, and public authority; and
- durable systems that collaborators could use and continue.

## Boundaries

Jamie did not individually own coalition, movement, legislative, agency, venue,
or participant outcomes. The record does not establish direct government
hiring-process ownership, formal agency authority, authorship of every shared
artifact, or permission to publish private work.
