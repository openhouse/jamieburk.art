# NYC Artist Coalition Shared-Folder Census

The July 19, 2026, authenticated read-only pass accounts for 2,408 unique
accessible items across 258 folders. Inventory, classification, disposition,
and population totals all equal 2,408. No folder remained queued or unresolved.

The [aggregate census](../../knowledge-bank/corpora/nycac-shared-folder-public-census-2026-07-19.json)
publishes format, source-class, mission-cluster, and disposition totals. The
[intake receipt](../../knowledge-bank/runs/2026-07-19-nycac-shared-folder-full-population.md)
explains the method and scope.

The source establishes population closure and governed custody. It does not
establish Jamie's authorship of every item, permission to publish underlying
files, universal content or rights review, completeness beyond the accessible
snapshot, or individual causality for public outcomes.

The exact item-level manifest remains outside the public repository. The
public census contains no source identifiers, resource keys, filenames, raw
document bodies, participant records, or authenticated-session state.
