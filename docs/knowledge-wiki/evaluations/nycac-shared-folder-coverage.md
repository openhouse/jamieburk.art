# NYC Artist Coalition Shared-Folder Coverage Evaluation

Evaluate the [archival-production run](../research-runs/nycac-shared-folder-2026.md)
and its [application evidence route](../applications/nycac-civic-operations-evidence.md)
as separate outputs of one governed record.

## Automated Criteria

1. Population, inventory, classification, and disposition totals all equal
   2,408.
2. All 258 folders are closed; none remain queued or unresolved.
3. Format, source-class, disposition, and mission-cluster totals reconcile.
4. No exact private locator, filename, raw body, credential, contact record, or
   authenticated-session state enters the public repository.
5. Every public claim carries source support, boundaries, anti-claims, and a
   review date.
6. Collective credit and item-level authorship limits remain explicit.
7. The application brief names Jamie's action, what became usable, and the
   evidence limit.
8. Mutation probes fail when counts, leakage controls, authorship boundaries,
   human gates, or application wording are weakened.

## Human Criteria

These remain open until named people perform them against an exact commit:

- Jamie confirms that the portfolio wording is true, useful, and public-safe.
- A collaborator-aware reviewer confirms that individual and collective credit
  are fair.
- A trusted hiring reader can name a role they would refer Jamie for and the
  evidence supporting that referral.
- Rights and consent reviewers approve any proposed media independently.

Automation may report its own checks. It may not claim human comprehension,
consent, collaborator approval, application approval, indexing approval, or
production readiness.
