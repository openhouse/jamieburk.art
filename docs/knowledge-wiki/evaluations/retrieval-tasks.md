# Retrieval Tasks

These are protocols, not completed human evaluations. Record a result only
after a named person performs the task against an exact commit.

## Orientation

1. Find the current CallNYC chronology.
2. Find why the January 30, 2016 date and 1-3 p.m. hours are supported.
3. Find what the Digital District photograph establishes and what it cannot.
4. Find the correction to the earlier CallNYC years.

## Professional Proof

1. Find the project that demonstrates public-data translation.
2. Find Jamie's strongest bounded Technical Operations proof.
3. Find which evidence is relevant to the current OTI opportunity.
4. Find OTI requirements that are visible and proven on the public site.
5. Find the OTI structured-hiring requirement that remains a direct experience gap.
6. Find the ACLU artifact that must be completed before applying.
7. Find why enterprise SaaS migration is not treated as proven for Asana.

## Governance

1. Determine whether the Digital District photograph may be displayed.
2. Find every pilot record projected to `/work/callnyc`.
3. Find the anti-claim preventing official-Council-product wording.

## Result Contract

Record:

```text
candidate commit
reviewer
task
success or failure
elapsed time
pages visited
wrong turns
coaching required
trusted answer: yes/no
notes
```

Automation may print these tasks and verify the paths. It may not claim human
comprehension, privacy judgment, or hiring-reader response.

Return to the [Knowledge Wiki start page](../README.md) after completing or
recording a task.
