# Editorial Depth Evaluation

## Purpose

This evaluation protects four forms of depth that should not collapse into a
single career narrative: shared identity, learning through making, situated
practice, and first-person stakes.

## Deterministic Criteria

The machine evaluation must fail when:

- one of the four pages is absent, unindexed, or missing canonical authority;
- source support and non-support or the fresh source-return path disappears;
- identity establishment is collapsed into post authorship, project ownership,
  or blanket rights to reuse media and fonts;
- the learning page erases either analytical writing or embodied inquiry,
  exposes protected academic records, or inflates prototypes into deployments;
- the place page turns people into evidence or infers present access, safety,
  ownership, or consent;
- the first-person draft loses its AI-assisted label, pending projection state,
  requested authorship review, or Jamie-only resolution rule;
- the source-return receipt omits protected handling, librarian questions, or
  governed re-entry; or
- mutation tests cannot demonstrate those failures.

The machine score covers structure and explicit safeguards only. It cannot
evaluate emotional truth, voice fidelity, collaborator experience, rights,
consent, editorial usefulness, or publication readiness.

## Human Evaluation

Jamie should review the first-person draft line by line and may rewrite, reject,
or retire it. Jamie and trusted reviewers should separately ask:

- Does the identity page distinguish contribution without hiding Jamie's work?
- Does the learning page preserve both artistic inquiry and writing discipline?
- Do the places remain specific without making other people into scenery?
- What feels true, untrue, private, overstated, or missing from the stakes draft?

The first-person authorship review is **requested**, but not completed. A
passing machine score must not change that state.

## Hill-Climb Rule

Run the deterministic evaluation and all mutations. Repair every failure on the
same candidate, rerun the focused suite, then run Wiki, composite, and complete
repository checks. Any content change invalidates the prior result. Human
authorship, collaborator review, rights, consent, production approval, and
indexing remain separate gates.
