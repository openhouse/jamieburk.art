# Evidence Debt And Promotion Queue

## Orientation

Evidence debt is a visible research obligation, not a defect to hide and not a
license to weaken strong supported claims. A claim enters this queue when a
better source, collaborator correction, rights decision, causal boundary, or
editorial choice could materially change its use.

## Present Reading

### Priority 1: Role And Handoff Corroboration

- KC Town Hall general-contractor and stewardship-transition roles;
- collaborator use and maintenance of NYC Artist Coalition identity systems;
- recipient accounts for product, documentation, and operational handoffs.

### Priority 2: Decision And Outcome Traces

- how Commercial Rent Stabilization artifacts changed collaborator decisions;
- which specific coalition inputs agencies or Council offices acted on;
- where public participation records connect to implementation rather than
  only visibility.

### Priority 3: Rights-Cleared Evidence

- product and archive screenshots without private data;
- construction and participatory-practice images with authorship, context,
  rights, and consent reviewed separately;
- collaborator quotations approved for the exact intended surface.

### Priority 4: Chronology And Counterevidence

- dormant periods, failed experiments, reversals, and maintenance costs;
- precise project endpoints and transitions;
- records that complicate an overly smooth participation lineage.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 return-to-source run](../research-runs/missing-pages-return-to-source-2026.md)
closed several structural questions while deliberately leaving human and
rights gates open.

**Establishes:** which current claims have canonical support and which named
questions still require better evidence or human judgment.

**Does not establish:** that the queue is exhaustive, that a missing source
disproves a memory, or that automated checks can approve publication.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** every item above maps to an existing source, claim, or research
  inquiry rather than an invented content wish.
- **Open:** priority can change with a job, collaborator, recovered source, or
  editorial purpose.
- **Protected:** source locations, private records, participant data, and the
  personal circumstances behind boundaries.

## Promotion Rule

Promote only after the canonical claim and evidence relationship are updated,
the anti-claim still holds, public wording fits the audience, and any required
Jamie, collaborator, rights, or consent review is complete. Begin with the
[open-questions index](open-questions.md) or the
[source-backed memory method](../methods/source-backed-team-memory.md).

## Next Research

Begin with recipient and collaborator accounts that can test handoff and role
claims. Record contrary evidence and failed adoption as carefully as supporting
evidence; both make the public account more useful.
