# Canonical Story Bank

Each reusable story preserves five parts: **situation, Jamie's action, what
became usable, collective context, and honest limit**. Removing any part makes
the story less useful or less trustworthy.

## Story Contract

1. **Situation:** What condition, pressure, or transition made action useful?
2. **Jamie's action:** What did Jamie personally build, coordinate, translate,
   facilitate, document, or maintain?
3. **Usable result:** What artifact, workflow, interface, decision, or operating
   capacity existed afterward?
4. **Collective context:** Who else held authorship, authority, labor, knowledge,
   participation, or institutional decision rights?
5. **Honest limit:** What does the evidence not establish about adoption,
   causality, scale, durability, rights, or attribution?

## Selected Stories

### CallNYC

- **Situation:** Newly released constituent-services data was difficult for a
  resident to navigate as an issue pathway.
- **Jamie's action:** Jamie designed and built an independent prototype and
  iterated its language and interface.
- **Usable result:** Residents could move from an issue category toward an
  appropriate Council contact path.
- **Collective context:** CouncilStat supplied the public data; Council offices,
  residents, civic-technology peers, and public institutions held separate
  roles.
- **Honest limit:** The evidence does not establish official Council ownership,
  adoption, or policy impact.

### NYC Artist Coalition Campaign Systems

- **Situation:** Cultural-space experience needed routes into shared action and
  formal civic processes.
- **Jamie's action:** Jamie co-founded the coalition and contributed recurring
  participation, public identity, campaign web infrastructure, safety study,
  testimony, and source-backed memory.
- **Usable result:** Meetings, campaign sites, public explanations, action
  routes, testimony support, and shared records could reinforce one another.
- **Collective context:** Artists, venue operators, organizers, partner groups,
  Council members, agencies, and many others held distinct roles.
- **Honest limit:** The record does not establish sole campaign leadership,
  sole authorship, or sole legislative causality.

### KC Town Hall

- **Situation:** A long-abandoned building required physical stabilization while
  its future public use remained open.
- **Jamie's action:** The evidence supports project stewardship, construction
  coordination, design and survey work, and neighborhood-facing operations;
  Jamie's precise general-contractor title remains held for corroboration.
- **Usable result:** A completed first-phase cold shell and a participation
  structure connected restoration to neighborhood learning.
- **Collective context:** Julia Cole, residents, associations, tradespeople,
  design professionals, funders, and municipal actors held distinct knowledge
  and authority.
- **Honest limit:** The record does not establish representative consensus,
  every remembered construction detail, or a completed later program.

## Use Rule

An application may compress a story, but it may not discard the collective
context or honest limit. New detail must first mature through the canonical
claim and source system.
