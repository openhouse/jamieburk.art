# Photo And Media Review

An image can be useful evidence while remaining unavailable for reproduction.
The Wiki keeps factual support, creator attribution, rights, participant
consent, and display approval as separate questions.

- [Digital District photograph record](../assets/digital-district-photo.md)

The photograph itself and its private locator are not committed. The record is
a public-safe review surface, not permission to publish.
