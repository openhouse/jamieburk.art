# Decision Records

A decision record is a bounded reconstruction, not permission to invent a
meeting that the archive does not contain. Each record distinguishes documented
choices from options that are merely not observed in the surviving record.

- [CallNYC issue pathways](../decisions/callnyc-issue-pathways.md)
- [KC Town Hall listening within fieldwork](../decisions/kc-town-hall-listening-fieldwork.md)
- [Knowledge Wiki selective projection](../decisions/knowledge-wiki-selective-projection.md)

The pilot begins with decisions that have clear current sources and project
context. Open House and Harry J. Epstein decision records remain potential
future additions after their authority references and Wiki project contexts are
reconciled.
