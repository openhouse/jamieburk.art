# Operational Evidence Map

supported responsibility, what became usable, the wider result, and the
boundary on interpretation.

## Maintenance And Care

Maintenance is operational labor, not a personality adjective. Evidence should
name the mode: physical repair, digital continuity, recurring participation,
information stewardship, governed memory, or responsible transition. A handoff
is supported only at the documented level, and failed, abandoned, or burdensome
maintenance belongs in the record beside successful continuity.

No row may collapse difficulty, responsibility, delivery, team outcome,
institutional action, and personal impact into one sentence.
