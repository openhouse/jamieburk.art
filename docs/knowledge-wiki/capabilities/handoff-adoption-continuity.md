# Handoffs, Adoption, And Continuity

## Orientation

A system becomes more valuable when another person can use it without needing
its originator in the room. Jamie's archive contains meaningful evidence of
collaborator use, organizer-facing deployment, shared account operation,
member-made onboarding, archive recovery, and mission-aligned transition. The
evidence is not yet uniform enough for one sweeping public claim.

## Present Reading

The strongest version of this capability is plural and bounded:

- WOW List supplied organizer-facing calendars and a member-created tutorial;
- NYC Artist Coalition's public identity and campaign family were used across
  years by more than one teammate;
- source-backed team memory is explicitly designed for retrieval and handoff;
  and
- Jamie reports transitioning KC Town Hall to a mission-aligned organization,
  while the personal reason for that transition remains outside this public
  record.

## Evidence States

- **Designed:** requirements, workflows, interfaces, plans, or operating
  structures exist.
- **Launched:** the system reached a real public or team-use context.
- **Used:** another person demonstrably acted through it.
- **Adopted:** a team incorporated it into recurring practice.
- **Handed off:** ownership, documentation, or operating custody transferred.
- **Sustained:** the structure remained useful after Jamie's direct work.

One state does not prove the next. Evidence of launch is not evidence of
adoption; adoption is not evidence of a successful handoff; survival is not
proof that maintenance was easy. Failed, abandoned, or burdensome handoffs are
part of the professional record and belong in future source return.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 return-to-source run](../research-runs/missing-pages-return-to-source-2026.md)
rechecked product, shared-archive, campaign-memory, and stewardship authorities.
The evidence supports several concrete continuity mechanisms but does not yet
support a portfolio-wide adoption rate or one standardized handoff outcome.

**Establishes:** repeated design for use by other people, selected examples of
continued collaborator use, and a governed method for preserving operational
memory.

**Does not establish:** that every handoff succeeded, that Jamie owned every
system being transferred, or that continued use proves impact or individual
authorship.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** concrete artifacts and workflows intended for reuse, shared
  operation, and later retrieval.
- **Open:** recipient confirmation, before-and-after adoption evidence,
  abandoned systems, maintenance burden, and the KC Town Hall stewardship
  record.
- **Protected:** family-crisis context, private correspondence, account access,
  internal user lists, and unapproved collaborator statements.

## Next Research

Ask recipients what they inherited, what they could operate independently,
what failed, and what they changed. Record corrections in the
[evidence debt and promotion queue](../indexes/evidence-debt-and-promotion-queue.md)
before strengthening any public claim.
