# Working Across The Civic Interface

## Orientation

The civic interface is the place where lived problems, community language,
agency responsibilities, legislative process, and implementation constraints
meet. Jamie's role has often been to help information cross that interface
without erasing the people who supplied it.

## Present Reading

The NYC Artist Coalition record shows several connected actions: recurring
listening in cultural spaces, practical fire-safety work, public campaign
systems, testimony, and recommendations carried into DCLA and Council
processes. In official 2017 testimony, Commissioner Tom Finkelpearl cited the
coalition's formation after a DCLA DIY-arts meeting as an example of the power
of direct feedback and bringing people together around a common cause. That is
evidence of institutional usefulness, not a personal endorsement.

## What Became Usable

- lived experience became organized public testimony and recommendations;
- artists and venues gained routes to practical safety knowledge;
- agencies and elected officials received concrete, community-defined input;
- campaigns translated specialized policy into actions and public records;
  and
- later researchers can separate public evidence from inferred motive.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 return-to-source run](../research-runs/missing-pages-return-to-source-2026.md)
reread pages 91-93 of the official May 19, 2017 Council transcript. The wording
locates the coalition inside DCLA's account of reciprocal public engagement
and direct feedback.

**Establishes:** that Finkelpearl publicly regarded coalition formation as a
useful result of direct engagement, and that Jamie later documented his own
bounded translation work in public testimony.

**Does not establish:** Finkelpearl's private motives, DCLA ownership of the
coalition, Jamie's sole founding or leadership credit, or single-cause policy
outcomes.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** official recognition of the interface, Jamie's public testimony,
  and the coalition's documented safety, campaign, and recommendation routes.
- **Open:** collaborator accounts of division of labor and finer evaluation of
  what agencies or Council offices changed because of specific inputs.
- **Protected:** private strategy, vulnerable venue details, private motives,
  and unapproved correspondence.
