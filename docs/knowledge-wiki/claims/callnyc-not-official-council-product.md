# CallNYC Was Not An Official Council Product

Do not imply that CallNYC was:

- commissioned by the New York City Council;
- an official Council product;
- a documented formal hackathon submission;
- a winning submission;
- evidence that Jamie worked for the Council;
- adopted because Council-member accounts interacted with it.

The supported account is the
[independent follow-on claim](callnyc-independent-follow-on.md). Institutional
context and public interaction do not become ownership, employment,
endorsement, adoption, or causation through compression.
