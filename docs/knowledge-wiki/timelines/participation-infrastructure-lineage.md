# Participation Infrastructure Lineage

## Orientation

This is a lineage of practices, not a claim that each project mechanically
caused the next. Across two decades, Jamie repeatedly worked on the conditions
that help people arrive, contribute, find one another, carry memory, and enter
public decisions.

## Present Reading

The strongest throughline moves from embodied participation toward durable
infrastructure while retaining the earlier concern with presence:

1. **2003-2006:** Shop Shows and Open House made invitation, hospitality,
   shared responsibility, and documentation part of the work.
2. **2007-2011:** waterways projects and NTER CHNG connected movement, public
   encounter, installation, and communication systems.
3. **2012-2017:** Sunday Dinner and WOW List developed recurring gathering,
   attendance operations, event publishing, and distributed calendars.
4. **2016 onward:** CallNYC and NYC Artist Coalition translated participation
   into civic data, public meetings, practical safety work, campaigns, and
   testimony.
5. **2026 onward:** source-backed team memory makes claims, sources,
   anti-claims, handoffs, and public boundaries maintainable.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 return-to-source run](../research-runs/missing-pages-return-to-source-2026.md)
reopened the Sunday Dinner workbook in aggregate-only mode alongside the WOW
List production archive and public historical sources. The workbook preserves
event-prefixed columns and invitation or attendance markers; it also contains
private contact data that must not leave the protected source environment.

**Establishes:** recurring operational forms and a defensible continuity of
questions about participation, documentation, and public entry.

**Does not establish:** a single master plan, sole authorship, causal dependence
between every project, or permission to publish participant-level history.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** dated project sequence, recurring operating patterns, and bounded
  links among gathering, calendars, civic action, and documentation.
- **Open:** exact handoff points, collaborator interpretations, abandoned
  experiments, and counterexamples that may complicate the lineage.
- **Protected:** names, contact data, attendance rows, private event discussion,
  and family or participant circumstances.

## Next Research

Look for discontinuities as actively as continuities. Ask collaborators where
the lineage feels accurate, where it credits the wrong person, and where an
apparently failed or minor project changed the method. Compare with the broader
[selected chronology](selected-practice-chronology.md).
