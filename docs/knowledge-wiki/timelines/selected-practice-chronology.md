# Selected Practice Chronology

## Orientation

This chronology is a retrieval aid, not a complete life history. It selects
source-backed moments that help readers see Jamie's range and recurring
concerns without forcing every project into one argument.

## Present Reading

- **2003-2006:** Shop Shows and the 10-day Open House experiment develop
  participation, hospitality, collective decision-making, and distributed
  documentation.
- **2007-2009:** a collaborative bicycle-powered raft expedition and Great
  Accommodations use waterways as sites and networks for public encounter.
- **2010-2011:** NTER CHNG creates an interactive texting installation with
  Drew Bolton and Garrett Fuselier and is included in *America: Now and Here*.
- **2012-2021:** Sunday Dinner develops recurring gathering, invitation,
  attendance, theme, and residency operations.
- **2014-2017:** WOW List turns community calendar practice into a co-built
  Django, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, and Ember product used across many city scenes.
- **2016:** CallNYC becomes an independent civic-data prototype after a New
  York City Council constituent-services hackathon.
- **2017 onward:** Jamie co-founds NYC Artist Coalition and contributes to
  safety work, public meetings, campaign systems, testimony, and policy
  translation.
- **2018-2019:** KC Town Hall completes a documented $189,629 cold-shell phase
  while combining construction, neighborhood listening, and municipal review.
- **2022-2026:** Fair Rent NYC and Commercial Rent Stabilization work develop
  campaign operations, legislative provenance, and public-data proposals.
- **2026:** source-backed team memory and the Knowledge Wiki make the claim,
  source, boundary, and projection lifecycle explicit.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 return-to-source run](../research-runs/missing-pages-return-to-source-2026.md)
sampled original public and protected records from across this span. Dates and
descriptions above are bounded to existing canonical claims; no chronology gap
has been filled from intuition alone.

**Establishes:** a long, varied record with recurring attention to
participation, implementation, civic translation, and durable memory.

**Does not establish:** exhaustive coverage, uninterrupted continuity, equal
importance among projects, or a causal sequence from one project to the next.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** the selected dated moments and their canonical source boundaries.
- **Open:** missing projects, dormant periods, failed experiments, precise
  transition dates, and collaborator corrections.
- **Protected:** private life chronology, family circumstances, participant
  records, and source locations not needed for public understanding.
