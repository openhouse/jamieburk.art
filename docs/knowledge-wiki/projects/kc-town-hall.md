# KC Town Hall

## Orientation

KC Town Hall was conceived as a neighborhood-led transformation of a long
abandoned Kansas City building into a community resource. Jamie's record spans
physical restoration, on-site coordination, public listening, visual and print
systems, and municipal process. The project was collective and depended on
Julia Fredenberg, neighborhood organizations, contractors, design and
engineering professionals, city staff, and residents.

## Present Reading

The project makes Jamie's operating range unusually concrete. The 2019 public
packet labels the $189,629 Phase One cold-shell work completed and itemizes
roof-deck repair, insulation and TPO membrane, masonry, floor framing, water,
access, safety, transport, and cleanup. The same packet reproduces a
neighborhood survey developed with Oak Park Neighborhood Association and New
Horizon Missionary Baptist Church and says its results directly shaped the
proposal.

## What Became Usable

- a stabilized cold shell with documented construction scope;
- a listening mechanism connecting resident ideas to the proposal;
- maps, handbills, presentation materials, and municipal review artifacts;
- a public account of project cost and phase boundaries; and
- a record that can distinguish completed Phase One work from later plans.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 return-to-source run](../research-runs/missing-pages-return-to-source-2026.md)
visually reinspected the public municipal packet. Its Finance pages label Phase
One completed in 2019, total it at $189,629, and separate the completed work
from the proposed later phase.

**Establishes:** the completed cold-shell scope, the stated cost, the
collaborative neighborhood survey, and Jamie's founder and presenter role.

**Does not establish:** Jamie's general-contractor title, sole performance of
every trade, completion of the later redevelopment, or receipt of every
recommended dollar.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** the municipal packet's Phase One status and scope, the survey's
  collaborative context, and the later City Council allocation process.
- **Open:** independent corroboration of Jamie's general-contractor title,
  exact individual authorship within the survey system, and the bounded public
  wording for the stewardship transition.
- **Protected:** private banking and legal records, contact details, survey
  responses, the family crisis surrounding the transition, and any account
  that would turn stewardship into personal spectacle.
