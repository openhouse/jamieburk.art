# NYC Artist Coalition

## Orientation

Jamie co-founded NYC Artist Coalition and helped connect recurring
participation, public identity, practical safety work, campaign websites,
testimony, and coalition memory. The work created routes through which artists'
and small cultural spaces' lived experience could enter shared action and
civic processes.

The project record preserves Jamie as an actor without converting coalition,
movement, legislative, agency, venue, or participant outcomes into individual
ownership.

## What Became Usable

- recurring meetings and public actions rooted in small cultural spaces;
- a durable public identity and campaign family collaborators could carry;
- safety, testimony, recommendation, and government-interface pathways;
- campaign websites and public communication systems; and
- source-backed project memory able to survive handoff and later research.

## Governed Shared Archive

The [public-safe census](../sources/nycac-shared-folder-census-2026.md)
accounts for 2,408 unique accessible items across 258 folders. Every item was
inventoried, classified, and assigned one primary disposition. The exact
manifest and underlying archive remain protected.

The [archival-production run](../research-runs/nycac-shared-folder-2026.md)
shows how population closure differs from content reading, rights review, claim
maturation, and portfolio selection.

## Evidence And Limits

Official Council and DCLA records, independent reporting, public campaign
surfaces, population-accounted event and social records, and bounded
first-person context remain the authorities for the public account. The shared
archive adds depth and research leads. It does not establish authorship from
folder placement or modification metadata, grant publication permission, or
prove policy causality.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** Jamie's bounded co-founding and operating role, the recurring
  participation and public-identity systems, source-specific civic translation,
  and complete accounting of the accessible shared archive.
- **Open:** item-level creation and production roles, further public-source
  matches, collaborator corrections, and visual or quotation approvals.
- **Protected:** exact source coordinates, private notes and strategy,
  participant and contact data, completed forms, identifiable media, font
  binaries, credentials, and unapproved quotations.
