# Fair Rent NYC And Commercial Rent Stabilization

## Orientation

This record joins Fair Rent NYC campaign operations with Jamie's later
source-backed work on commercial rent stabilization, legislative provenance,
and public data. The useful unit is not a single bill or website. It is an
operating memory that helps collaborators see what came from where, what was
decided, what remains open, and what may safely be said in public.

## Present Reading

Jamie's contribution is strongest where policy complexity becomes shared
working structure. The archive supports website operations for Fair Rent NYC,
running minutes with explicit consent and public-safety rules, a legislative
provenance redline that separates source layers from individual authorship,
and public-facing proposals for a fuller vacancy and lease-cost baseline.

## What Became Usable

- a shared record of meetings, decisions, action items, and open questions;
- traceable legislative lineage across related proposals;
- public-safe distinctions between policy analysis and legal advice;
- maintained web and campaign operations; and
- a route from advocacy questions to bounded data opportunities.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 return-to-source run](../research-runs/missing-pages-return-to-source-2026.md)
reread the running minutes and the provenance redline. The minutes explicitly
define a readable campaign memory and bar sensitive tenant, business, and
landlord information without consent. The redline says reviewer names identify
source layers rather than individual drafting authorship and labels itself
unofficial discussion material, not legal advice.

**Establishes:** Jamie's source-backed documentation practice, web operations,
and production of a provenance artifact that makes policy inheritance easier
to inspect.

**Does not establish:** authorship of every legislative provision, adoption of
every recommendation, legal authority, or sole ownership of collective policy
work.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** maintained campaign memory, bounded web operations, legislative
  source comparison, and public-data proposal work.
- **Open:** collaborator review of specific drafting roles, measurement of how
  each artifact changed decisions, and publication choices for current work.
- **Protected:** private strategy, vulnerable tenant or business information,
  confidential landlord details, legal-review material, and unapproved names.
