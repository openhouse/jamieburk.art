# WOW List

## Orientation

Jamie co-built and operated WOW List with Richard Renaldi as a community
calendar and participation system for DIY arts and music communities. It joined
software, organizer support, public communication, and field learning rather
than treating event listings as a neutral feed.

## Present Reading

The clearest professional throughline is not merely that the application had
features. Jamie helped turn a recurring social practice into usable product
infrastructure: natural-language event entry, followable keyword communities,
digest and calendar distribution, embeddable surfaces, deployment, and public
support conversations. A July 2017 snapshot supports a bounded scale of 1,846
users, 16,142 post or event records, and activity across at least 35 city
scenes.

## What Became Usable

- organizers could publish and distribute events across several surfaces;
- participants could follow place- and interest-based communities;
- public support exchanges exposed friction and informed product direction;
- members could create onboarding and field-learning resources; and
- later archival review could recover scale without publishing person-level
  data.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 return-to-source run](../research-runs/missing-pages-return-to-source-2026.md)
reopened the July 22, 2017 production dump in aggregate-only mode. The archive
header, table structure, and protected row counts reproduced the canonical
1,846-user and 16,142-post-or-event figures. No person-level row was brought
into the repository.

**Establishes:** a historical production snapshot, Jamie's bounded co-builder
and product-operator role, and concrete organizer-facing product surfaces.

**Does not establish:** current activity, official chapters, sole authorship,
or that every record represents a completed public event.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** historical aggregate scale, technical stack, distribution
  workflows, support patterns, and selected civic and care uses.
- **Open:** finer feature chronology, collaborator corrections, adoption
  narratives by individual city, and approved visual selections.
- **Protected:** user rows, contact data, follows, stars, geolocation records,
  credentials, and raw community activity.
