# Participatory Public Practice

## Orientation

Before Jamie described his work as technical operations, he built situations
in which people could enter, contribute, decide, document, and change what the
work became. Open House, the river practice, NTER CHNG, Shop Shows, and Sunday
Dinner belong here as distinct projects connected by attention to how people
inhabit systems.

## Present Reading

This history matters because it explains why Jamie's operating structures are
not conventional administration. The work treats hospitality, atmosphere,
shared authorship, documentation, and the design of entry points as material.
It also requires strict boundaries: participation does not grant permanent
permission to identify, quote, or republish participants.

## What Became Usable

- public experiments with shared responsibility and iterative governance;
- moving and site-specific forms of encounter;
- interfaces connecting text, installation, event, and conversation;
- recurring gathering formats that could carry participation over time; and
- a practice of documenting enough structure for later learning without
  claiming ownership of what participants made.

## Source Return

The [July 2026 return-to-source run](../research-runs/missing-pages-return-to-source-2026.md)
reread the 2006 Open House article, the 2007 Kansas City Star raft feature, and
the archived 2011 NTER CHNG site. Together they show initiation and tending,
collaborative construction and travel, invitation and public encounter, and a
three-person interactive texting installation.

**Establishes:** a long participatory practice in which Jamie initiated,
co-built, facilitated, and documented public situations.

**Does not establish:** sole authorship of collective activity, complete
participant rosters, permission to republish images or vulnerable details, or
lasting impact for every encounter.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** bounded initiation and facilitation roles, named collaborators,
  public durations and formats, and the relation between participation and
  documentation.
- **Open:** fuller project chronologies, collaborator correction, approved
  visual evidence, and the bridge between each experiment and present work.
- **Protected:** participant identities and circumstances not necessary to the
  claim, unapproved photographs, raw correspondence, and private event records.
