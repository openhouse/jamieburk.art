# Feature evals composite integration ledger

Date: 2026-07-16

Branch: `feature/knowledge-e`

Apply target: `develop`

This ledger accounts for every frozen `feature/evals-*` pull request. It records
the distinctive strength of each branch, the integration decision, and the
reason that decision serves the canonical knowledge-bank and portfolio system.
The integration does not merge the branches wholesale. It preserves the
strongest capability from each while avoiding parallel schemas, duplicated
corpora, stale assessment artifacts, and public claims that have not passed the
canonical evidence and composition gates.

## Governing decisions

- Keep the two-system architecture established on `feature/knowledge-e`: a
  deeper public-safe knowledge bank and a deliberately selective website
  projection.
- Treat every frozen branch as evidence and design input, not automatically as
  merge-ready code.
- Prefer one canonical record graph, one launch-readiness suite, and one
  knowledge-lifecycle suite.
- Bind qualitative assessments to the exact governed candidate they score.
- Reject semantic inflation before it reaches public prose.
- Preserve named human gates for rights, consent, collaborator-sensitive
  credit, factual corroboration, blind-reader response, and production launch.

## New composite capabilities

### Candidate-bound assessments

Every committed semantic assessment records:

- the candidate commit;
- a fingerprint of all governed source, script, eval, and knowledge-bank files;
- a fingerprint of the suite contract; and
- the number of governed files.

The gate rejects an assessment when the candidate tree or suite has changed.
Assessment files, generated reports, pull-request prose, and integration notes
are excluded from the governed fingerprint so a completed assessment can be
committed without invalidating the candidate it describes.

### Semantic mutation boundary

The launch suite rejects transformations that would turn:

- silence into endorsement;
- a proposal into delivered work;
- an appropriation into receipt or completed outcome;
- a post count into a complete lifetime archive;
- reactions into unique people;
- a shared account into individual authorship;
- a reference into endorsement;
- public availability into permission;
- an event response into attendance;
- circulation into agreement;
- or agent review into human validation.

These are executable anti-overclaim controls, not style suggestions.

### Daily knowledge operations

The composite adds dry-run-first intake, public-safe queries, health reporting,
undispositioned-intake blocking, and compiled-output leak detection. See
`docs/knowledge-bank/operations.md`.

### Public composition improvement

The Sunday Dinner case study now distinguishes two related practices:

- Jamie co-hosted Sunday Dinner with Julia Fredenberg; the surviving operating
  record documents more than 300 gatherings, not 300 unique participants.
- Jamie separately founded the 196 Artists Residency; the public resume
  self-reports more than 20 participants.

The wording preserves collective credit, keeps the self-report qualifier
visible, and does not expose the private onboarding artifact that documents the
residency's participation method.

## Deferred on purpose

- Production deployment and merge approval.
- Media rights and consent.
- Collaborator-sensitive credit decisions not already approved in public-safe
  records.
- Human corroboration of protected or self-reported claims.
- Blind-reader validation.
- A broader NYC Artist Coalition route redesign.
- New portfolio imagery or private archival media.
- Automatic promotion of queued intake or mature claims.

These are not missing agent work. They are explicit human gates or separate
compositional decisions.
