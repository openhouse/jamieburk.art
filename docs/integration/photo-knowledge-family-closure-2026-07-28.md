Date: 2026-07-28

Status: decision for `feature/pre-launch-A`

The `feature/photo-knowledge-*` family is frozen. Its branches remain
reference material; none should be merged wholesale into the pre-launch
candidate.

## Decision

Use `feature/photo-knowledge-A` as the release spine. Selectively preserve
invariants from B through E without importing parallel registries, duplicate
records, or alternate public compositions.

## Why A

A makes the fastest defensible public argument. A hiring reader can locate the
Technical Operations path, understand Jamie's role, and encounter project
photography without first learning the Knowledge Wiki. Its homepage Council
chamber image, FairRentNYC sequence, and KC Town Hall proposal artifacts make
Jamie visible in institutional, civic, and field contexts while preserving
the project's collective frame.

The other branches contain real strengths. Their value is highest as contract
improvements around A, not as competing information architectures or another
round of public layout expansion.

## Integration rules

1. Keep A's routes, page hierarchy, and public photographic composition unless
   a release-blocking defect is found.
2. Every visible photograph must resolve to a governed asset and exact
   occurrence.
3. Public records may retain an opaque private binding, never a private path,
   source digest, face record, coordinate, or archive manifest.
4. Availability is not permission. Creator rights, represented-person review,
   caption, credit, crop, collective credit, production, and indexing remain
   named human gates.
5. Archive censuses distinguish photographs from graphics, maps, press
   reproductions, official reference portraits, and unknown material.
6. Deterministic checks run before browser or model review. Expensive
   candidate-bound judgments run only for relevant semantic changes and only
   after the candidate is frozen.
7. Any candidate-affecting change invalidates prior fingerprints, browser
   receipts, accessibility matrices, and independent judgments.

## Launch consequence

This decision closes branch selection. Work on `feature/pre-launch-A` is
limited to evidence ingestion, launch-blocking corrections, bounded content
improvements, cost-aware evaluation, and verification of one unchanged
candidate. It is not an invitation to begin another branch-family tournament.
