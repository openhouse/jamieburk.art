# Knowledge Wiki Family Closure

Date: 2026-07-19

This integration closes the frozen `feature/knowledge-wiki-A` through
`feature/knowledge-wiki-E` family onto one canonical implementation for
`feature/wiki-C`.

## Decision

Use `feature/knowledge-wiki-E` as the architectural foundation. Adapt the
distinctive editorial, artistic, hiring, relational, and operational strengths
of A through D into E's governed Markdown graph. Do not merge parallel
compilers, lifecycle stores, or evaluation roots.

The exact donor heads and destinations are recorded in
[`knowledge-wiki-family-closure.json`](knowledge-wiki-family-closure.json).

## What Was Preserved

| Donor | Distinctive strength | Canonical destination |

| A | Source-to-story composition and rights-aware visual sequencing | Canonical Story Bank, public-knowledge method, existing visual-evidence index |
| B | Shared identity, embodied learning, and human-authored stakes | Three governed method pages with explicit authorship and projection gates |

## Census Reconciliation

The five donors reported five internally closed NYC Artist Coalition shared-
folder populations. They used different snapshot dates, labels, folder totals,
and traversal representations. The integration preserves those observations as
non-interchangeable research results. It does not average them, select the
largest as truth, or publish one as a universal archive-size claim.

The E value remains attached to E's specific authenticated research run because
that run is part of the selected foundation. Cross-family completeness remains
unresolved until one reproducible census protocol is rerun against one declared
root and inclusion contract.

See the governed [family closure research run](../knowledge-bank/research-runs/knowledge-wiki-family-closure-2026-07-19.md).

## Release Boundary

This closure adds no public Knowledge Wiki or proofs route. Automated checks can
establish structural integrity, public-safety controls, and exact donor
accounting. They cannot grant collaborator consent, rights clearance, Jamie's
editorial approval, production approval, or acceptance by hiring readers.
