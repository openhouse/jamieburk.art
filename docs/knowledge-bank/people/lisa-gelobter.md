# Lisa Gelobter

The Mayor's Office identifies Lisa Gelobter as NYC Chief Technology Officer and
OTI Commissioner and as an executive leader of the PIT Crew initiative. Her
public delivery vision centers user needs, outcomes, rapid learning, public
trust, and stronger in-house government capacity.

The Senior Product Manager posting does not identify Gelobter as the direct
manager or establish her participation in a particular hiring decision.
