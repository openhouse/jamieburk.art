# Denny Vrandečić

Wikimedia's public Abstract Wikipedia team page identifies Denny Vrandečić as
Head of Special Projects and describes language-independent knowledge creation
from shared structured data. This record uses that public work to ask how stable
semantic identity and source relationships can support different reviewed
expressions for different people, agents, languages, and audiences.

## Design question

Can knowledge retain stable semantic identity and source relationships while
different people, agents, languages, and audiences receive different reviewed
projections?

## Boundary

Jamie selected this public work as a comparative design lens. This record does
not establish that Denny Vrandečić knows about, influenced, participated in,
reviewed, approved, or endorsed Jamie's Knowledge Wiki practice. Any named
role-play review is an explicitly fictionalized model artifact, not Denny
Vrandečić's opinion. Jamie remains the human publication and design decision
owner.
