# Sophie Sharps

The Campaign Finance Board publicly identifies Sophie Sharps as Director of
Policy and Research. The official job posting says the Associate Director of
Policy Implementation reports to that title, making her the nearest publicly
identified likely direct manager.

The public staff page describes her unit as leading research, data analysis,
policy development, implementation, and evaluation focused on voter behavior,
campaign finance, and the matching-funds program.

## Boundary

Public role and title alignment do not establish awareness of Jamie,
application review, interview participation, recommendation willingness, or
endorsement.
