# Warren Sack

UC Santa Cruz identifies Warren Sack as a media theorist, software designer,
artist, and professor whose work includes online public space and public
discussion. Protected educational records contain dated instructor evaluations
of Jamie's digital-media and social-software work from 2004 and 2006. The public
Wiki paraphrases their evidentiary scope without reproducing the protected text.

Warren also appears in Jamie's governed photographic field from MIT's 2024
Interrogative Design Symposium. That is event documentation, not proof of a
current professional assessment of Jamie.

## Boundary

The record supports historical teacher-student context and dated educational
reception. It does not establish Warren's current knowledge of Jamie's work,
recommendation willingness, recent supervision, permission to quote protected
evaluations, or present endorsement.
