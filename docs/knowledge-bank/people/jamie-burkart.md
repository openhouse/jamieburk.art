# Jamie Burkart

Jamie creates operating structure for complex public-facing teams. He turns
emerging work into requirements, workflows, documentation, decision trails,
launch support, onboarding materials, and durable handoffs.

The longer practice is not only administrative. Jamie looks for a structure
already latent in the people, material, place, and technical conditions of a
project, then gives it enough form to become usable without erasing how it came
to be. That throughline connects software, civic participation, cultural
infrastructure, construction coordination, public information, and archival
production.

## What Jamie does

- makes requirements and ownership visible;
- translates between technical, institutional, and public language;
- builds repeatable participation and documentation systems;
- supports implementation from unclear beginning through usable handoff;
- preserves collective credit, protected absence, and correction paths.

The July 19, 2026 encounter returned to the approved resume family, current
