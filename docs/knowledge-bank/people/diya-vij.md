# Diya Vij

The New York City Mayor's Office announced Diya Vij as Commissioner of the
Department of Cultural Affairs in February 2026. Its public biography describes
her earlier DCLA work on the Public Artists in Residence program, equity
initiatives, and CreateNYC.

This institutional context intersects with subjects documented elsewhere in
the Wiki, including CreateNYC participation and NYC Artist Coalition advocacy.
The overlap is useful for research and possible outreach, but it is not itself
a claim about a private relationship.

## Boundary

The official announcement does not establish Jamie-specific knowledge,
supervision, collaboration, recommendation willingness, permission to quote a
private exchange, or endorsement by Diya, DCLA, or the City of New York.
