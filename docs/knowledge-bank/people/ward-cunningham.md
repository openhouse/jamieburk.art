# Ward Cunningham

Ward Cunningham's public Smallest Federated Wiki materials describe a wiki that
shares through federation, composes by refactoring, and can wrap data with
visualization. This record uses that public work to ask whether a Knowledge Wiki
can preserve plurality, revision, and provenance without imposing a single
central account.

## Design question

Can a participant copy, revise, reconsider, or disagree without erasing
provenance or forcing every context into one centrally controlled account?

## Boundary

Jamie selected this public work as a comparative design lens. This record does
not establish that Ward Cunningham knows about, influenced, participated in,
reviewed, approved, or endorsed Jamie's Knowledge Wiki practice. Any named
role-play review is an explicitly fictionalized model artifact, not Ward
Cunningham's opinion. Jamie remains the human publication and design decision
owner.
