# Learning Through Making

Jamie's technical practice did not appear suddenly as administration. It grew
through art, media archaeology, social-software experiments, installations,
writing, and the repeated act of making an idea inhabitable enough to test.

## Recurring practice

- bring a historical media object into the present and learn from its behavior;
- model relationships in software and build an interface people can enter;
- translate an online structure into physical movement and space;
- make a participatory situation, observe what happens, and revise its rules;
- use writing, diagrams, sources, and documentation to distinguish intuition
  from a supported account.

The educational record contains strong analysis and moments when embodied
projects outpaced an assigned essay form. That tension belongs in the account.
Learning through making is not an excuse to avoid writing; documentation is
among the things Jamie makes.

## What became usable

The record supports a recursive model-build-observe-revise practice, movement
between physical and computational systems, and early prototypes that made
social relations inspectable. It does not establish production deployment of
classroom prototypes, field-wide invention, sole authorship of collaborative
installations, or current blanket faculty endorsement.

## Boundary

Protected educational records remain summary-only. Recover public project
documentation and collaborator credit before using a specific early work as a
public professional proof.
