# Learning Through Making

Jamie's technical practice did not appear suddenly as administration. It grew
through art, media archaeology, social-software experiments, installations,
writing, and the repeated act of making an idea inhabitable enough to test.

The public-safe record supports a method with several forms:

- bring a historical medium into the present and learn from its behavior;
- model relationships and build an interface people can enter;
- translate an information structure into physical movement and space;
- make a participatory situation, observe what happens, and revise its rules;
- document closely enough that someone who was not present can inspect it.

Learning through making is not an excuse to avoid writing. Writing, diagrams,
source trails, tests, and documentation are among the things Jamie makes.

## What Became Usable

- a recursive model-build-observe-revise practice;
- movement between physical, social, and computational systems;
- prototypes and installations that made abstract relations inspectable; and
- an understanding that documentation is part of another person's ability to
  enter and continue work.

## Boundary

This lineage does not establish production deployment of classroom prototypes,
field-wide invention, sole authorship of collaborative installations, or a
current endorsement from historical instructors. Protected evaluations,
student records, correspondence, and source locations remain outside this
public repository.
