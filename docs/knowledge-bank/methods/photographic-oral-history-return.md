# Photographic oral-history return

A photograph can hold a scene without explaining the work that made the scene
possible. Returning one governed image to Jamie creates room for situated
memory while preserving the difference between recollection, observation,
evidence, and permission.

## Return one exact image

The prompt must identify one governed public derivative, not a visually similar
private original or an untracked export. It should ask:

1. What was happening just before and after the photograph?
2. Who made it, if remembered?
3. What brought Jamie into the place?
4. What tools, papers, or actions are visible?
5. Who else shaped the work?
6. What should a stranger understand that the image cannot show?
7. Which parts remain uncertain, collective, protected, or unsuitable for publication?

## Fan out to the scene cluster

Jamie often photographs as a stop-motion documentary animator: a scene, person,
space, or idea may exist across many adjacent frames. After the exact return,
search for a bounded cluster using time, place, visible material, people,
sequence, and project context. Preserve the relationship among frames without
publishing private album names, asset identifiers, person tags, timestamps,
coordinates, or source paths.

Oral history may orient the cluster, but each factual statement still belongs
to the asset or source that supports it. One vivid account does not
automatically describe every frame. Choosing the best image is also separate
from evidence maturity, creator rights, represented-person review, captioning,
and publication.

## Receive without collapsing

The response enters the Wiki first as a dated first-person recollection or
bounded intake. It is not automatically:

- a verified event date;
- photographer attribution or rights clearance;
- consent from another represented person;
- a corroborated outcome claim;
- an approved caption, crop, credit, destination, or indexing decision.

Editors decompose the recollection into visible observation, first-person
memory, research lead, claim candidate, collective-credit question, protected
detail, and publication decision. Each part keeps its source type.

## Return to sources

Where the recollection creates a factual lead, research returns to the present:
the exact image record, contemporary documents, public records, collaborator
memory, and creator confirmation when appropriate. Contradictions remain
visible. A vivid memory is valuable evidence, but it cannot clear rights or
speak for another person.

The return should leave behind a source capsule, scene-cluster record, research
inquiry, and explicit disposition for every supplied image. A held image is not
discarded; it is available for later research without being mistaken for
approved public media.

## Promote through review

An asset record, occurrence, caption, or portfolio projection changes only
after the relevant evidence and human gates are reviewed. Jamie retains final
publication authority. Photographer rights, represented-person context,
collective credit, exact crop, caption, credit, destination, and indexing
remain distinct decisions.
