# Present-Grounded Source Return

Archival interpretation is situated. A source read for a resume claim may reveal
different relationships when reread for collaborator credit, a project history,
a photo brief, or a public-institutional chronology. The Wiki therefore records
dated source encounters instead of letting one summary become a permanent
substitute for the source.

## Encounter contract

Every governed source return records:

- encounter date and planned return date;
- whether the encounter reached an original source, a source family, or only a
  canonical public-safe record;
- access state and source classes, without protected locators;
- what the present reading changed or confirmed;
- what remains unresolved;
- a public-safe request to Jamie when the needed material cannot be reached;
- which human rights, consent, credit, or publication gates remain open.

## Access sequence

1. Begin with the canonical claim and its cited source IDs.
2. Follow public URLs or an authorized local source-access registry outside the
   public repository.
3. Confirm that the source is materially available and identify its version or
   cutoff.
4. Read the source itself when permitted; do not silently substitute an older
   summary.
5. Compare the new reading with current claims, anti-claims, corrections, and
   collective-credit boundaries.
6. Record a public-safe encounter here and keep exact protected coordinates in
   the authorized private environment.

When a source cannot be found or materialized, ask Jamie, the personal
librarian, for help locating or authorizing it. Record `blocked` or `partial`;
do not invent access, contents, or absence.

`npm run wiki:tasks` reports wanted pages, overdue returns, and blocked
encounters. A blocked encounter must say what help is needed without publishing
the protected locator itself.

## Publication boundary

Source access is not publication permission. A successful reread does not clear
rights, establish consent, approve collaborator naming, authorize a stronger
claim, or require portfolio projection.
