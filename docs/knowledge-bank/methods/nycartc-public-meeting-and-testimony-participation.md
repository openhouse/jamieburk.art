# NYC Artist Coalition Public-Meeting And Testimony Participation

Two protected coalition documents reveal a participation method with an outer
production layer and an inner participant-support layer.

The public-meeting checklist coordinates personal outreach to individuals,
partners, and elected officials with email, social, testimony, press, web,
print, signage, calls to action, and day-of follow-through. The testimony guide
helps participants turn lived experience into a brief first-person account and
offers an alternate submission route when appearing in person is not possible.

Read together, the documents show that participation was treated as something
to produce and support, not merely announce. The meeting needed a coordinated
public surface; participants needed usable ways to enter it.

## Present Source Encounter

Both native Google Docs were re-opened on July 19, 2026 through Jamie's
authenticated account. The present encounter confirmed the two-layer pattern
and added the alternate submission route to the Wiki's account of participation
design.

The evidence remains collective. It does not establish Jamie as original
author of either guide, assign him every production task, show that every step
occurred at every event, provide usage or attendance totals, or prove policy
impact. Public projection remains pending attribution and editorial review.
