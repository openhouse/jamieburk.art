# Source-Backed Team Memory

Source-Backed Team Memory is a bounded lab method for helping knowledge-heavy
teams turn working material into reviewable, human-correctable, source-linked
operating memory.

The method begins with an approved safe or synthetic source bundle. It produces
inspectable records, concrete onboarding and decision-memory artifacts, privacy
and retention notes, and an explicit continue, revise, or stop decision. Human
review remains part of the system.

## Current boundary

This is a developing method and consulting practice, not a finished SaaS
product. Private collaborator, pricing, and proposal details remain outside the
public projection. The detailed public-safe claim is maintained in
[claims.md](../claims.md#source-backed-team-memory-method-source-backed-team-memory).

## Why it belongs in the wiki

The method demonstrates how the system should behave: preserve context,
separate authored and derived records, make uncertainty visible, and leave a
durable handoff for the next reader. It is connected to the
[Technical Operations capability](../capabilities/technical-operations.md), but
the two are not interchangeable.
