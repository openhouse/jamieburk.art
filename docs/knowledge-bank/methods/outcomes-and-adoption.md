# Outcomes and adoption

The Wiki must not use impact as a container for unlike things. A strong project
account separates what Jamie delivered, what another person used, what an
institution formally adopted, and what changed later.

## Four-part test

| Layer | Evidence needed | Example question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jamie delivered | Artifact, action, role, or completion record | What did Jamie make, operate, decide, or hand off? |
| Collaborators used | Attributable use, reuse, handoff, or workflow evidence | Who used the form, and in what bounded way? |
| Institution adopted | Vote, enactment, policy, release, contract, or owner decision | Which authority adopted what, and when? |
| Conditions changed | Measured result or carefully bounded chronology | What changed afterward, and what causal link is supported? |

## Selected distinctions

| Project | Jamie delivered | Collective or institutional outcome | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Let NYC Dance | Organizing action and public campaign infrastructure | Council repeal of the Cabaret Law | No individual legislative causality |
| Talks Not Raids | Testimony and campaign infrastructure | Local Law 220; later City replacement of MARCH with CURE | Aligned chronology, not sole causality or an end to all enforcement |
| Office town hall | Documented organizer and production contribution | A completed public dialogue in a cultural venue | No proof every recommendation was adopted |
| KC Town Hall | Completed Phase One delivery and neighborhood process | Later Phase Two authorization, withdrawal, and unused-fund reappropriation | Authorization was not receipt or completion |
| Fair Rent NYC | Coalition operating design and public-data requirements | Emerging work | No adopted City program or released dataset |
| Harry J. Epstein | Long-term modernization and workflow contribution | Period of revenue growth | Contribution language; private attribution data remains protected |

## Anti-inflation rules

- A plan is not delivery.
- Delivery is not adoption.
- Adoption is not implementation.
- A later institutional change is not proof of campaign causality.
- A public response is not verified participation or impact.
- Revenue growth during Jamie's work is not growth caused solely by Jamie.
- A funding appropriation is not receipt or expenditure.

Every outcome claim should name its evidence state, the authority that could
establish the next state, and the smallest evidence needed to advance.
