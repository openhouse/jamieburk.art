# Maintenance, handoff, and stewardship

A usable result includes the conditions under which another person can operate,
question, repair, or replace it. Maintenance and handoff are part of delivery,
not an epilogue added after the interesting work is over.

## Working sequence

### Learn the inherited system

Identify who already carries knowledge, where the current process succeeds,
which workarounds are protective, and what cannot be changed without authority
or consent.

### Make operation visible

Expose dependencies, ownership boundaries, recurring tasks, exceptional cases,
rights, risks, and decision history. Documentation should serve the future
reader who was not in the room.

### Maintain in use

Treat corrections, questions, support requests, renewals, and changing
conditions as operational evidence. A launched interface or event is not proof
that the system is understood or sustainable.

### Transfer responsibility

Name the successor, custodian, or decision-making body when the evidence allows.
Provide source context, access boundaries, open issues, and a way to revise the
system without depending indefinitely on its original implementer.

### Preserve a bounded record

Record what was transferred and what remains unknown, protected, temporary, or
person-dependent. A graceful handoff may include retiring a tool, withholding
private context, or acknowledging that continuity is not yet secured.

## Across projects

- At [Harry J. Epstein Company](../projects/harry-j-epstein.md), continuity
  meant translating specialized staff and catalog knowledge into maintainable
  commerce and operating forms without erasing the store's voice.
- In [KC Spaces Fund](../projects/kc-spaces-fund-orientation.md), Jamie's
  documented contribution was technical and operational support, while public
  organizer credit remained with the named organizers.
- In [KC Town Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall-orientation.md), the public Wiki
  preserves a bounded transition and deliberately omits private family context.
- In the [Knowledge Wiki](../indexes/living-archive.md), maintenance includes
  source-return dates, contradictions, wanted records, compiler checks, human
  gates, and selective public projection.

## Boundary

Stewardship is not permanent personal ownership, invisible unpaid availability,
or permission to retain access after responsibility changes. A handoff record
does not prove adoption or relieve receiving people and institutions of their
own authority and care.

## Evidence and credit

Treat continued operation, repair, archival survival, retirement,
administrative transfer, and social handoff as separate states. For each state,
record the actor, artifact, authority, and date only to the resolution the
evidence supports. Continued custody does not establish authorship; an artifact
surviving does not establish adoption; and a transition record may remain
publicly bounded to protect personal context.
