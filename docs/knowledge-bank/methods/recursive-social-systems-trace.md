# Recursive social-systems trace

This page asks whether Jamie's recurring systems method can be reconstructed
from current public-safe work. It is an evaluative cross-project view, not a
claim that the projects are identical or that every stage belonged to Jamie
alone.

The sequence is:

1. observe relationships;
2. model a system;
3. prototype an interface;
4. test it through social or collective use;
5. record what the use taught;
6. document enough for another person to continue, question, or revise it.

## Fair Rent NYC

**Current status:** The 2026 record documents ongoing campaign-memory and
coordination work with current small-business and policy partners.

- **Relationships observed:** cultural-space and small-business experience,
  recurring meetings, public sources, policy and legal questions, and distinct
  city and state actors.
- **System modeled:** city and state lanes, legislative provenance, source
  status, decision memory, assigned work, and open questions.
- **Interface and collective use:** campaign sites, meetings, hearings, public
  action pathways, and more than 30 pages of shared campaign-memory and
  coordination infrastructure used in collective work.
- **Learning:** running minutes and review states preserve changes, disputes,
  decisions, and next steps rather than forcing agreement into one account.
- **Handoff:** source maps, question logs, action trackers, and bounded
  publication rules let collaborators recover context and continue.

The project is collective. This trace does not make Jamie the sole campaign
leader, bill author, legal authority, or cause of legislative action.

## Source-Backed Team Memory and the Knowledge Wiki

**Current status:** This portfolio and its public-safe Knowledge Wiki are a
live operating test of the method. The separate lab page remains accurately
described as early research and consulting practice, not production SaaS or
evidence of client adoption.

- **Relationships observed:** sources, claims, contributors, contradictions,
  open questions, protected context, audiences, and publication decisions.
- **System modeled:** stable records and typed relations distinguish known,
  open, protected, corrected, and projected states.
- **Interface and collective use:** Jamie and AI-assisted editors use the
  Markdown Wiki, graph compiler, selective website projections, tests, and
  independent evaluation receipts to research and revise this portfolio.
- **Learning:** corrections, failed evaluations, wanted records, source-return
  dates, and publication holds remain inspectable instead of disappearing in a
  final narrative.
- **Handoff:** schemas, record contracts, generated indexes, source boundaries,
  and repeatable checks let another editor inspect, challenge, and continue the
  work without receiving the private archive.

This is proof of a current method in use on Jamie's own professional archive.
It is not proof of external organizational adoption or production-scale
deployment.

## Living photographic knowledge

**Current status:** The living photographic-knowledge practice is a governed
pilot joining selection, composition, exact-use review, oral-history return,
and rollback.

- **Relationships observed:** source photographs, creators, represented people,
  editors, project histories, destinations, crops, captions, permissions, and
  readers.
- **System modeled:** private source assets, public-safe derivatives, photo
  sets, exact occurrences, rights summaries, recollections, decisions, and
  human release gates remain distinct.
- **Interface and collective use:** Jamie and AI-assisted editors use private
  curation fields, the public-safe Wiki, active website compositions, browser
  review, and pull-request evaluation to make and assess specific proposals.
- **Learning:** composition can return an image to inquiry. In this pass, a
  held gathering photograph produced a source-free oral-history prompt rather
  than entering the site without context, credit, or represented-person review.
- **Handoff:** exact-candidate receipts, occurrence records, permissions,
  captions, crop checks, canaries, and rollback instructions preserve what a
  future editor needs to review or revise the composition.

The pilot does not clear photographs for publication. Jamie approval, creator
and rights-holder authority, exact crop and credit, represented-person dignity,
production deployment, and indexing remain human gates.

## What the comparison does not claim

The trace does not establish disciplinary invention, peer-reviewed research,
production deployment, sole authorship, or current proficiency in historical
course tools. Historical projects remain valuable lineage, but they are not
counted here as the three current traces. Similarity means a recurring working
sequence, not that civic organizing, business operations, knowledge systems,
and photographic practice are interchangeable.
