# Structure grows from the material

This is a synthesis of a recurring method, not a quotation or a claim that one
formula governed every project.

Jamie often begins by assuming that a situation already contains an emerging
structure. It may be embodied in staff memory, neighborhood stories, paper
records, code, event rhythms, a building, a catalog, a campaign archive, or a
conflict that has not yet found adequate public language. The work is to
observe closely enough that the structure becomes visible, then build a form
people can inhabit and continue.

## The method

### Observe

Enter the situation. Listen to the language people already use. Examine the
documents, tools, material constraints, and informal workarounds.

### Model

Separate known, open, and protected knowledge. Identify actors, decisions,
dependencies, risks, and the future reader who was not in the room.

### Prototype

Make the smallest useful form: a page, map, handbill, agenda, database,
interface, checklist, event, or physical repair sequence.

### Use and learn

Let the form meet people and reality. Treat confusion, workarounds, changed
conditions, and disagreement as information rather than disobedience.

### Revise and transfer

Improve the structure, document its limits, and leave another person enough
context to maintain, question, or replace it.

## Examples

- At Harry J. Epstein Company, specialized store knowledge became searchable
  e-commerce and operational workflows without erasing the store's voice.
- In WOWList, organizer practice became followable keyword communities and
  reusable event-distribution patterns.
- In KC Town Hall, building work and neighborhood encounters informed surveys,
  public-benefit documentation, and resident-service interfaces.
- In NYC Artist Coalition, recurring listening became campaign artifacts,
  public forums, websites, and shared memory.
- In the Knowledge Wiki, archival fragments become bounded claims while
  protected material remains absent from public Git.

## Professional translation

In a hiring context, this method appears as discovery, requirements definition,
workflow mapping, implementation planning, stakeholder translation, quality
assurance, adoption support, documentation, and handoff.

## Boundary

Emergent structure does not mean the designer is neutral or that community
knowledge can be extracted without consent. The method requires attribution,
participation, correction, and the possibility that the right answer is to
leave something unbuilt or unpublished.
