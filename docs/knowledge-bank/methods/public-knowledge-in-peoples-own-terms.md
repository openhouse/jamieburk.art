# Public Knowledge In People's Own Terms

Public systems become more accurate and usable when the people living the
conditions can name what is happening, what matters, and what action is
possible. The method creates forms for contribution without converting private
speech into extractable portfolio material.

## Practice

- preserve public language and self-description before translating it;
- distinguish a participant's account from Jamie's interpretation;
- record authorship of public statements when attribution is known and fair;
- let surveys, meetings, testimony, and public calls change the working model;
- summarize patterns without publishing person-level records; and
- retain disagreement, uncertainty, nonparticipation, and protected absence as
  meaningful states.

## Project Anchors

[KC Town Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall.md) joined site work with a neighborhood
survey and recurring conversation. [NYC Artist Coalition](../projects/nyc-artist-coalition.md)
translated cultural-space concerns into meetings, testimony, campaign pathways,
and public resources through collective work. [WOW List](../projects/wowlist.md)
supported shared cultural information without turning contributors into Jamie's
audience property.

## Protected Boundary

Raw community records, private correspondence, participant lists, unapproved
quotations, sensitive testimony, and relationship graphs remain outside this
public repository. Public usefulness does not cancel consent or context.
