# Public Knowledge in People's Own Terms

Public systems become more accurate and more usable when the people living the
conditions can name what is happening, what matters, and what action is
possible. The method is to create forms for contribution without converting
private speech into extractable portfolio material.

## Practice

- preserve public language and self-description before translating it;
- distinguish a participant's account from Jamie's interpretation;
- record who authored public statements when attribution is known and fair;
- let surveys, meetings, testimony, and public calls change the working model;
- summarize patterns without publishing person-level records;
- retain disagreement, uncertainty, and nonparticipation as meaningful states.

## Project anchors

- [KC Town Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall.md) joined daily site work with a
  neighborhood survey and recurring conversation.
- [NYC Artist Coalition](../organizations/nyc-artist-coalition.md) translated
  artist and cultural-space concerns into meetings, testimony, campaign
  pathways, and public resources through collective work.
- [WOWList](../projects/wowlist.md) provided shared event-distribution
  infrastructure without turning contributors into official chapters or
  Jamie's audience property.

## Protected boundary

Raw community records, private correspondence, participant lists, unapproved
quotations, and sensitive testimony remain outside this public repository.
Public usefulness does not cancel consent or context.
