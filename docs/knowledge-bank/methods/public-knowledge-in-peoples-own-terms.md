# Public knowledge in people's own terms

Public systems become more accurate and usable when people living the
conditions can name what is happening, what matters, and what action is
possible. The method creates forms for contribution without converting private
speech into extractable portfolio material.

## Practice

- preserve public language and self-description before translating it;
- distinguish a participant's account from Jamie's interpretation;
- record authorship of public statements when attribution is known and fair;
- let surveys, meetings, testimony, and public calls change the working model;
- summarize patterns without publishing person-level records; and
- retain disagreement, uncertainty, nonparticipation, and refusal as meaningful
  states.

## Project anchors

- [KC Town Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall-orientation.md) joined daily site work
  with a neighborhood survey and recurring conversation.
- [NYC Artist Coalition](../projects/nyc-artist-coalition-2017.md) translated
  artist and cultural-space concerns into meetings, testimony, campaign
  pathways, and public resources through collective work.
- [WOWList](../projects/wowlist-orientation.md) supported shared event
  distribution without turning contributors into official chapters or Jamie's
  audience property.

## Boundary

Raw community records, private correspondence, participant lists, unapproved
quotations, and sensitive testimony remain outside this public repository.
Public usefulness does not cancel consent, context, credit, or the right to
remain absent.
