# Photographic archive fieldwork

Jamie maintains a lifetime-scale private photographic archive. It can support
memory, research, project reconstruction, visual storytelling, photo-editor
briefs, and new source-backed claims. It must not be copied into this public
repository or treated as a pool of automatically publishable images.

The source archive remains read-only. Every working selection needs a stable
route back to the exact source while filenames, private paths, EXIF and location
data, face or people labels, sensitive subjects, and detailed safety annotations
remain in a protected local layer.

## Distinctions to preserve

- Discovery is not selection.
- Selection is not safety clearance.
- Safety clearance is not rights or represented-person consent.
- Rights and consent do not determine editorial usefulness.
- Editorial usefulness is not publication approval.
- Jamie's custody of a file does not by itself establish authorship or rights.
- A photograph may generate a research lead without becoming public evidence.

## Bounded field protocol

1. Name the present editorial or research question.
2. Identify a bounded source cohort without changing the library.
3. Verify that candidate files are materially reachable and decodable.
4. Preserve source identity and duplicate, sequence, and burst relationships.
5. Inspect the image itself; metadata and machine descriptions are navigation
   aids, not substitutes for looking.
6. Record only what is visibly supportable, with uncertainty and anti-claims.
7. Seek independent sources for identity, date, role, event, and outcome claims.
8. Review safety, authorship, rights, represented people, crop, caption, and
   context as separate questions.
9. Bind any approval to an exact asset version and a specific public use.
10. Return new factual leads to the Knowledge Bank intake and review lifecycle.

## Event transcript and prior-select retrieval

Public-event recordings and documentary photo sequences can be researched
together. Use the [event transcript and photo-select workflow
record](../sources/jamie-event-photo-select-workflow-recollection-2026-07.md)
to create a protected local candidate cohort from event time, place, speakers,
existing Apple Photos People associations, album context, and prior export
lineage.

Recursive `_keep` depth is a useful prior-curation signal because Jamie's Photo
Select tool re-runs selection inside each retained set. It is not a score,
publication approval, or substitute for looking. A People association is a
private retrieval aid and must never be treated as represented-person consent.
Every event-photo association needs visual review and a recorded basis; ambiguous
matches stay hypotheses.

## Private catalog mutation boundary

The source library remains read-only at the database layer. When Jamie grants a
specific Apple Photos workspace, a governed helper may create folders or albums
and add existing membership only within that workspace. Every such operation
requires a frozen source contract, exact plan, excluded HOLD set, fresh app
receipt, nonce-distinct rerun, and independent WAL-aware read-only verification.
It may not delete assets, move prior collections, change originals or metadata,
alter favorites, infer or edit People, or treat catalog membership as
publication clearance.

## What an image does not establish

A visible detail does not establish the full event, identity, role, date, or
outcome around it. Proximity does not establish relationship, participation,
endorsement, authorship, or consent. Technical metadata may help locate a
question, but it does not replace visual inspection or independent
corroboration.

## Public-safe notebook boundary

The [working notebook](../indexes/photography-working-notebook.md) may retain
aggregate, non-sensitive field descriptions; opaque identities; bounded visual
observations; anti-claims; project and source relationships; photo-editor
questions; and held or specific-use states. It may not expose the private
working field or enough detail to reconstruct a protected locator.

## Publication gate

A photograph remains held unless its exact source identity is stable, the file
has been visually reviewed, the proposed caption is evidence-bounded, safety
review passes, authorship and rights are understood, represented-person review
is complete where required, the crop and context are approved, and Jamie
approves that exact version for that exact surface.

An AI system may assist discovery, grouping, description, and evaluation. It
may not clear a safety hold, infer consent, or grant publication permission.

## Revision

The method is intentionally revisable. The first private field should teach us
what the archive affords before later versions impose more structure. New
categories should follow repeated encounters, not precede them as rigid quotas.
