# Collective Credit and Protected Absence

Collective language should not make Jamie disappear, and individual role
language should not absorb work that belonged to a coalition, team, community,
or institution.

## Practice

- Name Jamie's documented action directly.
- Name the collective outcome as collective.
- Separate infrastructure contribution from policy causality.
- Preserve collaborator names only when public, relevant, fair, and approved.
- Treat intentionally omitted material as a boundary, not as nonexistence.
- Keep rights, consent, access, evidence, and publication as separate states.
