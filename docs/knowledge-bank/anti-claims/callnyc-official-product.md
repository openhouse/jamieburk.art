# CallNYC was not an official Council product

CallNYC was an independent follow-on project. The record does not support
describing it as a New York City Council product, commissioned service, formal
hackathon submission, or documented winner.

This boundary protects the credibility of the
[independent follow-on claim](../claims/callnyc-independent-follow-on.md) while
still making Jamie's actual design and implementation work visible.
