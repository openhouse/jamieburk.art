## Chad Lens

Lead with Jamie as the actor, then name the team and the limit:

> Jamie co-founded NYC Artist Coalition and helped build the participation,
> public identity, campaign, web, safety, testimony, and memory systems that
> made a complex field easier to organize and continue. Coalition and policy
> outcomes belong to collaborators, movement partners, legislators, agencies,
> venues, and public participants.

This wording is stronger than hiding Jamie inside collective nouns. It is also
more defensible than claiming that he individually created the Office of
Nightlife, repealed the Cabaret Law, ended M.A.R.C.H., or authored the entire
shared archive.
