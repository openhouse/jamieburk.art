## Return Format

For each candidate, return only a protected reference plus:

- likely project and approximate date;
- visible action or artifact;
- known and uncertain identities;
- photographer and rights status;
- participant or venue consent concerns;
- the claim or inquiry it may clarify;
- what the image cannot establish; and
- recommended disposition: reject, retain for research, request context,
  request permission, or propose for independent projection review.

## Boundaries

- A visual match is a research lead, not proof of identity, date, role,
  authorship, consent, project meaning, scale, causation, or impact.
- Social-media publication is not permission for portfolio republication.
- Private photo locators, contact information, faces not needed for the
  professional argument, and unapproved images remain outside this repo.
- Community, collaborator, artist, venue, and public-official credit stays
  attached to the relevant project.
- No image can mature or activate a claim automatically.
