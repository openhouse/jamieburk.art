# Photo Feedback Loop

Photo selection is reciprocal research, not decoration after the claims are
finished.

1. Mature claims generate bounded photo briefs: project, timeframe, people or
   spaces to seek, visible actions, and consent risks.
2. A photo editor reviews the private archive and returns candidate references,
   not public assets by default.
3. Unexpected visual evidence enters the intake ledger as a photo lead.
4. A researcher identifies context, date, people, permissions, and corroborating
   sources before proposing a claim.
5. The claim and the image receive independent public-safety and projection
   decisions.

An image must never become evidence merely because it appears persuasive. It
may suggest a research question; publication requires contextual verification
and a separate consent review.
