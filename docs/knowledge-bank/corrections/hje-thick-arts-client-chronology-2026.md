# Harry J. Epstein Company and Thick Arts LLC client chronology correction

Jamie Burkart states that Harry J. Epstein Company was a client from 2009-2015.
During that engagement, he formalized his independent practice as Thick Arts
LLC, with HJE as the LLC's first client. The New York Department of State
record separately gives July 6, 2012 as the LLC's initial filing date.

The public-safe chronology therefore distinguishes three related facts:

- Jamie's professional practice was active by 2009 and continues;
- Thick Arts LLC was legally formed on July 6, 2012; and
- the HJE engagement ran from 2009-2015 and was not current after 2015.

This replaces both the earlier undifferentiated `2012-present` presentation and
the provisional `2009-2011` correction. It does not backdate the legal entity
to 2009 or use a corporate filing to infer client history.

## Boundary

Jamie's first-party correction establishes the company/client relationship and
the maintained portfolio range. The official filing record establishes the
legal formation date only. Neither source independently enumerates every
contract, invoice, billing month, or later maintenance interaction. Company
and client outcomes remain collectively produced and separately bounded.
