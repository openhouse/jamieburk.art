# Correct project photograph display credits

The correction separates public display credit from an unverified claim of
individual authorship. The project courtesy line is the visitor-facing credit;
the governed asset record may continue to preserve unresolved creator state.

This correction does not assign individual authorship to a project, extend the
existing publication permission to another destination, or change the rights,
consent, dignity, and exact-release gates for any occurrence.

The [superseded person record](../people/paul-mossine.md) remains available only
as correction history and must not support a current creator credit.
