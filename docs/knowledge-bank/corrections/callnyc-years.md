# CallNYC chronology correction

## Previous wording

Earlier portfolio material placed CallNYC in `2014-2015`.

## Corrected wording

The supported project year is `2016`.

## Reason

Recovered event, data-release, source-repository, and press chronology places
the Council event on January 30, the fuller data release in early March, Jamie's
independent development of CallNYC in March, and Politico coverage on March 14,
2016.

The canonical correction is `COR-CALLNYC-CHRONOLOGY-2026`. Affected surfaces
include the Work index, [CallNYC case study](../projections/callnyc-case-study.md),
Knowledge Wiki record, and resume.
