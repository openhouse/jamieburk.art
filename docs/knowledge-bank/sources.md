## Private Evidence Not Published

Private source material may support public-safe claims, but it must stay outside
the repo unless transformed into an approved public artifact.

Examples include private meeting notes, raw transcripts, internal dashboards,
private coalition records, legal-review context, raw community records, contact
lists, unreviewed screenshots, and working files.
