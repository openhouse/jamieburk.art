### Public-Safe Archive Summary

Aggregate or summarized knowledge derived from private records without exposing
the private records, private paths, names, contact lists, raw transcripts,
attendance records, private rows, screenshots, or correspondence.

The July 13, 2026 Google Drive Shared Drives pass adds bounded summaries of:

- a Jamie-authored commercial vacancy and lease-cost public-data proposal;
- one dated 196 Artists Residency acceptance and onboarding workflow;
- a collective 2017 NYC Artist Coalition mutual-support resource artifact;
- an unattributed 2019 machine-readable violation-reporting recommendation;
- a bounded Source-Backed Team Memory Sprint proposal; and
- metadata-only Sunday Dinner and commercial-vacancy visual leads.

The complete Shared Drive inventory, private drive names, file IDs, access
links, membership, private filenames, participant rows, and underlying records
remain protected. File presence or Shared Drive custody does not establish
authorship, distribution, adoption, public permission, or outcome.

### AI-Assisted Archival Review

Evidence-based notes generated by AI after Jamie reviews local/private archive
materials. These may support internal claim development only when the resulting
public claim is independently bounded, source-labeled, and stripped of private
paths, raw correspondence, lists, payment records, credentials, and sensitive
personal or financial material.

AI-assisted archival review is not a human collaborator testimonial.
