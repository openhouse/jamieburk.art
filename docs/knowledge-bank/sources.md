# Sources

This file defines source classes and protected source categories for
jamieburk.art.

The Knowledge Bank may mention source classes. It must not contain protected
source contents.

Never commit private source files to prove a claim. Use public-safe source
classes and summaries.

## Source Classes

### Approved resume

Claims present in the current approved resume PDF.

Safe behavior: use the exact approved resume wording or a narrower public
variant. Contact details in the PDF do not automatically become approved for
HTML.

### Approved resume candidate, pending Jamie launch approval

Resume material that appears ready but still needs Jamie's explicit launch
approval.

Safe behavior: keep in the Knowledge Bank or staging review until approved.

### Public project artifact

A project artifact already intended for public access, such as a public website,
public campaign page, public handout, public map, or public press page.

Safe behavior: link or summarize accurately. Do not infer private facts from a
public artifact.

### Public website / public artifact

Current public pages and artifacts approved as source context.

Safe behavior: cite or summarize, with dates if a fact may change.

### Public source / public record

Government records, public webpages, public press, public repositories, and
other externally public materials.

Safe behavior: use what the public source actually proves. Do not make a public
record carry private context it does not contain.

### Public-safe archive summary

A summary of protected or mixed source material rewritten to remove private
details, local paths, names, screenshots, quotes, and raw records.

Safe behavior: use the summary as confidence and wording support. Keep the
protected source outside Git.

### Firsthand / collaborator context

Direct knowledge from Jamie or collaborators that may support the claim but may
not be independently public.

Safe behavior: use only public-safe summaries and approved attribution.

### Conversation summary

A public-safe synthesis of a private or semi-private conversation.

Safe behavior: do not quote private calls, attribute private comments, or name
participants unless explicitly approved.

### Private-source-outside-repo

Protected source material that may inform confidence but must not be committed.

Safe behavior: reference only the source class and a public-safe summary.

### Approval pending

Material that may be true but still needs Jamie, collaborator, citation,
screenshot, or artifact approval before public use.

Safe behavior: keep out of production-facing content.

### Do-not-publish

Material or claim types that must not appear on the public site.

Safe behavior: keep out of public app content, public metadata, sitemap,
screenshots, and PR bodies intended as public proof.

## Protected Source Categories

- raw transcripts
- private correspondence
- private emails
- legal-review materials
- private coalition notes
- raw strategy context
- stakeholder lists
- guest lists
- resident records
- client-private documents
- internal analytics
- credentials
- unapproved screenshots
- unapproved quotes
- unapproved photos
- private financial records
- private legal records
- private personal records
- private phone numbers
- private / proprietary font files

## Operating Rules

- Public sources may be linked if appropriate.
- Approved resume claims may be used only if the exact resume PDF is approved.
- Public-safe summaries may be used without exposing protected evidence.
- Private archive summaries may inform confidence but must not reveal source
  contents.
- Conversation summaries must not quote private calls unless explicitly
  approved.
- Do not paste private local file paths into source notes.
- Do not use source material to create new public obligations for collaborators
  who have not approved the publication.
