# Creative Technology and Media

## Why This Dossier Exists

This dossier preserves a public-source-backed creative-technology lineage that
is useful for future portfolio composition but is not currently projected onto
the primary website. The records show Jamie moving among code, interaction,
physical installation, sound, and moving image while working within collective
credits.

## Sorted Audio

Monthly Music Hackathon NYC documented Jamie's February 2013 Max/MSP prototype.
It divided an audio work into small segments and reordered the clips by pitch.

Claim: `CLM-MUSIC-HACKATHON-SORTED-AUDIO` - confirmed with boundary.

Use this as evidence of rapid technical prototyping and computational media
experimentation. Do not describe it as a production product, commercial
deployment, or long-term adoption.

## NTER CHNG

The Pitch described NTER CHNG as a 2010 installation combining software and
architecture: visitors sent cellphone messages through both faces of a digital
wall, where the messages accumulated into a public virtual dialogue.

The project video credits Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier as
designers, Garrett as programmer, and Mary Nichols as helping engineer and
construct the wall.

Claim: `CLM-NTER-CHNG-INTERACTIVE-INSTALLATION` - confirmed with boundary.

Preserve every collaborator's credit. Do not assign Garrett's programmer credit
to Jamie or imply recovered attendance, message volume, or long-term deployment.

## Horse Lords - Truthers

The official Horse Lords video description credits M.C. Schmidt and Jamie with
the video for *Truthers*. NPR editorial coverage independently attributes the
work to both collaborators and reports the band's view that its repetition and
variation mirrored the music.

Claim: `CLM-HORSE-LORDS-TRUTHERS-VIDEO` - confirmed with boundary.

Use “co-created” or “video by M.C. Schmidt and Jamie Burkart.” Do not imply sole
direction, a complete recovered labor division, or unverified audience and
commercial outcomes.
