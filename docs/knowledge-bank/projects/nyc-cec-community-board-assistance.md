# CEC community-board assistance and training

The [official CEC training page](https://home4.nyc.gov/site/civicengagement/our-programs/community-boards.page) describes assistance for all 59 community boards and names Benjamin Solotaire as its contact.

## Documented civic-technology links

- **NYC Office of Data Analytics:** Open Data workshops offered June 12, 24 and 26, 2026, using the [Open Data Ambassadors curriculum](https://www.nyc.gov/discoveropendata).
- **BetaNYC:** AI training offered April 21, 23 and 28, 2026. The separately maintained [BetaNYC source](../sources/betanyc-civic-tech-newsletter-2026-08-20.md) documents its recurring civic-technology discovery channel, not these workshop dates.

These are historical program offerings and institutional connections. They do not establish that Jamie attended, knows the presenters, or has a referral route. Future trainings require a fresh schedule check.
