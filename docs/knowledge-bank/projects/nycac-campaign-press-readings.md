# NYC Artist Coalition campaign press readings

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-14

**Scope:** Forty-four distinct articles represented by 45 placements in the
Let NYC Dance, Talks Not Raids, Save NYC Spaces, and Fair Rent NYC campaign
press sections.

## Recovery result

- 32 readings use recovered publisher pages.
- 12 readings use Wayback captures, including a CityLab redirect that led to a
  generic Bloomberg page rather than the requested article.
- 43 records contain recovered article or program-page text.
- The Crain's record is explicitly limited to its archived headline and deck.
- Every record retains a public URL, article-specific locator, reviewed-content
  fingerprint, bounded paraphrase, supported propositions, and non-claims in
  `apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/nycac-press-readings.ts`.

Copyrighted bodies remain outside the public repository. The ledger records
what the reviewed source reports or argues; it does not convert every reported
proposition into an independently verified portfolio claim.

## Boundaries

- Campaign placement establishes curation, not endorsement.
- Article reporting establishes what an outlet reported or argued, not the
  independent truth of every proposition.
- A source may name Jamie or NYC Artist Coalition without establishing sole
  authorship, sole leadership, or causation of a collective or government
  outcome.
- Evidentiary maturity and public-site selection remain separate decisions.
- “Not fully recovered” remains distinct from “did not exist.”
