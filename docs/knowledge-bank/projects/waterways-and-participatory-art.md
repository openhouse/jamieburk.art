# Waterways and Participatory Art

Status: developed in the knowledge bank; not selected for the current public
portfolio.

## Source-Backed Claims

### CLM-WATERWAYS-EXPEDITION-CONCEPTION

Contemporaneous reporting supports that Jamie conceived an experiential boat
expedition from an inquiry into Kansas City's transportation history and the
Missouri River. The article describes the group crossing Missouri on a
homemade raft made from recycled materials.

Boundary: preserve the collective expedition frame. This source does not
establish that Jamie alone built or operated the raft, the full route, or a
Gulf arrival.

Source: [Eric Barton, "When Artists Turn Huck Finn," *The Pitch*, August 9,
2007](https://www.thepitchkc.com/when-artists-turn-huck-finn/).

### CLM-WATERWAYS-GREAT-ACCOMMODATIONS-PROGRAM

Charlotte Street's institutional record supports that Jamie spearheaded Great
Accommodations as a participatory exhibition and public-program framework. It
used rivers to connect communities, included outreach with Suzanne Hogan, and
combined interactive video, participatory sculpture, computer programs,
community projects, working documentation, and facilitation.

Boundary: credit Suzanne Hogan's documented outreach collaboration and do not
absorb participant stories into Jamie's authorship. The page does not measure
participation or impact.

Source: [Charlotte Street, "Great Accommodations with Jamie Burkart - Imagining
Lifestyles for Cities on the Water," September 1,
2009](https://charlottestreet.org/event/great-accommodations-with-jamie-burkart-imagining-lifestyles-for-cities-on-the-water/).

### CLM-OPEN-HOUSE-PARTICIPATORY-DESIGN

Good Times supports that Jamie initiated and tended Open House as a ten-day gallery-based
communal-living experiment, building on the earlier collective Shop Shows. The
article reports that Open House placed responsibility and decision-making with
the group while Jamie remained visibly responsible for welcoming people and
tending the conditions that let the experiment continue.

Boundary: do not erase housemates and participants, and do not republish
sensitive personal circumstances included in the article.

Source: [Laura Mattingly, "Open House," *Good Times*, June 28,
2006](https://www.goodtimes.sc/archives/metro-santa-cruz/06.28.06/open-house-0626.html).
