# NYC Artist Coalition: 2017 organizing record

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-12

**Public projection:** Two direct claims selected for `/work/fair-rent-nyc`.

## Defensible claims

- Gothamist documented Jamie organizing fire-code study groups for DIY venues,
  rallying at City Hall for full Cabaret Law repeal, speaking as part of NYC
  Artist Coalition, and articulating how licensing fear could deter spaces from
  seeking fire-safety guidance.
- A Greene Hill Food Co-op profile documented Jamie and Julia working through
  NYC Artist Coalition on Cabaret Law repeal and inviting residents to a
  September 28 town hall for dialogue with the new Office of Nightlife.
- The same community profile documented weekly Sunday dinners open to the
  community and community-event publishing through WOW List. That claim remains
  held for a future composition.

## Boundaries

These sources support direct participation, organizing, public communication,
and stated intent. They do not establish that Jamie alone repealed the Cabaret
Law, created NYC Artist Coalition, created the Office of Nightlife, produced
every town hall, or caused a policy outcome.
