# Teams archive production - 2026-07-14

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-14

**Scope:** `Jamie Projects History`, `CRS`, and `job-hunt` in Jamie's iCloud
working archive

The canonical records from this pass live in
`apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/teams-archive-production.ts` and are merged
into `records.ts`. This note records method and editorial decisions; it is not a
second source of truth.

## Method

This pass treated the folders as a working archive rather than a collection of
files to publish. It:

1. mapped the required folder families and read their overview records;
2. close-read promising public captures and private working artifacts;
3. decomposed each useful item into atomic observations;
4. created bounded source records that say both what the item supports and what
   it does not establish;
5. matured defensible claims while retaining public, held, and protected states;
6. connected the resulting evidence to current proof-coverage targets;
7. promoted only one claim whose source and editorial use were ready now.

The result is 12 intake items, 12 bounded sources, 17 observations, 10 claims,
and 6 research inquiries across the three required archive families.

## Public-safety boundary

This repository does not contain the underlying private plans, minutes,
proposal, legislative-review artifact, messages, participant records, legal
questions, live strategy, private source paths, or private contact information.
Private sources use opaque protected locator IDs and cannot render as public
citations.

## iCloud materialization limit

iCloud may expose a filename and logical size before its bytes are locally
available. At least one promising file behaved that way during this pass. The
research record therefore says "not materialized in this pass," never "empty"
or "did not exist." Future archive passes should retry deferred files and add
new records rather than overwrite this limitation.
