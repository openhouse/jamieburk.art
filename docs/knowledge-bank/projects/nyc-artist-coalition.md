# NYC Artist Coalition

This dossier collects public-safe sources, claims, inquiries, boundaries, and
future photo research concerning Jamie's coalition-building, civic systems,
public web infrastructure, and cultural-space advocacy.

The first lifecycle run focuses on founding-role attribution and 2017 Cabaret
Law advocacy. Stronger memories concerning the Office of Nightlife, public town
halls, Talks Not Raids, M.A.R.C.H. enforcement, and later policy outcomes remain
research inquiries until their exact source relationships are developed.

## Confirmed In This Run

- Contemporaneous reporting identified Jamie as a founding member of NYC Artist Coalition. NPR used the variant name "NYC Arts Coalition"; Gothamist corroborates the coalition affiliation and current name.
- Gothamist documented Jamie organizing fire-code study groups for DIY venues and publicly calling for full Cabaret Law repeal.
- NPR documented NYC Artist Coalition mobilizing with other organizations under Let NYC Dance.
- A recovered Greene Hill Food Co-op Q&A documents Jamie and Julia inviting readers to a September 28 town hall for dialogue with the new Office of Nightlife. It establishes the invitation, not a completed event or its effects.
- NPR and the Council record establish the 2017 Office of Nightlife institutional context, but not Jamie's exact causal contribution.
- The Council record establishes the October 31 vote context for Introduction 1652-A, but this run does not treat the press release as a complete final-enactment or advocacy-causality record.
