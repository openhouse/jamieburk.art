# NTER CHNG and America: Now and Here, 2011

Last reviewed: July 15, 2026

## Answer first

Jamie Burkart collaborated with Drew Bolton and Garrett Fuselier on NTER CHNG,
an interactive text-messaging installation that joined software and gallery
architecture to turn private one-to-one texting into a public group exchange.
The archived America: Now and Here Kansas City site lists all three among its
visual artists and publishes their project statement.

This is a collective credit. The recovered sources do not assign programming,
scenic design, motion graphics, fabrication, or production duties to an
individual collaborator.

## Claim state

### Known

- NTER CHNG was credited to Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier.
- The original site called it an interactive texting installation and placed it
  at the Arts Incubator Cocoon Gallery in Kansas City.
- America: Now and Here's official Kansas City site listed the trio as visual
  artists and published their NTER CHNG statement.
- The artists described a gallery system combining software and architectural
  installation to make texting into a public many-to-many exchange.

### Open

- The production and implementation responsibilities of each collaborator.
- The original opening date and the year associated with the archived project's
  "Open until Jan. 24th" line.
- The contents of the linked project press release, which was not recovered in
  this pass.
- NTER CHNG's exact America: Now and Here venue placement, installation dates,
  attendance, reception, and measured impact.
- Installation media suitable for publication after rights and participant
  review.

### Protected

- Participant messages and telephone numbers reproduced on the archived
  exhibition page.
- Unreviewed photographs, video, source code, correspondence, and production
  records.

## Public-use boundaries

Use the America: Now and Here artist page as the direct source for exhibition
inclusion. The Nerman Museum page documents the broader exhibition and a
separate Barbara Kruger presentation; it does not mention NTER CHNG and must not
be used to place NTER CHNG at the museum.

Do not say Jamie solely created, programmed, designed, fabricated, or produced
the installation. Do not infer individual roles from the statement's list of
the trio's combined professional backgrounds.

Do not copy participant messages or telephone numbers from the archived artist
page into the repository. Their presence is part of the source's form, not
permission for republication.
