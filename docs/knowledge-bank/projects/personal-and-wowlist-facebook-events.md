## Answer First

The authenticated personal Past Events surface exposed 511 event cards
representing 502 distinct base event IDs. Twenty cards explicitly identified
Jamie as organizer; 491 were merely associated with his profile. That
distinction is decisive: a profile-associated card does not establish that
Jamie attended, expressed interest, organized, supported, or endorsed the
event.

The 20 explicit organizer records span December 2006 through February 2017.
A public-safe selection makes a long-running event practice visible across
participatory art, waterways and mobility, small-space performance, communal
meals, media discussion, and civic gathering. This is useful evidence of
Jamie's sustained public-event organizing across different formats and civic
or cultural settings. It is not a complete production history, a participant
census, or proof of each event's outcome.

One authenticated rendering of WOW List's current Facebook page exposed no
event-detail anchors and no Events profile section. This is a dated interface
observation, not evidence that WOW List never created or circulated Facebook
events, nor proof that another session would render identically. The project's event
practice remains documented more strongly by the production database, public
account corpus, organizer-use sources, and project archive already in the
knowledge bank.

## Sunday Dinner Milestone

The March 9, 2014 detail page is titled **SUNDAY DINNER Turns 100!**, identifies
Jamie as host, and displays 21 responses. It is direct public evidence that the
project publicly marked a 100th-event milestone by that date.

The page title is a contemporaneous project assertion, not an independently
audited count of completed gatherings. It strengthens the chronology but does
not by itself prove the portfolio's separate 300+ gathering claim.

## Source Discovery

The explicit organizer event title led to a newly recovered independent source:
Eric Barton's November 12, 2007 *Pitch* article, ["Artists Turned Huck Finn,
Part III"](https://www.thepitchkc.com/artists-turned-huck-finn-part-iii/).
It names Jamie Burkart and Libby Hendon, reports that they spent weeks
constructing the recycled-material raft for a Kansas City-to-Gulf journey, and
says the group had traveled more than 1,000 miles before a Coast Guard
interruption.

That article adds a mid-journey checkpoint. It does not establish a complete
route, every participant, or the later Gulf terminus. The existing 2009 *Pitch*
follow-up remains the independent source for the crew reaching the Gulf after
four months.

No external article destination was exposed as an anchor on the 20 reviewed
organizer-detail pages. Absence from the current render is not proof that no
destination was ever posted.

## Public-Safety Boundary

The protected capture contains the complete card index and research detail.
The public repository excludes bulk titles, venues, cities, and organizer
names; ordinary-life and non-mission records; guest, invitee, attendee, friend,
commenter, and reactor identities; raw descriptions; comments; reactions;
private controls; and authenticated-session state.
