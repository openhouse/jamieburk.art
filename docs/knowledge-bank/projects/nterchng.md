# NTER CHNG

Status: developed in the knowledge bank; not selected for the current public
portfolio.

## Conclusion

Public archival evidence establishes NTER CHNG as a collaborative interactive
texting installation by Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier. The
official archived America: Now and Here Kansas City website later listed all
three collaborators as visual artists and presented NTER CHNG on their shared
artist page during the project's May 2011 Kansas City launch.

The strongest current account is about a participatory system and collective
practice, not solo authorship. NTER CHNG combined software, spatial
installation, participant cell phones, and both faces of a digital gallery
wall so private one-to-one messages could accumulate into a shared real-time
dialog.

## Source-Backed Claims

### CLM-NTERCHNG-COLLABORATIVE-INSTALLATION

The [archived NTER CHNG project
homepage](https://web.archive.org/web/20110128193350/http://nterchng.com/)
names Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier, calls the work an
interactive texting installation, and locates it at Arts Incubator Cocoon
Gallery in Kansas City. [The Pitch's January 7, 2010
listing](https://www.thepitchkc.com/ntr-chng/) independently describes the
software-and-architecture form, two-sided digital wall, cell-phone input, and
real-time participant dialog.

An official America: Now and Here artist statement adds that the collaborators
combined scenic design, computer programming, motion graphics, and
experiential production to create a social information space. That source is
retained as public metadata only: its archived page reproduces historical
personal phone numbers, so the URL and raw text are intentionally excluded from
the repository and citation layer.

Boundary: always credit Bolton and Fuselier with Jamie. The recovered sources
do not assign coding, wall design, motion graphics, installation, or production
responsibilities to an individual collaborator. They do not supply attendance,
message-volume, or audience-impact measures.

### CLM-NTERCHNG-AMERICA-NOW-HERE-EXHIBITION

The official archived [America: Now and Here Kansas City artist
index](https://web.archive.org/web/20110516144827/http://kansascity.americanowandhere.org/artists/)
lists Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier in its Visual Artists
section and links all three names to one shared artist page. The recovered
shared page identifies NTER CHNG as their work.

[KC Studio's contemporaneous exhibition
overview](https://kcstudio.org/america-now-and-here/) establishes the May 6-28,
2011 Kansas City launch, its national-and-local artist structure, and its
public-dialogue purpose. It also directs readers to the recovered official
Kansas City website.

Boundary: this was inclusion in a multidisciplinary group exhibition, not a
solo exhibition. The [Nerman Museum
page](https://www.nermanmuseum.org/exhibitions/2011-05-11-kruger-barbara-america-now-here.html)
documents the broader Kansas City project and a separate Barbara Kruger truck
stop on May 11-12. It does not place NTER CHNG at the Nerman Museum or establish
a direct collaboration between Jamie and Kruger.
