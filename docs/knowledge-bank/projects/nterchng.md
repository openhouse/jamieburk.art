# NTER CHNG

## Defensible project summary

`NTER CHNG` was an interactive texting installation collaboratively created by
Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier. A contemporaneous event
listing places its opening at the Arts Incubator's Cocoon Gallery in Kansas
City on January 8, 2010.

Recovered descriptions present the work as equal parts software application
and architectural installation. Visitors used their phones to send messages
through both faces of a digital wall, making the usually private one-to-one
exchange of texting into a public many-to-many gallery experience.

## America: Now and Here

The 2011 `America: Now and Here Kansas City` site provides direct institutional
evidence of the later exhibition relationship. Its visual-artist index lists
Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier, and its dedicated page for
the trio presents the `NTER CHNG` artist statement.

This supports:

> The official America: Now and Here Kansas City site listed Drew Bolton,
> Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier as visual artists and presented NTER
> CHNG as their collaborative work during the 2011 Kansas City launch.

It does not support:

- participation at every proposed national-tour stop;
- display of `NTER CHNG` at the Nerman Museum's Barbara Kruger truck stop;
- sole authorship by Jamie;
- individual division of labor; or
- a measured attendance or impact claim.

## Professional relevance

Held for future composition, the project is early evidence of Jamie's
collaborative practice at the intersection of software, physical space,
interaction design, and public participation. The source itself describes the
trio as combining scenic design, computer programming, motion graphics, and
experiential production; it does not assign those disciplines individually.

## Known / Open / Protected

**Known:** collaboration, installation form, 2010 Cocoon Gallery opening, 2011
Kansas City exhibition inclusion, and the official artist-statement framing.

**Open:** technical architecture, individual responsibilities, complete
installation record, participation totals, participant experience, and the
scope of any later presentation.

**Protected:** historical participant phone numbers printed on the archived
artist page are not reproduced. No message corpus or participant record is
present in the repository.
