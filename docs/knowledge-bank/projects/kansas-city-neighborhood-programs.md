## TiredOfTires

Jamie reports that, as members of Oak Park Neighborhood Association, he and
other neighborhood participants operated a free monthly tire-pickup program
coordinated with Kansas City. Jamie's reported contribution included identity
and handbill design, city coordination, monthly collection, transport to the
East Bottoms recycling center, unloading, and spreadsheet haul logging. He
reports that the service later
extended to Indian Mound and connected with cleanup communications from Oak
Park, Cleveland Avenue Unify to Beautify, and the Chestnut Street Resource
Center.

## Cleveland Avenue Unify to Beautify

Jamie reports co-founding Cleveland Avenue Unify to Beautify within the
Historic East Neighborhoods Coalition. Pastor Lee originated the central idea:
Cleveland Avenue connected multiple East Side neighborhood-association areas
and could give residents and city leaders a shared corridor through which to
understand a historically underserved part of Kansas City.

Jamie's reported contribution included identity and logo design, photography,
social media, listening-session maps, resident-reporting tools, and production
of hundreds of printed handbills. He recalls elected officials participating in
the public meetings and the process contributing context for corridor capital
improvement discussions.

Claim: `CLM-KC-CLEVELAND-UNIFY-TO-BEAUTIFY` - use with care.

Do not transfer Pastor Lee's concept authorship to Jamie. Do not claim Jamie
personally determined or secured public funding. Preserve HENC, neighborhood
associations, residents, clergy, elected officials, and other organizers as
collective actors.

## Shared Operating Pattern

The accounts describe Jamie functioning as a pro bono neighborhood design
studio and print shop: making handbills, maps, visual identities, photographs,
social accounts, meeting packets, and data-collection tools while also doing
the physical work of distribution, pickup, transport, and recordkeeping. This
is a promising professional throughline, but it remains a research hypothesis
until the underlying artifact set is recovered.
