# KC Spaces Fund

**Period:** April-July 2020

**Status:** Historical

**Portfolio surface:** Technical Operations candidate

## Project Context

KC Spaces Fund was a collaborator-led emergency mutual-aid campaign for
grassroots arts and culture spaces in the Kansas City region during the first
months of COVID-19. Public materials named Caitlin Horsmon, Jordan Carr, Kendell
Harbin, and Megan Pobywajlo as organizers and identified Allied Media Projects
as fiscal sponsor.

The public system joined a campaign site, GoFundMe, application flow, mailing
and contact routes, social identities, named support announcements, and a
mutual-aid print ecosystem. The GoFundMe currently displays $9,590 raised
against a $9,500 goal from 107 donations.

## Jamie's Contribution

The surviving implementation archive supports a bounded but substantial role:
Jamie implemented and maintained the Ghost site, campaign-theme changes,
fundraising-display widget, deployment configuration, and web action
affordances. He was not a named public organizer, and he separately states that
he was not the stakeholder or owner posting through the Facebook account. The
records do not make him fundraiser owner, grant decision-maker, fiscal sponsor,
or established author of the Page's posts.

Jamie remembers also supporting selection of a name available consistently
across campaign domains and social platforms. Public identity uniformity is
visible; the decision chronology and collaborator roles remain open research.
