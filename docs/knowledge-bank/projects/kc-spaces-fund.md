# KC Spaces Fund

KC Spaces Fund was a 2020 Kansas City mutual-aid effort for grassroots arts and
culture spaces during COVID-era closures. Public campaign materials named
Caitlin Horsmon, Jordan Carr, Kendell Harbin, and Megan Pobywajlo as organizers.

Jamie's documented contribution was behind-the-scenes digital infrastructure:
building and maintaining the Ghost-based campaign site, adapting a reusable
campaign theme, and implementing web affordances for donation, application,
sign-up, and fundraising display.
