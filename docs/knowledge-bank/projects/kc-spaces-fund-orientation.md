# KC Spaces Fund

KC Spaces Fund was a collaborator-led mutual-aid campaign for Kansas City arts
and cultural spaces during the first months of the COVID-19 pandemic. Jamie's
documented contribution is behind-the-scenes digital operations: campaign-site
implementation, theme and fundraising-display work, domain and platform
continuity, and support for a name available across web and social channels.

The [Facebook population report](kc-spaces-fund-facebook-posts.md) preserves the
public campaign record and its capture limits. Public organizer credit remains
with the people named by the campaign; technical support does not establish
grantmaking authority, authorship of every post, or public-organizer status.

## Hiring-facing projection - August 20, 2026

The public case study at `/work/kc-spaces-fund` uses the live campaign site's
finished visual surface to make Jamie's implementation craft immediately
legible. Its copy stays within the established evidence: Ghost web-stack
maintenance, reusable-theme customization, deployment, donation, application,
sign-up, and contact pathways, and a fundraising progress display.
The [portfolio projection](../projections/work-kc-spaces-fund.md) records the
exact public language, visual selection, and credit guardrail.

The homepage places this proof after NYC Artist Coalition and CallNYC. In that
sequence it answers a distinct hiring question: can Jamie turn urgent,
collaborative work into a polished public system? The visual selection does not
change the campaign's credit model or turn historical donation and application
language into a current service offer.
