# WOW List Facebook posts

Reviewed: 2026-07-15

## Public-safe conclusion

Facebook's surviving management record supports a strong, bounded role claim:
Jamie stewarded WOW List's Facebook publishing from 2015 through 2018. All 50
post details that still render display the admin-only attribution **Published by
Jamie Burkart**. Four additional posts remain visible only in Facebook's
Lifetime management table, producing a 54-record complete-as-materialized
inventory.

WOW List was a shared project. A Page-publisher byline does not make Jamie the
sole author of quoted or shared material, the sole owner of the product, or the
exclusive manager of every project platform. Those distinctions remain attached
wherever the role claim is projected.

## Population reconciliation

The authenticated WOW List Page was reviewed through **Professional Dashboard
> Content Library > Published > Lifetime**. The virtualized table was scrolled
until it reached a terminal no-growth state, and every materialized post ID was
then opened directly.

- 54 unique records materialized.
- The chronology runs from April 25, 2015 through March 22, 2018.
- 50 detail pages rendered.
- Four records remained table-only.
- All 50 rendered details displayed Jamie's Page-publisher byline.
- Every row has a stable post ID, recovery state, thematic disposition, public
  link inventory, and bounded metric snapshot.

This is 100% of the surviving records exposed by the authenticated Lifetime
table on July 15, 2026. It is not a Meta owner export and cannot establish that
deleted, removed, or otherwise unexposed posts never existed.

The [public-safe corpus](../corpora/wowlist-facebook-posts-full-population.json)
excludes full post text, comments, engager identities, private profile links,
and authenticated-session state. Protected captures remain outside the public
repository.

## Source inventory

The 54 records preserve 42 normalized public destinations after Facebook and
campaign tracking parameters are removed. They include WOW List city and event
surfaces, local DIY calendars, artist tours, cultural-space fundraising and
recovery pages, civic-action calendars, and published reporting.

One source was close-read in this pass: Patricia Calhoun's March 9, 2017
*Denver Westword* article, "City Partners With Meow Wolf on $20,000 Denver DIY
Spaces Fund." The article documents a Denver Arts & Venues contribution to a
Meow Wolf-administered fund for DIY and alternative spaces. WOW List circulated
the article on its publication date. That establishes source curation, not that
WOW List or Jamie created, administered, or caused the fund.

The other destinations remain in the research lifecycle. A posted URL proves
association and circulation, not authorship, endorsement, current availability,
audience use, or the truth of the destination.
