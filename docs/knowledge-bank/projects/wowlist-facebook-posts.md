## Answer First

The authenticated legacy Lifetime table exposed 54 WOW List Facebook post
records dated April 25, 2015, through March 22, 2018. The table reached the
same stable terminal population in forward and reverse review. This is 100
percent accounting for the records Meta materialized on the capture date, not
an assertion that deleted, removed, or otherwise unexposed history never
existed.

The reconciled public routes contain 51 renderable post details that identify
Jamie Burkart as Page publisher and three video redirects that expose no
publisher byline. That supports a bounded professional claim: as one of WOW
List's co-builders, Jamie stewarded its Facebook publishing across this
period. The byline does not make Jamie the author of shared sources or quoted
material, the sole owner of WOW List, or the exclusive manager of every
project platform. WOW List was co-built by Jamie and Richard Album.

## Native Owner Export

Meta's export interface limits custom post exports to one-year windows. Three
jobs spanning April 2015 through March 2018 displayed Completed status. The
browser client blocked two downloads; completed status is therefore recorded
without claiming file verification.

The recovered middle-window file contains 29 rows. Its post links exactly
match canonical records 4 through 32, in order. This independent native
segment confirms both the canonical identifiers and the need to preserve
legacy management IDs separately. The file reports 22 rows under the current
Page ID and seven under another Page ID, all with the Page name WOW List. The
bank preserves that split as an unresolved Meta identity or migration artifact,
not evidence of two projects. Twenty-seven export dates match the rendered
chronology directly; two late-evening timestamps resolve to the prior calendar
day. Those shifts are preserved as an interface/timezone boundary.

## Mission-Relevant Practice

joined product operations to field participation. Its overlapping themes are:

- civic mobilization;
- community governance and product feedback;
- community care and remembrance;

These labels overlap and cannot be summed into a unique post count. The
42-link inventory shows what the Page circulated, not who endorsed WOW List or
whether WOW List caused a linked campaign's result.

## Traction And Stakeholders

No public stakeholder-group engagement count is promoted. The captures do not
expose an identity-complete population of reactors or commenters. Source
authors, shared-post originators, mentions, and linked organizations are not
silently reclassified as engagers, partners, or endorsers.

Meta's legacy table and native export also use differently named and bounded
analytics. The private values are retained only for methodological comparison.
They are not published as accomplishment metrics. The Page's current
185-follower display is a dated July 15, 2026 snapshot, not historical reach.

## Public-Safety Boundary

The public ledger preserves identifiers, dates, public URLs, classifications,
source dispositions, counts, and integrity controls. It excludes raw post
text, comments, reactor and commenter identities, administrator analytics,
authenticated-session data, and private filesystem paths.
