# WOW List Facebook posts: full-population archival production

**Review date:** July 15, 2026  
**Project:** WOW List  
**Surface:** Facebook page posts  
**Projection decision:** Selective case-study projection; the corpus remains in the knowledge bank

## Answer first

The complete recovered WOW List Facebook population contains **57 unique posts**
dated April 25, 2015 through March 23, 2018. A protected owner-post capture
terminated after 19 pages without a repeated cursor, and every recovered record
received a public-safe census row and a live disposition.

Facebook's manager-only post interface displayed **Published by Jamie Burkart**
on **51 of the 57** recovered post identities. It displayed no other publisher.
Publisher attribution was not recovered for six records: three unavailable post
wrappers and three video redirects. This is strong evidence that Jamie managed
WOW List's Facebook publishing across the recovered corpus. It is not evidence
that he authored every attached source statement, published the six unresolved
records, controlled every social platform, or was the project's sole creator.
WOW List remains credited as Jamie and Richard Caceres's shared project.

The content record is also stronger than the generic phrase *social media
management*. The account functioned as an operating and editorial layer for a
participatory event system: contributor onboarding, event circulation, peer
learning, cultural-space support, mutual aid, safety information, and civic
advocacy all appear in the complete population.

> Jamie managed WOW List's Facebook publishing across the recovered 2015-2018
> corpus. Facebook's manager-only interface attributes 51 of 57 recovered post
> identities to him, attributes none to another publisher, and leaves six

Use that wording with these boundaries:

- preserve Richard Caceres's shared project credit;
- do not assign Jamie the words of attached or quoted source posts;
- do not assign the six unresolved records to Jamie;
- do not expand a Facebook finding into every social platform;
- do not convert publishing responsibility into sole project ownership.

## Engagement boundary

No stable full-population reaction, comment, share, reach, or audience-identity
dataset was recovered. Meta Business Suite labeled its **Lifetime** content
range as March 31, 2019 through July 14, 2026, after the recovered 2015-2018
corpus, and showed no activity in that later range. That zero-state is a
management-interface coverage gap. It does not mean the 57 earlier posts did
not exist.

A current follower number or an isolated interaction label would be a mutable
capture-time observation, not lifetime traction, unique reach, adoption,
endorsement, attendance, or impact. The knowledge bank deliberately makes no
Facebook engagement-total claim from this pass.

- public posted URLs except one held route;

- raw post bodies and attached-source text;
- comments, commenters, reactions, and ordinary individual source-account
  names;
- phone numbers, email addresses, and contact details;
- the held Google document route;
- account IDs, administration details, cookies, session state, and local paths;
- unsupported engagement, reach, adoption, endorsement, or outcome claims.
