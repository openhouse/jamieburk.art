# iCloud Teams archive deepening

**Date:** 2026-07-15
**Scope:** Selective close reading across `Jamie Projects History`, `CRS`, and
`job-hunt`
**Disposition:** Public-safe Knowledge Bank production; source artifacts and
private locators remain outside the repository

## Purpose

This pass revisited three working archives to connect existing portfolio claims
to stronger source relationships. It did not treat cloud custody, a polished
resume sentence, or a working-file title as proof. Each recovered relationship
was decomposed into:

- the source object;
- the exact proposition it can support;
- the proposition it cannot support;
- the resulting claim and its boundaries;
- the current projection decision.

The authenticated iCloud Drive interface confirmed that all three target
folders remained present. Close reading used materialized working copies where
available, because they allowed bounded inspection without publishing cloud
identifiers or private links. Differences between cloud and local listings were
treated as synchronization limits, not as evidence of absence.

## Results

### Commercial Rent Stabilization provenance redline

A protected tracked-change document updated in May 2026 identifies Jamie as its
preparer. It begins with New York City Council Intro 93 and makes successive
source layers visible through Fair Rent NYC recommendations, Small Business
Survival Act lineage, and Albany Senate Bill S8319 revisions.

This supports a concrete operating claim: Jamie prepared a legislative
provenance redline that made inheritance and revision paths inspectable across
city, campaign, and state policy texts.

It does not establish that Jamie authored the underlying legislation, produced
official legislative history, provided legal advice, or secured adoption,
endorsement, passage, or implementation. Reviewer labels identify source layers
and do not necessarily identify individual drafting authors. The working
artifact remains protected and receives no public link.

**Projection:** Active as bounded Fair Rent and Technical Operations language;
no public citation to the protected artifact.

## The role of `job-hunt`

The job-search archive remains a useful routing and synthesis layer. Its resume,
prep, and positioning materials identify consequential claims that deserve
stronger support, but they do not become independent proof merely because the
language is approved or polished. In this pass, the dossier led back to the AI
Evals certificate and the policy-provenance artifact; those source objects, not
the dossier, carry the new evidence relationships.
