# Teams Archive Production

Status: bounded archival-production pass completed July 14, 2026. Selected
claims were promoted to the Fair Rent NYC case study; deeper material remains
in the knowledge bank.

## Scope And Method

This pass close-read materialized working records from three cloud-backed
project corpora: `Jamie Projects History`, `CRS`, and `job-hunt`. It used each
corpus's overview or current working document as an anchor, followed specific
high-value source trails, and normalized only what was actually readable.

The public repository contains source metadata, atomic observations, bounded
claims, and research state. It does not contain raw transcripts, correspondence,
contact details, private strategy, legal-review context, vulnerable business
information, or local filesystem paths.

  now names the clear front door, recurring room, shared public line, stewarded

The claim remains a future feature candidate. The reviewed sources support
travel toward the Gulf, not arrival there. Endpoint corroboration is an open
research task.

### Public-Knowledge Production

The public revision history corroborates the workflow. Wikipedia publication

### Product Discovery

- `CLM-SOURCE-BACKED-MEMORY-DISCOVERY-PROCESS`: two private discovery

This supports a bounded discovery method, not market validation, purchase, or
public endorsement. The collaborator remains unnamed and the transcripts remain
