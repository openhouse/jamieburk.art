# Urbanhermit Public Record

## Public-safe baseline

Jamie's personal [@urbanhermit](https://x.com/urbanhermit) account preserves a
cross-project public record spanning 2008 through 2023. An authenticated July
2026 pass reconciled every one of the 434 records represented by the live
profile counter: 340 originals, 13 replies, and 81 native reposts from external
sources.

This is a complete review of the capture-date live profile population, not an
account-owner export or proof that no older record was deleted or absent before
capture.

## Recurring functions

Auditable overlapping rules recover recurring public signals across:

- community platforms and gatherings;
- civic participation and service;
- cultural-space advocacy;
- public history, place, and waterways;
- creative technology and media;
- neighborhood mutual aid.

The patterns describe what the source records discuss or circulate. They do
not measure work performed, professional priority, agreement, reach, or impact.
For external-source native reposts, authorship remains with the original source.

## Source and stakeholder production

The corpus inventories 321 distinct posted short URLs. Selected sources matured
into bounded evidence for WOW List's public origin, the Horse Lords `Truthers`
video, the 8th Street Tunnel program, NYC Artist Coalition advocacy statements,
and Tired of Tires participation. The complete link inventory is not a claim
that all destinations were resolved or close-read.

A year-bounded public-index search recovered 15 mission-relevant third-party
responses from nine accounts and two additional conversation-context records.
That is a bounded public sample, not a complete engagement census or an
endorsement count.

## Selected claims

The account functioned as a personal public working surface across multiple
project lineages. The source-maturation pass also produced defensible records
that:

- a public peer credited Jamie as a Music Hackathon co-organizer and WOW List
  maker;
- NPR and contemporaneous posts credit Jamie and M.C. Schmidt for a published
  Horse Lords video;
- KCUR documents Jamie's participatory 8th Street Tunnel route and screening;

These records stay distinct. A peer attribution is not independent proof of
every product responsibility; a quote is not sole ownership of a coalition
argument; and direct participation is not sole program operation.

All eight Urbanhermit-derived claims remain held from the current
job-application website. The site can draw on this depth later when a specific
audience, role, or editorial argument makes it useful.

## Boundaries

- Jamie did not author the 81 native repost source records.
- A posted source establishes circulation, not authorship, endorsement,
  readership, partnership, participation, or impact.
- Mission-signal counts overlap and are retrieval aids, not accomplishment
  metrics.
- Visible interactions are volatile interface events, not unique people,
  reach, conversion, attendance, or impact.
- The incoming search excludes likes, private activity, deleted or unindexed
  records, direct messages, and engagement that omits the handle.
- Shared projects retain collaborator, participant, artist, organization, and
  public-official credit stated in each claim's collective-work record.
- Raw post text, private analytics, authentication state, historical contact
  details, and irrelevant personal context do not belong in the public repo.
