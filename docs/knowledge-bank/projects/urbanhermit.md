# @urbanhermit personal public record

Reviewed: 2026-07-15

## Public-safe conclusion

Jamie's personal `@urbanhermit` account preserves a 2008-2023 cross-project
public record spanning community platforms and gatherings, civic participation,
cultural-space advocacy, public history and place, creative technology, and
neighborhood work.

The knowledge bank now contains a metadata-only inventory of every one of the
434 records counted by the live profile on July 15, 2026. The corpus is depth
for research and future composition; it is not a new public website page.

## Full-population method

Jamie's authenticated browser session was used to traverse both Posts and
Replies to the October 2008 beginning and through repeated no-growth passes.

- Posts yielded 421 primary records.
- Replies rendered 436 article cards.
- Two Replies cards were conversation parents authored by another account and
  were classified as context, not Jamie's profile records.
- The remaining 434-record union reconciles exactly to the profile counter.
- The union contains 340 account originals, 13 account replies, and 81
  external-source native reposts.

This is 100% of the live profile-counted population visible on the capture
date. It does not prove that no older post was deleted, withheld, or otherwise
absent before the capture. The separate account-owner archive remains an open
historical-completeness task.

The committed fixture is
`apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/fixtures/urbanhermit-full-population.json`.
It preserves status URLs, dates, source authorship, record types, tab
provenance, posted-link metadata, public handles and hashtags, dated visible
interaction counts, mission-signal evidence, and classification-input hashes.
It excludes raw post text, profile biography text, historical contact details,
cookies, session state, direct messages, private activity, and local paths.

## Claims promoted into the bank

### Horse Lords, "Truthers"

NPR directly credits M.C. Schmidt and Jamie Burkart as the co-creators of the
music video for Horse Lords' "Truthers." The article describes its rhythmic
sequence of glitchy shapes and letters and attributes to Horse Lords member
Andrew Bernstein an explanation of how simple materials, repetition, subtle
variation, color, and text mirror the band's music. Jamie's contemporaneous
post, Drew Daniel, and Julia Fredenburg independently align with the co-credit.

Defensible claim:

> Co-created the music video for Horse Lords' "Truthers" with M.C. Schmidt.
> NPR published the video and credited both collaborators; Horse Lords member
> Andrew Bernstein described how its visual materials and rhythms mirrored the
> band's music.

Boundaries:

- Always credit M.C. Schmidt.
- The sources do not establish the precise production split.
- NPR publication does not establish commission terms, reach, or impact.
- Do not embed or republish the video before rights and playability review.

Sources:

### 8th Street Tunnel participatory screening

KCUR reports that in 2006 Jamie led participants on a scavenger hunt through
downtown Kansas City and hosted a film screening inside the historic 8th Street
Tunnel. The program combined an artist's call for shared space, a popular film,
and an archival streetcar training film. KCUR also records Jamie's argument for
public access as a way to engage Kansas City's heritage and help young people
imagine the city.

Defensible claim:

> In 2006, led participants through downtown Kansas City on a scavenger hunt
> and hosted a film screening inside the historic 8th Street Tunnel, combining
> artist, popular, and archival media to connect hidden infrastructure with
> public history and imagination.

Boundaries:

- This is a participatory screening and public-history program, not tunnel
  restoration, ownership, or a permanent public opening.
- The source does not establish access terms, attendance, or measured impact.
- Future public treatment should foreground current authorization and safety.

Sources:

- [KCUR article](https://www.kcur.org/arts-life/2016-09-15/the-8th-street-tunnel-is-a-gateway-to-kansas-citys-history-but-you-probably-cant-get-in)
- [Julia Fredenburg's public response](https://x.com/juliafredenburg/status/775795144553398272)
