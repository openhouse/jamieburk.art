# Urbanhermit Public Record

## Public-safe baseline

Jamie's personal [@urbanhermit](https://x.com/urbanhermit) account preserves a
cross-project public record spanning 2008 through 2023. Three independent
authenticated passes recovered the same 434 canonical status IDs, accounting
for every record represented by the live profile counter on July 15, 2026:
353 account-authored records and 81 native reposts from external sources.

This is a complete review of the capture-date live profile population, not an
account-owner export or proof that no older record was deleted or absent before
capture.

## Recurring functions

Auditable overlapping rules recover public signals across:

- community platforms and gatherings;
- civic participation and service;
- cultural-space advocacy;
- public history, place, and waterways;
- creative technology and media; and
- neighborhood mutual aid.

The patterns describe what source records discuss or circulate. They do not
measure work performed, professional priority, agreement, reach, or impact. For
external-source native reposts, authorship remains with the original source.

records from nine accounts and two additional mission-relevant conversation

URL, or metrics for them. This is a bounded sample, not a complete engagement
census or endorsement count.

## Boundaries

- Jamie did not author the 81 external-source native reposts.
- A posted source establishes circulation, not authorship, endorsement,
  readership, partnership, participation, or impact.
- Mission-signal counts overlap and are retrieval aids, not accomplishment
  metrics.
- The 243 dated visible interaction units are held; they are not unique people,
  reach, conversion, attendance, or impact.
- Incoming search excludes likes, private activity, deleted or unindexed
  records, direct messages, and engagement that omits the handle.
- Shared projects retain collaborator, participant, artist, organization, and
  public-official credit stated in each claim.
- Raw post text, ordinary-life context, other people's unnecessary traces,
  profile biography, private analytics, and authentication state do not belong
  in the public repo.
