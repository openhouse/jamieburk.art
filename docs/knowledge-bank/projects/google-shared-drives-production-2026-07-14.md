# Google Shared Drives archival-production pass

**Date:** 2026-07-14
**Scope:** Selective review of Jamie's accessible Google Shared Drives
**Disposition:** Public-safe Knowledge Bank production; underlying Drive material remains protected

## Purpose

This pass treated Shared Drives as collaborative operating archives. The goal was
not to publish their contents or convert folder custody into credit. It was to
find durable evidence relationships, distinguish authored work from collective
records, develop bounded professional claims, and retain future research leads.

The connected account exposed 110 Shared Drives. The pass excluded clearly
personal, family, legal, and sensitive drives before content review; sampled 23
professional or project drives across civic, cultural, technical, residency,
public-data, and knowledge-systems work; and close-read 11 text-bearing records.
Eight became protected source records. Three additional coalition context
records were retained through one opaque held intake with a specific future
review condition because their authorship was uncertain, their collaborator
detail was unnecessary, or they duplicated safer evidence.

## Method

1. Enumerate Shared Drives and classify them by public professional relevance.
2. Inspect root and nested folder structures before choosing source artifacts.
3. Prefer onboarding guides, implementation notes, runbooks, plans, briefs, and
   specifications over raw correspondence or bulk media.
4. Inspect revision metadata when a claim depends on who maintained a record.
5. Treat a signed artifact as direct authorship evidence and a Drive
   last-modifier field only as maintenance attribution.
6. Separate project intent, operating work, adoption, and outcome.
7. Keep private IDs, URLs, paths, contact data, correspondence, pricing,
   stakeholder details, and raw files outside the public repository.

Folder listings can return at most 100 direct children and may be partial. Empty
or date-only roots were treated as retrieval or materialization conditions, not
evidence that additional material does not exist.

## Public-safety boundary

This repository contains only public-safe source metadata, bounded propositions,
opaque protected locators, and projection decisions. It does not contain:

- Shared Drive or file IDs and URLs;
- raw Drive exports or folder paths;
- collaborator contact information or membership lists;
- private correspondence, transcripts, pricing, or company context;
- legal-review, family, financial, or sensitive transition material;
- photographs, video, or audio;
- participant identities not already approved for the public portfolio.

The durable lesson is simple: collaborative archives can support public claims
without becoming public archives.
