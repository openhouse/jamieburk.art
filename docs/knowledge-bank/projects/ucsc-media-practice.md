# UCSC Media Practice

## Public-safe finding

The evidence supports a longer professional throughline without requiring the
portfolio to become an art-school retrospective. Jamie's early practice joined
participatory art, social software, media tools, collective interpretation,
and physical space. The current portfolio can name that origin once, then show
how the same concerns continue through civic interfaces, community platforms,
coalition systems, and source-backed knowledge work.

## Margaret Morse lens

The portfolio should recognize forms of knowledge demonstrated through
embodiment, performance, attentive tending, hospitality, media archaeology,
and participatory situations. These are not decorative precursors to the
"real" operational work. They explain why Jamie's structures attend to how
people inhabit systems, remember together, and carry work forward.

This lens does not require every project to become poetic. It requires one
clear threshold where the artistic, civic, technical, and social practices
remain visibly connected while the hiring proposition stays decisive.

## Warren Sack lens

The portfolio should make visible a recurring systems sequence: analyze social
relationships, model them, prototype an interaction, build an interface,
translate between digital and physical conditions, and learn from collective
use. The protected record describes early instances; current governed proofs
show related practice in WOW List, CallNYC, NYC Artist Coalition
infrastructure, and Source-Backed Team Memory.

This lens does not support a public claim that Jamie independently invented
structural equivalence, solely designed a collective course installation, or
deployed a student prototype as production software.
