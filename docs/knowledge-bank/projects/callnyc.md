## Current Public Projection

On January 30, 2016, the New York City Council held a 1-3 p.m.
constituent-services hackathon at Civic Hall. The Council described the
gathering as its first CouncilStat hackathon. After the fuller CouncilStat
dataset was released, Jamie developed CallNYC.org as an independent
public-facing interpretation of those records.

CallNYC is an archived independent prototype, not an official or current City
service.
