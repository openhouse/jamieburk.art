# Participation Infrastructure Lineage

**Reviewed:** July 16, 2026

## Answer First

The reviewed record documents a bounded line of practice across four projects:

1. Sunday Dinner developed a long-running rhythm of invitations, gatherings,
   attendance tracking, and continuity.
2. WOW List turned recurring community calendars into followable keyword
   communities with distribution and organizer workflows.
3. `popular.vote` and Call Script adapted that model to post-election civic
   events, representative contact, and public-input gathering.
4. A January 2017 cultural-agency event discussion solicited practical asks and
   later routed participants to a recurring NYC Artist Coalition meeting.

This is evidence of an operating model moving across contexts. It is not a
claim that one tool or one person alone caused the formation of NYC Artist
Coalition, later policy outcomes, or every event in the sequence.

action proposition: call representatives and make change.

- Call Script invited participants to brainstorm practical asks;

This sequence documents public-input gathering, peer learning, and a route from
one agency convening into recurring coalition participation. It does not prove
