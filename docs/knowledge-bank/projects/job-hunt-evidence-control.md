# Job-Hunt Evidence Control

The private job-hunt archive is an orientation and composition surface. It
helps identify role frames, candidate case studies, evidence locations,
privacy boundaries, and claims that need verification. It is not independent
proof of the accomplishments it summarizes.

That frame is useful for selecting and sequencing evidence; it does not by
itself validate market fit or project outcomes.

## Control rule

Resume drafts, job-hunt outlines, AI-assisted syntheses, archive folders, and
private conversations may generate research questions. They cannot mature an
accomplishment claim without an underlying source record, explicit evidence
relationship, boundaries, anti-claims, and a projection decision.

Private messages, transcripts, legal records, financial material, contact
data, and archive paths remain outside this repository.
