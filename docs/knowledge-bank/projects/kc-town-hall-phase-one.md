   backing data system. The packet reproduces the card, names Oak Park
   Neighborhood Association and New Horizon Missionary Baptist Church as

   Association's TiredOfTires workflow with the City and performed recurring

City records say the $490,539 Phase Two appropriation remained unused and was
reappropriated after KC Town Hall withdrew. The portfolio must never imply that

## Claim posture

$189,629 cold-shell phase in 2019, with the scope named. It does not support a
completed mixed-use buildout or a claim that City funds paid for Phase One.

### General-contractor role

`CLM-KCTH-PHASE-ONE-GENERAL-CONTRACTOR-ROLE` is selected with attribution. The
packet independently supports founder/project-manager status, the team, scope,

collaborators and should never imply that Jamie personally performed licensed

Do not publish names, emails, phone numbers, raw responses, or an unsupported
response count. Say that input shaped the proposal and influenced proposed
uses, not that every suggestion was adopted.
