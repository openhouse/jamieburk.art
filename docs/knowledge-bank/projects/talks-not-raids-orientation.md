# Talks Not Raids orientation

Talks Not Raids translated reports from cultural spaces into a campaign for
M.A.R.C.H. transparency and engagement before punitive inspection, while
retaining serious investigation and safety exceptions. Jamie's bounded record
includes coalition participation and retained repository-level campaign-site
implementation.

The review-locked [bounded public record](talks-not-raids.md) separates
coalition advocacy, Council enactment, Office of Nightlife implementation, and
later institutional reporting. Neither the campaign nor Jamie is assigned sole
causal responsibility for the law or the replacement of M.A.R.C.H.
