# Claudette's Theatre on Wheels

## Confirmed Collaboration

Michael Rees's project page documents Jamie and Michael collaborating on an
augmented-reality experience for Make Use Visible Munich. The work used short
video clips in an app-based portrait of Claudette. Jamie shares video-production
credit with Anne Dufy Burkart and Julia Fredenberg.

Source: `SRC-CLAUDETTE-MICHAEL-REES-AR`.

formulation.
