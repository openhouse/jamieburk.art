## What The Archive Makes Legible

The archive's shape supports a professional throughline already visible in
stronger public evidence: Jamie helped translate distributed cultural-space
experience into usable participation routes, identity systems, public
communications, testimony, recommendations, campaign surfaces, and operating
memory. It also shows why the work cannot be reduced to communications alone.
Research, facilitation, logistics, design, web implementation, public process,
and handoff repeatedly meet in the same project record.

That interpretation is suitable for application research. It is not a new
historical impact claim and should not replace source-specific evidence.

## Rights And Consent Queue

No underlying item is cleared by the census. Before any visual, document,
quote, screenshot, map, or recording is proposed for public use, record:

1. creator and rights holder;
2. people, venues, and private information depicted;
3. consent status and public-interest necessity;
4. collaborator attribution and collective context;
5. whether an approved public version already exists;
6. the exact claim the item would clarify;
7. what the item cannot establish; and
8. Jamie's separate projection decision.

The one inventoried font asset remains metadata-only unless its license and
redistribution rights are documented.

   asset proposal.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** the accessible population, folder closure, source classes,
  dispositions, aggregate mission breadth, and the strict public/private
  boundary.
- **Open:** most item-level authorship, production, outcome, rights, consent,
  and publication decisions.
- **Protected:** the exact manifest, filenames, source identifiers, document
  bodies, private notes, participant and contact data, strategy, media,
  credentials, and unapproved quotations.

The portfolio remains a selective projection. `Eligible but unselected` is a
successful disposition, not lost work.
