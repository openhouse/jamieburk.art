# Claudette's Theatre on Wheels

## Public-Safe Project Record

Claudette's Theatre on Wheels was presented as an augmented-reality work in
the #MakeUsVisible Munich program in October 2022. A live primary project page
by Michael Rees credits Jamie Burkart and Michael Rees with collaborating on
the augmented-reality experience. It separately credits Jamie Burkart, Anne
Dufy Burkart, and Julia Fredenburg with Claudette on the source video, filmed
in 2017.

An archived public event page corroborates the October 1-31, 2022,
presentation at Residenz Theatre, displays Michael Rees and Jamie Burkart as
artists, and describes short video clips activated by clicking tondos in the
app.

## Defensible Assertion

Jamie collaborated with Michael Rees to create the augmented-reality
experience for Claudette's Theatre on Wheels. The work incorporated video
produced by Jamie, Anne Dufy Burkart, and Julia Fredenburg with Claudette.
