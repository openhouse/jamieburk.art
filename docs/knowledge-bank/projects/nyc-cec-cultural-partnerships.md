# CEC cultural-institution partnerships

The [official partnership record](../sources/nyc-cec-cultural-partnerships-2026.md) connects CEC and [The People's Creative Institute](https://www.thepeoplescreative.org/) to:

- [BRIC](https://bricartsmedia.org/)
- [Bronx Museum of the Arts](https://bronxmuseum.org/)
- [El Museo del Barrio, as identified by CEC](https://www.nyc.gov/site/civicengagement/about/cultural-institutions-group.page)
- [Flushing Town Hall](https://www.flushingtownhall.org/)
- [Snug Harbor Cultural Center & Botanical Garden](https://snug-harbor.org/)

The May 19, 2026 newsletter dates these institutions' role as engagement hubs during that vote phase. The later-observed program page describes a year-long partnership without exact term dates.

This is an institutional bridge between arts and civic participation. It is not evidence of a partnership with NYC Artist Coalition, a personal connection, employment access, or a hiring recommendation.
