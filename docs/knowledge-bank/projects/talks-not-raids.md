# Talks Not Raids

Talks Not Raids was a coalition campaign seeking transparency, notice, and more
direct problem-solving around New York City's Multi-Agency Response to
Community Hotspots program. Its public website connected venue testimony,
reporting, a Council bill, a call script, sponsor tracking, coalition identity,
and campaign media.

## Jamie's Supported Role

The official February 2019 Council record identifies Jamie as an NYC Artist
Coalition member asking for Talks Not Raids, public MARCH information, and
direct work among the Office of Nightlife, cultural liaisons, and
establishments. Canonical records also support his contribution to the campaign
web system. Protected working records do not assign every calculation,
artifact, or line of copy to Jamie.

## Outcomes And Chronology

Intro 1156-A became Local Law 220 of 2019. In 2023 the City announced that an
engagement-first CURE process replaced MARCH. These are aligned institutional
outcomes in the campaign chronology, not proof that Jamie, NYC Artist Coalition,
or Talks Not Raids alone caused enactment or replacement. Replacement does not
establish that every form of related enforcement ended.

Campaign-era numerical claims remain held unless their denominators,
transformations, and source data are independently reproduced.
