# NYC Artist Coalition X Population Review

Date reviewed: 2026-07-14  
Account: [@NYCArtC](https://x.com/NYCArtC)  
Method: authenticated, read-only X review; profile, Replies, Posts, historical
search, and public-link reconciliation

## Editorial conclusion

This pass dispositions the complete 5,124-slot population reported by the
authenticated `@NYCArtC` profile on the review date. It is a complete accounting
ledger, not a claim that X exposed every historical item.

The review recovered 3,367 unique public items at item level: 715 statuses
authored by the coalition account and 2,652 reposts. The remaining 1,757
profile-count slots stay explicitly unresolved. No status ID, date, author,
content, relationship, or deletion reason is inferred for those slots.

That boundary matters. The account-level evidence is strong enough to show a
durable shared publishing system spanning the coalition's four named campaigns,
public-resource routing, civic participation, artist-labor advocacy, and
amplification of collaborators. It is not a platform export, deletion history,
complete impression report, or record of who operated the shared account for a
particular post.

## Public-safe ledgers

- [`nycartc-public-post-ledger.json`](../data/nycartc-public-post-ledger.json)
  stores one disposition for every observed profile-count slot. Recovered rows
  include public status metadata, outbound-link counts and digests,
  primary-theme labels, and a content digest, but not full post text or raw
  linked-artifact URLs.
- [`nycartc-public-engagement-ledger.json`](../data/nycartc-public-engagement-ledger.json)
  stores the bounded public mention-search disposition and stakeholder-group
  floors.

The ledgers omit authenticated session data, private analytics, mutable reaction
metrics, messages, credentials, follower identities, private correspondence,
raw post text, and raw outbound URLs. Four independently close-read public
sources are promoted in the source table above; unreviewed linked artifacts are
not published merely because they appeared in a post.

## Reuse rules

1. Say "5,124 of 5,124 profile-count slots dispositioned," not "all 5,124
   tweets recovered."
2. Keep the 3,367 recovered-item count and 1,757 unresolved-slot count together.
3. Treat 715 as recovered account-authored statuses and 2,652 as recovered
   reposts; do not assign either population to Jamie personally.
4. Treat hashtag counts, themes, stakeholder groups, and link counts as
   recovered floors shaped by the surviving platform record.
5. Do not convert reposts into inbound endorsement or current reaction metrics
   into historical impact.
6. Keep the dedicated 20-post, seven-Council-account review as the governing
   Council-engagement claim.
7. Use linked reporting for the propositions it actually establishes. A posted
   URL is a source lead until it is independently opened and reviewed.
8. Keep this population study as Knowledge Bank depth unless a future public
   composition genuinely benefits from a small, reader-centered projection.
