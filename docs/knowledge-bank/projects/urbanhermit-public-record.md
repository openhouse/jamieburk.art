# Urbanhermit Public Record

## Public-Safe Baseline

Jamie's personal [@urbanhermit](https://x.com/urbanhermit) account preserves a
cross-project public record spanning 2008 through 2023. An authenticated July
2026 pass reconciled every one of the 434 records represented by the live
profile counter: 340 originals, 13 replies, and 81 native reposts from external
sources.

This is a complete review of the capture-date live profile population, not an
account-owner export or proof that no older record was deleted or absent before
capture.

## Recurring Functions

Auditable overlapping rules recover recurring public signals across community
platforms and gatherings; civic participation and service; cultural-space
advocacy; public history, place, and waterways; creative technology and media;
and neighborhood mutual aid.

The patterns describe what records discuss or circulate. They do not measure
work performed, professional priority, agreement, reach, or impact. For native
reposts, authorship remains with the original source.

## Selected Defensible Claims

### Horse Lords, `Truthers`

> Co-created the music video for Horse Lords' `Truthers` with M.C. Schmidt.
> NPR published the video and credited both collaborators.

Always credit M.C. Schmidt. The sources do not allocate production roles,
establish commission terms or impact, or clear the video for republication.

Sources: [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2016/04/29/476020413/video-horse-lords-hypnotic-truthers-will-blast-your-noodle),
[Jamie](https://x.com/urbanhermit/status/726144972802691073),
[Drew Daniel](https://x.com/DDDrewDaniel/status/726096416070836224), and
[Julia Fredenburg](https://x.com/juliafredenburg/status/726238278433804288).

### 8th Street Tunnel

> In 2006, led participants through downtown Kansas City on a scavenger hunt
> and hosted a three-part film screening inside the historic 8th Street Tunnel.

This was a participatory screening, not tunnel restoration, ownership, or a
permanent public opening. Access terms, attendance, and measured impact are not
established.

Sources: [KCUR](https://www.kcur.org/arts-life/2016-09-15/the-8th-street-tunnel-is-a-gateway-to-kansas-citys-history-but-you-probably-cant-get-in)
and [Julia Fredenburg](https://x.com/juliafredenburg/status/775795144553398272).

### TiredOfTires Participation

> Directly participated in KC Town Hall's recurring TiredOfTires neighborhood
> workflow, including a documented dump-truck pickup shift around Northeast
> Kansas City.

This establishes direct participation, not sole ownership, complete program
coordination, every shift, or audited tire and savings totals.

Sources: [Jimmy Fitzner](https://x.com/JimmyFitzner/status/1510067983456026629)
and [KC Town Hall](https://x.com/KCTownHall/status/1135246124883861504).

## Editorial Disposition

The Horse Lords and 8th Street Tunnel records are strong future feature
candidates. The direct tire-pickup claim strengthens existing neighborhood
field-operations research. None is selected for the current job-application
website: composition should respond to audience and purpose, not archive size.

Raw post text and protected personal or authenticated-session context remain
outside the public repository. No `/proofs`, `/knowledge-bank`, `/urbanhermit`,
or other new public route is created by this archival pass.

See the [full-population receipt](../intake/2026-07-15-urbanhermit-x-full-population.md)
