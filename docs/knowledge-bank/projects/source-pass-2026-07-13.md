# Ten-source portfolio research pass

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-13

**Status:** Public-safe Knowledge Bank research record

**Projection:** Five source-backed claims strengthened public case studies;
three additional claims remain bank-only; two sources primarily corroborate
existing claims and define new research boundaries.

## Method

This pass began with the Greene Hill Food Co-op profile supplied by Jamie, then
followed its project and policy lineages across campaign archives, independent
reporting, official hearing records, municipal records, and project artifacts.
Each source received an intake record, a stable source ID, an explicit statement
of what it supports, and a boundary describing what it does not establish.

## Claim changes

### Strengthened public projections

- **Sunday Dinner:** added public corroboration for weekly shared hosting and an
  open-community invitation while retaining separate documentary support for
  the 300+ gathering and 20+ resident-artist counts.
- **FairRentNYC and NYC Artist Coalition:** made Jamie's public invitation,
  town-hall speaking role, and 2018 commercial-rent testimony visible while
  preserving collective credit for campaigns, legislation, and institutional
  outcomes.
- **KC Town Hall:** replaced a success-only funding description with the complete
  public record: unanimous recommendation, appropriation, later withdrawal, and
  reappropriation, without speculating about cause.

### New bank depth

- 8th Street Tunnel public-history programming;
- Claudette augmented-reality and source-video collaboration;
- NYC Artist Coalition commercial-rent testimony as a documented bridge between
  cultural-space safety and the later commercial-rent body of work.

### Research still open

- Jamie's complete production responsibilities for the Office of Nightlife town
  hall;
- the raft's exact Gulf landing place and complete participant responsibility
  map;
- the Claudette technical stack, exhibition date, and asset rights;
- the reason for KC Town Hall's withdrawal, which should not be inferred from
  the municipal record.

## Editorial result

The public site now says more where the sources support more and says less where
the sources stop. The new material is not treated as a demand to display every
claim. It expands the factual palette available to future portfolio, resume,
application, and photo-editor passes while keeping the current site composed for
reader clarity.
