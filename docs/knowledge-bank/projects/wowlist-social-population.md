## Operating model

The population documents a product-operations loop rather than a single launch
announcement:

1. **Shared provenance.** The first WOW List-authored post credits Richard and
   Jamie and says the project grew from calendars made at Sunday Dinner.
2. **Followed discovery.** April 2015 support replies describe a home calendar
   assembled from followed local lists and a planned local/everywhere mode.
3. **Profiles and publishing.** The same thread explains user profiles, adding
   an event, and adding one event to multiple WOW Lists.
4. **Onboarding and distribution.** A 2015 independent tutorial and a 2016
   support thread document joining, adding shows, and receiving a weekly email.
5. **Scene-to-scene routing.** Posts distribute events in Kansas City, Chicago,
   New York, San Francisco, and a four-state tour, alongside a Detroit movement
   convening.
6. **Care and mobilization.** The account also routes demonstrations, marches,
   mutual-aid fundraisers, vigils, and support for DIY cultural spaces.

The social record corroborates historical organizer workflows. It does not
establish every product feature, the aggregate user/event counts documented by
separate archives, or current product availability.

## Portfolio decision

The public case study receives one concise projection: shared Sunday Dinner
provenance, historical organizer workflows, and bounded independent adoption.
The 38-row census, all 35 links, engagement ledger, civic/mutual-aid routing,
and source-role detail remain in the knowledge bank as recomposable depth.

That choice follows Chad's lens: lead with the work an employer can use now.
WOW List shows Jamie translating community vocabulary and organizer behavior
into a maintainable product, supporting real users, and operating a distributed
public identity across scenes. The deeper archive remains available for future
résumé, case-study, interview, and photo-editor compositions.

## Anti-claims

Do not claim that:

- Jamie alone created WOW List or authored every account post;
- the 38-profile-object census is a complete product or interaction archive;
- the product is currently operating;
- eight externally authored linking posts are the complete adoption census;
- engagement on external posts reposted by WOW List belongs to WOW List;
- mission-context articles are coverage of WOW List; or
- social evidence establishes the separate 1,800+ user, 16,000+ event, or
  roughly 35-city-ecosystem scale claims.
