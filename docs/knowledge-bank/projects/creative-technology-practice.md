# Creative Technology Practice

**Editorial status:** Reserve

**Period documented:** 2006-2016

This project record preserves independently documented creative-technology
work without displacing the portfolio's current technical project management,
product operations, and implementation argument.

## 2006: Time Is Long

[Cool Hunting](https://coolhunting.com/culture/bap-lab-part-1/) identifies
Jamie with *Time is Long*, an installation that records viewers and returns
their image approximately 20 minutes later through an extended VHS process.

**Supported:** artist attribution, viewer participation, and the time-delay
mechanism.

**Boundary:** the article does not provide complete engineering, fabrication,
exhibition, audience, or media-rights records.

## 2010-2011: NTER CHNG

[The Pitch](https://www.thepitchkc.com/ntr-chng/) describes NTER CHNG as equal
parts software application and architectural installation, with audience text
messages appearing on a digital wall in real time. The public
[project video](https://vimeo.com/21395655) credits Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart,
and Garrett Fuselier with the interactive texting installation and separately
credits construction and performance collaborators.

**Supported:** collaborative maker credit, the software-and-architecture form,
and real-time text participation.

**Boundary:** the public records do not establish the detailed division of
software, design, construction, or production labor. This is not a solo-credit
claim.

## 2013: A Sorted Audio File

[Monthly Music Hackathon](https://monthlymusichackathon.org/post/44177616179/sortedaudio)
says Jamie made a Max/MSP program that divided audio into small segments and
sorted them according to a selected clip feature, demonstrated by ordering
song fragments by pitch.

**Supported:** maker attribution, Max/MSP implementation, and the documented
segment-and-sort behavior.

**Boundary:** the source documents a hack and demonstration, not production
deployment, commercial adoption, complete code history, or impact.

## 2016: Truthers

[NPR Music](https://www.npr.org/2016/04/29/476020413/video-horse-lords-hypnotic-truthers-will-blast-your-noodle)
credits Jamie Burkart and M.C. Schmidt with the official Horse Lords video for
*Truthers*.

**Supported:** joint maker credit and official music-video context.

**Boundary:** preserve M.C. Schmidt's shared credit. The source does not grant
republication rights or establish audience and commercial impact.

## Bounded Throughline

Independent public sources document Jamie's creative-technology work at four
intervals across a decade: time-delayed video, real-time text interaction,
Max/MSP audio transformation, and collaborative music visuals.

This is not proof of uninterrupted full-time practice. Each project retains
its own collaborators, context, authorship, and rights. The knowledge bank
holds the throughline in reserve until a future audience need makes it more
useful than the site's current composition.
