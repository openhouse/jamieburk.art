# NYC Artist Coalition Facebook Post Archival Production

Date: 2026-07-15

Projection decision: **Knowledge bank only. No website page was changed.**

## Question

What does the complete surviving NYC Artist Coalition Facebook Page-post
population establish about public operating infrastructure, campaign
continuity, source routing, stakeholder addressing, visible response, and
Jamie's role without converting a shared Page identity into individual
authorship or social activity into impact?

## Population Control

Two authenticated Page traversals used distinct scroll cadences across 824
combined scroll operations. They recovered the same **444 distinct surviving
Page-post identities**, spanning January 29, 2017, through September 15, 2021.
The pass was accepted only after the exact identity sets matched and 42 and 41
additional terminal scrolls produced no new records.

Every recovered record received a public-safe disposition. The public ledger
retains only an anonymous stable ID, sequence, form, theme and stakeholder
classifications, classification counts, outbound-URL count, and a visible-
interaction boolean. Unlinkable value-frequency tables make the aggregate
interaction floors reproducible without tying a count to a post. Raw text,
comments, names, the full post-URL set, per-record metrics, media, relationship
data, account state, authentication material, and sensitive or stale action
routes remain outside the repository.

The public disposition digest regenerates exactly as the SHA-256 of the 444
sorted public record IDs joined by line feeds. A separate protected digest
controls the exact-match browser population without exposing source identities.

This is 100 percent disposition coverage of the surviving July 2026 Page
surface. It is not a native Meta export, deletion history, lifetime total, or
proof that no historical post is missing.

| 2020 | 69 | January 9 through December 23 |
| 2021 | 5 | January 13 through September 15 |
| **Total** | **185** | **February 2019 through September 2021** |

The structured CSV audit found no duplicate nonblank post IDs. The exports
include post-type, share-status, reach, reaction, comment, share, and click
fields. They do not cover 2017 or 2018, do not include the public event-route
population in an equivalent form, and do not expose a historical human-level
publisher field. They are a crosscheck, not the 2017-2021 denominator.

The post-level Reach values in those files must not be added and called unique
reach because audiences may overlap and the export does not define that sum as
a unique-person measure.

normalized public-safe routes. They include coalition campaign sites, Council

conversion, formal partnership, endorsement, or impact.

## Visible Response Boundary

On July 14, 2026, 389 of 444 records retained at least one visible reaction,
comment, or share. The currently visible counters sum to floors of:

- 2,374 reactions;
- 212 comments; and
- 611 shares.

These are mutable interface observations. They may include attention to
reshared source material and do not identify unique people. They are not
historical analytics, reach, attendance, conversion, stakeholder endorsement,
causality, policy influence, or impact.

## Jamie's Role

Jamie's first-person account is accessioned precisely:

> Jamie remembers being the predominant person using the NYC Artist Coalition
> Facebook Page while also remembering that other coalition participants used
> it.

That memory is important and remains research-stage. The current interface and
the 2019-2021 manager exports do not expose human publishers for historical
posts. A native Meta administrator chronology, record-level publisher export,
or collaborator proof notes are needed before quantifying or promoting the
Page-publishing claim.

This pass does not use account access, Page identity, tone, chronology, or
Jamie's known campaign role to manufacture individual authorship. Existing
public sources about Jamie's coalition systems and advocacy work remain in the
bank with their own evidence and collective-credit boundaries.

## Anti-Claims

- Do not call this a native Meta export or deletion history.
- Do not say NYC Artist Coalition published only 444 Facebook posts.
- Do not say Jamie authored or published all 444 records.
- Do not infer a human publisher from Page-level action controls.
- Do not say Jamie was the sole Page administrator or sole author of the
  coalition's public voice.
- Do not transfer credit from Olympia Kazi, collaborators, event hosts,
  sources, campaigns, Council members, agencies, or cultural spaces.
- Do not convert tags, mentions, links, quotations, or reshares into verified
  stakeholder engagement or endorsement.
- Do not convert current counters or summed export fields into unique reach,
  adoption, attendance, conversion, policy influence, causality, or impact.

## Lifecycle Decision

- The population, operating-surface, route, stakeholder, and response-floor
  claims are corroborated reserve knowledge.
- Jamie's memory of predominant but shared Page use remains research-stage,
  with no public projection.
- No website copy or route changes in this pass.
- A native Meta export and collaborator proof notes remain open research routes
  for deleted-post accounting, administrator chronology, and division of
  publishing labor.
