# NYC Artist Coalition Facebook post population

Reviewed: July 15, 2026

## Answer first

An authenticated owner-data pass accounted for **all 444 rows** in annual Meta
Business Suite Published exports across the NYC Artist Coalition Page's
surviving chronology: **January 29, 2017 through September 15, 2021**. A
separate terminal-scroll pass of the Page feed reconciled to the same
denominator after duplicate and embedded render fragments were removed. The record
documents a sustained coalition communications surface connecting cultural-space
survival, Cabaret Law repeal, M.A.R.C.H. accountability, nightlife governance,
commercial-rent advocacy, relief work, artists, agencies, elected officials, and
published media.

This is 100% accounting for the annual Published-export population available on
July 15, 2026, not proof of every post ever created. Deleted, hidden, private,
unpublished, or no-longer-retained posts may remain outside both the export and
feed surfaces. The shared Page identity also does not identify the historical
human author of each post.

## Population and method

Meta Business Suite exports were generated for every year represented by the
surviving Page chronology, using **Published**, **Lifetime** data view, **Post**
content level, and the **Creation date** filter. The five exports contain 444
rows and 444 unique post IDs: 185 in 2017, 74 in 2018, 111 in 2019, 69 in 2020,
and five in 2021. The raw CSVs contain post bodies, permalinks, and platform
identities and therefore remain protected outside the public repository.

Repeated authenticated lazy scrolling also reached the bottom of the live Page feed.
The protected population capture held at a 298,573-pixel document height for
seven terminal checks and produced no new records. A separate authenticated
endpoint recheck again preserved the January 2017 Cultural Affairs
recommendation post through seven checks with no loading boundary; transient
interface layout varied within a 128-pixel range, so endpoint identity, not
height alone, remains the decisive stop signal. The owner exports controlled the
denominator while private platform identities removed 154 duplicate or embedded
render variants among 598 encountered feed rows, producing 444 distinct posts.
Those private identities, authenticated URLs, and raw bodies are excluded from
the public repository. Each public row instead
carries a unique one-way reconciliation hash derived from its private platform
identity. All 444 hashes are unique. They make row distinction auditable without
disclosing the underlying identity; content hashes may repeat when captured
bodies or source cards repeat and are not used as the row identity.

## Native metric snapshot

The annual owner exports provide current row-level metrics for all 444 exported
posts. They contain 2,589 reactions, 295 comments, and 552 shares, for 3,436
reaction, comment, and share actions. They also contain a 48,044 sum of
post-level reach and 2,190 total clicks. Three hundred seventy-five rows show at
least one reaction, comment, or share; 364 show nonzero reach.

These are capture-date platform values. The 48,044 figure is a sum across posts,
not 48,044 unique people. None of the values identifies the stakeholder groups
behind an action, and none is attendance, conversion, endorsement, mandate, or
policy impact. A separate feed interface displayed 2,289 reactions and 212
comments but did not expose share labels consistently; the two metric surfaces
are preserved separately and are not combined.

## Authorship and stewardship

Jamie remembers that he predominantly used the Page while believing other
collaborators also used it. That participant memory is preserved as a research
lead, not promoted as settled fact. Current authenticated management access
establishes current custody only. Collaborator testimony or native publishing
records are needed to distinguish account creation, identity design, publishing,
moderation, campaign work, and period-specific stewardship.

The defensible public interpretation remains collective: the corpus documents a
durable NYC Artist Coalition communications and civic-relay system. It does not
support saying Jamie authored every post.

## Publication decision

The three bounded claims remain knowledge-bank depth. No website copy changes
are warranted in this pass: the public portfolio already carries stronger,
independently sourced evidence of Jamie's coalition role, while a shared Page
cannot assign post-level authorship. The 444-post census and 67-route inventory
remain available for future composition without forcing every archival finding
onto the current site.

Individual post bodies, comment or reaction identities, follower data,
authenticated session data, and sensitive exact routes remain out of the public
site and repository. The full public-safe row ledger lives in
[the population corpus](../corpora/nycartc-facebook-posts-full-population.json).
