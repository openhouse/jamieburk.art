# Office of Nightlife Town Halls

NYC Artist Coalition's nightlife convenings created a public setting in which
artists and cultural-space operators could state priorities to the officials
shaping New York City's new Office of Nightlife. The October 11, 2017 Market
Hotel town hall brought grassroots cultural voices into direct conversation
with city and cultural-affairs officials around safety, support, affordability,
and displacement.

## Jamie's role

Jamie helped establish and produce the coalition's recurring participation
system and appeared as an NYC Artist Coalition speaker in contemporaneous
reporting on the Market Hotel town hall. His wider contribution included the
coalition identity, event and campaign web infrastructure, public testimony,
and documentation systems that helped concerns move from gathering to public
record.

## What became usable

- a public occasion centered on small, diverse cultural spaces;
- an agenda connecting criminalization, regulatory support, and affordability;
- direct exchange among cultural participants, commissioners, and a Council
  sponsor;
- a reusable participation pattern extending beyond one event page.

The City created the Office of Nightlife through legislation sponsored by
Council Member Rafael Espinal and signed in 2017. The record shows coalition
participation and institutional use; it does not establish Jamie, the town hall,
or NYC Artist Coalition as the sole cause of the office.

## Present-grounded source return

The July 19, 2026 encounter reread the coalition's original town-hall page and
the Mayor's Office of Media and Entertainment record establishing the office.
The original page preserves the agenda, participants, venue, and public-input
purpose; the government record preserves sponsorship, institutional placement,
and official duties. Read together, they clarify contribution without
collapsing public participation into legislative authorship.
