# Office of Nightlife town halls

NYC Artist Coalition used recurring events and public forums to help artists,
venue workers, organizers, elected officials, commissioners, and agency staff
hear one another inside small cultural spaces.

The event form mattered. A meeting hosted where culture was actually made
could bring institutional attention into contact with lived conditions that
were otherwise fragmented across complaints, inspections, permits, leases,
and private conversations.

Protected production records support Jamie and Olympia Kazi opening one major
program inside a wider shared production plan. Other records support Jamie's
facilitation, digital identity, documentation, photo-animation, and follow-up
contributions across the broader event practice. These records do not make him
the sole producer or author.

The Office of Nightlife was created by City law and government action. Coalition
events can be described as a channel of public knowledge, participation, and
accountability, not as sole cause or institutional ownership.
