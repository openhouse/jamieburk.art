# KC Spaces Fund Facebook post population

Reviewed: July 15, 2026

## Answer first

An authenticated pass accounted for **all 38 distinct records exposed by the
KC Spaces Fund Facebook Page's 2020 filter** on July 15, 2026. The surviving
chronology runs from **April 7 through July 9, 2020** and documents a public
operating surface for emergency-relief applications, fundraising, volunteer
participation, grantee and recipient context, and local-media circulation.

This is 100 percent accounting for the surviving public Page surface that
Facebook exposed on the review date. It is not a Meta owner export, proof of
every post ever created, or deleted-post recovery. Deleted, hidden, private,
unpublished, or no-longer-retained records may remain outside the result.

## Population and method

The authenticated Page was filtered to 2020 and scrolled repeatedly to its
terminal record. The traversal stopped only after four consecutive checks held
at the same terminal state, with no visible loading boundary and no new record.
Protected rendered identifiers distinguished the records; the public
[38-row corpus](../corpora/kcspacesfund-facebook-posts-full-population.json)
retains only one-way reconciliation hashes.

## Source routing

The population contains nine distinct posted external routes:

- the KC Spaces Fund campaign home, application, and volunteer routes;
- the public GoFundMe campaign;
- two Oddities Prints fundraiser routes;
- the Farewell Transmission Facebook Page;
- a Trans Women of Color Collective donation route; and
- Do816's *The Daily DoGood: Kansas City*.

The Page also belongs in a broader public-source record. The official campaign
site describes rolling April-June 2020 emergency-relief applications, grants up
to $500, priority and eligibility criteria, Allied Media Projects as fiscal
sponsor, and public organizer credit. A surviving Oddities Prints page states
that proceeds from its fundraiser series partially benefited KC Spaces Fund and
KC Tenants. Search-indexed Kansas City Star text independently listed the fund
and campaign site among ways to support local artists and artisans at launch.

The Do816 and Kansas City Star article bodies were not directly retrievable
during this pass. Their recovered title, route, and indexed context are stored
with that limitation; no unrecovered article-body claim is made.

A posted route establishes public circulation through the Page. It does not
transfer authorship, prove endorsement by the linked organization, establish a
formal partnership, or show that a post caused a donation, application, grant,
or other result.

## Traction and stakeholder boundary

Twenty-eight records displayed at least one reaction on July 15, 2026, for 119
displayed reactions across the population. The largest value on one record was
15. No visible comment or share count labels were treated as zero-valued
engagement.

These are capture-date, post-level interface values. They are not 119 unique
people, historical reach, donors, applicants, attendees, endorsements,
conversions, or impact. The Page displayed 108 followers on the review date;
that current profile snapshot is not evidence of its 2020 audience size.

The Page references grantees, applicants, donors, volunteers, and media, but
those are outgoing references and invitations. Reaction and commenter
identities were not recovered. Incoming engagement by key stakeholder groups
therefore remains unmeasured.

## Authorship, ownership, and credit

The Page identity does not identify the historical human publisher of each
post. Jamie states that he was not the stakeholder or owner posting to this
account. The official campaign site names Caitlin Horsmon, Jordan Carr, Kendell
Harbin, and Megan Pobywajlo as organizers; that collective and organizer credit
remains intact.

Jamie's separately documented contribution remains behind-the-scenes digital
infrastructure: the campaign web stack, theme and fundraising-widget work, and
public donation, application, sign-up, and contact affordances. His memory that
he also supported selection of a name available across domains and social
platforms is preserved as a research lead. It requires dated naming records or
collaborator corroboration before it becomes a public accomplishment claim.

## Publication decision

This pass strengthens the knowledge bank and the existing archive-note claim.
It does not change website copy. The current public portfolio already gives
Jamie the strongest role this evidence supports: behind-the-scenes digital
infrastructure. The Facebook population enriches the collective campaign's
operating history but does not support adding Page ownership, social publishing,
organizer, grant-selection, or fundraising-control language to Jamie's case
study.
