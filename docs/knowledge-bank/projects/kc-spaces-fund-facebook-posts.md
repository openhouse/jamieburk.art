# KC Spaces Fund Facebook post population

Reviewed: July 16, 2026

## Answer first

An authenticated, repeated terminal-scroll pass recovered every record
Facebook exposed through the KC Spaces Fund Page feed on the capture date:
**40 surviving public records with visible endpoints from April 7 through July
9, 2020**. The record shows a compact mutual-aid communications system:
application and fundraising routes, resource amplification, and 11 public
funded-space spotlights.

This finding is narrower than a lifetime archive. The feed is not a native Meta
owner export, and non-media remnants sometimes lack durable public permalinks,
full messages, or individual dates. The Page identity also does not identify
the human publisher of each record.

## Population control

Three complete authenticated traversals recovered 40, 38, and 40 records. The
middle traversal moved faster and missed two non-media remnants; the final
slower traversal reproduced 40 and then held for 24 no-addition passes. The same
21 public media IDs appeared across all three traversals.

Two later verification traversals recovered 38 and 39 records after 24
no-addition passes apiece while reproducing the same 21 media IDs. This
documents Facebook's unstable progressive rendering. The canonical 40-row
union remains the population reproduced by the earlier terminal traversals.

A fresh independent pass on July 16 captured 61 overlapping scroll states,
again recovered all 40 distinct records, and held for eight stable endpoint
checks. Its raw authenticated capture and one-way digest remain protected
outside the public repository.

## Displayed interaction signals

Twenty-eight records retain at least one visible reaction signal, with a
capture-date aggregate floor of 119. Four records retain visible public comment
relationships, including three classified only as cultural-space accounts.
Personal identities and comment text are not published.

The three public account-level relationships are mission-relevant cultural-
space signals. They are not an identity-complete engagement census. No incoming
engagement by elected officials or other named stakeholder classes was
established, and outgoing tags, routes, and spotlights remain distinct from
incoming reactions, comments, or shares.

These volatile labels are not unique people, reach, impressions, attendance,
conversion, endorsement, mandate, causality, or impact. The current profile's
108 followers and one following are likewise capture-date labels, not historical
audience measures.

## Jamie's bounded contribution

Protected code and project records support Jamie's implementation of the
Ghost-based campaign site, reusable campaign theme, fundraising widget, public
action affordances, deployment, and continuity. Jamie also recalls supporting
the choice of a project name available across domain and social channels.

The public Page, `kcspacesfund.com`, and the GoFundMe route use that identity
consistently. They corroborate the result, not individual naming authorship.
Jamie explicitly states that he was not the stakeholder or owner posting on the
Facebook Page. Collaborator confirmation remains appropriate for the exact
naming and account-establishment process.

The authenticated Meta Business Suite asset selector did not expose KC Spaces
Fund among Jamie's current Page assets. That explains why no owner export was
available in this pass; it does not establish historical access, stewardship,
or authorship.

The strongest defensible public formulation is:

> For KC Spaces Fund, Jamie built campaign web infrastructure and supported an
> available cross-channel identity. Collaborator-led channels used it to route
> applications, donations, resources, and funded-space updates.

This must not become a claim that Jamie organized the campaign, authored the
Page, alone named the project, ran the fundraiser, made grant decisions, or
served as fiscal sponsor.

## Publication decision

The technical-operations page already selected KC Spaces Fund as a bounded
implementation proof. This pass strengthens that proof with cross-channel
identity support and the explicit non-publishing boundary. Population counts,
mutable interaction signals, and source-research findings remain archival depth.

Raw bodies, comments, personal social-graph identities, private analytics,
authenticated state, Page-management records, contact details, applicant or
grantee files, donor data, payment records, and private project materials stay
out of the public repository. The public-safe row ledger lives in [the
population corpus](../corpora/kcspacesfund-facebook-posts-full-population.json).
