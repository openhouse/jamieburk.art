# Water Publics

This dossier connects the raft expedition, Great Accommodations, public
programs, correspondence, installation work, and other participatory projects
through which Jamie invited city residents to encounter the waterways linking
their communities.

The bank distinguishes contemporaneous reporting, Jamie's attributed
recollections, institutional program descriptions, and future archival or
visual discoveries.

## Confirmed In This Run

- The Pitch credited Jamie with originating the 2007 experiential raft project from an investigation of Kansas City transportation history and the Missouri River.
- The Kansas City Star independently credited the project idea to Jamie while naming Libby Hendon and Laura Mattingly as the other two crew members at the reported stage of the journey.
- The Star's November 15, 2007 front-page report documented the crew south of Baton Rouge after passing the 1,000-mile marker; a November 12 Pitch update independently corroborates travel beyond 1,000 miles and the Vicksburg interruption.
- The Star described the roughly 12-by-13-foot craft as powered by two bicycles linked to a paddlewheel and assembled in three weeks from discarded building remnants, civic refuse, and reused syrup drums. The report does not allocate every design, fabrication, repair, or operating task among the crew and supporters.
- The Star quoted Jamie describing the river as an overlooked Kansas City civic space and imagining a cultural connection between the West Bottoms and Delta towns. This is an attributed project premise, not a measured community outcome.
- Charlotte Street presented Jamie as spearheading Great Accommodations and described its outreach, collected river stories, planned interactive installation and public programs, and Jamie's planned facilitator role.
- Charlotte Street preserved Jamie's attributed account of traveling by bicycle-powered raft from Kansas City down the Missouri and Mississippi for four months until the water tasted salty.

The contemporaneous November reporting is a mid-voyage record: it does not
establish later Gulf completion, an uninterrupted route, a complete participant
or labor roster, or sole authorship by Jamie. The later Pitch follow-up supports
collective Gulf completion and a four-month duration. The Charlotte Street
account retains attribution for route and duration detail. The program page
describes planned exhibition activity, so the bank does not treat every planned
element as a completed outcome.

## Projection Decision

These claims are mature and public-safe but remain editorially unused. They are
available when a future portfolio argument needs Jamie's long lineage of
participatory systems, public engagement, artifact-centered implementation, or
water-based civic imagination. The Star article materially strengthens a future
case study, but does not by itself require immediate projection onto the current
hiring path.
