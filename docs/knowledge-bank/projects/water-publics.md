# Water Publics

This dossier connects the raft expedition, Great Accommodations, public
programs, correspondence, installation work, and other participatory projects
through which Jamie invited city residents to encounter the waterways linking
their communities.

The bank distinguishes contemporaneous reporting, Jamie's attributed
recollections, institutional program descriptions, and future archival or
visual discoveries.

## Confirmed In This Run

- The Pitch credited Jamie with originating the 2007 experiential raft project from an investigation of Kansas City transportation history and the Missouri River.
- Charlotte Street presented Jamie as spearheading Great Accommodations and described its outreach, collected river stories, planned interactive installation and public programs, and Jamie's planned facilitator role.
- Charlotte Street preserved Jamie's attributed account of traveling by bicycle-powered raft from Kansas City down the Missouri and Mississippi for four months until the water tasted salty.

The supplied sources do not independently verify every route and duration
detail or name a Gulf destination. Those details retain attribution. The
program page describes planned exhibition activity, so the bank does not treat
every planned element as a completed outcome.
