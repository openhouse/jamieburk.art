# Source-Backed Team Memory

Jamie developed a bounded method for turning approved source material into
reviewable operating memory. The method separates Known, Open, and Protected
material and keeps source links, human correction, and source authority in the
loop.

## Defined method

The reviewed pilot materials define a limited starting surface and concrete
outputs: a friction map, source and workflow inventory, one reviewable memory
artifact, reusable templates, a human review workflow, recommendations for
what AI should and should not do, and a continue-revise-stop decision.

The public lab page accurately projects the method without exposing private
proposal context.

## Boundaries

- This is a developing method, not a finished SaaS product or production
  deployment.
- It is not a chatbot or unrestricted private-archive browser.
- It does not replace human judgment or source authority.
- Private collaborator identity, organization context, pricing, transcripts,
  and proposal records remain outside the repository.

## Research state

`CLM-SOURCE-BACKED-TEAM-MEMORY-METHOD` is use-with-care. Its method, starting
conditions, deliverables, and safety boundaries are established. A completed
approved pilot, review evidence, and bounded outcome measures would be needed
for a validated implementation claim.
