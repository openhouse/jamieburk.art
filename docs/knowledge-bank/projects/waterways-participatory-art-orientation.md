# Waterways and participatory art orientation

This project family uses waterways, communal living, field encounters, and
public programs as structures for participation and attention. A contemporary
newspaper report documents Jamie, Libby Hendon, and Laura Mattingly more than
1,000 miles into a reclaimed-material raft journey from Kansas City into
Louisiana; it does not establish the final endpoint or sole authorship.

The review-locked [project record](waterways-and-participatory-art.md) keeps the
raft, Great Accommodations, Open House, and Shop Shows claims together with
their source and collaborator boundaries.
