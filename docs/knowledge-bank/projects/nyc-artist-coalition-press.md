# NYC Artist Coalition Campaign Press Corpus

This dossier inventories the Press sections captured from four campaign sites
associated with Jamie's NYC Artist Coalition work. It is complete for the
inspected site captures, not necessarily for every article ever published about
the campaigns. Website authorship and campaign outcomes remain separate claims
with their own evidence and collective-credit boundaries.

The campaign pages establish that an article appeared in a Press section. They
do not, by themselves, establish that Jamie authored the article, appeared in
it, endorsed every assertion, or individually caused the outcome it describes.
Each article must be close-read before it is attached to a more specific claim.

The typed corpus in `apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/campaign-press.ts`
preserves a normalized identity and retrievable Wayback path for every unique
article. The capture fixture separately preserves each exact campaign-listed
URL and its source-page order.
