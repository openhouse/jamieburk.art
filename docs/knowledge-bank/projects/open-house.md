# Open House

This dossier preserves public-safe evidence for the 2006 Porter Bridge Gallery
Open House and its relationship to Jamie's longer participatory practice. It
retains collective credit, distinguishes facilitation from control, and
protects participants from unnecessary exposure.

## Confirmed In This Run

Good Times documented Jamie instigating and continuously tending a ten-day
gallery experiment organized around communal responsibility, open
participation, and reflexive documentation. The article also connects Open
House to earlier Shop Shows and records Jamie explicitly declining a sole-leader
role.

## Boundary

The source does not authorize republication of every participant name or
photograph, establish a quantified attendance total, or turn collective
activity into Jamie's sole authorship.

## Projection Decision

The claim is mature and public-safe with collective-work qualification, but it
remains editorially unused in the current portfolio composition.

Look for wide views of the inhabited gallery, shared making and decision
processes, Jamie facilitating or tending the space, public participation, and
