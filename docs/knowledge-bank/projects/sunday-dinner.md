# Sunday Dinner And 196

Sunday Dinner and 196 made recurring gathering into participation
infrastructure: invitations, themes, meals, hosted space, records, and a reason
for artists and collaborators to return over time.

Protected workbook and archive aggregates support continuity and operating
depth. They do not authorize person-level publication or establish that a row
is a unique person, that attendance implies endorsement, or that a gathering
caused a later project or professional outcome.

## What Became Usable

- a repeatable hosting and invitation practice;
- continuity across gatherings and later remote formats;
- an environment where people could bring work, questions, and relationships;
  and
- operational records that can support bounded aggregate history while keeping
  participants protected.

The project's value includes atmosphere and relationship, but those qualities
cannot be converted into a metric or appropriated as an individual impact
claim.
