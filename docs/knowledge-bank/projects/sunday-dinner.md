# Sunday Dinner and 196

Sunday Dinner was a recurring hospitality and participation practice connected
to 196, an artists' residency and shared cultural space. Jamie designed and
maintained a longitudinal system connecting numbered gatherings, invitations,
responses, attendance logic, themes, hosts, and reusable follow-through.

## What became usable

The system made it possible for a long-running gathering to remember itself,
welcome new people, distribute hosting, coordinate resident artists, and carry
relationships and ideas from one event into the next. The primary workbook
contains 340 distinct numbered gathering identifiers through 345 and explicitly
labels a 300th Sunday Dinner; it does not establish a safe public attendee total.

## Present-grounded source return

The July 19, 2026 encounter revisited the public-safe structural audit of the
longitudinal workbook alongside public invitations and project records. It
strengthened the operational-system framing and retained privacy boundaries
around participant names, contact details, attendance states, and private
community history.
