# Jamie Burkart and WOW List Facebook events

Reviewed: 2026-07-15

## Public-safe conclusion

Jamie's current personal hosted-event archive preserves a 21-record chronology
from December 2006 through February 2019. It connects participatory art,
waterways, public history, recurring hospitality, DIY cultural-space support,
civic participation, and venue safety across Kansas City, Santa Cruz, and New
York City.

The strongest immediate website evidence is a Sunday Dinner chronology. Six
event titles explicitly identify Sunday Dinner, including the 100th gathering
in March 2014 and the 200th in June 2016. The 200th event names Julia
Fredenburg and Jamie Burkart as hosts and links to a corresponding WOW List
event record. This is specific evidence of continuity and cross-surface event
distribution. It is not the complete Sunday Dinner population.

## Historical plot points

The complete row-level chronology lives in the fixture. Representative moments
show the range and continuity of Jamie's event practice:

- **December 2, 2006, Santa Cruz:** *Musicians for a Semantic Web* connected
  semantic-web concepts to music and scene networks.
- **January 6, 2007, Kansas City:** *Pirate Trolley-In!!* used the 8th Street
  Trolley Tunnel for site-specific film and public-history programming.
- **July 14, 2007, Kansas City:** *Release Yourself onto the Water Until it
  Tastes of Salt* invited the public into a collaboratively built raft project.
- **January 8, 2010, Kansas City:** *NTER CHNG* presented a text-message-driven
  participatory communication installation with Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart,
  and Garrett Fuselier displayed as hosts.
- **March 9, 2014, Brooklyn:** *Sunday Dinner Turns 100!* marks a documented
  recurring-hospitality milestone.
- **June 26, 2016, Brooklyn:** the 200th Sunday Dinner names Julia Fredenburg
  and Jamie as hosts and links to WOW List.
- **January 15, 2017, Brooklyn:** a Sunday Dinner potluck combined hospitality,
  sign-making, costumes, and civic participation.
- **February 24, 2019, New York City:** *Free Venue Fire Safety Course*, hosted
  publicly by Tre Mc Manus, Jamie, and Combustion Inc., offered free safety
  education for people who produce, work at, or attend events.

These are displayed host relations and public invitations. They do not assign
Jamie sole production credit for cohosted work or recover every collaborator's
division of labor.

## Source and traction inventory

Seven recovered descriptions contain 16 outbound links. They include artist
and music pages, WOW List and Sunday Dinner event surfaces, a community-scene
site, project media, and reference sources. Posting a URL establishes a dated
event-source association; it does not establish authorship, endorsement,
audience use, or the destination's truth.

Seventeen detail pages display 608 aggregate Facebook response actions. Eight
display at least 20 and three at least 100. These are point-in-time platform
actions, not unique people, verified attendance, reach, conversion,
endorsement, or impact. The response aggregate remains knowledge-bank depth and
is not selected for the website.

## WOW List boundary

While switched into the WOW List Page, the current authenticated hosted-past
surface materialized zero records. The current public Page Events tab also
displayed **No events to show**.

This is a complete result for the two current migrated Page surfaces, not for
WOW List's historical event practice. It does not establish that WOW List never
created or cohosted a Facebook event. A Page owner export, archived event-ID
