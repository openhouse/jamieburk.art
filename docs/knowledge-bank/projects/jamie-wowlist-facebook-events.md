# Jamie Burkart and WOWList Facebook Event Records

**Research date:** 2026-07-15
**Projection decision:** Public knowledge-bank reserve only. No website, event,
proof, or archive route was added.

## What Was Reviewed

This archival-production pass used an authenticated Facebook session to review:

1. the complete event-card population currently exposed by Jamie Burkart's
   personal Past Events surface;
2. every exposed personal event detail page;
3. the complete current WOWList Events surface;
4. every Facebook event URL in the complete preserved WOWList page-post corpus;
   and
5. five additional event routes from Jamie's protected personal-post archive
   as candidates for the one unresolved personal control slot.

The personal host card displayed **21 past-event slots**. Repeated terminal
scrolling exposed **20 unique event cards**, and all 20 detail pages were
reviewed. The final slot did not materialize and remains unresolved. The five
candidate routes did not list Jamie as organizer or co-organizer, so none was
used to fill the gap.

WOWList's current Events surface displayed **"No events to show"** and no event
cards. The preserved page-post corpus contains two distinct Facebook event
routes, both externally hosted. These are separate denominators: a current
zero-state is not a historical hosted-event count, and a shared event link is
not a WOWList event.

## Governed Findings

### Jamie's event practice

Twenty recovered event pages from December 2006 through February 2019 list
Jamie as organizer or co-organizer. Thirteen display Jamie alone and seven
preserve cohost credit. Together they document a long practice of giving
emerging cultural, civic, and community premises a concrete form people could
enter:

- participatory art and interactive media;
- public-history screenings and place-based inquiry;
- DIY music and touring support;
- recurring meals, rotating home hosting, and collective memory;
- mutual aid and shelter;
- civic preparation and public discussion; and
- practical cultural-space safety training.

An organizer display does not establish Jamie's complete task list, sole
authorship, production budget, attendance, or measured impact. The recovered
population is bounded by Facebook's current interface and is not Jamie's
complete event history.

### Response signals

Six recovered pages display historical Facebook response labels. Five show at
least 20 responses and two show at least 100. The labels are event-level
interface signals, not verified attendance, unique people, reach,
participation, endorsement, conversion, mandate, or impact. They are not summed
into a people-reached figure.

### WOWList's cross-surface event routes

Two Facebook event routes survive in the complete preserved WOWList page-post
corpus:

1. A 2015 Chicago DIY gathering whose event description linked a dedicated
   WOWList scene page.
2. A 2017 New York cultural-space safety meeting circulated by the WOWList
   account as important to local arts and DIY spaces.

Neither was hosted by WOWList. They preserve a bounded pattern of shared
project-account curation and routing across event, scene, calendar, and civic
surfaces. They do not establish Jamie's individual authorship of either post,
broad WOWList adoption, attendance, endorsement, or impact.

## Mission-Relevant Resource Routes

The event descriptions contained 18 normalized public-resource occurrences
resolving to 17 distinct URLs. They include artist pages, a preserved WOWList
event route for the 200th Sunday Dinner, Sunday Dinner project links, scene
resources, participatory-art media, and conceptual references. One unresolved
short link is retained only as a withheld-link category. Tracking parameters
are removed.

The strongest connections to already governed records are:

- NTER CHNG and its later America: Now and Here exhibition provenance;
- the raft and waterways practice;
- the 8th Street Tunnel participatory public-history screening;
- Sunday Dinner's recurring hosting milestones; and
- WOWList as infrastructure linking a gathering to a durable event page.

Event-page links establish source routes. They do not make every statement at
the destination a Jamie-authored claim or prove adoption and impact.

## Public-Safety Boundary

The repository excludes:

- exact residential addresses and access directions;
- historical phone numbers and email addresses;
- guests, attendees, comments, reactions, and invite context;
- raw event descriptions containing protected details;
- account-administration and authenticated-session data;
- local private-archive paths; and
- tracking parameters and unresolved short-link destinations.

Organizer names are retained only where the public event page presented them
in that role and collective credit matters to the historical record.
