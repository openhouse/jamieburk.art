# Let NYC Dance

Let NYC Dance was a public campaign surface within NYC Artist Coalition's
collective advocacy concerning the Cabaret Law. The site paired an issue
explainer with public license and citation data, maps, source links, and action
pathways.

Retained repository history supports Jamie's implementation and maintenance of
campaign web infrastructure. It does not establish sole authorship of campaign
strategy, research, copy, design, organizing, or the law's repeal.

The New York City Council repealed the Cabaret Law in 2017. That was a Council
action shaped by a larger history of artists, venues, advocates, officials,
staff, reporting, and public testimony. The campaign belongs in that collective
history without being inflated into sole causality.
