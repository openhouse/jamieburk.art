# WOW List

## Public-safe position

WOW List was a co-built community-calendar platform organized around followable keyword communities. Public and archival evidence supports Jamie's role as a co-builder and product operator alongside Richard and other collaborators. The account record should be attributed to the project team unless a specific post identifies its author.

The account's earliest recovered authored post says Richard and Jamie were developing a project based on calendars made at Sunday Dinner. A later post describes NYCdiy as running on WOW List and identifies the platform as a DIY community-calendar project from the Sunday Dinner potluck.

## Product operation

Three public conversation parents preserve concrete user questions and workflow friction:

1. How can someone see events beyond their current location?
2. How can someone find their personal WOW Lists?
3. How can someone add an event from the home screen without so many clicks?

The project account answered each question with the current workflow or a stated interface direction. These threads support a public product-support loop. They do not establish a representative usability study, the individual author of each reply, or that every implied change shipped.

The account also thanked a user for producing a public WOW List tutorial and linked the join flow. This is evidence of user-created onboarding, not a complete adoption measure.

## Stakeholder patterns

Reposting an institution such as Meow Wolf is curation by WOW List, not evidence that the institution engaged back. Visible reply, repost, and like counts are dated observations and do not identify stakeholder type.

## Projection decision

Promote:

- Sunday Dinner lineage and co-building credit;
- observable organizer use;
- the public product-support loop;
- the bounded civic/care use pattern.

Hold:

- July 2026 engagement totals;
- any claim that Jamie authored every account post;
- complete adoption or impact language;
- claims based on the unrecovered *Zines 2.0* article body;
- a same-account bridge between `@wowlistnyc` and `@wowlist` until a dated public source is recovered.
