# WOW List social and public-use record

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-15
**Public surface:** `/work/wowlist`

The dedicated public identity is [`@wowlist`](https://x.com/wowlist). An
authenticated archival pass recovered all 38 records in the current profile
control. Posts yielded 37 records; one additional account reply appeared only
in Replies, bringing the deduplicated union to 38. This completeness is bounded
to the displayed profile control and does not prove that no earlier post was
deleted, withheld, or left unindexed.

The complete population comprises 16 original posts, 6 replies, and 16 reposts.
Thirty-one records contain an external link, producing 35 distinct short-URL
occurrences. The 22 account-authored records show recurring operating uses:

- inline product support and event-publishing guidance;
- community-created onboarding;
- event and calendar distribution;
- Sunday Dinner and DIY-scene lineage;
- mission-relevant source curation; and
- rapid civic and mutual-aid coordination.

A separate bounded incoming search recovered 16 records. Ten mission-relevant
third-party accounts span DIY arts and music, calendar-community,
neighborhood-civic, and creative-technology groups; nine independently posted
a WOW List URL. Punks & Criminals directly described adding shows to WOW List,
and Music Hackathon publicly described the service as made by its co-organizer
Jamie Burkart. These are public-use and distribution signals, not a complete
reception census or a measure of adoption, reach, attendance, endorsement, or
impact.

Representative contemporaneous records support the selected public claim:

- A February 2014 post names Jamie and a collaborator and describes WOW List
  as based on calendars made at Sunday Dinner.
- An August 2015 post thanks a community member for creating a tutorial for
  other people using the platform.

The complete population also surfaced nine representative mission-relevant
destinations, including DIY cultural reporting, an all-ages organizing manual,
the Allied Media Conference, cultural-space safety and mourning records,
mutual-aid resources, and the archived popular.vote civic-coordination surface.
These sources establish what the account circulated, not WOW List authorship,
partnership, or ownership of the underlying work.
