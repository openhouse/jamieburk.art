# WOW List

## Public-safe project baseline

WOWList.org was a co-built community-calendar platform organized around
followable keyword communities. Historical aggregate records support 1,800+
users, 16,000+ posts/events, and adoption across roughly 35 city ecosystems.
Jamie co-built the product model, event workflows, and public-facing web
system.

## Social corpus

The July 2026 archival production recovered all 38 records represented by the
surviving [@wowlist profile](https://x.com/wowlist): 16 account posts, six
account replies, and 16 reposts from 13 other public accounts.

All six account replies provide product support, onboarding, or local-calendar
identity guidance. Three account posts route scene knowledge. Five account
posts and five reposts connect event discovery with civic mobilization, mutual
aid, memorial, and support for DIY cultural spaces. All 35 shortened-link
occurrences were dispositioned to 34 distinct destinations.

## Selected claim

The public account Jamie reports establishing became a direct support surface
for the product he co-built. The six surviving replies explain feed scope,
profile navigation, multi-list event submission, local-calendar onboarding,
and the relationship among NYCDIY, WOW List, and Sunday Dinner.

## Boundaries

- The 38-record result is complete recovery of the surviving profile control,
  not a native X export or deletion history.
- Shared-account authorship does not identify which teammate composed a post.
- Links and reposts establish circulation and context, not coverage,
  partnership, endorsement, organization, adoption, or impact.
- Mutable public counters are dated events, not unique people or identified
  stakeholder accounts.
- Raw product rows, private user data, credentials, messages, and analytics do
  not belong in the public repository.
