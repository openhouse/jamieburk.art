# WOW List

WOW List was a community calendar and cultural-information system co-created by
Jamie Burkart and Richard Viverios. It joined followed interests, shared
editing, distributed publishing, organizer support, and recurring social
communication.

The surviving database and social records support substantial product and
operating depth. They do not make every row a person, every city label an
official chapter, every post Jamie's authorship, or every response a measure of
attendance, reach, or impact.

## What Became Usable

- reusable event publishing and distribution;
- relational discovery through interests and shared tags;
- organizer-facing communication and public identity; and
- a bridge between online cultural information and occasions to be present in
  person.

Individual participant, subscriber, and attendance data remain protected.
Later links to Sunday Dinner, Call Script, and NYC Artist Coalition are a
documented practice lineage, not proof that one project caused the next.
