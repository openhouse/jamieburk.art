# WOWList

WOWList was a co-built community calendar and social-discovery platform for DIY
arts, music, and local-interest scenes. It combined a Django/PostgreSQL/PostGIS
backend with an Ember interface and supported events, followable lists, follows,
stars, attendance intent, mentions, email settings, geolocation, and Google
Calendar synchronization.

## Jamie's role

Jamie co-built and supported the product model, technical system, event
workflows, and public-facing experience. Surviving account records also show the
identity functioning as a direct support surface where product behavior,
onboarding, local calendars, and contribution paths were explained.

## What became usable

The July 2017 production snapshot contained 1,846 users, 16,142 posts or events,
23,864 tags or lists, 28,837 list follows, and 20,927 saved or starred records.
Thirty-five city labels met a threshold of at least 50 geocoded records. That is
a bounded distribution measure, not a claim of official chapters.

## Present-grounded source return

The July 19, 2026 encounter revisited the read-only database audit, source-code
and product archive summaries, and complete surviving social-account census. It
confirmed the strength of the product and support-system claims while retaining
privacy boundaries around user records, raw database rows, and community media.
