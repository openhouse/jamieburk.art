# Save NYC Spaces

Save NYC Spaces framed small cultural spaces as essential civic and cultural
infrastructure rather than disposable event inventory. The campaign surface
connected public reporting, cultural-space testimony, action pathways, and the
coalition's longer affordability work.

Jamie's documented contribution is strongest at the level of campaign-site and
public-information infrastructure. Coalition strategy, testimony, research,
copy, and advocacy were collective and changed over time.

The campaign should not be used to claim that one website saved a venue,
prevented displacement, represented every space, or produced a measured policy
outcome. Its defensible value is as a durable public container for a collective
problem, source network, and course of action.
