# Google Drive Shared Drive Archive Production

This page records the public-safe method and dispositions from a bounded July
2026 review of Jamie Burkart's Google Drive Shared Drives. It does not reproduce
the drive inventory, underlying links, raw documents, person-level records, or
media.

## Scope and selection

The pass inventoried 110 accessible Shared Drive roots. Before reading content,
it excluded personal, legal, wedding, bulk-photo, synchronization, and
relationship archives. Fifteen professional roots were selected across civic
systems, public data, cultural infrastructure, technical work, and externally
attributable collaboration.

The review kept six states separate:

1. A drive or folder is inventoried.
2. A folder is presently readable.
3. A record has been closely read.
4. A source proposition is qualified.
5. A claim is mature.
6. A claim is selected for a public surface.

Access did not imply permission. A folder title did not establish authorship,
delivery, or public status.

## Privacy and publication rules

- No Drive identifiers or links enter the public repository for protected
  sources.
- No person-level rows, raw working documents, private names, access details, or
  unapproved media are copied into the bank.
- Collaborator credit is preserved whenever the source establishes it.
- Empty or inaccessible folders remain unavailable, not nonexistent.
- The 110-drive inventory is not represented as an exhaustive file-level
  review.
