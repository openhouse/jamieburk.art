# Google Drive Shared Drive archival production - 2026-07-14

## Purpose

This pass treated Jamie's Google Drive Shared Drives as a working archive: a
source of handoff records, operating artifacts, and research leads that may
strengthen the knowledge bank without becoming a public document dump.

The review inventoried 110 Shared Drives, then close-read a bounded set of
project roots relevant to the current portfolio. Personal, legal, wedding,
divorce, billing, credential, and sync-recovery material was excluded. Drive
names, file IDs, URLs, participant rows, contact details, private documents,
and raw media are not stored in this public repository.

## What matured

Two protected artifacts support narrower, more useful public claims on the
196 Artists Residency / Sunday Dinner case study:

- a 2023 acceptance workflow signed by Jamie documents proposal review,
  video-call onboarding, space configuration, and independent access handoff
  for an accepted artist and collaborator;
- a 2025 operating-sheet schema documents recurring event-date and theme,
  invitation, response-status, attendance, and follow-up fields.

These records support workflow structure. They do not establish artist
outcomes, community impact, participant consent, total gathering count, total
attendance, or the number of resident artists. No participant-level value from
the operating sheet was copied into the repository.

## What remains held

Three photo collections and one meeting video were retained only as protected
research leads:

- Save Jimmy's Corner public-event documentation;
- Open Data Week photographs and branded motion assets;
- ACT at MIT public-stage documentation;
- a WOW List members-meeting video.

Each lead has an inquiry for public context, authorship or creator credit,
rights, represented-person consent, and editorial use. Folder custody,
filename language, visible branding, and generic visual content are not enough
to establish Jamie's role. No image, video, participant identity, raw quote,
or private locator enters the public bundle.

## Projection decision

The 196 Artists Residency / Sunday Dinner page now projects the two mature
workflow claims. The visual and meeting-video leads remain off every public
surface until corroboration and permissions are complete.

The existing 300-plus gathering and 20-plus resident-artist statements remain
first-party professional claims. The Shared Drive records reviewed here do not
independently prove those totals, so this pass does not use them as metric
corroboration.
