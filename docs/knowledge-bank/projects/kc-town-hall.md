# KC Town Hall

KC Town Hall joined restoration of a long-disused neighborhood building with
public listening, documentation, and municipal process. The record supports
Jamie's Phase One planning, daily coordination across specialist teams,
measured-drawing and survey workflows, and public presentation work.

## What Became Usable

- a completed cold-shell stabilization phase documented in the municipal grant
  packet;
- coordination across historic masonry, roofing, carpentry, welding,
  engineering, architecture, and plumbing scopes;
- a neighborhood survey and contact workflow connecting site encounters to
  participatory vision; and
- a public record through which CCED and Council action can be inspected.

## Outcomes And Transition

Municipal records document the board recommendation, Council appropriation,
and later repeal of the unspent allocation. Those institutional acts are not
Jamie's acts. Project transition to a mission-aligned organization remains
distinct from funding chronology, and private family circumstances near the
conclusion of Jamie's work are intentionally outside this account.

The record supports a project-level general-contractor role in Phase One. It
does not establish a trade license, sole labor, every subcontracting decision,
or complete attribution for every neighborhood interaction.
