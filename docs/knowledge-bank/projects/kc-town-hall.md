# KC Town Hall public funding record

**Status:** Confirmed with boundaries

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-13
**Approval owner:** Jamie Burkart

## Defensible public claim

After the Central City Economic Development (CCED) Sales Tax Board recommended
$490,539 for KC Town Hall, the Kansas City Council accepted the recommendation
and appropriated the amount to a City project account on September 26, 2019.

That appropriation was not a payment to KC Town Hall. A May 10, 2022 City update
reported $0 disbursed and contract execution still pending. After the project
withdrew, the Council returned the unused $490,539 to the fund in 2024.

## Decision chain

1. **July 16, 2019:** The CCED Board voted to recommend $490,539 for KC Town
   Hall, according to the official Resolution 190649 fact sheet.
2. **September 26, 2019:** The Council adopted Resolution 190649 as substituted,
   accepting the recommendation and authorizing negotiation of a funding
   agreement.
3. **September 26, 2019:** The Council passed Committee Substitute for Ordinance
   190642, appropriating $490,539 to a Central City Sales Tax project account for
   KC Town Hall.
4. **May 10, 2022:** The City's CCED update listed $0 disbursed and contract
   execution pending an escrow agent.
5. **March 28, 2024:** After KC Town Hall withdrew, Ordinance 240317 returned the
   unused $490,539 to the Central City Sales Tax housing budget.
