# KC Town Hall

## Public record

Official Kansas City records identify Jamie as KC Town Hall's presenter and
trace the project from a unanimous $490,539 CCED Board recommendation through
City Council adoption and appropriation. Later official records show that no
funds were disbursed, the project withdrew, and the unused amount was
reappropriated in 2024.

That sequence does not establish a unanimous Council vote, an executed funding
agreement, receipt or expenditure of funds, completed redevelopment, current
property status, or Jamie's responsibility for the later withdrawal.

## Phase One restoration

The 2019 municipal packet labels the $189,629 Phase One cold-shell work
completed that year. Its scope includes roof-deck repair, insulation and TPO
membrane, masonry restoration, floor framing, water connection, access,
safety, transport, cleanup, and related work. This is completed implementation
evidence for Phase One, not completion of the later redevelopment plan.

Jamie reports serving as general contractor for this phase: hiring and
coordinating specialized teams, managing sequencing from basement to roof, and
maintaining a daily site presence. The packet identifies Jamie as founder and
project presenter but does not independently assign the general-contractor
title, so the role remains a bounded claim held from the website and resume
while contracts, permits, schedules, invoices, or collaborator confirmation
are reviewed.

## Neighborhood listening

The same packet reproduces a neighborhood survey developed with Oak Park
Neighborhood Association and New Horizon Missionary Baptist Church and says
its results directly shaped the proposal. A protected project-site archive
preserves the published survey page and backing Google Form reference.

Jamie reports designing the 4-by-6 handbill and data-collection system and
using daily site presence to gather neighborhood histories, ideas, and contact
offers over time. Individual design authorship remains a research question.
Raw responses, names, phone numbers, email addresses, and contact records stay
private.

## Stewardship research lead

Jamie reports that when his direct involvement concluded, he transitioned
project stewardship to a mission-aligned organization. This source-free

The receiving organization, timing, scope, handoff mechanics, and subsequent
