# KC Town Hall

Status: bounded public projection with official status correction.

## Supported chronology

- On June 12, 2019, Kansas City CCED public minutes listed Jamie as the KC Town
  Hall presenter for proposal 16.
- The minutes recorded a requested amount of $490,539 and a proposed program
  of four retail spaces and three apartments at 36th Street and Indiana
  Avenue.
- Kansas City Ordinance 240317, passed March 28, 2024, records that KC Town
  Hall withdrew and would no longer proceed under the CCED award. It
  reappropriated the unused $490,539.

## Approved public claim

Jamie co-led planning and public-benefit documentation for the proposed
mixed-use rehabilitation and presented the proposal in the public funding
process. The project received a public funding recommendation and later
withdrew before proceeding under that award.

The approved resume uses the contribution wording "secured a $490,539 public
funding recommendation." The knowledge bank preserves that as Jamie's approved
self-report; it does not treat the verb as proof of sole causality, final funds
received, or completed construction.

## Boundaries

- Do not say the redevelopment was completed.
- Do not say Jamie personally received $490,539.
- Do not say the project remains active under the award.
- Do not infer why the project withdrew or assign fault.
- Do not expose private property, financial, legal, banking, or stakeholder

The transferable proof is the operating work: turning a long-horizon,
multi-stakeholder idea into a documented proposal that could enter municipal
