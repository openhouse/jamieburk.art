# KC Town Hall

## Official Record

Kansas City's Central City Economic Development Sales Tax Board minutes identify
Jamie as presenter of the KC Town-Hall proposal. The proposal described adaptive
reuse of an existing building for four retail spaces and three apartment units.
The minutes record a unanimous board recommendation to City Council for $490,539
in funding.

Source: `SRC-KC-TOWN-HALL-CCED-MINUTES-2019`.

## Council Action

On September 26, 2019, the Kansas City Council took two distinct actions:

- it adopted Second Committee Substitute for Resolution 190649, accepting the
  CCED Board's recommendation and authorizing negotiation of a funding agreement
  for an amount not to exceed $490,539; and
- it passed Committee Substitute for Ordinance 190642, appropriating $490,539
  to KC Town Hall in the Central City Sales Tax-Projects account.

Sources: `SRC-KC-TOWN-HALL-RESOLUTION-190649`,
`SRC-KC-TOWN-HALL-ORDINANCE-190642`.

## Later Disposition

A May 17, 2022 city update listed no funds disbursed and described the funding
agreement as under negotiation. In 2024, Ordinance 240317 recorded that KC Town
Hall had withdrawn; the City reduced the full $490,539 project account and
returned the unused appropriation to the Central City Sales Tax Fund.

Sources: `SRC-KC-TOWN-HALL-CCED-UPDATE-2022-05-17`,
`SRC-KC-TOWN-HALL-ORDINANCE-240317`.

## Defensible Claim

Jamie presented the adaptive-reuse proposal. The board unanimously recommended
$490,539, and the Council subsequently accepted the recommendation and
appropriated that amount. This supports a stronger, bounded account of the
proposal advancing through municipal review.

## Boundary

Council appropriation is not receipt, disbursement, expenditure, an executed
funding agreement, or project completion. The public record does not establish
Jamie's individual role in securing the Council actions. The later withdrawal
and reclamation must accompany any account of the funding outcome. Current
property status and private financial, legal, property, and stakeholder details
remain outside the public repository.
