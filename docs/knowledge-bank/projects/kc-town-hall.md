# KC Town Hall

Jamie Burkart and Julia Fredenburg co-founded KC Town Hall around the
neighborhood-led adaptive reuse of a long-vacant historic building at 36th
Street and Indiana Avenue in Kansas City.

## Jamie's role

Jamie served as Phase One's functional general contractor and daily on-site
construction lead. He hired and coordinated masonry, roofing, carpentry,
welding, engineering, architecture, plumbing, and related specialist teams,
connecting decisions from the basement through the roof and parapet. He also
created a neighborhood survey handbill and backing contact-intake system whose
ongoing results shaped the proposal.

## What became usable

Phase One completed a cold shell in 2019 across roof, masonry, floor-framing,
water-service, access, safety, and related work. The project also created a way
for construction and public visioning to inform each other rather than treating
neighborhood knowledge as an afterthought.

Official records separately document a unanimous CCED Board recommendation and
later Council authorization and appropriation of $490,539 for a subsequent
phase. The record does not establish receipt or expenditure; the project later
withdrew and the unused allocation was reappropriated after Jamie transitioned
the work to a mission-aligned organization.

## Present-grounded source return

The July 19, 2026 encounter directly reread the original proposal's neighborhood
process, Phase One scope, budget, and founder sections. It confirmed the
construction-plus-participation framing and preserved the distinction between a
