# KC Town Hall

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-12

Kansas City's Central City Economic Development Sales Tax Board minutes provide
a strong public milestone. The June 2019 presentation table identifies Jamie as
KC Town Hall's presenter and describes a $490,539 adaptive-reuse request for
four retail and three residential spaces. The July 2019 minutes record the
board's unanimous recommendation of that amount to City Council.

This is a recommendation, not proof of final City Council approval, contract
execution, disbursement, completed construction, or current property status.
Those remain open research and protected-context questions.
