# Kansas City Public Programs

## 8th Street Tunnel

KCUR documented Jamie leading participants through downtown Kansas City to a
three-film screening he programmed inside the 8th Street Tunnel in 2006. The
program connected transit history, artistic and shared space, and a vision for
educational public access.

Source: `SRC-KC-EIGHTH-STREET-TUNNEL-KCUR-2016-09-15`.

## We Can Be Dreamers

The Frontier archive documents Jamie conducting interviews and reenactment
sessions, making a short research video, and contributing to a public program
about youth, curfews, assembly, and the urban commons. Councilman John Sharp and
ACLU of Kansas City Legal Director Doug Bonney participated in the public
discussion.

Source: `SRC-KC-FRONTIER-DREAMERS-2012-05-17`.

## Through-Line

Both projects used research, movement through place, media, and facilitated
public discussion to help people interpret civic systems and imagine different
relationships with shared infrastructure.

## Boundary

These were collective, historical programs. The tunnel record does not imply
current access or safety, and the public-space project is not current legal
guidance.
