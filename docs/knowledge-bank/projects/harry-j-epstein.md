# Harry J. Epstein Company

Since 2012, Jamie has helped an established Kansas City industrial supply
business adapt its deep inventory knowledge, customer relationships, and
distinctive public voice to changing e-commerce systems. This is the clearest
long-duration example of his technical and operational stewardship inside a
working business.

## Jamie's role

Jamie's public-safe role spans web systems, e-commerce, analytics, marketing,
content, and operational workflows. He translates between technical systems
and the people who know the products, customers, suppliers, and daily work.
The purpose is continuity with greater usability: modernize what needs to
change without treating existing knowledge or voice as obsolete.

This work is collaborative. The Harry J. Epstein Company team retains the
business knowledge, customer service, purchasing, fulfillment, merchandising,
and institutional relationships that make the public system possible.

## What became usable

- Legacy product and inventory knowledge became more searchable and workable
  in an e-commerce environment.
- Public content and marketing could preserve a recognizable company voice
  while supporting online discovery and sales.
- Analytics and workflow improvements gave the team better operating feedback
  across technical and nontechnical work.
- Long-term stewardship connected successive platform, content, and business
  changes instead of treating each as an isolated redesign.

## Business evidence

The approved public claim is that Jamie's web, e-commerce, analytics,
marketing, content, and workflow improvements contributed to a period of 2x
revenue growth. "Contributed" is essential. The record does not assign all
growth to Jamie or disclose private figures, time series, attribution models,
customer data, or internal dashboards.
