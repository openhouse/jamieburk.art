# Jamie personal Facebook posts: archival production

Date: July 15, 2026

Status: public-safe research dossier. The raw record-level corpus remains
outside the public repository.

## Research question

What can the complete population returned by Jamie Burkart's authenticated
personal Facebook **Manage Posts > Posted by: You** surface add to the
knowledge bank without turning private life, mutable counters, or platform
associations into public professional claims?

## Population and denominator

The July 13 traversal reached `has_next_page: false` after 621 pages. It returned
3,728 nodes that deduplicated to 1,243 owner-filtered records from December 19,
2006 through June 12, 2022. An authenticated July 15 replay applied the same
filter and confirmed that June 2022 remained the first exposed month.

**100 percent** means every unique record returned by this owner-filtered
connection received a disposition. It does not mean a native Meta export,
deletion history, or immutable lifetime population.

## Public-safety treatment

Audience labels were not exposed for 973 of 1,243 records. One record was
friends-only and one was only-me. The full corpus therefore remains protected.
Only six individually reopened public posts were promoted as public sources.
The repository retains aggregate controls, source relationships, claims,
anti-claims, and one opaque private-artifact ID.

## Full-population findings

A deterministic second pass routed 181 records into overlapping mission-related
categories. WOW List (48), Sunday Dinner (44), NYC Artist Coalition (34), Let
NYC Dance (33), creative-technical practice (19), civic-technical practice
(13), water publics (10), and Save NYC Spaces (10) were the largest routes.
These are research aids, not effort, priority, engagement, or impact scores.

The corpus contains 430 URL-bearing records and 549 unique normalized external
destinations. Each destination remains a source lead until recovered,
close-read, and decomposed into supported propositions and boundaries.

Stakeholder-routing rules found 20 New York City Council, 18 Rafael Espinal,
nine Market Hotel, six Office of Nightlife, five Antonio Reynoso, one Quinton
Lucas, and one Helen Rosenthal occurrences. These are Jamie's mentions, tags,
quotations, and links. They are not actions by those stakeholders and do not
establish engagement, endorsement, attendance, partnership, response,
influence, or impact.

## What the corpus changes

The selected public record supports a bounded practice-level finding: Jamie
repeatedly connected project explanation to practical participation routes.
The pattern spans joining a WOW List calendar, contacting Council through Let
NYC Dance, entering a KC Town Hall neighborhood process, and attending or
amplifying a Talks Not Raids hearing. It does not establish whether people
clicked, attended, called, converted, or caused an outcome.

No public website copy changes in this pass. The material is stronger as
governed bank depth for future audience-specific composition, source research,
and photo-editor briefs.
