Date: July 15, 2026

Status: public-safe research dossier. The raw record-level corpus remains
outside the public repository.

## Research question

What can the complete population returned by Jamie Burkart's authenticated
personal Facebook **Manage Posts > Posted by: You** surface add to the
knowledge bank without turning private life, mutable counters, or platform
associations into public professional claims?

## Population and denominator

The July 13 traversal reached `has_next_page: false` after 621 pages. It
returned 3,728 nodes that deduplicated to 1,243 owner-filtered records from
December 19, 2006 through June 12, 2022. An authenticated July 15 replay
applied the same filter and confirmed that June 2022 remained the first
exposed month.

**100 percent** means every unique record returned by this owner-filtered
connection received a disposition. It does not mean a native Meta export,
deletion history, or immutable lifetime population.

## Public-safety treatment

Audience labels were not exposed for 973 of 1,243 records. One record was
friends-only and one was only-me. The record-level corpus therefore remains
protected. Only six individually reopened public posts were promoted as
public sources. The repository retains aggregate controls, source
relationships, claims, anti-claims, and one opaque protected-artifact ID.
