# Jamie personal Facebook posts: archival production

Date: July 15, 2026

Status: public-safe research dossier. The raw record-level corpus remains
outside the public repository.

## Research question

What can the complete population returned by Jamie Burkart's authenticated
personal Facebook **Manage Posts > Posted by: You** surface add to the
knowledge bank without turning private life, mutable social counters, or
platform associations into public professional claims?

## Population and denominator

The July 13 traversal reached `has_next_page: false` after 621 cursor pages. It
returned 3,728 nodes that deduplicated to 1,243 owner-filtered records, with
zero missing dates and zero records lacking Jamie in the returned owner data.
The chronology runs from December 19, 2006 through June 12, 2022.

On July 15, a fresh authenticated browser control opened Jamie's profile,
selected **Manage posts**, applied **Posted by: You**, and confirmed that the
filtered chronology began in June 2022. Six selected project posts were also
individually reopened and rechecked as public before entering the source layer.

Here, **100 percent** means every unique record returned by the owner-filtered
surface received a disposition. It does not mean a native Meta export, deletion
history, or proof that no hidden, removed, migrated, or otherwise unavailable
post ever existed.

## Public-safety treatment

The crawl exposed `Public` for 268 records, `Only me` for one, `Friends` for
one, and no audience label for 973. An unexposed label is unknown, not public.
The full corpus therefore remains protected.

Raw text, exact record-level dates and identifiers, ordinary-life material,
comments, responder rosters, media, authentication context, and record-level
counters are not committed. The repo retains aggregate controls, selected
public sources, bounded claims, anti-claims, and an opaque protected-artifact
identifier.

## Full-population findings

Eight coarse record forms reconcile exactly to 1,243: 335 text records, 244
shared stories, 221 photos, 159 unavailable-media-or-text records, 135 photo
albums, 58 events, 55 external links, and 36 videos. These categories are an
accounting control, not a statement that Jamie authored every embedded or
shared artifact.

A deterministic pass routed 181 unique records into overlapping
mission-related categories. The largest routes were WOW List (48), Sunday
Dinner (44), NYC Artist Coalition (34), Let NYC Dance (33), creative-technical
practice (19), civic-technical practice (13), water publics (10), and Save NYC
Spaces (10). Categories overlap and are research aids, not effort, importance,
audience, engagement, or impact scores.

The population contains 430 URL-bearing records and 549 unique normalized
external destinations. These routes include project sites, public processes,
action paths, independent reporting, and cultural media. A posted URL remains
a source lead until recovered and close-read.

Stakeholder-routing rules found 20 New York City Council, 18 Rafael Espinal,
nine Market Hotel, six Office of Nightlife, five Antonio Reynoso, one Quinton
Lucas, and one Helen Rosenthal record occurrences. These are Jamie's outbound
mentions, tags, quotations, and links. They are not actions by the named
stakeholders and do not establish engagement, response, endorsement,
attendance, partnership, or impact.

## Practice finding

The selected public record supports a bounded practice-level finding: Jamie
repeatedly connected project explanation to a usable participation route. The
examples move from a WOW List join path, to Cabaret Law press and Council
contact, to a neighborhood-process invitation, to a City Hall hearing and
bill-action route.

That is evidence of implementation-minded public communication. It does not
establish whether people clicked, joined, called, attended, converted, or
caused an outcome.
