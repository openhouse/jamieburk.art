# Jamie personal Facebook posts: archival production

Date: July 16, 2026

Status: public-safe research dossier. The raw record-level corpus remains
outside the public repository.

## Research question

What can the complete population returned by Jamie Burkart's authenticated
personal Facebook owner-post surface add to the knowledge bank without turning
private life, mutable social counters, or platform associations into public
professional claims?

## Population and denominator

The July 13 cursor traversal reached `has_next_page: false` after 621 pages. It
returned 3,728 nodes that deduplicated to 1,243 owner-filtered records, with
zero missing dates and zero records lacking Jamie in the returned owner data.
The returned chronology runs from December 19, 2006 through June 12, 2022.

On July 16, the authenticated Activity Log category **Your posts, photos and
videos** independently showed the same June 12, 2022 top edge. A fresh
classification pass processed all 1,243 protected records and reproduced the
year, form, theme, and professional-relevance controls.

Here, **100 percent** means every unique record returned by the terminal
owner-filtered cursor connection received a fresh analytical disposition. It
does not mean a native Meta export, deletion history, or proof that no hidden,
removed, migrated, or otherwise unavailable post ever existed.

## Public-safety treatment

Audience labels were not exposed for 973 of the 1,243 crawl records. The full
corpus therefore remains protected. Raw text, native Facebook identifiers,
exact record dates, ordinary-life material, comments, responder identities,
media, authenticated routes, and record-level counters are not committed.

Only six posts individually reopened and rechecked as public were promoted as
metadata-only source records. Their public source metadata remains citable,
while protected locators preserve the underlying relationship outside the
repository.

## Full-population findings

The fresh broad classifier routed 158 records as project-specific and 64 as
practice-related. A separate overlapping mission pass routed 181 unique
records. Its strongest project routes were WOW List (48), Sunday Dinner (44),
NYC Artist Coalition (34), Let NYC Dance (33), creative-technical practice
(19), civic-technical practice (13), water publics (10), and Save NYC Spaces
(10). These categories are research aids, not measures of effort, importance,
priority, audience, or impact.

The population contains 430 URL-bearing records and 549 unique normalized
external destinations. Posted destinations include project sites, official
processes, action routes, independent reporting, and cultural media. A posted
URL is a source lead, not provenance or endorsement by itself.

Deterministic reference rules found recurring civic routes: New York City
Council appeared in 20 records, Rafael Espinal in 18, Market Hotel in nine, the
Office of Nightlife in six, Antonio Reynoso in five, Quinton Lucas in one, and
Helen Rosenthal in one. These are Jamie's mentions, tags, quotations, and
links. They are not actions by those stakeholders and do not establish
engagement, attendance, endorsement, partnership, response, influence, or
impact.

## Posted articles and source discovery

The population preserved routes to sources already governed elsewhere in the
bank, including NPR's September 2017 Cabaret Law reporting and The Pitch's
waterways reporting. This pass adds Jake Offenhartz's March 31, 2017 Gothamist
article, *Movement For Repealing NYC's Archaic 'No Dancing' Law Gains

repeal effort. It supplies independent campaign context, not Jamie's individual
role or a precise attendance count.

## Practice finding

The selected public record supports a bounded practice-level finding: Jamie
repeatedly connected project explanation to a usable participation route. The
examples move from a WOW List join path, to a Cabaret Law press-and-call
script, to a neighborhood-process invitation, to a City Hall hearing and
bill-action route. This is implementation and public communication work, while
the archive does not establish whether people clicked, attended, called,
converted, or caused an outcome.

No public website copy changes in this pass. The material is stronger as
governed bank depth for future audience-specific composition, source research,
and photo-editor briefs.
