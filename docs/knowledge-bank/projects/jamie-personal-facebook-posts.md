## Decision

This pass accounts for 100 percent of the 1,243 records currently returned by
the owner-filtered Manage Posts surface. It does not claim a native Meta export,
deleted-post history, or immutable lifetime population.

The protected archive keeps the item-level records. The public repository keeps
only aggregate controls, one-way commitments, six individually rechecked
Public sources, reviewed source routes, claims, inquiries, and explicit
boundaries. No bulk post text, nonpublic record content, comments, ordinary
personal identities, media, private routes, or authenticated state is
published.

The visual pass recovered more current labels. Neither method establishes the
audience setting at publication time. Unlabeled never means public.

external destinations. A visual rendered-text cross-check recovered 291 records
with outbound URLs and 363 unique destinations. The structural fields have
higher recall for embedded and shared destinations; the two denominators remain
separate.

A posted URL is an intake lead, not automatic evidence. Selected routes were
independently reopened or matched to existing close-read records:

The posted Rolling Stone night-mayor video route remains preserved as a source

## Mission Routing

mission categories. The largest routes were WOW List (48), Sunday Dinner (44),
NYC Artist Coalition (34), Let NYC Dance (33), creative-technical work (19), and

These counts organize research. They are not exclusive semantic judgments,
separate projects, effort measures, professional priorities, or impact scores.

remain a cross-check rather than being mixed into these denominators.

## Traction And Stakeholders

The full Manage Posts population did not expose reliable population-wide
reaction, comment, or share totals. Four selected public posts retained dated
aggregate counters on July 16, 2026:

- KC Town Hall announcement: 106 reactions, 14 comments, three shares;
- WOW List nine-city post: 28 likes, eight comments;
- Let NYC Dance press-and-action post: 24 reactions;
- CouncilStat job-route post: seven likes, no comments.

These are mutable interface observations. They cannot be summed into unique
people, reach, stakeholder-group engagement, endorsement, attendance,
conversion, causality, or impact. Comment text and responder identities remain
outside the public artifact.

The structural classifier also counted outgoing references, including NYC
Council in 20 mission-routed records and Rafael Espinal in 18. Those are
Jamie's mentions, tags, quotations, event references, or links. They are not
incoming actions by Council members or other stakeholders.

## Projection Decision

No new personal-Facebook metric goes onto the portfolio site or resume. The
current site already has stronger project-specific, collaborator-aware, and
official or independently reported evidence.

The bank gains three things for future compositions:

1. a complete capture-date control and privacy contract;
2. a 549-route source-research queue with reviewed associations;
3. public first-party traces that can corroborate chronology, collective credit,
   and Jamie's participation-routing practice.

## Anti-Claims

- Do not say 181 routed records are 181 projects or a measure of effort.
- Do not treat 549 posted links as 549 corroborating sources.
- Do not convert outgoing stakeholder references into incoming engagement.
- Do not sum reactions, comments, or shares into people reached.
- Do not infer Jamie's CouncilStat employment, title, contract, team membership,
  or hiring authority from the job-routing post.
- Do not assign collective campaign outcomes or shared material solely to Jamie.
