# TRIE Neighborhood Initiative

The [official program description](https://www.nyc.gov/site/civicengagement/our-programs/trie-initiative.page) identifies CEC's collaboration with the Mayor's Office of Equity and Young Men's Initiative. Funded Neighborhood Administrators build local coalitions, connect residents to resources, and support civic participation including The People's Money.

[Haydon John](../people/haydon-john.md) is publicly named as the Special Projects Manager overseeing this initiative. The linked partner directory and deliverables are labeled 2025–2026; preserve that date boundary rather than assuming an unchanged next-year roster. No individual coalition membership or relationship to Jamie is inferred.
