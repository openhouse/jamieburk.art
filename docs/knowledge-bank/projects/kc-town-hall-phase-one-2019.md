# KC Town Hall Phase One construction and neighborhood process

**Review date:** 2026-07-15
**Reviewer:** Codex archival production with Jamie Burkart
**Status:** Public-safe knowledge integrated; selected role and process claims projected
**Raw source packet:** Protected and not committed

## Why this record matters

The earlier portfolio projection made KC Town Hall legible primarily as
planning, documentation, and a municipal funding process. That was accurate but
materially incomplete. Jamie's work also included field-based construction
management: he co-founded and project-managed the restoration and directly
confirms serving as general contractor for Phase One.

The stronger professional story is not that Jamie wrote about a complicated
building. It is that he helped make the work operable across specialist trades,
preservation constraints, daily site conditions, neighborhood knowledge, public
purpose, and municipal review.

## Sources reviewed

### 2019 CCED proposal packet

The 24-page packet contains:

- a project narrative naming Jamie Burkart and Julia Fredenburg as founders and
  project managers;
- a multidisciplinary team page;
- a Neighborhood Process page reproducing the survey card;
- a Local Industry page describing contractor and masonry-training practices;
- a budget narrative defining Phase One as a cold shell;
- a Phase One budget table totaling $189,629;
- support letters;
- banking, signature, contact, property, and other protected attachments.

The packet was extracted to text and every page was inspected as a rendered
image. The raw PDF is not committed because public-safe evidentiary value can be
retained without publishing the protected attachments.

The proposal is a contemporaneous applicant-authored record. It is not an
independent construction audit, final inspection certificate, or proof of City
fund disbursement.

### Official CCED meeting packet

The City of Kansas City's public July 29, 2019 CCED Board meeting packet
contains June 12 minutes listing Jamie as KC Town Hall's developer/presenter.
The minutes also record three public speakers in support of the project and the
later July 16 Board recommendation.

This official source supports Jamie's public developer/presenter role. It does
not use the title general contractor or enumerate his daily construction duties.

### Jamie's first-person Phase One account

Jamie directly confirms that:

- he served as Phase One general contractor;
- Phase One was completed in 2019;
- he was on site each morning coordinating work from basement to scaffolding to
  roof deck;
- the work crossed masonry, roofing, carpentry, welding, engineering,
  architecture, plumbing, and related specialist teams;
- he carried measured drawings and survey cards through the work;
- the TPO membrane, restored parapet, and ceramic parapet caps required precise
  sequencing; and
- he created the four-by-six survey handbill and its backing contact and
  data-collection system.

This is direct participant testimony and Jamie-approved public truth. It is not
misrepresented as an independent inspection or collaborator testimonial.
