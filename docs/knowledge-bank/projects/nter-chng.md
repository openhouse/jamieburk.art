# NTER CHNG

NTER CHNG was an interactive texting installation credited to Drew Bolton,
Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier. Public text messages entered a shared
software and gallery environment, where they appeared as a live dialogue
across the installation.

Public records place the work at the Arts Incubator Cocoon Gallery in Kansas
City in January 2010 and later document its inclusion in America: Now and
Here's Kansas City presentation in May 2011. Protected working artifacts
corroborate a hybrid installation-production system while leaving the division
of component-level labor unresolved.

## Why it matters in the practice timeline

The project connects social software, architectural space, public invitation,
real-time language, and collective authorship. It anticipates later work in
which a public identity and technical system become a container others can use.

## Boundary

- Jamie did not alone create NTER CHNG.
- The current record does not assign sole software or installation authorship.
- America: Now and Here inclusion does not establish commission mechanics,
  awards, exhibition-wide outcomes, or display at every venue in the larger
  project.
- Participant messages and identities remain protected.
