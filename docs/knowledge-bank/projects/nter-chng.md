# NTER CHNG

## Current Defensible Record

Jamie Burkart co-created *NTER CHNG* with Drew Bolton and Garrett Fuselier. A
contemporaneous *Pitch* listing records a January 8, 2010 public event and
describes the work as equal parts software application and architectural
installation: visitors sent text messages that appeared in real time across
both faces of a digital wall.

The collaborators' artist statement framed the installation as a reversal of
ordinary text messaging. A private one-to-one exchange became a public,
many-to-many spatial experience. That concept connects Jamie's early
participatory art practice with later work building public-facing systems for
collective use.

The record is explicitly collective. The surviving project video credits Drew
Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier; it also credits Mary Nichols for
help engineering and constructing the wall and Megan Mantia and Elisha Stetson
as actors. The current sources do not assign software, scenic-design,
motion-graphics, fabrication, or production responsibilities to a particular
co-creator.

## America: Now and Here

The Wayback Machine preserves the exhibition's own Kansas City website. Its
visual-artists section has a dedicated page for Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and
Garrett Fuselier and publishes the *NTER CHNG* artist statement. This directly
supports the project's inclusion in the Kansas City visual-artists program for
*America: Now and Here* in 2011.

The Nerman Museum's surviving exhibition record supplies the larger context:
*America: Now and Here* launched in Kansas City as a national project combining
national and local artists to create public dialogue, with a Nerman Museum
presentation on May 11 and 12, 2011. The museum page does not itself name
*NTER CHNG*; the archived exhibition artist page makes that connection.

## Active Claim Projections

- Jamie co-created NTER CHNG with Drew Bolton and Garrett Fuselier, an
  interactive texting installation presented in Kansas City in 2010.
- NTER CHNG combined software and spatial installation to turn private texting
  into a real-time, many-to-many public exchange across a digital gallery wall.
- In 2011, America: Now and Here's Kansas City visual-artists program included
  NTER CHNG and credited Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier.
