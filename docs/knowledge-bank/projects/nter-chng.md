# NTER CHNG

## Public-safe project record

NTER CHNG was an interactive texting installation by Drew Bolton, Jamie
Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier. An archived first-party project page places it
at Arts Incubator Cocoon Gallery in Kansas City. A later archived America: Now
and Here Kansas City artist page describes the work as both software and
architectural installation: visitors used their phones to contribute in real
time to a shared dialogue presented through a digital wall.

The interaction model is professionally relevant because it joins software,
spatial production, public participation, and facilitation. The sources support
that as the team's collective work. They do not assign concept, programming,
fabrication, motion graphics, scenic design, or experiential production tasks
to any one collaborator.

## America: Now and Here

The archived America: Now and Here Kansas City visual-artists index lists Drew
Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier individually and marks each as an
America: Now and Here/Kansas City artist. The exhibition site also preserves a
dedicated page for the trio carrying the NTER CHNG artist statement.

That evidence supports the collaborators' inclusion in the 2011 Kansas City
program and the project's relationship to their listing. It does not establish
the exact physical location, configuration, or run dates of NTER CHNG within
the program.

The Nerman Museum page is useful institutional context for the broader Kansas
City launch and for the museum's May 11-12 Barbara Kruger truck stop. It does
not name Jamie or NTER CHNG and must not be used to claim that NTER CHNG was
displayed at the Nerman Museum.

## Atomic claims

### Collective installation

**Status:** Confirmed with boundary

NTER CHNG was an interactive texting installation by Drew Bolton, Jamie
Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier, documented at Arts Incubator Cocoon Gallery in
Kansas City.

### Participatory system

**Status:** Confirmed with boundary

The team combined software and architectural installation so gallery visitors
could use their phones to contribute in real time to a shared many-to-many
dialogue.

### Exhibition-site inclusion

**Status:** Confirmed with boundary

America: Now and Here's archived Kansas City site lists all three collaborators
as Kansas City visual artists and gives the trio a dedicated page carrying the
NTER CHNG artist statement.

## Editorial state

The claims are public-linkable and defensible but remain candidates in the
knowledge bank. They are not projected onto the portfolio in this pass. Future
public composition should ask whether this early participatory system helps a
specific reader understand Jamie's present operating practice. If it does,
credit all three collaborators and keep the individual task split open unless
stronger records or collaborator review resolve it.
