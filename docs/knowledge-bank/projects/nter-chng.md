# NTER CHNG

NTER CHNG was a collaborative interactive installation credited in public
records to Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier. It used text
messages, projection, and a shared spatial interface, and was included in the
Kansas City launch of *America: Now and Here* in 2011.

Protected working records preserve technical, spatial, and restaging plans.
They support production depth without allocating every design, code, content,
installation, or facilitation task among collaborators.

## Boundary

The record does not support sole authorship, publication of protected planning
documents, or reuse of installation media without rights and consent review.
The current Wiki uses public archived records and bounded metadata; it does not
claim a complete exhibition history or audience outcome.
