# Waterway Participation

## Current Defensible Record

Public reporting currently supports three parts of a longer practice:

- In 2006, *Good Times* reported that Jamie initiated *Open House*, a temporary
  communal-living experiment in a UCSC gallery that extended the participatory
  Shop Shows. The same account describes responsibility and decision-making as
  communal, so Jamie's initiating role should not become sole-leader language.
- In 2007, *The Pitch* reported that Jamie originated the idea for a
  recycled-material Missouri River expedition connecting participatory art,
  Kansas City transportation history, and the city's relationship with the
  river. The expedition was collective work.
- Charlotte Street's archive lists *Great Accommodations with Jamie Burkart -
  Imagining Lifestyles for Cities on the Water* for September 1 through October
  3, 2009.

These records support a durable professional throughline: Jamie creates
participatory structures through which people can examine how they inhabit a
place together. They do not yet establish every route, participant, method, or
outcome across the full body of water-based work.
