# Social-Media Archive Production

Reviewed: July 15, 2026

This public-safe inventory treats project social accounts as engagement,
documentation, and operating surfaces. The research was conducted in Jamie
Burkart's authenticated X session and checked against public post URLs, public
profile state, official roster evidence where needed, and earlier
population-accounted archive work. No cookies, session data, private messages,
private accounts, follower exports, or hidden platform data were recorded.

Public posts establish what the named account published. They do not, by
themselves, identify the person who wrote a shared-account post, establish
endorsement, prove policy causation, or produce a complete lifetime engagement
census.

### Account establishment and shared authorship

Jamie states that he established these project accounts and their public-facing
identity systems. The public interface does not expose historical administrator
records, so this remains an explicitly first-person contribution claim. It is
corroborated by durable, attributable collaborator use rather than by assigning
shared-account speech to Jamie.

Olympia Kazi used `@NYCArtC` publicly across multiple years for distinct
coalition purposes:

- [March 27, 2020](https://x.com/olympiakazi/status/1243375751149346819):
  relief advocacy for musicians, DJs, and grassroots cultural venues;
- [September 10, 2021](https://x.com/olympiakazi/status/1436442036249497600):
  participation in a Commercial Rent Stabilization hearing; and
- [April 18, 2022](https://x.com/olympiakazi/status/1516092153893691392):
  nightlife oversight connected to Save NYC Spaces, Talks Not Raids, and Fair
  Rent NYC.

These records support a durable shared identity system that a collaborator used
with her own voice. They do not establish who authored every coalition post,
sole account control, or sole campaign ownership. The campaign-press corpus in
this bank remains the article-level source layer for Cabaret Law repeal,
nightlife governance, cultural-space preservation, M.A.R.C.H. accountability,
and commercial-rent work.

[list discovery](https://x.com/wowlist/status/591666366215811073), and

This is useful evidence of shared project provenance and organizer-facing
product operations. It does not prove that every requested product change
shipped, that the questions were representative usability research, or that the
historical service remains available.

## Public-Use Contract

Use lower-bound language. Attribute posts to the named author or shared account.
Keep identity-system establishment, campaign strategy, account administration,
and individual post authorship as separate contribution types. Do not convert
social engagement into endorsement, policy causation, official-service status,
or current-service guidance.
