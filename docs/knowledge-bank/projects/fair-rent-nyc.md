# FairRentNYC

FairRentNYC is a collective campaign and source-memory system supporting
Commercial Rent Stabilization and storefront stability in New York City. Its
public surface connects policy explanation, legislative progress, calls to
action, research, testimony, reporting, and a maintained reference library.

## Jamie's role

Jamie translated campaign needs into a concrete web and data implementation
system: responsive public presentation, mapped and verified location data,
reusable forms, sponsor sequencing, partner and press modules, social
presentation, and clear calls to action. He later authored a bounded 90-day
operating plan for shared campaign infrastructure, decision norms, action
tracking, consent-aware stories, and durable memory.

## What became usable

- a public route from policy context to legislative and constituent action;
- structured campaign data and reusable presentation modules;
- a reference library distinguishing public or public-share-approved material
  from internal coalition records;
- shared documentation for priorities, decisions, outreach, and handoff.

The implementation record supports Jamie's documented workstream. Policy,
organizing, partnership, copy, and legislative outcomes remain collective, and
the architecture and operating-plan records do not prove that every proposed
feature or deliverable shipped unchanged.

## Present-grounded source return

The July 19, 2026 encounter reread the current public library and campaign
surface alongside the governed public-safe findings from protected
implementation and operating records. The public library's explicit scope and
sharing boundary reinforce the larger method: usable public knowledge depends
on maintaining a clear difference between available evidence and material held
in coalition confidence.
