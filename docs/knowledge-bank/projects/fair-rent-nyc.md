# Fair Rent NYC

Fair Rent NYC is a collective campaign for commercial rent stabilization and
storefront stability. It connects small-business and cultural-space experience
with public education, coalition relationships, legislative language, legal
questions, public-data framing, and city and state strategy.

Jamie's bounded role includes campaign web infrastructure, source and decision
memory, running minutes, legislative provenance, public-data materials,
movement mapping, action tools, and stakeholder follow-up. The work is shared
with organizers, small-business owners, cultural-space participants, lawyers,
policy partners, elected officials, and staff.

## Present focus

The 2026 record documents work to preserve earlier Fair Rent NYC campaign
history while coordinating with current small-business and policy partners. It
keeps city and state lanes distinct, identifies legal and policy questions,
and treats data, contacts, stories, and publication as governed resources.

In July 2026, Jamie provided a pre-publication review of Action Lab storefront
vacancy research focused on factual accuracy, source clarity, and the scope of
the report's claims. Correspondence reviewed for the Wiki says Action Lab
incorporated many edits and invited an acknowledgement. Jamie did not rerun the
underlying analysis; unresolved workbook, code, methods, legal, and author
questions remained with their responsible owners.

## Public-record corpus

The Wiki now preserves the official Council lineage from Int 1796-2019 and Int
0093-2022 to Res 0496-2026; the complete recovered 2021 oral support cohort; 41
attributed supportive or qualified-support written submissions; relevant
stated-meeting sponsor records; and supportive public-official statements with
their exact scope. Direct bill support, related-measure support, recognition of
the rent crisis, and disagreement about mechanism remain distinct.

The state and public-event extension adds the active A5568A and S8319 sponsor
memoranda, nine complete statements from official state releases, and
timestamp-bound speeches from four public events. These include Shahana
Hanif's and Brad Lander's November 2025 statements, Eon Huntley's March 2026
candidate statement, and the named Jimmy's Corner rally chorus. A related
method can return from those timestamps to Jamie's simultaneous documentary
photography, but no image becomes publication-ready through temporal or People
metadata alone.

This corpus is a governed research and speech-preparation resource. It does not
turn witness claims into verified facts, imply that every participant supported
every provision, or establish Jamie as sole author of the campaign or bills.

## Boundary

Do not describe Jamie as sole campaign leader, bill author, legal authority, or
cause of legislative action. Running minutes document coordination and assigned
work; they do not prove unanimous agreement or final institutional decisions.
