# Fair Rent NYC and Commercial Rent Stabilization

Fair Rent NYC is collective work around commercial affordability, small
business and cultural-space stability, policy lineage, public data, and the
operating conditions required for a durable campaign. Jamie's contribution is
best understood as campaign memory, requirements, provenance, coordination,
and public-data implementation design.

## Operating structure

Jamie designed a six-part coalition operating system: a clear front door, a
recurring room, a shared public line, a stewarded story bank, an
implementation-readiness packet, and a durable source-of-truth spine. The
structure is a designed operating plan, not proof that every component has been
fully implemented.

The collaborative running minutes turn emerging work into a navigable present.
They separate decisions from proposals, track owners, status and next steps,
preserve “no decision” states, distinguish raw or internal source material from
shareable memory, and require source, consent, owner, and follow-up clarity for
contacts and stories. Jamie maintains the structure; collaborators retain
authorship of their ideas, actions, and edits.

## Policy provenance and public data

Jamie's legislative provenance work organizes public policy lineages and review
questions without claiming legal authorship or authority. It helps
collaborators distinguish inherited language, proposed revisions, unresolved
legal questions, and decisions that require broader review.

His open-data work translates a policy opportunity into bounded product
requirements: aggregate vacancy and lease-cost indicators, coverage and
suppression rules, a methods note, and field-level release requirements. The
proposal excludes confidential filings, tenant identities, and parcel-level
rent records. It is a proposal for a privacy-preserving public baseline, not an
adopted agency product.

This continues an earlier NYC Artist Coalition pattern visible in Jamie's 2019
Council testimony: ask what information small operators repeatedly need, then
express it as machine-readable fields that agencies and public tools could use.

## Emerging work

The present collaboration includes city and state policy lanes, coalition
alignment, cultural-space listening, public-data framing, legal and source
memory, and consent-aware follow-up. Plans and drafts are kept as plans and
drafts. An introductory conversation is not represented as a partnership; an
open task is not represented as adoption; a collaborator's limited capacity is
not treated as a defect.

## Present-tense source return

- **Question brought to the source:** What does the current collaboration show
  about Jamie's ability to structure emerging work without manufacturing
  certainty or burdening collaborators?
- **Original material reopened:** the protected collaborative running minutes
  through the current working period, with attention to action tables,
  decisions, no-decision states, source lineage, consent, ownership, and
  follow-up; canonical public-data requirements were reviewed alongside it.
- **Scope and completeness:** bounded close reading of the operating structure
  and current coordination record, not publication of raw meeting notes,
  strategy, contacts, vulnerable business information, or legal-review detail.
- **What changed in this reading:** restraint is part of the operating system.
  The record repeatedly preserves “not decided,” “introductory,” “internal
  only,” “capacity limited,” and “owner not yet clear” as actionable states
  rather than smoothing them into momentum claims.
- **What did not change:** the source does not prove that every designed
  component was implemented, every collaborator adopted the workflow, an
  agency accepted the data proposal, or Jamie authored collective policy.
- **Access boundary:** the running minutes and related working sources are
  protected collaborative material. Only approved claims, opaque source IDs,
  and public-safe method observations appear here.
- **Next return:** revisit after a concrete campaign decision, public-data
  response, or handoff occurs; ask collaborators which parts of the shared
  memory reduced work and which created burden.

## Boundaries

- Credit the campaign, small-business leaders, cultural-space participants,
  partner organizations, advocates, legal and policy reviewers, and public
  institutions according to the evidence for each action.
- Do not publish raw notes, legal-review context, private stories, contacts, or
  vulnerable business details.
- Do not call a designed plan adopted, a proposal implemented, or a meeting a
  partnership without evidence.
- Do not represent Jamie as author of legislation or provider of legal advice.
