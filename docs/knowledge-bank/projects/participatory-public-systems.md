# Participatory Public Systems

## Public-Safe Throughline

Public reporting from 2006, 2007, and 2009 supports a long-running pattern in
Jamie's work: creating structures people could inhabit, document, and shape
together.

- **Open House / Shop Shows:** public communal space, shared responsibility,
  open participation, facilitation, and distributed documentation.
- **River raft project:** a collaborative expedition connecting Kansas City to
  the Missouri and Mississippi River system.
- **Great Accommodations:** river-community outreach, participatory programs,
  public story contribution, active accommodation, and working documentation.

The About page uses this pattern once as professional context. It does not turn
the projects into formal employment history or claim solo authorship.

## Open Research

The supplied sources document a journey from Kansas City down the Missouri and
Mississippi Rivers until the water tasted salty. They do not yet establish the
exact final landing point, complete route chronology, or full collaborator
record. The remembered Kansas City-to-Gulf formulation remains a candidate
claim under active research rather than a discarded memory or promoted fact.
