# Personal Facebook Authored-Post Archive

## Public-Safe Baseline

An authenticated owner-visible pass reviewed the full capture-date population
of posts returned by Facebook's `Manage posts` surface after applying
`Posted by: You` across all years and privacy states.

The terminal population contained **1,243 authored-post cards** spanning
**December 19, 2006 through June 12, 2022**. The count remained stable at the
oldest record with no loading indicator. This is 100% of the authored-post
population exposed by that surface on July 16, 2026.

It is not a native owner export or an all-ever count. It cannot recover records
deleted, withheld, or otherwise absent before capture.

## Privacy Boundary

The compact cards displayed these visibility labels:

| Label | Cards |

| Friends | 204 |
| Only me | 98 |
| No compact-card privacy label | 270 |

An absent label is unknown, not public. Only the 671 explicitly Public cards
contribute to public content findings. Raw post bodies, item-level personal
chronology, ordinary identities, comments, images, private and malformed URLs,
and authenticated state remain outside the repository.

across **363 distinct routes**. The most frequent hosts included Flickr routes,

authorship of the destination, endorsement, readership, conversion,
participation, or impact. Not all 363 destinations have been resolved and
close-read.

## Stakeholders And Traction

Explicitly public posts repeatedly referenced City Council, the Office of
Nightlife, NYC Artist Coalition, cultural spaces, and allied cultural
organizations. These are outgoing references, not incoming engagement.

Twenty mission-relevant cards rendered Facebook event social-context phrases
between 6 and 157 friends. Those mutable interface phrases are not attendance,
RSVP totals, post engagement, unique people reached, endorsement, or impact.

The complete `Manage posts` surface did **not** expose reaction, comment, share,
or incoming engager-identity fields. This pass therefore makes no
full-population Facebook engagement total and no key-stakeholder engagement
claim. It also does not claim zero engagement. A future engagement study would
need a minimized native export or a bounded professional subset with explicit
privacy controls.

## Editorial Disposition

The population and public-relay claims are promoted into the internal
knowledge bank with every public website projection held. The engagement claim
remains held for evidence. Selected posted destinations remain source leads
until individually recovered and close-read.

The current job-application website does not need another metric or archive
surface. It already communicates the stronger project-specific results. This
pass gives future agents a deeper source reserve for a particular brief, a
claim challenge, or a photo-editor inquiry.

There is no `/proofs`, `/knowledge-bank`, or personal-Facebook archive page.
