## Reuse rules

1. Treat account establishment as Jamie's first-person contribution unless a
   collaborator or platform record supplies independent corroboration.
2. Treat public profiles and project sites as independent support for account
   identity and project relationship, not creation history.
3. Treat individual posts as account-level evidence unless authorship is
   separately established.
4. Count only returned posts that survive temporal and identity review.
5. Do not convert follows, tags, likes, views, or current counters into impact.
6. Keep social engagement separate from endorsement, adoption, commissioning,
   partnership, and policy causality.
7. Keep private messages, account access, analytics, credentials, unpublished
   drafts, follower identities, and personal contact information outside the
   public repository.
