# WOW List - Senior Product Manager role-fit brief

## Conclusion

WOW List makes Jamie Burkart exceptionally well matched on product practice for
NYC Office of Technology and Innovation (OTI) Senior Product Manager Job ID
782366 and similar resident-facing roles. Jamie and Richard Caceres co-built a
working community-calendar and discovery service. Jamie's documented practice
spans problem framing, product definition, hands-on implementation,
participatory discovery, adoption, public communication, instrumentation, and
long-term stewardship.

That combination is the case—not a founder superlative. The record shows a
builder who understood that a public-facing product succeeds through software,
operating practice, human welcome, and legible communication together. A strong
fit argument does not guarantee an award, establish civil-service eligibility,
or replace employer review.

## Why the match is unusually strong

**The work reached production.** WOW List was not a speculative concept. The
public-safe record preserves a multi-user product with editable events,
followable interests and people, profiles, calendar connections, personalized
email, support workflows, and historical production instrumentation.

**Discovery was situated in use.** Sunday Dinner calendars supplied an initial
problem and practice. Public support records and organizer-facing materials
show people being helped to join, publish, follow calendars, and sustain local
lists. This is strong participatory discovery and adoption evidence, even
though a formal moderated-research program is not established.

**The social layer was product work.** Facilitation, onboarding, editorial
introductions, organizer support, and distributed calendar maintenance were not
merely marketing around the service. They made the product intelligible and
usable within existing communities.

**Jamie can reason about evidence after launch.** The present public-safe record
distinguishes historical system counts from active users, outcomes, and causal
impact. It also separates evidence from consent and publication authority. That
is mature product judgment for environments with imperfect data and public
obligations.

## Explicit gaps and human gates

- The posting's civil-service minimum qualifications require human review of
  Jamie's complete education and employment chronology. WOW List alone cannot
  establish that eligibility.
- Historical WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508 conformance have not been verified.
  Accessibility experience must come from another evidenced project or remain a
  development area.
- WOW List does not by itself establish formal agency product ownership,
  government policy or legal review, procurement, executive-briefing authority,
  or work inside a regulated government production environment.
- The record supports onboarding, facilitation, and distributed leadership, not
  yet formal product hiring, personnel management, or PM mentorship claims.
- Jamie controls application wording and submission. OTI controls eligibility,
  assessment, interviews, references, and selection.

## Collective credit

Keep Jamie's agency visible and keep the system's distributed authorship
visible. Richard Caceres retains co-builder credit. Guest writers, editors, list
maintainers, facilitators, organizers, venues, and participants helped make WOW
List socially operative. Do not turn leadership into sole creation or treat
community activity as Jamie's individual outcome.
