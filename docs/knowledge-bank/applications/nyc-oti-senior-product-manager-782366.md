## Reusable workflow learning

1. **Keep the tailored resume authoritative.** Automated profile parsing
   recovered broad chronology but distorted at least one title/company split,
   introduced unsupported date precision, flattened punctuation, and
   misclassified professional development as education.
2. **Prefer the manual application route when the form permits it.** Leaving
   optional structured chronology blank preserved the reviewed resume as the
   consistent account instead of repairing a degraded duplicate.
3. **Inspect the complete rendered application.** Long-page captures made
   required fields, optional sections, attachment state, and validation errors
   reviewable without treating the capture as a publication artifact.
4. **Reserve legal attestations and submission for Jamie.** Research,
   drafting, comparison, and quality assurance can be delegated; truth and
   accuracy certification, terms acceptance, privacy consent, and submission
   remain human-authorized actions.
5. **Separate package quality from hiring outcome.** A strong application is a
   controlled input. Eligibility screening, interview decisions, selection,
   and team fit remain external outcomes.

## Next-stage operating loop

1. **Confirmation:** Jamie privately preserves the confirmation email and any
   employer-assigned reference; neither enters the public repository.
2. **Qualification:** assemble a truthful, date-bounded education and employment
   chronology for any civil-service eligibility review, keeping unknown hours,
   overlap, and title equivalencies explicit.
3. **Interview preparation:** prepare concise stories for resident discovery,
   version-one scoping, cross-functional delivery, accessibility and privacy
   decisions, public launch, measurement, and post-launch learning.
4. **Follow-up:** conduct respectful, role-relevant outreach without implying a
   private relationship, referral, endorsement, or knowledge of the hiring
   process that the public record does not establish.
5. **Outcome review:** keep employer correspondence and status tracking private.
   Add any later public-safe outcome only after Jamie explicitly approves its
   wording and publication boundary.
6. **Portfolio learning:** after an interview, rejection, offer, or withdrawal,
   capture what the process revealed about evidence gaps and reader needs
   without rewriting history to make the outcome seem inevitable.

Success for this system is not one guaranteed result. It is a repeatable
practice that gets strong truthful applications submitted on time, preserves
Jamie and collaborators' dignity, improves from observed experience, and keeps
Jamie ready for the next human conversation.
