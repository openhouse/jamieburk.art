## Public-Safe Reconstruction

Civic Hall announced a January 30, 2016, 1-3 p.m. New York City Council
hackathon focused on constituent services. A Council event-day post identified
the Civic Hall gathering as its first CouncilStat hackathon, while a
Council-branded graphic read "New York City Council Hackathon."

Jamie's protected participant archive separately records a visible breakout
prompt, "Digital District - Help improve City Council District office
operations," at approximately 2:10 p.m. The time is a photograph timestamp,
not an event start time. The photograph, private metadata, filename, and
depicted identities remain unpublished.

Politico New York independently reported that Jamie encountered CouncilStat
data at a Council hackathon at Civic Hall in January 2016 and developed CallNYC
after the full dataset became public. The article also reported that Council
offices used CouncilStat in different ways and with different frequency. The
portfolio therefore treats CallNYC as an independent, archived, unofficial
follow-on prototype and does not turn raw case counts into office scores.

## Negative Search

The inquiry reviewed:

- 4,630 deduplicated HTML captures;
- 1,240 original URLs;
- 296 distinct event URL keys;
- 215 successful event pages;
- 74 redirects;
- 7 captured 404 responses.

No dedicated Civic Hall calendar or event-detail page was recovered in this
research. The result describes the searched Wayback/CDX corpus; it does not
prove that no page ever existed.

The search also did not recover a full agenda, a full schedule beyond the
announced window, a breakout roster, a complete attendee or facilitator list,
registration-form contents, formal winners, or a published project roster.
