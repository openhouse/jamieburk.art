# NYC Artist Coalition

NYC Artist Coalition formed in 2017 as a collective public-facing structure for
artists, cultural-space operators, advocates, and collaborators to share
knowledge, identify priorities, convene public meetings, and act around safety,
nightlife, enforcement, rent, and the survival of small cultural spaces.

Jamie's direct contribution included co-founding the coalition; creating its
logo and campaign website family; establishing the shared public identity used
across web and social surfaces; helping build recurring event and participation
formats; preparing public testimony; and supporting source-backed campaign and
coordination systems. Teammates sustained, authored through, and expanded that
identity over time.

## What became usable

- one recognizable identity across coalition and campaign work;
- public action routes, guides, hearing context, and press collections;
- recurring meetings and town halls that could move from issue discovery to
  follow-through;
- source-backed records that City agencies and Council members could reference
  in public engagement and oversight.

## Present-grounded source return

The July 19, 2026 encounter revisited the authenticated shared-folder census,
selected original campaign working records, official Council transcripts, and
the surviving public campaign sites. The record supports Jamie's substantial
infrastructure and implementation role. It does not support sole authorship,
sole campaign ownership, or individual causality for collective policy
outcomes.
