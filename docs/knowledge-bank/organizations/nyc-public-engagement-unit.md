# NYC Public Engagement Unit

PEU is a City outreach and service-navigation unit. Its official public
description emphasizes meeting New Yorkers where they are, using
community-organizing practices and proactive contact rather than expecting
residents to find their own way through a complex bureaucracy.

The September 2026 Chief Strategy Officer posting describes an organization
with more than 100 outreach staff and partnerships spanning City Hall, the
Office of Mass Engagement, DSS, HPD, DOF, NYC Public Schools, OTI, and other
agencies. That is posting-specific organizational context, not a complete
staffing census or proof that every named entity has the same relationship to
every PEU project.
