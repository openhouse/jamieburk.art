# NYC Civic Engagement Commission

The [official mandate](../sources/nyc-cec-about-and-governance-2026-10-18.md) connects civic participation to resident decision-making, language access, community partnerships and support for community boards. CEC was established through the 2018 Charter revision.

This **Civic Engagement Commission** is distinct from NYC Public Schools' Community Education Councils, which also use the acronym CEC.

The [public staff reading](../sources/nyc-cec-staff-2026-10-18.md) maps program, technology, budget and operations functions. The [Programs Project Manager opportunity](../opportunities/oti-cec-programs-project-manager-797003.md) is a separate record: an OTI posting label does not by itself establish a full CEC/OTI organization chart.

## Relationship boundary

September 8, 2026 Executive Order 24 gives the [Office of Mass Engagement](nyc-office-of-mass-engagement.md) a liaison and activity-review role with CEC. The graph uses a qualified related relationship, not an unsupported direct reporting line. Public institutional context does not prove a personal connection, eligibility, hiring authority or an application outcome.
