# CEC Digital Equity Team

[Dated official evidence](../sources/nyc-cec-digital-equity-2026-10-18.md) describes a team formed in fall 2024, with CIO Oscar Romero leading technology, design, communications and analysis capacity across CEC programs. Sonia Putzel's official biography identifies her current published role as its Technology Strategist.

The team's connection to The People's Money includes online participation through Decidim. Historical platform and hosting descriptions remain dated, not assertions that today's complete architecture or security posture has been audited. Program coordination with this team is not a proven reporting line for the job.
