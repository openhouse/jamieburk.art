# NYC Office of Mass Engagement

[Executive Order 24, September 8, 2026, section 15](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/09/executive-order-no--24) distinguishes the entities that report to the Office of Mass Engagement from its relationship to CEC: OME maintains liaison with and reviews CEC's activities.

The [Public Engagement Unit](nyc-public-engagement-unit.md) is separately named among the reporting entities. This node preserves that distinction. It does not infer OME staff involvement in the CEC Programs Project Manager recruitment.
