# Circle O

Circle O is a dance-centered organization founded and led by Kayla Hamilton.
Its public description connects education, consulting, performance and global
exchange and centers Black Disabled and multiply marginalized artists.

The organization's phrase "relationships are the infrastructure" is especially
relevant to the Operations Manager opening: the role is designed to make the
calendar, coordination, knowledge, communication and program systems around
those relationships dependable.

## Boundary

Public mission alignment does not establish a relationship with Jamie,
application status, endorsement, selection or employment outcome.
