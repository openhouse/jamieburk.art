# Interpretive Eval Lenses

These two portfolio evals were derived by Jamie Burkart and Codex on July 15,
2026, from protected educational records Jamie supplied and a user-supplied
image of a public professional recommendation. They are contemporary editorial
instruments. They are not quotations, testimonials, or rubrics written,
approved, or currently endorsed by Margaret Morse or Warren Sack.

The source records remain protected. This repository does not contain their
payloads, archival locators, educational identifiers, private correspondence,
contact details, or screenshots. The records inform the public-safe questions
below; they are not themselves public proof.

## Margaret Morse Lens

**Question:** Does the portfolio preserve Jamie's embodied, artistic, and
relational intelligence while keeping the hiring argument clear?

Required signals:

- Artistic and embodied inquiry appears as a way of knowing and making, not as
  an ornamental biography or a failed version of prose.
- Experimentation, participation, memory, place, atmosphere, hospitality, and
  how people inhabit a structure remain visible concerns.
- At least one threshold keeps artistic, civic, technical, and social practice
  visibly connected.
- The portfolio explains how these concerns improve Jamie's operating work
  without reducing every relationship or experiment to organizational utility.

Failure modes:

- The portfolio presents only the competent operator and deletes the artist,
  host, investigator, or participant.
- Artistic language is atmospheric but cannot be connected to a method,
  decision, interface, gathering, or usable system.
- The interpretive layer obscures the role Jamie seeks or the work he can own.
- Historical praise is presented as a professor-authored current endorsement.

Scoring:

- `0`: absent, source-unsafe, or falsely attributed.
- `1`: the artistic history is named but detached from present practice.
- `2`: a connection is suggested, but it remains abstract or ornamental.
- `3`: the public candidate visibly connects embodied inquiry to present
  operating method while retaining a clear hiring path.
- `4`: an independent reviewer finds the connection specific, memorable, and
  integral without additional explanation.

## Warren Sack Lens

**Question:** Does the portfolio demonstrate Jamie's recursive systems thinking
as a movement from relational observation to working form and social use?

Required signals:

- Jamie follows relationships across systems and makes a latent structure
  visible through source-backed analysis.
- Analysis becomes a working interface, prototype, process, representation, or
  facilitated social situation.
- The work is tested or encountered in a real setting, allowing use to produce
  further learning.
- Multiple representations serve the problem when useful: code, diagrams,
  prose, interfaces, models, images, events, or operational records.
- Collective contribution and technical authorship remain calibrated.

Failure modes:

- The portfolio claims systems thinking without showing a relationship,
  transformation, or test.
- Technical novelty is detached from sources, people, or situated use.
- A prototype is treated as adoption or impact without evidence.
- Collaborative work is absorbed into individual invention.
- Historical praise is presented as a professor-authored current endorsement.

Scoring:

- `0`: absent, source-unsafe, or falsely attributed.
- `1`: systems language appears without a demonstrable sequence.
- `2`: analysis and making are both present but their relationship is unclear.
- `3`: the public candidate shows a credible sequence from relational analysis
  to working form, situated use, and bounded learning.
- `4`: an independent reviewer can reconstruct that sequence from specific
  evidence and finds it distinctive without additional explanation.

## Holdout Protocol

The local runner performs only a structural preflight and may award at most
`3`. A score of `4`, and completion of either eval, requires an independent
reviewer who did not make the patch. The reviewer receives the unchanged
candidate, this frozen rubric, and no explanation of the intended revision.
