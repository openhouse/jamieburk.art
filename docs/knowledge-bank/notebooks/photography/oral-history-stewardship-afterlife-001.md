# Oral history prompt 001: Stewardship afterlife

## Present state

On 2026-07-26, one photograph from the previously verified private editorial
field was selected for attention. It is visible to Jamie in the working
conversation but is not stored in this repository.

The frame visibly shows two adults standing together inside an unfinished
brick interior. One wears a hard hat and holds a flashlight. The other carries
keys and a portable light. A hand rests on a shoulder. Exposed wood, brick, and
openings situate the encounter in a building under care or inspection.

Private archive context provisionally associates the scene with KC Town Hall
and a later phase of stewardship. That association is a retrieval hypothesis,
not a conclusion established by the photograph.

## Why ask now

The existing KC Town Hall record distinguishes Phase One delivery from the
later mission-aligned transition and keeps the surrounding family crisis
private. This photograph may help Jamie describe the professional substance of
the handoff: relationship, stewardship, building knowledge, continuity, and
what another organization was prepared to carry forward.

The useful question is not whether the photograph proves a success. It is what
Jamie remembers that can guide a return to records, collaborator knowledge,
and appropriately bounded public language.

## Invitation to Jamie

Please respond in your own voice, as freely or briefly as feels right:

1. What moment are we looking at, and what do you remember before and after it?
2. Who is present, what was each person's relationship to the project, and how
   should collective credit be handled?
3. What had changed in the building and the project since Phase One?
4. What made the receiving organization or stewardship relationship
   mission-aligned?
5. What knowledge, responsibility, access, or possibility were you trying to
   carry across the transition?
6. What became possible afterward, and what remained unfinished or unknown?
7. What part of this history should remain private?
8. Who made the photograph, and whose permission would be needed before any
   portfolio use?

Jamie may answer questions the prompt did not know to ask. Silence, uncertainty,
correction, and refusal are valid responses.

## Intake contract

Jamie's response will enter as a dated first-person recollection source. It
will not automatically become:

- a verified claim;
- a public caption or alt text;
- identification or consent for another depicted person;
- creator credit, rights, or license evidence;
- proof of project outcome, causation, or endorsement; or
- approval to copy the photograph into the portfolio.

A subsequent archival pass should decompose the response into atomic
observations, distinguish memory from visible observation, seek corroborating
records and collaborator knowledge, preserve counterevidence, and keep private
context protected. Claims may mature only through the normal Knowledge Wiki
lifecycle and human gates.

## Protected boundary

The photograph, filename, source and collection identifiers, local paths,
previews, People associations, exact date and location metadata, raw oral
history, private names, and rights records remain outside public Git. This page
contains no reconstructive locator.

## Current disposition

- Oral history: requested, not yet received.
- Claim state: none created.
- Portfolio state: held.
- Rights and consent: unresolved.
- Public image: not added.
- Next action: receive Jamie's account, then decide what requires corroboration,
  collaborator review, protection, or no further action.
