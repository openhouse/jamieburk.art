# Photography notebook templates

Use these only for notes that are already safe to commit publicly. Private image
identities, reconnecting locators, people tags, rights-holder details, and
sensitive context belong in the private field workspace.

## Image close-reading note

```markdown
# [Opaque photo ID or public-safe working title]

- Field version:
- Editorial state: keep exploring | sequence candidate | defer | protected | decline
- Related governed records:

## What is visibly present

Describe only what a viewer can responsibly observe in the frame.

## What metadata reports

Record only reviewed, public-safe metadata. Say when metadata is missing,
approximate, or conflicting.

## What Jamie or a collaborator remembers

Treat memory as attributed context and a research lead, not automatic proof.

## Editorial interpretation

Name possible meanings, formal qualities, tensions, and competing readings.

## Research leads

List questions, sources to locate, people to consult, and possible Wiki intake.

## Supported claim link

Link only to a separately governed claim whose evidence relationship is already
recorded. Otherwise write: none yet.

## Care and publication boundaries

- Rights state: unknown | review needed | cleared for a specified use | do not publish
- Consent state: unknown | review needed | cleared for a specified use | do not publish
- Represented-person concerns:
- Protected context or absence:
- Approved public surface: none unless Jamie records a separate approval
```

## Sequence experiment

```markdown
# [Sequence name and version]

- Question being tested:
- Included opaque photo IDs:
- Ordering rationale:
- What becomes legible:
- What disappears or is overdetermined:
- Collective-credit and represented-person review:
- Contradictions and outliers retained:
- Next revision:
- Publication status: hold
```

## Field-version review

```markdown
# [Field version] review

- Exact private manifest receipt:
- Target and verified counts:
- Selection method and known blind spots:
- Projects, periods, places, and scene types reached:
- Material absences:
- New motifs or vocabulary:
- Claims or project pages opened for research:
- Rights, consent, and safety queue:
- What should change in the next field:
- Human editorial decision: continue | branch | freeze | supersede
```

These templates preserve a path from seeing to research without collapsing
seeing into knowing. A notebook entry can remain valuable even when it never
becomes a public caption, claim, or image.
