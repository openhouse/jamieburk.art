# Field Corpus 001

## Present state

Jamie plans to assemble an exploratory rough-draft selection of approximately
1,000 photographs from a lifetime archive he describes as containing more than
600,000 photographs. Neither number is an independently frozen source count.

The private field has not yet been frozen or ingested. No photographs, source
identifiers, image-level metadata, or private encounter notes are recorded on
this page.

This field is selected for attention, not publication. It is not a
representative sample, completeness claim, evidence set, shortlist, or promise
that any included photograph will become public.

## Working premise

The first pass may be creative, intuitive, experimental, and revisable. Its
purpose is to create enough shared material for close reading and discovery,
including discoveries that the present portfolio language cannot foresee.

The selection may include:

- sequences and near-duplicates;
- photographs before and after an apparent event;
- ordinary labor, maintenance, hospitality, weather, waiting, and repair;
- images that attract, trouble, surprise, remember, or remain hard to place;
- scenes in which Jamie is not visible; and
- material that resists the current project taxonomy or professional story.

No coverage quota is required for a project, period, person, place, or existing
portfolio claim.

## Provisional visual grammar

This is an opening hypothesis, not a classification system:

> threshold -> container -> shared labor -> encounter -> confluence ->
> transformation -> afterlife

For the waterways work, Jamie has proposed another provisional movement:

> release yourself -> onto the water -> until it tastes of salt

These phrases may guide looking, but the photographs are allowed to complicate,
contradict, or replace them.

## First close-reading questions

- Where can a stranger see the choice to enter, stay, leave, or participate?
- What material infrastructure makes care, safety, access, or belonging
  possible?
- Where do participants remain individual agents rather than illustrations of
  an abstract community?
- What work occurs at the edge of the frame, between official events, or after
  an apparent conclusion?
- Which sequences reveal duration, maintenance, changing relationships, or a
  project moving outside its original banks?
- Which photographs exert pressure on Jamie's current account of his work?
- What does the field repeatedly make visible that the Knowledge Wiki does not
  yet know how to ask about?

## Sequence sketches

No photograph sequence has been established. When sequences emerge, record
what becomes perceptible across images that cannot be supported by one frame
alone. Preserve alternate edits and disagreement rather than treating the
first coherent arrangement as inevitable.

## Negative space and counterreadings

The first pass should notice what is missing as well as what is present:

- periods or practices Jamie expected but did not encounter;
- people or forms of labor made peripheral by the camera;
- events whose surviving images privilege institutions over participants;
- photographs whose remembered context conflicts with visible evidence; and
- beautiful or emotionally powerful images that do not support the proposed
  interpretation.

Absence from this bounded field does not establish absence from the lifetime
archive or from the historical situation.

## Research leads

Image-derived leads begin as questions. Before a factual proposition matures,
separate visible observation from memory, seek corroborating sources and
collaborator knowledge, preserve counterevidence, and resolve agency and credit
scope. A photograph alone cannot establish identity, consent, authorship,
causation, endorsement, or the complete meaning of an event.

No research leads have yet been produced from the private field.

## Publication gates

All gates remain open:

- [ ] exact source and version identity;
- [ ] photographer and credit;
- [ ] depicted-person consent where applicable;
- [ ] rights and license;
- [ ] privacy and safety;
- [ ] supported context and prohibited inferences;
- [ ] approved crop and edit limits;
- [ ] caption and alt text;
- [ ] exact destination and duration; and
- [ ] Jamie's final editorial approval.

Selection, aesthetic force, evidentiary usefulness, or prior public appearance
cannot close these gates.
