# Open questions for photographic reading

These questions are prompts for looking, not a required taxonomy. They may be
revised, contradicted, combined, or abandoned as the photographic field begins
to answer back.

## Invitation and threshold

- Where can we see the moment a person chooses whether to enter?
- What makes the boundary of a project legible without making it closed?
- Which images give a stranger enough orientation to imagine participating?

## Container and care

- Where is safety, maintenance, preparation, hosting, or repair visible?
- What forms of care happen before the event and after attention moves away?
- Where is Jamie absent or off-center while stewardship remains perceptible?
- Can love become visible as room made for another person's agency rather than
  as sentiment alone?

## Multiplicity and agency

- Which scenes keep many people active rather than converting them into an
  audience around one protagonist?
- Whose labor or knowledge is outside the frame?
- What image would a represented person recognize as dignified and true enough
  to their own agency?
- Where do collaboration, friendship, disagreement, and interdependence remain
  distinct rather than becoming one uncomplicated story?

## Duration and transformation

- Which photographs show waiting, repetition, seasonal change, repair, delay,
  handoff, or return?
- What changes when images are read as a sequence instead of individually?
- Which consequences continued beyond Jamie's direct involvement?
- Where does a project's apparent ending become another person's beginning?

## Scale and ecology

- How can intimate gestures coexist with the scale of a watershed, city,
  institution, neighborhood, or network?
- Where do physical and interpersonal dynamics become visible together?
- Which recurring forms connect projects without proving causality or a single
  predetermined lineage?

## Friction and counter-reading

- Which photographs resist the professional story the portfolio currently
  tells?
- What scenes are ambiguous, uncomfortable, funny, technically imperfect, or
  difficult to classify but remain alive?
- Which images invite incompatible readings that should remain unresolved?
- What can the photograph not tell us, even when it feels persuasive?

## Editorial and ethical return

- Is an image functioning as evidence, atmosphere, character, transition,
  inquiry, contradiction, or none of these yet?
- What public context would be required to prevent an extractive or misleading
  reading?
- Who must participate before an image can be credited, captioned, cropped, or
  displayed?
- What deserves protection, non-identification, or permanent absence?
