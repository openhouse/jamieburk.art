# Sunday Dinner map-table oral-history prompt

## Reading date and reader

On July 26, 2026, an AI-assisted photo-editor pass returned to one image inside
a locally verified private editor field. The photograph, catalog coordinate,
filename, source path, and represented-person identities remain outside public
Git. The image is shown privately to Jamie in the accompanying conversation so
he may decide whether and how to respond.

Jamie's oral history has not yet been recorded. No answer is presumed below.

## Visible observation

Several adults gather closely around a dining table. One person stands with
raised arms and papers while other people look, laugh, or gesture. Food,
drinking vessels, a candle, and a large hand-drawn diagram resembling connected
waterways or city geography are visible.

The pixels do not establish the gathering's name, date, location, participants,
photographer, purpose, sequence, or relationship to Jamie's professional work.

## Felt response and interpretation

The image suggests a room in which hospitality, performance, geography, and
shared attention are active at once. It may offer a visual language for the way
a meal can become a place of imagination and participation. This is a
curatorial reading, not a fact carried by the photograph.

## Counter-reading

The scene may primarily belong to the people pictured as friendship, home life,
celebration, or an unrepeatable private moment. Turning it into professional
evidence could diminish that meaning or make represented people serve as
scenery around Jamie's story.

## What the frame withholds

The image does not show the preparation and cleanup, who made the diagram, what
was being said, how the group gathered, what happened before or after, who
pressed the shutter, or what each represented person would want remembered,
credited, corrected, or protected.

## Oral-history invitation for Jamie

- What is happening in this photograph?
- What do you remember immediately before and after this moment?
- What is the hand-drawn geography, and who made or used it?
- What role, if any, did this gathering play in Sunday Dinner or another
  project?
- What did you contribute, and what was made possible by other people?
- Who made the photograph?
- Which people should participate before the image or story is described
  publicly?
- What should remain private even if it would strengthen the portfolio?
- What do you see now that you could not have said at the time?

## Evidence lanes after a response

Jamie's answer should first become a dated first-person source with its
uncertainty intact. Visible observation, first-person memory, collaborator
confirmation, and public-source corroboration remain separate evidence lanes.
A recollection may open research and change interpretation; it does not
authorize a caption, claim, asset record, or site change.

## Publication gates

Default: **hold**. Any future public occurrence requires separate review of
photographer and rights, represented-person dignity and consent, collective
credit, sensitivity, exact derivative, crop, caption, alt text, destination,
audience, and Jamie's approval. A private oral-history conversation does not
alter the existing protected-absence decision for intimate gatherings.

## Next action

Invite Jamie's oral history from the privately displayed image. Preserve his
response as a new dated source only after he reviews the transcription and
public-safe boundary. Make no automatic portfolio change.
