# Photography field notebook

This is a public-safe working notebook and sketchpad for photographic
fieldwork. It is a place to preserve questions, provisional visual grammars,
sequence ideas, counterreadings, and public-safe changes in understanding.

It is intentionally more exploratory than a claim page and less resolved than
a portfolio composition. Everything written here is still in a public
repository and must be safe to quote in a newspaper.

The governing proposal is
[RFC 0002: Lifetime Photo Archive Fieldwork and Selective Publication](../../../../rfcs/0002-lifetime-photo-archive-fieldwork.md).
The RFC remains proposed. This notebook does not authorize archive access,
private-workspace implementation, image ingestion, or publication.

## Working agreement

- Select for attention, not publication.
- Treat visible observations, memories, interpretations, and supported facts
  as different things.
- Let sequences, near-duplicates, ordinary fragments, anomalies, and
  unclassified images remain available for thought.
- Do not require every photograph to represent a known project or place Jamie
  in the frame.
- Record uncertainty as a question. Do not infer identity, consent, authorship,
  causation, relationship, or sensitive traits from an image alone.
- Revise additively and date each encounter so that changed readings remain
  visible.
- Keep exact source identifiers, filenames, paths, previews, contact sheets,
  faces, raw OCR, EXIF, GPS, and private rights records outside public Git.

## Four layers

| Layer | Purpose | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lifetime source records | Preserve the authoritative originals, edits, metadata, and pre-existing organization. | Private and read-only for fieldwork; outside this repository. |
| Private field corpus | Hold the versioned selection, source bindings, previews, image-level working notes, and additive workspace collections. | Private working space; workspace collections may add membership pointers but may not alter source records or pre-existing organization. Selection is not publication clearance. |
| Public-safe notebook | Retain aggregate observations, questions, sequence sketches, limitations, and changes in interpretation. | This directory; no protected pixels or reconstructive locators. |
| Selective public projection | Compose the smallest sufficient set for a particular reader and purpose. | Separate human review of the exact photograph, crop, caption, credit, alt text, and destination. |

Movement between layers is never automatic. A photograph may be useful to
think with and still remain private forever.

## Notebook practice

After a private viewing session, add a dated encounter to the relevant field
page:

1. Name the question and field version encountered.
2. Separate what is visibly observable from remembered context.
3. Record recurring forms, sequences, surprises, contradictions, and absences.
4. Note which prior reading changed and which interpretation remains open.
5. Route factual propositions to a research inquiry for corroboration rather
   than promoting the photograph into proof.
6. Name protected people or contexts only in the private workspace. Record the
   public-safe boundary here.
7. End with the next looking or research question, not a forced conclusion.

## Entry contract

A public-safe field page should preserve:

- encounter date and private field version, expressed without a source locator;
- current question;
- visible patterns and sequence sketches;
- anomalies and images that resist the current account;
- prior readings consulted and changed interpretations;
- uncertainty, counterevidence, and prohibited inferences;
- research leads and public-safe collaborator questions;
- rights, consent, credit, safety, context, and accessibility holds; and
- the next bounded pass.

An empty section is an honest state. It must not be filled with invented
observations merely to make the notebook look complete.
