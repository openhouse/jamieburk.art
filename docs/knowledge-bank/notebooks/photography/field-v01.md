# Photography field v01

## State

`v01` is assembling. Its target population is 1,000 photographs. The current
committed public-safe notebook count is zero because no private photo identity,
preview, or note has yet passed into this repository. This does not measure work
that may be underway in a private curation environment.

The field will become a bounded working population only when a private manifest
and its verification receipt agree on the same 1,000 stable photo identities.
That receipt will establish which field was read. It will not establish archive
completeness, editorial quality, rights, consent, or publication readiness.

## Purpose

Create enough room to encounter the archive before compressing it into a public
argument. This pass should include obvious project anchors as well as intervals,
apparatus, thresholds, maintenance, travel, weather, empty places, aftermaths,
and images whose relevance is not yet understood.

The accepted [residency proposal](residency-proposal-v01.md) is an invitation
to begin, not a contract for what the field must become. The method and eventual
form may change in response to the photographs.

## Assembly guardrails

- Do not optimize for a predetermined portfolio story or numerical aesthetic score.
- Do not describe this as the best, top, complete, or representative 1,000.
- Preserve technical failures and quiet frames when they hold editorial possibility.
- Avoid redundant near-duplicates while retaining meaningful temporal sequences.
- Do not use people tags, face recognition, or inferred identity as public metadata.
- Keep original assets and reconnecting locators in the private field workspace.
- Carry forward safety holds without treating them as editorial rejection.

## First close-reading questions

- Where does someone encounter a threshold and decide whether to enter?
- What makes the project container legible, sound, welcoming, and revisable?
- Where can care be seen in preparation, maintenance, repair, or attention?
- Where do participants alter what the project becomes?
- Which sequences preserve several voices without forcing consensus?
- What changes across embarkation, duration, interruption, and afterlife?
- What remains outside the frame, and what should remain protected?
- Which images surprise or contradict the Wiki's current account?

## Completion boundary

Field assembly is complete only when the exact private population, version,
count, and verification receipt agree. Close reading, sequencing, factual
research, rights review, represented-person care, Jamie's editorial approval,
and public composition remain later and independent gates.
