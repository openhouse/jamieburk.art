# Release / water / salt - visual sequence sketch

This is a working interpretation supplied by Jamie during the photography
workstream on July 22, 2026. It is not a verified chronology, a fixed portfolio
sequence, or a claim that every participant experienced the project in the
same way.

> Release yourself onto the water until it tastes of salt.

The phrase proposes three movements. Photography may test, complicate, or
replace this structure.

## Release yourself

The first movement is a threshold: approach, preparation, invitation, choice,
departure, and uncertainty. Possible images might show a vessel touching shore,
someone deciding whether to board, materials being assembled, or a space made
ready before participants arrive.

The editorial question is not simply "How did the trip begin?" It is: what
conditions made entry imaginable, legible, and safe enough to choose?

## Onto the water

The second movement is shared duration: motion, waiting, maintenance, weather,
conflict, repair, play, exhaustion, food, navigation, and relations changing
under physical conditions no individual controls.

Jamie has described his role as caring for the container rather than serving
as captain or sole author. This remains first-person interpretation requiring
project-specific corroboration before it supports public factual claims.

Possible montage may place visible operational work beside unplanned social
life. The apparatus and the emerging community belong in the same frame, but
neither should be reduced to proof of the other.

## Until it tastes of salt

The third movement is transformation and afterlife: arrival, continuation,
handoff, gifts, changed relationships, new homes, and consequences that exceed
the originating plan.

Salt need not function as a triumphant ending. It can mark changed conditions,
another threshold, or the point at which the project can no longer determine
what its participants make of it.

## Love as infrastructure

A working proposition for the photographic layer is that love may become
visible through active accommodation: a safe container, food, repair, an open
seat, an adjusted life jacket, a person listening, a gift left behind, or work
continuing after Jamie leaves the frame.

This is an interpretive lens, not a factual label for a person's emotion or a
claim that every project relation was harmonious.

## Montage questions

- Does the sequence make room for participants to remain agents?
- Can it show the container without making operations the only protagonist?
- Where does the river, weather, building, room, or city act on the project?
- What changes when intimate care and geographic scale appear beside one
  another?
- Which image interrupts this three-part structure and demands a different one?

## Hold

No photograph is selected or cleared by this sketch. Image identity, sequence,
rights, consent, credit, caption, crop, alt text, and public surface remain open
human decisions.
