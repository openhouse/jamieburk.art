# Layout study D

## Status

Jamie authorized a bounded branch experiment in which seven
metadata-minimized photographic derivatives enter the portfolio layout. The
experiment tests the total visual experience before production approval. It
does not publish the private 1,000-photo editor field, expose reconnecting
identifiers, or establish permission for reuse outside this portfolio.

The photographs are active in the branch application for Jamie's explicitly
authorized layout review. Every image remains on production hold. This notebook
is a non-authoritative editorial record with no power to clear rights, consent,
credit, factual claims, or production publication.

## Question

How can photography help a stranger understand that Jamie's operational work
takes place among people, in physical and civic settings, and through forms of
preparation and care, while the portfolio remains clear enough for a hiring
reader to use quickly?

Public project-screen captures remain distinct from photographs. They show

## Compositional findings

1. **Use photographs as landmarks.** The hearing room, river, kitchen,
   worksite, and cultural venue give a reader distinct places from which to
   understand the work.
2. **Keep the hiring route explicit.** Role language, selected work, résumé,
   and contact remain available before the images ask for a deeper reading.
3. **Let one image change the register.** The raft section interrupts the
   project-card rhythm and connects artistic inquiry to operational
   stewardship without making every project illustrate the river.
4. **Show labor around the event.** Preparation, repair, listening, and
   maintenance matter alongside public moments.
5. **Do not make every surface photographic.** The résumé, proof strip,
   capabilities, references, and evidence-heavy sections remain quieter
   reading tools.

## Human review still required

- Jamie's final editorial approval of every placement and crop;
- image-by-image rights and credit review;
- represented-person dignity, context, and consent review where appropriate;
- caption and factual review against the Knowledge Wiki;
- mobile, zoom, keyboard, and assistive-technology review;
- production observation after deployment.

Application readiness does not imply production approval. A photograph may be
removed, replaced, protected, or returned to the private field without being
judged a failed image.
