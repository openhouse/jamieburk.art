# Provisional photography vocabulary

These terms are lenses, not tags that every photograph must satisfy and not a
deterministic scoring system. Their meanings may split, merge, or be retired as
the field teaches us how it wants to be read.

## Threshold

A moment of invitation or choice: shoreline, doorway, gangplank, meeting
entrance, empty chair, form, sign, or another passage between outside and inside.

## Container

The social and technical apparatus that gives participation enough stability to
develop: vessel, room, website, schedule, scaffolding, table, route, protocol,
identity system, or repeated gathering.

## Care

Preparation, attention, repair, feeding, maintenance, safety, accommodation,
translation, and the ordinary work of keeping a shared situation inhabitable.

## Agency

A participant choosing, making, refusing, revising, leading, teaching, or
changing the project's course. Agency is not inferred from expression alone.

## Polyphony

Several voices, uses, tempos, or perspectives remaining perceptible together.
The goal is not to make difference look frictionless.

## Transformation

A visible change in person, place, relation, artifact, or shared capacity. The
image may document change without proving what caused it.

## Afterlife

What continues after the named event or Jamie's direct stewardship: a handoff,
habit, relationship, place, reused system, memory, or new work carried by others.

## Invitation

Evidence that there is room for another person in the story. Invitation includes
the material conditions that make entry possible, not merely welcoming language.

## Boundary

The edge of the project's world, the limits of the evidence, or a deliberate
decision not to show. Boundaries can protect people while preserving the fact
that something consequential remains outside the frame.

## Apparatus

The usually backgrounded infrastructure of collective experience: tools,
knots, maps, cables, forms, vehicles, kitchens, repair, setup, and cleanup.

## Outlier

An image that resists the current vocabulary or project story. Outliers remain
available because contradiction and surprise are information, not failed scores.
