# Public-safe photographic field-note method

Use this form only for notes safe to publish in this repository. Do not include
the photograph, a private asset identifier, filename, source path, coordinate,
protected identity, or private relationship detail. Keep asset-level working
notes in the separately governed private layer.

Not every heading needs an answer. `Unknown`, `held`, `contradictory`, and
`not understood yet` are valid states.

## Entry template

### Working title

Use a provisional phrase, not a factual conclusion.

### Reading date and reader

Identify this encounter with the material. Do not imply eyewitness authority
for the photographed event.

### Visible observation

Describe only what can be responsibly observed. Avoid inferred identity,
emotion, relationship, intention, authorship, chronology, or causality.

### Felt response and interpretation

Record what the image evokes or suggests. Mark it explicitly as a reading,
not a fact carried by the pixels.

### Possible relations

List tentative project, place, event, artifact, gesture, or motif relations.
A possible relation is a research lead, not evidence.

### Counter-reading

Describe at least one materially different interpretation. Note who might read
the scene differently and why.

### What the frame withholds

Record important absences, off-frame labor, missing voices, uncertain sequence,
or context the image cannot establish.

### People, dignity, and collective credit

Ask whose agency is visible, whose work may be hidden, and whether the proposed
reading turns anyone into scenery, evidence, endorsement, or a type.

### Research questions

Return questions of identity, date, place, project, authorship, role, and event
meaning to source research. Do not solve them from appearance alone.

### Editorial possibility

Describe what becomes possible if the image is present and what a stranger
would need in order to enter the sequence without being told what to feel.

### Publication gates

Default: **hold**. Review photographer and rights holder, represented-person
concerns, consent, collective credit, sensitivity, crop, caption, alt text,
context, exact derivative, and named surface. Jamie approval is required, and
other people may hold authority for a particular use.

### Next action

Choose one: keep in the field, revisit, research, seek human context, develop a
brief, hold, protect, or remove from consideration. None of these states
authorizes publication.
