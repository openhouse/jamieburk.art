# Publication Rules

The public site is a projection of this knowledge bank.

## Homepage

Use short, approved or softened claims that a busy reader can understand in one
pass. Lead with role, operating structure, and public-facing technical work.

Do not use active-project details, unresolved citations, dense metrics, or
approval-gated exact wording.

## Work Index

Each card should answer:

- What kind of work was it?
- What did Jamie make usable?
- Why does it matter for role fit?
- What boundary remains protected?

## Case Studies

Case studies may carry more nuance than the homepage, but they should still
avoid source dumping. Use role, public-safe context, what was unclear, what
became usable, artifacts by type, source class, and protected boundaries.

## Technical Operations

Use claims that map to delivery coordination, risk surfacing, planning rhythms,
status reporting, decision records, onboarding, runbooks, operating
documentation, stakeholder updates, public-facing launches, retrospectives, and
durable handoffs.

## Lab

Source-Backed Team Memory is a bounded lab / method / consulting practice for
V1.

Use:

> AI drafts. Humans review. The shared record remains inspectable and
> correctable.

Do not present it as production SaaS, broad ingestion, private archive
browsing, or a replacement for human judgment.

## Resume

Use the approved PDF at the stable path. The PDF may include phone if Jamie has
approved that version. Do not put phone in HTML unless separately approved.

## Metadata

Use role, operating structure, and selected proof language only. Avoid dense
metrics, active-project detail, and unresolved citations.

## Colophon

Explain the public-safety and proof standard, not private evidence.

## Redirects And Sitemap

The sitemap should include canonical live URLs only. Redirect sources, resume
PDFs, draft routes, and noncanonical work routes should stay out of the
sitemap.
