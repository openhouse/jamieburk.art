# Publication Rules

A claim is publishable only if it is true enough to trust, safe enough to
publish, clear enough for a hiring reader, and bounded enough to avoid
overclaiming.

## Homepage

Use the clearest cross-project throughline and a few selected proof points. Do
not turn the homepage into a metrics wall or knowledge-bank browser.

## Work Index

Work cards should answer what was unclear, what became usable, what Jamie did,
and why it matters. Use collective-work language where appropriate.

## Case-Study Pages

Case studies may carry more nuance than the homepage. Preserve Known / Open /
Protected notes, care notes, source-layer descriptions, and protected-boundary
language.

## Technical Operations Page

Translate proof into role fit: delivery coordination, planning cycles, decision
frameworks, status reporting, risk surfacing, handbooks, runbooks, onboarding,
retrospectives, cross-team coordination, and documentation that people actually
use.

## Lab Page

Source-Backed Team Memory stays in `/lab/source-backed-team-memory`. Frame it as
a bounded method or bridge-work offer, not a finished SaaS product and not the
homepage identity.

## Resume Page

The page may summarize approved résumé claims and link the PDF. The PDF itself
stays noindex for V1 and is omitted from the sitemap.

## Contact

Show only approved contact paths from environment variables. Do not display
phone, email, LinkedIn, or GitHub values unless Jamie has approved the exact
public surface.

## Metadata And OpenGraph

Metadata should use the active `SITE_URL`, not hard-coded staging or production
URLs. It should not strengthen claims beyond the visible page copy.

## Colophon

The colophon may explain the public-safe design system, typefaces, and
deployment posture. It should not expose private source workflows.
