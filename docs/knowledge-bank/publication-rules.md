# Publication Rules

The website is a projection from public-safe proof, not a dump of private
evidence.

## Homepage

Use only core positioning and fully public-safe proof-strip claims. The homepage
should say what Jamie makes possible without becoming a source ledger.

Allowed V1 posture:

- operating structure for complex public-facing teams;
- emerging, stakeholder-heavy work becomes usable systems;
- 14+ years building operating structure;
- softened project proof from HJE, CRS, WOWList, and 196 / Sunday Dinner.

## Work Index

Use short claims with clear role, scope, status, and public-safety posture. Each
card should answer:

- What kind of work was it?
- What did Jamie make usable?
- Why does it matter for role fit?

## Case-Study Pages

Case-study pages may carry more nuance than the homepage. They may describe what
was unclear, what became usable, artifact types, source layer, known/open/
protected boundaries, and care notes.

They must not publish private source records, private local paths, unapproved
quotes, unapproved screenshots, unapproved collaborator names, legal-review
detail, private strategy, or raw community records.

## Technical Operations Page

Use claims that map to delivery coordination, risk surfacing, planning rhythms,
operating rituals, decision records, action trackers, onboarding, handbooks,
runbooks, stakeholder updates, public-facing launch support, retrospectives,
handoffs, and documentation systems people actually use.

## Lab Page

Source-Backed Team Memory is an early method / consulting practice for V1.

Use:

> AI drafts. Humans review. The shared record remains inspectable and
> correctable.

Do not present it as production SaaS, broad ingestion, private archive browsing,
or a replacement for human judgment.

## Resume Page

Use the approved PDF only. The PDF may include phone if Jamie has approved that
version. Do not put phone in HTML unless separately approved.

## Metadata And OpenGraph

Use role, operating-structure, and selected proof language only. Avoid dense
metrics, active-project details, unresolved citations, and approval-gated
claims.

## Colophon

Explain the public-safety and proof standard, not private evidence.

## Redirects And Sitemap

The sitemap should include canonical live URLs only. Redirect sources, resume
PDF files, private pages, draft pages, unapproved pages, and
`/work/source-backed-team-memory` stay out of the sitemap.
