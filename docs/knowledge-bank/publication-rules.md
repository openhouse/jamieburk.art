# Publication Rules

The public site is a projection of this knowledge bank.

Production must not show TODO, pending, approval required, placeholder, or
private-source wording.

## Homepage

Use only CORE claims and fully approved proof-strip claims.

Allowed:

- CORE-001
- CORE-002
- CORE-003
- HJE-002
- CRS-001
- WOW-001
- WOW-002
- SD-001

Do not use active-project details, collaborator names, unresolved citations, or
dense metrics that make the homepage feel like a source ledger.

## Work Index

Use short claims with clear role, scope, status, and public-safety posture.

Each card should answer:

- What kind of work was it?
- What did Jamie make usable?
- Why does it matter for role fit?

## Case-Study Pages

Case-study pages may use project claims plus Known / Open / Protected limits.
They may carry more nuance than the homepage, but they should still avoid source
dumping.

Allowed:

- role;
- public-safe context;
- what was unclear;
- what became usable;
- artifacts by type;
- source class;
- protected boundary.

Not allowed:

- private source records;
- private local paths;
- unapproved quotes;
- unapproved screenshots;
- unapproved collaborator names;
- legal-review detail;
- private strategy.

## Technical Operations Page

Use claims that map to:

- delivery coordination;
- risk surfacing;
- planning cycles;
- status reporting;
- decision frameworks;
- onboarding;
- runbooks;
- operating documentation;
- team health;
- operational metrics;
- cross-functional dependencies;
- durable handoffs.

Do not clutter the public page with claim IDs. Keep IDs in this knowledge bank.

## Lab Page

Source-Backed Team Memory is lab / method / proof-of-practice / consulting
practice language for V1.

Use:

> AI drafts. Humans review. The shared record remains inspectable and
> correctable.

Do not present it as production SaaS, broad ingestion, private archive browsing,
or a replacement for human judgment.

## Resume Page

Use the approved PDF only. The PDF may include phone if Jamie has approved that
version. Do not put phone in HTML unless separately approved.

## Metadata And OpenGraph

Use role, operating-structure, and selected proof language only. Avoid dense
metrics, active-project details, and unresolved citations.

## Colophon

Explain the public-safety and proof standard, not private evidence.

## Redirects And Sitemap

Sitemap should include canonical live URLs only. Redirect sources, resume PDFs,
private pages, draft pages, unapproved pages, and `/work/source-backed-team-memory`
must stay out of the sitemap.
