# Publication Rules

This file is the concise rules companion to `publishing-governance.md`.

## Claim Flow

1. Private material exists outside the repo.
2. Jamie or a reviewer creates a public-safe summary.
3. The claim enters `docs/knowledge-bank/claims.md` and
   `apps/www/src/data/proofs.ts`.
4. The claim receives status, support level, source basis, guardrail,
   protected boundaries, and last-reviewed date.
5. Public pages may project only `ready` or `careful` claims.

## Public Copy

Do not strengthen a public page beyond the knowledge-bank wording. If stronger
wording is needed, update the bank first.

## Approval

Collaborator-sensitive names, credits, screenshots, quotes, photos, and private
artifacts require explicit approval or omission.
