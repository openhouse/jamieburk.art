# MARCH Data-Design Attribution And Implementation Inquiry

## Question

What part of the collaborative MARCH open-data and preventive-alert design can
be attributed to Jamie, and did any part of the proposed system advance from a
working concept into reporting legislation, an implemented tool, or a tested
operational practice?

## Present Finding

The native MARCH working document was re-opened on July 19, 2026. Its visible
suggestion interface attributes to Jamie a February 1, 2019 intervention asking
whether existing FOIL data could support the proposed work and, if not, what a
reporting bill would need to provide.

That is direct support for a narrow contribution connecting available records,
data sufficiency, and legislative reporting. The wider document remains
collaborative and contains additional reporting, alerting, and bias-risk ideas
whose complete authorship cannot yet be allocated.

## What Remains Open

- Review the full revision sequence rather than relying only on visible current
  suggestion metadata.
- Ask collaborators to corroborate roles and the meaning of the working notes.
- Locate any related FOIL extracts, reporting-bill drafts, field definitions,
  implementation artifacts, tests, or operating handoffs.
- Independently verify adjacent campaign statistics before using them.

## Closure Boundary

This inquiry must not be promoted into a claim that Jamie independently
designed or shipped a MARCH prediction product. The current evidence does not
establish complete authorship, implementation, validation, safety, adoption, or
causal impact.
