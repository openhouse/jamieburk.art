# Visual Evidence

This register records public-safe patterns discovered through a local,
whole-library photo curation pass. It does not publish photographs, private
identifiers, raw metadata, People labels, filenames, album inventories, or
operational manifests.

The photographs are corroborating evidence. They can make a documented claim
more concrete, but they do not independently establish Jamie's role, project
ownership, causality, scale, consent, or an outcome.

## Cross-Project Finding

The strongest visual throughline is a situated, material operating practice.
Jamie is most legible inside real environments: among people, artifacts,
rooms, public places, and technical systems. The archive repeatedly shows work
becoming usable through facilitation, preparation, arrangement, documentation,
implementation, and care.

This finding supports a more concrete reading of "operating structure":

- requirements and decisions appear as cards, notes, diagrams, source sets,
  laptops, and working tables;
- implementation appears through machines, displays, tools, materials, work
  sites, and built artifacts;
- public-facing work appears through handbills, signs, panels, rallies,
  storefronts, streets, and civic spaces;
- participation infrastructure appears through preparation, food, tables,
  rooms, gatherings, and the aftermath of hosting;
- collective work appears through people in relation to a speaker, artifact,
  place, or shared task rather than through isolated portraits alone.

The most useful professional photographs therefore show action plus context.
A recognizable portrait without visible work is weaker evidence than Jamie or
collaborators handling material, facilitating a room, reviewing sources, or
working beside a system.

## Project Corroboration

### FairRentNYC / NYC Artist Coalition

The archive contains strong public-context imagery: rallies, panels, public
officials, campaign language, handbills, and civic places. These images can
corroborate campaign communications, public web infrastructure, and coalition
operations. They do not prove that Jamie authored every message or caused a
policy outcome.

### KC Town Hall

The archive makes adaptive reuse tangible through building condition, repair,
facade and porch details, surrounding streets, materials, and site-walk or
planning context. These images can corroborate the built-environment process.
They do not establish funding receipt, current property status, ownership, or
private financial details.

### Harry J. Epstein Company

Industrial settings, workers, stacked materials, machinery, workbenches, and
product knowledge are visible. Some visually plausible images remain weakly
attributed, so HJE use requires independent project provenance. Material
similarity alone is not enough.

### 196 Artists Residency / Sunday Dinner

Preparation labor, kitchens, tables, shared rooms, gatherings, and aftermath
make hospitality visible as operating work rather than atmosphere. These
images can corroborate repeatable hosting and participation infrastructure.
They do not establish attendance totals, participant roles, or permission to
publish identifiable guests or minors.

### Source-Backed Team Memory

Archive workspaces, bounded document sets, cards, diagrams, notebooks, and
laptops make source review and information structuring materially legible.
These images support the method's human-review and decision-lineage story.
They do not prove a deployed client product or authorize publication of the
underlying sources.

### Technical Operations / Implementation

Displays, installations, machines, tools, materials, and people working beside
systems provide credible environmental evidence of implementation work. A
caption must still establish Jamie's specific role from an independent source.

### CallNYC / WOWList

The photo archive contains adjacent civic, cultural, event-distribution, and
public-technology scenes, but project attribution is often uncertain. Public
repositories, archived interfaces, public coverage, and verified screenshots
remain the primary evidence for these projects. Do not use a related-looking
photograph as substitute provenance.

## Editorial Direction

Prefer:

- environmental working presence over context-free portraits;
- people in relation to an artifact, system, room, or shared task;
- process and preparation alongside finished public moments;
- legible public artifacts and public settings;
- photographs that add information a caption can state precisely.

Avoid:

- private or domestic images used merely to make the site feel personal;
- identifiable minors without explicit approval;
- crowds used as implied proof of Jamie's leadership or project scale;
- visual similarity used as project attribution;
- screenshots or documents that expose private text, contact information,
  credentials, legal material, or sensitive strategy;
- publishing People labels or treating a named association as proof of a
  professional relationship.

## Evidence Promotion Gate

Before a photograph reaches the website:

1. Establish project and date provenance independently of visual resemblance.
2. Link the proposed caption to an existing proof ID.
3. State only what is visible or supported by another approved source.
4. Confirm photographer rights and publication permission.
5. Review every identifiable person, especially minors and people in private
   or sensitive settings.
6. Check the frame for private documents, screens, addresses, financial
   details, medical information, credentials, or protected locations.
7. Record whether the image is public, approved, approval-pending, or private.
8. Keep the original photograph and raw metadata outside this repository.

## What The Photos Do Not Prove

The editor-field size and project-view counts are curation parameters, not
project metrics. They must not be used as evidence of attendance, duration,
adoption, output, revenue, funding, or impact.

A face, People association, favorite, edit, album membership, date, or location
is a retrieval clue, not publication clearance and not proof of a person's
role. Project claims still depend on the approved resume, public sources,
public-safe archive summaries, Jamie's review, and collaborator confirmation
where required.
