- Civic Hall announced a January 30, 2016 New York City Council
  constituent-services hackathon for 1-3 p.m.
- The New York City Council described the gathering as its first CouncilStat
  hackathon.

- A Wayback capture preserves embedded social-feed evidence; it is not treated

  developed CallNYC.org as a resident-facing interpretation of the records.
