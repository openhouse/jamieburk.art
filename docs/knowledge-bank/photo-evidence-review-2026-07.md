# Photo Evidence Review - July 2026

This is a public-safe evidence summary. The private photographs, previews,
identifiers, People associations, OCR, metadata, and archive paths remain
outside this repository.

## Purpose

The review asked what Jamie's photographs can responsibly add to the knowledge
bank. Photographs are strongest when they corroborate visible presence,
settings, artifacts, repeated practices, and material conditions. They are weak
when asked to prove role, ownership, leadership, authorship, outcomes, consent,
or a complete account of collective work.

The July 2026 local-only curation inspected an 8,000-image candidate field and
produced a 4,000-image editor field. A separate safety review held 2,106
candidates outside the master. Final stratified evaluation judged 60 images and
measured 92.86% precision. These figures describe the curation process, not the
size or significance of Jamie's professional record.

## What the photographs corroborate

### Public advocacy and coalition presence

The strongest project-specific visual lane covers NYC Artist Coalition and
FairRentNYC contexts from 2017 through 2025. Repeated visible elements include
public and collaborative settings, people gathered together, signs, banners,
and candidates showing Jamie within the work's social environment.

This supports a careful statement that Jamie participated over time in public
advocacy and coalition settings. It does not, by itself, prove coalition
leadership, campaign ownership, policy authorship, website authorship, or
campaign outcomes. Those claims continue to depend on public sites, documentary
records, Jamie's confirmation, and collaborator context.

### Cultural hosting as material practice

The 196 Artists Residency and Sunday Dinner lane spans many years and is
visually rich in documents, handwriting, artworks, tables, utensils, prepared
rooms, plants, and traces of use. The pattern supports the existing description
of hosting, facilitation, documentation, and continuity as practical work. The
photographs make care visible as arrangement, maintenance, invitation, and
follow-through rather than as an abstract value.

The images do not independently verify the approved figures of 300+ gatherings
or 20+ resident artists. They also do not authorize publication of guests,
artists, homes, addresses, or private gatherings.

Related claim:

- `sunday-dinner-196-participation-infrastructure`

### Jamie in real working environments

A dedicated working-presence lane produced candidates showing Jamie in public,
collaborative, cultural, and documentation-rich settings. This is

The lane is an editorial starting point, not evidence of a particular job title

### Source-backed memory has a material vocabulary

A smaller process lane consistently surfaced documents, handwriting, working
surfaces, boards, and connective materials. These images can help show that
source-backed memory is a practice of arranging and reviewing evidence, not an
abstract AI aesthetic.

They do not prove that a production product was deployed for a client, that the
method is finished, or that private source material is available to browse.

## What the review did not establish

This run did not produce sufficiently defensible project attribution for Harry
J. Epstein Company, CallNYC, WOWList, KC Town Hall, the general Technical
Operations category, or Colophon. Earlier generated editor-album memberships
had entered the retrieval provenance, so those labels were withheld rather than
treated as independent evidence.

This is not evidence that the work did not happen, and it does not weaken claims
supported by resumes, public artifacts, code, records, or collaborators. It
means photographs from this run should not yet be used as project-specific proof
for those areas. Fresh retrieval should start from independently verified dates,
places, public artifacts, or collaborator-confirmed events.

## Safety findings

- People associations are useful retrieval signals, not consent or role data.
- A visible face does not establish identity, project membership, or permission.
- A project album is a hypothesis until checked against independent context.
- Sensitive or uncertain photographs stay outside public projection.
- No photograph becomes publishable merely because it entered the editor field.
- Private homes, minors, intimate gatherings, addresses, screens, documents,
  and bystanders require especially conservative review.

## Asset-specific publication gate

Before any photograph reaches the site, record:

1. verified identity of people who need to be named;
2. verified project, event, place, and date;
3. photographer or creator and the right to publish;
4. consent or a documented public-event basis appropriate to the use;
5. the claim ID the image corroborates;
6. a factual caption that does not infer more than the image and sources show;
7. crop and context review, including visible documents, screens, addresses,
   minors, bystanders, and private interiors;
8. Jamie's final approval for the specific image and placement.

Until all eight checks pass, keep the image in private editorial review.
