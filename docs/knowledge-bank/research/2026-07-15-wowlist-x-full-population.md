# WOW List X full-population archival production

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-15  
**Account:** [`@wowlist`](https://x.com/wowlist)  
**Public fixture:** [`wowlist-full-population.json`](https://github.com/openhouse/jamieburk.art/blob/develop/apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/fixtures/wowlist-full-population.json)

## Question

What mission-relevant publishing, posted-source, visible-engagement, and
stakeholder-response patterns appear across the full population displayed by
the @wowlist profile?

## Population reconciliation

The authenticated profile reported 38 posts. Repeated no-growth traversal
produced 37 unique primary records in Posts and 38 in Replies. One @wowlist
reply materialized only in Replies. The deduplicated union contains 38 unique
status IDs, exactly matching the profile control. Every recovered record was
reviewed.

The same archival pass recovered a May 12, 2017 Wayback capture of the WOW
List home page. The capture is retained as direct evidence of the public
interface and find-and-share positioning, not as evidence of usage scale or
platform adoption.

The population contains:

- 16 original posts;
- 6 replies;
- 16 reposts;
- 22 account-authored records; and
- 16 redistributed records.

This is 100% of the population displayed by the July 15, 2026 profile control.
It is not a claim that deleted, private, withheld, or historically unindexed
records never existed.

## Posted URLs and source field

Thirty-one records contain external links, producing 35 occurrences and 35
distinct short URLs. Nineteen of the 22 account-authored records contain links,
with 23 account-authored link occurrences.

The source field includes:

- DIY cultural reporting and self-documentation;
- all-ages organizing resources;
- cultural-space funding and safety records;
- participatory-media convening;
- Indigenous-led mutual-aid reporting;
- community-created product instruction;
- event and calendar distribution; and
- the archived popular.vote civic-coordination surface.

These records establish what the account circulated. They do not make WOW List
the author, organizer, partner, or owner of the underlying work.

## Operating pattern

The account-authored population shows social media operating as part of the
product and community practice:

- a 2014 origin record connects WOW List to Sunday Dinner calendars;
- a 2015 reply gives step-by-step event-publishing support;
- a community-created tutorial is routed into the join flow;
- a 2016 reply explains how NYC DIY ran on the platform;
- event links and calendar routes distribute community activity; and
- the system is adapted for rapid multi-city civic coordination after the 2016
  election.

At access time, 12 of 22 account-authored records displayed at least one
interaction, totaling 2 replies, 20 reposts, and 21 likes. These 43 displayed
units are volatile interface observations, not unique people, reach,
conversion, endorsement, event attendance, adoption, or impact. Metrics on the
16 reposted source records are excluded from this account-authored total.

## Bounded stakeholder inventory

Authenticated searches for current and historical handle strings, wowlist.org,
and project-name plus event references recovered 16 incoming records. They were
all dispositioned:

- 10 mission-relevant third-party records from 10 accounts;
- 1 project-contributor self-documentation record;
- 2 context-limited greetings; and
- 3 unrelated or false-positive records.

The 10 mission-relevant accounts span seven DIY arts and music accounts, one
calendar-community account, one neighborhood-civic account, and one
creative-technology peer. Nine independently posted a WOW List event, list, or
project URL.

## Public-safety disposition

The repository fixture retains public status URLs, dates, author handles,
record types, posted-link metadata, aggregate counts, classifications, and
limitations. It excludes raw post text, cookies, credentials, private account
state, private messages, analytics, and session data.

Institutional-account records are not attributed post by post to Jamie without
separate authorship evidence. The historical relationship between @WOWListNYC
and @wowlist remains unresolved.

## Selected projection

The case study receives one composed claim about the operating pattern and
bounded distribution signals. The complete corpus, source field, dated
engagement context, stakeholder dispositions, and anti-claims remain in the
knowledge bank so future compositions can draw from them without turning the
public page into an evidence dump.
