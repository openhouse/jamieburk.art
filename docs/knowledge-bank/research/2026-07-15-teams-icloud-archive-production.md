# Teams iCloud archive production, 2026-07-15

## Purpose

Develop public-safe, role-relevant knowledge from three long-running working
collections: Jamie Projects History, Commercial Rent Stabilization, and
job-hunt. This note records the archival method, promoted claims, holds, and
privacy boundaries. It does not reproduce private source material.

## Method

1. Inventoried all three required collections and selected high-signal primary
   and contemporaneous records.
2. Allowed cloud-backed files time to materialize. A file that did not
   materialize during this run was treated as unavailable, not nonexistent.
3. Close-read the selected records and visually reviewed pivotal PDF artifacts.
4. Decomposed each source into atomic observations, limitations, anti-claims,
   and collective-credit requirements.
5. Promoted only claims that met their stated evidence threshold. Kept all new
   projections on hold pending audience selection and exact-copy review.

## Jamie Projects History

### Promoted

Contemporary reporting supports a stronger bounded account of the 2007 raft
expedition. Jamie organized and initiated the experiential premise for a
collective recycled-material, bicycle-powered raft expedition that traveled
more than 1,000 miles from Kansas City into Louisiana. The crew modified the
raft and resumed after a regulatory pause with community and legal support in
Vicksburg.

This corrects an earlier knowledge-bank summary that stopped at travel across
Missouri. The expanded account is supported by a preserved November 15, 2007,
Kansas City Star feature and live contemporaneous reporting from
[WLBT](https://www.wlbt.com/story/7123200/rafters-beached-on-mississippi-river/)
and [The Pitch](https://www.thepitchkc.com/artists-turned-huck-finn-part-iii/).

### Held

No reviewed source establishes arrival at the Gulf of Mexico. The confirmed
route into Louisiana is substantial and should not be weakened by attaching an
unsupported endpoint. Future composition should preserve the expedition as
collective work and credit named crew and other builders when space permits.

bounded Source-Backed Team Memory Sprint. The offer begins with one approved

The proposal establishes offer and method design only. It does not establish
acceptance, a client engagement, completed delivery, production deployment, or

## Public-use decision

These findings deepen the knowledge bank and strengthen the source basis of
existing Fair Rent and source-backed-memory proof records. They do not require
new website copy in this pass. Public pages remain a purpose-built projection
of the bank rather than an exhaustive archive.

## Protected boundaries

The public repository excludes private collaborators and correspondence,
campaign strategy, community records, legal-review materials, recipient and
company context, pricing, raw transcripts, filesystem locations, and any
cloud-backed source that did not materialize for review.
