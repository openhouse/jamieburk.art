# NTER CHNG working-document recovery

**Research date:** 2026-07-16  
**Status:** Partially recovered; completion and public corroboration remain open

## Question

What can two newly surfaced NTER CHNG Google Docs establish about the original
exhibition and the project's 2011 America: Now and Here staging?

## Method

1. Close-read both supplied documents through Jamie's authenticated Google
   Drive connection.
2. Review file metadata for titles and creation dates without copying private
   links or Google file identifiers into the repository.
3. Query the available Drive revision-history endpoint for both documents.
4. Compare the working records with the previously recovered public NTER CHNG
   and America: Now and Here pages.
5. Run bounded exact-name public-web searches for the January 2010 dates and
   the Leedy-Voulkos staging.
6. Attempt a dated Leedy-Voulkos Wayback/CDX query.
7. Separate production planning, first-party exhibit information, public
   corroboration, and protected message-level material.

The connector returned no available revision history for either document.
File creation metadata is useful provenance, but it does not prove that every
current passage has remained unchanged since 2011.

The exact-name public-web searches did not recover an independent January 2010
listing or a Leedy-Voulkos NTER CHNG page. The Wayback/CDX request timed out
before returning a gallery-domain inventory, so this is bounded non-recovery,
not evidence that no public page existed.

## Findings

### Original exhibit information

One protected working document contains a section labeled `January 2010
Exhibit Information`. It records:

- NTER CHNG as the project title;
- Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier as collaborators;
- January 8-29, 2010 as the exhibit dates;
- the work's real-time, many-to-many text-messaging interaction model;
- the team's combined scenic-design, computer-programming, motion-graphics,
  and experiential-production range; and
- a statement attributed to Jamie about inviting visitors beyond their
  existing contact lists.

The dates and statement remain first-party project-archive evidence. No
recovered public publication currently establishes where the text appeared.

The same document also contains phone numbers and message-level material.
Those fields are unnecessary to the professional claim and remain protected.
The records do not establish that the message samples came from gallery
visitors or that their authors consented to republication.

### America: Now and Here production plan

The second protected working document is headed as a staging plan for America:
Now and Here in May 2011. It identifies Leedy-Voulkos as the planned install
site, an April 22 installation start, and a production sequence spanning:

- communication and permissions with show organizers;
- software reliability and message-queue behavior;
- hosting and display computers;
- projection and projector mounting;
- wall fabrication, painting, transport, and floor attachment;
- wiring and network needs; and
- final tuning of the gallery experience.

This is strong evidence for the scope and interdependence of the production
plan. It is not a completion report. It does not establish that the work was
installed at Leedy-Voulkos exactly as planned, that every task was completed,
or that Jamie personally performed every task.

## Knowledge added

The close reading adds three atomic claims, all held from public projection:

1. A first-party exhibit-information record dates the original NTER CHNG run
   to January 8-29, 2010.
2. An April 2011 working document planned an America: Now and Here staging at
   Leedy-Voulkos beginning April 22, with a cross-functional production scope.
3. A first-party exhibit-information record describes the three collaborators
   as combining scenic, software, motion, and experiential production
   practices.

These records deepen the professional interpretation of NTER CHNG as an early
example of participatory technology implementation across software, physical
production, logistics, and public experience. They do not resolve the
individual division of labor.

## Evidence boundary

- Credit Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier collectively.
- Treat Leedy-Voulkos and April 22 as planned installation details until a
  completion record, program listing, photograph, or collaborator confirmation
  is recovered.
- Treat January 8-29, 2010 as first-party project-archive dates until an
  independent public source corroborates them.
- Do not present the protected working text as a recovered published press
  release.
- Do not reproduce phone numbers, message samples, private links, Google file
  identifiers, or the complete protected documents.
- Do not assign scenic design, programming, motion graphics, experiential
  production, or individual installation tasks to a particular collaborator
  from these records alone.
