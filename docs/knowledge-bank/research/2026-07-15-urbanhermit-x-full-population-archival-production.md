## Public-safe artifact

The public aggregate fixture at
`apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/fixtures/urbanhermit-full-population.json`
retains:

- aggregate date and source-authorship counts;
- original, reply, and native-repost relationship counts;
- Posts and Replies recovery arithmetic;
- aggregate external-link and mission-signal counts;
- aggregate stakeholder-group and dated visible interaction counts;
- the strict classifier rule manifest; and
- SHA-256 digests of the protected population and incoming-response sets.

It excludes raw post text, non-evidence item identifiers and timestamps,
profile biography text, historical contact details, cookies, session data,
direct messages, private activity, and local archive paths. Claim-specific
status URLs are accessioned separately after source and privacy review. The
protected row-level capture remains outside the public repository.
