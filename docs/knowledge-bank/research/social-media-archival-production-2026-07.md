# Social Media Archival Production - July 2026

## Purpose

This pass treated public social accounts as project-documentation and source-
discovery surfaces. It did not treat post count, mention count, repost count, or
follower count as impact by itself. Public posts can establish chronology,
public language, links, and visible stakeholder response; stronger project and
outcome claims still require the linked original, official records, independent
reporting, operational records, or collaborator corroboration.

The typed lifecycle record is
`apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/social-archive-production.ts`. Full and
partial research corpora remain outside the public repository and are
represented only by opaque locator IDs.

## Method

1. Used an authenticated X browser session.
2. Crawled Posts and Replies in small increments and deduplicated by status URL.
3. Reconciled recovered records to each profile's displayed count.
4. Used live from-account and month-bounded searches where the profile cursor
   stopped early.
5. Classified authored posts, replies, and reposts without converting mentions
   into replies, endorsements, or engagement.
6. Inspected mission-relevant links, named stakeholder responses, campaign
   language, and cross-project chronology.
7. Preserved retrieval gaps as inquiries. Current-population completeness does
   not recover posts removed before capture.

## Eval Contract

The lifecycle eval fails if a complete current population stops reconciling,
if an incomplete corpus becomes a population-wide claim, if a private corpus is
exposed, if Council-member amplification becomes formal endorsement, if
mentions become engagement, or if the NYC Artist Coalition memory loses its
multi-author boundary.
