# KC Town Hall Full-Population Social Census - July 14, 2026

## Purpose

This public-safe archival-production record gives every item represented by the
surviving `@KCTownHall` profile control a disposition. It identifies sources,
operational patterns, and bounded stakeholder traction without publishing raw
post text, authenticated-session data, private records, contact details, or an
inferred author for shared-account posts.

The typed records live in
`apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/kctownhall-social-census-2026-07-14.ts`.
The redacted item ledger lives in
`docs/knowledge-bank/data/kctownhall-public-post-ledger.json`.

## Public-Safe Ledger

The ledger retains canonical status IDs and URLs, dates, account relationship,
route provenance, public mentions, classifications, posted-link resolutions,
current visible metrics, media signals, character counts, and content digests.
It deliberately omits raw post text and authenticated-session material.

The content digest lets a later reviewer compare a newly recovered status with
the July 2026 record without placing the underlying text in this public repo.

## Mission-Relevant Patterns

### Resident tire intake and operations

One hundred of 183 records belong to the resident tire-intake and operations
workflow. Ninety-eight distinct records carry `#TiredOfTires`. The pattern
includes calls for residents to identify tire locations, replies that clarify
service boundaries or acknowledge reports, recurring schedule information,
before-and-after follow-up, and dated progress reports.

Archived project pages add the role evidence the account alone cannot supply:

- the [home page](https://web.archive.org/web/20190506115340/http://kctownhall.com/)
  attributes project posts to Julia and Jamie and describes monthly free pickup
  with Oak Park Neighborhood Association;
- the [about page](https://web.archive.org/web/20190813013145/http://kctownhall.com/about/)
  identifies Julia and Jamie as restoring the building through a neighborhood
  process toward a permanently affordable community space;
- the [tire page](https://web.archive.org/web/20210806195823/https://kctownhall.com/tires/)
  publishes a recurring schedule and form and phone or text intake routes.

**Defensible claim:** Jamie worked with Julia Fredenburg and Oak Park
Neighborhood Association to make a recurring free tire-pickup workflow
operable and visible through resident intake, scheduling, and public reporting.

**Limits:** Aggregate tire and savings figures remain project self-reports
pending reconciliation with operational records. The evidence does not assign
Jamie sole authorship, identify the author of every post, or establish audited
neighborhood impact.
