# iCloud Teams Archival Production - July 2026

## Purpose

This pass used Jamie's iCloud Teams working archive as a research environment
for developing the knowledge bank. The archive is not a public evidence dump.
It is a place to discover public sources, identify private artifacts that may
support carefully bounded claims, and preserve questions that still need
verification.

The required scope covered:

- Jamie Projects History;
- CRS;
- job-hunt.

The typed lifecycle record is
`apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/icloud-archive-production.ts`.

## Method

1. Inventoried each required archive and began with its orientation documents
   rather than treating a recursive filename scan as research.
2. Selected high-signal public and private artifacts across creative technical
   work, coalition operations, policy research, and source-backed memory.
3. Waited for iCloud hydration where possible and recorded materialization
   limits rather than treating delayed files as absent.
4. Closely read each promoted artifact into support scope, non-support scope,
   locator, project context, claim relationship, and anti-claim.
5. Verified public URLs independently of the local capture. Normal builds do
   not depend on live network access.
6. Represented private originals only through public-safe metadata and opaque
   protected locator IDs.
7. Kept every newly recovered claim out of active site composition during this
   archival-production pass.

## Boundaries

- A working plan proves authored planning, not completion or adoption.
- A proposal proves engagement design, not delivery or client outcome.
- A collective artifact does not allocate individual authorship.
- A project video credit does not reveal the division of technical labor.
- A private synthesis document is a research map, not independent proof.
- A file that has not materialized locally is unresolved, not absent.
- Mature knowledge does not require immediate public-site projection.
