## What was ingested

Every placement preserves:

- campaign and original order;
- publisher and title as displayed by the campaign site;
- the exact URL listed by the campaign;
- one canonical article source ID;
- current identity status;
- an archival fallback where the destination is dead, soft-redirected, or
  blocked to automated review;
- an explicit boundary separating campaign placement from article-level
  evidentiary support.

The 41 newly created article sources are `metadata-reviewed` and `unsurfaced`.
Three already close-read sources are reused: the Gothamist Cabaret Law report,
the NPR nightlife report, and the Bedford + Bowery Office of Nightlife town-hall
report. No newly cataloged article was automatically promoted into a claim or
onto the website.

## Evidentiary boundary

The campaign index can establish that an article was included in a campaign's
Press section. It cannot, by itself, establish that:

- the article names, quotes, or credits Jamie;
- Jamie authored or commissioned the article;
- every collaborator endorsed every article;
- the article supports every claim associated with the campaign;
- the campaign or Jamie caused the policy outcome described;
- the four lists exhaust all relevant coverage.

Article-level claims require a separate close reading, atomic claim, evidence
relationship, locator, and limitations. Editorial selection remains a later
decision even after a claim becomes defensible.
