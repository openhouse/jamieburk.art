# Google Drive Shared Drives Archive Production, July 2026

## Purpose

This pass examined Jamie's accessible Google Drive Shared Drives as a private
collaboration archive. The goal was to recover public-safe evidence about how
Jamie made projects usable and handoff-ready without publishing the drives,
raw files, private links, collaborator details, or sensitive working context.

The private archive remains outside the repository. The knowledge bank stores
only bounded source descriptions, atomic propositions, anti-claims, research
tasks, and editorial decisions.

## Scope and method

The authenticated account exposed 110 accessible Shared Drives on July 13,
2026. That count is an access inventory, not an ownership claim. Shared Drive
and folder names were used to route research, not to prove project claims.

The review then sampled central project workspaces across:

- 196 Artists Residency;
- Sunday Dinner;
- FairRentNYC and Commercial Rent Stabilization;
- commercial vacancy data;
- NYC Artist Coalition;
- KC Town Hall; and
- WOWList.

The close-reading sequence was:

1. Inventory the Shared Drive roots and select central project drives rather
   than collaborator-specific or personal workspaces.
2. Inspect direct children and follow high-signal documents, worksheets,
   source-corpus folders, recordings, and photo sets.
3. Read textual sources and relevant version history closely.
4. Distinguish document contents from Drive metadata and first-party memory.
5. Deduplicate sources already represented in the bank. The CRS running
   minutes remained one source; Drive version history entered as distinct
   corroborating metadata.
6. Promote only claims with proposition-level support and explicit collective
   and causal boundaries.
7. Route unread recordings and photographs to research tasks rather than
   treating their labels as claims.

## Mature findings

### 196 Artists Residency

A July 2023 acceptance letter is signed by Jamie, and its two recorded
revisions identify him as editor. The letter documents proposal evaluation,
a dated invitation, a planned space-configuration check-in, and a self-service
key workflow supporting independent participant access.

Safe finding:

> Jamie evaluated and accepted an exhibition-based residency proposal, then
> defined a practical onboarding path for space configuration and independent
> access.

Limits:

- The participant is not identified or quoted publicly.
- The artist's proposal and creative work remain the artist's.
- One acceptance letter does not establish the complete residency program or
  its outcomes.

### FairRentNYC web relaunch

January-February 2023 web-operations notes record a new site as live and join
website, email, signup, letter, press, testimony, sponsor, rally, and social
requirements in one working record. A companion worksheet structures domain,
identity, copy, signup, assets, campaign documents, contact, and reuse
constraints. Version histories identify Jamie and a campaign collaborator as
editors.

Safe finding:

> Jamie co-edited FairRentNYC's structured campaign inputs and web-operations
> record as the team relaunched the site and connected it to practical campaign
> action pathways.

Limits:

- The records are collaborative and do not assign every task or wording choice
  to Jamie.
- A launch note does not establish completion of every checklist item,
  measured web performance, or policy outcomes.
- Private administrative links and working details remain outside the repo.

### Commercial Rent Stabilization shared memory

The underlying running minutes were already represented in the Teams archive
production pass. Google Drive version history adds distinct corroboration: 20
recorded revisions between April 29 and May 29, 2026, with Jamie and a
collaborator identified as editors.

This strengthens the existing finding that Jamie implemented and maintained a
lightweight, consent-aware shared-memory system. It does not attribute every

### Commercial vacancy public-data pilot

A Jamie-authored March 2026 proposal defines a smallest serious pilot for
geography-aggregated commercial vacancy and lease-cost indicators. It treats
existing storefront reporting and filing-derived aggregates as complementary,
requires coverage, suppression, and methods documentation, and explicitly
excludes raw confidential filing, tenant, parcel, and lease records.

A related workspace inventory periodizes sources across 2005-2025 and includes
census, script, and web-output areas.

Safe finding:

> Jamie designed a privacy-preserving open-data pilot that would complement
> storefront reporting with aggregate commercial vacancy and lease-cost
> indicators, coverage and suppression tables, and a methods note.

Limits:

- The proposal does not establish agency acceptance or implementation.
- The indicators have not been represented as released or validated.
- Directory organization does not prove source completeness or a production
  data pipeline.

### Cross-project handoff practice

The three mature project families document concrete Shared Drive use across
participant communication, structured campaign inputs, and collaborative
operating memory.

Safe finding:

> Across 196 Artists Residency, FairRentNYC, and Commercial Rent Stabilization
> work from 2023-2026, Jamie used Shared Drives to create and maintain
> handoff-ready participant, campaign, and coalition artifacts.

Limits:

- The finding is limited to the three evidenced projects.
- It does not imply ownership or active maintenance of all 110 accessible
  Shared Drives.
- Cross-device purpose is Jamie's first-party account, not usage telemetry.
