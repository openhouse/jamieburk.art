# NYC Artist Coalition Full-Population Social Census - July 14, 2026

## Purpose

This public-safe archival-production record gives every slot represented by the
authenticated `@NYCArtC` profile count a disposition. It inventories recovered
posts, replies, reposts, posted sources, and bounded stakeholder patterns
without publishing raw post text, authenticated-session data, private
analytics, or an inferred author for shared-account communication.

`apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/nycartc-social-census-2026-07-14.ts`.

## Population Closure

The authenticated public profile displayed **5,124 posts**. Reconciliation of
the public Posts and Replies surfaces, a bounded historical authored-status
search, and an independent Posts-tab crosscheck produced:

- 3,367 unique recovered population items;
- 715 account statuses;
- 2,652 reposts;
- 22 conversation-context records excluded from the account population;
- 1,757 unresolved profile-count slots;
- 5,124 total dispositions.

The recovered records span February 3, 2017, through May 18, 2026. Item-level
recovery is **65.7 percent**. Disposition coverage is **100 percent**.

This is complete accounting, not complete item recovery. It is not a native X
export, deletion history, or proof that no older item was removed before
capture. No IDs, dates, types, text, themes, authors, or deletion reasons are
invented for unresolved slots. The recovered 65.7 percent is not assumed to
represent the unresolved 34.3 percent.
