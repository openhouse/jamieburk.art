# Google Shared Drive archive production, 2026-07-15

## Purpose

Develop public-safe, role-relevant knowledge from Jamie's Google Drive Shared
Drives without treating technical access, folder custody, or filename metadata
as authorship or publication permission. This note records method, selected
findings, holds, and exclusions. It does not reproduce private source material
or expose Drive links and item identifiers.

## Selection method

1. Inventoried the accessible Shared Drives as an online source of truth.
2. Excluded personal, family, legal, relationship, health, financial, and
   collaborator-private collections from project research.
3. Traversed project-scoped folders directly because account-wide search mixed
   Shared Drive results with unrelated records.
4. Close-read native documents and PDFs when they could support a bounded
   professional claim.
5. Reviewed photographic, video, design, and map collections at metadata level
   unless a specific research question justified content review.
6. Separated claim support from authorship, accuracy, collective credit,
   rights, consent, publication status, adoption, and impact.

The reviewed project-scoped set included 196 Artists Residency, Commercial
Vacancy, Chestnut Avenue Resource Center, NYC Artist Coalition, Sunday Dinner,
WOW List, Fair Rent and Commercial Rent Stabilization, KC Town Hall, ACT at
MIT, Rockaway Film Fest, and UnionDocs materials. A partial or empty connector
listing was treated as unavailable in this run, not as evidence that a drive or
record does not exist.

## Developed claims

### 196 Artists Residency

A protected 2023 acceptance record authored by Jamie documents a repeatable
operating workflow: proposal review, a scheduled residency window, pre-arrival
planning, space configuration, and independent access logistics. This
strengthens the method basis for the existing residency claim.

It does not establish the 20-plus resident aggregate, independently corroborate
the founder role, demonstrate resident outcomes, or authorize publication of a
resident's identity, contact information, work, or access instructions. The
aggregate remains Jamie-approved and self-reported through the public resume.

### Commercial vacancy and lease cost

A protected March 2026 proposal authored by Jamie supports a new bounded claim:
Jamie designed a policy-neutral, privacy-preserving pilot specification for
publishing geography-aggregated commercial vacancy and lease-cost indicators
derived from RPIE filings.

The proposal turns the data gap into a smallest serious release:

- an indicators table;
- a coverage and suppression table;
- a methods note;
- explicit geography and field choices;
- weighted rent and vacancy measures;
- release and suppression metadata;
- exclusions for raw confidential filings, identifiers, parcel- or unit-level
  rows, and lease documents.

The source establishes proposal and method design. It does not establish agency
acceptance, implementation, dataset publication, public presentation, or policy
impact. A related three-image Council District map package remains a metadata-
only research lead until authorship, methodology, accuracy, context, and rights
are verified.

### East Kansas City communications

The Chestnut Avenue Resource Center package establishes that a multi-format
Dumpster Day communications artifact set exists, including editable banner and
flier files, export variants, a distribution photograph, and geospatial
reference material.

This narrows the research gap but does not close it. Shared Drive custody and
artifact existence do not establish Jamie's authorship, printing or
distribution quantities, event outcomes, or permission to publish. The wider
pro bono communications candidate remains in research pending artifact-level
metadata and partner confirmation.

gathering count. Visible filenames do not establish data accuracy, event
meaning, participant consent, platform adoption, or Jamie's individual role.

ACT at MIT, Rockaway Film Fest, and UnionDocs media packages were inventoried
but not turned into accomplishment claims in this pass. Their project context,
Jamie's exact role, and publication rights require separate research before

## Website decision

No archive-derived website claim or project copy is warranted in this pass.
The commercial-vacancy method claim and media leads deepen the palette
available to future audience briefs, while the 196 record quietly strengthens
the source basis of an existing public claim. Separate eval-driven interface
and resume edits may improve the portfolio without projecting this protected
archive. The portfolio remains a selective composition, not an archive index.

## Protected boundaries

The public repository excludes raw Drive links and item identifiers, resident
and participant identities, contact and access details, meeting contents,
private correspondence, community records, legal and family context, raw
recordings, unapproved images, and source files. KC Town Hall family-crisis and
project-transition circumstances remain deliberately unencoded.
