# CallNYC Hackathon Research Note, 2016

This is a public-safe research note for the CallNYC citation pass. It records
what the public sources support, what remains unresolved, and which claims should
not be projected into the public case study yet.

## Recovered Public Sources

- Civic Hall announced a New York City Council hackathon focused on constituent
  services for January 30, 2016, from 1-3 p.m.
- An event-day New York City Council post identified the gathering at Civic Hall
  as its first CouncilStat hackathon.
- A promotional graphic attached to the Civic Hall post used the branding "New
  York City Council Hackathon" and displayed `labs.council.nyc`.
- A Wayback capture of Civic Hall's events page preserves the Civic Hall and New
  York City Council social posts through the page's embedded feed.
- Politico New York covered CallNYC on March 14, 2016 and supports Jamie's
  participation in the January Civic Hall event, the independent follow-on
  project after the fuller dataset release, and the Council's caution that
  offices used CouncilStat differently and with different frequency.
- The public CallNYC GitHub repository supports the existence of the independent
  public code artifact.

## Negative Findings

- No dedicated Civic Hall calendar listing or event-detail page was recovered.
- The Wayback page preserves embedded social posts; it is not the missing
  calendar listing.
- No agenda was recovered.
- No breakout roster was recovered.
- No registration-form contents were recovered.
- No complete attendee list was recovered.
- No published project roster or formal winners were recovered.
- The unapproved location-label claim remains supported only by the
  participant photograph archive.
- The participant photograph's approximately 2:10 p.m. timestamp is not the
  event start time.
- The formal official event title remains unrecovered.

## Search Scope

The reviewed research covered:

- 4,630 deduplicated HTML captures;
- 1,240 original URLs;
- 296 distinct event URL keys;
- 215 successful event pages;
- 74 redirects;
- 7 captured 404s.

This is a scoped negative finding, not proof that no listing ever existed.

## Participant Photograph Boundary

Do not create a public source link for the participant photograph until:

- the image is added to a public-safe asset path;
- photographer and rights status are known;
- recognizable participants are reviewed;
- caption context is approved.

Until then, the location-label claim remains a research note, not public
case-study copy.
