# Social Account Archive Production - July 14, 2026

## Purpose

This public-safe research record treats project social accounts as engagement,
operations, and public-documentation surfaces. It preserves useful findings
without assuming that Jamie authored every post or that a social interaction
proves endorsement, adoption, constituent use, or policy causation.

The structured records live in
`apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/social-account-archive-production-2026-07-14.ts`.

## Method

The review used an authenticated, read-only X session. It inspected public
profiles, scrolled available timelines, ran from-account campaign searches,
collected indexed mentions, deduplicated status URLs, and classified selected
public-official accounts against office dates.

All counts below are recovered minimums from July 14, 2026. X search and
timeline interfaces do not provide a stable or complete historical denominator,
especially for likes, follows, deleted posts, or unindexed replies.
