# NYC Artist Coalition Facebook Post Archival Production

Date: 2026-07-14

## Question

What does the complete currently recoverable NYC Artist Coalition Facebook
Page-post population establish about collective participation infrastructure,
campaign continuity, source routing, stakeholder address, visible response,
and Jamie's role without turning Page identity into individual authorship or
social activity into impact?

## Population Control

Two independent authenticated traversals, using different scroll cadences,
recovered the same **444 post identities**. The first traversal stopped after
42 consecutive scroll actions added nothing; the second stopped after 41. The
current chronology runs from January 29, 2017, through September 15, 2021.
Both sorted identity sets produce the same non-identifying SHA-256 fingerprint:
`f1f00d902415ebad0aa37043d7f64070d754c17449c396ac61aa34dec7733955`.
The canonical preimage is one exact, unnormalized UTF-8 `records[].key` value
per line, sorted in ascending bytewise order under `LC_ALL=C`, with LF
delimiters and a final LF. Each of the 444 unique retained keys appears once.

Every retained record received a form, primary-theme, route, and visible-counter
disposition. The record-level census remains outside the public repository.
This repo stores public-safe aggregates and selected public sources only.

This is 100 percent disposition coverage of the July 2026 Page surface. It is
not a native Meta export, deletion history, or proof that no historical post is
missing.

An earlier 441-record checkpoint missed three surviving routes: two unavailable
Page-status shells and one Brooklyn Anti-Gentrification Network event route.
The independent 444-ID rerun resolves that checkpoint discrepancy.

## Visible Response Boundary

The stronger finding is structural: over several years, the Page repeatedly
connected people, places, meetings, campaigns, institutions, sources, and
practical resources through a coherent collective identity.

## Jamie's Role

Facebook currently lists NYC Artist Coalition among Pages Jamie manages and
states that he has task access to manage it with tools. Jamie remembers being
predominantly the person using the Page while also remembering that other
coalition participants used it.

These are separate evidence classes. Current management access does not prove
historical exclusivity. First-person memory does not identify the publisher of
any specific post or quantify a share of publishing labor. The reviewed public
timeline and Meta content control expose the institutional Page identity, not a
historic human publisher field.

The defensible current posture is therefore:

- current Page management and task access: confirmed, protected source;
- predominant historic use: Jamie-attributed, use with care;
- shared use by other coalition participants: explicit boundary;
- individual historic post authorship: unresolved;
- sole administration or sole authorship: disallowed.

## Anti-Claims

- Do not call 444 a lifetime post total or native Meta export.
- Do not say Jamie published or authored all 444 records.
- Do not infer a human publisher from Page identity or Page-level controls.
- Do not say Jamie was the sole Page administrator or sole author of the
  coalition's public voice.
- Do not transfer credit from Olympia Kazi, collaborators, event hosts,
  campaign participants, sources, public officials, agencies, or spaces.
- Do not convert outgoing stakeholder references into inbound engagement.
- Do not convert mutable counters or rounded follower displays into exact
  analytics, unique people, attendance, reach, endorsement, causality, or
  impact.
- Do not infer policy causality from the social record.
