**Reviewed:** 2026-07-14
**Method:** authenticated, read-only review of public X profiles, timelines,
status pages, replies, and campaign-site links

## Purpose

Project social accounts are both engagement surfaces and public-documentation
records. This pass identifies the project identities Jamie established,
recovers mission-relevant interaction evidence, and routes defensible findings
through the knowledge-bank lifecycle without assigning every team post to
Jamie or converting social activity into institutional endorsement.

Authenticated access was used only to inspect public material. Private
messages, analytics, credentials, cookies, drafts, and session data were not
collected or published.

## Engagement rule

For this production pass, a Council-member account counts as directly engaged
when a recoverable member-authored post, quote post, repost, or reply names,
amplifies, or participates in a thread with the project account. A one-way tag
from the project account, an unattributed aggregate reaction, a follow, or an
inferred view does not count.

The result describes account-level public behavior. It does not establish an
official Council position, endorsement, adoption, reach, policy causality, or
the individual teammate who authored the project-side post.
