# KC Spaces Fund Facebook Post Census

Date: 2026-07-14

## Question

What does the complete surviving observed KC Spaces Fund Facebook Page surface
establish about public operations, grantee recognition, source and action
routing, visible response, and Jamie's digital-operations role without
converting Page identity into individual authorship or campaign activity into
impact?

## Population Control

An authenticated top-to-bottom traversal surfaced **38 post or remnant
records** spanning April 7 through July 9, 2020. The capture was treated as
terminal after 40 consecutive scrolls produced no additional records. Every
surfaced record received a public-safe disposition.

This is 100 percent disposition coverage of the surviving observed July 2026
Page surface. It is not a native Meta export, deletion history, or proof that
no historical post is missing. Facebook's live interface re-renders some card
states; the canonical ledger preserves the stable 38-record accounting rather
than treating every render state as a new post.

The public ledger retains only stable IDs, sequence, form, primary theme,
classification counts, visible-signal booleans, and disposition status. Raw
post text, comments, per-record metrics, relationship data, unstable action
links, authentication material, and manager state remain outside the public
repository.

## Jamie's Role

The existing archival record supports a bounded role: Jamie provided
behind-the-scenes digital infrastructure, including the Ghost campaign site,
campaign-theme implementation, fundraising-widget work, deployment, and
platform continuity. He was not the public stakeholder or owner posting on the
Facebook Page, and the Page record should not be used to assign him campaign
organizer, fundraiser, grant-making, or post-authorship credit.

Jamie also recalls supporting selection of the KC Spaces Fund name so one
identity was available across social platforms and domain names. The resulting
public identity is directly inspectable: kcspacesfund.com links matching
KCSpacesFund identities on Facebook, X, and Instagram.

The result and the role are separate claims. Uniform surviving routes establish
the identity system. Jamie's first-person clarification supports a bounded
contribution to selecting an available cross-channel name; name-development
records, account and domain creation chronology, and collaborator proof notes
would be required to claim sole naming authorship or account ownership.
