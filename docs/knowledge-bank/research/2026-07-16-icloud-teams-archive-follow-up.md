**Method:** prior-pass reconciliation, data-fork availability checks, anchor-first close reading, duplicate review, atomic claim decomposition, public-safety review, and eval-backed validation

## Purpose

This follow-up develops the July 14 archival-production pass without mistaking
folder depth for evidentiary depth. It asks a narrower question: what
non-duplicative professional evidence can one additional close reading recover
from each required collection, and what must remain held?

The website is a rhetorical projection of the knowledge bank. None of the
records below was selected for the current public-site argument merely because
it became defensible enough to enter the bank.

## Reconciliation

The follow-up began with the existing source, claim, intake, and inquiry records.
Candidates already represented by stronger sources were not re-ingested.
Selected iCloud placeholders were re-requested and checked for allocated,
readable data rather than inferred from filenames or logical sizes.

The Sunday Dinner root capture remained dataless after a fresh request. It was
therefore left in the materialization inquiry and was not counted as a reviewed
