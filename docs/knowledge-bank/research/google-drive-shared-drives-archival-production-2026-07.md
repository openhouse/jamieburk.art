# Google Drive Shared Drives Archival Production - July 2026

## Purpose

This bounded pass used Jamie's Google Drive Shared Drives as a private research
environment for source discovery, workflow evidence, and future photo-editor
work. No Drive ID, private URL, raw document, participant name, access
instruction, or sensitive excluded material is stored in the public repository.

The typed lifecycle record is
`apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/google-drive-archive-production.ts`.

## Method

1. Inventoried the available Shared Drives and selected project-relevant
   materials rather than recursively publishing file lists.
2. Closely read one private operating record and performed metadata review on
   three audiovisual archives.
3. Converted useful evidence into public-safe source descriptions, atomic
   claims, anti-claims, and inquiries.
4. Used opaque protected locator IDs in place of Drive IDs or URLs.
5. Kept all claims out of active public composition and kept every media asset
   on rights and consent hold.
6. Left excluded personal and sensitive materials outside the knowledge bank.

## Result

The pass added four intake records, four protected source records, three
bounded claims, and three research inquiries.

### 196 Artists Residency

A private acceptance and onboarding record supports Jamie's operational role
across proposal review, invitations, check-ins, space configuration, and access
coordination. It demonstrates one workflow, not residency-wide scale or
participant outcomes. Participant identity and access instructions remain
private.

### WOW List

Shared-drive metadata identifies a 2015 video titled "Members Meeting: What is
wowlist.org?" The video is a high-value research candidate, but it has not been
transcribed or visually reviewed. It remains blocked from substantive claims
and public use pending speaker, participant, and rights review.

### Fair Rent NYC

A February 2026 event-workflow folder contains 42 photograph files and two
launch-video files. Metadata establishes archival depth and a photo-editor
queue, not who appears, what occurred, attendance, photographer credit,
consent, or public-use rights. Every asset remains on hold.

### Sunday Dinner

A private Zoom archive contains 33 video, conversion, and split-archive assets.
That is an asset count, not an event, attendance, participant, or outcome
count. The archive requires materialization, reconstruction, and consent review
before it can support distinct-event or public-media claims.

## Boundaries

- Private operational evidence can support a public-safe claim without making
  the underlying artifact public.
- Metadata establishes that an artifact exists and describes its file context;
  it does not establish the artifact's substantive content.
- Thirty-three stored assets do not establish thirty-three events.
- Forty-two photographs do not establish forty-two participants or moments.
- A media archive is not approved portfolio media until identity, credit,
  consent, rights, and editorial purpose are reviewed.
- Sensitive excluded context does not enter the professional knowledge bank
  merely because it exists in an archive.

## Eval Contract

The lifecycle eval fails if a Drive identifier or URL enters public data, a
private source becomes renderable, the WOW List video becomes substantive
evidence before review, Fair Rent media loses its rights hold, the residency
record exposes participant or access details, or the Sunday Dinner asset count
becomes an event count.
