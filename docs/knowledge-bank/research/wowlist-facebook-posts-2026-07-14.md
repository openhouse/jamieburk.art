# WOW List Facebook Post Census

Date: 2026-07-14

## Question

What does the complete surviving WOW List Facebook Page-post population
establish about Jamie's publishing role, project practice, source routing, and
stakeholder participation without converting social activity into sole
authorship, adoption, endorsement, or impact?

## Population Control

The authenticated Page-manager interface was traversed twice from the top of
the WOW List Page-post timeline to a stable terminal position. Each independent
pass recovered 54 rendered Page-post records. One May 23, 2016,
community-philosophy post appeared both in the Featured module and in its
chronological position, leaving **53 distinct surviving posts**.

Every rendered record received a disposition. Facebook's manager-only
publisher label displayed **Published by Jamie Burkart** on all 54 rendered
records in both passes.

This is 100 percent disposition coverage of the surviving July 2026 Page
surface. It is not a native Meta export, deletion history, or proof that no post
was deleted, hidden, or made unavailable before capture.

The recovered range runs from April 25, 2015, through March 22, 2018. The
current Page displayed 185 followers and two following on July 14, 2026.
Those mutable values are interface context, not historical reach or adoption.

## Separate Content-Library Control

The authenticated Meta Content Library was filtered to Lifetime and displayed
five published rows:

- March 22, 2018;
- July 3, 2017;
- April 14, 2017;
- March 22, 2017; and
- March 15, 2017.

The older public Page timeline exposed a much larger surviving population. The
five-row modern library is therefore retained as a separate product-surface
control, not treated as the historical denominator.

## Jamie's Role

The recovered manager attribution materially strengthens Jamie's account of
managing WOW List's social presence. The strongest defensible statement is:

> Jamie operated WOW List's Facebook Page publishing surface. Across the
> complete surviving Page-post record, Facebook's manager view attributed all
> 53 distinct posts to Jamie.

This supports publishing action. It does not prove sole lifetime administration
of the Page or every WOW List social account. It also does not turn Jamie into
the author of every shared quotation, image, event, campaign statement, linked
article, or participant contribution.

WOW List was Jamie and Richard's project and depended on members, organizers,
artists, venues, and local scene maintainers. That collective context remains
part of the claim rather than a footnote to it.

## What The Record Shows

### Product and community practice

The Page repeatedly translated the product into practical invitations: join a
local calendar, add events, receive a weekly list, give product feedback, or
make a local identity useful to a scene. It also praised contributors who added
events and amplified a member-created tutorial.

A featured May 2016 post preserves Shermy Turtel's public account of a WOW List
members' online hangout. The statement describes a community-first design that
reduced ownership friction and made gathering easier. This is valuable
participant testimony, not a representative satisfaction survey.

### Cultural-space care

The largest category routes campaigns, reporting, funds, meetings, and mutual
aid related to threatened DIY and all-ages spaces, Ghost Ship, venue safety,
post-fire recovery, displacement, rent pressure, and public or philanthropic
support.

Two newly close-read articles strengthen this context:

- Patricia Calhoun,
  [City Partners With Meow Wolf on $20,000 Denver DIY Spaces Fund](https://www.westword.com/arts-culture/city-partners-with-meow-wolf-on-20-000-denver-diy-spaces-fund-8782025/),
  *Denver Westword*, March 9, 2017. The article documents a Denver Arts &
  Venues and Meow Wolf funding arrangement after venue closures and increased
  safety scrutiny.
- Matthew Singer,
  [The Know Is Closing](https://www.wweek.com/bars/2016/07/01/the-know-is-closing/),
  *Willamette Week*, July 1, 2016. The article attributes a Portland punk
  venue's planned closure to a substantial rent increase while noting an
  intended relocation.

These are sources WOW List routed. They are not press coverage of WOW List and
do not make WOW List or Jamie the organizer, partner, beneficiary, or cause of
the work they describe.

### Civic translation

The Page adapted a community-calendar form to Standing Rock actions, the
Women's March, anti-racist and anti-sexist public gatherings, and related civic
mobilization.

The July 3, 2017,
[Let NYC Dance post](https://www.facebook.com/wowlist/posts/702379893302154)
is especially important. Jamie's publisher attribution is joined to a public
post that frames Cabaret Law repeal as support for community spaces and routes
readers into NYC Artist Coalition action and reporting. It demonstrates a
cross-project bridge between event-sharing infrastructure and cultural-policy
advocacy.

The shared campaign material retains NYC Artist Coalition and collective
credit. Tags addressed elected and cultural stakeholders; they do not prove
that each stakeholder saw, endorsed, or acted on the post, and the post does
not establish causality for Cabaret Law repeal.

## Stakeholder Participation Pattern

The surviving record includes public evidence of local-scene maintainers and
members:

- promoting WOW List as an alternative way to find events;
- updating Phoenix, Los Angeles, Santa Barbara, Seattle, San Francisco,
  Chicago, and Iowa City calendar identities;
- adding events and inviting others to contribute;
- making a product tutorial;
- using the platform for tours and cultural events; and
- articulating community-first project values.

This is mission-relevant traction in the form of participation and public use.
It is not a complete user census, a representative satisfaction measure,
formal partnership evidence, or proof of platform-wide adoption.

## Posted Destinations

The current rendered interface exposed 29 destination occurrences across 26
records, resolving to 27 canonical destinations:

- 19 WOW List routes; and
- 8 external scene, organizing, fundraising, or cultural-space routes.

Some historical link cards exposed a title and domain but no exact destination
URL. Those remain labeled research routes rather than receiving invented URLs.
Current URL normalization does not prove that each destination resolved
identically when posted.

## Engagement Boundary

The current Page retained follower displays and scattered reaction, comment,
event-response, and shared-source metrics. The interface also exposed at least
one later public comment expressing continued interest in WOW List.

These values were not aggregated into an impact claim because:

- they are mutable July 2026 observations;
- some belong to embedded or shared source material;
- labels were not stable across all renderings;
- they do not identify unique people; and
- they do not establish active use, adoption, endorsement, attendance,
  causality, or impact.

The stronger traction evidence is the bounded participant-use pattern in the
post corpus, not a brittle sum of current interface counters.

## Anti-Claims

- Do not call this a native Meta export or deletion history.
- Do not say WOW List published only 53 Facebook posts in its lifetime.
- Do not say Jamie was the sole lifetime administrator of every WOW List
  social account.
- Do not say Jamie authored every sentence, quotation, event, image, campaign,
  or linked source.
- Do not transfer source, organizer, participant, venue, or campaign credit to
  Jamie or WOW List.
- Do not convert follower or interaction displays into historical analytics,
  unique people, adoption, endorsement, reach, or impact.
- Do not convert tagging into stakeholder engagement or endorsement.
- Do not infer causality for Cabaret Law repeal or any other public outcome
  from a social post.

## Lifecycle Decisions

- The 53-post population, theme, destination, and stakeholder-pattern claims
  remain corroborated reserve knowledge.
- Jamie's Page-publishing practice is promoted to public-ready reserve depth
  with collective-credit and causality boundaries.
- All five claims remain deferred from the website pending audience-specific
  composition and human editorial review.
- A native Meta Page export and collaborator proof notes remain open research
  routes for administrator chronology, deleted-post accounting, and division
  of labor.
