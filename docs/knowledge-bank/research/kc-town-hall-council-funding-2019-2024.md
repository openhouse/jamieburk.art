# KC Town Hall: Board Recommendation, Council Action, and Later Disposition

Reviewed: 2026-07-14
Public-safe research status: Recovered

## Result

The official record supports a stronger and more exact sequence than the
portfolio's former recommendation-only wording:

1. The CCED Board packet names Jamie Burkart as the KC Town Hall presenter.
2. On July 16, 2019, all four participating Board members voted to recommend
   the $490,539 proposal to the City Council for approval and funding.
3. On September 26, 2019, the Council adopted Resolution 190649 as substituted,
   accepting the recommendation and authorizing funding-agreement negotiations.
4. The Council also passed Ordinance 190642 as substituted. Its authenticated
   text appropriates Round Two CCED project funds and lists a $490,539 KC Town
   Hall award.
5. Ordinance 240317 records that KC Town Hall later withdrew and that the City
   reclaimed the unused $490,539 allocation in 2024.

The defensible public claim is **Council appropriation**, not only Board
recommendation. Appropriation does not establish an executed funding agreement,
disbursement, receipt, expenditure, construction, or completion.

## Claim Boundaries

- The four affirmative votes belong to the CCED Board, not to a recovered
  Council roll-call tally.
- Presenter does not mean sole author, sole owner, or sole organizer.
- Council adoption and appropriation are government actions; do not convert
  them into sole causality by Jamie or KC Town Hall.
- Resolution 190649 authorized negotiations. It did not itself execute a
  funding agreement.
- Ordinance 240317 does not state why the project withdrew or assign individual
  responsibility.
- The City's administrative wording does not establish that Jamie abandoned
  the project or describe how project stewardship changed.
- The later unused-funds record does not erase the earlier recommendation,
  adoption, or appropriation.
