## Data-Quality Assessment

**Intended use:** Develop source-associated professional knowledge and a ranked
research path. This set is not sufficient by itself to publish Jamie-specific
campaign authorship, policy causality, aggregate Sunday Dinner metrics, or a
claim that MARCH was disbanded.

**Coverage:** The set spans one contemporaneous person-specific interview, one
current project surface, five coalition-authored campaign or resource artifacts,
one independent article, and two government primary records. That combination
is useful for separating Jamie's public role from collective apparatus and
institutional outcome.

**Uniqueness:** All ten canonical URLs and stable source IDs are new to the bank.
The pages overlap thematically but have distinct evidentiary jobs: interview,
project surface, campaign artifact, event credit, collective policy text,
safety resource, independent coverage, and enacted law.

**Validity:** Every record names affirmative support and non-support. Current
live pages were accessed on July 13, 2026. Publication dates are omitted where
the page did not make them visible rather than inferred from an event date.

**Known uncertainty:** The Greene Hill title says Julie Fredenberg while the body
says Julia. The source inconsistency is preserved. The current Sunday Dinner
homepage is sparse. Coalition pages do not expose individual implementation
credit. Government records establish law and scope, not advocacy causality.
