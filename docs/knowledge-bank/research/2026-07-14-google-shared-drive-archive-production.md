# Google Shared Drive archive production

**Research date:** 2026-07-14
**Account scope:** 110 accessible Shared Drives in the dated inventory snapshot
**Method:** governed inventory, professionally relevant selection, close reading, revision-history review, atomic claim decomposition, public-safety review, editorial selection, and eval-backed validation

## Purpose

Jamie uses Shared Drives as handoff infrastructure across devices and
collaborators. This pass treats that practice as a source environment, not a
publication queue. The goal is to recover defensible operating evidence while
keeping access, authorship, consent, and public use as separate decisions.

Comprehensive coverage means that every accessible Shared Drive entered a
governed inventory. It does not mean indiscriminate file-by-file extraction.
Close reading focused on professionally relevant, text-rich project records
with a credible relationship to current portfolio questions. Sensitive
personal, family, legal, financial, credential-bearing, and collaborator-private
spaces were excluded from content ingestion.

## Evidence rules

- A drive title or file listing is an artifact lead, not role evidence.
- Revision history can support authorship or maintenance only for the reviewed record.
- A self-authored proposal supports the proposal and its method, not adoption or outcomes.
- A research draft can guide source discovery without corroborating its own claims.
- A spreadsheet schema can document workflow design without making its rows public.
- Storage access is not publication permission.
- Visual material requires separate role, rights, subject-consent, caption, and evidence-scope review.

Private Drive identifiers, links, local sync paths, contact details, credentials,
meeting links, and row-level records are not stored in the public repository.

## Public selections

Four public-safe workflow claims were selected because they materially reduce a
hiring reader's burden:

1. A consent-aware stakeholder operations tracker for Fair Rent NYC and Commercial Rent Stabilization.
2. A multilingual Fair Rent NYC / Action Lab alignment record connecting facilitation to decisions and next steps.
3. A concrete 196 Artists Residency onboarding and access workflow.
4. A recurring Sunday Dinner invitation and continuity system.

Each selected sentence is backed by a non-rendered private evidence relationship.
The website does not cite or link the underlying Shared Drive files.

The Fair Rent web launch runbook and Comptroller scoping brief remain in the
knowledge bank as defensible but unselected claims. This preserves depth without
turning the case study into an evidence inventory.

## Protected boundaries

This pass does not publish:

- Shared Drive or file identifiers and links;
- names, emails, phone numbers, relationship notes, quotations, or stakeholder rows;
- credentials, meeting links, administration details, or billing information;
- raw meeting records or private campaign strategy;
- resident-artist and guest identities, attendance histories, or access instructions;
- visual assets without rights, consent, role, and caption review;
- sensitive personal, family, legal, financial, or collaborator-private material.
