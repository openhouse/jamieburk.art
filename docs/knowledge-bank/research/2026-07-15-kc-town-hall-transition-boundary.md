# KC Town Hall transition boundary: July 15, 2026

Jamie clarified that he transitioned the KC Town Hall project to a
mission-aligned organization. This is an authorized first-person account of a
professional action. It is not independent corroboration.

## Separate evidence tracks

Kansas City records establish the administrative funding history: the Council
appropriated $490,539 to the project account, the funding agreement was not
completed, no funds were disbursed, and the unused award was reappropriated in
2024.

Those records do not establish that Jamie abandoned the project. Jamie's
account separately supports the statement that he transitioned the project to
a mission-aligned organization.

The portfolio must not collapse these records into a single inferred causal
story. The City record describes the funding process. Jamie's account describes
his project transition.

## Public-safe wording

Approved wording for the KC Town Hall case study:

> Jamie reports later transitioning the project to a mission-aligned
> organization.

This qualifier makes the evidence class visible without burdening the reader.

## Boundaries

- Do not identify the receiving organization without separate evidence and
  approval.
- Do not infer a sale, deed transfer, ownership transfer, property disposition,
  or current operating status.
- Do not describe withdrawal from the funding process as project abandonment.
- Do not investigate, record, or publish private circumstances surrounding the
  transition.
- Keep the Council appropriation, non-disbursement, and reappropriation record
  intact.

The transition claim is approved for the knowledge bank and the KC Town Hall
case study. It is not selected for the work index, Technical Operations page,
or downloadable resume.
