## Projection boundary

The hiring argument is not that Jamie should be hired as a video producer,
artist, or social-network researcher. The useful claim is narrower and stronger:
his operating practice includes the capacity to notice a naturally occurring
structure, form an original model, prototype and explain it, and make it
inhabitable with other people. This helps explain the character of later civic,
community, technical, and implementation work without forcing every early
project onto the website.
