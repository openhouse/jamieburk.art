## Method and counting rules

The review used an authenticated browser session because logged-out X views were incomplete and inconsistent. The collection process:

1. Opened each public profile in Jamie's authenticated session and recorded a dated profile-post count.
2. Reconciled Posts and Replies views where a bounded profile population was feasible.
3. Opened public repost lists for CallNYC-authored posts and replies that displayed repost activity.
4. Preserved named public interaction URLs and deduplicated people across interaction types.
5. Cross-checked officeholder status against official Council records for the relevant period.
6. Excluded likes, private activity, direct messages, private analytics, account settings, and metrics attached to external posts merely reposted by a project account.
7. Treated deleted, private, suspended, hidden, renamed, and unindexed records as limits on recovery.

An **engaged Council-member account** means a public account belonging to a person serving on the Council at the time appeared in a CallNYC public repost list or authored a recoverable public post, quote, or reply involving the project. It does not mean endorsement, adoption, formal partnership, or agreement with every project claim.
