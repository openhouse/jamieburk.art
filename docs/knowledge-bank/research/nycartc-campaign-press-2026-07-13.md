# NYC Artist Coalition campaign press corpus

Date recovered: July 13, 2026

This public-safe research pass recovered the explicit press sections from four
campaign sites associated with NYC Artist Coalition. Three sections remain on
live campaign pages. The FairRentNYC section was recovered from the supplied
December 1, 2021 Wayback capture because the current homepage no longer
presents that historical press list.

The corpus contains 45 campaign placements representing 44 distinct articles:

- Let NYC Dance: 21 placements;
- Talks Not Raids: 7 placements;
- Save NYC Spaces: 8 placements;
- FairRentNYC: 9 placements.

NPR's Cabaret Law article appears on both Let NYC Dance and Save NYC Spaces.
That repeated placement remains visible while the article has one stable source
identity. The Gothamist Cabaret Law article, the NPR article, and the Bedford +
Bowery nightlife-town-hall article already had canonical records; this pass
reuses those records and creates 41 new article sources.

## Evidence posture

The four campaign-index records are closely read because their article
placements are directly inspectable. The 41 newly created article records are
queued for article-level close reading. Four typed research tasks direct future
agents to recover live or archived text, extract atomic propositions, preserve
collective credit, and distinguish direct role, campaign context, reported
opinion, institutional outcome, and source limitations.

The aggregate corpus claim remains corroborated and unprojected. Press-section
selection does not establish audience reach, favorable sentiment, publisher
endorsement, Jamie's authorship of independent articles, or campaign causation
for Cabaret Law repeal, Office of Nightlife creation, MARCH changes, or
commercial-rent policy outcomes. The press sections may not be exhaustive.
