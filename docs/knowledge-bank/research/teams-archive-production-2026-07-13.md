# Teams Archive Production, July 2026

## Purpose

This pass examined selected working records from three private Teams archive
areas: Jamie Projects History, CRS, and job-hunt. The goal was to develop the
knowledge bank without publishing private source files or treating every
archived fragment as a public claim.

The private archive remains outside the repository. The knowledge bank stores
only public-safe source descriptions, bounded propositions, anti-claims, and
editorial decisions.

## Method

1. Began with generated archive overviews and existing orientation documents.
2. Moved to a small set of authored or operational artifacts identified by the
   first reading.
3. Waited for selected iCloud-backed files to materialize rather than forcing
   full-tree hydration.
4. Separated planning evidence from implemented work.
5. Separated private source support from permission to publish the source.
6. Added each mature claim to the knowledge graph with an explicit defer
   decision, so evidentiary strength does not automatically change the site.

## Recursive eval hill climb

An independent judge found the new archive records defensible but identified a
legacy-system weakness: nine already projected CallNYC, Harry J. Epstein,
FairRentNYC, and WOWList claims still relied on migration-era source links
without proposition-level support contracts.

The remediation added nine bounded close readings and wired each inherited
claim through atomic propositions and required semantic support tags. The
lifecycle evaluator now enforces that contract for migrated claims as soon as
support tags are declared. This preserves gradual migration while preventing a
hardened claim from silently falling back to the legacy exception.
