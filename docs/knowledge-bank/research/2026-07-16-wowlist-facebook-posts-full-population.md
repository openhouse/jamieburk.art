## Question

What mission-relevant sources, claims, operating patterns, and recoverable
stakeholder responses appear across the full capture-date population of WOW
List Facebook Page posts?

## Population reconciliation

Three authenticated traversals reached repeated no-growth terminal states. The
interface rendered 79, 81, and 90 raw cards because Facebook loaded the same
post more than once as link previews appeared and randomized the
single-character accessibility text used for nested timestamps.

After normalizing those interface artifacts without removing substantive post
language, all three traversals produced the same 57 semantic posts and the same
SHA-256 population fingerprint. The visible post-date range was April 25, 2015
through March 22, 2018.

This is 100 percent of the Page stream exposed during the July 16 review. It is
not a native Meta owner export, deletion history, or proof that no post was ever
deleted, withheld, made private, or left unindexed. Meta Business Suite did not
offer WOW List in its available Page-export control during the review, so no
owner export was generated or claimed.

## Source field

Fifty-one of 57 semantic posts contained a recovered destination. Twenty-eight
contained an external destination. The deduplicated field contains 57 URLs:

- 30 WOW List destinations; and
- 27 external destinations.

The public manifest retains 24 public-safe external URLs. Two historic
direct-aid destinations are counted but withheld because their surviving pages
may expose sensitive beneficiary information. One malformed video destination
is counted but not published as a usable source.

Five destinations were close-read and decomposed:

- Meow Wolf's 2016 DIY Fund announcement;
- Westword's 2017 reporting on a Denver DIY-spaces fund;
- Willamette Week's 2016 report on the Know's closure;
- East Bay Express reporting after the Ghost Ship fire; and
- the archived Bring Back Shea Stadium Kickstarter campaign.

Together they show that the Page's source field repeatedly returned to the
continuity, safety, financing, legal conditions, and displacement of small
cultural spaces. They establish what the Page circulated. They do not make WOW
List the author, partner, administrator, or cause of the underlying work.

## Operating practice

The complete population makes Facebook legible as an operating surface around
the product rather than a detached promotional channel. Repeated uses include:

- routing people from Facebook toward WOW List and independent community
  calendars;
- distributing tours and events across cities;
- inviting member meetings, feedback, and community-created product
  instruction;
- amplifying space-funding, safety, closure, and emergency-support resources;
- adapting the event model to mutual-aid and civic collections; and
- recirculating organizers' own language while preserving their authorship.

The Page includes examples connected to Phoenix, Los Angeles, Seattle, Santa
Barbara, Chicago, Iowa City, Kansas City, and touring projects. These are
documented uses and circulation paths, not official chapters, a complete
adoption census, or evidence of attendance.

## Traction and stakeholder boundary

The capture exposed one identifiable incoming public comment saying the
project was missed and suggesting it might have renewed relevance as Twitter
destabilized. A second comment body was visible without recoverable authorship
and is not counted as direct incoming engagement.

The Page's nested source posts and shared content involve DIY organizers,
cultural spaces, touring artists, arts organizations, and coalition work. Those
records show circulation among relevant communities. A Page share is not, by
itself, independent stakeholder engagement with WOW List.

The current profile displayed 185 followers and 2 following. These are mutable
reconciliation controls, not historical reach, unique people, conversion,
adoption, attendance, endorsement, or impact.

## Jamie's role

Jamie remembers managing WOW List's social presence and names WOW List as a
project he built with Richard. The memory now has a stable protected intake and
research inquiry. It is not independent corroboration, it does not erase
Richard's project credit, and it does not assign every shared Page post to
Jamie.

The role claim remains held pending a collaborator proof note, a durable Page
administration record, or post-level author evidence that can be reviewed
without publishing private account data.
