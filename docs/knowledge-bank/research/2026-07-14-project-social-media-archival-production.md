## Method

The review used a visibly authenticated X session as Jamie, not logged-out web
results. For each account, it recorded the profile's displayed post count,
deduplicated visible status URLs while scrolling the virtualized timeline, and
classified each recovered status by the account in its URL. It then used X's
chronological live search for direct account mentions and inspected ambiguous
conversation threads.

Historical Council-member names and handles were matched against 2017-era and
2019 roster snapshots preserved with the Talks Not Raids application. Those
project datasets are useful classification evidence, not an official complete
roster or proof of account control on every date.

Recovery counts are minimums:

- profile post counts can include items not recoverable as unique status URLs;
- live search can omit deleted, unavailable, deindexed, restricted, or renamed
  material;
- a search result may match a wider conversation rather than the authored post;
- likes, followers, and reposts expose unstable counts without complete actor
  identity;
- a shared project account does not identify the human author of each post.

## What This Adds To Jamie's Record

The account system is itself professional evidence when stated carefully:

- Jamie confirms that he established durable project identities; that actor
  claim remains a memory lead pending independent corroboration.
- The identities carried usable issue explanations, calls to action, event
  links, public resources, and campaign continuity.
- Shared teams and admired collaborators used those identities over years.
- Public officials replied, amplified, linked, attended, thanked, and described
  joint work through those surfaces.
- The accounts preserve handoff and continuity patterns beyond a single person.

The strongest future claim is not "Jamie wrote thousands of posts." If
account-creation or collaborator evidence is recovered, it may become possible
to say that he helped create public-facing identity and information
infrastructure sturdy enough for collective work to continue. Until then, the
bank keeps that contribution as a memory lead outside website projection.

## Boundaries And Next Research

- Preserve "not recovered" as different from "did not exist."
- Never publish credentials, administrator records, private messages, follower
  exports, private analytics, or account-recovery information.
- Do not infer individual authorship from a shared account.
- Do not turn mentions, likes, reposts, or follower counts into endorsement,
  adoption, policy causality, or a composite engagement score.
- Request an X account-data export to recover missing profile-counted items and
  more stable interaction identities.
- Seek collaborator confirmation for account establishment, shared stewardship,
  and post-level authorship where it materially affects a public claim.
- Preserve high-value status URLs through stable web archives before further
  platform loss.
