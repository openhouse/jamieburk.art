# NTER CHNG archive recovery

**Research date:** 2026-07-15
**Status:** Recovered with physical-display details still open

## Question

Can the archived America: Now and Here website verify Jamie Burkart and the
NTER CHNG collaborators' relationship to its 2011 Kansas City program?

## Method

1. Close-read the supplied January 28, 2011 NTER CHNG Wayback capture.
2. Enumerate the surviving public `nterchng.com` Wayback inventory.
3. Identify the former `kansascity.americanowandhere.org` domain through
   contemporaneous public reporting.
4. Review 217 deduplicated successful captures in that domain's public CDX
   inventory for artist, project, and program records.
5. Close-read the recovered visual-artists index and the dedicated page for
   Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier.
6. Compare those records with the Nerman Museum's institutional exhibition
   context.

Temporary downloads and raw CDX output were used only for research and were not
committed.

## Findings

- The NTER CHNG project site identifies an interactive texting installation by
  Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier at Arts Incubator Cocoon
  Gallery in Kansas City.
- The America: Now and Here Kansas City visual-artists index lists all three
  collaborators and marks each as a Kansas City artist.
- A dedicated exhibition-site page identifies the trio as visual artists and
  publishes a NTER CHNG artist statement.
- The statement documents a participatory system joining software and spatial
  installation: visitors used phones to contribute to a real-time shared
  gallery dialogue.
- The Nerman Museum page confirms the broader Kansas City launch and its own
  May 11-12 Barbara Kruger truck stop. It does not identify NTER CHNG or its
  collaborators.
- The first-party press release linked from the NTER CHNG page was not present
  in the recovered `nterchng.com` file inventory.

## Evidence boundary

The exhibition archive directly supports the collaborators' Kansas City
visual-artist listing and the dedicated NTER CHNG project context. It does not
show the exact physical display, venue, configuration, or run dates of NTER
CHNG within America: Now and Here. The Nerman Museum page cannot fill that gap.

The archive also does not assign work among the collaborators. Portfolio
language may say Jamie co-created the installation with Drew Bolton and Garrett
Fuselier. It may not assign Jamie sole authorship, sole programming, or sole
installation design without additional evidence.
