# KC Town Hall CCED Allocation Research

Date: 2026-07-13  
Status: Public-record sequence recovered; governed-proof reconciliation open  
Projection: None

## Answer

The Kansas City Council did more than receive the CCED Sales Tax Board's
recommendation. On September 26, 2019, it adopted Resolution 190649 accepting
the Board's $490,539 recommendation for KC Town Hall and authorizing negotiation
of a funding agreement. The same day, it passed Ordinance 190642, which
appropriated $490,539 to the KC Town Hall project account.

This was a Council-approved allocation, not a completed funding outcome. A 2024
Council ordinance says KC Town Hall later withdrew, the allocation remained
unused, and the Council reappropriated the money to the Housing Budget Fund.

The strongest bounded statement currently supported by the public record is:

> KC Town Hall advanced from a CCED Board recommendation to Council acceptance
> and appropriation of $490,539, but the public record does not establish receipt
> or expenditure and later says the project withdrew and the unused allocation
> was reappropriated.

This statement is knowledge-bank material, not approved website copy. The
official records establish institutional actions and outcomes; they do not
establish Jamie's individual role.

## Chronology

1. **July 16, 2019:** The CCED Sales Tax Board voted to recommend $490,539 for
   KC Town Hall.
2. **September 26, 2019:** The Council adopted Resolution 190649, accepting the
   recommendation and authorizing negotiation of an agreement for up to
   $490,539.
3. **September 26, 2019:** The Council passed Ordinance 190642, appropriating
   $490,539 to the KC Town Hall project account.
4. **March 28, 2024:** The Council passed Ordinance 240317, recording that KC
   Town Hall had withdrawn and reappropriating the unused funds.
