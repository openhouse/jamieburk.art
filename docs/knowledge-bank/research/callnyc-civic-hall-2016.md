# CallNYC Civic Hall Research, 2016

## Public-safe finding

Public sources support a January 30, 2016 New York City Council
constituent-services hackathon at Civic Hall. Civic Hall announced a 1-3 p.m.
window. The Council described the gathering as its first CouncilStat hackathon.

The surviving Wayback page preserves Civic Hall and Council social posts through
an embedded feed. It is not represented as a recovered dedicated event listing.

## Bounded archival search

`RUN-CALL-CIVIC-HALL-CDX-2026-07` records a broad search across 4,630
deduplicated HTML captures, 1,240 original URLs, and 296 event URL keys. The pass
included 215 successful pages, 74 redirects, and 7 not-found captures.

No dedicated Civic Hall calendar listing, complete agenda, breakout roster, or
registration-form contents were recovered. Failure to recover those records does
not prove that they never existed.

Raw search output, downloaded archives, browser state, and machine-local paths
remain outside the repository.
