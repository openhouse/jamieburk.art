# CallNYC and Civic Hall, 2016

Last reviewed: 2026-07-11

This public-safe research note explains the CallNYC chronology and the limits
behind its public case-study citations. It contains no participant image, local
path, raw metadata, private filename, GPS record, face tag, or unapproved name.

## Best-supported chronology

- January 30, 2016: The New York City Council held what it described as its
  first CouncilStat hackathon at Civic Hall.
- Civic Hall announced the event for 1-3 p.m. and focused the invitation on
  constituent services. These are announced hours, not proof of actual event
  duration.
- The attached Council-branded graphic reads "New York City Council Hackathon"
  and includes "labs.council.nyc." This is promotional branding, not proof of a
  longer formal registration title.
- Early March 2016: CouncilStat constituent-services data became publicly
  available, according to contemporaneous independent reporting.
- March 2016: Jamie independently developed CallNYC.org as a resident-facing
  civic-data prototype.
- March 14, 2016: Politico New York covered CallNYC.

CallNYC was an independent follow-on project, not an official Council product,
documented formal hackathon submission, or current public-service directory.

## Research pass

The July 11, 2026 research run used Wayback CDX enumeration and event-prefix
review. It covered 4,630 deduplicated HTML captures, 1,240 original URLs, 296
event URL keys, 215 successful pages, 74 redirects, and 7 not-found captures.

The pass recovered Civic Hall embedded social-feed evidence, the Civic Hall
announcement, the New York City Council event-day post, and the promotional
graphic. It did not recover a dedicated Civic Hall calendar listing, dedicated
event-detail page, agenda, breakout roster, or registration-form contents.

The bounded finding is: no dedicated Civic Hall calendar listing or event-detail
page was recovered in this research pass. Absence from the recovered capture set
is not proof that a page never existed. The embedded feed is archival Civic Hall
site evidence, not a recovered calendar listing.

## Known, open, protected

**Known:** January 30 date; Civic Hall venue; announced 1-3 p.m.;
constituent-services focus; exact first CouncilStat wording; promotional
branding; later independent CallNYC development; March 2016 Politico coverage.

**Open:** Formal registration title, complete agenda, full participant roster,
breakout facilitator, other tracks, registration contents, exact Council
recommendations, and any explanation for conflicting historical location data.

**Protected:** Participant photographs, private archive locators, raw EXIF and
GPS data, face tags, unapproved identities, private correspondence, and
unreviewed images.
