# CallNYC and Civic Hall, 2016

**Status:** Public-safe research record
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-11
**Public page:** `/work/callnyc`

This record explains the current CallNYC chronology, the exact support for each
public claim, and the boundaries that remain open or protected. It contains no
private photograph, path, location record, face tag, or unapproved participant
identity.

## Best-Supported Chronology

- **January 30, 2016:** The New York City Council held its first CouncilStat
  hackathon at Civic Hall.
- **January 30, 1–3 p.m.:** Civic Hall's announcement described a New York City
  Council hackathon focused on constituent services.
- **Event setting:** Participant photographs show a placard reading “Digital
  District — Help improve City Council District office operations.” An
  approximate 2:10 p.m. photograph timestamp is evidence about the image, not
  the event's start time.
- **Early March 2016:** The complete CouncilStat constituent-services dataset
  was released publicly.
- **March 2016:** Jamie independently developed CallNYC.org as a follow-on
  resident-facing civic-data prototype.
- **March 14, 2016:** Politico New York covered CallNYC.

CallNYC was not built at the hackathon, commissioned by the Council, or released
as an official Council product.

## Known / Reconstructed / Open / Protected

### Known

The exact event date, Civic Hall venue, CouncilStat wording, Civic Hall's
advertised time and purpose, promotional branding, visible Digital District
placard, independent CallNYC project, and March 2016 Politico coverage are
supported within the boundaries above.

### Reconstructed

The event-to-project chronology combines official posts, participant evidence,
the subsequent dataset release, the archived project, and independent reporting.
No single source supplies the entire sequence.

### Open

The formal registration title, complete agenda, full participant roster,
breakout facilitator, other breakout tracks, registration-form contents, exact
Council recommendations, and explanation for the conflicting photo map pin
remain unresolved.

### Protected

The participant photographs, private archive structure, detailed location and
camera metadata, face tags, unapproved identities, private correspondence, and
unreviewed images remain offline.

## Public Wording Rules

- Say “first CouncilStat hackathon,” not “first civic-data hackathon.”
- Say “Digital District” was a documented breakout label, not the event title.
- Do not present the approximate photograph timestamp as the event start time.
- Say no dedicated listing was recovered in the documented search, not that one
  never existed.
- Describe CallNYC as an independent archived follow-on prototype.
