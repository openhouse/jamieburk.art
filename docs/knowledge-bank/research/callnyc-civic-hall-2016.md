# CallNYC / Civic Hall 2016 Research Note

This note records the public-safe research basis for the CallNYC citation pass.
Temporary research artifacts remain outside the repository.

## Recovered

Civic Hall announced a January 30, 2016, 1-3 p.m. New York City Council
hackathon focused on constituent services. The New York City Council described
the gathering on the event day as its first CouncilStat hackathon.

A surviving promotional graphic used New York City Council Hackathon branding
and included `labs.council.nyc`. This supports recovered event branding. It does
not prove the complete formal registration title.

A Wayback capture of Civic Hall's events page preserves the Civic Hall and NYC
Council posts through an embedded social feed. It is not a recovered dedicated
calendar listing or event-detail page.

## Participant-Archive Evidence

Participant photography documents a breakout placard reading:

```text
Digital District
Help improve City Council District office operations
```

This supports the existence of a Digital District breakout or table prompt
inside the larger event. It does not establish the formal title of the full
event, the facilitator, a breakout roster, a complete agenda, a required
deliverable, or that CallNYC was built during the event.

The participant photograph is not committed here, not linked here, and not used
as a public image. Public image use and photographer credit require separate
rights review.

## Not Recovered

A reviewed Wayback CDX pass did not recover:

- a Civic Hall calendar listing;
- a dedicated Civic Hall event-detail page;
- the event agenda;
- a breakout roster;
- registration-form contents.

The reviewed corpus included 4,630 deduplicated HTML captures representing
1,240 original URLs. A separate all-status event-prefix pass reviewed 296 event
URL keys: 215 successful pages, 74 redirects, and seven captured 404 responses.

The supported claim is: no listing was recovered in this reviewed corpus.

The unsupported claim is: no listing ever existed.

## RSVP Limitation

The RSVP short link resolved to a Google Form under Civic Hall's domain. The
form now returns 404, and no archived copy was recovered. The registration
contents should not be represented as known.

## Public CallNYC Relationship

CallNYC should be described as an independent follow-on project built after the
complete CouncilStat release. It should not be described as an official Council
product, a documented formal hackathon submission, a hackathon winner, or a
project commissioned by the Council.

CallNYC remains an archived civic-tech prototype. Its rankings, officeholders,
statistics, and contact information are historical.

## Anti-Claims

Do not say:

- Jamie built CallNYC during the hackathon.
- Jamie won the hackathon.
- CallNYC was a formal hackathon submission.
- CallNYC was commissioned by the Council.
- Digital District was the official event title.
- The event began at 2:10 p.m.
- The Council officially confirmed the participant-photo timestamp.
- A specific Jamie recommendation was adopted by the Council.
- CouncilStat case counts measured district-office performance.
