# Ten-source expansion: July 13, 2026

This public-safe research pass began with a Greene Hill Food Co-op interview
and added ten new public sources to the typed knowledge graph. Each source has
an accession, a close reading, atomic propositions, explicit limits, and at
least one linked claim. Public-ready claims are deferred from website
projection pending later compositional review.

## Sources and what they add

1. [Greene Hill Food Co-op Q&A](https://www.greenehillfood.coop/master-blog/2017/12/september-2017-newsletter)
   connects Jamie's weekly community dinners and WOWList practice with
   coalition mutual aid, Cabaret Law advocacy, and an Office of Nightlife town
   hall. It does not establish scale, founder status, or policy causation.
2. [Bedford + Bowery on making DIY spaces work](https://bedfordandbowery.com/2017/02/6-things-to-know-about-making-diy-spaces-work/)
   independently identifies Jamie as a NYC Artist Coalition organizer and
   reports his account of more than 100 mutual-aid-network signups. Signups are
   not treated as active membership or impact.
3. [Bedford + Bowery on the Night Mayor town hall](https://bedfordandbowery.com/2017/10/what-can-the-night-mayor-do-the-diy-scene-discusses/)
   says the coalition spearheaded the gathering and names Jamie among coalition
   speakers. The event remains collective work and is not proof of sole policy
   causation.
4. [Save NYC Spaces](https://savenycspaces.nycartc.com/) preserves a
   multi-organization platform concerning MARCH transparency, practical support
   for small cultural spaces, anti-displacement work, and an inclusive Office
   of Nightlife. It is a project-owned record of stated priorities, not proof
   of implementation.
5. [New York City Council Committee on Small Business transcript](https://legistar.council.nyc.gov/View.ashx?GUID=3BAD981A-69D8-4D99-A882-52442F36F5A2&ID=6792384&M=F)
   preserves Jamie's October 2018 testimony connecting cultural-space safety
   and survival with commercial affordability and fair lease renewal. It proves
   testimony, not legislative authorship or outcome.
6. [Kansas City Central City Economic Development board packet](https://www.kcmo.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/3533/637145055055230000)
   names Jamie as presenter of a KC Town-Hall proposal for four retail spaces
   and three apartments, with a $490,539 request. The July 16 minutes record a
   unanimous board recommendation to City Council for approval and funding.
   That recommendation is not final Council action, an executed agreement,
   disbursement, or completed development.
7. [KCUR on the Eighth Street Tunnel](https://www.kcur.org/arts-life/2016-09-15/the-8th-street-tunnel-is-a-gateway-to-kansas-citys-history-but-you-probably-cant-get-in)
   reports Jamie's 2006 participatory downtown route and three-film tunnel
   screening, including his public-history purpose. It does not establish
   authorization, attendance, or measured impact.
8. [The Pitch on Great Accommodations](https://www.thepitchkc.com/former-huck-finn-artist-now-working-in-a-pink-plastic-bubble/)
   independently reports that the collective raft reached the Gulf after four
   months and connects the expedition with Jamie's continued river inquiry.
9. [WLBT on the river expedition](https://www.wlbt.com/story/7123200/rafters-beached-on-mississippi-river/)
   contemporaneously identifies Jamie as organizer, names Libby Hendon and
   Laura Mattingly as crew members, and reports more than one thousand miles
   traveled. It predates and therefore does not prove final arrival.
10. [Studio Michael Rees on Claudette's Theatre on Wheels](https://michaelrees.org/claudette)
    documents Jamie and Michael Rees's augmented-reality collaboration and
    shared video-production credit with Anne Dufy Burkart, Julia Fredenburg,
    and Claudette. It does not specify the technical implementation split.

- the Claudette augmented-reality collaboration.

The new evidence does not close the larger research questions about NYC Artist
Coalition's full founding chronology, Jamie's precise causal contribution to
creating the Office of Nightlife, the institutional outcome of Talks Not Raids,
or the complete development history of KC Town-Hall.

The typed KC Town-Hall research task now directs a future pass to recover any
City Council action, executed agreement, disbursement record, and documented
development outcome without collapsing those stages into the board vote.
