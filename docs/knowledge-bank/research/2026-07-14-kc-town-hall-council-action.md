# KC Town Hall Council Action

Date reviewed: 2026-07-14  
Research status: decomposed and selected for bounded public use

## Finding

The municipal record establishes a two-part Kansas City Council action on
September 26, 2019, following the CCED Board's July 16 recommendation for KC
Town Hall:

1. The Council adopted Resolution 190649, accepting the Board's recommendation
   of up to $490,539 and authorizing the City Manager to negotiate a funding
   agreement.
2. At the same meeting, the Council passed Ordinance 190642. The ordinance's
   Round Two project table appropriated $490,539 to KC Town Hall.

This supports the public wording that the Council accepted the recommendation
and appropriated $490,539 for the project. It is stronger and more precise than
stopping at “funding recommendation.”

## Evidence boundary

The records do not establish that:

- a funding agreement was executed;
- money was paid or disbursed;
- Jamie personally received funds;
- construction began or was completed;
- Jamie alone caused the Board recommendation or Council action.

Kansas City Ordinance 240317 remains necessary context: it records that the
project later withdrew and that the unused $490,539 was reappropriated. The
complete public sequence is proposal, recommendation, Council acceptance and
appropriation, then later withdrawal and reappropriation.
