# NTER CHNG Google Drive Working Artifacts - 2010-2011

## Purpose

On July 15, 2026, Jamie supplied two native Google Docs from the NTER CHNG
working archive. This pass close-read them as protected sources and extracted
public-safe knowledge-bank relationships without storing Drive identifiers,
sharing URLs, raw document text, phone numbers, or participant messages.

The documents deepen the production and design record. They do not replace the
public sources that establish creator credit, the original installation, or
NTER CHNG's inclusion in America: Now and Here.

## Artifacts

### America: Now and Here staging plan

Drive metadata dates the working document to April 2011. The plan covers:

- communication with exhibition organizers;
- software revision across server-side and installation-side systems;
- display computers, projection, wiring, networking, and mounting;
- reconstruction, transport, and installation of the architectural wall;
- floor markings and the participant action route;
- gallery-experience fine-tuning, a collective interview, and teardown.

This supports a bounded claim about a contemporaneous cross-disciplinary
restaging plan. It does not establish that every task was completed exactly as
written, who performed each task, a complete labor roster, or sole work by
Jamie. The existing America: Now and Here organizer source separately confirms
that NTER CHNG was installed and used by participants.

### Working prompt and exhibit-description compilation

Drive metadata dates the second document to April 2011. It combines a 2010
exhibit-description section with 2011 prompts and response material. The safe
extract preserves:

- joint credit for Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier;
- the project's description as software and architectural installation;
- its framing of private one-to-one texting becoming shared many-to-many
  dialogue;
- the phrase and concept of a social information space;
- evidence that prompts and responses were part of project development.

The artifact may combine exhibit copy, collaborator language, prompts, and
participant responses. It does not establish sole prose authorship, the public
provenance of every passage, the identity or consent of contributors, or that
every prompt or response appeared in the exhibition.

## Privacy

The working compilation contains phone numbers and message text. Those remain
protected. The public repository stores only an opaque locator and a bounded
description of what the source supports and does not establish.

Do not commit or publish:

- Google Drive identifiers or sharing URLs;
- raw document exports or screenshots;
- phone numbers or personal contact details;
- participant message text or inferred identities;
- unsupported individual authorship or division-of-labor claims.
