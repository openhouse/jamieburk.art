# Jamie personal Facebook posts: archival production

Date: July 15, 2026

Status: internal research note. The raw record-level corpus remains outside the
public repository.

## Research question

What can the complete population currently returned by Jamie Burkart's
authenticated personal Facebook **Manage Posts > Posted by You** surface add to
the knowledge bank without turning private life, mutable social counters, or
platform associations into public claims?

## Population and denominator

The cursor traversal reached a terminal `has_next_page: false` after 621 pages.
It returned 3,728 nodes that deduplicated to 1,243 owner-filtered records, with
zero missing dates and zero records lacking Jamie in the returned owner data.
The current observable chronology runs from December 19, 2006, through June 12,
2022.

The authenticated interface was independently checked at both ends of that
range on July 15. The oldest 2006 and newest 2022 records matched the crawl.

Here, **100 percent** means every record currently returned by that specific
owner-filtered control. It does not mean a native Meta export, deletion history,
or proof that no hidden, removed, migrated, or otherwise unavailable post ever
existed.

## Public-safety treatment

Audience labels were not exposed for 973 of the 1,243 crawl records. The full
corpus therefore remains private. Raw post text, addresses, phone numbers,
friend-only material, private interaction context, and responder rosters are
not committed.

Only posts individually reopened in the authenticated interface and rechecked
as **Shared with Public** were promoted as public source records. The public
repo stores aggregate controls, source relationships, claims, anti-claims, and
an opaque private-artifact ID, not the record-level census.

## Full-population findings

A deterministic second pass routed 181 unique records into overlapping
mission-related categories. The most frequent routes were WOW List (48),
Sunday Dinner (44), NYC Artist Coalition (34), Let NYC Dance (33),
creative-technical practice (19), civic-technical practice (13), and water
publics (10). Categories overlap and are research aids, not effort measures,
exclusive semantic judgments, public metrics, or impact scores.

The corpus contains 430 records with external URLs and 549 unique normalized
external destinations. The most common routes include Flickr, WOW List, Vimeo,
YouTube, the KC Masterpiece Society blog, NYC Artist Coalition campaign sites,
GLI.TC, and CreateNYC. A posted URL is a source lead, not provenance by itself.

Deterministic mention and link rules found recurring civic routes: New York City
Council appeared in 20 mission records, Rafael Espinal in 18, Market Hotel in
nine, the Office of Nightlife in six, and Antonio Reynoso in five. These are
Jamie's mentions, tags, quotations, and routes. They are not actions by those
stakeholders and do not establish engagement, attendance, endorsement,
partnership, or impact.

## Newly promoted independent source

The posted-source pass surfaced Carolyn Szczepanski's September 3, 2009, Pitch
article, *Former Huck Finn artist now working in a pink, plastic bubble*. The
article independently reports that Jamie and his fellow artists reached the
Gulf of Mexico four months after leaving Kansas City and connects the journey
to Great Accommodations. This is stronger completion evidence than Jamie's own
later recollection, and it now supports a new collective-credit claim.

## What the corpus changes

The personal account is best understood as a public routing and relationship
surface. Across projects, Jamie repeatedly connected cultural participation to
project identities, press, public meetings, campaign tools, official forms,
legislation, and in-person invitations. That is a defensible practice-level
finding when kept separate from claims about audience behavior or policy
causality.

The post record also strengthens existing claims without replacing their
primary evidence:

- NTER CHNG gains a dated public opening trace with collaborator credit.
- WOW List gains a personal-account counterpart to its nine-city Page post.
- KC Town Hall gains a contemporaneous first-person co-initiation trace.
- The raft journey gains independent retrospective reporting of collective Gulf
  completion and duration.
