# Acceptance of *Release Yourself Into the Archive*

On July 22, 2026, Jamie Burkart, acting as host of 196 Artists Residency,
accepted the working proposal for the first photo-archive pass and welcomed the
artist into residence:

> Your proposal is accepted. Welcome.

The residency begins under the proposal's stated conditions: up to two weeks
of attention at 196, a provisional private field of approximately 1,000
photographs, permission to wander and change course, and no obligation to
produce a finished or public work.

## What acceptance changes

- The proposal is no longer waiting for a residency decision.
- The first working period may begin.
- Time, attention, rest, experiment, and departure from the initial plan are
  recognized as legitimate parts of the residency.
- The notebook may record public-safe questions, discoveries, counter-readings,
  and changes of mind produced during the encounter.

## What acceptance does not change

The AI-assisted first-person proposal remains available for Jamie's line-by-line
authorship review. Acceptance does not approve any photograph, identity,
caption, claim, sequence, crop, rights determination, consent determination, or
public use. It does not turn Field Set 001 into an audited population or a
publication set.

The welcome opens the working room. It does not predetermine what the work must
become.
