# Photography working notebook

This is the Knowledge Wiki's public-safe working room for photography. It can
hold unfinished questions, recurring visual motifs, sequence experiments,
possible relationships, counter-readings, and changes of mind. It is designed
to help us play and notice before asking the material to become a portfolio.

The photographic corpus itself is private and remains outside this repository.
No image, contact sheet, private filename, source-library identifier, precise
location, or represented-person identity belongs here unless it has separately
completed the review required for that exact public use.

## Purpose

- Create enough continuity that an observation can be revisited and revised.
- Keep visual observation separate from interpretation and factual claim.
- Preserve uncertainty, contradiction, repetition, and protected absence.
- Explore how images can make invitation, care, participation, and emergence
  legible to a stranger.
- Let visual findings create research questions without automatically becoming
  evidence or public copy.

Jamie has proposed assembling a private rough-draft field of approximately
1,000 photographs. That target is a creative orientation, not an audited corpus
count, completed intake, rights-cleared set, or promise of publication.

## Working agreements

1. **Play before convergence.** Early notes may be associative, partial, or
   contradictory. They become stronger through return and comparison.
2. **Preserve sequences and ordinary moments.** Near-duplicates, preparations,
   intervals, maintenance, and aftermath may carry more meaning than a single
   representative image.
3. **Describe before interpreting.** Record what is visibly present before
   proposing who, where, why, or what it means.
4. **Keep exact identity private.** Reproducible membership belongs in the
   private review system; this notebook uses public-safe aggregate descriptions.
5. **Do not promote by enthusiasm.** Relevance, beauty, model confidence, and
   emotional force cannot clear provenance, rights, consent, factual, or
   publication holds.
6. **Compose for a stranger.** Ask whether a sequence gives a new reader a
   threshold, an intelligible situation, collective agency, and room to imagine
   participation without turning people into scenery.
7. **Allow an empty outcome.** A close-reading pass may produce learning and no
   publication candidates.

## Public and private boundary

| This public-safe notebook may contain | This repository must not contain |
| --- | --- |
| Research questions and editorial hypotheses | Private originals, previews, or contact sheets |
| Aggregate, non-identifying observations | Private filenames, source paths, or precise locations |
| Public-safe project and method relationships | Unreviewed names, faces-as-identifiers, or relationship maps |
| Anti-claims and unresolved questions | Private EXIF, device identifiers, or archive credentials |
| Review-state definitions and next actions | Rights, consent, or publication decisions invented by an agent |
| Approved public asset links | Held or rejected images copied for convenience |

The private system may retain exact source identity, candidate membership,
technical metadata, review receipts, and authorized decisions. Only a bounded,
separately reviewed public-safe projection may cross into this repository.

## Current questions

- Which scenes let a stranger recognize the moment of invitation or choice?
- What forms of preparation, maintenance, and coordination make participation
  possible but usually remain outside the frame?
- How can Jamie be visible as an active steward without displacing collective
  authorship?
- Where do sequences reveal reciprocity, emergence, change, or afterlife that a
  single image cannot establish?
- Which absences are research needs, and which should remain protected?
- What visual forms carry warmth and inclusion without sentimentalizing or
  making unsupported claims about the people shown?

## How to add an entry

Start from the [notebook entry template](notebook-entry-template.md). Give the
entry a public-safe question, relate it to governed Wiki records, and write
separate sections for visible observations, interpretations, anti-claims,
protected boundaries, and next actions. Return to the entry as the field changes.

No notebook entry may activate a claim, asset, or public projection by itself.
Agents may detect missing information and enforce gates; they may not clear
safety, provenance, rights, consent, attribution, or publication holds.
