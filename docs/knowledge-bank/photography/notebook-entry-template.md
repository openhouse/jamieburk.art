# Photography notebook entry template

Use this form for public-safe notes that benefit from durable Wiki relationships.
Do not use it to store photographs, contact sheets, private filenames, source
locators, precise locations, unreviewed identities, or sensitive EXIF. Those
belong in the separately governed private review system.

An entry may remain fragmentary. Write `unknown`, `open`, or `protected` rather
than filling a gap with an inference.

## Entry status

- **Working title:**
- **Date of encounter:**
- **Reader or readers:**
- **Related public-safe Wiki records:**
- **Private field-set version:** recorded outside public Git
- **Notebook state:** observation, hypothesis, counter-reading, research lead,
  sequence experiment, or deferred

## Prompt or question

What were we looking for, and why might the question matter to a stranger?

## Material reviewed

Describe only the public-safe aggregate scope. Keep exact membership, filenames,
paths, previews, and technical receipts in the private system.

## What is visible

Describe people without identifying them, objects, actions, spatial relationships,
legible text, light, weather, framing, repetition, and sequence. Separate direct
observation from remembered or externally sourced context.

## Interpretations and hypotheses

What might the material suggest? Name alternative readings and the evidence that
would be needed to distinguish them. Hypotheses remain revisable.

## What this does not establish

List prohibited inferences, unsupported identities, uncertain chronology,
authorship boundaries, and outcomes the material cannot prove.

## Relationships and collective credit

Whose agency is visible? Whose contribution or presence may be outside the frame?
What credit requires corroboration or collaborator review?

## Safety, rights, and consent

Record only public-safe aggregate states. Any uncertainty remains a hold. Agents
may identify missing review but cannot grant rights, consent, attribution,
factual clearance, or publication permission.

## Editorial function

Could the material help establish a threshold, apparatus, reciprocity, emergence,
afterlife, counterexample, transition, or protected absence? It may serve more
than one function or none.

## Open questions

What should be checked against project records, chronology, collaborator memory,
public sources, or other photographs?

## Decision and next action

Choose one provisional action: revisit, compare, research, request human review,
hold, defer, or close. Do not promote the entry into a claim, asset, caption, or
public projection without the separate review required by that record type.
