# Field Set 001 completion

On July 22, 2026, the first residency pass produced an exact private master of
1,000 unique still photographs. This is the broad field from which Jamie and
collaborators can begin considering photographs for the portfolio. It is an
editor-ready rough select, not a final edit and not a public collection.

## What was completed

- 1,800 candidate images were locally inspected without external upload.
- Sensitive-material checks placed 140 images in a separate private HOLD set;
  none entered the master.
- The exact 1,000-image master was organized into eight provisional editorial
  views while preserving an unclassified editor field.
- Four recursive visual-review rounds hill climbed the deterministic sample.
- The final 64-image review recorded 61 fits, one rejection, and two
  uncertainties: 98.39% precision and 100% coverage under the declared policy.
- A ten-image canary passed before the production write.
- The unchanged production plan ran twice with identical folder, album, and
  membership results.
- A separate read-only verifier confirmed 13 governed albums, exact membership,
  no unsafe overlap, source continuity, and no unexpected additions.

Two private overlays remain useful for later research: 601 master images carry
an existing People association, and 56 remain context-uncertain. These counts
describe the working field only. They do not identify anyone publicly or imply
permission, attribution, relationship, project participation, or narrative
meaning.

## What stayed unchanged

Original photographs, metadata, People associations, dates, places, favorites,
source membership, and prior working versions were not edited. The operation
created only new folders and albums inside the authorized residency workspace
and added existing photographs as album memberships. No pixels, previews,
metadata, or identifiers were uploaded to an external service.

## Editorial meaning

The achievement is not that a machine found the best thousand photographs. It
is that a broad, revisable field now exists where looking can become
conversation. Its views include people and friendship, work and apparatus,
thresholds and invitation, water and public geography, civic interfaces, art
and experiment, maintenance and traces, and material that remains deliberately
unclassified.

The field is large enough to resist a single story. It can disclose scenes,
sequences, relationships, absences, counterexamples, and visual questions that
were not available when the proposal was written.

## Publication boundary

Selection does not confer publication permission. No photograph leaves the
private field or enters the portfolio without separate provenance, factual,
photographer, rights, consent, represented-person, caption, crop, context,
audience, design, and Jamie-approval decisions for that exact use.

The next step is slow looking: converse over the field, make sequences, return
to original sources, invite correction, and allow the photographs to change
the structure of the project.
