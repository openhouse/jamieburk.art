## Stakeholder And Traction Boundaries

The personal association surface displays recurring host clusters connected to
cultural spaces, arts organizations, advocacy groups, and public institutions.
These clusters are useful source-discovery routes. They do not establish that
Jamie attended, endorsed, produced, or had a professional relationship to every
associated event, and they are not counts of stakeholder engagement with Jamie.

Facebook event-card numeric displays were unstable across authenticated
renders, and the accessible text did not consistently expose a platform label.

observed values ranging from 5 to 128. They are retained as a data-quality finding only:
do not sum them or describe them as unique people, attendance, reach,
endorsement, causality, or impact.

## Public Safety

The repository retains aggregate accounting and selected professionally
relevant public event sources. It does not reproduce the 502 association rows,
the three hosted-tab-only records as a relational ledger,
other host names as a personal graph, guest identities, friend or invite
context, exact private locations, comments, raw descriptions, account

guests, and mutable numeric-display data. Selected public event pages are separately modeled
as sources only where their professional relevance is clear.
