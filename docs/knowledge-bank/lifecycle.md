# Knowledge Lifecycle

The knowledge bank preserves depth; the public site composes from that depth.
Nothing is discarded silently, and nothing is published automatically.

## Flow

1. **Capture:** Give every submitted URL, memory, metric, artifact, photograph,
   lead, or correction a stable intake ID.
2. **Triage:** Record project and entity hints, availability, sensitivity, and
   the next research action.
3. **Review:** Closely read promoted sources. Record what each supports and what
   it does not establish.
4. **Decompose:** Create atomic claims with explicit evidence relationships,
   qualifications, anti-claims, and open inquiries.
5. **Situate:** Connect records to projects, entities, timelines, related work,
   and photo-research prompts.
6. **Promote:** Set knowledge maturity, publication safety, and editorial
   selection independently.
7. **Project:** Compose only the subset needed by a particular public surface.
8. **Maintain:** Preserve corrections, dead links, negative findings, and
   superseded wording so later agents do not repeat or weaken the work.

## Status Dimensions

- **Knowledge maturity** asks how strongly the claim is known.
- **Publication safety** asks whether and how the claim may leave the bank.
- **Editorial selection** asks whether the claim serves a current composition.

A confirmed claim may remain unused. A qualified claim may be selected with its
boundary. An unresolved memory may remain valuable as a research inquiry.

## Public Repository Boundary

This repository is public. Intake descriptions must be safe to quote in a
newspaper. Private originals, transcripts, correspondence, contact information,
and raw archives remain outside the repository. A public-safe record may point
to them only through an opaque `protectedLocatorId`.

## Agent Protocol

- Capture before interpreting.
- Never convert a memory directly into a confirmed claim.
- Preserve exact attribution and collective credit.
- Record source non-support as carefully as source support.
- Turn dead links and stronger unresolved memories into bounded inquiries.
- Update the bank before strengthening the public site.
- Do not project every mature claim.
- Run `npm run evals:knowledge` before requesting review.

## Photo Feedback

Project records include photo-research prompts. A photo editor may return a
candidate image, visible phrase, date, person, place, or event as a new intake
item. Visual evidence can begin research, but it does not establish an
institutional or causal claim by itself.
