# Knowledge lifecycle

The knowledge bank is a public-safe research and editorial system. It preserves
potentially valuable fragments, develops them into defensible claims, and gives
future writers a deep palette from which to compose the public site. It is not
the private archive, and it is not a queue that automatically publishes what it
receives.

## Three zones

1. **Private archive and intake environment.** Raw photographs, messages,
   transcripts, collaborator notes, private filenames, and local paths remain
   outside this public repository.
2. **Public-safe knowledge bank.** The repo stores stable IDs, safe summaries,
   public URLs, proposition-level source readings, evidence relationships,
   limits, anti-claims, research tasks, and editorial decisions.
3. **Purposeful public projections.** The website selects and composes only the
   claims needed for a particular audience and purpose. Public readiness does
   not require selection.

## Lifecycle

```text
fragment or artifact
  -> intake record
  -> source record
  -> close reading and atomic propositions
  -> claim candidate
  -> corroboration, boundaries, and anti-claims
  -> public-ready claim
  -> publish, defer, retire, or disallow decision
  -> optional website projection and citation
```

Corrections, contradictions, rejected claims, and superseded wording remain in
the system. “Nothing lost” means preserving the research trail and editorial
history, not committing raw private material.

## Intake

Create an intake record as soon as Jamie or a collaborator supplies a URL,
memory, artifact lead, correction, or photo observation. The intake record must
contain only public-safe metadata. It links to at least one source, claim seed,
or research task, unless it is explicitly held.

Use `public-source-only` when the underlying object is already public. Use
`protected-outside-repo` when the underlying evidence is private. Never put the
private path or raw content in the public-safe summary.

A photo editor may submit an observation such as “a sequence appears to show a
public workshop beside a river.” Record that as a `photo-observation`, link it
to the relevant entity, and open a research task. The observation is a lead,
not proof of the event, role, date, consent, or publication rights.

## Close reading

A source reading decomposes a source into atomic propositions. Each proposition
states whether it supports:

- Jamie's direct role;
- a collective role;
- project context;
- institutional or public outcome context; or
- a limitation.

Every close reading records limits. A source may establish an outcome without
establishing Jamie's contribution to it. An inaccessible source remains
`needs-access` and must have a research task; its title alone supports no claim.

## Claim maturity

- `captured`: retained as a lead.
- `researching`: important but not adequately evidenced.
- `corroborated`: evidence exists, but another source, permission, or boundary
  remains material.
- `public-ready`: reviewed, source-backed, bounded, and safe to use.
- `projected`: selected and authorized for at least one public surface.
- `rejected`: examined and found unsupported or misleading.
- `superseded`: replaced by a more accurate claim or wording.

Maturity is not importance. A consequential memory can remain `researching`
without being minimized. A modest fact can become `public-ready` quickly.

Claims at `corroborated` or above require evidence, boundaries, anti-claims,
and review history. Captured and researching claims require an executable
research route and cannot have an active public projection.

Qualifying evidence is linked to the exact atomic proposition IDs extracted
from its source reading. Required semantic support tags prevent a valid but
irrelevant proposition from satisfying a different claim. Context-only,
contradicting, or limited-confidence evidence cannot promote a claim by itself.
Public-ready claims also store the
action, intended end, usable result, audience, collective credit, and causal
boundary needed for later composition with Chad's lens.

That action must be supported by at least one linked atomic proposition marked
as Jamie's `direct-role` or a `collective-role`. Project context and outcome
evidence can establish why the work mattered, but they cannot by themselves be
rewritten as Jamie's agency. This contract is enforced by the lifecycle gate.

Rejected and superseded claims retain a dated reason and predecessor/successor
links. They cannot have an active projection. This keeps discarded
interpretations inspectable without allowing them to drift back into public
copy.

## Publication decisions

Publication is compositional. A `public-ready` claim may be deferred because it
does not serve the current audience, because another example is clearer, or
because a future case study should hold the fuller story. Record that choice as
a projection decision so absence from the site is not mistaken for evidentiary
weakness.

Every active claim/surface pair requires an explicit `publish` decision. A
`defer`, `retire`, or `disallow` decision blocks an active projection on the
same surface. The generated public citation registry refuses claims without
projected maturity and a matching publish decision.

Use Chad's lens when composing a projection:

1. Put Jamie's agency in the sentence.
2. Name the end the work served.
3. State what became usable.
4. Translate specialized language.
5. Preserve collective credit and causal limits.
6. Reduce the hiring reader's interpretive burden.

## Research tasks

A research task should let another person or agent begin cold. Include the
question, priority, linked intake/source/claim IDs, and concrete next actions.
For causal contribution claims, recover the chain in separate steps:

```text
Jamie's documented action
  -> collective program or advocacy
  -> institutional response
  -> enacted or operational outcome
  -> defensible statement of contribution
```

Do not bridge those steps with confidence or rhetoric alone.

- contemporaneous reporting on Open House;

The pass promotes only what the current sources establish. It retains the
larger assertions as high-priority research without projecting them to the

The
[NYC Artist Coalition campaign press corpus](research/nycartc-campaign-press-2026-07-13.md)
preserves 45 placements across four campaign press sections as 44 distinct
article identities. Campaign-index pages are closely read; article-level
sources remain queued where propositions have not yet been extracted. Repeated
placement stays visible without duplicating the source, and dead links retain
their recovery status rather than disappearing from the record.

The
[July 14 social-account archive production](research/social-account-archive-production-2026-07-14.md)
maps five project accounts, recovers bounded engagement and campaign patterns,
and routes authorship and account-establishment memories into further research.
It preserves recovered-minimum counts rather than treating incomplete timelines
as exhaustive, and it keeps every newly mature social claim deferred from the
website pending a later compositional decision.

The
[CallNYC full-population social census](research/callnyc-full-population-social-census-2026-07-14.md)
then applies the lifecycle at item level. It gives all 110 observed profile-count
slots a disposition, retains 107 public status records in a redacted ledger,
routes three unknown slots to further research, extracts source and stakeholder
patterns, rejects an unsupported service-outcome conversion, and defers two
new public-ready claims from the website.

The
[WOW List full-population social census](research/wowlist-full-population-social-census-2026-07-14.md)
closes all 38 slots in its observed profile control through the Posts and
Replies route union. It promotes the earlier sample-based support finding into
a complete six-reply product-support pattern, preserves mission-relevant source
and stakeholder-network findings in reserve, and keeps account authorship,
historical metrics, adoption, and amplified-work credit explicit.

Migration accessions connect every source and claim that predated the lifecycle
framework to the same entity and publication graph. They preserve existing
review status without pretending that a new proposition-level close reading
was performed during migration. Future close readings can replace that bounded
exception source by source.

## Commands

```bash
npm run check:knowledge-lifecycle
npm run test:knowledge-lifecycle
npm run eval:knowledge-lifecycle
npm run eval:knowledge-lifecycle:gate
```

The recursive protocol and independent judging rubric live in
`evals/knowledge-lifecycle/`. Deterministic success means the graph is coherent
and safely staged; it does not substitute for Jamie's approval or collaborator
permission.

The gate command requires the checked-in independent assessment, verifies its
file-and-record evidence against the repository, and enforces the score floors.
It also compares the assessment's `repositorySnapshot` with a deterministic
fingerprint of the canonical graph. Any graph change makes the prior assessment
stale and requires a fresh deterministic run and independent review.
Human claim approval, collaborator permission, and media rights cannot be
authenticated by an agent-authored file; production release remains an
external human process.
