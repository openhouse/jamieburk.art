# Knowledge Bank

This directory is the public-safe knowledge bank for jamieburk.art.

The public site may use only approved, public-safe claims from this bank. It is
not a source dump, archive browser, litigation file, private notebook, or place
to store protected evidence.

## What This Bank Does

- Records what the site can safely claim.
- Separates approved wording from fallback wording.
- Names source classes without committing protected source contents.
- Tracks approval owners and publication boundaries.
- Gives future edits a review path before page copy is strengthened.

## Operating Rule

Protected evidence stays outside the repo. The site projects selected,
defensible claims for clarity, audience, and purpose.

## Public-Safe Source Flow

1. Private or mixed source material is reviewed outside the repo.
2. A public-safe claim is written in `claims.md` and `claims.json`.
3. The claim receives a status and source class.
4. Public pages use only `approved` or `softened-for-v1` wording.
5. Exact metrics, screenshots, quotes, photos, names, and private records remain
   approval-gated until Jamie explicitly approves them for public use.

## Claim Statuses

- `approved`: may appear in public app content.
- `softened-for-v1`: may appear in public app content in conservative form.
- `needs-jamie-approval`: tracked for review; do not publish in app content.
- `do-not-publish`: protected or rejected; do not publish.
- `future-v1-1`: possible later improvement; do not rely on for V1 launch.

## Core Principle

Structure grows out of the material. This portfolio should show how emerging,
stakeholder-heavy work becomes usable systems: requirements, workflows,
documentation, decision trails, launch support, onboarding, and durable
handoffs.
