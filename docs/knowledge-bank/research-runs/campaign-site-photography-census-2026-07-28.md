# Campaign-Site Photography Census, July 28, 2026

This pass enumerated the complete available source population for five campaign
sites and visually reviewed every distinct work. The resulting 225
photographic entries are a durable research field from which later oral
history, rights work, private family matching, and portfolio selection can
proceed.

The machine check verifies population arithmetic, stable IDs, duplicate
checksums, public-safe locators, explicit rights boundaries, and opaque private
family references. It cannot grant rights, identify people, or choose a public
crop.
