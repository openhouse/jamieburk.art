# Knowledge Wiki branch-family closure review

## Scope

The review examined the exact public Git heads of A through E, their defining
authored records, commit histories, file inventories, and develop-relative
deltas. It treated the branches as editorial and technical donors, not as
independent evidence for Jamie's accomplishments.

Exact donor records:

- [A](../sources/knowledge-wiki-branch-a-2026-07-19.md)
- [B](../sources/knowledge-wiki-branch-b-2026-07-19.md)
- [C](../sources/knowledge-wiki-branch-c-2026-07-19.md)
- [D](../sources/knowledge-wiki-branch-d-2026-07-19.md)
- [E](../sources/knowledge-wiki-branch-e-2026-07-19.md)

## Integration method

E supplies the canonical schema and governance baseline. Distinctive ideas from
A through D are adapted into a smaller set of composite records. Every adapted
record points back to its donor source. Similar pages are consolidated when
separate copies would divide authority or make ordinary navigation harder.

Generated graphs, reports, screenshots, scorecards, and holdout judgments are
not merged from sibling branches. They must describe the final unchanged
candidate or remain historical evidence about an earlier candidate.

## What this review establishes

It establishes a transparent selection record for closing the branch family.
It does not strengthen any underlying career claim, reconcile every archive
census, grant publication rights, or complete a human authority gate.
