# Knowledge Wiki A-E family closure review

All five frozen heads passed their complete repository checks when evaluated in
clean, branch-appropriate environments. Their architectures nevertheless differ
substantially. Closure therefore required editorial selection, not a mechanical
union.

## Selected foundation

Branch E remains the sole compiler, graph, schema, generated-report, employment,
and selective-projection foundation. The integration adapts distinctive donor
ideas into that architecture.

## Donor findings

- **A:** strongest source-to-story and visual-rights editorial sequence.
- **B:** strongest account of shared identity, embodied learning, and human-only
  authorship authority.
- **C:** strongest hiring-readable separation of delivery, use, adoption, and
  later change.
- **D:** strongest human-readable explanation of relation semantics and
  operational evidence.
- **E:** strongest complete architecture, interpretive synthesis, source return,
  decision records, and opportunity context.

## Census finding

The [reconciliation control](../data/knowledge-wiki-family-census-reconciliation.json)
preserves every reported denominator, its exact branch, and its run language.
No universal metric is selected. E's 2,405-item figure remains attached only to
the selected E research run until a new common protocol is executed.

## Boundary

The integration makes no new claim about the underlying Drive population,
authorship, rights, consent, project impact, or publication approval. Human
review remains open.
