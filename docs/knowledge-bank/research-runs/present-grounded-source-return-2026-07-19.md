# Present-Grounded Source Return, July 19, 2026

## Question

What important Wiki pages are missing, and can they be grounded by returning to
source material rather than composing only from prior summaries?

## Encounter scope

The pass revisited representative original or near-original source families:

- the KC Town Hall CCED proposal and its construction, neighborhood-process,
  and budget sections;
- current and archived public evidence in the Harry J. Epstein source package;
- the WOWList database audit and Sunday Dinner workbook audit tied to preserved
  production sources;
- selected original NYC Artist Coalition shared-folder records, campaign
  surfaces, and official Council testimony through the authenticated July 2026
  census;
- the live Let NYC Dance, Talks Not Raids, FairRentNYC, Save NYC Spaces, and
  Office of Nightlife town-hall surfaces, with official creation, hearing,
  legislative, and MARCH-replacement records where relevant;
- NTER CHNG project-authored exhibit and installer records;
- the approved resume family and writer-voice source package.

Exact protected paths, participant records, credentials, private correspondence,
and raw source contents remain outside this public repository.

## Findings

1. The Wiki needed a person/practice spine so projects could be read as one
   evolving method rather than isolated achievements.
2. NYC Artist Coalition needed an organization page and separate campaign pages
   so Jamie's infrastructure role could be specific without absorbing collective
   outcomes.
3. HJE, WOWList, Sunday Dinner, KC Town Hall, NTER CHNG, and KC Spaces Fund had
   mature claims but lacked governed project orientation.
4. Source return needed a typed contract with dates, access state, present
   finding, unresolved questions, and a future return date.
5. A source encounter still cannot decide rights, consent, credit, publication,
   or Jamie's final approval.
6. The coalition campaign family needed separate pages because each made a
   different civic mechanism usable; one general coalition page obscured that
   implementation range.

## Librarian handoff

When an expected source is unavailable, cloud-only, ambiguous, or represented by
multiple versions, the agent should ask Jamie to locate or authorize the source.
The record should remain `partial` or `blocked` until the source is actually
encountered.
