# Portfolio project-cover private editor field

*Delegated editorial review, not a human rights or publication decision.*

The private workflow froze the visible-still source at 605,444 items, built a
fresh read-only inventory, verified the stable local Photos helper, and exported
two bounded 100-image candidate sets as metadata-free local previews. No image
or metadata left the machine, and no source, original, or prior curation version
was changed.

The first round used broad semantic and project-name retrieval. Its 38-image
fixed sample achieved full review coverage but only 44.44% precision. Generic
food, flowers, domestic objects, duplicate frames, and later civic-technology
scenes were incorrectly standing in for project evidence.

The second round changed only candidate provenance: exact visually confirmed or
strongly project-bounded private album sources replaced generic search terms.
Its 38-image sample reached 82.86% precision, with 29 fits, six rejections, and
three explicit uncertainties. Date review correctly rejected later images that
resembled CallNYC work but fell outside the project's documented period.

The second round remains held because delegated editorial inference cannot clear
represented-person dignity, photographer rights, attribution, or exact public
placement. Three promising new cover derivatives were moved into the private
run's HOLD area and are not present in public Git or staging.

The public candidate therefore uses five previously governed photographs: the
unchanged East River hero, the Save NYC Spaces and Shoestring Press sequence,
the Sunday Dinner shared-map cover, and the KC Town Hall roof-work cover. Harry
J. Epstein, CallNYC, and WOW List retain truthful project-specific public-surface
captures until Jamie clears exact photographic replacements.
