## Why these examples belong

Wonderville shows a plan made useful to venue staff and fellow organizers.
The animation shows field documentation reaching a published article with
separate credit for writer and animator. Red Hook shows versioned audio delivery
and checks that connect printed labels to their intended destinations.

These facts support operating practice. They do not establish an attendance
total, policy change, client acceptance, payment, installation or employment
outcome. The gathering's occurrence does not prove every planned cue happened.
Visible track titles do not prove which audio edition was published.

## Evaluation scope

Regression checks distinguish scheduled, occurred, delivered, accepted,
proof-checked, installed and published states. They enforce source bindings,
date order, text-only scope and designated surfaces. Synthetic failures test
unjustified state promotion and protected locator leakage. They cannot verify
external events or grant consent. An editorial review and source comparison
remain necessary for the meaning of the prose.
