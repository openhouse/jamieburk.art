# Professional record public-safe promotion review, August 3, 2026

## Decision

metadata-only educational context, and a held photo-set record. Withhold all raw
communication, private transcript text, recommendation strategy, contact data,
source-system locators, message counts, and image payloads.

The public website receives no new claim or photograph. Promotion into the
public-safe Wiki is not promotion onto the portfolio.
