# Foundational Wiki page source return, July 19, 2026

## Purpose

This encounter returns to original and near-original material before filling
the Wiki's highest-priority orientation gaps. It records the present questions,
what changed in the reading, what remains unresolved, and where librarian help
would materially improve the record.

## Coverage statement

Eight source records were materially reached for this encounter. The cohort is
not the complete archive and does not claim full-population coverage. Exact
private locations, raw protected text, educational identifiers, contact data,
and authentication details remain outside the repository.

## Present reading

The rereading supports a more integrated account of Jamie's practice. Early
records do not merely foreshadow a project manager; they show an artist and
systems thinker testing how social structures, images, spaces, technologies,
and attention interact. Later civic and operational work develops those
questions under different stakes: implementation, public information,
collective agency, institutional action, maintenance, and handoff.

That throughline is useful only if discontinuity and credit remain visible.
The raft report is an in-progress snapshot. HJE component claims require more
corroboration. Coalition work remains collective. The 2006 installation title
remains disputed. These boundaries belong inside the synthesis.

## Publication boundary

Jamie authorized research access. Rights, consent, quotation, collaborator
review, and publication remain separate decisions. This encounter authorizes
only the public-safe synthesis committed in the linked Wiki pages.
