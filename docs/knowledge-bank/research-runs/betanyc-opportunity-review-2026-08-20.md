# BetaNYC opportunity review, August 20, 2026

## Decision

The issue surfaced nineteen leads across City government, state government,
civic technology, policy advocacy, and fellowship programs. Two roles cleared
the maintained thresholds and entered provisional intake only:

1. **Associate Director of Policy Implementation at the New York City Campaign
   Finance Board** (Job ID 792925), with a $125,000–$135,000 salary range and an
   October 13, 2026 deadline.
2. **Product Manager (First Product Hire) at Polimorphic** (Job ID
   `polimorphic-123173`), with a $165,000–$195,000 salary range and no deadline
   stated on the reviewed official page.

The Campaign Finance Board role's agency-wide implementation process,
cross-divisional coordination,
project management, operating guidance, documentation, and public-service
mission align strongly with Jamie's maintained evidence. Its five years of
related experience and three years of staff-supervision requirements still need
human qualification review before canonical promotion or application.

Polimorphic's first-product-hire role combines roadmap ownership, customer and
go-to-market work, delivery operations, quality assurance, and resident-facing
government software. Its requested product-management tenure and technical
shipping background likewise remain human qualification questions. Neither
provisional result changes the public resume, portfolio, or application state.

## Error analysis and hill climb

The baseline scorer underrated the role because `Associate Director` received
a seniority penalty while `Policy Implementation` carried no positive title
signal. The revised evaluator recognizes policy implementation, calibrates the
associate-director penalty separately from executive-director roles, and parses
experience years written as words. The reviewed example now clears provisional
intake without reducing the human qualification gate.

The HRA Project Manager lead exposed the opposite error: its title and delivery
language looked relevant, but the official minimum qualifications require all
candidates to hold at least a master's degree. A new hard-screen regression
test stops that mismatch before scoring or model review.

Other reviewed leads were not promoted because of salary, specialist subject
matter, required credentials, location, or sub-threshold fit and likely screen
survival. Dated external-employer reviews expire back into verification so an
old result cannot remain active by inertia.
