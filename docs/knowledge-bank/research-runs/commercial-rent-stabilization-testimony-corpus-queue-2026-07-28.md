## Inclusion rule

Include full attributed speaking turns when the words:

- introduce or explain a verified City or State bill as prime sponsor;
- expressly support Commercial Rent Stabilization or a verified City or State
  bill; or
- support the bill with stated amendments or conditions while preserving those
  conditions.

Apply the same rule to every speaker. Do not omit a supportive contribution
because its speaker is a City Council member, State legislator, committee chair,
agency representative, comptroller, or other public official. Preserve the
speaker's name, institutional role at the time, meeting and agenda context, full
speaking turn, and page or timestamp locator. A title, sponsor listing, vote,
press-event appearance, or public office does not by itself establish that a
statement was supportive.

Keep opposition, neutral analysis, procedural speech, uncertain position, and
fragments in an exclusion ledger. Do not infer support from organizational
affiliation, attendance, applause, sponsor listing, or proximity to advocates.
