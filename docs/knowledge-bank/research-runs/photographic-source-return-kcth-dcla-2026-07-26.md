# KC Town Hall and DCLA photographic source-return pass

## DCLA result

The pass recovered a contemporaneous sequence: Jamie's January 26 WOW List
invitation; a January 27 two-person naming sheet with Jamie and Julia; public
coalition contact and document surfaces created that day; a February 3
interactive visualization; a February 4 follow-up inviting continued
participation; and February press reuse of a visualization detail.

The records support Jamie's role in connective, identity, web, and
close-listening infrastructure. They do not support sole founding, sole
authorship, or hosting the official DCLA event. The remembered meeting audio
was not recovered and remains an open librarian request.

## KC Town Hall result

Two oral histories locate Jamie within daily field coordination, craft
relationships, site research, and neighborhood listening. The protected
proposal independently supports co-founder/project-manager attribution, a
multi-trade Phase One scope, and a neighborhood survey process. It does not
independently establish every remembered detail.

Two proposal crops are safe enough for branch review: the blank neighborhood
survey and the Engine of Opportunity rendering. Construction portraits remain
held for source, rights, and represented-person review.

## Council chamber result

Jamie returned an exact-photo oral history describing the coalition
participation form and commercial-rent data proposal on his clipboard.
Contemporary records establish the event as a March 26, 2026 NYC Open Data
Week session organized by the New York City Council Data Team and locate a
closely timed proposal trail. The branch-review caption can now name that
bounded context.

Registration alone does not prove attendance, and neither registration nor the
photograph establishes proposal delivery, a particular recipient,
institutional response, policy effect, creator identity, or publication
permission.

## Public/private boundary

No private path, account identifier, raw correspondence, recipient list,
contact detail, signature, banking field, participant speech, source-system
identifier, original photo filename, or person tag enters this record.
