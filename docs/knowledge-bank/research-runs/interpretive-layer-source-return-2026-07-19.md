# Interpretive layer source return, July 19, 2026

## Purpose

This encounter returns to original and near-original material before composing
the Wiki's comparative layer. It asks not only what happened, but what pressure
made action necessary, what moved between projects, which choices can be
reconstructed, and what another person could maintain or inherit.

## Present reading

The strongest throughline is not a single medium or job title. It is a repeated
practice of entering an inhabited system, listening for knowledge already held
by people and place, translating that knowledge into a usable form, and
preparing the form for participation, maintenance, or handoff.

That interpretation remains bounded. Earlier art was not secretly project
management. Repeated patterns are not proof of formal decisions. Shared work
does not become individual authorship. A later vocabulary can help readers
compare records without claiming that it determined them.
