# Restored project sites close reading

## Method and limit

On August 14, 2026, the three restored domains were inspected through direct
HTTP responses, parsed public HTML, same-origin navigation, headings, metadata,
links, forms, and bounded first-party asset checks. The available rendered
browser could not attach after the prescribed troubleshooting retry, so this
run does **not** claim a rendered visual, responsive, keyboard, screen-reader,
console, or end-to-end form audit. No form was submitted.

## Cross-site finding

The three sites show a consistent practice: digital systems become public
participation infrastructure when they make a social action easier to enter,
understand, and continue.

- [KC Town Hall](../sources/kc-town-hall-live-site-2026-08-14.md) joins
  listening, field operations, practical public guidance, and resident intake.
- [WOW List](../sources/wowlist-live-site-2026-08-14.md) makes a simple
  natural-language object carry community discovery and shared maintenance.
- [CallNYC](../sources/callnyc-live-site-2026-08-14.md) turns public data into
  repeatable resident-facing issue pathways.

A site can be live as a public artifact while the service, campaign, or data it
describes is historical. “It responds” and “people should act on it now” are
different claims. The portfolio should link to these surfaces because they let
a hiring reader inspect real systems, while placing the archive or relaunch
status before any ambiguity.
