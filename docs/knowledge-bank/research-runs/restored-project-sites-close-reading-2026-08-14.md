# Restored project sites close reading, August 14, 2026

The three restored domains expose three different kinds of continuity:

- [KC Town Hall](https://kctownhall.com/) is a neighborhood project record whose
  public-service and field-learning pages remain useful evidence even though
  their dates, forms, and invitations are historical.
- [WOW List](https://wowlist.org/) is a deliberately small, noindex public
  return: a landing page, a provenance-rich About page, and an invitation to
  help test what comes next.
- [CallNYC](https://callnyc.org/) is an explicitly archived and unofficial
  static prototype that still demonstrates issue-first resident guidance at
  substantial route scale.

Their shared lesson is not simply longevity. Jamie's web work repeatedly makes
a public problem actionable: learn about a neighborhood project, request or
help with a resident service, decide what should exist within walking distance,
join a test cohort, or move from an issue toward a relevant civic office. The
live-project index preserves that throughline without describing historical
forms, contact data, or services as current.
