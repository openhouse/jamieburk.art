## Report close reading

The final report's most important relationship to Jamie's review is its
methodological boundary. It now states that owner-reported data quality,
missing and implausible values, district aggregation, nominal rent, and
registered-versus-market rent distinctions constrain the results. It also says
the analysis cannot establish individual closure cause, landlord motive,
financing behavior, or causation.

Those qualifications closely track Jamie's review questions. They make his
contribution legible as evidence and claim-quality work. They do not authorize
a claim that he wrote those sentences or validated the report as a whole. The
main text still contains rhetoric that can outrun the methods section; the Wiki
should preserve that tension rather than smoothing it away.

## Speech close reading

The delivered remarks were more important than the prepared email draft. Jamie
began with neighborhood institutions and the rented rooms where culture is
made, situated current advocacy in a long cultural-space safety and
affordability lineage, credited the report team and open-data work, explained
Commercial Rent Stabilization in plain language, and closed with distinct
state and city asks. The speech joined lived experience, evidence, policy
translation, institutional targets, and mobilization.

The distinctive strength is not maximal rhetoric. It is movement between
registers without abandoning precision: culture, neighborhood value, data,
legislative mechanism, and a direct call to act. The exact transcript still
needs an audio pass because the machine version misrecognizes names and words.

## Official-relationship close reading

The evidence supports direct work with legislative staff in both city and
state lanes. It does not yet support the broader phrase “working directly with
city and state elected officials” for this 30-day window. The public program
listed elected officials, but a run of show is not attendance evidence. The
strong, accurate statement is that Jamie coordinated directly with staff in a
City Council office and an Assembly office, shared an evidence artifact, and
helped establish a recurring work channel.

That bounded wording is not a downgrade. It names the real operating work and
keeps the next evidentiary question visible: which elected-official
