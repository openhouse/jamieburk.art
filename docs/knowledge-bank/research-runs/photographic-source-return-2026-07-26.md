# Photographic source return for DCLA, KC Town Hall, and Council fieldwork

## Result

Four photographs entered the Knowledge Wiki as governed research objects. None
was copied into the public site. Their visible observations, first-person
recollections, corroboration states, rights questions, and non-claims remain
separate.

One proposal graphic became a bounded public derivative after obsolete contact
details were cropped and embedded metadata was stripped. Its clearance does not
transfer to the full proposal bundle or the held photographs.

## Apple Photos capability boundary

The static private photo inventory and previously verified field remain useful
context. During this pass, however, a fresh live authorization receipt did not
materialize. No full-library scan, new album, catalog write, or source mutation
is claimed.

## Composition

The Knowledge Wiki grows additively; the portfolio remains subtractive. The
current site receives one map that reduces a hiring reader's burden. The richer
photographic and oral-history field remains available for future research,
credit, consent, and editorial decisions.
