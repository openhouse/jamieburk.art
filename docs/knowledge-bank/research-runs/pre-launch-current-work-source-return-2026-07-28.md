# Pre-launch current-work source return, July 28, 2026

## What entered the Wiki

Three source states were preserved:

1. **Completed bounded work:** an invited prepublication review that
   distinguished findings from hypotheses, clarified source and method limits,
   routed open questions, and corrected public-event details.
2. **Preparation in progress:** a long-form rehearsal for invited two-minute
   remarks, held until delivery and verification.
3. **Private reflective context:** a pre-launch peer conversation represented
   only by metadata and its already-governed editorial method.

## What this adds

The current work demonstrates the same practice visible in the historical
testimony: Jamie turns fragmented evidence, requirements, institutional roles,
and public language into a clearer path for action. The strongest immediate
professional evidence is the review process itself, not ownership of the
external report or prediction of a policy outcome.

## What did not enter

Raw transcripts, personal health and financial detail, telephone numbers,
private third-party assessments, prepublication report text, direct email
bodies, unverified political assertions, and the undelivered speech remain
outside the public repository.

## Return points

- After the July 29 event, recover the delivered remarks and public record.
- After report publication, compare the final public version with the bounded
  review and record only accepted, observable changes.
- Seek explicit credit and reuse language from the published acknowledgement
  rather than relying indefinitely on a private email.
