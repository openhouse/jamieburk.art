# First contact with the private photo field

## Present question

Can the residency make one small, real contact with Jamie's lifetime photo
archive while preserving the apartment as a place of play, the archive as a
protected source, and publication as a later human decision?

## Bounded source field

Jamie authorized local research access to an Apple Photos library containing
more than 600,000 photographs and designated one private workspace as the only
place where this residency may create or change folders and albums. Jamie also
authorized later private discovery through the People names he has manually
assigned over time. Those labels may guide private source return; they are not
identity inference, public evidence, consent, or publication permission.

This first contact used a deliberately tiny recent-still field. It did not
attempt the proposed rough-draft field, a representative sample, a project
census, or a search across named people.

## Material access state

- The installed curation skill matched its public upstream repository before
  the run.
- A zero-image live authorization check passed through the stable permissioned
  Photos helper.
- The visible still-photo source was frozen by exact membership count and
  identifier digest in the protected layer. This public record intentionally
  omits both values.
- Local PhotoKit image retrieval, private preview generation, decoding, and
  local visual inspection were available. No image or metadata was uploaded.
- A bounded one-record probe of the deeper metadata provider timed out. That
  provider remains **unverified**, and this run does not claim that every
  archive capability is available.

## What was looked at

A small field of recent still photographs was retrieved locally. Automated
safety holds remained excluded. Sanitized private previews were inspected in
the studio conversation rather than replaced by labels or metadata.

One person-free domestic still life was chosen by delegated editorial
inference for a private one-image proof album. The visible frame contains an
arranged cupboard of spices, dishes, and cooking materials. It suggested the
ordinary infrastructure of hospitality: a life provisioned for cooking,
hosting, and making room. This is a provisional reading, not a claim about the
photograph's date, authorship, larger scene, professional relevance, or final
meaning.

The exact one-image proposal passed full-coverage delegated evaluation and
structural validation before any catalog write. That pass establishes fidelity
to this narrow private brief, not human clearance or aesthetic authority.

## Verification record

The operation was constrained to the workspace Jamie granted. A read-only
preflight verified the exact existing three-level parent hierarchy and unique
titles before launch. The plan allowed only creation of one album beneath that
workspace and addition of one existing asset; it allowed no deletion, source
edit, metadata edit, favorite change, face or People change, date change,
location change, or external transfer.

The first launch exposed an incompatible direct folder-identifier lookup and
exited before a receipt. A read-only catalog check confirmed that no album had
been created. The retry used the helper's parent-constrained unique-title
resolver against the separately verified hierarchy. It then produced a fresh
app receipt for one album with one member.

The exact production plan was run and rerun with distinct nonces. A receipt
comparator and an independent WAL-aware, read-only catalog verifier both
passed. The source membership and prior hierarchy remained unchanged; only the
authorized private album membership was added.

## Emerging constellation

The first image did not arrive as a famous project scene. It arrived through a
cupboard. That matters. Hospitality, maintenance, readiness, and nourishment
may be part of the operating container before they become visible as public
work. The archive's first answer is not a verdict. It is an invitation to keep
looking at the ordinary structures that make a shared life possible.

## Contradictions and anti-claims

- A one-image proof is not the proposed rough-draft field and cannot establish
  the archive's range, themes, completeness, or quality.
- A person-free frame does not prove that its wider context is impersonal,
  non-sensitive, rights-cleared, or suitable for publication.
- Automated face absence is not identity review, consent, or a guarantee that
  every sensitive detail has been recognized.
- A domestic still life is not by itself evidence of professional
  accomplishment, project impact, hosting outcomes, or another person's
  experience.
- Successful catalog membership does not clear the image, caption,
  interpretation, crop, or any public destination.

## Protected absences

The image, source and album identifiers, exact source count and digest,
filenames, private paths, preview, metadata, timestamp, location, face and
People labels, safety notes, HOLD contents, receipts, and local run manifests
remain outside public Git. The exact private parent titles are also omitted.

## Source and librarian requests

- Before a People-centered retrieval, validate one bounded record through a
  reviewed metadata provider and keep names private unless separately approved.
- Return to the original image, nearby sequence, and source context before
  making any historical, relational, or project claim from this visual lead.
- Let Jamie decide whether this small domestic reading should remain private,
  become a notebook fragment, or disappear from later composition.

## Rights, consent, and safety state

The selected asset remains `publication-review-required`. No person, rights
holder, author, caption, crop, claim, or destination has been cleared. AI
assisted private discovery and evaluation; it did not clear a hold, infer
consent, or authorize publication.

## Editorial hypothesis

The image may be useful as a private orientation toward hospitality as
infrastructure. It should not be promoted because it satisfies the present
metaphor. Later photographs may deepen, contradict, or displace this reading.

## Publication decision

Hold. Public-safe process synthesis only. No photograph, private locator, or
reconstructable archive detail is committed or projected by this entry.

The executable [studio-connection evaluation](../evaluations/photography-studio-connection.md)
keeps the successful private operation separate from rights, consent,
interpretation, and publication authority.
