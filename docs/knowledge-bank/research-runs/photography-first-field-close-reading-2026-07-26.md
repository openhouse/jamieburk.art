# First field and website-shortlist close reading

## Present question

What became newly possible after creating and closely reading a broad private
field from Jamie's lifetime photo archive?

## Bounded source field

The residency completed a private field of exactly 1,000 unique still
photographs inside Jamie's authorized Apple Photos workspace. A subsequent
editorial pass inspected the full field and created an 18-image editorial
shortlist in a separately verified private album system.

This is not a publication edit. The field is provisional and
non-representative; the shortlist records attention, not permission or a final
argument.

## Material access state

The earlier field and shortlist runs produced exact, independently checked
membership receipts in the protected local layer. No private locator, source
identifier, filename, face label, contact sheet, or image file enters this
public repository.

During the July 26 return, the static machine profile and installed curation
bridge passed their local checks, but a fresh live PhotoKit authorization check
timed out. This record therefore relies on the completed, previously verified
field and does not claim a fresh whole-library authorization receipt.

## What was learned

- A large, deliberately varied private field can make recurrent gestures,
  materials, places, and working conditions visible across projects.
- A small shortlist can support close conversation without deciding what the
  site must publish.
- Images of tools and interfaces in use may reveal implementation more directly
  than generalized claims about technical capability.
- A compelling frame often opens factual questions about photographer,
  represented people, date, place, event, sequence, use, and afterlife.
- Oral history can develop an image as a research lead while the image itself
  remains held.

## CallNYC threshold

One held shortlist photograph appears to show a hand-held CallNYC issue-route
interface in use. It is a strong candidate for oral history because it may help
recover how the interface moved from code into a social or civic setting.

The visible frame does not establish who made the photograph, whose hand is
shown, the date or setting, who else was present, how the interface was being
used, whether an official saw or adopted it, what changed because of it, or
whether this exact image may be published.

## Rights, consent, and safety state

Every shortlisted image remains subject to exact-asset review for authorship,
rights, represented-person consent where applicable, privacy, public safety,
caption, crop, credit, context, editorial fit, and Jamie's specific-use
approval. Production, deployment, and indexing remain separate human gates.

## Publication decision

Hold. The field and shortlist remain private working material. The CallNYC
candidate proceeds only as an oral-history question until its context and
publication boundaries are reviewed.
