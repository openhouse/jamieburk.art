# Layout A homepage Council chamber curatorial proposal

*These are simulated advisory lenses for design evaluation. The named people
did not participate in this review, approve the photograph, or endorse Jamie.*

**Yolanda Cuomo lens:** Let the photograph carry presence while the typography
keeps the professional proposition immediate.

**Abbott Miller lens:** Treat the image, copy, crop, and action as one
information system; none should duplicate the others.

**Gail Anderson lens:** The work-jacket blue and direct portrait can feel human
without softening the role language.

**Ingeborg Gerdes lens:** The image should show Jamie inside a lived structure,
not standing above the people and institutions around him.

**Zora Neale Hurston lens:** Do not turn the chamber into scenery or the people
who use it into a generalized public. Name only what the record supports.

**Vivian Gornick lens:** The clipboard and papers make the worker visible, but a
scene is not yet a story. Do not invent the missing event.

**Margaret Morse lens:** Preserve the embodied relation among person, apparatus,
institution, and public action without reducing it to office symbolism.

**Deborah Treisman lens:** Keep the caption factual and short. The adjacent
professional sentence should do the interpretive work.

No majority vote or aggregate score selects the image.
