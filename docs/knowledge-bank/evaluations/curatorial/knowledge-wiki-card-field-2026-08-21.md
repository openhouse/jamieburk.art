# Knowledge Wiki Graph photographic metaphor review

The selected image makes the method legible without needing interface chrome or
an explanatory diagram. The cards are nodes, their proximity proposes
relations, and the visible floor leaves room for revision. Most importantly,
the participants' language remains the substance of the system.

The lab therefore uses the image as a transition from the current research
claim to the three-graph model. The composition does not convert the historical
session into evidence that the later architecture already existed.
