*Imagined editorial perspectives, not endorsements.*

Two archive photographs enter the public edition. The Sunday Dinner image shows a shared table, a hand-drawn map, and an active social moment. Neither image is used to prove an outcome, credential, sole authorship, or identity not established by an approved public source.

The six covers form a deliberate editorial sequence rather than six isolated decorations. Three public-interface captures, one interface-and-gathering capture, and two field photographs make the link between systems and lived participation visible. Captions and credits remain attached wherever the cover appears. The private corpus remains private.
