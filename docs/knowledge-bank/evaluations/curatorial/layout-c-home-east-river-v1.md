# Layout C homepage East River curatorial proposal

their exact public-safe input receipt, disagreement, and the movement between
looking and context. It does not simulate consensus and does not select or
publish by vote.

## Counterfactual

- **Selected occurrence:** creates bodily presence, public scale, and a threshold
  into the practice.
- **Meaningful alternative:** the raft is more collective and historically
  astonishing, but weaker for immediate present-role orientation.
- **No-photo version:** maximizes role clarity but loses material presence,
  public landscape, and the felt transition into the work.

The lead proposal is strongest only as part of the complete occurrence:
photograph, crop, role copy, caption, credit, next action, and viewport.

The contextual reader specifically held the corrected credit and final crop for
human review. That is a feature of the record: contextual coherence is not
publication clearance.
