# Layout C homepage East River curatorial proposal

*Imagined editorial perspectives, not endorsements.*

The curatorial session used a blind visual pass before returning to filename,
date, People relations, project context, and site purpose. The panel preserved
an alternative and dissent rather than turning simulated voices into a vote.

The lead proposal kept the East River photograph because it and the writing do
different jobs. The image creates presence, public scale, weather, equipment,
and movement. The copy establishes the literal role, operating proposition,
and next actions.

The alternative and dissent remain useful. They are not losing candidates, and
no aggregate score or ranked-choice procedure has publication authority.
