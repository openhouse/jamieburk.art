# Forwarded Team Memory hiring scenario gate

This gate tests whether the Team Memory page can travel as an internal referral.
The modeled sequence begins when a prospective technical leader shares the page
with company leaders who have budget and hiring authority. Those leaders may
navigate public routes beginning at the Team Memory page, inspect the public
resume, discuss the immediate organizational need, and return a binary hire or
pass decision. The advocate then relays that modeled decision and the smallest
truthful communication improvements to Jamie.

Deterministic checks run before any language-model call. They verify the exact
anonymous artifact allowlist, prohibit repository and transcript access, bind
navigation to the public local site, and require a forwardable decision brief,
explicit team attention, Jamie's responsibility, a small first engagement,
one-click resume and contact paths, and a strict binary decision shape.

The isolated model may receive only the anonymous technical-leader perspective,
the high-level conversational-voice analysis, and the locally rendered public
site. It may not receive Jamie's perspective document, raw or working
transcripts, private communications, repository source, protected company
identity or commercial details, or earlier judge output. Any real-person role
binding and raw modeled conversation remain outside this repository.

This is a development simulation, not the real people's participation,
endorsement, prediction, offer, or hiring decision. It remains advisory until
calibrated against at least twenty human-labeled hire examples and twenty
human-labeled pass examples. Jamie retains authority over outreach, scope,
commercial terms, publication, and acceptance.

## Current development result

The deterministic preflight passes all ten exact-input, public-navigation,
decision-brief, team-attention, Jamie-responsibility, resume, contact, and
calibration checks. Mutation tests verify that removing the internal decision
brief, team-side owner, resume path, why-Jamie statement, pre-kickoff terms, or
source boundary blocks modeled review.

An isolated development run on August 21, 2026 began with harness-captured
renderings of the local Team Memory page, public resume, resume landing page,
and Technical Operations page. It received only those public renderings plus
the anonymous technical-leader perspective and high-level conversational-voice
analysis. It did not receive repository source, Jamie's perspective document,
raw transcripts, private communications, company identity, commercial details,
or prior judge output.

The fictionalized advocate-forwardability and relay-usefulness gates passed.
The fictionalized company decision-maker gate returned `hire` for the focused
paid discovery sprint, subject to ordinary budget, contracting,
confidentiality, ownership, source-authority, retention, and internal approval.
The modeled readers saw the remaining uncertainty clearly: adoption inside the
prospective team is not established and is the question the sprint must test.

The result is an uncalibrated synthetic development signal. It does not
represent the protected person's opinion, the company's participation, a real
authorization, an offer, employment, or a commercial outcome.
