# Employment context coverage baseline - July 18, 2026

The matrices in frontmatter are diagnostic, not a score of Jamie's life. They
show whether a professional pattern is findable, supported, publicly visible,
or still dependent on bounded research.

## Private Source Vault rule

Raw evidence remains immutable and private. Normalization is repeatable,
generated views are rebuildable, and human annotations remain separate. Access
is not completeness, permission, publication approval, or identity certainty.

## External outcome boundary

Applications, referrals, recruiter responses, interviews, rejections, offers,
and one-year team health are private external outcomes. No automated evaluator
may mark them complete.
