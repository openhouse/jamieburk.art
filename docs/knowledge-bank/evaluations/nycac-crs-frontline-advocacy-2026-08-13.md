# NYCAC and Fair Rent NYC frontline advocacy eval

The hill climb began with a failing test because the governed 30-day source
return did not yet exist. The bounded implementation then added one canonical
source family, three held claims, one typed speaking relation, wiki records,
and a federated update map.

## Acceptance gates

The exact candidate must:

- declare the July 14-August 13 window;
- preserve the public report as the canonical public source;
- keep public sources and protected summaries distinct;
- bound credited review against authorship and data-analysis inflation;
- retain the report's registered-rent and causation limits;
- keep prepared and delivered remarks separate;
- reject a footage request as proof of delivery or coverage;
- reject city routing as endorsement;
- reject scheduled state alignment as an occurred meeting or commitment;
- reject a requested social collaboration as a completed collaboration;
- withhold protected URLs and disable their rendered citations;
- keep private payload and locators out of public-safe files;
- hold every new portfolio projection;
- preserve a typed, shared-credit speaking relation;
- materialize the governed wiki update map; and
- add no public archive or claims route.

Adversarial tests mutate each high-risk boundary and must fail closed. A passing
eval does not supply consent, rights clearance, factual authority beyond the
declared source population, editorial approval, merge authority, deployment,
indexing, or publication permission.
