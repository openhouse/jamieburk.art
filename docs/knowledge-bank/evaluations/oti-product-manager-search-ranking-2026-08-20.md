# OTI product-manager search - comparative fit review

## Method and boundary

The ranking weights evidence-backed role fit at 55% and realistic near-term
securability at 45%. Securability considers explicit minimum and preferred
qualifications, applicant restrictions, the legibility of Jamie's present
public evidence, and role specialization. It is a prioritization tool, not a
prediction of an interview, offer, civil-service eligibility decision, or
employer judgment.

Top 38% means the four highest-ranked roles: `floor(11 × 0.38) = 4`. Four of
eleven is 36.4%, the largest whole-number selection that does not exceed 38%.
There is no cutoff tie.

## Decision

Add ranks 1-4 to the active opportunity graph. Keep ranks 5-11 here so the
research remains complete and the cutoff remains auditable. Re-run the ranking
when an official listing changes, a hard-screen fact is resolved, or stronger
evidence materially changes fit; do not move the cutoff merely to improve the
public résumé's apparent breadth.

## Human gates

- Jamie decides which opportunities become applications.
- OTI alone determines minimum-qualification and civil-service eligibility.
- Scores do not authorize claims, applications, or named-person endorsements.
- A live application under consideration is private runtime state unless Jamie
  explicitly authorizes a public record.
