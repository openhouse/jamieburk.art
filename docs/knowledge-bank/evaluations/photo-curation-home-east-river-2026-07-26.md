# East River homepage curatorial proposal

## Complete occurrence

The proposal evaluates:

```text
image + crop + typography + copy + caption + credit + sequence + viewport + action
```

It does not rank the image in isolation.

## Recommendation

Retain the current Layout B placement for pull-request and staging review,
correct the creator credit, and keep production and indexing open. The
text-only alternative remains viable. The dissent is a real stop condition,
not a low score to average away.

No aggregate score or ranked-choice vote may become artistic or publication
authority.
