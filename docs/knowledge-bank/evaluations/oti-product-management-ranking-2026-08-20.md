# Jamie-specific ranking of the NYC OTI product-manager search corpus

This ranking compares eleven roles returned by the exact CityJobs search on
August 20, 2026. It is an evidence-based prioritization aid, not a hiring
probability or employer decision.

## Method

- `fit` (60%): overlap with Jamie's demonstrated work, mission, working style,
  compensation goal, and healthy next-step trajectory.
- `securability` (40%): vacancy count, civil-service and applicant eligibility,
  preferred-experience gaps, legibility of evidence, and salary compatibility.
- `combined = fit × 0.60 + securability × 0.40`.
- Scores are relative evidence scores on a 0-100 scale, not percentages.

## Staging acceptance signal

After the first staging release, four isolated public-web-only reader tasks
evaluated eight selected opportunity-reader gates. Two of eight strict gates
passed, both for Product Manager 784450. The other six held on material screens:
Senior IT Architect qualification, enterprise-data depth, or documented formal
budget, procurement, and contracting ownership. These are synthetic analytical
lenses, not actual employer opinions or hiring decisions.

That signal changes the immediate application order without erasing the harder
opportunities. Product Manager 784450 should be the first application. The PIT
Crew Senior Product Manager remains the strongest mission fit, SPEED Operations
remains a strong adjacent operating fit, and SPEED Senior Product Manager
remains a credible stretch; each needs truthful evidence or employer
qualification review rather than more assertive copy.

## 38% selection

The nearest whole-number selection is four roles: `round(11 × 0.38) = 4`.
Four of eleven is 36.4%; five would be 45.5% and would overshoot the requested
cut more substantially. The top four become maintained opportunity records.
