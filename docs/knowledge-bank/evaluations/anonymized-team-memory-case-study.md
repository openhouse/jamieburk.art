# Anonymized Source-Backed Team Memory case-study gate

The case-study candidate contains exactly three Markdown documents: the
technical leader's perspective, Jamie's perspective, and an analytical profile
of the technical leader's conversational voice. The private source family
remains outside the repository. The candidate does not reproduce transcript
quotations, identities, employer or product names, contact information, exact
chronology, staffing, commercial terms, family details, private paths, or
distinctive private anecdotes.

The deterministic preflight verifies document completeness, minimum depth,
required perspective structure, obvious identifier patterns, exact date and
commercial-detail patterns, transcript block quotations, preferred public
language, interpretive limits, and a non-impersonation statement. Mutation
tests demonstrate that injected contact information, a named organization,
commercial detail, exact date, transcript quotation, or impersonation
instruction blocks modeled review.

The deterministic result is a prerequisite, not an anonymity certificate.
Before any case-study material is projected onto the website, Jamie must review
the combined packet for re-identification risk and decide whether the remaining
detail is necessary, fair, and safe.

After that review, an isolated fictionalized-reader evaluation may test whether
both perspectives feel remarkably accurate in motivation, skepticism, care,
constraints, and unresolved outcome, and whether the voice profile is faithful
analysis rather than imitation. The judge remains advisory until calibrated
against human-labeled passing and failing examples.

## Current development result

The exact candidate passes twelve deterministic checks, including mutations
for contact information, a named organization, exact date and commercial
details, first-person protected testimony, transcript quotation, impersonation
instructions, targeted-reader prediction, and a distinctive relationship or
personal-practice cluster.

Three corpus-only development reviews drove successive privacy revisions. The
current run passes the technical-leader perspective, Jamie perspective, and
high-level communication-analysis gates. It fails the combined anonymity gate:
an informed reader might triangulate the relationship from cumulative detail
across the three requested documents even though direct identifiers and
distinctive private anecdotes are absent.

That synthetic result is advisory, but the risk is substantive. The case-study
candidate remains held from website projection until Jamie reviews the three
documents together and accepts, revises, or rejects the residual mosaic risk.
