# NYC Artist Coalition Facebook posts, 2017-2021

**Reviewed:** July 15, 2026

**Status:** Public-safe archival production; portfolio composition held

**Ledger:** [`data/nycartc-facebook-post-ledger.json`](data/nycartc-facebook-post-ledger.json)

## Answer first

The recovered Facebook record shows that NYC Artist Coalition used a shared
publishing surface as operating infrastructure: recurring meetings and public
actions were joined to campaign routes, practical resources, source reporting,
official data, cultural-space testimony, and solidarity.

Two overlapping controls preserve the population:

- a completed public chronology traversal produced **413 conservative content
  signatures** across displayed dates from January 29, 2017 through September
  15, 2021;
- native Meta Business Suite annual exports produced **185 exact unique Page
  post IDs** for 2019 through 2021.

These populations overlap. **They must never be added.** The first has broader
displayed chronology but weaker record identity; the second has exact IDs and
dates for fewer years.

Every recovered item has a redacted disposition in the ledger. Raw captions,
comments, people, per-post private analytics, administrator state, working
documents, meeting routes, financial routes, and participant relationship data
remain outside the public repository.

## Population controls

### Public chronology

The authenticated forward traversal required 522 overlapping scroll steps and
12 stable terminal confirmations. It recovered:

- 634 candidate cards;
- 460 distinct normalized records;
- 413 distinct content signatures.

The 413 count is a conservative count of content exposed by the completed
public-surface traversal. It is not an exact lifetime post count: repeated
identical posts may merge, and deleted, hidden, unavailable, or unrendered
records are outside the surface.

A reverse traversal failed because Facebook did not rehydrate older cards. That
failed control is part of the record; it is not represented as independent
confirmation.

## Traction boundary

The July 2026 native exports display aggregate lifetime values of:

- **1,006 reactions**;
- **164 comments**;
- **208 shares**.

These are mutable platform labels, not 1,378 people. They do not identify
elected officials, agencies, artists, venues, or other stakeholder groups, and
they do not measure historical reach, adoption, attendance, endorsement,
causality, or impact. Per-post reach, click, and engagement fields remain
protected.

No defensible count of reciprocal engagement by key stakeholder groups was
recovered in this pass. The archive does preserve repeated *addressing* of
public institutions and routing to public records; neither is converted into
engagement authored by those institutions.

## Jamie's role and collective credit

Jamie remembers that he was predominantly the person who used the NYC Artist
Coalition Facebook Page, while other coalition members used it as well. The
native export does not include a publishing-administrator field. The memory is
therefore retained as an attributed, held claim rather than published as a
verified individual credit.

The population supports a coherent shared publishing system. It does not make
Jamie the sole administrator or author of every post, campaign, event, source,
image, quotation, or idea. NYC Artist Coalition was collective work.

## Composition decision

No new claim was added to the visible portfolio in this pass. The current site
already presents the coalition's participation system and Jamie's source-backed
organizing role. The deeper Facebook corpus now remains available for a future
page, application, résumé, photo brief, or source note when that composition
reduces a reader's burden.

## Protected absences

- administrator and authentication data;
- Google Docs and Drive working routes;
- Zoom and meeting credentials;
- financial-transfer routes;
- participant and profile relationship graphs;
- raw crawl or export files;
- private filesystem paths.

The absence of these fields is part of the archive's care, not a claim that the
records do not exist.
