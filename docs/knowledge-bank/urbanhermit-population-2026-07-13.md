## Population And Method

The population control is the 434-post count displayed beside the account name
on July 13, 2026.

1. Read the authenticated live profile, October 2008 join date, and 434-post
   control.
2. Harvested the Posts timeline to its October 2008 endpoint, recovering 421
   unique visible records.
3. Harvested the Replies surface and filtered out conversation context not
   authored by Jamie.
4. Recovered 13 Jamie-authored replies absent from Posts.
5. Deduplicated the Posts and Replies union by status ID, producing exactly 434
   surviving records.
6. Classified every record by year, record type, and one primary interpretive
   theme. Records without readable prose received a media-only or
   text-unavailable label.
7. Separated authored records from reposts before interpreting the account.
8. Close-read public-safe professional candidates and followed their source
   links outward.
9. Independently corroborated the strongest new credit through NPR Music.
10. Replaced the protected research corpus with an aggregate-only public ledger
    before repository ingestion.

## Privacy And Public-Safety Boundary

Historic personal records include ordinary-life, relationship, health,
location, and superseded contact material. Although individual records may
remain publicly reachable, aggregating their full text into a public repository
would create a new dossier. This pass therefore uses a stricter boundary than
the project-account censuses.

The public repository excludes:

- full post text and quoted-post text;
- status IDs, status URLs, exact timestamps, and mentioned handles;
- historic contact and location details;
- relationship, health, and family material;
- raw interaction counts and account analytics;
- protected browser captures and extraction artifacts;
- media whose content, rights, or context has not been reviewed.
