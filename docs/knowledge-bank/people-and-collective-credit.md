# People, Collective Credit, and Protected Absences

The Knowledge Wiki should make Jamie's work visible without turning collective
history into individual property. It should also preserve the presence of
people whose names, roles, testimony, or records cannot appropriately become
public payload.

## Credit record

Every material project narrative should distinguish:

| Question | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| What did Jamie do? | Direct action, artifact, decision, or operating role supported by evidence |
| Who worked with him? | Named public collaborators where the record and context support naming |
| What remained collective? | Campaign, event, institutional, community, and participant labor not assignable to Jamie |
| What authorship is unknown? | Shared accounts, drafts, media, or artifacts without reliable creator evidence |
| Who holds formal authority? | Council, agency, employer, owner, funder, or other decision maker |
| What cannot be published? | Protected identities, contacts, testimony, vulnerabilities, records, or media |

## Protected absence

Absence from a public page can mean many things: not recovered, not
materialized, deliberately unprocessed, not rights-cleared, not consented,
unsafe to expose, irrelevant to the current purpose, or simply unknown. It
must not be rewritten as nonexistence or lack of contribution.

Use a role placeholder or protected-source class when that preserves a
relationship without exposing the person. Do not create pressure for a person
to become public merely because their contribution would strengthen a claim.

## Collective outcomes

A collective outcome does not require Jamie to disappear. Name his documented
action and then name the larger decision system. For example:

- Jamie organized, built, testified, documented, or facilitated.
- Collaborators and participants contributed distinct labor and knowledge.
- A coalition sustained the public campaign.
- A legislature, agency, employer, or project owner made the formal decision.
- Later conditions may have changed for reasons the current record cannot
  isolate.

## Correction and consent

Collaborator corrections should enter through a bounded proof note or governed
record, with source class, scope, permission, and intended use stated. A
testimonial is not required: a collaborator may correct role, chronology,
credit, or boundary without endorsing Jamie or agreeing to public quotation.

Access authorization is not permission to retain, quote, name, publish, or
promote. Those decisions remain separable and revocable where appropriate.

## Eval contract

A page fails this policy when it:

- assigns a collective campaign or governmental decision solely to Jamie;
- names a private person or role without a public-safe basis;
- treats account custody as authorship of every post;
- treats a plan as completion or a response counter as attendance;
- omits an evidenced collaborator from a role-bearing sentence;
- converts protected or unknown authorship into Jamie's authorship; or
- treats an accessible record as permission to publish it.
