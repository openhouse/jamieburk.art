# CallNYC: 2016 Citation Record

## Public chronology

- January 29, 2016: Civic Hall announced a January 30 New York City Council
  hackathon focused on constituent services, scheduled for 1-3 p.m.
- January 30, 2016: a New York City Council post located the gathering at Civic
  Hall and described it as the Council's first CouncilStat hackathon.
- A recovered Council-branded graphic reads `New York City Council Hackathon`.
- After the full CouncilStat dataset became public, Jamie independently developed
  CallNYC as a resident-facing interpretation of constituent-services records.
- Politico New York covered CallNYC in March 2016; the public repository preserves
  the surviving implementation.

The official posts establish the event wording and context. They do not establish
CallNYC's later functionality. The Politico coverage and public repository support
the independent follow-on product claim.

## Research run

The public-safe research run records 4,630 deduplicated Civic Hall captures,
1,240 original URLs, 296 event URL keys, 215 successful pages, 74 redirects, and
seven captured 404s. No dedicated calendar listing or event-detail page was
recovered.

This does not prove that no page ever existed, that Civic Hall did not promote the
event elsewhere, or that an external registration page was never available. The
Wayback page is an embedded-social-feed carrier, not the missing listing.

## Corrections

Two resolved records preserve the material changes:

- CallNYC chronology: `2014-2015` corrected to `2016`.
- Event wording: the unqualified `first civic-data hackathon` corrected to the
  attributed statement that the Council called the gathering its first
  `CouncilStat hackathon`.

follow-on product, public repository, press coverage, and bounded research result
have public-safe support.

**Open:** No editable resume source is present in the repository. The approved
binary PDF retains the former event wording and needs a separate Jamie-reviewed
