# CallNYC: 2016 Citation Record

## Public chronology

- January 29, 2016: Civic Hall announced a January 30 New York City Council
  hackathon focused on constituent services, scheduled for 1-3 p.m.
- January 30, 2016: an event-day New York City Council post located the gathering
  at Civic Hall and described it as the Council's first CouncilStat hackathon.
- Jamie later independently built CallNYC.org as a resident-facing
  interpretation of CouncilStat constituent-services data.
- Politico New York covered CallNYC in 2016; that existing proof remains separate
  from the new event sources.

The public page uses the first two propositions with citations. The new social
and archival sources do not establish the exact dataset-release date, CallNYC's
launch date, full event agenda, attendance, or a formal submission relationship.

## Archival finding

The Wayback capture preserves the relevant Civic Hall and New York City Council
posts in Civic Hall's embedded social feed. It is archival context, not a
recovered calendar listing or event-detail page.

The public-safe research audit records a search of 4,630 deduplicated Civic Hall
captures, 1,240 original URLs, and 296 event URL keys. No dedicated listing was
recovered. This does not prove that no listing ever existed or that no external
registration page was available.

venue, and the Council's exact CouncilStat wording are supported by public
sources and archive context.
