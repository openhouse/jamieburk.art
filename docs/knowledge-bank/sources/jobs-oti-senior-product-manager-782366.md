# NYC Jobs - Senior Product Manager, Job ID 782366

The official posting identifies four full-time NYC Office of Technology and
Innovation Senior Product Manager positions in the Public Interest Technology
Crew. The earlier vacancy expired August 14, 2026. The official page was
reposted August 17, 2026 and, when rechecked August 20, listed a
$100,000-$180,000 salary range and an October 16, 2026 deadline.

The work spans resident-facing problem definition, discovery, rapid
prototyping, MVP and pilot decisions, public launch, cross-functional product
leadership, agency and policy translation, production quality, accessibility
and compliance, measurement, public rollout, documentation, and product
culture. The preferred-skills section asks for five or more years of product
management and experience carrying digital products from concept to public
launch in complex environments.

The civil-service title is Senior IT Architect. Its minimum-qualification
language is a separate screen framed around education and substantial
architecture, solutions, network, infrastructure, or related experience. No
project-level fit analysis can decide that screen without Jamie's complete
education and employment chronology and employer review.
