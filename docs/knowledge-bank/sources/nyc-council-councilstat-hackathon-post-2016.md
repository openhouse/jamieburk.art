# New York City Council Event-Day Post

The Council's contemporaneous post identifies the January 30 event at Civic
Hall and describes it as the Council's first CouncilStat hackathon. That
description must remain attributed to the Council.

It does not establish a broader first civic-data event, full agenda, complete
attendance, formal winners, or CallNYC as an official submission.
