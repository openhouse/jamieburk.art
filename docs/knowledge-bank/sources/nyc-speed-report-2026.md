# NYC SPEED report and release, May 2026

The official report expands SPEED as **Streamlining Procedures to Expedite
Equitable Development**. The interagency task force was established by
Executive Order 5 to reduce administrative and permitting delays in affordable
housing production.

The report says the task force consulted more than 100 industry participants,
more than 100 City employees, and more than 500 submitted recommendations. Its
seven major initiatives include reducing pre-certification time, assigning a
central project-management team to City-financed affordable projects, improving
specific permitting and inspection processes, reworking the housing lottery,
and accelerating placement from shelter into permanent housing.

This context changes how the associated OTI roles should be read. They are not
generic internal software jobs: they support multi-agency delivery, public
accountability, data and process modernization, and measurable reductions in
the time and burden between a policy commitment and housing for New Yorkers.

The associated [official release](https://home4.nyc.gov/site/hpd/news/035-26/mamdani-administration-releases-speed-reforms-deliver-affordable-housing-faster)
states that the package is intended to cut development timelines by eight
months generally and by as much as two years for projects requiring a zoning
change. Those are program goals, not outcomes attributable to any one future
hire.
