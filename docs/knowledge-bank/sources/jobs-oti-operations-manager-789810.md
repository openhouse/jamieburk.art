# NYC Jobs - Operations Manager 789810

The official posting describes one full-time OTI Operations Manager on the
SPEED team, using the IT Project Specialist title, at $75,000-$160,000. It was
posted August 7, 2026 and closes October 4, 2026.

The role coordinates budget, procurement, legal, sourcing, cybersecurity, and
other support functions; tracks incoming, in-flight, and upcoming work;
allocates resources; manages expense and capital budgets; supports procurement
of tools and personnel; onboards new hires; and reports team health, project
status, and operational metrics. Preferred experience includes five or more
years in operations, program management, chief-of-staff, or similar work;
contracts and budgets; plain-language communication; and cross-disciplinary
digital product delivery.

The posting does not name a manager. Its preference for public-sector
contracting and procurement is meaningful but is not stated as the civil-
service minimum. Employer review controls eligibility.
