# Protected Commercial-Rent Current-Workstream Bundle

The private originals remain outside this repository. They include a repaired
conversation transcript, a repaired speech-rehearsal transcript, a
pre-publication report copy with tracked editorial work, and event-coordination
correspondence.

The public repository retains only a bounded account of the working methods
visible across the bundle:

- factual, methodological, legislative, attribution, and usability review;
- separation of observed association from causal or motive claims;
- questions about source comparability, weighting, missing values, district
  boundaries, reproducibility, and sensitivity analysis;
- requests for source links, correction contacts, accessible graphics, and
  precise legislative wording;
- preparation of a short cultural-space and public-data argument;
- explicit separation among a rehearsal, final speech, organizational
  position, published report, and independently verified finding.

## Does Not Establish

- consent to publish or quote a private conversation;
- final approval of the draft report, speech, acknowledgments, or run of show;
- that every proposed correction was accepted;
- that a rehearsal statement is factually verified or was delivered publicly;
- endorsement by a collaborator, campaign, public official, or institution;
- the final event location or attendance.
