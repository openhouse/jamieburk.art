# Official posting - University of Innsbruck ./studio3 postdoc ARCH-15927

The posting describes a `20 hours/week` University Assistant postdoc based in
Innsbruck, beginning October 1, 2026, with a four-year fixed term and an August
27, 2026 application deadline. Compensation is listed as EUR 2,507 gross per
month, paid fourteen times per year.

The work combines independent experimental-architecture and artistic-design
research; third-party project acquisition, support, and leadership; teaching
and examinations; student and bachelor-thesis supervision; administration,
evaluation, and public relations; and professional development.

The posting expressly requires both a completed diploma, Magister, or master's
degree in Architecture **and** a completed doctorate or PhD in Architecture.
It also asks for research excellence; contemporary architectural theory,
critical discourse, and artistic-research methods; real and virtual spatial
research using multimedia and digital methods; immersive projection,
multidimensional animation, and realtime generation; research, grant,
teaching, and university-administration experience; and very good German and
English.

Aline Krabacher is named as the contact for questions. The posting does not
identify a direct manager or hiring decision-maker.

Recheck the [official posting](https://lfuonline.uibk.ac.at/public/karriereportal.details?asg_id_in=15927)
before outward use.
