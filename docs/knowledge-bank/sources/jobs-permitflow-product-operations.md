# Official posting - PermitFlow Product Operations Manager

The official posting describes a New York hybrid role connecting Product,
Engineering, Analytics, AI, Operations, Sales, and Customer Success. It
emphasizes product data and customer insight, launch readiness, dashboards,
experimentation, AI-assisted workflows, and bottleneck removal in fragmented
physical-world permitting processes. The August 15 posting asks for two or
more years of relevant product, business, or cross-functional operations work
and does not disclose current compensation.
