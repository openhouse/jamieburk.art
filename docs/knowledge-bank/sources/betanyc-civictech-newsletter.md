# BetaNYC weekly civic-tech newsletter

BetaNYC describes this as a free weekly newsletter about NYC civic technology,
open data, and public-interest technology. Its Jobs & Opportunities section is
valuable because it crosses City agencies, other public institutions,
mission-driven companies, advocacy organizations, and fellowships. The rest of
each issue supplies field context: who is building, what is changing, where
people are gathering, and what public-service problems need attention.

An authorized catch-up review covered eight distinct weekly issues from July 2
through August 20, 2026. The August 20 issue contained nineteen job or
fellowship leads. Thirteen resolved to official NYC Jobs records. One—the NYC
Campaign Finance Board Associate Director of Policy Implementation role—cleared
the governed deterministic gates and then received an individual official-
posting review.

## Authority and privacy

The recurring email is the delivery signal and the public archive is the
automation-safe retrieval surface. Duplicate deliveries are one issue, not new
opportunities. Subscriber identifiers, recipient details, message identifiers,
and tracking URLs are not retained.

The newsletter is discovery evidence, not employer authority. An employer's
official posting controls title, compensation, deadline, applicant access,
minimum qualifications, and application instructions. A source match can enter
a private action queue; it cannot submit an application or establish that a
person will be hired.
