# Empty Storefronts, High Rents final report edition

The governed archive now holds the exact 20-page final edition dated July 28,

## What the edition establishes

The acknowledgment page thanks Jamie, identified as a member of NYC Artist
Coalition participating in Fair Rent NYC, for “thoughtful and careful review of
the draft report.” Protected comparison records show that his bounded review
addressed factual accuracy, source and method clarity, current bill language,
and claims likely to travel through press and public speaking.

This is meaningful external credit. It is not authorship. The edition does not
identify Jamie as an author, analyst, lawyer, editor, or owner of the report, and
the review did not rerun the underlying analysis.

## Finding boundary

The report's rent and vacancy comparison supports a descriptive finding: high
registered rents and high storefront vacancy can coexist. That comparison does
not by itself establish landlord motive, deliberate warehousing, or a financing
mechanism. Those stronger explanations remain hypotheses requiring additional
methods, data, and legal or editorial review.

The report retains stronger causal language than that evidence boundary. The
Knowledge Wiki therefore records the public report accurately without adopting
all of its causal language as a canonical finding.
