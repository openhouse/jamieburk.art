# Official PermitFlow leadership and product-vision context

PermitFlow's official Series B announcement identifies Francis Thumpasery as
Co-Founder and CEO and describes the company's effort to automate fragmented
construction workflows. This supports only a senior vision relationship to the
Product Operations Manager role; it does not establish a direct manager,
interviewer, hiring decision-maker, or private opinion.
