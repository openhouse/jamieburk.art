# NYC Artist Coalition DCLA formation source return, 2026

## Public-safe source note

An authenticated return to the protected source field recovered the official
and coalition event frames, early issue gathering and naming materials,
successive letter versions, meeting notes, approval-ballot records, working
group and mutual-aid infrastructure, and a tailored venue-outreach sequence.

Protected communications support a pattern of deliberate, venue-specific
stakeholder outreach before the meeting. That is not an audited delivery,
attendance, response, or impact count. The sequence also makes the continuity
from earlier participation infrastructure into coalition mobilization visible
without assigning sole authorship or origin to one person or project.

The meeting audio, speaker research, and interactive visualization were not
recovered in this pass and remain wanted sources.

## Credit boundary

Jamie helped build communications and participation infrastructure through
which the emerging coalition gathered concerns, mobilized attendance, recruited
venue representation and facilitation, shared a public letter and meeting
record, convened follow-through, selected priorities, and established routes
into mutual aid, working groups, and continuing advocacy.

This was collective work. The record does not establish Jamie's authorship of
every message, document, decision, or campaign outcome, and public credit should
not erase collaborators whose exact roles require further review.

## Protection

Raw communications, recipient and participant lists, private filenames and
paths, message bodies, source identifiers, and unpublished shared records remain
outside public Git. This capsule authorizes public-safe synthesis only; it does
not clear a photograph, quote, identity, or private record for publication.
