# Governed professional-record research comparison, August 2026

Three exact private candidates were compared for source coverage, public-safe
facts, unresolved gaps, and promotion boundaries. The public repository retains
only content fingerprints, source-level conclusions, and bounded synthesis.

It does not contain raw correspondence, messages, private transcripts, contact
routes, communication counts, recommendation strategy, local paths, account
inventories, or unapproved photographs. A private candidate is research input;
it is not publication permission.
