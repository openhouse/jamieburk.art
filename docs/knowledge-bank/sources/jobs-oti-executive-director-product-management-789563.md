# NYC Jobs - Executive Director, Product Management, Job ID 789563

The official posting describes an open Executive Director role that would lead,
manage, and coach product managers, build strategic relationships across
agencies, remove organizational roadblocks, and support SPEED outcomes. It does
not name an incumbent, state that every product or operations role reports to
this position, or identify an actual hiring manager.

This record therefore supports a **role-only** hiring-reader lens. It must not be
converted into a fictional person, a direct-report claim, or evidence that the
role has been filled.
