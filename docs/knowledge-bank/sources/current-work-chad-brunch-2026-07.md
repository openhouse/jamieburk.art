# Protected portfolio and current-work conversation, July 2026

## Public-safe orientation

Jamie and a trusted friend discussed the portfolio, job search, relationship
strategy, Commercial Rent Stabilization work, evidence standards, and the
public/private Knowledge Wiki architecture over brunch.

The conversation sharpened principles already visible in public work:

- a portfolio is an argument of fit, not an exhaustive archive;
- project selection should reveal a coherent professional constellation;
- hiring readers need plain language, concrete responsibility, and completed
  outcomes;
- trusted human champions and durable relationships matter alongside a strong
  website;
- the invisible operating frame must become legible without erasing the people
  and projects it supported; and
- current public claims should remain bounded by the evidence actually
  reviewed.

## Boundary

This record contains no transcript, private quotation, personal detail, contact
information, or attributed advice from the other participant. The private
conversation may inform Jamie's decisions; it is not a public testimonial,
endorsement, or independent proof of portfolio claims.
