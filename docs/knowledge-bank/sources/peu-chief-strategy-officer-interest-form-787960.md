# PEU Chief Strategy Officer Interest Form 787960

The public form identifies the employer as the NYC Public Engagement Unit, the
role as Chief Strategy Officer, the location as New York City, and the salary
as $120,000-$140,000. It says the work is expected to be in office three to
four days per week, with one to two remote days.

The form states that only candidates with direct supervisory experience will
be considered and says experience managing managers is especially valued. It
describes five or more years of relevant experience as a minimum and says the
successful candidate is likely to have at least ten years.

Besides contact information and a resume upload or online-resume URL, it asks
three required questions, each capped at 200 words:

1. Why are you a good fit for this role?
2. Briefly describe a project in which you used data to shape a program's
   goals or strategy.
3. Give facts and figures about management experience, including direct and
   indirect reports, whether managers were managed, years of management, and
   other relevant statistics.

The form was inspected read-only. No personal information was entered and no
response was submitted. Its relationship to the formal City Jobs and
SmartRecruiters application is not stated; it must not be represented as a
completed application, referral, or employer preference.
