# New York Small Business Rent Stabilization Act Legislative Records

The official records identify Senator Julia Salazar as sponsor of S8319 and
Assembly Member Emily Gallagher as sponsor of A5568A. The records identify the
bills as same-as measures in the 2025-2026 legislative session and preserve
their text, actions, and committee status as encountered on July 28, 2026.

These records establish sponsorship, bill text, and legislative history. They
do not establish that either sponsor delivered a particular oral statement,
that every listed cosponsor spoke publicly, or that the bills passed.
