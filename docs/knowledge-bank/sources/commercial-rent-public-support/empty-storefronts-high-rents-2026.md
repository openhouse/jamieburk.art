# Empty Storefronts, High Rents

[Empty Storefronts, High Rents: Commercial Vacancy and Rent Across New York
City](https://smallbizunited.com/reports/260728_SBU_FinalReport.pdf) is a July
2026 report by lead author and data analyst Mahin Rahman Tawrat, published by
Small Business United with Action Lab, Main Street Alliance, and Small Business
Majority.

The report publicly thanks staff of the two state bill sponsors for advice on
research design. It separately thanks Jamie Burkart, identified as an NYC
Artist Coalition member participating in Fair Rent NYC, for a thoughtful and
careful review of the draft. That establishes a credited review contribution;
it does not make Jamie the author, analyst, research designer, or owner of the
report's collective work.

## Bounded findings

The report says its Department of Finance registry analysis found citywide
commercial vacancy rising from 9.2% in 2020 to 11.9% in 2024 while median
registered rent declined from $9.14 to $8.55 per square foot. It reports a
district-level association between earlier registered rent and later vacancy.

The same source limits those findings:

- the registry is owner-reported and does not contain every commercial space;
- registered rent is not the same as asking, executed, or net-effective rent;
- district-level association cannot determine why an individual space became
  vacant; and
- the analysis does not establish landlord motive, financing constraints, or
  causation.

These limitations are part of the finding, not detachable fine print. The final
report shows the published boundary language but does not identify who proposed
any particular sentence during draft review.

## Contribution boundary

Protected correspondence supports the narrower statement that many of Jamie's
detailed review edits were incorporated and that he approved the acknowledgment
wording. The messages, draft markup, private document locators, and item-level
edit history are not published here. Public credit and private edit-integration
evidence remain distinct.

The source does not establish legislative passage, implementation, measured
policy effects, or the motive of any property owner.
