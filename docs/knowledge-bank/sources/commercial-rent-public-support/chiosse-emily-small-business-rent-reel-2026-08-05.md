# Chi Ossé and Emily Gallagher Small Business Rent Stabilization Reel

On August 5, 2026, Council Member Chi Ossé and Assembly Member Emily Gallagher
published a joint Reel advocating for the state Small Business Rent
Stabilization Act. The speakers connect commercial displacement with
neighborhood continuity, explain a proposed commercial rent guidelines board,
and ask viewers to contact state representatives about sponsoring the bill.

The [canonical archive source edition](https://github.com/openhouse/commercial-rent-stabilization-public-support/blob/ea5497dd910f3402c01e8b560b149d6674f951cc/sources/instagram/2026-08-05-chiosse-instagram-reel-DbqIqG8PoAQ/README.md)
preserves the 95.846-second media, public metadata, raw machine receipts,
failure evidence, and a corrected 32-turn diarized transcript. The archived

`cdef31ffe73e50f70a0d09b32b7863810d39c55bcde4b06c949cafa7595bee01`.

advocacy in the Reel. It does not establish that Jamie authored, appeared in,
or was endorsed by the Reel; support for a distinct city bill; legislative
