# NYC Artist Coalition Cultural Space Rent Stabilization Reel

On August 16, 2026, the NYC Artist Coalition published a 31-second Instagram
Reel using the same edited Wonderville audio as its August 15 Story. The Reel
retains the city-and-state call for Cultural Space Rent Stabilization and adds a
durable public caption thanking Fair Rent NYC's Assembly, Senate, and City
Council sponsors, linking the campaign solution, and inviting organizers to an
August 18 meetup.

The complete source edition, full caption, capture manifest, poster, contact
sheet, machine receipts, corrected diarized transcript, and media file are
maintained in the [Commercial Rent Stabilization public-support archive](https://github.com/openhouse/commercial-rent-stabilization-public-support/blob/e23822538dd6a7464503df6c585dcc680d0cd823/statements/campaigns/fair-rent-nyc/commercial-rent-stabilization/2026-08-16-nycartc-instagram-reel-dchbb6ix2pd/nyc-artist-coalition.md).
The archived media SHA-256 is
`91ad379f6edabeea1d83f6970e97917480b6aed5fa4a537de136fcea85107636`.
Decoding the Reel and Story audio to 48 kHz mono PCM produced the same SHA-256,
`bffd96151404c8d23bac2a5ba10dcc147a0a2215d98975ad17fe254f94fbaacd`.
This Knowledge Wiki record does not duplicate the transcript or media.

## Evidence boundary

The same decoded audio does not collapse the Story and Reel into one source:
they have different dates, URLs, platform IDs, visual treatments, captions, and
media-file checksums. Coalition-account publication does not establish that
Jamie solely authored, edited, or published the Reel. Sponsor acknowledgements
are coalition-account words, not newly recovered statements by each named
official. The invitation is not attendance, speech, consent, endorsement, or
proof of an event outcome.

Final human listening/approval remains separate. Media display remains
metadata-only until rights, consent, credit, and destination review are
complete. No protected August 18 organizer directory, audio, transcript, or
participant information is projected from this public record.
