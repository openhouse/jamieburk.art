# Empty Storefronts, High Rents

Small Business United, The Action Lab, Main Street Alliance, and Small
Business Majority released this twenty-page report in July 2026. Its central
descriptive finding is that high registered rents and high vacancy coexist in
the analyzed data. Its recommendations include the Small Business Rent
Stabilization Act.

The acknowledgements name Mahin Rahman Tawrat as lead author and data analyst,
identify other research and editorial contributors, and credit Jamie Burkart,
described as an NYC Artist Coalition member participating in Fair Rent NYC,
for a thoughtful and careful review of the draft report.

## Method boundary

The report says its coverage and owner-reported registration data have
limitations. It does not use its analysis to establish the cause of any
individual closure, landlord motive, financing behavior, or causation.

Those limitations matter to Jamie's credit. Protected provenance supports a
substantial pre-publication review concerned with factual accuracy, source and
methods clarity, causal scope, bill language, attribution, accessibility,
citations, and release hygiene. The public acknowledgement and private
incorporation record together support review credit. They do not support
authorship, methods ownership, independent replication, legal approval, a
contribution percentage, or a claim that every proposed edit was adopted.
