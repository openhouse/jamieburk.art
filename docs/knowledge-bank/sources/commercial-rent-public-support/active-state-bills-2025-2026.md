# Active New York State commercial rent stabilization bills

## S8319

- **Official title:** Enacts the "New York city small business rent stabilization act"
- **Sponsor:** State Senator Julia Salazar
- **Co-sponsors recorded on review:** Jabari Brisport, Kristen Gonzalez, and Gustavo Rivera
- **Introduced:** 2025-06-02
- **Current committee recorded on review:** Senate Cities 1
- **Latest recorded action:** Referred to Cities 1 on 2026-01-07
- **Official record:** [New York State Senate](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S8319)

The official page includes the bill text and sponsor memo. The memo is an
official justificatory document, not a speech.

## A5568A

- **Official title:** Enacts the "New York city small business rent stabilization act"
- **Sponsor:** Assembly Member Emily Gallagher
- **Introduced:** 2025-02-14
- **Amended and recommitted:** 2025-05-27
- **Current committee recorded on review:** Assembly Cities
- **Latest recorded action:** Referred to Cities on 2026-01-07
- **Official record:** [New York State Senate legislation portal](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/A5568/amendment/A)

## Search finding

On 2026-07-28, a bounded review of the official bill pages, action histories,
and the New York State Senate newsroom recovered the sponsor memo and two
official statement releases. It did not recover an Assembly or Senate floor
speech on either bill. This is a null finding within the declared official
population, not proof that no supportive statement exists elsewhere.
