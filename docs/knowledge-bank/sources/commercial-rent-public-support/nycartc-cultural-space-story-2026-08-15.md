# NYC Artist Coalition Cultural Space Rent Stabilization Story

On August 15, 2026, the NYC Artist Coalition published a 31-second Instagram
Story using an edited excerpt of Jamie Burkart's May 19 Wonderville remarks.
The Story connects cultural-space continuity and collective agency to a call
for commercial-rent action by both Albany and the New York City Council.

The complete source edition, capture manifest, poster, contact sheet, machine
receipts, corrected diarized transcript, and media file are maintained in the
[Commercial Rent Stabilization public-support archive](https://github.com/openhouse/commercial-rent-stabilization-public-support/blob/ea5497dd910f3402c01e8b560b149d6674f951cc/statements/campaigns/fair-rent-nyc/commercial-rent-stabilization/2026-08-15-nycartc-instagram-story-3964470891412306511/nyc-artist-coalition.md).
The archived media SHA-256 is
`24808b127cd7af7bf0e804db0e27ec59b82d57d96ebf62a2f1e617ed6845caef`.
This Knowledge Wiki record does not duplicate the transcript or media.

## Evidence boundary

The Story establishes publication by the coalition account and words
attributed to Jamie. It does not establish that Jamie solely authored, edited,
or published the Story; that tagged accounts or sponsor acknowledgements are
endorsements; that every named person supports every proposal; or that the
publication caused legislative action.

Local ASR, animated Story captions, Jamie's supplied cut, and the source-event
transcript were reconciled into a corrected diarized transcript, restoring the
interleaved Joe Ahearn, Stephanie Gross, and room turns. Final human
listening/approval remains separate. Media display remains metadata-only until
rights, consent, credit, and destination review are complete.
