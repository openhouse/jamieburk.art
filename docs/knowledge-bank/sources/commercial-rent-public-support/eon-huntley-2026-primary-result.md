# 2026 Democratic primary election-night result for Assembly District 56

The New York State Board of Elections' **Unofficial Election Night Results**
page recorded 70 of 70 election districts reporting in the Democratic primary
for Assembly District 56:

| Candidate | Votes | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Eon Huntley | 8,438 | 57.12% |
| Stefani L. Zinerman | 5,320 | 36.01% |
| Michael Bailey | 697 | 4.72% |

This supports describing Huntley on 2026-07-28 as the apparent Democratic
primary winner in the complete election-night tally. It does not support
calling him an Assembly Member before the general election, certification,
and assumption of office.

Official source: [New York State Board of Elections](https://nyenr.elections.ny.gov/HomeNoJS.aspx)
