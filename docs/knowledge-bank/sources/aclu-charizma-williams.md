# ACLU official biography - Charizma Williams

The ACLU identifies Charizma Williams as Chief Operating Officer. Its biography
places business operations, accessibility, information technology and security,
enterprise risk, finance, human resources, and facilities in her portfolio.

This supports a senior vision context because the job is expressly located in
the Office of the COO. It does not establish that Williams directly supervises
the Senior Project Manager or participates in this hiring process.

See the [official biography](https://www.aclu.org/bios/charizma-williams).
