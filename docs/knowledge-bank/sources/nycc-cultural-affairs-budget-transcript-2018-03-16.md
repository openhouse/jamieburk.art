# NYC Council Cultural Affairs Budget Transcript

The public Council transcript supplies attributed turns and page locators for
the March 16, 2018 hearing. The normalized corpus includes speakers addressing
DCLA funding, CreateNYC, artist support, and closely related cultural
infrastructure; library testimony remains in the census but outside that
full-text selection.
