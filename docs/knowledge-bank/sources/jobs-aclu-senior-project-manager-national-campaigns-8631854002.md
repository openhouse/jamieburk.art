# ACLU - Senior Project Manager, National Campaigns 8631854002

The official posting describes a full-time, three-year, term-limited Senior
Project Manager role in the ACLU National Political Advocacy Department. It is
hybrid in New York or Washington, DC, reports to the Senior Director of
National Campaigns, and lists a New York salary of $128,294.

The work covers complex project delivery, schedules and metrics, collaboration
systems, internal communications, research, talking points, stakeholder
coordination, budget administration, and big-picture campaign awareness. The
posting names the manager's title but not a person.

The [official source](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/aclu/jobs/8631854002)
remained application-enabled when rechecked. A separate official leadership
source is required before naming any person in the reporting or vision chain.
