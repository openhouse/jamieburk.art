# NYC Artist Coalition Public-Meeting Production Playbook

A protected coalition playbook lays out a three-week workflow across partner,
participant, elected-official, testimony, press, web, print, social, video, and
day-of work. This synopsis records the source's evidentiary value without
publishing its body or private coordinates.

It supports a collective operating design. It does not establish who first
authored the document, Jamie's responsibility for every task, execution of
every step at every event, attendance, or policy impact. Revision-history and
collaborator review remain open.

See the [public-meeting operating-system claim](../claims/nycartc-public-meeting-operating-system.md).
