# CodePath team and senior leadership - August 2026

CodePath's official team page identifies Quinton Ma as Director of Business
Operations and Zack Parker as Vice President of Engineering. Matching those
current title-holders to the reporting titles in the two job postings is a
high-confidence public inference, not the same as either posting naming its
hiring manager.

The page also identifies Brian Madigan as Chief Operating Officer and Chris
Coleman as Chief Product Officer. CodePath's official announcements say
[Madigan oversees business operations and organizational scalability](https://www.codepath.org/news/codepath-announces-brian-madigan-as-new-chief-operating-officer)
and [Coleman leads its product engineering delivery team](https://www.codepath.org/news/codepath-announces-chris-coleman-as-new-chief-product-officer).
Those public responsibilities support
using Madigan as the nearest senior vision owner for AI operations and Coleman
as the nearest senior vision owner for engineering delivery. They do not
establish interview participation or private reporting details.
