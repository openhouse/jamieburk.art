# NYC Artist Coalition Cabaret Law Testimony Guide

A protected 2017 coalition guide helps participants prepare short first-person
Cabaret Law testimony. It tells participants that hearing time is brief, centers
true lived experience, and provides an alternate submission route for people
unable to testify in person.

The native Google Doc was re-opened on July 19, 2026. That encounter supports
the guide's format and alternate participation path. It does not establish who
originally authored the guide, how many people used it, whose testimony was
accepted, or what legislative outcome any testimony caused.

The source body and contact details remain outside the repository. See the
[participation method](../methods/nycartc-public-meeting-and-testimony-participation.md).
