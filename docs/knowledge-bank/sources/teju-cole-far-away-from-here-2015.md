# Teju Cole's Zurich residency and *Far Away From Here*

This note grounds a story Jamie often tells artists at 196. The core movement is
supported, with one useful qualification.

Cole was the Literaturhaus Zurich and PWG Foundation writer in residence from
June through November 2014. He arrived with a nonfiction manuscript about Lagos
and expected the quiet of Zurich to support that writing. In a later annotated
interview about his essay *Far Away From Here*, Cole said that he did some Lagos
and other writing, while the majority of his time went into traveling around
Switzerland and making photographs. The longer photographic inquiry developed
across 2014-2019 into *Fernweh*.

## Public sources reviewed

- [Teju Cole on *Far Away From Here*](https://niemanstoryboard.org/annotation-tuesday-teju-cole-on-far-away-from-here/),
  Nieman Storyboard's annotated interview with Cole.
- [Teju Cole, writer in residence](https://www.writer-in-residence.ch/author/teju_cole/),
  the residency program's record of his June-November 2014 stay in Zurich.
- [*Fernweh*](https://www.tejucole.com/fernweh), Cole's official project page,
  which dates the Swiss photographic work to 2014-2019.
- [*Far Away From Here*](https://www.nytimes.com/2015/09/27/magazine/far-away-from-here.html),
  the September 2015 New York Times Magazine essay named by the interview.

## Supported use

This source can support a bounded account of a residency in which close
attention to an encountered place changed the center of an intended writing
period. It supports the principle that a proposal can establish care and time
without dictating the form that attention must take.

## Boundary

Do not sharpen the story into "Cole went to write and wrote nothing." His own
account says he wrote some. Do not present Jamie's retelling as a quotation or
claim that *Fernweh* was completed during the six-month residency. The official
project page describes photographs made over five years.
