# NYC Mayor's Office - Lisa Gelobter appointment

The Mayor's Office identifies Lisa Gelobter as New York City Chief Technology
Officer and Commissioner of the Office of Technology and Innovation. It says
she oversees city technology infrastructure, cybersecurity, and data-management
initiatives and frames her direction around public-good technology, service
delivery, accountability, digital equity, and the needs of New Yorkers.

This source establishes senior agency leadership and public vision. It does not
identify the direct manager for Job 782369, establish participation in that
hiring decision, or express a private opinion about Jamie.

See the [official appointment announcement](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/02/mayor-mamdani-announces-key-appointments-to-three-city-agencies-).
