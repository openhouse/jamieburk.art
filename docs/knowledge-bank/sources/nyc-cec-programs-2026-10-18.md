# NYC Civic Engagement Commission - operating programs

## Program sources observed October 18, 2026

- [TRIE Neighborhood Initiative](https://www.nyc.gov/site/civicengagement/our-programs/trie-initiative.page): CEC runs coalition and capacity-building work with the Mayor's Office of Equity and Young Men's Initiative. Community-based Neighborhood Administrators build local networks and participation. The linked partner directory and deliverables are labeled 2025–2026, not FY27.
- [Community Boards](https://home4.nyc.gov/site/civicengagement/our-programs/community-boards.page): CEC provides assistance and training across all 59 boards. The archive identifies NYC Office of Data Analytics Open Data workshops on June 12, 24 and 26, 2026, using Open Data Ambassadors curriculum; and BetaNYC AI training on April 21, 23 and 28, 2026. Benjamin Solotaire is the named program contact.
- [CEC homepage](https://www.nyc.gov/site/civicengagement/index.page): identifies The People's Money, Voter Language Assistance and community-board assistance as core activities.
- [Volunteer page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/civicengagement/get-involved/volunteer.page): describes volunteer pathways for civic-engagement training, participatory budgeting and language assistance.

## Limits

Training dates establish historical offerings, not Jamie's attendance or a present invitation. Program ownership does not identify a recruiter. Page schedules and directories need a fresh check before scheduling or outreach.
