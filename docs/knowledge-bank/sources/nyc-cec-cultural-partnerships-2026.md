# CEC cultural-institution partnerships, 2026

The [CEC Cultural Institutions Group page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/civicengagement/about/cultural-institutions-group.page), observed October 18, 2026, describes a year-long collaboration between CEC, The People's Creative Institute, and five cultural institutions: BRIC, Bronx Museum of the Arts, El Museo del Barrio, Flushing Town Hall, and Snug Harbor Cultural Center & Botanical Garden. They cross-promote The People's Money and cultural programming.

The [May 19, 2026 CEC newsletter](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/civicengagement/newsletters/NYCCivicEngagement_2026_05_19.html) independently dates the announcement of the five institutions as participation and engagement hubs during that voting phase.

The program page has no publication date and does not specify a precise start/end date for its year-long term. Do not infer that every activity continues unchanged in October. These institutional links do not establish contacts, endorsements, referrals or participation by Jamie.
