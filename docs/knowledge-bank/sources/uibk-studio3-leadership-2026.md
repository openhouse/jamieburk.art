# Official University of Innsbruck and ./studio3 leadership context

The University of Innsbruck identifies Kathrin Aste as head of the Institute
of Experimental Architecture. Its institute page identifies the ./studio3
artistic-design working group with Kathrin Aste and Thomas Edlinger. The
./studio3 team page describes Aste as `Head of Institute` and Edlinger in
`Künstlerische Diskurse` (artistic discourse).

These pages establish public institutional and intellectual context. They do
not establish the postdoc's direct reporting line, either person's role in the
selection process, a private view of Jamie, or an endorsement.
