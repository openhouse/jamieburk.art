# Jamie Burkart testimony on small-business open data, March 18, 2019

## Official transcript text

The text below is reflowed from committee transcript pages 142-144. Wording,
speaker label, spelling, and apparent transcription errors are preserved.

> **MR. JAMIE BURKHART:** Hello. I’m Jamie Burkhart. I’m a member of the New
> York City Artist Coalition. We advocate for the safety and preservation of New
> York City Small Diverse Neighborhood Cultural Spaces. We strongly support the
> introductions discussed at today’s hearing.
>
> Cultural spaces our small businesses. Cultural spaces closed due to rent,
> harassment, and eviction, cultural spaces strive to be in regulatory
> compliance. So I wanted to focus in and offer some specific recommendations
> to expand Intro 1466 and 1467. Both require reporting on the city’s laws and
> practices related to small business, and both have the potential to feel a
> critical information gap that would help many small businesses more easily
> discover and navigate the path to legality, safety and compliance.
>
> As a member of the Artist Coalition, I meet frequently with cultural space
> operators who strive to create and sustain small businesses with limited
> means. I meet with city agency staff and volunteer defacto community
> caseworkers who help spaces navigate licenses, permits, certificate
> inspections in pursuit of compliance. A comment sentiment is the city can
> provide a clear unified way to understand the path to legality, a map of the
> process for navigating the cities many agencies and processes and answer to
> the questions every small business must face.
>
> So we can expand 1466 and 1467 to create data sets on the city’s open data
> portal and that we and city agencies can build off to create online tools for
> New York City small businesses to discover and navigate the path to legality
> and compliance. SBS has a step-by-step business wizard website that asks
> operators questions --
>
> **CHAIRPERSON GJONAJ:** Thirty seconds.
>
> **MR. JAMIE BURKHART:** -- brings forward more than 40 permits and
> certificates. Included here is a list of questions that I’d like to be rows
> and columns in a data set on the open portal that have to do with, like how
> does this take and what are the requirements and how much does it cost?

## Editorial reading notes

- The official transcript labels Jamie “Burkhart”; his name is Jamie Burkart.
- Apparent errors such as “our small businesses,” “feel a critical information
  gap,” “comment sentiment,” and “cities many agencies” remain uncorrected in
  the transcript block.
- The text establishes the proposal Jamie made, not enactment or adoption.

## Boundary

This record supports a bounded professional claim: Jamie publicly translated
small-business compliance experience into a concrete open-data product
proposal. It does not establish that the Council or an agency implemented the
proposal.
