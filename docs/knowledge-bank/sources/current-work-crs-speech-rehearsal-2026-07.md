# Commercial-rent speech rehearsal, late July 2026

## Orientation

The protected transcript records Jamie rehearsing a two-minute public argument
at the intersection of cultural-space continuity, commercial-rent
stabilization, storefront vacancy, and privacy-aware open data.

The draft explores a request for geographically aggregated commercial-rent and
vacancy indicators on the NYC Open Data Portal, including responsible use of
Real Property Income and Expense information. It also tries to close with
specific actions available to elected officials, agencies, journalists,
advocates, and members of the public.

## Boundary

This was an internal rehearsal, not delivered public testimony. It had not
completed factual, legal, coalition, source, timing, or speaker approval when
reviewed. The full draft and its unverified claims remain outside public Git.
Any later delivered speech requires a separate source record.
