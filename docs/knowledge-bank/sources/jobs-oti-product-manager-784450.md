# NYC Jobs - Product Manager, Job ID 784450

The official posting lists one full-time Product Manager position in OTI's
Product Management department at 2 MetroTech. The salary range is
$75,000-$150,000, and the visible application deadline is September 4, 2026.

The role manages public digital products from conception through launch. It
asks for research and analytics synthesis, roadmaps, user-problem validation,
prioritization, outcome and KPI definition, user stories, acceptance criteria,
system requirements, and plain-language collaboration across program, UX,
engineering, and agency stakeholders.

The civil-service title is IT Project Specialist. The minimum qualifications
offer multiple education-and-experience routes, including a bachelor's degree
plus eight years of relevant full-time experience. NYC, not this record, must
decide whether Jamie's chronology satisfies that standard. The preferred
section asks for ten or more years in product work and end-to-end SDLC practice.
