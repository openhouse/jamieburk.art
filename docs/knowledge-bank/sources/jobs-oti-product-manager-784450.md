# NYC Jobs - Product Manager, Job ID 784450

The official posting identifies one full-time NYC Office of Technology and
Innovation Product Manager position at 2 MetroTech under the IT Project
Specialist civil-service title. It lists a $75,000-$150,000 salary range, a July
6, 2026 posting date, and a September 4, 2026 application deadline.

The work covers the complete product cycle: qualitative and quantitative
research, roadmaps, KPIs, prioritization, product requirements, user stories,
acceptance criteria, system requirements, launch, maintenance, and
cross-agency stakeholder collaboration. The posting asks for ten or more years
in product roles and data-informed product decisions, while its civil-service
minimum qualifications allow several education-and-experience paths.
