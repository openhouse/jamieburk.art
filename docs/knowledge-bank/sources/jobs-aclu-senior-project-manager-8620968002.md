# Official posting - ACLU Senior Project Manager

The posting describes a New York hybrid, two-year term role on the Learning &
Project Solutions team in Business Operations and Support Services, within the
Office of the Chief Operating Officer. It gives a fixed New York salary of
$128,294 and says the role reports to the Senior Director, Learning and Project
Solutions.

The posting does not name that Senior Director. It asks for complex systems and
process implementation, cross-department project strategy, business analysis,
budget and resource management, risk resolution, communications, closeout, and
organizational project-management standards. A writing sample is required.
