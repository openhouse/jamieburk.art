# NYC Jobs - Associate Director of Policy Implementation 792925

The official external posting describes one full-time NYC Campaign Finance
Board Associate Director of Policy Implementation, using the Executive Program
Manager civil-service title, at $125,000-$135,000. The application deadline is
October 13, 2026. A resume and cover letter are required.

The role leads a Policy Implementation team and reports to the Director of
Policy & Research. It manages the full policy-development and implementation
lifecycle; creates governance, intake, prioritization, documentation, review,
and publication systems; facilitates agencywide working groups; coordinates
implementation across divisions; develops project tools and templates;
supports testimony and reports; evaluates outcomes; and supervises Policy
Analysts.

The posting asks for five or more years in public policy, policy analysis,
research, program evaluation, or cross-functional project management,
including work assessing proposed policies or evaluating existing policies and
programs. It also asks for at least three years supervising staff. Prior
elections, ethics, campaign-finance, government-agency, elected-office, or
campaign experience is preferred rather than essential.

The civil-service minimum allows specified education plus experience, a high-
school diploma plus five years of related experience, or an equivalent
combination. The employer controls interpretation of Jamie's experience,
supervisory history, education, and eligibility.
