# Benepass leadership context - Jaclyn Chen

Benepass identifies Jaclyn Chen as co-founder and CEO. Its public leadership
material frames the company around simplifying and modernizing benefits while
giving employers and employees more flexible, usable infrastructure. That is
the nearest publicly stated company-level direction for the Product Operations
Manager role.
