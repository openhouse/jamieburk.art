# Action Lab Commercial Rent Stabilization campaign post

An authenticated public-post review on August 12 confirmed that Action Lab had
published its Commercial Rent Stabilization campaign carousel. The visible
collaborator list named Action Lab, Assembly Member Emily Gallagher, YANT Art
Space, Main Street Alliance Action Fund, and State Senator Julia Salazar.

NYC Artist Coalition was not visible in that collaborator list at capture time,
even though protected correspondence separately records an invitation and
Jamie's acceptance from the coalition account. The two sources therefore
establish three different states: invitation, acceptance, and the later visible
platform credit. They do not establish why those states differ.

## Boundaries

- The live post establishes Action Lab's campaign publication, not press
  coverage of Jamie or proof of Jamie's July 29 speaking role.
- The post does not identify Jamie as author, subject, organizer, or source.
- An accepted collaborator invitation does not establish completed platform
  credit, and a missing collaborator label does not establish fault or intent.
- The post's causal policy rhetoric is not independent proof of landlord motive
  or individual closure cause.
- Public visibility does not clear the carousel images for copying into this
  repository. No image is retained here.
