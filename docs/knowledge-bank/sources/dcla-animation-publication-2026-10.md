## What the reel shows

The downloaded platform copy is 30 seconds long, in a vertical format. Review sampled one frame per second across the full duration and inspected a final response frame at full resolution. This is a visual and technical review, not a continuous playback or auditory review.

The visible sequence moves from the value of cultural space to artists' requests: senior studio space, accountability, and cultural-space rent stabilization. The ending acknowledges the accountability request and names vacant storefronts, commercial rent stabilization, and public schools as topics for conversation.

That ending matters: the edited artifact includes a response, not only requests. It does not establish a policy commitment, adoption, consensus, or enactment. The sampled photographs stay with the listening session; they do not establish the wider coalition-history sequence proposed in earlier drafts.

These observations describe on-screen captions. They do not certify the spoken words, identify speakers from faces, or establish that each caption belongs to the person pictured beside it. The separate full-transcript inquiry remains open.

## Attribution and reuse boundaries

The supplied collaborator-panel screenshot lists 651 ARTS, NYC Department of Cultural Affairs, Brooklyn Arts Council, Lin Tyrpien, Jamie Burkart, and NYC Artist Coalition accounts. That is platform collaboration context, not proof of attendance, an institutional endorsement, or permission to reuse all components.

The public article credit is not a professional performance testimonial. View and reaction counts are not treated as evidence of impact. No private correspondence, raw article body, signed media URL, or downloaded media is committed here.

The downloaded copy is a platform derivative, not the editing master or original session recording. Its SHA-256 is `bc97a92809597e9a02bf72f443456c582ce31c60dbb4537b204ecba9790d530a`; its size is 4,363,226 bytes. The file contains 1080 × 1920 video at 30 frames per second and a stereo audio track. These measurements support identification, not rights clearance or audio fidelity.
