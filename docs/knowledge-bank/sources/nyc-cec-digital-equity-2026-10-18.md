# CEC Digital Equity Team - dated public evidence

## Dated evidence, not a current system audit

The indexed text of the [2025 annual report](https://home4.nyc.gov/assets/civicengagement/downloads/pdf/CEC-2025-Annual-Report-Final.pdf) describes the Digital Equity Team's creation in fall 2024, leadership by CIO Oscar Romero, and cross-program technology, design, communications and analysis work. It reports an upgrade to Decidim and City hosting for participate.nyc.gov. Those are **2025 report facts**; the complete report was not reviewed page by page.

[Sonia Putzel's official biography](https://www.nyc.gov/site/civicengagement/about/bios/sonia-putzel.page), read October 18, 2026, identifies her as Technology Strategist on the Digital Equity Team, working on online participation and civic-engagement tools with attention to accessibility and fairness.

The [November 17, 2025 CEC newsletter](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/civicengagement/newsletters/NYCCivicEngagement_2025_11-17.html) describes the use of Decidim for The People's Money and an institutional collaboration with other public-sector Decidim users. No version, current security state, or complete technical architecture was verified in this intake.
