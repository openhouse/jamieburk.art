# NYC Jobs - Technical Operations Manager 782369

The official posting identifies a full-time, experienced non-manager role in
OTI's Commissioner's Office at 15 MetroTech, with one position, a posted salary
range of $75,000 to $160,000, and an August 7, 2026 deadline. On August 13, the
official page explicitly said the vacancy had expired. It describes ownership
of hiring, planning, delivery tracking, onboarding, leadership reporting,
decisions, operating documentation, risk, and continuous improvement.

The posting says the role would report up to OTI leadership but does not name a
supervisor or hiring manager. Preserve the [official source](https://cityjobs.nyc.gov/job/technical-operations-manager-in-brooklyn-jid-44321)
as a historical benchmark; never route it as a live application.
