# NYC Artist Coalition DCLA formation materials, January-February 2017

## Dated sequence

- On January 26, 2017, Jamie wrote from a WOW List address that he had found
  DCLA's post-Ghost-Ship gathering, wanted DIY projects represented, and had
  created a public event to provide context and invite participation.
- A dated January 27 two-person ranking sheet records Jamie and Julia comparing
  six possible coalition names. It preserves a collaborative naming process,
  not a sole-author claim.
- Preserved public-site records show contact and document surfaces created on
  January 27, followed by a February 3 post that embedded the interactive
  `Path to Being Legal` visualization.
- On February 4, Jamie followed up from a coalition address after the packed
  meeting, inviting participation in a general meeting and linking the public
  letter, notes, and visualization.
- February 2017 reporting reproduced a detail from the visualization, credited
  the image to NYC Artist Coalition, and identified Jamie as a coalition
  organizer.

Together, these materials support a bounded account of connective
infrastructure: discovery and mobilization through WOW List, collaborative
naming, a public coalition identity and web surface, close-listening artifacts,
and a pathway into continued meetings. They do not establish that Jamie hosted
the official DCLA event, acted alone, authored every artifact, or caused the
coalition to form by himself.
