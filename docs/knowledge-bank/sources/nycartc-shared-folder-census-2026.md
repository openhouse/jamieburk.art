# NYC Artist Coalition Shared-Folder Governed Census

An authenticated July 2026 traversal accounts for 2,192 reachable descendants:
257 nested folders and 1,935 files. Every descendant received one protected
primary disposition; 18 initially exceptional folders were separately
confirmed empty, and no unresolved traversal errors remained.

The exact identifiers, paths, filenames, owners, and item-level dispositions
remain in a protected manifest outside git. The census establishes population
closure for the named snapshot. It does not establish authorship, rights,
content-level review of every file, or access to deleted and unshared material.

See the [archival-production report](../research/nycartc-shared-folder-archival-production-2026-07.md).
