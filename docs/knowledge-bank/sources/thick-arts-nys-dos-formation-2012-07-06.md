# New York State formation record for THICK ARTS LLC

An exact-name query of the New York State Department of State's official
[Active Corporations dataset](https://data.ny.gov/resource/n9v6-gdp6.json?$select=dos_id,current_entity_name,initial_dos_filing_date,county,jurisdiction,entity_type&$where=upper(current_entity_name)%20=%20%22THICK%20ARTS%20LLC%22)
returns one record:

- current entity name: THICK ARTS LLC;
- DOS ID: 4267566;
- initial DOS filing date: July 6, 2012;
- jurisdiction: New York;
- entity type: domestic limited liability company.

## Interpretation

Jamie states that his work with Harry J. Epstein Company began in 2009. During
that engagement, he formalized the practice as Thick Arts LLC, with HJE as the
LLC's first client. The government record independently verifies the company
formation date; the work sequence and first-client identity remain Jamie's
authorized first-party professional account.

## Boundary

The formation date does not move the underlying work's beginning from 2009 to
2012 and does not establish exact contract, invoice, payment, or client-end
dates. New York State describes this open dataset as general-public information,
not certified documentation for legal proceedings.
