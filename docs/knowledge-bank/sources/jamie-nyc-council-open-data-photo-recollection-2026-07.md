# Jamie's NYC Council and open-data photograph recollection, July 2026

On July 26, 2026, Jamie recorded a first-person oral history while looking at a
supplied portrait of himself at a New York City Council setting. A
best-effort repaired working transcript was reviewed together with the image.
The transcript is explicitly not audio-verified or fact-checked.

## Public-safe recollection

Jamie identifies the setting as the upper floors of 250 Broadway. He recalls
carrying NYC Artist Coalition materials, including a member sign-in form he
designed to offer graduated choices about email, public visibility, shared
folder access, and mutual-aid participation.

Jamie recalls attending a Council data-team session associated with BetaNYC's
2026 NYC School of Data and discussing a proposal for privacy-preserving,
geographically aggregated commercial-rent and vacancy data derived from Real
Property Income and Expense filings. He connects the day to an earlier School
of Data encounter involving CallNYC.

These are useful leads about Jamie's practice: translating participation into
forms, carrying operational materials into public institutions, and connecting
community-facing work with public-data proposals.

## Source boundaries

This is dated first-person memory, not independent corroboration. The portrait
supports only what is visibly present. It does not establish the event's exact
date or title, the proposal's contents or reception, the people Jamie spoke
with, the origin of the participation-form model, the photographer's identity,
or publication permission.

This source concerns the supplied 250 Broadway portrait. It does not answer or
clear the separate held CallNYC interface-in-use photograph showing a hand and
phone.

The repaired transcript is retained as protected working evidence. Private
archive locations, audio, correspondence, draft follow-ups, and source files
remain outside public Git.
