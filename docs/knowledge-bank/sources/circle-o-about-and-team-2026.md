# Circle O About and Team

Circle O's official pages describe a living web shaped by dance and shared
learning, with a focus on changing conditions so that Black Disabled and
multiply marginalized artists can inhabit their bodies with freedom, care and
imagination. Its work connects education, consulting, performance and global
exchange.

The official pages identify Kayla Hamilton as Founder and Artistic Director.
They do not establish any Jamie-specific relationship, contact, endorsement or
recommendation.
