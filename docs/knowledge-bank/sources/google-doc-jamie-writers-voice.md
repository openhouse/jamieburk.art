## Application-letter profile

For a role-specific cover letter, the source currently supports this bounded
editorial profile:

- Begin with one concrete encounter, person, object, place, or practical
  problem.
- Move from that encounter to the human stake and the hidden operating system.
- Make one precise hinge distinction, then end with a usable possibility.
- Write as a witness-participant: inside the work, attentive to where personal
  authority ends, and proportionate about collective outcomes.
- Keep the tone warm, precise, curious, protective, hopeful without naivete,
  and lyrical only after the concrete world has been established.
- Use one governing question and no more than one governing metaphor.
- Cut repeated intention and self-justification. Let the quality and specificity
  of the work make the argument.
- Preserve source from inference, access from consent, individual action from
  collective result, and personal support from institutional endorsement.
- Treat operations, documentation, attribution, review, and handoff as forms
  that allow care and knowledge to endure.
