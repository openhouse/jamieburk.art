# The Kansas City Star raft report, November 15, 2007

## Source identity

The contemporary report describes Jamie Burkart, Libby Hendon, and Laura
Mattingly traveling more than 1,000 miles from Kansas City into Louisiana on a
raft built largely from reclaimed materials and propelled by a bicycle-linked
paddle wheel when steering or additional power was needed.

## What it supports

- A collective, in-progress river journey in November 2007.
- Jamie's stated interest in waterways as connections among places and people.
- Material improvisation, public encounters, field operations, interruption,
  adaptation, and continued travel.

## What it does not establish

- The expedition's final endpoint or complete route.
- A complete crew or collaborator roster.
- Sole authorship, a complete division of labor, or measured civic impact.
- Permission to reproduce the article text or photographs.

The article remains an in-progress snapshot, not a completion certificate.
