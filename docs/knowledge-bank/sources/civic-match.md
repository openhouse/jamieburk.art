# Civic Match candidate network and job board

Civic Match is Work for America's free talent network for state and local
government. It is more than a resume bank: a candidate maintains one profile,
can search and save opportunities, and may be found or invited by participating
government employers. Work for America also describes human talent matching,
virtual events, and job-search resources.

This source complements the machine-readable NYC Jobs feed. Its distinctive
value is profile-mediated discovery and relationship support across
jurisdictions, not bulk row ingestion. Profile state, job-board observations,
and individual posting requirements therefore have separate review clocks.

## Affordances

- a maintained candidate profile and resume;
- government-employer search and invitations;
- candidate search, saved opportunities, and application-status updates;
- human matching by Civic Match staff;
- public-service career events and resources.

## Boundaries

- Work for America-only intake answers are not employer-visible profile fields.
- Contact, protected eligibility, visibility, terms, and submission choices
  remain Jamie-controlled external actions.
- State and local roles commonly have jurisdiction-specific residency and other
  eligibility requirements; network membership does not establish eligibility.
- A synthetic reader result is not an actual view, invitation, interview,
  endorsement, offer, or hire.
