# Austrian Federal Government - Mobility of EU citizens within the EU

Austria's migration portal says EEA and Swiss citizens enjoy free movement of
workers and may take employment in Austria under the same labour-market-access
conditions as Austrian citizens. It separately notes residence-registration
duties. Austria's public-administration guidance says an EU citizen staying
longer than three months under EU residence rights must request a registration
certificate within four months of arrival; taking accommodation can trigger a
separate address-registration duty.

This guidance supports treating Austria as a potentially eligible search
geography. It does not make a particular employer, remote-work arrangement, or
length of stay compliant without further review.

Recheck the [official mobility source](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/types-of-immigration/mobility-within-the-eu/)
and [official residence guidance](https://eausweise.oesterreich.gv.at/en/themen/menschen_aus_anderen_staaten/aufenthalt/4/Seite.120210)
before acting.
