# BetaNYC weekly civic-technology newsletter and public archive

BetaNYC publishes a recurring civic-technology newsletter with jobs and
opportunities alongside community events, public-service news, tools, and
media. The August 20, 2026 public issue contained nineteen opportunity leads.
The public archive and feed allow routine review without retaining private
mailbox identifiers, account locators, tracking tokens, or message state.

This source has editorial affordances that differ from the official NYC Jobs
dataset. It can surface civic employers outside City government, reveal events
where teams and candidates meet, and supply context about emerging public
service needs. It is not an authority for whether a role remains open or whether
Jamie qualifies. The official employer posting controls those facts.

The maintained adapter checks the latest public issue date, extracts only the
Jobs and Opportunities section, resolves official URLs, joins City roles to the
official City dataset when possible, and sends only verified strong matches to
provisional intake. External-employer facts use expiring, dated official reviews;
an expired or unmodeled review returns to explicit verification rather than
silently retaining an old result.
