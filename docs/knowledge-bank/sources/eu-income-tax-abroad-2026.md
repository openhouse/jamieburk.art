# Your Europe - Income taxes abroad

Your Europe says there are no EU-wide rules determining how every
cross-border worker is taxed. National definitions and tax treaties matter;
more than six months in a country commonly affects tax residence, while a
permanent home and stronger personal or economic ties can complicate the
result.

This source is a screening caution, not individualized tax advice.

Recheck the [official source](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/work/taxes/income-taxes-abroad/index_en.htm)
and obtain qualified advice for a real arrangement.
