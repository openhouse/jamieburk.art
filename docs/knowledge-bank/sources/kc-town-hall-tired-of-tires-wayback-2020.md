# Tired of Tires public service page, archived October 30, 2020

## What the capture establishes

The archived KC Town Hall page presents Tired of Tires as a recurring free
service offered with Oak Park Neighborhood Association for homes in historic
East Kansas City neighborhoods. It explains residential eligibility, a
Friday-before-first-Saturday cadence, form and phone intake, a volunteer
pathway, neighborhood collection, and a City recycling handoff. The page uses
the shared byline “Julia and Jamie.”

The page also reports that the program had saved residents $17,768 in disposal
costs by the archived revision. That is preserved as first-party program
reporting, not an independent audit.

## What it does not establish

The shared page byline does not identify who designed each graphic, form,
workflow, route, or operating decision. The page does not prove that Jamie
alone created or operated the service, document every shift, or independently
audit tire totals, households served, coverage, savings, or resident outcomes.

Contact information, embedded form identifiers, and implementation details are
not reproduced in this repository. The public capture is evidence; it is not
permission to republish resident reports or private service data.
