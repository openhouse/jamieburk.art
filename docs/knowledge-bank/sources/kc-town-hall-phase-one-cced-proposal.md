# KC Town Hall Phase One CCED proposal appraisal

Reviewed: 2026-07-15

Source: KC Town Hall LLC, Central City Economic Development proposal and
support letters, 24-page packet, March 2019.

Repository treatment: public metadata and page-level findings only. The source
packet remains outside this public repository because appended materials contain
financial and contact information.

## What the source establishes

- The project-team page identifies Julia Fredenburg and Jamie Burkart as
  founders and project managers.
- The same page identifies distinct historic-masonry, architecture, roofing,
  concrete, electrical, and legal contributors.
- The neighborhood-process page documents a survey conducted with Oak Park
  Neighborhood Association and New Horizon Missionary Baptist Church and says
  that ongoing survey results directly shaped the proposal.
- The finance section defines Phase One as a cold-shell scope involving roof,
  structural-masonry, and floor-framing work.
- The Phase One budget detail labels the cold-shell phase completed in 2019 and
  separates costs completed in 2018 from those completed in 2019.
- A support letter describes Jamie and Julia as invested in a listening-driven
  process intended to inform renovation and center surrounding-neighborhood
  assets and needs.

## What the source does not establish

- It does not use the title general contractor for Jamie.
- It does not assign every hiring, supervision, sequencing, or trade-management
  action to Jamie.
- It does not identify the individual designer of the survey card or its
  backing data system.
- It does not establish completion of Phase Two or the full redevelopment.
- It does not establish execution, receipt, or disbursement of later CCED
  funding.
- It does not establish current property or project status.
