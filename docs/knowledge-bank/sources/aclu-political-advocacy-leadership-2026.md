# ACLU political and advocacy leadership - August 2026

The official ACLU biography identifies Deirdre Schifeling as Chief Political
and Advocacy Officer and says she leads the National Political Advocacy
Department, including national and state policy and campaigns, national
grassroots organizing infrastructure, and electoral programming.

This supports describing Schifeling as the nearest publicly named senior leader
whose departmental vision the Senior Project Manager would help implement. It
does not establish that she is the role's direct manager, interviewer, or
hiring decision-maker.
