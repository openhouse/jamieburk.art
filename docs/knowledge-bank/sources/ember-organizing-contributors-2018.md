# Ember Team - Organizing Our Contributors

The Ember Team's 2018 article describes open-source leadership without formal
hierarchy through consensus, persuasion, shared direction, peer core teams, and
explicit coordination responsibility. It names Yehuda Katz among the initial
Steering Committee members.

The article is collective Ember Team authorship. It supports project-governance
context and does not assign every idea in the article to Yehuda Katz. It does
not discuss Jamie or constitute Yehuda Katz's participation, review, approval,
or endorsement of Jamie's Knowledge Wiki practice. The source is held from
direct portfolio projection pending a separate human composition decision.
