# Action Lab report prepublication-review summary, July 2026

Private working records document that Jamie completed a bounded
prepublication review of an Action Lab report about storefront vacancy and
registered commercial rents. His stated review scope was factual accuracy,
source and methods clarity, current bill language, and claims likely to travel
through press or public remarks.

The exchange records that the report team incorporated many of Jamie's edits
and invited an acknowledgement. Jamie explicitly stated that he had not rerun
the underlying analysis. He separated corrections he could verify from
questions requiring the authors' workbook, code, legal review, or editorial
judgment.

## Boundary

This public-safe summary does not reproduce the draft report, comments,
correspondence, contact information, unpublished findings, or third-party
statements. It does not establish coauthorship, analytic replication, legal
review, final-publication credit, or adoption of every recommendation.

Public projection remains on hold until the report and acknowledgement are
published or the organization separately approves a public formulation.
