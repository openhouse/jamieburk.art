# Action Lab storefront-report prepublication review, July 2026

## Public-safe source note

An external partner invited Jamie to give a rapid prepublication review of a
storefront-vacancy and commercial-rent report. Jamie bounded his pass to
factual accuracy, source and methods clarity, current legislative language,
and claims likely to travel through press coverage or speaker remarks.

The protected exchange records that Jamie:

- distinguished the report's descriptive findings from proposed explanations
  the stated analysis did not independently establish;
- preserved those explanations as hypotheses where stronger evidence or author
  judgment was still needed;
- left focused questions for the workbook, code, legal, communications,
  legislative, or author owner instead of presenting himself as all of them;
- invited a merchant-serving partner's perspective without delaying the
  immediate handoff; and
- corrected event-address, calendar, affiliation, and jurisdiction wording
  before public circulation.

The recipient reported incorporating many edits and asked permission to
acknowledge Jamie. Jamie approved a bounded description of his contribution.
The final report, acknowledgement, event, and exact accepted edits had not yet
been publicly verified when this record was reviewed.

## Boundary

The report copy, comments, email bodies, direct contact information, third-party
communications, and unverified findings remain outside public Git. Do not infer
authorship of the report, independent replication of its analysis, legal
review, or endorsement of every final claim.
