# UCSC narrative evaluations, 2004-2006

## Public-safe source note

The recovered educational record contains instructor evaluations from Warren
Sack and Margaret Morse. The evaluations describe early work with online
dictionary sources, social-network analysis, Flickr-based image retrieval,
interactive installation design, media archaeology, and performance as a form
of theoretical argument.

The raw record and student identifiers remain outside this repository. This
page does not reproduce the evaluations or present them as current
endorsements.

## Boundaries

- An instructor evaluation supports a dated educational observation, not a
  present-day product or employment claim.
- Group work remains group work; the record does not divide every component.
- A title conflict concerning a 2006 New York installation remains unresolved.
- Publication or quotation requires a separate editorial and rights decision.
