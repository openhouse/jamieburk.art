# Politico New York coverage of CallNYC

Politico New York reported on CallNYC on March 14, 2016. The reporting connects
Jamie to the January Council event, the fuller CouncilStat data release, and
his independent development and iteration of CallNYC.

The source supports the project's existence, Jamie's relationship to it, the
event and data-release sequence, and contemporaneous press coverage. It does
not establish CallNYC as an official Council product, formal hackathon
submission, or documented winner.
