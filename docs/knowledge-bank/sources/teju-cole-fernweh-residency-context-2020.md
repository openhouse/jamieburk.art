# Estranged in Switzerland - Teju Cole pictures the silence

Swissinfo reports that Cole spent part of 2014 traveling and photographing
Switzerland during a Zurich residency while writing a project related to
Lagos. The article discusses the selection process and the photographic body
that became *Fernweh*.

This supports the narrower point that a writing residency and an emergent
photographic practice coexisted in response to place. It does not establish
that Cole's original proposal promised only writing, that he abandoned the
writing project, or that Jamie's remembered account appears in a particular
book. Those details remain an open citation inquiry.
