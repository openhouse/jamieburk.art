# New York City Council event-day CouncilStat post

The Council's January 30, 2016 post described its first CouncilStat hackathon
at Civic Hall and invited civic technologists and Council staff to work with
constituent-services data.

This supports the narrower phrase **first CouncilStat hackathon**. It does not
support broader claims such as first civic-data hackathon, nor does it establish
CallNYC as a Council product, formal submission, or winner.

See the [event record](../events/nyc-council-constituent-services-hackathon-2016.md)
and [CallNYC project record](../projects/callnyc.md).
