# Commercial Rent Stabilization: official Albany public statements, 2026

These are full attributed quotations as published by the New York State
Senate. They are official written public statements, not necessarily verbatim
transcripts of delivered speeches.

## Senator Salazar and Assembly Member Gallagher introduce the Small Business Survival Act

**Date:** 2026-02-17

**Official source:** [New York State Senate press release](https://www.nysenate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026/julia-salazar/senator-salazar-and-assembly-member-gallagher-introduce)

The complete attributed pro-bill quotations are retained. These are published statements, not represented as chamber-floor transcripts.

### Julia Salazar

- **Role at statement:** New York State Senator and S8319 prime sponsor
- **Classification:** `direct-bill-support`

> The Small Business Survival Act would be a lifeline for small businesses in New York City by capping outrageous rent hikes and guaranteeing lease renewals. Corporate landlords are charging rents so high that the only business owners who can afford to pay are corporate chains. Or, they’re keeping storefronts vacant until larger, wealthier tenants come along. This is a toxic and now commonplace pattern that is shuttering our local businesses, putting New Yorkers out of work, and turning our vibrant neighborhoods into chain malls.

### Emily Gallagher

- **Role at statement:** New York State Assembly Member and A5568A prime sponsor
- **Classification:** `direct-bill-support`

> Small businesses are what make New York special, but they are increasingly being pushed out by greedy corporate actors. In the 20 years I’ve been in my neighborhood, I’ve seen so many beloved businesses close because of unaffordable rents or because big landlords would rather wait for a corporate chain to come along. With the Small Business Survival Act, we will cap rent, guarantee leases, and ensure our community businesses are actually owned by the members of our community.

### Olympia Kazi

- **Role at statement:** Urban activist
- **Classification:** `direct-bill-support`

> I want to thank State Senator Julia Salazar and Assembly Member Emily Gallagher for introducing this critical legislation to enact a common-sense, fundamental protection for commercial tenants. For more than half a century, communities have fought to defend beloved small businesses, nonprofits, and cultural institutions from displacement and erasure. With renewed leadership in New York State and New York City that centers affordability and justice, I am hopeful—and confident—that the moment to pass commercial rent stabilization has arrived.

### Small Business United

- **Role at statement:** Small-business organizing coalition
- **Classification:** `direct-bill-support`

> Small businesses are struggling to survive in New York City because they cannot keep up with continuously rising costs, including the rising cost of rent. This crisis is the result of treating commercial space as a commodity to make corporate real estate companies richer, rather than as community infrastructure to support our neighborhoods. New York must pass the Small Business Survival Act before our local business owners are forced to close their doors and seek opportunities elsewhere.

## Senator Salazar rallies for Jimmy's Corner and Small Business Rent Stabilization

**Date:** 2026-04-10

**Official source:** [New York State Senate press release](https://www.nysenate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026/julia-salazar/senator-salazar-rallies-jimmys-corner-and-small-business)

The complete attributed pro-bill quotations in the official release are retained. They supplement, but do not replace, the event transcript.

### Julia Salazar

- **Role at statement:** New York State Senator and S8319 prime sponsor
- **Classification:** `direct-bill-support`

> Jimmy’s Corner is a beloved small business and member of the community. It has had an impact that corporate businesses could never replicate. Unfortunately, there are countless small businesses just like Jimmy’s that are being squeezed out of our city. Big landlords are charging rents so high that the only business owners who can afford to pay are corporate chains. Or, they’re keeping storefronts vacant until larger, wealthier tenants come along. This is a toxic and now commonplace pattern that is shuttering our small businesses, putting New Yorkers out of work, and turning our vibrant neighborhoods into chain malls. Small Business Rent Stabilization would be a lifeline for local businesses in New York City by capping outrageous rent hikes, guaranteeing lease renewals, and protecting at-will tenants.

### Emily Gallagher

- **Role at statement:** New York State Assembly Member and A5568A prime sponsor
- **Classification:** `direct-bill-support`

> Small, local businesses make our neighborhoods vibrant, our communities safer, and our city unique. But increasingly, you have to be a huge chain or a venture-backed business to make it in this city. Landlords leave storefronts vacant while they wait for these cookie cutter establishments to come in and price out beloved local counterparts. The Small Business Rent Stabilization Act will protect our local businesses and ensure NYC continues to be a place where they can thrive.

### Olympia Kazi

- **Role at statement:** NYC Artist Coalition and Music Workers Alliance co-founder
- **Classification:** `direct-bill-support`

> What is happening with Jimmy's Corner—recently described by a patron as “the only real bar in the neighborhood”—perfectly illustrates how unfairly tilted the scales are for small businesses right now. Our government has left New York City’s commercial tenants largely unprotected. Current policies fail to recognize that these establishments provide essential services, create jobs, and contribute deep social and cultural value—often for decades. In this case, that legacy spans 54 years. Yet all of that can be erased overnight, and even leveraged for profit by landlords. The story of Jimmy’s Corner is a wake-up call. It underscores the urgent need for the city to finally establish meaningful protections for commercial tenants and we need to start with the most fundamental of them all, the passage of commercial rent stabilization.

### Linda B. Rosenthal

- **Role at statement:** New York State Assembly Member and Chair of the Assembly Committee on Housing
- **Classification:** `direct-bill-support`

> Jimmy's Corner is a New York institution, but could soon be forced to close its doors as a result of a dubious eviction proceeding. Nearly half of New York State's workforce is employed by small businesses, but drastic rent hikes and corporate greed have pushed thousands of these businesses to close in recent years. We need to act to lend a hand to New York City's beleaguered businesses by supporting Assemblymember Gallagher's legislation, the Small Business Rent Stabilization Act, which will protect at-will tenants, prohibit unaffordable rent hikes and guarantee lease renewals for small businesses across our city.

### Jo Anne Simon

- **Role at statement:** New York State Assembly Member and A5568A co-sponsor
- **Classification:** `direct-bill-support`

> Local businesses are the backbone of New York City, but far too many are being forced out by skyrocketing commercial rents and price-gouging. Small-business rent stabilization will help protect the very neighborhood institutions that create jobs, build community, and give NYC its quintessential character. I am proud to support this effort and thank Assemblymember Gallagher and Senator Salazar for sponsoring this important legislation.
