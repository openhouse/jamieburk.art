# ACLU biography - Terence Dougherty

The official biography says Terence Dougherty oversees the operational
functions and processes supporting the ACLU, including finance, legal,
compliance, data, technology, accessibility, human resources, and facilities.
It establishes senior operational scope; it does not publish the Learning and
Project Solutions reporting chain.
