# Wikimedia - Abstract Wikipedia team

The official team page identifies Denny Vrandečić as Head of Special Projects.
It describes a system for language-independent knowledge creation and a
longer-term goal of using shared structured data and abstract concepts to
support collaboration and reduce duplicated maintenance across language
communities.

discuss Jamie, establish a historical influence on Jamie's work, or constitute
Denny Vrandečić's participation, review, approval, or endorsement. The source
