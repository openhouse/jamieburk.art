# Jamie's canoe-commuting recollection, July 2026

Encountering the photograph prompted Jamie to remember journeys in which a
bicycle and canoe made New York waterways part of ordinary movement through
the city. The recollection connects operational preparation, public landscape,
movement, and the possibility of entering a project at the water's edge.

## Boundary

This is dated first-person memory. It is not an audited journey count, route
log, event chronology, proof of a recurring frequency, or account of other
people's experience. It opens a research inquiry and does not automatically
expand the homepage. It cannot alone establish a numeric public claim.
