# Circle O Open Positions - Operations Manager

Circle O's official posting, read September 27, described a part-time Operations Manager contractor
role averaging 20 hours per week at $50 per hour. The role works closely with
Founder and Artistic Director Kayla Hamilton across team collaboration, systems
and knowledge, communications and marketing, and program support.

On September 27, 2026, the page listed a September 30 application deadline,
November onboarding, and a twelve-month 2027 term. This is a time-bound reading;
the historical reading does not establish a current opening.

On October 13, the original URL returned HTTP 404. The
[employer-authored Dance/NYC listing](https://www.dance.nyc/for-artists/listings/2026/09/Circle-O-Operations-Manager-part-time-contractor/)
remained available with the same deadline. The
[official application page](https://form.jotform.com/262424060516146) directly
confirmed closure on September 30. A missing posting alone would not prove closure.

The source does not establish an application, interview, recommendation,
selection or employment outcome for Jamie.
