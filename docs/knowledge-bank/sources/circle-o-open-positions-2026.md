# Circle O Open Positions - Operations Manager

Circle O's official posting describes a part-time Operations Manager contractor
role averaging 20 hours per week at $50 per hour. The role works closely with
Founder and Artistic Director Kayla Hamilton across team collaboration, systems
and knowledge, communications and marketing, and program support.

On September 27, 2026, the page listed a September 30 application deadline,
November onboarding, and a twelve-month 2027 term. This is a time-bound reading;
the live official page controls if its details change.

The source does not establish an application, interview, recommendation,
selection or employment outcome for Jamie.
