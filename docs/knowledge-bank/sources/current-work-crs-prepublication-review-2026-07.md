# Commercial-rent storefront-vacancy pre-publication review, July 2026

## Public-safe proof

Jamie provided Action Lab a pre-publication review of storefront-vacancy
research focused on factual accuracy, source clarity, current bill language,
methods visibility, and the scope of claims likely to travel through press or
speaker remarks.

The reviewed correspondence supports the following bounded account:

- Action Lab said it incorporated many of Jamie's edits and asked permission
  to acknowledge him.
- Jamie proposed this accurate public formulation: “We also thank Jamie
  Burkart, a member of NYC Artist Coalition, which participates in Fair Rent
  NYC, for a pre-publication review focused on factual accuracy, source
  clarity, and the scope of the report’s claims.”
- The review caught a public event address discrepancy and distinguished “state
  lawmakers” from the less precise “city leaders” for legislation pending in
  Albany.
- Jamie identified places where a descriptive relationship between high
  registered rents and high vacancy did not independently establish landlord
  motive, financing mechanisms, or deliberate warehousing.
- He preserved those possible mechanisms as hypotheses unless the authors held
  additional support.
- He did not rerun the underlying analysis. Questions requiring the workbook,
  code, methods owners, legal review, or author judgment remained with those
  responsible parties.
- Jamie routed the draft toward additional merchant-serving review rather than
  treating his perspective as sufficient.

## What it demonstrates

The record demonstrates an active practice of source-aware review, claim
calibration, operational error detection, responsible escalation, and
stakeholder inclusion under a compressed publication timeline.
