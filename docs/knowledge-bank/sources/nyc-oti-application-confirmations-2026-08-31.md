# NYC OTI application confirmation review - August 31, 2026

Jamie supplied two successful application screens and two City confirmation
messages for bounded review. Together they establish date-level receipt of the
Product Manager application, reference 784450, and the Operations Manager
application, reference 789810, on August 31, 2026.

The screens and messages are corroborating artifacts from the same application
system, not independent sources. Raw images, correspondence, private application
access links, contact details, applicant identifiers, delivery times, and
message-specific salutations remain outside Git. The graph retains only the
minimum role, reference, date, and received state that Jamie asked it to record.

Receipt does not establish civil-service eligibility, completed hiring-team
review, an interview, an offer, an appointment, or a hiring outcome. It also
does not establish that a Civic Match introduction or personal referral was
requested, made, accepted, or used. Those remain separate future observations.
