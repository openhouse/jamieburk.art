# NYC Council Open Data Week event and proposal-trail records, March 2026

## Contemporary event metadata

An event registration received March 23 and a logistics message received March
25 identify the session as:

- **From Data to Policy: How the NYC Council Turns City Data Into Action**;
- March 26, 2026, 3:00-4:00 p.m.;
- New York City Council, 250 Broadway, eighth-floor overflow room;
- organized by the New York City Council Data Team;
- presented within NYC Open Data Week.

Only this bounded metadata enters public Git. Account identifiers, ticket
numbers, contact details, message bodies, and private source locators remain
outside the repository.

## Contemporary proposal trail

A Drive search also surfaced a document titled **Toward a Fuller Public
Baseline for Commercial Vacancy and Lease Cost in NYC**, modified March 27,
2026, and a mapped PDF version modified soon afterward. The close temporal fit
and subject matter corroborate the existence of Jamie's commercial-rent and
vacancy data work around the event.

They do not establish that either surfaced file is the exact packet visible in
the photograph. They also do not prove delivery, discussion with a particular
person, adoption, endorsement, or policy effect.

## Evidentiary use

The event records can establish the official title, date, time, venue, and
organizer. The photograph visibly establishes Jamie at a New York City Council
setting with a clipboard. Jamie's later oral history identifies the working
materials and connects the scene to the event and proposal.

The combined record supports a bounded event-level caption. It does not close
photographer identity, permission, private source binding, exact packet
version, recipient, conversation, or outcome.
