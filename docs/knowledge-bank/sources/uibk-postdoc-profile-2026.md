# University of Innsbruck scientific-staff profile - postdocs

The University's English-language profile says postdoctoral roles ordinarily
reserve at least half of working time for independent research. They also
include independent teaching and examinations, student and bachelor-thesis
supervision, research and administrative collaboration, evaluation, and
continuing education. The ordinary teaching load is four semester hours for a
40-hour appointment, prorated for part-time employment.

The profile lists B1/3 compensation at EUR 5,014.30 gross per month for full
time, describes the salary as all-in, says work must be performed at the
University, and says these postdoctoral positions are usually limited to four
years. The individual posting remains controlling for this opportunity.
