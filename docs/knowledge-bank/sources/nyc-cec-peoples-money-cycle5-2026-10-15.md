# The People's Money - fifth-cycle launch, October 15, 2026

[Official announcement, October 15, 2026](https://www.nyc.gov/site/civicengagement/news/cec-launches-fifth-cycle-of-citywide-participatory-budgeting.page), read October 18.

The fifth cycle began October 14 with a $4 million allocation. Its published phases are:

| Phase | Published window |
| --- | --- |
| Idea generation | October 14–November 18, 2026 |
| Borough Assemblies | January 23–February 21, 2027 |
| Voting | May 7–June 20, 2027 |
| Implementation | December 2027–December 2028 |

The release names 74 workshop partner organizations, including 32 TRIE partners. It separately describes Cycle 4 as beginning implementation and seeking community-organization implementers. These are overlapping cycles, not one timeline.

The release identifies Hillary Carelli-Donnell as Director of The People's Money and Sarah Sayeed as CEC Chair and Executive Director. Neither attribution establishes hiring authority.

The linked [2026 annual report](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/civicengagement/downloads/pdf/CEC-2026-Annual-Report.pdf) was located but **not read**: the research browser rejected its 24 MB response. No conclusion here is attributed to that unread report.
