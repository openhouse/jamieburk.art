# Official Asana product leadership context

Asana's official leadership page identifies Arnab Bose as Chief Product
Officer. Asana's official appointment announcement says he leads product
strategy and innovation focused on human and AI teamwork. This supports only a
senior product-vision relationship to the AI Implementation Manager role; it
does not establish a direct manager, interviewer, hiring decision-maker, or
private opinion.

- [Official leadership page](https://asana.com/leadership)
- [Official appointment announcement](https://investors.asana.com/news-releases/news-release-details/asana-appoints-arnab-bose-chief-product-officer)
