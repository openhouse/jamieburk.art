# Harry J. Epstein historic storefront Wayback chronology

This public-source record separates the storefront's observable web history
from Jamie's first-party account of the Thick Arts LLC client relationship.

| Observation | What the capture establishes |
| --- | --- |
| [January 1, 2010](https://web.archive.org/web/20100101090939/http://store.harryepstein.com/) | The earliest reviewed host capture redirects into the `merchant2` commerce application. A directly captured storefront homepage follows on March 3, 2010. |
| [July 3, 2015](https://web.archive.org/web/20150703004948/http://store.harryepstein.com/) | A functioning customer-facing storefront shows catalog navigation, search, products and prices, account and checkout routes, and current 2015 publishing. |
| [August 1, 2015](https://web.archive.org/web/20150801160416/http://store.harryepstein.com/) | The historic storefront host redirects to the successor website at `www.harryepstein.com/index.php/`. |

## Interpretation

Jamie states that his HJE work began in 2009 and that he formalized the practice
as Thick Arts LLC during the engagement, with HJE as its first client. New York
State independently records the LLC's initial filing on July 6, 2012. The work
sequence and first-client relationship are first-party professional evidence.
The captures independently support January 2010-July 2015 observation bounds
and the July-August 2015 host transition; they do not establish exact contract,
invoice, payment, or billing dates.

## Boundary

The current Harry J. Epstein website is a successor site and may be linked for
present-day business context. Neither that live site nor the archived redirect
is evidence that Jamie built or currently maintains the successor system.
