# East River photograph public-safe metadata summary

## Supported

- capture year `2022`;
- dimensions of the reviewed source representation: `1280 × 960`;
- visual agreement with the public derivative;
- neighborhood-scale place wording: East River beneath the Manhattan Bridge;
- a protected private binding exists for the source and public derivative.

## Not published

The source identifier, original filename, catalog path, full capture timestamp,
device metadata, private album membership, precise coordinates, private
fingerprints, and local receipt location remain outside public Git.

The place and year labels are intentionally less precise than the protected
metadata. This source does not establish the photographer or permission; those
belong to a separate source record.
