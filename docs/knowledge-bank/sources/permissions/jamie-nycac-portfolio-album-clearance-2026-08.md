# NYC Artist Coalition portfolio-album clearance

Jamie explicitly marked the publication-clearance box as checked for
photographs in the identified Apple Photos album when used in his professional
portfolio. The private album identifier, library path, Photos UUIDs, People
relations, and location metadata remain outside Git.

This is destination-specific publication authority, not an unrestricted
license or a claim of authorship. Selection, crop, caption, creator attribution,
collective credit, dignity, production publication, and indexing remain
separate fields. The two selected occurrences use a custody credit because
creator attribution is unresolved.
