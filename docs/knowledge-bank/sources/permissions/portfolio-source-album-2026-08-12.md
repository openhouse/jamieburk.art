# Portfolio source album publication authorization

On August 12, 2026, Jamie confirmed that every photograph in one bounded
source album is cleared for publication on his portfolio site, including
permission from represented people. That authorization resolves the rights and
represented-person publication gate for the selected portfolio occurrences.

The private album name, asset identifiers, People tags, locations, and source
paths remain outside Git. Creator identity, exact caption, collective context,
and crop remain occurrence-specific. This capsule does not claim an
unrestricted license for other sites, campaigns, resale, or transfer.
