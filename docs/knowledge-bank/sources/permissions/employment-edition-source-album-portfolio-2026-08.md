# Employment-edition source album portfolio permission summary

On August 12, 2026, Jamie Burkart confirmed that every photograph in the
bounded source album has publication rights and permission for use on the
`jamieburk.art` portfolio. This checks the source-album rights and permission
gate for the six selected derivatives.

The private album locator, original files, Photos identifiers, and supporting
archive records remain outside Git. This summary does not assert that Jamie
made every photograph or license the images for another site, resale, or
transfer. Known creators still require explicit credit; where authorship is not
established, the public page states archive custody rather than inventing a
photographer credit.

Production publication and indexing remain separate Jamie decisions after the
exact composition is reviewed.
