# CodePath - Senior AI Operations Lead 5175813007

The official posting describes a US-remote role reporting to the Director of
Business Operations with a salary range of $110,000 to $150,000. The role is to
embed with functions, map workflows, build and productionize human-in-the-loop
agents and automations, establish rubrics and evals, create reusable assets,
coach teams to self-sufficiency, and measure time saved and quality gained.

The posting requires five or more years in automation or operations tooling,
including recent professional LLM or agent work. Its application asks for a
personally built and shipped LLM workflow and a measured operational change.
Those are application screens, not generic interest prompts.

The [official source](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/codepath/jobs/5175813007)
remained application-enabled when rechecked. It names the manager's title but
not a person.
