# NYC Mayor's Office - Public Interest Technology Crew launch

The Mayor's Office announced five cross-functional, in-house PIT Crews that
will work with agencies and New Yorkers to build resident-facing services on
accelerated timelines. The release identifies Lisa Gelobter as Chief
Technology Officer and OTI Commissioner and records her direction: reduce the
burden of navigating government by understanding problems, testing, launching,
learning from users, and demonstrating better delivery.
