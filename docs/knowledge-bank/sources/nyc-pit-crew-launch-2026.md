# NYC Mayor's Office - PIT Crew launch announcement

The Mayor's Office announcement identifies Lisa Gelobter as Commissioner and
Chief Technology Officer and describes five cross-functional PIT Crews building
resident-facing digital services with city agencies. Gelobter's stated public
direction emphasizes understanding problems, testing rapidly, launching,
learning from users, strengthening trust, and proving better ways to deliver.

The announcement establishes initiative leadership and public purpose. It does
not publish a product-manager reporting line.

See the [official announcement](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/07/mayor-mamdani-launches--public-interest-technology--pit--crew--t).
