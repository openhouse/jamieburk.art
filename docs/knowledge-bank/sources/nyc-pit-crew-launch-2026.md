# NYC launches the Public Interest Technology Crew - July 2026

The official City announcement identifies Lisa Gelobter as Chief Technology
Officer and OTI Commissioner and describes five Public Interest Technology
Crews that will build public-facing digital solutions with product managers,
designers, engineers, researchers, and data experts.

Gelobter's public vision is to treat technology as part of how people experience
government: understand real problems, test ideas rapidly, launch, learn from
users, and make City services simpler and more trustworthy. That establishes
her as the nearest publicly named senior vision owner for the PIT Crew roles.
Neither job posting names a direct manager, so this source does not convert the
Commissioner into the day-to-day supervisor or hiring manager.
