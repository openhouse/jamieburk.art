# Open House at UC Santa Cruz

## What the article documents

Laura Mattingly's contemporary account describes Jamie initiating a ten-day
experiment in which a university gallery became a communal living, art, and
participation space. The article reports that shared responsibility and
decision-making were premeditated parts of the project, while daily uses and
events continued to change through participation.

It also documents institutional concerns, negotiated operating boundaries,
multi-perspective recording, a highly visible location, and Jamie's comparison
of the project to inhabited social-software spaces.

## Boundary

The report does not make Jamie the sole author of what participants did, supply
a complete participant roster, establish that everyone experienced the space
the same way, or grant permission to republish article text or photographs.
