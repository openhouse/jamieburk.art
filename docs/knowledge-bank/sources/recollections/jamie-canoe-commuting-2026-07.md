# Jamie Burkart Recollection of Bicycle-Canoe Journeys

Seeing the East River photograph in a composed homepage prompted Jamie to
describe a recurring practice of transporting a canoe by bicycle with a small
handmade trailer. He recalled a trailer made partly from an IKEA futon and two
children's bicycle forks, with the paddle helping couple and offset the canoe.
He understood the assembled bicycle, trailer, paddle, canoe, people, land, and
water as a lightweight participatory operating system for moving through the
city differently.

This is a dated first-person recollection. It may guide source return and future
research, but it does not establish a count, full chronology, or complete
technical specification. The current homepage remains concise.
