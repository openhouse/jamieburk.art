# Jamie Burkart recollection of bicycle-canoe commuting

Seeing the East River image in composition brought back the design of a small
canoe trailer built from part of an IKEA futon and two front forks from
children's bicycles. The canoe could be lashed to the trailer. The paddle handle
fit beneath the bicycle saddle, while the paddle was lashed toward the canoe's
nose to create a useful offset. One assembly performed several transportation
and coupling functions across streets and water.

Jamie remembers this as a recurring practice of moving through the city by
bicycle and canoe. The recollection makes the image more resonant, but it does
not authorize a journey count, exact chronology, or expanded homepage caption.
Those questions return to the archive through a separate inquiry.
