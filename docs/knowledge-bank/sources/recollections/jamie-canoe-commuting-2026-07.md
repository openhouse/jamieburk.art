# Jamie Burkart recollection of bicycle-canoe journeys

Seeing the homepage composition returned Jamie to a recurring practice of
transporting a canoe by bicycle with a handmade trailer, then moving between
land and water with a small coupled system of bicycle, trailer, canoe, paddle,
and ordinary public infrastructure.

This is a dated first-person recollection, not a count, chronology, or
independently corroborated claim. It does not automatically alter the homepage.
It remains held from public projection while related photographs and event-level
instances are researched.
