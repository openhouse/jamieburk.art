# NYC Council field-kit photograph oral history, July 2026

## Public-safe source note

Jamie identifies himself outside New York City Council hearing rooms during a
2026 civic-data program. He recalls carrying a coalition field sign-up kit and
distributing a proposal for privacy-preserving geographic aggregation of
commercial lease-cost and vacancy information. He situates the moment within a
longer practice connecting WOW List's mutual-aid precedent, CallNYC's
constituent-action tooling, NYC Artist Coalition participation infrastructure,
and later commercial-rent data research.

Jamie supplied a repaired, generalized working transcript on July 26, 2026. It
is suitable for governed research use, but it remains a first-person
recollection: the underlying audio was not verified in this pass, and the exact
event title, date, proposal version, recipients, conversations, and outcomes
remain open.

A contemporaneous working folder independently locates the Council data event
and retains proposal and audio materials. This corroborates the broad event
context, not every recollected conversation, outcome, authorship claim, or
follow-up.

The photographer was another attendee. The person's identity and permission
were not recovered in this pass.
