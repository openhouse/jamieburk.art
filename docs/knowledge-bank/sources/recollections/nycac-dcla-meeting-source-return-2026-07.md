# Jamie recollection on the formative DCLA meeting

Jamie remembers making an audio recording of the first DCLA gathering,
listening back closely over several days, researching and mapping the speakers
in a spreadsheet, and building an interactive visualization from what he
heard. He recalls that a detail from this visualization later appeared in
Bedford + Bowery's coverage.

Contemporaneous website and press records corroborate the existence,
publication, and press reuse of the visualization. The audio recording has not
yet been recovered. The recollection remains valuable for directing research,
but it does not replace the missing recording or allocate every participant's
contribution.
