# Jamie recollection on tending public-facing civic work

Jamie describes operational production as caring for a shared container:
listening closely, giving emerging structure a resilient form, and helping
people recognize that their knowledge and agency matter. He has described his
NYC Artist Coalition contribution as including public-facing identity and
campaign web systems that teammates could use over time.
