# Jamie recollections on two KC Town Hall Phase One photographs

## Hard-hat field portrait

Jamie identifies himself at KC Town Hall wearing a yellow hard hat beside
scaffolding. He remembers coordinating the sequence among masonry, roofing,
carpentry, structural, plumbing, welding, architectural, and engineering work;
learning scaffold and masonry practice with Earl Brown; tending the building
day by day; and carrying the neighborhood survey into conversations around the
site. He remembers Julia Fredenburg as the photographer, but that attribution
has not been independently confirmed.

## Interior portrait with Harold Mason

Jamie identifies Harold Mason beside him in an unfinished second-floor room.
He remembers field walks, measured drawings, open-data and mapping work used to
identify candidate neighborhood sites, and a craft lineage connecting Earl
Brown and Harold Mason to the restoration. He remembers Earl Brown as the
photographer, but that attribution has not been independently confirmed.

## Boundary

The recollections add embodied work, relationships, and research leads. They do
not independently verify the title `general contractor`, final completion,
exact dates, creator rights, represented-person consent, or component-level
authorship. The two photographs remain out of the public site while those
questions are open.
