# KC Town Hall photographic oral histories, July 2026

## Source state

Jamie recorded two first-person accounts while looking closely at supplied KC
Town Hall photographs. The protected source editions are repaired reading
transcripts, not forensic transcripts and not audio-verified fact checks. They
contain personal, relational, exact-location, and third-party context that does
not belong in public Git.

## What the recollections open

The hard-hat photograph prompted detailed recollection about daily site access,
trade coordination, masonry learning, scaffolding, roof and structural work,
street-level listening, neighborhood-survey practice, the ten-minute-walk
frame, and a proposed small-business incubator model.

The interior photograph prompted recollection about access and equipment
systems, a longer geospatial site-selection process, building assessment,
structural repair, craft lineages, a building tour, and later stewardship.

These are research leads. The proposal bundle, municipal record, retained code,
project website archive, receipts, correspondence, collaborator recollections,
and image metadata may corroborate or correct individual propositions.

## Boundaries

- The recollections do not establish an exact capture date.
- Creator and depicted-person identifications remain first-person
  recollections until separately confirmed and approved.
- Remembered quotations remain attributed memory.
- Related frames do not multiply evidentiary weight.
- The oral histories do not clear rights, consent, dignity, captions, claims,
  or portfolio placement.
- Protected personal context, exact locations, and unnecessary third-party
  identities remain outside this capsule.
