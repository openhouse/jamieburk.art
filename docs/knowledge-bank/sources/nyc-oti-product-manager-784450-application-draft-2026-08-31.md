# NYC OTI Product Manager 784450 application-draft review

Jamie supplied two full-page exports from the City of New York's
SmartRecruiters application flow for review on August 31, 2026. The raw exports
remain outside Git. This record excludes contact details, applicant identifiers,
protected demographic and veteran information, and any private file locator.

The bounded review can establish only that the application was prepared but not
submitted; five collapsed experience cards displayed year ranges; résumé and
signed cover-letter filenames appeared selected; and required preliminary
questions and attestations remained incomplete. The exports do not expose the
months stored in the experience controls, certify that the entered ranges are
correct, establish consent to terms or privacy notices, or prove submission.

The source may support a private application checklist and a public-safe workflow
lesson. It may not be committed, quoted as personal disclosure, treated as a
submission receipt, or used to infer demographic, veteran, household-service,
work-authorization, civil-service, or legal-consent answers.
