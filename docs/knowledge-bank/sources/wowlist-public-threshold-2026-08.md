# WOW List public return threshold, August 2026

On August 13, 2026, the public site presented a recognizable landing page, an About page, and a consented invitation to join testing. Response headers kept the threshold noindex. No private historical corpus or member data appeared in the public application.

This record keeps the portfolio current without treating a small public threshold as the restored community calendar or evidence of adoption.

The August 14 close reading adds the product argument visible across the two
pages. The landing compresses the promise to finding and sharing things to do
in real life. The About page grounds that promise in Sunday Dinner, explicitly
credits Richard Caceres and Jamie Burkart, frames event publishing as an
invitation for other people to be there, and ends with a bounded `coming back`
status. The tester form requests only an email address and explains the narrow
contact purpose. No sitemap was present, consistent with the explicit noindex
threshold.
