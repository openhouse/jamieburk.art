# Protected Commercial-Rent Public-Event Recording Family, 2025-2026

An authorized Otter source return located four distinct recordings relevant to
the supportive-statement corpus:

- a November 10, 2025 bill presentation and discussion that includes supportive
  remarks by Assembly Member Emily Gallagher and Senator Julia Salazar;
- a November 13, 2025 public event that includes separate supportive remarks by
  Council Member Shahana Hanif, then-Comptroller Brad Lander, Assembly Member
  Emily Gallagher, Olympia Kazi, and other speakers;
- a February 17, 2026 public event that includes supportive remarks by Eon
  Huntley, Assembly Member Emily Gallagher, Olympia Kazi, and other speakers;
- an April 10, 2026 public rally recording with supportive remarks by Assembly
  Member Emily Gallagher, Senator Julia Salazar, small-business owners,
  coalition speakers, and other legislators.

Two Otter records represent raw and cleaned versions of the April 10 event.
They must be reconciled as one event and one speech population.

## Transcript Conditions

The Otter text is an automated working transcript, not a publication-ready
record. The source return found material speaker-label and word-recognition
errors. In particular, the February recording misrecognizes Eon Huntley's first
name. The audio, event materials, and independent public sources must be used
to correct speaker identity and wording before quotation.

The private recordings, raw transcript bodies, account URLs, and connector IDs
remain outside this public repository. The public record does not yet grant
blanket permission to republish every non-official speaker's full remarks.

## Does Not Establish

- a complete citywide population of supportive speeches;
- verbatim accuracy of automated transcription;
- publication consent for every recorded speaker;
- that attendance, office, candidacy, sponsorship, or electoral result is a
  substitute for an attributable supportive statement;
- that a cleaned and raw recording are separate public events.
