# Known and Strange Things catalog and contents record

The Open Library catalog record lists *Far Away From Here* in the "Being there"
section of Teju Cole's 2016 essay collection *Known and Strange Things*. This
helps identify the book and essay Jamie remembers when explaining the freedom a
residency can protect.

## Boundary

The catalog record confirms publication context, not Jamie's recollection word
for word. It does not establish a relationship between Cole and the 196 Artists
Residency or grant permission to reproduce the essay.
