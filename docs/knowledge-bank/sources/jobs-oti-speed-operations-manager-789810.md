# NYC Jobs - SPEED Operations Manager, Job ID 789810

The official posting lists one full-time Operations Manager position in OTI's
Product Management department at 2 MetroTech. The salary range is
$75,000-$160,000, and the visible application deadline is October 4, 2026.

The role coordinates budget, procurement, legal, sourcing, cyber, and product
dependencies; manages resource allocation, incoming requests, in-flight work,
expense and capital budgets, procurement for tools and personnel, onboarding,
and reporting on team health, project status, and operational metrics.

The civil-service title is IT Project Specialist, with several
education-and-experience routes. The preferred profile asks for five or more
years in operations, program management, chief-of-staff work, or a similar
role, plus experience with budgets, contracts, procurement, cross-disciplinary
digital-product teams, and plain-language executive communication.
