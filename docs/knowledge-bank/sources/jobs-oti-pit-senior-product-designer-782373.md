# NYC Jobs - PIT Crew Senior Product Designer, Job ID 782373

The official posting lists one full-time Senior Product Designer position in
OTI's Commissioner's Office at 2 MetroTech. The salary range is
$75,000-$160,000, and the visible application deadline is October 16, 2026.

The role spans resident research, interaction design, content design, visual
design, prototyping, usability testing, information architecture, plain
language, accessibility, design systems, engineering handoff, and public
launch. The preferred profile asks for five or more years of professional
product-design experience and a portfolio showing shipped user-facing products.

The civil-service title is IT Project Specialist. Jamie's shipped web systems,
graphic-design practice, participatory discovery, and public portfolio create a
real adjacent fit. The record does not yet establish the conventional Figma,
design-system, and senior product-design depth the posting prefers.
