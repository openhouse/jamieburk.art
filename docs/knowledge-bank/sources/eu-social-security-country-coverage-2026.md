# Your Europe - Social-security cover across EU countries

Your Europe explains that EU rules coordinate national social-security
systems, but the applicable country depends on the actual work and residence
pattern. Work in more than one country, postings, employer location, and the
share of activity in a country can change the result.

This source is a screening caution, not an eligibility or coverage
determination for Jamie or an employer.

Recheck the [official source](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/work/social-security-and-benefits/country-coverage/index_en.htm)
and obtain qualified advice for a real arrangement.
