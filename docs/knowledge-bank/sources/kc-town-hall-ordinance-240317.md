# KC Town Hall: Ordinance 240317 source appraisal

Last reviewed: 2026-07-15

## Source

- **Record:** City of Kansas City, Missouri, Ordinance 240317
- **Final action:** Passed March 28, 2024
- **Public record:** [Kansas City Legistar](https://kansascity.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?G=D2E89A09-8736-4EFB-B4AE-572E0903BD5A&GUID=E3F31A7F-65F8-464E-ABD4-197DEB6D80C8&ID=6586846&Options=&Search=)
- **Source class:** Official municipal legislative record

## What it establishes

- Kansas City recorded KC Town Hall among three withdrawn CCED projects.
- The ordinance describes the associated project funds as unused.
- It reduces $490,539 from the KC Town Hall project account.
- It reappropriates the combined withdrawn-project funds to the Housing Budget.

## What it does not establish

- why KC Town Hall withdrew
- who initiated or decided the withdrawal
- Jamie's role in the withdrawal
- that the separately documented Phase One cold-shell work was not completed
- the present status of the property or any successor work

## Public-use boundary

Use the ordinance to complete the municipal funding sequence: recommendation,
negotiation authority, later project withdrawal, and reappropriation of the
unused allocation. Do not turn the institutional record into a personal account
of Jamie's transition or publish private family context.
