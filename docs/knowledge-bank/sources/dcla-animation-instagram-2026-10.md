and five screenshots, then authorized this bounded source and portfolio update.
The animation already had a publication record through Lin Tyrpien's article.
This return adds the direct reel destination and its platform relationships;
it does not retroactively turn the August planning record into delivery evidence.

## What was inspected

On October 18, 2026, yt-dlp retrieved the public reel without account cookies.
The downloaded edition is 30 seconds long at 1080 × 1920 pixels and includes
video and audio streams. Visual review used frames at two-second intervals
throughout, plus one-second intervals from 20 through 29 seconds. This is a
sampled visual review, not a full auditory review or a verified transcript.

The visible sequence moves from the room to participants' requests concerning
artists, cultural space, senior studio space and accountability. Later text
acknowledges the accountability request and names vacant storefronts,
commercial rent stabilization and public schools as subjects for discussion.
This is evidence of an edited public account of the conversation, not evidence
that a policy was adopted or that the images identify each speaking voice.

The video credits Lin Tyrpien's article on screen. Its retrieved caption points
readers to that article but does not provide complete photo, voice or music
credits. Those credits remain unverified for this edition. The Instagram
"Original audio" label alone would not establish ownership of music or voices.
