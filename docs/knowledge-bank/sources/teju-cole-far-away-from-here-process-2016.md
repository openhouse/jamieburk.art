# Teju Cole on the making of Far Away From Here

## What the interview documents

In a published conversation with Jennifer B. McDonald, Teju Cole describes a
six-month Zurich residency that he entered with a nonfiction manuscript about
Lagos. He says that he wrote some of that project, but that most of his time
moved toward traveling around Switzerland, looking closely, and making
photographs. He later edited thousands of photographs into a smaller field and
developed the essay *Far Away From Here* around that process of discovery.

The source supports a bounded proposition: a residency can create conditions in
which an artist's actual work becomes different from the work anticipated at
the beginning.
