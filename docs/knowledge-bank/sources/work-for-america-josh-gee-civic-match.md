# Work for America announcement of Josh Gee as Civic Match vice president

Work for America announced Josh Gee as Vice President of Civic Match and
described his remit as leading the platform's next phase of growth. The
announcement connects his experience in government technology, public-service
delivery, and product leadership to the network's work with job seekers and
government hiring teams.

This source supports a public-context analytical lens about platform strategy.
It does not establish awareness of Jamie, participation in this project,
recommendation, endorsement, or hiring intent.
