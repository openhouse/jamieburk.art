# Work for America announcement naming Courtney Kishbaugh as Civic Match director

Work for America identified Courtney Kishbaugh as Director of Civic Match and
described her background in process innovation, human-centered design, public
service, and program management. That role places her close to the program's
candidate experience and matching operations.

This source supports a public-context analytical lens about candidate clarity
and program fit. It does not establish awareness of Jamie, participation in
this project, recommendation, endorsement, or hiring intent.
