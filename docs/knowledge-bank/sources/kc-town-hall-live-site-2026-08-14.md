# KC Town Hall restored public site

The bounded crawl reached 10 same-origin HTML pages and all 10 responded. It
checked 69 unique first-party assets without finding a failed first-party
asset. No form or service request was submitted.

The site is strong evidence of participation infrastructure rather than a
conventional real-estate brochure. It joins neighborhood listening, a public
survey, mailing-list onboarding, resident-service intake, COVID resources,
Tired of Tires coordination, youth basketball documentation, temporary-power
instructions, and a practical account of handling dumped paint. The About page
credits Julia and Jamie together.

The public surface must be treated as a historical project record. It still
contains active-sounding invitations and time-sensitive service language,
including a “monthly” tire pickup and pandemic-era resources, although the
governed project record documents Jamie's later transition away from the
project. A portfolio link should therefore say “historical project site,” not
imply that the services are current.

Maintenance observations: the site reports Ghost 1.23; its canonical, Open
Graph, Twitter, image, and sitemap references use `http://` even though the
site redirects to HTTPS and sends HSTS. These are maintenance leads, not a
security or service-failure diagnosis.
