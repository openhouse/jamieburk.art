# NYC Council Commercial Rent Stabilization Transcript

This 327-page public transcript preserves Council Member Stephen Levin's prime-
sponsor statement and testimony from small-business owners, worker
cooperatives, artists, cultural-space operators, landlords, brokers, lawyers,
administrators, and other New Yorkers.

The normalized corpus includes the complete attributed turn population for
each speaker classified as supportive or supportive in part. Opposition,
mixed positions, other-bill testimony, and unresolved labels remain in the
speaker census rather than being erased.
