# CodePath - Engineering Project Manager 5160542007

The official posting describes a remote Engineering Project Manager role
reporting to the Vice President of Engineering. The US salary range is $140,000
to $178,000.

The role organizes a small engineering team's work, owns hands-on quality
assurance and the bug pipeline, uses AI to automate repetitive work, connects
engineering, product, and internal users, and introduces process only when its
value exceeds its friction. Required technical signals include reproducing a
bug, reading a stack trace, and writing a test case; Rails or Django and
Capybara or RSpec are preferred.

The [official source](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/codepath/jobs/5160542007)
remained application-enabled when rechecked. It names the manager's title but
not a person.
