# ACLU - Product Manager II, Discovery 8482872002

the Head of Product. It emphasizes structured discovery, facilitation without
formal authority, privacy and security constraints, build-buy-do-not-build
judgment, low-risk pilots, and traceable Gen AI governance decisions. The
application requires a redacted PRD or product brief from a launched product.
