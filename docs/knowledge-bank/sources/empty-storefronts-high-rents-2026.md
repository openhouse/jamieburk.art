# *Empty Storefronts, High Rents* final report

The 20-page final report is titled *Empty Storefronts, High Rents: Why New York
City Needs Commercial Rent Stabilization*. The final PDF identifies Mahin
Rahman Tawrat as lead author and data analyst, credits Gianpaolo Baiocchi for
support and guidance, and names Small Business United, The Action Lab, Main
Street Alliance, and Small Business Majority as publishing organizations.

The acknowledgment is the strongest public-safe evidence of Jamie's report
contribution. It thanks Jamie Burkart, identifies him as an NYC Artist
Coalition member participating in Fair Rent NYC, and describes his contribution
as a thoughtful and careful review of the draft.

## What the review supports

Protected dated correspondence shows that Jamie reviewed factual accuracy,
source and method clarity, current bill language, accessibility, attribution,
and release hygiene. His review repeatedly protected the distinction between a
district-level association and unproved claims about landlord motive,
warehousing, or individual business closure. The organizer later wrote that
many of Jamie's edits had been incorporated.

The final methodology section now expressly limits the analysis: owner-reported
data, reporting completeness, implausible or missing values, district
aggregation, nominal rents, and the difference between registered, asking,
executed, concession, and net-effective rent all constrain interpretation. It
also states that the district-level analysis cannot establish individual
closure cause, landlord motive, financing behavior, or causation.

This alignment is meaningful but not a change-attribution proof. A complete
immutable draft-to-final version chain was not recovered, and broader causal
rhetoric remains elsewhere in the report.

## Boundaries

- Use `reviewer` or `contributor`, not `author`, `researcher`, `data analyst`,
  `methods validator`, or `legal reviewer`.
- The printed acknowledgment does not mean Jamie or NYC Artist Coalition
  endorsed every claim, method, recommendation, or rhetorical passage.
- Do not count accepted edits or assign particular final sentences to Jamie
  without a preserved version chain and publisher approval.
- The final PDF binary, email attachment locator, draft text, tracked changes,
  and private correspondence stay outside this public repository.
- No stable public PDF route was recovered on August 12. The source remains
  metadata-only until a publisher-controlled route is available.
