# NYC Jobs - SPEED Senior Product Manager, Job ID 789610

The official posting lists one full-time Senior Product Manager position in
OTI's Product Management department at 2 MetroTech. The salary range is
$100,000-$180,000, and the visible application deadline is October 4, 2026.

The role creates business cases and roadmaps for SPEED, diagnoses root
challenges, manages product development from conception through launch,
synthesizes research, analytics, and systems analysis, defines outcomes and
KPIs, and translates user needs into requirements, user stories, acceptance
criteria, and system requirements. The posting emphasizes plain-language
communication with nontechnical stakeholders and influence without authority.

The civil-service title is Senior IT Architect. One route requires a bachelor's
degree plus ten years of full-time work related to enterprise, solutions, or
network architecture or IT infrastructure. Jamie has a long technical-practice
chronology, but whether it meets this exact standard is a formal City review,
not a conclusion this record can make.
