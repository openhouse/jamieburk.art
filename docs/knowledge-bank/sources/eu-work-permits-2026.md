# Your Europe - Work permits

The European Union's Your Europe service says EU nationals generally do not
need a work permit to work anywhere in the EU. It advises workers to confirm
country-specific procedures before taking up work.

This source supplies a general rule. It does not verify Jamie's nationality or
resolve residence, tax, payroll, social-security, employer-policy, or
profession-specific requirements.

Recheck the [official source](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/work/work-abroad/work-permits/index_en.htm)
before outward use.
