# New York Department of State record for Thick Arts LLC

The Department of State's Active Corporations dataset identifies `THICK ARTS
LLC` as a New York domestic limited liability company and gives an initial DOS
filing date of July 6, 2012. The public query linked above is deliberately
limited to entity identity, filing date, county, jurisdiction, and entity type.

## Boundary

The official entity record establishes the legal filing date. It does not
establish that Jamie's professional practice began in 2009, identify Harry J.
Epstein Company or any other client, document when an engagement began or
ended, or describe the work performed. Those portfolio facts require separate
sources and remain separately attributed.
