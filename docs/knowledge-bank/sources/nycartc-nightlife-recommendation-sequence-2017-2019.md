# NYC Artist Coalition Nightlife Recommendation Sequence

A protected 2017-2019 sequence of recommendations, letters, testimony, and
priority notes repeatedly translates cultural-space experience into concrete
agency proposals. The record includes confidential support, regulatory
navigation, M.A.R.C.H. transparency, public-space access, lease and landlord
mechanisms, zoning, affordability, and displacement.

The sequence supports continuity and implementation-oriented prioritization.
It does not establish that Jamie authored every proposal, that every working
document was delivered, that agencies adopted every recommendation, or that
the coalition alone caused an institutional outcome.

See the [recommendation-continuity claim](../claims/nycartc-nightlife-recommendation-continuity.md).
