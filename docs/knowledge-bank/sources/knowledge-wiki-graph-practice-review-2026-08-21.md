# Knowledge Wiki Graph practice ecosystem review

This public-safe record summarizes a read-only August 21, 2026, close reading
of the current portfolio projection, public-record edition, issue-source
edition, governed research-production system, graph-packet compiler, protected
subject-knowledge system, project-specific public threshold, and the earlier
Noting.us prototype.

The shared architecture is not one synchronized database. Each project keeps a
universe of materials, sources, meanings, decisions, evaluations, gaps, and
human authorities. Stable identities and explicit handoffs allow selected
knowledge to travel without making one repository canonical for every purpose.

## Generalized operating pattern

1. **Source graph** preserves what material exists, where authority resides,
   and what may be inspected, retained, or disclosed.
2. **Evidence graph** records what a source supports, complicates, contradicts, or
   leaves unresolved.
3. **Semantic graph** connects projects, people, decisions, claims,
   capabilities, methods, corrections, and open questions.
4. **Projection** composes eligible material for one recipient and purpose,
   subject to rights, consent, credit, editorial, and release review.

Deterministic evaluations guard structure, provenance, privacy, freshness, and
candidate identity before interpretive or human review. They reduce needless
review work; they do not decide truth, usefulness, consent, rights, publication,
or whether a person should be hired.

## Repository roles

The current federation exposes seven operating roles rather than one preferred
repository shape:

- selective portfolio projection;
- Jamie-centered public-source orientation;
- issue-specific canonical public source bodies;
- governed archival research and delivery packages;
- deterministic graph-neighborhood materialization;
- protected subject knowledge and source lineage; and
- project-specific public experience.

Noting.us is best understood as a predecessor prototype. It established a trust
loop from source artifact to stable segment, Markdown bundle, evaluations,
human review, and export. The current practice extends that question across
specialized repositories and project-specific projections; it does not claim
that Noting.us was a production deployment or that the later system is merely a
rename.

## Public representation decision

The portfolio should reveal the practice without becoming an operator manual:

- the colophon names the Knowledge Wiki Graph as the system behind the site;
- the Source-Backed Team Memory lab page explains the three graphs, the
  Noting.us lineage, and the projection model;
- the internal Knowledge Wiki retains the deeper repository map, evidence,
  correction history, and authority boundaries.

Protected repository names, local paths, source bodies, account state,
credentials, private records, and unpublished artifacts remain outside the
public surface. RFC 0005 remains `exploring`; this review does not advance its
stage or authorize a migration, source read, merge, deployment, or indexing.
