# NYC Civic Engagement Commission - mandate and governance

## Sources and dated reading

- [CEC About](https://www.nyc.gov/site/civicengagement/about/about.page), observed October 18, 2026: the 2018 Charter revision created the Commission; its remit includes citywide participatory budgeting, community partnerships, language access, and community-board assistance.
- [Executive Order 24](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2026/09/executive-order-no--24), September 8, 2026, section 15: the Office of Mass Engagement maintains liaison with and reviews CEC's activities. Section 16 separately identifies OTI's CTO reporting to the Deputy Mayor for Operations.
- [NYC Public Schools Education Councils](https://www.schools.nyc.gov/get-involved/families/education-councils), observed October 18, 2026: Community Education Councils are school-governance bodies, distinct from the Civic Engagement Commission.
