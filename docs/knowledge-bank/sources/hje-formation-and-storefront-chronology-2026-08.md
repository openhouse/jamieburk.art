# Thick Arts formation and Harry J. Epstein storefront chronology

## Finding

Jamie dates his Harry J. Epstein Company client relationship from 2009 through
2015. He identifies the engagement as work that began before he formally
organized Thick Arts LLC and then became the LLC's first client relationship.
That relationship and first-client status are Jamie's authorized first-party
professional account.

The New York State Department of State's Active Corporations dataset
independently records **Thick Arts LLC**, DOS ID **4267566**, as a New York
domestic limited liability company with an initial filing date of **July 6,
2012**, in Kings County. The record does not identify the LLC's members or
clients.

Archive.org supplies a separate technical chronology for the historic commerce
host:

- [January 1, 2010](https://web.archive.org/web/20100101090939/http://store.harryepstein.com/): the host redirects into its `merchant2` commerce application;
- [March 3, 2010](https://web.archive.org/web/20100303123533/http://store.harryepstein.com/): a directly captured storefront homepage;
- [July 3, 2015](https://web.archive.org/web/20150703004948/http://store.harryepstein.com/): a functioning storefront with catalog navigation, search, products, prices, account and checkout routes, and current publishing;
- [August 1, 2015](https://web.archive.org/web/20150801160416/http://store.harryepstein.com/): the host redirects to the successor `www.harryepstein.com` site.

## Boundaries

- Do not describe Harry J. Epstein Company as a Thick Arts LLC client before
  July 6, 2012; describe it as Jamie's existing client engagement that became
  the LLC's first client relationship after formation.
- The official entity record establishes formation, entity type, jurisdiction,
  county, and DOS ID. It does not establish membership, clients, contracts,
  invoices, or Jamie's component-level work.
- Archive.org captures provide observation bounds, not exact launch, contract,
  billing, or decommission dates and not proof of continuous uptime.
- The live successor site provides current company context. It is not evidence
  of Jamie's present maintenance or a current client relationship.
- Preserve shared credit for the Sackin family's authority and product
  knowledge, staff labor, photography and content work, customer relationships,
  vendors, and later maintainers.
