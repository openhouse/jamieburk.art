# Collaborative MARCH Open-Data Design Notes

A protected collaborative working document explores machine-readable M.A.R.C.H.
reporting, links to frequently updated public datasets, possible preventive
alerts, and the risk that selective or culturally biased alerting could harm
the communities the work was intended to support.

The notes establish a technically significant design lead and an ethical risk
analysis. They do not allocate the complete passage to Jamie, verify adjacent
campaign statistics, or establish that a predictive system was implemented,
validated, safe, or used.

See the [held data-design lead](../claims/nycartc-march-data-design-lead.md).
