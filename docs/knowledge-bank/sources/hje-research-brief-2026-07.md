# Harry J. Epstein Company archival research brief, July 2026

The working brief maps public company history, archived websites, reporting,
and Jamie's firsthand implementation account. It is useful because it clearly
separates independently documented facts from propositions that still need
code, analytics, contracts, correspondence, or collaborator corroboration.

The governed update records Jamie's first-party account that his HJE work began
in 2009; that he formalized the practice as Thick Arts LLC during the
engagement, with HJE as the LLC's first client; and that the relationship
continued through 2015. New York State's official active-corporations dataset
independently records the LLC's initial filing on July 6, 2012. A separate
public Wayback chronology establishes the storefront observation bounds:
January 2010, a functioning capture through July 2015, and a successor-site
redirect by August 2015.

The private brief and its protected visual leads remain outside this public
repository. Public reporting about the company does not automatically establish
Jamie's component-level role or causal contribution to business outcomes.
