# Benepass official team page - Jaclyn Chen

Benepass identifies Jaclyn Chen as its CEO and co-founder. The role posting and
company materials frame the company mission as making benefits easy and helping
companies rethink how they care for their people.

This supports company-level vision context. It does not establish that Chen is
the direct manager or a participant in this hiring process.

See the [official team page](https://getbenepass.com/team/jaclyn-chen).
