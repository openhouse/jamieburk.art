## Role substance

The posting asks the CSO to help more than 100 outreach staff reach more New
Yorkers and connect them more effectively with City services. It emphasizes:

- setting and socializing clearer outreach goals;
- creating continuous-improvement loops;
- capturing and interpreting outcome data;
- synthesizing staff knowledge with quantitative evidence;
- managing complex, fast-moving operations;
- building relationships across City Hall and agency partners; and
- managing staff with varied skill sets.

The minimum qualification is a relevant bachelor's degree plus four years of
specified management, analysis, operations-research, systems, financial, cost,
or policy/program research, design, or evaluation experience, including at
least one year in a responsible administrative or managerial capacity; an
equivalent education-and-experience route is also listed.

The preferred section asks for ten or more years in government, community
organizing, issue advocacy, or labor, plus staff-management experience. NYC
must decide whether Jamie's record satisfies the formal title route.
