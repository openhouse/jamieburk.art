# NYC Public Engagement Unit - About PEU

The official agency page says PEU was created to develop a government-outreach
model grounded in community-organizing principles. Instead of requiring people
to navigate City bureaucracy on their own, PEU reaches residents at their
doors, by phone, through social media, and in their communities, then combines
that outreach with case management and service navigation.

The page describes the Tenant Support Unit and a Special Projects Team that
uses interagency resources, City staff volunteers, technology, and field
outreach for urgent public-service priorities. It establishes PEU's public
mission and operating model. It does not establish the duties, authority,
staffing, or hiring outcome of a particular job beyond what an exact posting
states.
