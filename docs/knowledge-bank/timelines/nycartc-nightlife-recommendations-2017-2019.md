# NYC Artist Coalition Nightlife Recommendations, 2017-2019

This is a bounded recommendation timeline, not a complete campaign chronology.
It makes visible a recurring translation from cultural-space experience into
agency-facing proposals.

## January 2017

A delivered coalition letter addressed to DCLA Commissioner Tom Finkelpearl
argued for citywide work to increase the safety of independent cultural spaces
without displacing vulnerable community members. The native document was
re-opened on July 19, 2026 and retains a collective "we" frame.

## 2017-2018

The protected recommendation sequence continues through letters, testimony,
and Office of Nightlife materials concerning confidential support, regulatory
navigation, MARCH transparency, affordability, and displacement. Not every
document in this interval was re-opened during the present encounter, so this
page relies on the governed source record rather than reconstructing a complete
month-by-month account.

## March 2019

A native priority sheet headed "NYC ARTIST COALITION" was visually inspected
on July 19, 2026. It prioritized actions within the Office of Nightlife's
practical reach, including cultural liaisons, transparency about MARCH
operations and targeting, a faster Department of Buildings review path, and a
pool of pre-approved professionals able to work with cultural spaces.

## Boundary

The sequence supports continuity and implementation-oriented prioritization.
It does not establish that Jamie authored every proposal, that every working
document was delivered, that an agency adopted every recommendation, or that
the coalition alone caused any institutional outcome.
