# Jamie Burkart Practice Timeline, 2004-Present

| Period | Practice and evidence-bearing work | Durable pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2004-2006 | Social software, network analysis, interactive media, media archaeology, installations | Finds social structure in technical and cultural material; makes it experiential and discussable |
| 2006-2011 | Open House, waterways programs, raft expedition, NTER CHNG | Builds invitations, shared spaces, journeys, and interfaces through which people can participate |
| 2010-present | Harry J. Epstein Company and related small-business systems | Translates tacit operating knowledge into searchable, transactional, measurable digital infrastructure |
| 2012-2021 | Sunday Dinner, 196, WOWList | Creates recurring hospitality, participation, publishing, calendar, and continuity systems |
| 2016 | CallNYC | Turns public administrative data into resident-facing issue pathways while preserving unofficial status |
| 2017-present | NYC Artist Coalition and campaign family | Connects public identity, participation, testimony, source memory, campaign websites, and government interface |
| 2017-2021 | KC Town Hall and East Kansas City neighborhood practice | Joins field implementation, specialist coordination, public funding context, and neighborhood-led visioning |
| 2025-present | Commercial Rent Stabilization memory, public-data proposals, Source-Backed Team Memory | Makes source lineage, decisions, open questions, privacy boundaries, and handoffs usable for teams |
