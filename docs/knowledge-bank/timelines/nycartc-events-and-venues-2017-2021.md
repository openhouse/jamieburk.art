Facebook control into a complete institutional history. The source-centered

## What The Sequence Supports

- Twelve recovered records document recurring coalition meetings: ten in ten
  named physical cultural spaces and two virtual meetings.
- The wider sequence joins practical safety work, listening, issue formation,
  campaign action, hearings, rallies, venue support, and relief coordination.
- Event descriptions created public routes into testimony, calls, study
  sessions, campaigns, hearings, and working groups.
- Jamie describes helping establish and produce this recurring participation
  layer. The event archive establishes the collective system, not his exact
  responsibility for every event.
## Boundaries And Open Slot

"Direct" means the current event card lists NYC Artist Coalition as host.
"Associated" preserves the host displayed by Facebook; association is not
silently promoted to coalition hosting. A named or scheduled public official is
not thereby confirmed as attending. Facebook response displays are not
attendance, reach, demographic, endorsement, or policy-impact measures.

The unresolved thirty-fourth control slot stays unresolved. A native Meta
export or another authenticated account record is needed before assigning it a
date, title, venue, host, or campaign.
