# Technical Operations

## Orientation

Jamie creates operating structure for complex public-facing teams, turning
emerging work into requirements, workflows, documentation, decision trails,
launch support, onboarding materials, and durable handoffs.

## What becomes usable

Teams gain a shared way to see work, surface risk, make decisions, launch with
care, onboard collaborators, and continue without requiring one person to hold
the entire system in memory.

## Evidence path

[CallNYC](../projects/callnyc.md) demonstrates public-data interpretation and
bounded civic implementation. [Source-Backed Team Memory](../methods/source-backed-team-memory.md)
makes the documentation and correction method explicit. The
[OTI opportunity lens](../opportunities/oti-technical-operations.md) maps this
practice to a specific role without turning the public portfolio into a single
application.
