# Implementation, Adoption, And Handoff

Jamie works beyond artifact production: he helps emerging structures enter use,
become maintainable, and move into other people's hands. The evidence is
strongest when each stage remains separate.

## Evidence States

- **Designed:** requirements, workflows, interfaces, plans, or operating
  materials exist.
- **Launched:** the system reached a real team or public use context.
- **Used:** people demonstrably acted through it.
- **Adopted:** a team incorporated it into recurring practice.
- **Handed off:** ownership, documentation, or operating custody transferred.
- **Sustained:** the structure remained useful after Jamie's direct work
  changed or concluded.

One state does not prove the next. Artifact existence is not adoption, public
launch is not durable transfer, and later continuity does not prove Jamie
authored every subsequent action.

## Evidence Paths

- [NYC Artist Coalition](../projects/nyc-artist-coalition.md) preserves public
  meeting, testimony, and campaign systems that collaborators inhabited, while
  shared identity remains collective.
- [KC Town Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall.md) separates completed Phase One
  work, neighborhood listening infrastructure, later municipal action, and
  project transition.
- [Sunday Dinner](../projects/sunday-dinner.md) records recurring participation
  without turning attendance records into proof of later impact.
- [Talks Not Raids](../projects/talks-not-raids.md) distinguishes a functioning
  public campaign surface from legislation and later agency change.

## Boundary

Each public use must retain the project-specific evidence, collective-credit,
rights, consent, and transition boundary. This capability page creates no new
claim of adoption, sustainability, title, or causal impact.
