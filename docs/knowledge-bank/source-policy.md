# Source Policy

The knowledge bank may summarize evidence. It must not become the evidence
dump.

## Allowed In This Repo

- public-safe summaries;
- public URLs and public project context;
- approved resume language;
- aggregate archive summaries;
- claim IDs, evidence classes, guardrails, and projection guidance;
- public-safe application and outreach drafts.

## Not Allowed In This Repo

- raw transcripts;
- private emails;
- private coalition notes;
- legal-review materials;
- private analytics;
- customer, donor, subscriber, applicant, resident, or guest rows;
- credentials or billing details;
- unapproved screenshots;
- unapproved photos;
- unapproved quotes;
- private font files;
- private path inventories.

## AI-Assisted Review

AI-assisted archival review can help find evidence trails and draft public-safe
synthesis. It is not eyewitness testimony. Public copy still needs source
discipline, human review, and collaborator-sensitive guardrails.
