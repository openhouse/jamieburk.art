# Approval Register

This register tracks claims or assets that need Jamie or collaborator review
before they become stronger public copy.

## Current Rule

Ready and careful claims may appear on the website only within their guardrails.
Pending or private claims must stay out of public projection.

## Items To Watch

- Collaborator-sensitive collective work should use helped, supported,
  co-built, contributed to, or stewarded unless a collaborator approves stronger
  language.
- Metrics should be softened, attributed, or bounded when causality is shared.
- Screenshots, photos, raw notes, private documents, and quotes need explicit
  public-use approval before they enter the repo or the site.
- Press claims should have a public citation or a public-safe archive citation
  before they become visible site copy.

## Open Approval Slots

- No open public-page approval blockers are intentionally projected.
- Add future items here before strengthening a public claim beyond
  `apps/www/src/data/proofs.ts`.
