# Approval Register

This is a lightweight working register for public-site approval posture.

## Current Launch Authorization

- Public email, LinkedIn, GitHub, location, and current resume PDF are approved
  for this authorized launch branch.
- Strong proof metrics may be used with their guardrails.
- Careful claims must keep contribution, co-built, supported, or collective-work
  language where the proof bank requires it.

## Still Requires Separate Approval

- Named collaborator quotes.
- Unpublished screenshots.
- Private project artifacts.
- Photos involving people or private spaces.
- Raw transcripts, private correspondence, or internal records.
- Stronger ownership or causality claims than the proof bank permits.
