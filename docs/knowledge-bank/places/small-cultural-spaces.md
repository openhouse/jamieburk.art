# Small Cultural Spaces Where Culture Is Born

Small cultural spaces were not interchangeable containers for coalition work.
They were places where artists, workers, organizers, neighbors, public
officials, and agency staff could encounter conditions that formal civic
processes often described only abstractly.

Recurring NYC Artist Coalition meetings moved among different spaces. This
helped the coalition listen across distinct operating conditions and translate
experience into public questions, testimony, campaigns, practical support, and
government-facing recommendations.

## What the category supports

- place shaped what could be noticed and who could enter the conversation;
- cultural-space knowledge could become usable civic language without losing
  its origin entirely;
- recurring public occasions could connect local experience with elected and
  agency attention.

## Boundary

This is a place category, not a venue directory or proof that every listed
person attended, agreed, or was influenced. It does not establish that one
event caused an institutional decision. Private addresses, vulnerable
circumstances, attendance records, and unapproved images remain protected.
