# CallNYC Knowledge Bank Note

## Public Claim Boundary

CallNYC may be described as an archived, unofficial 2016 civic-data prototype
Jamie independently developed after the January 30, 2016 CouncilStat hackathon
context. It translated constituent-service records into issue-oriented public
pages and resident-facing next-step guidance.

## Support

- Civic Hall public post: supports the January 30, 2016 advertised 1-3 p.m. NYC
  Council hackathon focused on constituent services.
- Civic Hall promotional graphic: supports public event branding.
- New York City Council public post: supports the Council's description of the
  gathering as the first CouncilStat hackathon.
- Public-safe participant photo summary: supports only the Digital District
  breakout label and the visible prompt, "Help improve City Council District
  office operations."
- Politico New York archived PDF: supports public coverage of CallNYC as a site
  for exploring City Council constituent-services data.
- Public GitHub repository: supports project existence and implementation
  context.
- July 2026 Wayback CDX research run: supports the archival limitation that no
  dedicated Civic Hall event page, full agenda, breakout roster, or
  registration-form contents were recovered in that run.

## Guardrails

- Always describe CallNYC as archived and unofficial.
- Do not call it an official Council service, current city service, emergency
  guidance, legal guidance, or comprehensive civic guidance.
- Do not present Digital District as the full event title.
- Treat the 1-3 p.m. language as advertised time, not confirmed total duration.
- Treat CouncilStat case counts as imperfect operational traces, not simple
  performance measures.
- Say `not recovered`; do not assert nonexistence for the event page, agenda,
  roster, or registration materials.

## Protected Boundaries

Do not publish local archive paths, raw CDX exports, private participant photo
files, participant identities, photo metadata, private development notes,
private user data, or unverified service guidance.
