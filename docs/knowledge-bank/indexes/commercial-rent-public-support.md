# Commercial Rent Stabilization public support

This index preserves the current state-bill record and a bounded population of
public statements in support of commercial rent stabilization. It keeps
speech, official release quotation, legislative record, campaign context, and
current election status distinct.

The active state pair is [S8319 and A5568A](../sources/commercial-rent-public-support/active-state-bills-2025-2026.md),
sponsored by State Senator Julia Salazar and Assembly Member Emily Gallagher.
The official records place both bills in committee. No floor speech was
recovered in the bounded official-source review.

## Editorial boundary

The public repository does not expose authenticated transcript URLs, private
file paths, raw audio locators, raw third-party speech, or personal contact
details. Full derivatives remain available only to authorized researchers for
close reading, correction, and consent-aware selection. No speaker's policy
support is converted into an endorsement of Jamie or his portfolio.
