# Research Agenda and Held Claims

This page is a public-safe work queue, not a second claim registry. Canonical
claim, evidence, inquiry, correction, rights, and promotion authority remains in
the structured records linked from the [claims and corrections
index](claims-and-corrections.md).

## Active research families

### Attribution and collective credit

Where stronger individual-role language would change collective credit, seek
public evidence or collaborator corroboration. Do not treat shared access,
sequence, or public visibility as proof of sole authorship.

### Adoption, transfer, and durability

Distinguish what was designed, launched, used, adopted, handed off, and
sustained. Return to post-launch records before making continuity claims.

### Chronology and not-recovered material

The BetaNYC School of Data presentation, parts of the waterways chronology, and
other surviving leads remain bounded questions. Not recovered does not mean the
event or record never existed.

### Metrics and causal claims

Hold unsupported totals, causal attributions, reach claims, and policy-impact
language until the denominator, source position, and competing explanations are
clear.

### Visual evidence and rights

An image may support research while remaining unavailable for public display.
Use the [Visual Evidence and Rights Queue](visual-evidence-and-rights-queue.md)
to keep evidence, identity, rights, consent, and publication separate.

## Required shape for an open question

Every maintained question should preserve:

1. the proposition or uncertainty;
2. current supporting and limiting evidence;
3. the anti-claim or stronger wording being held;
4. the next source class or review action;
5. the public-use boundary;
6. the date for returning to the source family.

Run `npm run wiki:tasks` for the current wanted-page, source-return, and
personal-librarian queues.
