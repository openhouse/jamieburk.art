# Research Agenda and Held Claims

This is a public-safe work queue, not a second claim registry. Canonical claim,
evidence, inquiry, correction, rights, and projection authority remains with
the governed record that owns it.

## Maturity states

| State | Editorial meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Fragment | Preserve the lead and its provenance; do not infer a claim. |
| Inquiry | Name the proposition, missing evidence, anti-claim, and next source. |
| Bounded claim | Source support and limits are explicit. |
| Corroboration needed | Stronger role or collective-credit wording requires another authority. |
| Rights or consent hold | Evidence may inform research but cannot be displayed or attributed as proposed. |
| Mature but unselected | Defensible knowledge exists but is not needed for the present public argument. |
| Projected | A separate editorial decision has selected reviewed wording for a named surface. |

Claim maturity is not publication permission.

## Active research families

- **Attribution and collective credit:** seek small, artifact-specific proof
  before strengthening individual-role language.
- **Adoption, transfer, and durability:** distinguish designed, launched, used,
  adopted, handed off, and sustained.
- **Chronology and not-recovered material:** a bounded failure to recover a
  record never establishes nonexistence.
- **Metrics and causality:** hold totals, reach, conversion, and policy-impact
  language until denominator, source position, and competing explanations are
  clear.
- **Visual evidence and rights:** keep evidentiary value separate from identity,
  copyright, consent, context, and display permission.
- **Protected absence:** do not create a research task merely to expose private
  context that is unnecessary to the professional account.

## Required shape for an open question

Every maintained question should preserve the proposition, current support,
limiting evidence, stronger wording being held, next source or human authority,
public-use boundary, and return date. Use `npm run wiki:tasks` to retrieve the
current wanted-page and librarian queues.
