# Scenes Of The Work

This page preserves concrete, source-bounded scenes that can later help an
editor compose Jamie as an actor. It is not public portfolio copy, a visual
specimen, or permission to reproduce photographs of participants.

## Tending An Inhabited Gallery, 2006

Good Times described Jamie receiving access to the Porter Bridge Gallery,
remaining present while it became a ten-day communal experiment, and helping
participants negotiate shared responsibility, documentation, safety, and the
meaning of the space. The article records Jamie rejecting a sole-leader frame.
It supports instigation and continuous tending, not control of every decision
or action.

## Serving As Active Accommodator, 2009

Charlotte Street's Great Accommodations program described Jamie as the planned
gallery attendant, active accommodator, and facilitator within an installation
made from river stories, public programs, interactive systems, and recycled
materials. This supports the intended role and program design. A program page
does not prove that every planned activity occurred or establish audience
effects.

## Building A Path From Meeting To Action, 2017-2019

The coalition's event and working-archive records show public meetings that
joined venue knowledge, practical safety, testimony preparation, public
officials, campaign routes, and hearings. Jamie describes helping establish
and produce this recurring participation layer. The source scene is collective:
it does not make him the author, facilitator, or producer of every event.

## Coordinating From Basement To Roof, 2018-2019

Jamie remembers arriving first at KC Town Hall with measured drawings and
survey cards, coordinating specialized teams across masonry, roofing,
carpentry, welding, engineering, architecture, and plumbing. The protected
project packet corroborates the multi-trade Phase One scope and neighborhood
survey artifact, but not the general-contractor title or every task allocation.
This scene remains attributed, internal-only, and pending contracts, permits,
correspondence, or collaborator confirmation.

## Present Source Encounter

On July 19, 2026, the Good Times Open House feature and Charlotte Street Great
Accommodations program page were reopened in their public representations; the
authenticated NYC Artist Coalition event surface was also revisited. The KC
Town Hall scene remains governed by its protected packet review and Jamie's
first-person account rather than a newly recovered public source.

Each future use should retain the scene's evidence label. A vivid first-person
memory does not become independently corroborated because it is specific.
Photographs require a separate creator-credit, subject-consent, rights, and
editorial decision.
