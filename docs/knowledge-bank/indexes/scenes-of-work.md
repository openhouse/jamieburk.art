# Scenes of Work

These are source-backed scene seeds, not reconstructed dialogue or fictional
interiority. They make labor visible while preserving the limit of each record.

## KC Town Hall, basement to roof

Jamie arrived with measured drawings, survey cards, and the day's coordination
questions, moving between trades and schedule dependencies across the building.
The governed record supports daily on-site leadership and multidisciplinary
coordination; it does not establish a contractor license or personal
performance of every trade. See [KC Town Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall.md).

## Neighborhood field route

The monthly tire route joined direct service, printed neighborhood information,
survey intake, counting, delivery, and conversations with city staff. The
record supports Jamie's initial operating role and integrated field practice;
it does not establish later program operations, independently audited totals,
or every participant's account.

## Translating the tool store

At [Harry J. Epstein](../projects/harry-j-epstein.md), paper, phone, catalog,
dealer, surplus, and customer-service knowledge had to become searchable,
purchasable, maintainable web structure without losing the store's voice. The
scene supports long-term translation and stewardship, not sole responsibility
for every technical layer or business result.

## Publishing a public action pathway

Across [NYC Artist Coalition](../organizations/nyc-artist-coalition.md)
campaigns, legislation, testimony, meetings, sponsor status, and safety or
support resources became web pathways people could use. The scene supports
Jamie's documented identity and implementation work; the campaign voice and
policy outcomes remain collective and institutional.

## Returning to the source

In source-backed memory work, Jamie moves from transcript or record to bounded
claim, decision trail, open question, and durable handoff. The value is not the
volume of archived material but the reduction of ambiguity for the next
decision. See [Source-Backed Team Memory](../methods/source-backed-team-memory.md).
