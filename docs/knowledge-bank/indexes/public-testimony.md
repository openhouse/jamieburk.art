## Sequence

The six appearances form a public record of an evolving operating argument:

1. safety requires trust between cultural spaces and government;
2. discriminatory licensing can obstruct that trust;
3. public-process infrastructure can connect informal communities with city
   services;
4. cultural continuity is inseparable from commercial affordability;
5. enforcement needs transparency, proportionality, and problem-solving; and
6. compliance becomes more achievable when government processes are published
   as usable data.

This sequence is an editorial reading of the record, not a claim that Jamie
alone authored reforms or caused legislative outcomes.

## The many-voiced record

The [public testimony heteroglossia](public-testimony-heteroglossia.md)
preserves full attributed transcript text for 187 speakers who supported,
qualified, or materially developed aligned issues across these six hearings
and the 2021 Commercial Rent Stabilization hearing. The
[Commercial Rent Stabilization collection](commercial-rent-stabilization-testimony.md)
adds written support, sponsor records, public-official statements, and the
current resolution lineage. Jamie's records remain individually retrievable,
while the event corpus restores the surrounding civic field and makes
collective authorship harder to erase.

## Attribution boundary

These six records contain Jamie's first-person oral testimony as transcribed by
the Council's contractor. Separate coalition statements delivered by Alicia
Kazi and Olympia Kazi are collective NYC Artist Coalition testimony, not
Jamie's individual testimony, and are not reassigned here.

The records preserve official transcript errors and add clearly separated
editorial notes. Statements made in testimony remain attributed statements;
their inclusion does not independently verify every historical, quantitative,
or causal assertion.

## Completeness boundary

The inventory is complete for the six first-person appearances recovered in
the present official Council transcript search. It is not a proof that Jamie
gave no other public testimony. Written submissions, testimony outside the
Council, unavailable records, videos without transcripts, town-hall remarks,
and future public appearances remain separate research cohorts.
