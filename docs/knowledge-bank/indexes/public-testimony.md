especially legible. A short statement has to connect lived experience, public
systems, factual grounding, and an action a governing body can take.

## Supportive-speaker heteroglossia

The event-level corpus preserves attributed speakers, roles, page locators,
turn counts, and bounded stance findings for public officials, invited
witnesses, and public witnesses. It keeps unconditional support,
institutional support, issue support, and support with conditions or
amendments distinct without republishing raw third-party testimony.

This is not a consensus claim. Opposition, neutral expert testimony, unresolved
stances, presiding remarks, and Council questions remain in the controlling
official transcripts. The configured population, recorded labels, attribution
notes, locators, stance distinctions, and turn counts are also preserved in
`docs/knowledge-bank/data/hearing-heteroglossia-corpus-2026-07-28.json`.
Across the four chapters, 15 attributed speakers are explicitly marked as
public officials so that institutional speech is not silently treated as
background context.

For the full hearing population, including opposition, questions, and unresolved
positions, use the [complete official-hearing corpus](public-testimony-and-hearing-voices.md).
Raw third-party testimony and incidental personal details remain in the
protected research layer; the linked official transcripts are the public
authority for complete wording.
The [Commercial Rent Stabilization testimony index](commercial-rent-testimony.md)
also preserves the relevant bill introductions, sponsor findings, oral hearing,
and queued written-submission review.

storefront-vacancy research, and a privacy-aware public-data proposal. It is a

prepared-text version.

## Corpus boundary

The machine-readable corpus is
`docs/knowledge-bank/data/public-testimony-corpus-2026-07-28.json`. It is
complete for testimony already identified in the governed source record, not
proof that Jamie never testified elsewhere. The separate supportive-speaker
corpus is complete for the configured supportive population of these four
official transcripts. It does not claim that every speaker agreed, that support
was identical, or that a public statement proves its factual assertions.
