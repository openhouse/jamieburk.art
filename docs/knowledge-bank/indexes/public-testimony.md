# Public Testimony

This index returns Jamie's prepared public words to the Knowledge Wiki as
primary evidence. It does not treat public testimony as proof that every claim
made during advocacy was independently verified, or that Jamie alone produced
the coalition work being described.

## Supportive event voices

The companion [supportive public testimony reading copy](../data/supportive-public-testimony-corpus-2026-07.md),
backed by its [structured corpus](../data/supportive-public-testimony-corpus-2026-07.json),
preserves the full attributed contributions of every substantive speaker whose
words unambiguously supported, or supported with stated conditions, an issue
Jamie championed in the same five hearings. It contains 135 contributors, 581
speaking turns, and 96,892 words. The manually governed
[scope and classification ledger](../data/supportive-testimony-scope-2026-07.json)
also retains opposition, neutrality, unresolved positions, and fragments so
that disagreement is not made invisible.

The [research note](../research-runs/supportive-public-testimony-corpus-2026-07-28.md)
describes the population, method, and interpretive limits. The corpus is a
reading room for collective civic speech. It is not evidence that every
speaker endorsed every coalition demand, that the coalition authored their
words, or that any one participant caused a legislative outcome.

## Reading rule

Each source page:

- links to the official Council transcript;
- identifies the official transcript page range;
- preserves the full stenographic wording while normalizing line breaks;
- marks obvious transcription problems instead of silently rewriting history;
- separates Jamie's personal testimony from statements read on behalf of a
  collective; and
- remains unprojected until a separate editorial decision selects a quotation
  or claim for a named public surface.

## Companion material and open work

hearings. They remain collective records even when Jamie helped prepare,
research, design, or publish the campaign's public identity and materials.

A July 2026 Commercial Rent Stabilization speech rehearsal is not included as
delivered testimony. It remains an internal draft until the event occurs and a
public or approved record can replace it.

The [transcript-linked photographic source-return
