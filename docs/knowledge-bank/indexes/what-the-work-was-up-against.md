# What The Work Was Up Against

Projects become legible when the situation appears before the response. This
page names recurring pressures without treating residents, artists, workers,
customers, neighborhoods, or collaborators as deficits. People and places were
holders of knowledge and agency; the problem was often that an existing system
could not receive, preserve, or act on what they knew.

| Project | Situation and pressure | Bounded response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CallNYC](../projects/callnyc.md) | Public constituent-services data did not by itself give residents an issue pathway or next step. | Jamie independently interpreted records as resident-facing issue pages. Adoption is not established. |
| [NYC Artist Coalition](../projects/nyc-artist-coalition.md) | Cultural-space experience did not reliably enter institutional language or process. | Collective listening, events, campaigns, testimony, and public tools carried accounts into civic settings. Outcomes remain campaign-specific. |
| [WOW List](../projects/wowlist.md) | Cultural information was distributed across organizers, inboxes, calendars, and relationships. | A co-created calendar made publishing, discovery, and redistribution more reusable. Complete reach is not established. |
| [KC Town Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall.md) | A long-disused building required stabilization while its future remained a neighborhood question. | Restoration, site encounters, surveys, and public process were brought together. Private transition context remains protected. |
| [Water publics](../projects/water-publics.md) | Waterways connected jurisdictions and lives while everyday civic experience often obscured those relations. | Collective travel, installation, and public encounters made connection experiential. Sources do not support sole authorship. |
| [Source-backed team memory](../methods/source-backed-team-memory.md) | Decisions and provenance disappear when records remain fragmented or only in individual memory. | A governed practice separates sources, claims, contradictions, absences, and publication decisions. Evidence is not permission. |

The recurring pressure is often a failure of reception. The response is an
interface among people, records, places, and authority, not a claim to speak for
everyone involved. This comparison is interpretive: it does not turn hardship
into a portfolio asset or imply every project shared one cause.
