# What the work was up against

Projects become legible when the situation appears before the response. This
page names recurring pressures without treating residents, artists, workers,
customers, neighborhoods, or collaborators as deficits. People and places
were holders of knowledge and agency; the problem was often that an existing
system could not receive, preserve, or act on what they knew.

## Pressure before response

| Project | Situation and pressure | Bounded response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CallNYC](../projects/callnyc.md) | Official constituent-services data was public, but a raw release did not by itself give residents an issue pathway or next step. | Jamie independently interpreted the records as resident-facing issue pages. The prototype was not an official Council product and adoption is not established. |
| [NYC Artist Coalition](../organizations/nyc-artist-coalition.md) | Artists and small cultural spaces described enforcement, licensing, affordability, and representation problems that did not reliably enter institutional language. | Coalition listening, events, campaigns, testimony, and digital tools helped carry those accounts into public processes. Outcomes and credit remain campaign-specific and collective. |
| [Harry J. Epstein Company](../projects/harry-j-epstein.md) | Specialized product and service knowledge lived in people, paper, store rituals, and an inherited catalog that generic modernization could flatten. | Jamie's documented work translated parts of that knowledge into searchable commerce and operating workflows while preserving continuity. Component attribution still needs corroboration. |
| [WOWList](../projects/wowlist-orientation.md) | Cultural information was distributed across organizers, inboxes, calendars, and social ties; finding and sharing relevant events required repeated manual work. | Jamie and Richard developed a community calendar with followed interests, shared editing, and reusable distribution. The surviving transcript does not settle every feature decision or adoption claim. |
| [Sunday Dinner and 196](../projects/sunday-dinner-196-orientation.md) | Artists needed recurring occasions and places where relationships and work could develop outside a single transactional program. | Hosted gatherings and residency practice created situated participation. Attendance does not by itself prove representation or later impact. |
| [KC Town Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall-orientation.md) | A long-disused building required physical stabilization while its future public use remained a neighborhood question rather than a settled program. | Restoration work, site encounters, listening, and survey infrastructure were brought together. Private transition context and some field-practice details remain protected or uncorroborated. |
| [Waterways and NTER CHNG](../projects/waterways-participatory-art-orientation.md) | Waterways connected jurisdictions and lives while everyday civic experience often obscured those relations. | Collective travel, exchange, installation, and public encounters made connection experiential. Sources do not support sole authorship or every proposed endpoint. |
| [Source-Backed Team Memory](../methods/source-backed-team-memory.md) | Decisions and provenance disappear when records remain fragmented or only in the memories of people who were present. | A governed knowledge practice separates sources, claims, contradictions, protected absences, and publication decisions. Cataloguing evidence is not permission to publish it. |

## Recurring pattern

The pressure is often a failure of reception: an institution cannot yet hear a
community account, a dataset cannot yet answer a resident's question, a
business system cannot yet carry staff knowledge, or a handoff cannot yet
preserve why a workflow exists. The response is an interface among people,
records, places, and authority, not a claim to speak for everyone involved.

## Boundary

This comparison is an interpretive layer, not proof that every project shared
one cause or method. It does not turn hardship into a portfolio asset, cast a
community as broken, or imply that Jamie alone supplied the knowledge or
authority required for change.
