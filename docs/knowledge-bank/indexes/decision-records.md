# Decision Records

These pages reconstruct choices only to the resolution the sources support.
They are not retrospective success stories. A record names unobserved
alternatives rather than inventing a workshop, vote, or product process the
archive does not preserve.

## Contract

Each record identifies a period, question, actors, constraints, observed or
unobserved options, chosen course, artifacts, outcome boundary, unknowns,
anti-claims, and projection status. “Not observed” is an evidence state, not
evidence that an option was considered and rejected.

Decision records remain held from the portfolio until Jamie and relevant
collaborators or editors review wording, credit, and purpose.
