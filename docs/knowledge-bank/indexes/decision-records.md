# Decision records

These pages reconstruct choices only to the resolution the sources support.
They are not retrospective success stories. A record names unobserved
alternatives rather than inventing a workshop, vote, or product process that
the archive does not preserve.

## Contract

Each record identifies a period, question, actors, constraints, observed or
unobserved options, one chosen course, resulting artifacts, outcome boundary,
unknowns, anti-claims, and a public-projection status. “Not observed” is an
evidence state, not evidence that an option was considered and rejected.

Decision records remain held from active portfolio projection until Jamie and
relevant collaborators or editors review their wording, credit, and purpose.
