# Visual Evidence and Rights Queue

This index helps photo editors and agents find visual leads without treating
discovery as publication clearance. The canonical asset record and
[approval register](../approval-register.md) retain item-level authority.

## Current evidence families

| Evidence family | What it may make visible | Required review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CallNYC Digital District photograph](../media/digital-district-photo.md) | Product context and visible table language | Rights and consent remain uncleared; public display is held |
| [KC Town Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall.md) construction record | Measured drawings, specialist coordination, survey cards, and phase completion | Photographer, participant, site, caption, and date review |
| [NTER CHNG](../projects/nter-chng.md) installation record | Shared projection, SMS interaction, and collaborative installation | Creator credit, venue or publisher rights, participant privacy |
| [Participatory Public Systems](../projects/participatory-public-systems.md) | Raft construction, public encounter, and inhabited programs | Newspaper or photographer rights, collaborator identity, chronology |
| [Campaign Identity and Web Systems](../capabilities/campaign-identity-and-web-systems.md) | Logos, campaign relationships, public calls to action, and interface evolution | Capture provenance, authorship, current accuracy, fair contextual use |
| [Harry J. Epstein](../projects/harry-j-epstein.md) web history | Legacy-to-web translation and store-specific interaction | Archive provenance, business approval, accurate dating and captioning |

## Promotion sequence

1. Record the visual lead without importing a protected asset.
2. State the claim or scene it may support.
3. Verify identity, date, place, and caption against the source record.
4. Determine copyright, license, consent, and fair-use posture.
5. Obtain Jamie's editorial approval and any necessary collaborator approval.
6. Publish only through an approved asset path and rerun public-safety checks.

An image can remain useful research evidence while permanently held from public
display.
