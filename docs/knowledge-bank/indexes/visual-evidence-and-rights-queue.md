# Visual Evidence And Rights Queue

This index helps photo editors and agents find visual leads without treating
discovery as publication clearance. Evidentiary usefulness, identity, rights,
consent, caption accuracy, credit, and public-display permission are separate
decisions.

| Evidence family | What it may make visible | Required review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CallNYC Digital District photograph](../assets/digital-district-photo.md) | Product context and visible table language | Rights and consent remain uncleared; display is held |
| [KC Town Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall.md) | Measured drawings, specialist coordination, survey cards, and phase work | Photographer, participant, site, caption, and date review |
| [NTER CHNG](../projects/nter-chng.md) | Shared projection, texting interaction, and collaborative installation | Creator credit, venue or publisher rights, participant privacy |
| [Art, life, and waterways](../timelines/art-life-waterways-and-media-archaeology-2003-2011.md) | Inhabited galleries, raft construction, public encounter, and participatory programs | Source rights, collaborator identity, chronology, and consent |

## Promotion Sequence

1. Record the lead without importing a protected asset.
2. State the claim or scene it may support.
3. Verify identity, date, place, and caption against source records.
4. Determine copyright, license, privacy, consent, and fair-use posture.
5. Obtain Jamie's editorial approval and any necessary collaborator approval.
6. Publish only through an approved asset path and rerun public-safety checks.

An image can remain useful research evidence while permanently held from public
display. This queue clears no asset by itself.
