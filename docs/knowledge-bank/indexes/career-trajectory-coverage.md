# Career-trajectory coverage

This matrix identifies where the Wiki can already support a professional
argument and where a source or projection gap remains. Density is not expected
to be equal across every period.

| Period | Representative work | Role families and capabilities | Stakeholders and systems | Current coverage | Next responsible action |

| 2022-2026 | HJE modernization, public-data work, Source-Backed Team Memory, AI evals | technical project management, implementation, product operations, evidence governance | small business, civic teams, technical and nontechnical collaborators | Strong present-tense fit; customer adoption and quantified operations are thinner | Prioritize direct implementation, adoption, and metrics examples |

## Cross-period patterns

- Jamie repeatedly turns emerging work into requirements, pathways,
  documentation, decision trails, and handoffs.
- The artistic, civic, technical, and social practices remain connected through
  participation, memory, place, and care.
- Collective work requires explicit role wording; project achievement is not
  automatic evidence of individual authorship.
- Recent AI and source-governance work is relevant, but it should not crowd out
  long-running delivery and public-facing implementation experience.

## Known gaps

- Direct customer implementation and adoption authority is less visible than
  internal and public-facing implementation work.
- Operational metrics are unevenly sourced and should not be padded with
  unverified precision.
- Some early projects have rich archival depth but weak task-oriented entry
  points for hiring readers.
