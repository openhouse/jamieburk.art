# Campaign-Site Photography

The campaign sites are not merely sources of reusable image files. Their page,
post, title, date, and campaign context provide an initial account of what each
image was doing in public.

The complete machine-readable census contains 225 photographic entries across
NYC Artist Coalition, FairRentNYC 2019, Talks Not Raids, Let NYC Dance, and
Save NYC Spaces. Each entry has a stable ID, content checksum, occurrence
context, public URL where available, visual-review status, and explicit rights,
credit, represented-person, and portfolio-placement boundaries.

The census is a research field, not a publication queue. Exact Apple Photos
identifiers, face metadata, nearby private frames, and local coordinates remain
outside the public repository.

See the [July 28, 2026 campaign-site photography source
return](../research-runs/campaign-site-photography-census-2026-07-28.md) for
the full-population method, limits, and next librarian handoffs.
