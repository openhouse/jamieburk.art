# Visual evidence index

Visual records are evidence, editorial material, and records of other people's
presence. Those functions must remain distinct.

## Current states

### Approved public media

Project marks, screenshots, and documents already cleared for a particular
public surface may be composed into the portfolio. Approval remains specific
to the asset, context, and use.

### Metadata-only evidence

The [Digital District photograph](../assets/digital-district-photo.md) is the
current governed pilot. Its visible placard can support a bounded observation,
while rights, represented-person consent, and public display remain on hold.

### Held visual cohorts

The archive contains potential evidence for waterways work, NTER CHNG, Harry
J. Epstein Company, KC Town Hall, NYC Artist Coalition events, WOWList, Sunday
Dinner, 196 Artists Residency, and neighborhood projects. A source inventory is
not permission to display those images.

### Missing or research-needed

Priority searches include contemporaneous UCSC installation records, complete
project identity systems, construction sequences, campaign artifacts in use,
and scenes that show Jamie's work without exposing private participants.

## Photo-editor brief contract

A future brief should provide the project question, supported claim, desired
scene or artifact, known time and place, rights and consent state, protected
identities, prohibited inferences, and the exact public surface under review.

Visual discovery may suggest new research. It cannot promote a claim or clear
an image automatically.
