## Operating rules

- Moving a row does not mutate the canonical record.
- A stronger source can improve confidence without granting publication rights.
- A rights decision can permit display without proving a claim.
- A collaborator correction can narrow a claim and still represent progress.
- An inaccessible or deliberately unprocessed source may remain a durable hold.
- Jamie's exact-candidate decision controls projection and release.
