# Knowledge Wiki Family Closure

This is the editorial map for closing the frozen
`feature/knowledge-wiki-A` through `feature/knowledge-wiki-E` family. It is not
a claim that every branch artifact belongs in the product. The governing
decision is to preserve each branch's strongest contribution inside one
coherent Wiki and to leave incompatible or insufficiently reviewable material
out.

## What carries forward

- **A:** the governed page contract, graph, source-return practice, CI, and
  hiring checks;
- **B:** adversarial editorial criteria and the insight that identity systems
  can become shared operational infrastructure;
- **C:** hiring-facing attention to outcomes, adoption, and scenes of practice;
- **D:** relational synthesis, collective-authorship boundaries, and explicit
  pull-request review units;
- **E:** project lineages, interpretive translation, handoff, stewardship, and
  decision records.

The source branches remain frozen evidence of design exploration. This page
records the selected product, not a ranking of their worth.

## Closure boundary

The NYC Artist Coalition shared-folder totals cannot be merged by voting,
averaging, or choosing the largest capture. See the
[census reconciliation](../research-runs/nycac-shared-folder-census-reconciliation-2026-07-19.md).
The exact population total remains held while the stronger project and practice
knowledge developed from source readings continues to be governed on its own
evidence.

## Review path

1. Review architecture, source boundaries, and the census hold.
2. Review the project and cross-project synthesis pages.
3. Review website projections and hiring clarity.
4. Confirm the deterministic checks and mutation tests.
5. Keep rights, content approval, production approval, and the merge decision
   open until Jamie acts on them.

The exact diff-to-review-unit assignment lives in
`docs/review/wiki-A-review-units.json`. It is a navigation aid, not approval.
