# Knowledge Wiki branch-family closure

Closure means one canonical candidate can explain what it selected, what it
consolidated, what it deferred, and what it deliberately did not merge. The
frozen sibling branches remain public history; this index is the integration
decision.

## Donor map

| Donor | Distinctive strength retained | Composite destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Editorial work queues, story structure, concrete scenes, identity/web capability, and adoption-state precision | Canonical Story Bank, Research Agenda, Scenes and Places, Identity Systems, and Implementation, Adoption, and Handoff |
| B | Identity as shared infrastructure, learning through making, situated practice, and carefully held first-person stakes | Identity Systems, Learning Through Making, Scenes and Places, and What Is at Stake for Me |
| C | Concrete professional action, participation practice, claim maturity, small cultural spaces, outcomes, and deployment-minded legibility | Jamie at Work, Participation and Relational Infrastructure, Research Agenda, Small Cultural Spaces, and Outcomes, Adoption, and Afterlives |
| D | Relation semantics, decision/result separation, maintenance, afterlives, authorship, protected absence, and review discipline | Relational Infrastructure Atlas, Decisions and Operational Outcomes, Outcomes and Afterlives, and Role, Authorship, and Protected Absence |
| E | Canonical Markdown root, schema, privacy, source return, decision contracts, opportunity context, and exact-candidate evaluation | Retained as the baseline architecture and regenerated against this candidate |

## Deliberately not merged

- competing physical Wiki roots or parallel schemas;
- duplicate pages whose useful ideas have been consolidated here;
- branch-specific generated graphs, reports, screenshots, and scorecards;
- prior candidate-bound judgments presented as evidence for this candidate;
- incompatible archive totals presented as though they used one census contract;
- factual or public claims stronger than the current canonical evidence allows.
