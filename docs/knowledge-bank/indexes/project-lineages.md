# Project Lineages

A lineage is a documented inheritance, adaptation, or divergence. It is not a
claim that every later project was predetermined, that one project caused the
next, or that a varied life was secretly one inevitable career plan.

## Social software, shared space, and participation

Early work with social networks, image systems, interfaces, and inhabited
installations developed questions about how people live inside information
systems. [WOWList](../projects/wowlist.md) later made followed interests, shared
editing, and cultural discovery into a maintained community product. The
inheritance is real; classroom work, art, and public software still have
different users, obligations, and evidence of use.

## Cultural discovery, gathering, and coalition infrastructure

[WOWList](../projects/wowlist.md), [Sunday Dinner and 196](../projects/sunday-dinner.md),
and [CallNYC](../projects/callnyc.md) connected information to participation in
different ways. The [NYC Artist Coalition](../projects/nyc-artist-coalition-2017.md)
adapted recurring events, websites, public records, campaign identities, and
facilitation to collective civic work. Coalition positions and accomplishments
belong to collective actors; they cannot be derived from an earlier personal
project.

## Campaign systems and source-backed memory

[Let NYC Dance](../projects/let-nyc-dance.md), [Office of Nightlife town
halls](../projects/office-of-nightlife-town-halls.md), [Talks Not
Raids](../projects/talks-not-raids.md), [Fair Rent NYC](../projects/fair-rent-nyc.md),
and [Save NYC Spaces](../projects/save-nyc-spaces.md) produced sites, events,
testimony, press records, operating documents, and contested histories.
[Source-Backed Team Memory](../methods/source-backed-team-memory.md) adapts the
need for public legibility into provenance, contradiction, decision, and
handoff contracts. An archive can describe action; it cannot replace
participants or authorize publication.

## Continuity and stewardship

[Harry J. Epstein Company](../projects/harry-j-epstein.md), [KC Town
Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall.md), and this Wiki all make continuity visible:
inherited knowledge or infrastructure must remain usable through change. The
shared pattern is stewardship, not sameness.

Read every edge in both directions: what carried forward, what changed, and
what did not transfer. Chronology and resemblance are prompts for research,
not proof of causation, intent, ownership, or inevitability.
