# Project Lineages

A lineage is a documented inheritance, adaptation, or divergence. It is not a
claim that every later project was predetermined, that one project caused the
next, or that a varied life was secretly one inevitable career plan.

## Social Software, Shared Space, And Public Participation

[Open House](../projects/open-house.md), [NTER CHNG](../projects/nter-chng.md),
and [WOW List](../projects/wowlist.md) show distinct ways of making social and
informational structures inhabitable. **Inheritance:** attention to how people
enter and shape systems. **Adaptation:** from installations and situated
experiments to a maintained cultural-information product. **Divergence:** art,
classroom work, and public products have different users, ownership, and proof
of use.

## Cultural Discovery, Gathering, And Coalition Infrastructure

[WOW List](../projects/wowlist.md), [Sunday Dinner](../projects/sunday-dinner.md),
and later [NYC Artist Coalition](../projects/nyc-artist-coalition.md) work each
connect information to presence differently. **Inheritance:** cultural
discovery and being there as conditions for relationship. **Adaptation:** event
and product practice into listening, advocacy, and campaign operations.
**Divergence:** coalition positions and accomplishments belong to collective
actors and cannot be derived from a prior personal project.

## Campaign Systems And Source-Backed Memory

[Let NYC Dance](../projects/let-nyc-dance.md), [Talks Not
Raids](../projects/talks-not-raids.md), and the [Office of Nightlife town
hall](../events/office-of-nightlife-town-hall-2017.md) generated public and
protected records whose histories remain contested and collective. The
[source-backed team memory method](../methods/source-backed-team-memory.md)
adapts a concern for public legibility into provenance, contradiction,
decision, and handoff contracts.

## Reading Rule

Read every edge in both directions: what carried forward, what changed, and
what did not transfer. Chronology and resemblance are prompts for research, not
proof of causation, intent, ownership, or inevitability.
