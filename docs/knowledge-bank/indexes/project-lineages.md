# Project lineages

A lineage is a documented inheritance, adaptation, or divergence. It is not a
claim that every later project was predetermined, that one project caused the
next, or that a varied life was secretly one inevitable career plan.

## Social software, shared space, and public participation

The [UCSC evaluations](../sources/ucsc-narrative-evaluations-2004-2006.md)
document early work with social networks, image systems, and interfaces. The
[Open House report](../sources/good-times-open-house-2006-06-28.md) shows those
questions becoming an inhabited gallery experiment with communal
responsibility. [WOWList](../projects/wowlist-orientation.md) later made
followed interests, shared editing, and cultural discovery into a networked
product.

**Inheritance:** interest in how people inhabit information systems.
**Adaptation:** from prototypes and installations to a maintained community
calendar. **Divergence:** an art or classroom experiment and a public product
have different users, obligations, ownership, and evidence of use.

## Cultural discovery, gathering, and coalition infrastructure

[WOWList](../projects/wowlist-orientation.md), [Sunday Dinner and
196](../projects/sunday-dinner-196-orientation.md), and
[CallNYC](../projects/callnyc.md) each connected information to participation
in a different way. Coalition work then used recurring meetings, events,
campaign identities, websites, and public records to support collective civic
action through the [NYC Artist Coalition](../organizations/nyc-artist-coalition.md).

**Inheritance:** cultural discovery and “being there” as conditions for
relationship. **Adaptation:** event and software practice into listening,
advocacy, and campaign operations. **Divergence:** the coalition's positions
and accomplishments belong to collective actors and cannot be derived from a
prior personal project.

## Campaign systems and source-backed memory

[Let NYC Dance](../projects/let-nyc-dance.md), [Office of Nightlife town
halls](../projects/office-of-nightlife-town-halls.md), [Talks Not
Raids](../projects/talks-not-raids-orientation.md), [Fair Rent
NYC](../projects/fair-rent-nyc.md), and [Save NYC
Spaces](../projects/save-nyc-spaces.md) produced sites, events, testimony,
press records, operational documents, and contested histories. The
[source-backed team memory method](../methods/source-backed-team-memory.md)
adapts that concern for public legibility into provenance, contradiction,
decision, and handoff contracts.

**Inheritance:** a need to keep public work intelligible over time.
**Adaptation:** campaign archives into governed records. **Divergence:** an
archive can describe action but cannot replace participants, authorize a
claim, or decide what should be published.

## Continuity and stewardship

[Harry J. Epstein Company](../projects/harry-j-epstein.md), [KC Town
Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall-orientation.md), and the Knowledge Wiki all make
continuity visible: inherited knowledge or infrastructure must remain usable
through change. The shared pattern is stewardship, not sameness; a store, a
building, and a public knowledge system require different authorities and
forms of care.

## Reading rule

Read every edge in both directions: what carried forward, what changed, and
what did not transfer. Chronology and resemblance are prompts for research,
not proof of causation, intent, ownership, or inevitability.
