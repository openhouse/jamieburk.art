# Canonical story bank

Each story preserves five parts: **situation, Jamie's action, what became
usable, collective context, and honest limit**. This is an editorial selection
layer, not a replacement for canonical claims, evidence, or project pages.

## Translating a legacy business into a working web system

Harry J. Epstein Company carried deep paper, phone, catalog, inventory, and
customer knowledge. Jamie helped translate that material into e-commerce,
content, analytics, and operating workflows that remained recognizably the
store's. The work was embedded in a multigenerational business and contributed
to growth; it does not make Jamie solely responsible for every implementation
layer or outcome.

## Giving a coalition a durable public interface

An emerging artist coalition needed a recognizable identity and repeatable ways
to explain issues and invite action. Jamie co-founded the coalition and built
identity, web, campaign, data, and public-action systems that collaborators used
over time. Organizing voice, account activity, and policy accomplishments
remain collective.

## Coordinating construction with neighborhood knowledge

KC Town Hall needed both a completed stabilization phase and a way for local
knowledge to shape the place. Jamie coordinated specialist teams as the daily
Phase One construction lead and built a survey-to-contact workflow. Phase One
reached cold-shell completion; later funding and transition events remain
separate, and formal contractor classification is not claimed.

## Building distributed cultural information infrastructure

Artists and organizers needed a reusable way to publish and redistribute event
information. Jamie co-created WOWList's platform and operating system, which
supported use across multiple city ecosystems. The record supports approximate
distributed adoption, not official chapters, complete reach, or sole
authorship.

## Turning records into team memory

Complex teams can possess abundant records while still lacking a shared account
of decisions and open questions. Jamie's source-backed method turns source
material into bounded claims, requirements, decision trails, and handoffs. A
proposal or pilot specification demonstrates method and judgment; it does not
prove adoption or production-scale use.

## Editorial rule

Tailor the selection, not the facts. Return to the linked governed record before
changing role, result, scale, causality, transition language, or public surface.
