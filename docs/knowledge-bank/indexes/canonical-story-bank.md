# Canonical Story Bank

Each story preserves five parts: **situation, Jamie's supported action, what
became usable, collective context, and honest limit**. This is an editorial
selection layer, not a replacement for canonical claims or evidence.

## Giving A Coalition A Durable Public Interface

An emerging artist coalition needed a recognizable identity and repeatable
ways to explain issues and invite action. Public reporting and bounded archive
records support Jamie's founding and early organizing role and his contribution
to campaign and participation systems. Organizing voice, account activity,
policy outcomes, and later use remain collective and artifact-specific.

## Coordinating Construction With Neighborhood Knowledge

KC Town Hall needed both physical stabilization and ways for neighborhood
knowledge to shape a future place. Records support Jamie's daily Phase One
coordination and survey-system work. They do not turn later Council action into
Jamie's action or settle every title, interaction, and transition detail.

## Building Distributed Cultural Information Infrastructure

WOW List joined reusable publishing, followed interests, shared editing, and
cultural discovery. The record supports a co-created product and recurring
operating practice. It does not establish official chapters, complete reach,
or sole administration across every channel and period.

## Turning Records Into Team Memory

Complex teams can possess abundant records while lacking a shared account of
decisions, contradictions, and open questions. Jamie's source-backed method
turns material into bounded claims, requirements, decision trails, and
handoffs. A proposal or specification demonstrates method and judgment; it does
not prove adoption or production-scale use.

## Editorial Rule

Tailor the selection, not the facts. Return to the linked governed record before
changing role, result, scale, causality, adoption, or transition language.
