# Canonical Story Bank

Each story preserves five parts: **situation, Jamie's action, what became
usable, collective context, and honest limit**. This is a composition layer,
not a replacement for canonical claims or evidence.

## Translating a legacy business

Harry J. Epstein carried deep paper, phone, catalog, inventory, and customer
knowledge. Jamie translated that material into commerce, content, analytics,
and operating workflows that remained recognizably the store's. The work
contributed to growth inside a multigenerational business; it does not make
Jamie solely responsible for every implementation layer or result.

## Giving collective work a public interface

An emerging artist coalition needed recognizable identity and repeatable ways
to explain issues and invite action. Jamie helped establish the coalition and
built documented identity, website, campaign, data, and public-action systems.
Organizing voice, shared-account activity, and policy accomplishments remain
collective and institutional.

## Coordinating construction with neighborhood knowledge

KC Town Hall required both a completed stabilization phase and a way for local
knowledge to shape the place. The record supports Jamie's daily Phase One
coordination and survey-to-contact workflow. Later funding, handoff, and formal
contractor classification remain separate evidence questions.

## Building distributed cultural information infrastructure

WOW List gave artists and organizers reusable ways to publish and redistribute
event information. The record supports a co-created platform and operating
system used across multiple city ecosystems, not official chapters, complete
reach, or sole authorship.

## Turning records into team memory

Source-backed memory turns records into bounded claims, requirements, decision
trails, and handoffs. A proposal, prototype, or governed Wiki demonstrates
method and judgment; it does not by itself prove client adoption or
production-scale use.

## Editorial rule

Tailor the selection, not the facts. Return to the governed project and source
before changing role, result, scale, causality, chronology, or transition
language.
