# Relational infrastructure atlas

This is a human-readable guide to relationships represented in the Knowledge
Wiki. It is not a second graph, a private relationship map, or a claim that
every meaningful relationship can be reduced to an edge.

## Relations that carry meaning

| Relation | What it can establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project uses method | A documented practice appears in a project record | Jamie invented or applied the method alone or identically everywhere |
| Claim uses source | A bounded source relationship has been reviewed | The source supports every nearby sentence or grants publication rights |
| Event is located at place | A public record associates an occasion with a site | Attendance, endorsement, ownership, safety, or policy effect |
| Project supports capability | A project offers evidence for a professional reading path | A retroactive formal title or exclusive responsibility |
| Project resulted in outcome | A documented sequence connects work and result at the stated level | Sole causality, complete labor allocation, or unmeasured impact |
| Record projected to surface | Editorial selection connected governed knowledge to public composition | Permission to expose protected evidence or every mature claim |

Typed proximity is not causality, attendance, authorship, endorsement, or
measured impact. A backlink records a declared relationship, not a verdict.

## Four routes

### Occasion to civic participation

The NYC Artist Coalition event, venue, and campaign records show how an
occasion could connect a cultural space, an invitation, practical support,
testimony, and a later civic route. The sequence documents available paths. It
does not prove every invitee attended or one gathering caused a policy result.

### Information to resident action

[CallNYC](../projects/callnyc.md) connects an official data release, Jamie's
independent interpretation, resident-facing issue pages, public reporting, and
bounded Council-account amplification. It does not establish Council adoption,
usage totals, or constituent outcomes.

### Fragment to governed memory

The [situated source rereading](../methods/situated-source-rereading.md) and
[source-backed team memory](../methods/source-backed-team-memory.md) methods
connect an artifact to a source record, bounded claim, anti-claim, inquiry, and
selective projection decision. Association remains visible when a claim is
held.

### Maintenance to handoff

The [maintenance and stewardship method](../methods/maintenance-handoff-and-stewardship.md)
distinguishes continued operation, repair, archival survival, responsible
retirement, administrative action, and social handoff. Those states are
related, not interchangeable.

## Public and protected graphs

This public-safe atlas does not publish a private relationship graph. It omits
contact networks, attendee identities, inferred friendships, private messages,
resident records, donor or subscriber data, and collaborator-private revision
history. Archive custody does not establish authorship, consent, or permission.

The generated graph is an index, not an argument. This page supplies a bounded
interpretation while canonical records retain exact evidence relationships and
anti-claims.
