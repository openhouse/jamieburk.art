# Relational Infrastructure Atlas

This is a human-readable guide to relationships represented in the Knowledge
Wiki. It is not a second graph, a private social map, or a claim that every
meaningful relationship can be reduced to an edge.

| Relation | What it can establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project uses method | A documented practice appears in a project record | Jamie invented it alone or applied it identically everywhere |
| Claim uses source | A bounded evidence relationship has been reviewed | The source supports every nearby sentence or grants publication rights |
| Event is located at place | A public record associates an occasion with a site | Attendance, endorsement, ownership, safety, or policy effect |
| Project supports capability | A project offers evidence for a professional reading path | A retroactive title or exclusive responsibility |
| Project resulted in outcome | A documented sequence connects work and result at the stated level | Sole causality, complete labor allocation, or unmeasured impact |
| Record projected to surface | Editorial selection connects governed knowledge to public composition | Permission to expose protected evidence or every mature claim |

Typed proximity is not causality, attendance, authorship, endorsement, or
measured impact. A backlink records a declared relationship, not a verdict.

## Public and protected graphs

This atlas does not publish contact networks, attendee identities, inferred
friendships, private messages, resident records, donor or subscriber data, or
collaborator-private revision history. Archive custody does not establish
authorship, consent, or permission.

The generated graph is an index, not an argument. Generated outputs are rebuilt
from governed Markdown; editorial pages retain the interpretation and limits.
