# Relational Infrastructure Atlas

This is a human-readable guide to relationships already represented in the
Knowledge Wiki. It is not a second graph, a private relationship map, or a
claim that every meaningful relationship can be reduced to an edge.

The generated Wiki Graph answers structural questions: which records connect,
which relation type is asserted, and which records point back. This atlas asks
the editorial question: what kind of relationship is safe and useful to infer?

## Relations That Carry Meaning

| Relation | What it can establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project uses method | A documented practice or operating pattern appears in a project record. | That Jamie invented the method alone or applied it identically everywhere. |
| Claim uses source | A bounded source relationship has been reviewed. | That the source supports every nearby sentence or grants publication rights. |
| Event is located at place | A public record associates an occasion with a site or venue. | Attendance, endorsement, ownership, safety, or policy effect. |
| Project supports capability | A project offers evidence for a professional reading path. | A retroactive formal title or exclusive responsibility. |
| Project resulted in outcome | A documented sequence connects work and result at the stated level. | Sole causality, complete labor allocation, or unmeasured impact. |
| Record projected to surface | Editorial selection has connected governed knowledge to public composition. | Permission to expose protected evidence or every mature claim. |

Typed proximity is not causality, attendance, authorship, endorsement, or
measured impact. A backlink records a declared relationship, not a verdict.

## Four Routes Through The Atlas

### Occasion To Civic Participation

The [event-and-venue chronology](../timelines/nycartc-events-and-venues-2017-2021.md),
[people and places index](people-places-and-community-testimony.md), and
[public-meeting and testimony method](../methods/nycartc-public-meeting-and-testimony-participation.md)
show how an occasion could connect a cultural space, an invitation, practical
support, testimony, and a later civic route. The sequence documents available
paths. It does not prove that every invitee attended or that one gathering
caused a policy result.

### Information To Resident Action

[CallNYC](../projects/callnyc.md) connects an official data release, Jamie's
independent interpretation, resident-facing issue pages, public reporting, and
bounded Council-account amplification. The relationship supports translation
and implementation. It does not establish Council adoption, usage totals, or
constituent outcomes.

### Fragment To Governed Memory

The [source-reencounter method](../methods/source-re-encounter.md) and
[Source-Backed Team Memory](../methods/source-backed-team-memory.md) connect an
artifact to a source record, a bounded claim, an anti-claim, an inquiry, and a
selective projection decision. Source association is preserved even when the
claim remains held.

### Maintenance To Handoff

The [maintenance and care index](maintenance-and-care.md) and
[project-afterlives index](project-afterlives-and-handoffs.md) distinguish
continued operation, repair, archival survival, responsible retirement,
administrative action, and social handoff. Those states are related, but they
are not interchangeable.

## Public And Protected Graphs

This public-safe atlas does not publish a private relationship graph. It omits
contact networks, attendee identities, inferred friendships, private messages,
resident records, donor or subscriber data, and collaborator-private revision
history. Archive custody does not establish authorship, consent, or permission.

Aggregate WOW List tags and Sunday Dinner marks can reveal routing structures
without becoming counts of people, attendance, conversion, or impact. Public
event records can identify institutional roles without proving presence,
agreement, or influence.

## Machine And Editorial Layers

The generated graph is an index, not an argument. Generated outputs should be
regenerated from governed Markdown and never hand-edited. This page supplies a
bounded interpretation for human readers while the canonical claims retain
the exact evidence relationships and anti-claims.

This synthesis pass returned to governed claim records and source-reencounter
receipts. It did not reopen every underlying private artifact, clear new rights,
or settle person-level attribution.
