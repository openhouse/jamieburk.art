## Attribution Vocabulary

- **Independent:** Jamie's individual implementation is directly supported.
- **Originated:** a source credits an initiating idea, not every later task.
- **Co-created or jointly credited:** preserve every named collaborator.
- **Helped establish and produce:** meaningful contribution within a collective
  system whose complete task allocation is not recovered.
- **Supported:** bounded technical or operational contribution without organizer
  ownership.
- **Jamie states or recalls:** first-person evidence awaiting corroboration for
  stronger public use.
- **Unresolved:** do not complete the attribution from metadata, custody, or
  confidence.
## What Would Strengthen The Map

The next evidence should be small and specific: revision history for one
artifact, a contract or commit for one task, a collaborator proof note for one
role, or a public record connecting one named contribution to one outcome.
Collaborator review remains a human gate. Full archive access does not settle
authorship by itself.
