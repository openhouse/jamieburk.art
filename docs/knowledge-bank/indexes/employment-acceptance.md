# Employment Acceptance

This index helps the Wiki support a two-month employment search without turning
the public portfolio into six different application pages. The professional
frame remains **Technical Project Manager - Product Operations &
Implementation**. Opportunity records show where that frame is visible,
qualified, weak, blocked, or genuinely absent.

- [PermitFlow Product Operations Manager](../opportunities/permitflow-product-operations.md)

These are dated acceptance contexts, not endorsements or promises that a role
will remain open. Reverify official employer pages every 48 to 72 hours while a
role remains a priority. Private application status, compensation decisions,
warm paths, and protected applicant facts stay outside this public repository.

## Source-Channel Coverage

| Channel | Coverage state | Public-use boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official employer pages | Six Tier 1 roles checked July 18, 2026 | Current role facts and requirements may be summarized with a verification date. |
| Public portfolio and resume | Current branch candidate | Hiring evaluation may use only what a real reader can see. |
| Public web, press, and repositories | Project-dependent | Use through canonical source and claim records. |
| Google Drive and project archives | Bounded prior archival passes | Public-safe summaries only; originals remain outside the repository. |
| Gmail, Apple Messages, and Instagram | Private discovery channels with known coverage gaps | Never pass message bodies to the hiring evaluator or publish them as evidence. |
| Photographs and media | Rights and consent vary by asset | Metadata or bounded descriptions do not grant publication rights. |
| Collaborator confirmation | Project-dependent and incomplete | Preserve attribution, consent, dissent, and collective credit. |

“Not recovered” is not “did not happen.” “Not visible to a hiring reader” is
not “Jamie cannot do it.” The [hiring-acceptance protocol](../../qa/hiring-acceptance/README.md)
keeps those distinctions operational.
