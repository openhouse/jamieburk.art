# KC Town Hall Phase One photographic source-return cluster

The photographs are research candidates, not public assets. Their source
identifiers, locations, exact filenames, person labels, and original metadata
remain outside public Git.

Jamie has provided detailed first-person oral histories for both images. Those
memories guide research into field coordination, craft lineages, spatial
analysis, neighborhood listening, and proposal artifacts. They do not
themselves clear creator rights, represented-person consent, dates, or public
use.

The [open research inquiry](../../research-inquiries/photography/kc-town-hall-phase-one-source-return.md)
records the exact source, creator, consent, mapping, and field-corroboration
work still needed.
