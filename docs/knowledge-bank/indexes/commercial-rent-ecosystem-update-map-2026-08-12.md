## Why the source split matters

- The member Wiki knows the collaborative campaign context.
- The public-support repository should know what was actually said in public.
- The archival-research repository should hold protected provenance and exact
  custody evidence.
- The portfolio repository should know only what is defensible and usable on a
  public surface.

The same event may therefore have linked records in several repositories, but
those records are not duplicates: they answer different questions and carry
different rights, sensitivity, and authority.
