# Public testimony heteroglossia

This index preserves the duration and attribution of the public chorus around
six issues Jamie brought to the New York City Council between 2017 and 2019,
plus the 2021 Commercial Rent Stabilization hearing that extended work Jamie
continued through Fair Rent NYC. It is designed as a research reference, not as
portfolio copy and not as a claim that Jamie authored a movement.

Together, the records contain 187 included contributors and more than 736,000
characters of attributed transcript text.

## What inclusion means

Inclusion means a speaker supported, qualified, or materially developed the
issue Jamie championed in that hearing. It does not mean every speaker endorsed
the same bill, mechanism, historical account, or coalition statement.

Each event ledger distinguishes:

- `supportive`;
- `qualified-support`;
- `aligned-goal-opposes-mechanism`; and
- `aligned-goal-no-position`.

This distinction matters most in the commercial-rent record. A speaker can
substantively support protection from displacement while opposing SBJSA as
written. Their contribution is useful to the history because it describes the
actual field of argument rather than manufacturing unanimity.

## Completeness and return

The seven event records preserve every recovered transcript turn under each
included official speaker label, including answers and follow-up remarks. The
machine-readable corpus also retains every unselected parsed label so that
later source returns can revisit identity, role, scope, or position.

The current classification is an event-bounded editorial pass, not proof that
no supportive speaker was missed or that a transcript contractor attributed
every turn correctly. The official Council transcript remains the canonical
complete event record.

## Collective-credit boundary

The chorus demonstrates that these were collective civic projects involving
artists, venue operators, dancers, workers, advocates, business owners, public
officials, agency staff, neighborhood organizations, and policy specialists.
It should strengthen the account of Jamie's participation while making sole
authorship harder to imply.
