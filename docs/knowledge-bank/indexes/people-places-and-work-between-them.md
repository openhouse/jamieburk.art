# People, places, and the work between them

“Stakeholder” is too thin for many of these records. A gallery, river, store,
home, cultural venue, job site, or public chamber is not scenery around a
deliverable. Each setting changes what can be known, who can participate, and
which forms of authority become visible.

## Situated records

### Open House at Porter College

The [Open House experiment](../projects/waterways-participatory-art-orientation.md)
placed shared responsibility, institutional negotiation, and changing daily
use inside one gallery. Jamie initiated the frame; participants made the
inhabited project. The surviving article does not provide a complete roster or
make every participant's experience equivalent.

### Rivers, banks, and public encounters

[NTER CHNG](../projects/nter-chng.md) and the waterways practice treated travel
and exchange as ways to encounter relations among cities, infrastructure, and
residents. The KC Star record documents a collective raft journey in progress.
It does not make Jamie the sole author or establish every intended destination.

### The Harry J. Epstein store

At [Harry J. Epstein Company](../projects/harry-j-epstein.md), knowledge was
embodied in staff, customers, paper records, catalog language, and routines.
Digital implementation mattered because it had to carry that situated
knowledge forward, not because the existing place was deficient.

### Homes, dinners, and residency

[Sunday Dinner and 196](../projects/sunday-dinner-196-orientation.md) used
recurring hospitality as a medium through which artists and organizers could
meet. Private relationships and represented people are protected absences;
attendance records are not a directory for public extraction.

### Rotating cultural spaces

The [NYC Artist Coalition](../organizations/nyc-artist-coalition.md) met and
organized through cultural spaces whose experience informed campaign work.
The record supports a listening practice and venue-based public events, while
the decision history for rotation and cadence remains a librarian request.

### KC Town Hall and the block around it

At [KC Town Hall](../projects/kc-town-hall-orientation.md), physical work and
neighborhood encounters occurred together. The proposal supports
listening-driven intent and neighborhood guidance; private family context and
unverified survey-level details remain outside this public record.

## Collective-credit rule

Record who did what only at the resolution the sources support. Name public
institutional roles where appropriate; retain collective scope where work was
shared; and preserve protected absence where naming a person, relationship, or
location would exceed consent or public purpose.

This page is not a collaborator directory, private relationship map, or claim
that proximity equals consent, representation, influence, or endorsement.
