# Decisions, Deliverables, And Operational Outcomes

This page reduces a hiring reader's burden without compressing collective work
into a personal impact claim. For each example it separates the difficult
condition, Jamie's supported responsibility, what became usable, the wider
result, and what the evidence does not establish.

## Decision Vocabulary

Use the narrowest supported verb:

- **decided** only when Jamie held the decision right;
- **recommended** when he framed or proposed a course of action;
- **implemented** when records support direct production or delivery;
- **co-produced** or **helped establish** when the work was collective;
- **government acted** when a Board, Council, or agency took the formal action;
- **the public record shows** when the result is observable but causality is not;
- **Jamie recalls** when the role remains first-person and incompletely
  corroborated.

Government action is not Jamie's action. A delivered artifact is not an adopted
recommendation. A public outcome can coexist with unresolved causal
contribution.

## Editorial Rule

No row may collapse difficulty, responsibility, deliverable, team outcome, and
unknown into one sentence. The portfolio may select a shorter formulation, but
the underlying Wiki must retain all five layers and the relevant collective
credit. Stronger specificity is useful only when it lowers ambiguity without
inflating authority, completion, or impact.

This is an internal composition aid, not automatic public copy. Every future
projection still requires the canonical claim, citation policy, allowed
surface, and Jamie's editorial decision.
