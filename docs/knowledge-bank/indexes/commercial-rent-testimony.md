# Commercial rent stabilization testimony

This index preserves the Council record surrounding the two commercial rent
stabilization bills championed by Fair Rent NYC. It keeps introduction,
committee testimony, prime-sponsor speech, supportive voices, opposition, and
unresolved material distinct.

The 2021 [supportive-speaker index](../testimony/heteroglossia/2021-09-17-commercial-rent-stabilization-supportive-speakers.md)
accounts for 161 turns attributed to 35 public officials, invited witnesses,
and public witnesses who supported commercial rent stabilization, including
support with qualifications or amendments. The [official hearing source](../sources/public-hearing-transcripts/commercial-rent-stabilization-hearing-2021-09-17.md)
preserves access to the opposition, questions, and unresolved voices around
them.

The separate [public-support index](commercial-rent-public-support.md) follows
the issue into the active New York State bill pair and preserves public-safe
speaker and statement indexes for later public events, including Shahana
Hanif, Brad Lander, Emily Gallagher, Julia Salazar, Eon Huntley, and other
supporters. It keeps official release statements distinct from speeches and
hearing testimony.

## Written submissions

The official Council record also contains a 237-page [written-testimony
bundle](../sources/public-hearing-transcripts/commercial-rent-written-testimony-2021-09-17.md).
It is part of the declared population. Its source is preserved, but a local
full-text derivative is held until each submission is attributed and incidental
contact details are redacted. This is an active queue, not an assertion that
the oral transcript contains every submitted position.

## Editorial boundary

An introduction record is not a speech. A sponsor listing is not testimony.
Where the stated-meeting transcript does not contain bill-specific remarks by
the prime sponsor, this Wiki records the null finding rather than inventing a
statement. Presence in the same hearing does not establish collaboration with
Jamie or endorsement of his portfolio.
