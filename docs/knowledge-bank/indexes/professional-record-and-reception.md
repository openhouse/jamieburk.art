# Professional record and reception

This index makes professionally relevant accounts of Jamie easier to recover
without converting every trace into a testimonial. It distinguishes six
different propositions:

1. A **public record** establishes what an institution published.
2. An **encounter** establishes documented presence in a shared context.
3. A **relationship** requires evidence of a specific kind and duration.
4. **Reception** records what a source-bounded person or institution said or
   wrote about Jamie or his work at a particular time.
5. A **recommendation** requires the writer's present participation and agreed
   purpose.
6. An **endorsement** requires explicit attributable words and publication
   authority. It is never inferred from co-presence, employment, correspondence,
   an old evaluation, or an institutional title.

## Correction route

Corrections may change the source record, relationship category, wording,
credit, rights state, or projection decision independently. Removing a public
projection does not require deleting a valid source record, and withdrawing
permission to quote does not erase the existence of a dated encounter.
