# Places Where The Work Became Real

Place is not background in Jamie's work. A gallery, river, kitchen, cultural
space, street, neighborhood route, or construction site changes who can enter,
what can be noticed, and what kind of structure becomes possible. This is a
retrieval map, not a claim that every place served the same purpose.

## Place Families

- **Galleries and shared domestic space:** Shop Shows and Open House tested
  invitation, shared responsibility, art and life, and institutional boundary.
- **Waterways and moving public space:** collaborative travel and Great
  Accommodations treated rivers as physical routes and possible social
  connections without claiming consensus from every community.
- **Kitchens and recurring tables:** Sunday Dinner made repetition into
  infrastructure while person-level attendance remains protected.
- **Small cultural spaces:** NYC Artist Coalition meetings created civic
  interfaces across changing venues; event records do not prove attendance or
  policy influence.
- **Neighborhood routes and construction sites:** KC Town Hall joined physical
  implementation, recurring site encounters, survey work, and municipal
  process without making neighborhood testimony Jamie's property.

## Place And Person Boundary

No person becomes evidentiary texture because they appeared where Jamie worked.
Name, quote, photograph, attendance, and personal-history use each require their
own source, relevance, privacy, rights, consent, and context decision. This page
does not establish present-day access, safety, ownership, representative
community consent, or a single causal story connecting every site.
