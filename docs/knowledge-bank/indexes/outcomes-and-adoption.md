# Outcomes and Adoption

The Wiki must not use “impact” as a container for unlike things. A strong
project account separates what Jamie delivered, what another person used, what
an institution formally adopted, and what changed later.

| Layer | Evidence needed | Example question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jamie delivered | Artifact, action, role, or completion record | What did Jamie make, operate, decide, or hand off? |
| Collaborators used | Attributable use, reuse, handoff, or workflow evidence | Who used the form, and in what bounded way? |
| Institution adopted | Vote, enactment, policy, release, contract, or owner decision | Which authority adopted what, and when? |
| Conditions changed | Measured result or carefully bounded chronology | What changed afterward, and what causal link is supported? |

## Evidence states

- **Delivered:** direct completion or role evidence exists.
- **Used:** another actor's use is documented, with scope.
- **Adopted:** the authorized decision maker took a formal action.
- **Associated:** the work and later result share chronology or purpose, but
  causality is not established.
- **Emerging:** requirements, plans, prototypes, or collaboration are active
  without a completed outcome.
- **Held:** the record is insufficient, protected, or awaiting rights, credit,
  or corroboration.

## Selected distinctions

- **Let NYC Dance:** Jamie's organizing and public campaign infrastructure are
  documented; Council repeal of the Cabaret Law is collective and
  institutional, not an individual causal claim.
- **Talks Not Raids:** testimony and campaign infrastructure align with Local
  Law 220 and the City's later replacement of MARCH with CURE; the chronology
  does not prove sole causality or the end of all enforcement.
- **KC Town Hall:** Phase One delivery and neighborhood process are distinct
  from later municipal authorization, withdrawal, and unused-fund action. An
  appropriation is not receipt or completion.
- **Harry J. Epstein:** long-term modernization and workflow contribution
  occurred during a period of 2x revenue growth. Contribution language remains
  essential because private attribution data is protected.

A plan is not delivery. Delivery is not adoption. Adoption is not
implementation. A public response is not verified participation or impact.
Every advancement should name the authority and smallest evidence needed for
the next state.
