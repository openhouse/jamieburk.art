# Photography working notebook

This is a sketchpad for learning with Jamie's photographic archive. It can hold
public-safe questions, provisional constellations, bounded visual observations,
contradictions, absences, and editorial hypotheses. It cannot hold photographs,
private locators, raw metadata, face labels, or protected relationship history.

The planned rough-draft field is a provisional field of attention, not a
representative sample, final edit, complete census, or publication slate. Its
purpose is to create enough room for the archive to answer back.

## Current state

The proposed rough-draft field remains open and incomplete. A deliberately
small private technical contact is now complete: local previews were inspected,
one person-free still was placed in a one-image proof album inside Jamie's
granted Photos workspace, and its exact membership was independently verified.
The image itself remains outside Git and held from publication. The
[opening note](../research-runs/photography-notebook-opening-2026-07-22.md)
preserves what was known before contact; the
[first-contact entry](../research-runs/photography-studio-connection-2026-07-22.md)
records what changed.

## Rules of play

- Leave room for unexplained attraction, surprise, humor, awkwardness, and
  technically imperfect photographs with emotional or historical force.
- Keep sequences, repetitions, duplicates, and bursts when their relationship
  may matter.
- Treat the constellations below as prompts, not a mandatory taxonomy.
- Let one image belong to several possible stories or to none yet.
- Include ordinary life, preparation, maintenance, waiting, repair, and
  aftermath alongside recognized project moments.
- Preserve contradictions and images that resist the current portfolio story.
- Never convert access, relevance, selection, or beauty into permission.

## Provisional constellations

These are questions for looking, not quotas for selecting:

- thresholds, invitations, refusals, and moments of choice;
- containers, tools, maps, tables, rooms, vessels, and operating artifacts;
- preparation, safety, maintenance, repair, nourishment, and rest;
- polyphony, shared attention, collective agency, and people acting together;
- water, passage, place, weather, interruption, and changing conditions;
- accommodation when a project exceeds its original form;
- confluence, handoff, adoption, friendship, and afterlife;
- ordinary time, atmosphere, gesture, material, and the apparently incidental.

## Questions for the first field

- Which images make the conditions for participation perceptible?
- Where does operational production appear as active accommodation?
- Which photographs show Jamie tending a container without making him the
  protagonist of other people's lives?
- Which sequences reveal change that no single frame can establish?
- Which images complicate the present account of a project?
- What appears repeatedly across projects before we name it as a pattern?
- What important work remains absent, unphotographed, inaccessible, or held?

## Notebook entry contract

Each dated entry should include these headings, even when the answer is
"not yet known":

- **Present question**
- **Bounded source field**
- **Material access state**
- **What was looked at**
- **Emerging constellations**
- **Close visual observations**
- **Contradictions and anti-claims**
- **Protected absences**
- **Source and librarian requests**
- **Rights, consent, and safety state**
- **Editorial hypotheses**
- **Publication decision**

An entry may remain fragmentary. The headings preserve return paths and
unanswered questions; they do not require premature interpretation.

## Private working material

The exact selection manifest, source-library identifiers, filenames, paths,
checksums, technical metadata, contact sheets, face or people labels, safety
notes, and rights or consent contacts belong in a protected local layer. A
future public-safe entry may refer to an opaque field identity and aggregate
counts only when doing so is useful and non-identifying.

## Projection state

This notebook is held from portfolio projection. A mature visual observation
must re-enter the Knowledge Bank lifecycle as its own governed record. A
photograph requires separate exact-asset, caption, safety, authorship, rights,
represented-person, crop, context, and specific-surface approval.

The executable [notebook evaluation](../evaluations/photography-working-notebook.md)
protects these boundaries without determining what Jamie should find moving,
beautiful, strange, or worth returning to.
