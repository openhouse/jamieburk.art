# Outcomes, Adoption, and Afterlives

Projects do not all end in the same way. Keep these states separate:

- an artifact was delivered;
- people used it;
- a team adopted it;
- an institution acted;
- a public outcome is observable;
- a system was maintained;
- responsibility was handed off;
- an archive survives;
- a project was retired or its guidance became historical.

None of these states proves the others, and none independently measures Jamie's
causal contribution.

## Selected afterlives

| Work | Supported state | Open boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CallNYC | Archived independent prototype with surviving implementation and reporting | Archive is not a current or official Council service |
| NYC Artist Coalition | Surviving records document collective events, campaigns, and publishing | The Wiki does not declare the coalition ended or assign every later activity |
| KC Spaces Fund | Historical collaborator-led campaign with a surviving public fundraising result and Jamie's bounded digital contribution | Fundraising does not prove the website caused donations or make Jamie an organizer |
| KC Town Hall | Phase One, municipal actions, later non-disbursement, withdrawal, and unused appropriation are separately documented | Private transition circumstances remain absent; municipal disposition is not the social handoff |
| NTER CHNG | Joint public credit and later exhibition history survive | Protected production records do not allocate every task or authorize participant material |

## Maintenance and care

Physical repair, digital continuity, recurring participation, information
stewardship, governed memory, and responsible transition are different modes.
They should not collapse into one heroic story. Each retains its own actors,
period, evidence, and unknowns.

## Editorial rule

An archive is not proof of a current service. An unfinished record is not a
failed project. A handoff demonstrates stewardship only at the documented
recipient, time, and scope. A public outcome can coexist with unresolved causal
contribution.
