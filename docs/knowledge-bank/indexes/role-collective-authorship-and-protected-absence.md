# Role, Collective Authorship, and Protected Absence

This is a public-safe attribution map, not a private relationship graph or
artifact-by-artifact authorship ledger. Full archive access does not settle
authorship by itself.

| Work | Strongest current Jamie-role wording | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CallNYC | Jamie independently built and iterated the resident-facing prototype | It was not an official Council product or established hackathon deliverable |
| NYC Artist Coalition | Public reporting supports a founding role; governed records support bounded identity, technical, and participation contributions | Events, campaigns, testimony, shared accounts, and outcomes were collective |
| NTER CHNG | Public project records preserve joint creator credit | Protected plans do not allocate every software, spatial, writing, installation, or teardown task |
| Waterways raft | Contemporary reporting credits Jamie with originating the idea | Construction, travel, repair, hospitality, documentation, and performance were collective |
| KC Spaces Fund | Implementation evidence supports behind-the-scenes digital infrastructure | Public materials name other organizers; Jamie was not the stakeholder posting from the Page |
| KC Town Hall Phase One | Jamie attributes daily general-contractor and multi-trade coordination to himself; the project record corroborates the scope | Formal classification and complete labor allocation remain open; no claim of performing every trade |

## Attribution vocabulary

- **Independent:** individual implementation is directly supported.
- **Originated:** a source credits an initiating idea, not every later task.
- **Co-created or jointly credited:** preserve every named collaborator.
- **Helped establish and produce:** meaningful contribution within a collective
  system whose complete task allocation is not recovered.
- **Supported:** bounded technical or operational contribution without project
  ownership.
- **Jamie states or recalls:** first-person evidence awaiting corroboration for
  stronger public use.
- **Unresolved:** do not complete attribution from metadata, custody, sequence,
  or confidence.

## Absence and permission states

Not recovered, located but unread, protected evidence, unresolved collective
authorship, rights-blocked media, intentionally absent personal context,
attributed transition, and mature-but-unselected knowledge are different
states. Research access, repository inclusion, quotation, reproduction,
identification, attribution, and portfolio selection are separate permissions.

Protected absence is not missing work, a deficit, or an invitation to expose
private context. Improve the record by naming the smallest useful next request,
decision owner, and publication boundary.
