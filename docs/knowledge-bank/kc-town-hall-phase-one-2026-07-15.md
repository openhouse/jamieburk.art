# KC Town Hall Phase One Archival Production

Date: 2026-07-15

## Result

This pass corrects an important understatement in the portfolio. Jamie's KC
Town Hall contribution was not limited to planning support and public-benefit
documentation.

The bounded record now supports three claims:

1. Jamie served in the functional general-contractor role and as the daily
   on-site construction lead for Phase One.
2. He hired and coordinated specialist teams through completion of the
   building's cold shell in 2019.
3. He created a neighborhood survey handbill and backing contact-intake system
   that connected desired uses, offers of help, and recurring conversations to
   the developing plan.

Hiring-facing copy uses **Phase One construction lead**. The bank preserves
Jamie's more exact firsthand description, **functional general-contractor
role**, while holding any formal contractor-license, permit-holder, or legal
classification claim until those records are recovered.
