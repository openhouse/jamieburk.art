# Media Provenance

Media can support internal review without becoming public evidence.

Every media record should preserve:

- photographer or creator when known;
- rights holder when known;
- rights status;
- consent status;
- event association;
- visible evidence;
- public caption, if approved;
- archival caption, if needed privately;
- protected people;
- crop restrictions;
- publication status.

Protected or pending-rights media must not include a public URL. Local archive
paths, private filenames, and raw research locators must not be committed.

The CallNYC participant photograph remains protected. It may support internal
review of a location-label question, but it is not public evidence and should
not be rendered or linked from the site until rights, consent, crop, caption,
and attribution are approved.
