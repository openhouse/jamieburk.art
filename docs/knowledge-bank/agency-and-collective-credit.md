# Agency and collective credit

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-13

The canonical machine-readable graph lives in
`apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/agency-graph.ts`. It exists to make strong
claims without confusing individual contribution, shared production, coalition
action, public testimony, institutional authorship, or legislative enactment.

## Governing rule

Every mature contribution relation must name:

- the actor or actors;
- the bounded action;
- the object of the action;
- the purpose and usable result;
- the credit scope;
- supporting claims and public sources;
- the boundary that prevents overstatement.

Advocating, organizing, speaking, or testifying must never be silently rewritten
as authoring legislation, enacting a law, or solely causing a collective outcome.

## Intentionally unresolved

- **Institutional private motive and causal allocation:** public records show
  how DCLA, the Council, and Espinal used participation, testimony, and public
  forums. They do not reveal private motive, exclusive dependency, or the
  causal weight of any one intervention.
- **CallNYC Council-account engagement:** the memory lead is retained, but
  account identities, interaction types, date range, authenticated collection,
  deduplication, and reproducible counts remain unresolved.
- **Waterways terminus and full program inventory:** salt water and several
  public-program forms are supported; the exact route, full crew, complete
  program set, and outcomes remain under research.

An unresolved relation is not absent from the knowledge system. It remains an
inquiry until evidence supports a bounded promotion.
