## What the sources establish

`NTER CHNG` was a collaborative interactive texting installation by Drew
Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier. Public descriptions characterize
it as both software and architectural installation: gallery visitors used
their phones to communicate through a two-sided digital wall, turning a
private one-to-one medium into a public many-to-many exchange.

A contemporaneous listing places its opening at the Arts Incubator's Cocoon
Gallery on January 8, 2010. The official `America: Now and Here Kansas City`
archive later lists all three collaborators as visual artists and presents
`NTER CHNG` as their work during the May 2011 Kansas City launch.

## Source roles

The archived exhibition artist page and visual-artist index directly establish
the trio's inclusion. KC Studio establishes the wider Kansas City launch. The
Nerman Museum page establishes exhibition context and the May 11-12 Barbara
Kruger truck stop, but it does not name `NTER CHNG` or Jamie and cannot prove
that the project appeared at the Nerman stop.
