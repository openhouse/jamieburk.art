## Method

1. Used the authenticated live profile's displayed 434-post count as the
   population control.
2. Scrolled the Posts surface to its October 2008 endpoint, recovering 421
   unique records after sustained no-growth windows.
3. Independently scrolled the Replies surface, excluded two third-party records
   rendered as conversation context, and recovered 13 additional Jamie-authored
   replies absent from Posts.
4. Reconciled the union by status ID to exactly 434 current records.
5. Classified each record by year, relationship, one primary interpretive theme,
   and aggregate external-link presence.
6. Ran an authenticated public search for `@urbanhermit -from:urbanhermit` to a
   sustained no-growth endpoint and classified the 26 recoverable inbound
   records without publishing their personal or relational detail.
7. Resolved public short links where possible, then close-read only selected
   professional sources before promoting them into typed source and claim
   records.

No private messages, account analytics, cookies, credentials, session stores,
or other nonpublic account data were inspected or published.
