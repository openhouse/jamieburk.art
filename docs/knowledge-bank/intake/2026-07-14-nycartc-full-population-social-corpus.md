# NYC Artist Coalition full-population social corpus

Date: 2026-07-14
Account: [@NYCArtC](https://x.com/NYCArtC)
Disposition: integrated into the knowledge-bank lifecycle

## Why this pass exists

This pass treats the shared NYC Artist Coalition account as a public
documentation and engagement surface. Its purpose is to recover the surviving
account population, inventory public source routing, distinguish outbound
amplification from inbound interaction, and preserve strong claims without
assigning collective account activity to Jamie post by post.

The account identity names four campaign programs: Save NYC Spaces, Let NYC
Dance, Talks Not Raids, and FairRentNYC. Jamie confirms that he established the
account as shared public-facing infrastructure. The public record corroborates
its long use and changing stewardship, but does not expose the teammate behind
each post.

## Population control and method

The authenticated public profile displayed **5,124 posts** on the review date.
Recovery used three public surfaces:

1. A complete scroll of the profile Replies surface until three sustained
   no-growth windows and the oldest visible account activity.
2. A bounded authored-status search from account launch through the earliest
   date reached by the profile surface.
3. An independent Posts-surface crosscheck at a different scroll cadence.

The three passes were reconciled by status ID. Third-party posts appearing only
as conversation context were separated from the account population.

| Population disposition | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Account-authored statuses recovered | 715 |
| Reposts recovered | 2,652 |
| Unique public items recovered | 3,367 |
| Explicit unresolved profile-count slots | 1,757 |
| **Disposition total** | **5,124** |
| Third-party conversation-context records excluded | 22 |

This is **100 percent disposition of the live-profile count**, not 100 percent
item-level recovery. The 1,757 unresolved slots remain historical
profile-interface debt, likely concentrated in older reposts. They are not
described as deleted, nonexistent, or known in content. The result is not a
first-party platform export or deletion history.

The recoverable population spans February 3, 2017 through May 18, 2026. The
public item ledger lives at
`docs/knowledge-bank/data/nycartc-public-post-ledger.json`.

  post-level evidence.
