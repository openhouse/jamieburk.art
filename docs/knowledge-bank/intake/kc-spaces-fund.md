## Public-Safe Project Context

KC Spaces Fund, also described in project records as KC Arts & Culture Spaces
Mutual Aid Fund / KC Arts Spaces Mutual Aid Fund, was a 2020 Kansas City
mutual-aid fundraiser and emergency-relief effort for grassroots arts and
culture spaces during the COVID-19 shutdown period.

Public campaign materials named Caitlin Horsmon, Jordan Carr, Kendell Harbin,
and Megan Pobywajlo as organizers. Public GoFundMe text reviewed in the private
workspace described Kendell Harbin as organizer for Allied Media Projects Inc.
and reported $9,590 raised of a $9,500 goal from 107 donations.
