## Public Archive Close Reading

The [October 30, 2020 Wayback
capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20201030223311/http://kctownhall.com/tires/)
is the strongest recovered public description of the service. It names KC Town
Hall and Oak Park Neighborhood Association, limits the service to homes,
describes the recurring schedule, offers more than one intake channel and a
volunteer pathway, and explains the neighborhood-to-City recycling handoff.

The shared “Julia and Jamie” byline is meaningful authorship evidence for the
page. It is not evidence that either person individually authored every graphic,
form, operating rule, route, partnership, or pickup.

The page's $17,768 figure is explicitly retained as a project-reported savings
figure. A protected operating workbook was recovered after Jamie shared the
relevant project materials into the authenticated account, but its resident-level
rows, formulas, and derived figures are not independently audited and are not
projected here.
