# KC Spaces Fund Facebook Posts: Surviving Public Timeline

Date: 2026-07-14
Surface: [KC Spaces Fund on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/KCSpacesFund/)
Disposition: integrated with collective-credit, publication, and privacy boundaries

## What was counted

An authenticated top-to-bottom pass recovered **38 unique records** from the
currently accessible KC Spaces Fund public Page timeline. The surviving range
begins April 7, 2020, and ends July 9, 2020. Forty additional endpoint scrolls
produced no new records. Every recovered record has a row in the
[public-safe census](../data/kcspacesfund-facebook-post-census-2026-07-14.csv).

This is **100 percent of the surviving public Page timeline exposed by the
current interface**, not an official Meta export, deletion history, or proof
that every historical post or attachment survives.

## Campaign sequence

The readable half of the population records an operating sequence rather than
a press feed:

- campaign launch and donation routes;
- application deadlines, scope, and eligibility language;
- a shift to rolling emergency-relief grants;
- an expansion of the service area to Lawrence;
- mutual-aid print fundraising; and
- ten grantee-recognition posts.

The ten readable grantee records name Vulpes Bastille, SWAN, Kansas City
Textile Arts Center, Parker 2, Farewell Transmission, One Mic Stand, Blackbox
on Troost, Get Woke, UN/TUCK, and Latino Foundation for the Arts. These names
document who the campaign publicly recognized; the archive does not expose
applications, grant deliberations, payment records, or private grantee data.

## Stakeholder references

The readable messages route through a regional field of organizers, grantees,
arts intermediaries, cultural organizations, and Lawrence-area institutions.
The census records group-level reference counts without publishing a name-by-
name network or converting outgoing tags into inbound engagement.

A reference does not establish that a person or organization saw, authored,
endorsed, replied to, partnered on, funded, or acted because of a post. The
current interface did not expose a defensible stakeholder-identity census for
reactions.

## Visible reaction floor

Twenty-eight of 38 records retain at least one visible reaction. The dated
aggregate floor is **119 reactions** as observed July 14, 2026.

This is a mutable interface floor, not a historical peak. It is not 119 unique
people, reach, impressions, attendance, conversion, endorsement, causality, or
impact. Reaction identities, comments, and relationship data remain outside
the public repository.

## Jamie's role and collective credit

Jamie was **not the stakeholder or owner posting on this account**. He confirms
that his contribution was behind the scenes: supporting website creation,
digital operations, and the choice of a project name available consistently
across social and domain surfaces.

That correction is consistent with the pre-existing archival evidence for the
Ghost site, custom campaign theme, fundraising widget, launch assignment, and
domain continuity. The Page census corroborates the resulting identity and
public action routes; it does not independently assign Jamie a post or make him
the campaign voice.

Public organizer credit remains with the campaign's named organizers. The
defensible hiring claim is Jamie's bounded implementation and identity support
in service of a collaborator-led mutual-aid project.

## Protected material

The public repository does not contain raw post text, comments, reaction or
commenter identities, authentication state, administrator context, Page-
management data, public contact details copied from the Page, private Drive or
email material, applicant or grantee packets, donor or subscriber exports,
payment requests, credentials, billing records, or per-record human publisher
data.
