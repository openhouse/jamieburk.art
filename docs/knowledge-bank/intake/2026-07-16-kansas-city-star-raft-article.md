# Intake - Kansas City Star Raft Article

**Received:** 2026-07-16

**Intake ID:** `INTAKE-2026-07-16-KC-STAR-RAFT-ARTICLE`

**Project:** `waterways-participatory-art`

## Input

A two-page archival PDF scan of Darryl Levings's Kansas City Star article, "In
the name of art, go with the flow," published November 15, 2007, on pages A1
and A4.

The scan was text-extracted and both pages were rendered and visually reviewed.
It remains outside the public repository because it is a copyrighted newspaper
artifact containing photographs and published contact information that the
knowledge bank does not need to reproduce.

## Source Expansion

The review also recovered Eric Barton's contemporaneous public Pitch article,
["Artists Turned Huck Finn, Part III"](https://www.thepitchkc.com/artists-turned-huck-finn-part-iii/),
published November 12, 2007. It provides a stable public corroborating route for
the title, departure, recycled construction, 1,000-mile threshold, Coast Guard
interruption, and Vicksburg repair period.

## What The Sources Establish

- Jamie originated the idea for the expedition, while its execution was
  collaborative.
- The crew then traveling was Jamie Burkart, Libby Hendon, and Laura Mattingly.
- The approximately 12-by-13-foot craft used two bicycles linked to a
  paddlewheel and found or discarded materials.
- By mid-November, the crew had traveled more than 1,000 miles and was south of
  Baton Rouge.
- The project joined river travel to public encounter and inquiry into how
  Kansas City and other river communities relate to their waterways.
- A Coast Guard safety intervention interrupted the journey; legal assistance,
  temporary work, repair, and safety additions preceded relaunch.
- The Kansas City Star gave the project illustrated front-page coverage while
  it was still underway.

## Boundaries

- The November reports document the expedition in progress. They do not prove
  the eventual Gulf endpoint; later sources remain responsible for that fact.
- The named trio is the crew at the time of reporting, not necessarily a
  complete participant roster for every stage.
- The project is collaborative. The sources do not support sole authorship or
  sole execution by Jamie.
- Regulatory interruption and recovery should remain visible rather than being
  flattened into an uninterrupted adventure narrative.
- The scan and its photographs are not cleared for republication.
