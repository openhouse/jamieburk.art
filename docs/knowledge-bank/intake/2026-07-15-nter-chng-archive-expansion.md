# NTER CHNG Archive Expansion

**Review date:** 2026-07-15
**Intake thread:** `INTAKE-INTERACTIVE-MEDIA-PRACTICE-2026-07-14`
**Projection decision:** Knowledge-bank reserve. No website change.

## Submitted Lead

Jamie supplied the archived NTER CHNG project site and the Nerman Museum's
America: Now and Here exhibition page, with the hypothesis that the
exhibition's own archived site might document NTER CHNG.

## Recovered Source Chain

The review recovered four complementary records:

1. The [archived NTER CHNG project
   site](https://web.archive.org/web/20110128193350/http://nterchng.com/)
   describes an interactive texting installation by Drew Bolton, Jamie
   Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier and preserves its earlier Arts Incubator /
   Cocoon Gallery presentation.
2. America: Now and Here's official archived [Kansas City artist
   page](https://web.archive.org/web/20110518071626/http://kansascity.americanowandhere.org/the-visual-artists/drew-bolton-jamie-burkart-and-garrett-fuselier/)
   lists the three collaborators as visual artists and documents NTER CHNG in
   the 2011 exhibition.
3. The exhibition's first-party [I Text, Therefore I
   Am](https://web.archive.org/web/20121017090512/http://americanowandhere.org/2011/06/i-text-therefore-i-am/)
   describes visitors sending text messages and seeing the results projected
   on a floor-to-ceiling gauze display.
4. The [Nerman Museum exhibition
   page](https://www.nermanmuseum.org/exhibitions/2011-05-11-kruger-barbara-america-now-here.html)
   establishes the broader Kansas City launch and multi-venue exhibition
   context. It does not name NTER CHNG.

The linked `press_release.pdf` from the project site was not recovered through
the exact-URL or site-wide Internet Archive index review. This means "not
recovered," not "did not exist."

## Matured Claim

> America: Now and Here's official sites document NTER CHNG in its 2011 Kansas
> City exhibition and credit Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier
> together as visual artists.

This wording is stored as a governed archive-note projection in
`CLM-NTER-CHNG-AMERICA-NOW-HERE-2011`. It is reserve knowledge, not current
website copy.

## Evidence Boundaries

- Preserve Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier's shared credit.
- Preserve Mary Nichols's wall-engineering and construction contribution when
  describing the earlier project record supported by the Vimeo metadata.
- Do not infer Jamie's detailed software, fabrication, design, or production
  responsibilities from collective credit.
- The Nerman Museum page is institutional context, not direct proof of NTER
  CHNG's inclusion.
- Observed visitor use is not a measure of attendance, reach, endorsement,
  adoption, reception, or impact.
- Do not reproduce archived phone numbers, participant-submitted messages, or
  media without separate privacy and rights review.
