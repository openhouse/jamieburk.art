# Kansas City Star River-Raft Report Intake

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-16
**Disposition:** Integrated as protected-source evidence and reserve depth
**Project:** Great Accommodations / participatory public programs

## Source Metadata

Darryl Levings's report, "In the name of art, go with the flow," appeared in
The Kansas City Star on November 15, 2007, on the front page and page A4. The
reviewed source is a privately preserved, two-page newspaper PDF. Public use is
limited to bibliographic metadata and bounded paraphrase.

## Close Read

The report attributes the expedition's originating idea to Jamie Burkart. It
identifies Jamie, Libby Hendon, and Laura Mattingly as the three-person
traveling crew then on the river, while also noting that friends joined for
stretches. That is a moment-specific crew description, not a complete project
roster.

The article reports that the crew departed Kansas City's West Bottoms on July
21, 2007, after a three-week build. It describes a roughly 12-by-13-foot raft
made from discarded building materials and soda-syrup drums, with two bicycles
linked to a paddlewheel for propulsion when needed.

By publication, the crew had passed the 1,000-mile marker, resumed travel after
a 51-day interruption near Vicksburg, and was reported south of Baton Rouge.
The source frames the project as a living river experience organized around
meeting people, encountering river places, and reconsidering cultural
connections among communities along the route.

## What The Source Affords

- Contemporary independent support for Jamie's originating role.
- A dated departure, build description, propulsion method, and progress marker.
- A bounded description of the crew then traveling.
- Evidence of institutional interruption and community assistance.
- Contemporary support for the project's participatory river-and-community
  purpose.

## Boundaries

The report was published before the expedition ended. It does not establish
the final endpoint, completed arrival at salt water, final duration, complete
route, complete participant population, or every host community. It does not
establish Jamie as sole author or sole operator, and it does not establish
Coast Guard approval or endorsement.

The photograph and page-reproduction rights remain unresolved. The scan,
newspaper photographs, article body text, reporter contact details, and private
locator stay outside this public repository.

## Lifecycle Result

The source strengthens the existing expedition claim and moves the exact-route
inquiry from open to partially recovered. The evidence remains reserve depth:
it improves the knowledge bank and future editorial range without adding a new
claim to the current public hiring-site composition.
