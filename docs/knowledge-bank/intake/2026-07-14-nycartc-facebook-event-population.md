# NYC Artist Coalition Facebook Event Population Intake

**Received:** 2026-07-14

**Surface:** [NYC Artist Coalition Facebook events](https://www.facebook.com/nycartc/events)
**Disposition:** Integrated, with one unresolved control slot and held interpretive claims

## Why This Entered The Bank

Jamie identifies NYC Artist Coalition's public event layer as a major part of
his contribution: a repeatable participation system informed by WOWList,
rotated among cultural spaces, and used to listen, share practical knowledge,
build shared priorities, and connect artist concerns with civic action.

The complete event population matters beyond today's portfolio copy. Each
event can function as a historical plot point to which later reporting,
photographs, collaborator memories, campaign artifacts, and policy records may
attach.

This attributed wording combines Jamie's bounded first-person account, the complete

## Held Interpretation

Jamie describes the practice as a democracy lab: believing artists, gathering
where culture is made, translating lived experience into practical support and
shared priorities, and carrying those priorities into civic forums. The event
sequence is consistent with this interpretation. It does not prove that every
participant understood the work that way or that every campaign issue began in
an event. The language remains in the bank for later composition and research.
