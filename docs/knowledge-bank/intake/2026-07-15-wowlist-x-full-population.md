- how to add an event to multiple WOW Lists;

identity Jamie reports establishing became a direct support and onboarding

Jamie personally or measure the complete support workload.

### Distribution, scene knowledge, and product community

The corpus includes five account posts distributing events, three routing
scene knowledge, and three maintaining product-community infrastructure.
Five reposts show organizers or collaborators using WOW List pages or tagging
the account while distributing events. Together these records show the account
working as an interface among product use, local calendar identities, event
organizers, and grassroots cultural scenes.

The strongest linked scene-knowledge readings are:

- Grasstronaut's *In Every Town* guide to all-ages cultural infrastructure;
- Elise Granata's *Zines 2.0* reporting on documenting and connecting DIY

These are resources WOW List routed, not press coverage or endorsement of WOW
List.
