## What 100% means

The authenticated live page feed exposed **57 distinct dated posts** from April
25, 2015 through March 22, 2018. Repeated lazy scrolling reached the same
terminal post pair across seven consecutive checks. Every exposed post has a
public-safe ledger row.

This is **100% accounting for the capture-date authenticated live-feed
population**. It is not a native Meta export or proof of complete lifetime
history. Deleted, hidden, private, unpublished, or no-longer-retained posts can
remain outside the recovered population.

Meta Business Suite displayed an empty `Lifetime` content window beginning
March 31, 2019, after the final post recovered from the live feed. That bounded
window cannot account for the 2015-2018 population and cannot establish that no
historical posts existed.

  six route mutual aid or civic mobilization.

The strongest governed routes include an independently published Shelby Turner

resources, Meow Wolf's DIY Fund, and reporting from Denver Westword, East Bay
Express, and Willamette Week. Issue-context sources establish what the account
circulated; they are not press coverage of WOW List and do not establish WOW
List causation or impact.

## URL and traction findings

The posts contain **55 distinct cleaned routes**: 30 WOW List routes and 25
external routes. Seven resolve to governed source records. The other 48 are
explicitly `not-rechecked-in-this-pass` and `route-inventory-only`; that is a
research disposition, not a claim that a route is live, dead, or historically
nonexistent.

At capture time, Facebook displayed 87 likes across 41 rows, 16 comments across
12 rows, and 49 shares across ten rows. The October 5, 2015 nine-city calendar
introduction had the population maxima: 13 likes, three comments, and 29
shares. These are mutable interface counts, not unique people, reach,
attendance, conversion, endorsement, mandate, or impact.

research lead while leaving post-level authorship unassigned pending
