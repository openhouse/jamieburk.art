## Method

1. Verified the authenticated profile and its 434-post control.
2. Traversed Posts for 137 passes, including 14 repeated no-growth passes.
3. Traversed Replies for 141 passes, including 16 repeated no-growth passes.
4. Deduplicated primary records and separated two other-account conversation
   parents from the profile denominator.
5. Classified originals, replies, and native reposts while preserving source
   authorship for external records.
6. Inventoried links and applied a checked-in mission-signal rule manifest to
   source-post bodies, hashtags, and displayed link destinations.
7. Reviewed year-bounded incoming searches from 2008 through 2026.
8. Reduced non-mission personal or network context to aggregate dispositions.
