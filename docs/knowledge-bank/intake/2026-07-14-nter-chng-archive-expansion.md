## Recovered Source Chain

The project site's January 2011 capture describes NTER CHNG as an interactive
texting installation by Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett Fuselier. It
also preserves an earlier Kansas City presentation. This source establishes
the project and shared maker credit; it does not establish the later
exhibition by itself.

America: Now and Here's archived [Kansas City artist
page](https://web.archive.org/web/20110518071626/http://kansascity.americanowandhere.org/the-visual-artists/drew-bolton-jamie-burkart-and-garrett-fuselier/)
provides the direct exhibition record. It lists Bolton, Burkart, and Fuselier
together as visual artists and describes NTER CHNG's software, architectural,
real-time, and many-to-many participatory form.

The exhibition's archived first-party article [I Text, Therefore I
Am](https://web.archive.org/web/20121017090512/http://americanowandhere.org/2011/06/i-text-therefore-i-am/)
describes visitors using NTER CHNG by sending messages and seeing them
projected on a floor-to-ceiling gauze display. This establishes observed use,
not a total audience or impact measure.

The Nerman Museum page supplies institutional context for America: Now and
Here's 2011 Kansas City launch and multi-venue program. It does not name NTER
CHNG and is not used as the direct source for the project's inclusion.

## Matured Claim

America: Now and Here's official archived sites document NTER CHNG in its 2011
Kansas City exhibition and credit Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and Garrett
Fuselier together as visual artists. A separate official article documents
visitor use of the text-message and projected-display interaction.

## Boundaries

- Preserve the three-person maker credit; do not imply solo authorship.
- The records do not establish the detailed division of software, design,
  fabrication, or production labor.
- Do not convert observed use into attendance, unique participants, reach,
  endorsement, adoption, or impact.
- The Nerman Museum page establishes exhibition context but does not itself
  name NTER CHNG.
- Archived phone numbers and participant-submitted messages are intentionally
  excluded from the public repository.
- Public archive access does not grant image, video, participant-text, or other
  republication rights.
- A press-release PDF linked from the archived project site was not recovered
  in this pass. Not recovered is not evidence that it did not exist.
