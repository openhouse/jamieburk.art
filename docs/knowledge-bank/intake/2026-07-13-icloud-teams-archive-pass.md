## Archival posture

The working folders are a private source environment, not a publication
surface. This pass moved only public-safe descriptions, public URLs, opaque
protected locators, bounded claims, and explicit research debt into the public
repository.

iCloud hydration is part of the method. A file that had not materialized or
could not be durably read was recorded as not recovered in this pass. That does
not mean it did not exist.

Private material excluded from ingestion includes raw email and message text,
private transcripts, contact and participant records, legal and financial
records, live strategy, family context, credentials, and local filesystem
paths.
