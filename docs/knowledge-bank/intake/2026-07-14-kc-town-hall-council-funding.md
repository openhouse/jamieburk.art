# KC Town Hall Council Funding Research Receipt

**Received:** 2026-07-14

**Question:** Did the Kansas City Council act on the CCED Board's $490,539 KC
Town Hall recommendation, and what was the later disposition of the
appropriation?

## Recovered Decision Chain

1. Kansas City CCED Board minutes identify Jamie as proposal presenter and
   record a unanimous recommendation to City Council for $490,539.
2. On September 26, 2019, the Council adopted Resolution 190649 as substituted,
   accepting the recommendation and authorizing negotiation of a funding
   agreement for an amount not to exceed $490,539.
3. On the same date, the Council passed Ordinance 190642 as substituted,
   appropriating $490,539 to KC Town Hall in the Central City Sales Tax-Projects
   account.
4. A May 17, 2022 city update listed no funds disbursed and described the
   funding agreement as under negotiation.
5. Ordinance 240317, passed March 28, 2024, recorded that KC Town Hall had
   withdrawn; the City reduced the full $490,539 project account and returned
   the unused appropriation to the Central City Sales Tax Fund.

## Defensible Claim

The proposal advanced from a unanimous CCED Board recommendation to Council
acceptance and a $490,539 appropriation. That is a stronger and more specific
municipal-review accomplishment than a recommendation alone.

## Required Boundary

Appropriation is not receipt or expenditure. The reviewed records do not
establish Jamie's individual role in securing the Council actions. The project
later withdrew, and the full unused appropriation was reclaimed in 2024.
