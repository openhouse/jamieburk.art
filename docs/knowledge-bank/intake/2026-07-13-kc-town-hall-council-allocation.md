# KC Town Hall Council Allocation

**Reviewed:** 2026-07-13

## Question

Did the Kansas City Council act on the Central City Economic Development Sales
Tax Board's $490,539 recommendation for KC Town Hall?

## Primary records

1. [Committee Substitute for Ordinance No. 190642](https://clerk.kcmo.gov/LegislationDetail.aspx?FullText=1&GUID=B387009F-F7F7-454D-950A-E44588056314&ID=5515929)
   passed as substituted on September 26, 2019. Its appropriation table assigns
   $490,539 to KC Town Hall for redevelopment of the abandoned building at
   Indiana Avenue and 36th Street.
2. [Second Committee Substitute for Resolution No. 190649](https://clerk.kcmo.gov/LegislationDetail.aspx?FullText=1&GUID=44A50FFC-321A-41C7-9A86-6ADD9083B156&ID=5515936)
   was adopted as substituted on September 26, 2019. It accepts the board's
   recommendation that up to $490,539 be used for KC Town Hall project costs
   and authorizes the City Manager to negotiate a funding agreement.
3. [September 26, 2019 Council meeting record](https://kansascity.legistar.com/MeetingDetail.aspx?G=D2E89A09-8736-4EFB-B4AE-572E0903BD5A&GID=821&LEGID=14410)
   records Ordinance No. 190642 as passed and Resolution No. 190649 as adopted.

The authenticated legislation PDFs were also visually inspected during this
research pass. The ordinance's project table and the resolution's operative
sections agree with the legislation-detail records.

## Claim disposition

The public-safe sequence is now:

- official board minutes identify Jamie as KC Town Hall's presenter;
- the board unanimously recommended $490,539 to City Council;
- the Council allocated $490,539 through Ordinance No. 190642; and
- the Council accepted the recommendation and authorized funding-agreement
  negotiations through Resolution No. 190649.

This matures the public claim from a board recommendation to a documented
Council allocation and authorization. It does not establish an executed funding
agreement, receipt or disbursement of funds, project completion, current status,
or Jamie's sole causality for the board recommendation or Council action.
