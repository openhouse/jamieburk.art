### 32:44

And I want to underline some of the things that the bill does and does not do, okay? Because you're going to start to hear, especially after all of this support, you're going to start to hear mythologies about what our bill is going to do, and I'm telling you, they're probably wrong.

This is what the Small Business Rent Stabilization Act will do: it provides lease renewal rights with a default 10-year renewal term, protects at-will tenants with the right to request a written lease, and while it applies to all leases and rental agreements for commercial spaces, it levels the playing field for small businesses in particular. It creates a Commercial Rent Guidelines Board to set maximum annual rent increases for commercial tenants.

That means it's not a rent freeze. Ask me—it's not permanent. It is a board that's making a decision, rather than individuals or LLCs, let's be real. And it's going to enforce compliance through strong penalty provisions against overcharges and tenant harassment. Tenant harassment is one of the biggest issues my office has heard about from commercial landlords, and that is something we absolutely must put a stop to.

So it does not freeze rents entirely or roll back existing rents. It does not prevent landlords from making a fair return. And it does not apply retroactively to existing leases.
