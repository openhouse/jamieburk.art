### 08:03

Thank you so much, Assembly Member Gallagher. Good morning, everyone.

What a beautiful crowd we have out here for Jimmy's and for commercial rent stabilization. I am Senator Julia Salazar. I represent New York's 18th District in Brooklyn and Queens, but I am here today to support Jimmy's Corner, both as a patron but also as the Senate sponsor of the Small Business Rent Stabilization Act.

The great late Jimmy Glenn opened Jimmy's Corner back in 1971, way before Times Square was the neighborhood that it is today. Jimmy created a space where everyone would be welcome and where locals could actually afford to spend time. Today, Jimmy's is one of the last true dive bars left in this area.

The size of the crowd here demonstrates that there is a community surrounding this beloved business, which you heard Adam speak to. There is a community who doesn't want to see this special place be forced out. The impact that Jimmy's Corner and the Glenn family have had here is one that corporate chains could never replicate.

Many of the surrounding businesses have already either been forced to close or they've had to raise their prices astronomically to keep up with their costs. While we are here rallying to save Jimmy's, this problem is bigger than a single bar. This is about the fact that small businesses are being squeezed out of our city. Across every borough, community-oriented businesses are shutting down because their rents are rising to levels that only national chains can afford.

Commercial landlords are keeping storefronts empty as they wait for a higher-paying tenant to come along, rather than renting the space sooner to a local business owner. Our streets are losing the character that small businesses give us, replaced by corporate malls lined with Sweetgreen and Starbucks.

Just the other day, I passed a space in Brooklyn where Gino's Pizza used to be. A real estate company bought their building and raised the monthly rent from $10,000 a month to $25,000 a month. It's a shame. That's a 150% increase.

This is a culture crisis, an economic crisis. Nearly half of our state's workforce is employed by small businesses. When those businesses are forced to shut down, New Yorkers lose their jobs.

We need small business rent stabilization to save our local businesses. This bill would limit unaffordable rent hikes. It would guarantee lease renewals, and it would protect at-will tenants.

We will continue to fight for commercial rent stabilization. We will continue to fight for Jimmy's Corner and for every business that is struggling in our city and our state. Thank you.
