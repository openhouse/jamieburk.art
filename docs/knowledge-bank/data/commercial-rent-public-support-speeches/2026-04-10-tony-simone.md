### 13:47

Hi, everyone. Thank you all for being here. I want to thank the sponsors of this amazing bill, Assembly Member Emily Gallagher and my friend Julia Salazar from the State Senate. I'm also joined by my amazing colleague, Assembly Member Jo Anne Simon. They also often mix up our names.

But look, small business is the heart and soul of New York City, whether it's our bodegas, whether it's our dive bars. Who doesn't love a dive bar where you can still afford to drink a beer in Jimmy's?

If you want soulless corporate lobbies, move to Houston. No offense, but this is New York City. We don't need 10 Sweetgreens. We don't need 100 corporate-owned businesses. We need these small businesses to make New York City for who we are.

And it is a crime that you can raise the rent 100%, 200%, and then you have these empty storefronts. One of the biggest issues that came before me when I was a candidate, by folks in Hell's Kitchen and Chelsea, was: why are these storefronts so empty for so long? Why?

And we talked to the landlords, and we talked to the banks, like, "Oh, well, the market says, the market says." But clearly the market is broken. It's corporate greed, and it's capitalism run amok. So it's not working for our communities.

And look, with the onset of AI, we're going to be in a big fight for jobs. These small bodegas, these dive bars—they employ people from the community and real New Yorkers, and that's why I'm a big supporter of the Commercial Rent Stabilization Act.

We have to ensure that landlords cannot raise the rent so quickly simply because of greed, simply because they can. They need to have a heart again, and if not, we're going to go after them.

So I stand here proudly. Thank you for inviting me. Welcome to my district. Go have a beer at Jimmy's. We need to save Jimmy's bar—save Jimmy's—and we have to ensure that we pass this Commercial Rent Stabilization Act.
