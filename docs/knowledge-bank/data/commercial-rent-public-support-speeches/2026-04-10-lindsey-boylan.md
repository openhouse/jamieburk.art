You got to give it up to our two co-sponsors of this bill. They are incredible women, and they're not just doing this for the whole state—they're doing this for the city.

When this legislation passes, we will create so much opportunity. Look right behind us: "Retail available." I did not order that sign today, but that is exactly what you get in this environment. Without this bill, you get not only "retail available"—look around you, every single thing you see is a bank. We do not need another retail bank.

And if you don't have one of these two things—I live in Chelsea, our great leader Tony Simone was just talking about our Hell's Kitchen and Chelsea neighborhoods—so many empty storefronts. The corner of my block has been empty for years. Just down the block from that, an entire swath, especially new building, new construction.

Especially now that we have an incoming wave of new build in this city, we need to protect the future of small business.

I'll also say, having done economic development at the state, the idea that this somehow harms our economy is the exact perversion of reality. Small business is what creates new jobs, is what creates the economic opportunity, is what creates the future for our city. It's not the big businesses. It's not the national anchor tenants that we have that are giving more opportunity and giving it to more people. It is our small businesses. It's the Jimmy's Corner.

And so I couldn't be more proud to be here today. The only thing I'll say is: at night, these banks are all closed. "Retail available"—that's not opening up. It's our neighborhood tenants. It's our neighborhood businesses that keep us safe, that keep neighborhoods positive and progressing, employ our people, and it's the only way forward for a safe and quality-of-life New York City.

So I'm so excited to support. Thank you so much.
