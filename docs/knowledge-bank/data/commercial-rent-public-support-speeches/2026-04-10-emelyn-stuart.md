### 11:55

Hello, everyone. I am Emelyn Stuart, the first Afro-Latina to own a movie theater here in New York.

And so I built the theater in 2018, and in 2023 I decided I wanted to expand and build a theater in Sunset Park, my childhood neighborhood. And so I signed a 10-year lease, and I started working and hired architects and electricians and spent a ton of money, and three months later I was locked out of the space because the landlord had lost the building in an auction because he owed back taxes.

And things like this happen all the time. Most recently, at my existing theater, I expanded and added an auditorium. I spent almost $200,000 building this auditorium, and two months after I opened it, I got a 60-day eviction notice because the building had been sold.

And this is happening to many, many businesses in New York.

And we're not saying that commercial landlords shouldn't raise the rent. We get it. We have expenses too. We get that. What we're saying is we need better, fair practices. We need people to understand that small businesses—every time I am affected as a small business, my employees are affected. My community is affected.

This bill is super important. We need guardrails for the commercial landlords. Thank you.
