### 06:02

Hey, everybody. My name is Alison Hirsh, and I'm here with Action Lab and Small Business United.

Small Business United is a new organization made up of small business owners, for small business owners, in New York City. We talk a lot about the affordability crisis in New York City, but all too often we forget that small businesses are a huge component of what it means for people in New York City to be able to have economic mobility, to build a life here, and to build a community and neighborhood.

And all too often, because of people like Durst and companies like Durst, small businesses are thrown aside, and the communities that rely on them are worse off because of it.

So Small Business United is super proud to be here standing with Adam and Jimmy's Corner, with Assembly Member Gallagher and Senator Salazar, who are sponsoring Small Business rent stabilization in Albany and are 100% committed to seeing this fight through—both for businesses like Jimmy's Corner, for folks like Freddy, who you'll hear from later, who runs a restaurant in Jackson Heights, and for businesses all over New York City who need people to stand up and say small businesses are a core part of what make our neighborhoods special and unique, and we're going to continue fighting for them.

So thank you all for being here, and thank you so much.
