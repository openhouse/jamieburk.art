# Portfolio System Blind-Spot Register

**Reviewed:** July 15, 2026

**Purpose:** Keep the Knowledge Bank, public portfolio, evaluation system, and
job-search actions accountable to evidence they cannot generate internally.

The controls below are public-safe. Private reader feedback, collaborator
correspondence, application details, and relationship graphs stay outside the
repository. Only consented or aggregate findings may return to the bank.

## 1. External Reader Validation

**Current signal:** The repository can validate evidence and copy structure but
has no completed hiring-reader comprehension study or demonstrated interview
effect attributable to the site.

**Control:** Treat external effectiveness as `not-yet-measured`. By July 18,
2026, ask five consented readers, including at least two hiring-adjacent readers
and one implementation, civic-tech, or product-operations peer, to review a
frozen surface for five minutes without coaching. Assign two readers to home,
two to technical operations, and one to the 51-of-54 Fair Rent case; all five
must attempt the resume and contact destinations. Record aggregate entry
coverage, exposure completion, role identification, proof recall, role fit,
trust concerns, contact reason, and separate times to find resume and contact.

**Next evidence:** Dated aggregate findings and resulting copy decisions. Do
not commit names, private feedback, employers, applications, or individual
response transcripts.

## 2. Inquiry Closure

**Current signal:** 26 of 32 structured research inquiries are partially
recovered; one is recovered. Partial status is often correct, but it can become
an indefinite resting place.

**Control:** Prioritize inquiries by public-decision value, not archive size.
Each priority inquiry must name the exact corroborating artifact, public record,
or collaborator confirmation that would close it, plus the bounded wording to
retain if closure evidence is unavailable.

**Priority targets:** NYC Artist Coalition founding and responsibility map;
Office of Nightlife production role; Talks Not Raids individual role and causal
pathway; KC Town Hall Phase One role; NTER CHNG production and exhibition
history; and public-site source coverage. The structured control sidecar names
an evidence target, closure condition, bounded disposition, owner, and review
date for each.

## 3. Individual Role Corroboration

**Current signal:** Project and coalition importance is often strongly sourced,
while Jamie's complete responsibility map remains only partially recovered.

**Control:** Decompose broad role memories into action-level propositions such
as built, convened, maintained, testified, coordinated, researched, deployed,
or handed off. Strong individual wording requires one high-confidence direct
contemporaneous role source, or two independent corroborating sources including
a collaborator or official attribution. First-person-only wording must be
explicitly attributed and bounded or held.

The checker derives an exact population of every active or held Knowledge Bank
claim whose projection names Jamie, then requires a matching record in
`role-evidence-classifications.json`. Each record cites only sources already in
that claim's evidence graph and classifies the role basis as source-corroborated,
first-person, mixed, or open. First-person or mixed active wording must say that
Jamie reports, describes, states, or recalls the role; open role claims stay held.

**Next evidence:** A small set of high-value collaborator confirmations and
contemporaneous role artifacts, not a broad testimonial campaign.

## 4. Countability And Platform Bias

**Current signal:** The bank has 228 sources across public records, reporting,
project archives, web pages, social posts, and research runs. Source-class
diversity is healthy, but recent archival effort has favored surfaces that can
be enumerated. Trace volume does not measure importance, effort, or impact.

**Control:** Review each major project for less-countable operating work:
maintenance, meeting preparation, facilitation, field coordination, translation,
relationship repair, documentation, follow-through, and handoff. Open targeted
source leads where this work is central. Never rank claims by source count,
post count, reaction count, or ease of retrieval.

**Next evidence:** Role-specific records or collaborator confirmation for
otherwise invisible operating work.

## 5. Composition And Argument

**Current signal:** The graph contains 100 claims, including 60 active and 42
held projections. A mature claim is not automatically a selected claim.

**Control:** Every public route or route template has an audience, reader
decision, governing argument, primary action, claim budget, selection rule, and
omission rationale in `composition-manifest.json`. New evidence first
strengthens the bank. It enters the site only when it reduces reader uncertainty
or supports the page's current argument. Omission is an editorial success when
depth remains retrievable.

Every concrete route has an ordered, render-derived claim-key inventory. A work
card counts its subtitle, summary, uncertainty, usable transformation, and role
fit as separate propositions even though they share a container. Concrete case
studies add MDX prose and `<Claim>` nodes to the metadata actually rendered by
the shared layout; they do not inherit a generic count. In this snapshot the six
cases range from 24 to 51 claim units; Fair Rent NYC is the densest at 51 of 54.

**Next evidence:** Short reader-recall results and page-level subtraction tests,
not a larger page by default.

## 6. Mosaic Privacy

**Current signal:** Record-level checks cannot fully detect what names, dates,
locations, affiliations, and interactions reveal when assembled.

**Control:** After major ingestion, review the public graph as a whole for
unnecessary network maps, movement histories, sensitive collaborator patterns,
or inference paths. Minimize names and exact detail that do not strengthen the
professional argument. Public availability is not sufficient justification for
aggregation.

**Current receipt:** The July 15 combination-level review found risks around
account-level stakeholder relationships, platform custody being mistaken for
individual authorship, and identifiable reader feedback. It retains account
boundaries, requires attributed or corroborated role wording, and keeps private
feedback outside the repository. This is an internal control review, not legal,
participant, or collaborator approval.

**Next evidence:** Repeat the combination-level review after the next
substantial ingestion and before production release, without publishing a
relationship graph.

## 7. Evaluator Independence

**Current signal:** Deterministic checks prove structural consistency. LLM
rubrics can challenge evidence and composition. Neither proves how an external
human experiences the portfolio.

**Control:** Run deciding LLM judgments in distinct read-only sessions against
the same versioned bundle digest; retain each output as a separate immutable
artifact with session provenance, criterion scores, hard-gate findings, and
limitations. Never describe role-play labels as independent judges. Keep human
reader research as a separate evidence class and external state.

**Next evidence:** Independent judge outputs for internal control quality, then
separate consented aggregate human-reader findings for external usefulness.

## 8. Action Conversion

**Current signal:** Archive and portfolio work can always discover another
valuable refinement. That does not make further refinement the highest-value
next action.

**Control:** A substantial pass changes public positioning, activates a
consequential claim, or ingests ten or more sources. End it with a dated
external attempt. Repository work alone cannot satisfy the action. Time-box
further polishing unless new evidence would correct a consequential claim or
safety risk. Return only coarse status and aggregate learning to the bank.

**Current next action:** By July 18, share the staged portfolio with a small
relevant reader set and use aggregate comprehension findings to choose the next
subtraction or clarification pass. Then complete one outward application,
referral request, or comparable job-search action before another discretionary
archive pass. These actions are planned, not completed.

External receipts use explicit state machines. A receipt's coarse status must
match the protocol state; reviewed or learned states require aggregate findings
and a resulting content decision. Repository edits and LLM judgments cannot
advance those states. Reader receipts must reconcile global totals with
privacy-safe per-entry-surface counts and all nine promised study dimensions.

## Control Boundary

Passing the blind-spot eval means these risks have observable controls and
honest next tests. It does not mean the risks are eliminated or that external
outcomes have been achieved.
