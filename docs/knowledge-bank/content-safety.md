# Content Safety

The public repo should contain only material that can be shown in public without
violating privacy, trust, copyright, licensing, or coalition/client boundaries.

## Never Publish

- private emails
- raw transcripts
- private coalition notes
- legal-review materials
- health or financial details
- private correspondence
- unapproved photos
- private or proprietary fonts
- credentials
- stakeholder lists
- internal analytics
- client-private materials
- raw community records
- private AI/chat logs
- private source bundles
- private phone numbers or contact details

## Allowed Patterns

- Public-safe summaries.
- Redacted examples.
- Representative diagrams.
- Approved artifacts.
- Aggregate metrics with source-class notes.
- Careful collective-work language.
- Known / Open / Protected blocks.

## Approval Rule

When uncertain, do not publish the claim. Add it to
`public-claims-inventory.md` as `Candidate` with the needed approval.
