# NYC Artist Coalition Full-Population Archival Production

Run date: 2026-07-12

## Answer First

The live [@NYCArtC profile](https://x.com/NYCArtC) displays **5,124 posts**.
This pass created a [5,124-row accounting
ledger](nycartc-post-census-2026-07-12.csv):

- **892 surviving public records recovered**;
- **4,232 profile-counted slots unresolved**;
- **100% of the control total represented** as recovered or unresolved.

This is complete accounting, not complete recovery. The recovery rate is 17.4
percent. The unresolved 82.6 percent has no inferred IDs, dates, record types,
text, themes, or authors.

## Population And Method

The population control is the 5,124-post count displayed beside the account
name on July 12, 2026. A recovered record has a unique public status ID. An
unresolved slot preserves the gap between that control and recovered IDs.

1. Read the authenticated live profile, January 2017 join date, and 5,124-post
   control.
2. Harvested the live Posts timeline conservatively until X stopped pagination,
   recovering 607 unique records.
3. Ran a bounded 2022 authenticated search, recovering 49 additional records.
4. Reconciled the live corpus with 279 readable status records previously
   recovered through Wayback CDX and X oEmbed.
5. Deduplicated the union by status ID, producing 892 surviving public records.
6. Tested current and historical public guest API paths without using browser
   cookies. Profile metadata resolved, but public timeline pagination returned
   no records.
7. Classified recovered records by type, year, primary theme, mentions, and
   hashtags.
8. Added 4,232 unresolved rows so the ledger closes exactly against 5,124.
9. Kept full post text and protected research artifacts outside the public
   repository.

### A durable collective public identity

Jamie confirms that he established the account and created NYC Artist
Coalition's logo and campaign website family. The recovered timeline supports
the separate continuity claim: collaborators used the shared public identity
across years, campaigns, resources, and changing issues.

The account does not identify the human author of each record. Continuity of
the identity is not represented as Jamie's uninterrupted operation, and
collective campaign work is not converted into individual credit.

### An amplification surface

The 541 recovered reposts include public work by cultural-policy, artist-labor,
community-land, tenant, small-business, public-agency, library, and mutual-aid
accounts. In the recovered corpus, frequently amplified accounts include
Future of Music Coalition, WGA East, Vendor Power, United Musicians and Allied

This supports a bounded interpretation: the identity functioned as connective
public infrastructure. Repost frequency is not endorsement of every statement,
an audience measure, or evidence that Jamie selected each record.

## Council And Government Engagement Boundary

The account population contains outbound mentions, replies, quoted records,
and reposts involving public officials and agencies. Those are not counted as
official engagement merely because an office was named.

The separate roster-driven engagement study remains canonical: it documents a
recovered minimum of seven then-sitting New York City Council members directly
engaging @NYCArtC. This population pass does not replace that method or inflate
its count.

## Claim Boundaries

- Complete means every current profile-counted slot is represented in the
  ledger; it does not mean every record was recovered.
- Theme, year, type, campaign, and amplified-account counts describe only the
  892 recovered records.
- The recovered corpus is not assumed to represent the unresolved population.
- The shared account does not identify each record's human author.
- Jamie's documented contribution is establishing the identity system, not
  sole authorship or uninterrupted operation.
- Reposts and outbound mentions do not establish endorsement, direct
  engagement, policy adoption, audience reach, or causality.
- The current profile count cannot reveal records deleted before capture.
- Full post text and protected research artifacts remain outside the public
  repository.
