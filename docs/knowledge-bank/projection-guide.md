# Projection Guide

The public site should project from the Knowledge Bank, not mirror it.

The public site is smaller, faster, and more audience-specific than the bank.
The bank can carry status, source class, approval owner, and anti-claim detail.
Public pages should carry only the approved wording needed for the reader in
that moment.

## Claim Status

- Ready claims may appear on public pages.
- Careful claims may appear only with guardrails intact.
- Pending claims stay in the Knowledge Bank until approval, citation, or exact
  wording is resolved.
- Protected material never becomes site copy.
- Private material stays outside the repo and outside public pages.

## Surface Rules

Homepage: strongest approved claims only. Keep it fast for hiring readers.

Resume page: approved highlights, public-safe role framing, and PDF status.

Work index: short claims with role, scope, status, and public-safety posture.

Case studies: project-specific evidence plus Known / Open / Protected
boundaries.

Technical Operations: delivery, implementation, planning, documentation,
onboarding, runbooks, retros, risk, status, and handoff evidence.

Lab / Source-Backed Team Memory: lab / method / proof-of-practice, not
production SaaS.

Colophon: proof standard, not private evidence.

Metadata / OpenGraph: role, operating-structure, and selected proof; no dense
unresolved metrics.

## Sentence Test

Every public projection should answer:

- What did Jamie do?
- For whom?
- Toward what end?
- What became usable?
- Why does it matter for this role?
