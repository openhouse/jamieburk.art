# Projection Guide

The knowledge bank is richer than the public website. Projection is the act of
choosing which claim appears on which public surface.

## Surface Rules

- Homepage: strongest approved claims only. Keep it fast for a hiring reader.
- Resume page: approved highlights, public-safe role framing, and PDF status.
- Work pages: case-specific proof plus Known / Open / Protected boundaries.
- `/work/technical-operations`: role-specific evidence for delivery,
  implementation, planning, documentation, onboarding, runbooks, retros, risk,
  status, and handoff readers.
- `/lab/source-backed-team-memory`: lab / method / proof-of-practice. It is not
  a production SaaS, private archive browser, or replacement for judgment.
- Resume PDF: noindex for V1 even when the `/resume` page later becomes
  indexable.

## Projection Standard

Only project a claim when all are true:

1. The claim is specific enough to be useful.
2. The source class is known.
3. Public boundaries are clear.
4. The wording does not overclaim individual ownership of collective work.
5. Jamie approval is not pending for that exact claim.

## Lab and Sensitive Work

Source-Backed Team Memory may say:

> AI drafts. Humans review. The shared record remains inspectable and
> correctable.

It may not publish private transcripts, private source bundles, client context,
or collaborator names unless Jamie approves the exact material.
