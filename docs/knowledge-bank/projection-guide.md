# Projection Guide

The website is a projection from the knowledge bank, not a dump of the bank.

Use this path:

```text
Knowledge bank claim -> conservative public projection -> page-specific sentence -> Chad-lens review -> public-safety scan -> production preflight.
```

## Examples

Internal claim:

> Jamie contributed to a period of online growth for HJE.

Public projection:

> Long-term e-commerce and operations improvements contributed to online growth for an 80+ year-old industrial business.

Avoid:

> Jamie caused 2x revenue growth.

Internal claim:

> The CRS/FairRentNYC work includes dense running minutes, source maps, and policy questions.

Public projection:

> Helped structure shared civic campaign memory and public-data framing.

Avoid:

> Jamie led the movement or owned the bill.

Internal claim:

> Source-backed team memory connects source materials, meetings, uncertainty, and team answers.

Public projection:

> A research and consulting method for decision trails, onboarding context, meeting synthesis, and human review.

Avoid:

> A finished AI SaaS product or private archive browser.

## Page-Level Rules

- Homepage: shortest, most defensible claims only.
- Work cards: one conservative public projection per project.
- Case studies: more context, but still inside the evidence boundary.
- Resume page: hiring-readable claims only; no unresolved approval TODOs in visible copy.
- PDF resume: separately approved; do not assume website approval implies PDF approval.
