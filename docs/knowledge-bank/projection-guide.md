# Projection Guide

The public site should project from the Knowledge Bank, not mirror it.

For the homepage, prefer compact claims a busy reader can understand in one
pass.

For Technical Operations, prefer role-fit claims: requirements, workflow
mapping, documentation architecture, implementation support, user acceptance
testing, stakeholder updates, onboarding, runbooks, retrospectives, and durable
handoffs.

For case studies, prefer project-specific claims: what was unclear, what became
usable, what Jamie did, and what remains protected.

For the colophon, explain the proof standard, not private evidence.

For metadata and Open Graph, use role, operating-structure, and selected-project
framing; avoid dense metrics.

## Projection Rules

- `Ready` claims may appear on public pages.
- `Careful` claims may appear only with their guardrail intact.
- `Pending` claims stay in the Knowledge Bank until Jamie approval, collaborator
  approval, citation review, or exact wording is resolved.
- `Protected` material never becomes site copy.

## Known / Open / Protected

Known means public-safe and supported enough to say. Open means useful but
awaiting approval, citation, or stronger evidence. Protected means intentionally
omitted because privacy, consent, client trust, civic sensitivity, or public
safety requires it.

## Compatibility

Older composite branches used names such as `docs/proofs-bank.md` and
`docs/public-claim-register.md`. This branch consolidates that work under
`docs/knowledge-bank/` so future contributors do not maintain competing claim
systems.
