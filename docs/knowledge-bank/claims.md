stable ID, status, source class, approval owner, allowed pages, and anti-claim.

Statuses:

- `Ready`: safe for the listed public pages using the exact public wording or
  a narrower variant.
- `Careful`: usable only with the listed qualifiers, contribution language, or
  source-class boundary.
- `Pending`: supported enough to keep in the bank, but do not strengthen
  public copy until Jamie or a collaborator approves.
- `Do-not-publish`: keep out of public pages.
- `Protected`: protected context only; do not commit source evidence.
