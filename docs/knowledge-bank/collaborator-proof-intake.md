# Collaborator proof intake

Use this protocol for a person with direct knowledge of a project. It is not a
request for praise or a generic testimonial.

## Source position

Record whether the contributor directly observed the work, reviewed a
contemporaneous artifact, or is offering a later interpretation. Keep memory,
documentary evidence, and inference distinct.

## What Jamie did

Ask for one to three concrete actions Jamie personally performed. Prefer
bounded verbs, dates or phases, and named work products. Do not ask the
contributor to assign Jamie sole credit for collective work.

## What became usable

Ask what another person, team, organization, or public could do because of the
work. Separate a delivered output from observed use and downstream outcome.

## Collective context

Name collaborators, institutional hosts, community participants, and other
material causes. Invite correction of Jamie's role, chronology, terminology,
and remembered scope.

## Permission and reuse

The contributor must choose separately whether the note may be:

- used only as private research background;
- paraphrased in the public knowledge bank;
- quoted publicly with attribution;
- used in applications but not published on the website.

Record the decision and any expiration, review, or notification requirement.
Silence is not permission.

## Public repository boundary

Store only an approved public-safe summary, project association, evidence
position, and reuse boundary. Do not commit private email addresses, phone
numbers, raw correspondence, signatures, access links, or unapproved quoted
language.

## Acceptance checklist

A qualifying confirmation identifies the contributor's source position,
Jamie's bounded action, what became usable, collective context, corrections or
limits, and explicit permission and reuse terms. AI-generated archival notes do
not qualify as collaborator confirmations.
