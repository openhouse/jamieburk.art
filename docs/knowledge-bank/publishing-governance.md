# Publishing Governance

## Rule

A claim moves from private context to public website only through the knowledge
bank.

## Flow

1. Private material exists outside repo.
2. Jamie or reviewer creates a public-safe summary.
3. Summary is added to the knowledge bank with source-basis class and boundary.
4. Evidentiary maturity, publication safety, and editorial selection are judged
   separately.
5. Website projection uses only mature, public-safe, deliberately selected
   claims.
6. If a page needs stronger wording, update the bank first.
7. Public-safety check fails production if pending/private/approval-marker
   material is projected.

## Status Meanings

- Ready: strong enough for public site copy.
- Careful: usable publicly only with the stated guardrail.
- Pending: do not use publicly until missing approval/citation is resolved.
- Private: source material exists, but the claim should not be published.

These proof-bank labels do not replace canonical claim maturity or editorial
selection. A confirmed claim can remain in reserve; a public source can still
afford only a limited claim; and a promising interpretation can remain on hold.

## Intake Dispositions

No intake record is silently deleted. It must link to a source, claim, inquiry,
or project, or record that it was merged, protected, or rejected with a reason.

LLM agents may research and propose promotion. They may not convert a memory
directly into a confirmed claim or publish reserve material merely to satisfy an
eval.

## Chad-Lens Review

Before a claim appears publicly, ask:

- Is Jamie visible as the actor?
- Does the claim answer "toward what end?"
- Is it legible to a hiring manager, public-sector reader, or HR reviewer?
- Does it name the specific work rather than hiding behind "data," "systems,"
  or "AI"?
- Does it use stewardship language where appropriate?
- Does it avoid inflated ownership claims?
