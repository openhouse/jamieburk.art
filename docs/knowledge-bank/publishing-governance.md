# Publishing Governance

A claim can appear on the public site only if it is public-safe, audience-legible, and phrased within its evidence boundary.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Public use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Defensible | Strong enough for portfolio copy when phrased with stated boundaries. | May appear on public pages. |
| Use with care | Usable in summary form, but wording must stay conservative. | May appear when softened and contextualized. |
| Open | Needs citation, screenshot approval, collaborator approval, or stronger review. | Keep out of prominent site copy. |
| Protected | Intentionally omitted because privacy, consent, client trust, law, civic sensitivity, or community safety requires it. | Do not publish. |

## Publishing Rule

Knowledge bank claim -> conservative public projection -> page-specific sentence -> Chad-lens review -> public-safety scan -> production preflight.

Public pages should not expose every source. They should say only what is safe, useful, supportable, and professionally legible.

## Claim Strength

Do not use a stronger verb, metric, credit, or outcome claim on the website than the bank allows.

Examples:

- Use: "contributed to online growth"
- Avoid: "caused 2x revenue growth"

- Use: "helped build and steward shared campaign-memory infrastructure"
- Avoid: "led the movement" or "owned the bill"

- Use: "co-built a community-calendar platform"
- Avoid: "ran official city chapters"
