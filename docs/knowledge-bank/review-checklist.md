# Review Checklist

Use this checklist before strengthening public copy or promoting production.

- Is Jamie visible as the actor?
- Does the sentence answer toward what end the work mattered?
- Is the language legible to a hiring manager, HR reader, public-sector
  reviewer, referrer, or technical-operations lead?
- Are specialized terms translated once?
- Are acronyms spelled out or avoided?
- Are unfamiliar entities defined?
- Does the copy protect collective credit?
- Does it avoid overclaiming causality, ownership, or authority?
- Is the claim present in `docs/knowledge-bank/claims.md`?
- Does `apps/www/src/data/proofs.ts` match the approved public wording?
- Are allowed surfaces and approval status clear?
- Are boundaries visible where the claim could be misunderstood?
- Does `anti-claims.md` rule out an accidental implication?
- Does the page preserve Known / Open / Protected where appropriate?
- Are private source materials omitted from the repo?
- Do public-safety, route, build, and production preflight checks pass?
