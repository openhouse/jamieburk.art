# Polimorphic - Product Manager (First Product Hire)

This is a high-upside, unusually relevant role at the intersection of local
government, product discovery, implementation, quality, customer partnership,
and emerging technology. Jamie's strongest argument is not a title match. It is
the sustained responsibility for designing, shipping, operating, and improving
public-facing systems with communities and institutional partners.

The principal risk is that Polimorphic may read “three to five years of product
management” narrowly. Application materials should make the product decisions,
delivery responsibilities, technical collaboration, and customer-learning loop
plain without recasting unrelated work.
