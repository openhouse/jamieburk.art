## Application Message Draft

I am excited to apply for the Technical Operations Manager role because it names
the operating backbone I have repeatedly built: delivery coordination, planning
rhythms, decision logs, onboarding paths, operating documentation, stakeholder
updates, launch support, and durable handoffs for public-facing technical work.

Across 14+ years, I have helped small businesses, civic collaborations, cultural
organizations, and public-facing web projects turn ambiguous work into usable
systems. At THICK ARTS, I have translated stakeholder needs into requirements,
information architecture, implementation plans, maintenance workflows, adoption
materials, and handoff documentation. For Harry J. Epstein Company, I supported
long-running e-commerce, analytics, content, marketing, and operations
improvements for an 80+ year-old legacy business. For CallNYC.org, I translated
constituent-services open data into resident-facing find help / next steps
guidance. For FairRentNYC / Commercial Rent Stabilization work, I helped build
shared campaign-memory infrastructure: meeting synthesis, action trackers,
source maps, decision records, and public/private boundary management.

I am drawn to OTI because the work is public-serving, cross-functional, and
operationally concrete. I would bring calm execution, clear writing, strong
documentation habits, and careful judgment about how small high-output teams
stay focused, unblocked, and shipping.
