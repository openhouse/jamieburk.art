# OTI-Style Technical Operations Alignment

This is internal alignment context. Do not turn the public site into a one-job
microsite.

## Role Need

Technical Operations / Product Operations / Implementation roles need someone
who can help teams coordinate delivery, clarify requirements, surface risks,
maintain planning cycles, document decisions, onboard new people, and report
honestly on what is and is not working.

## Portfolio Evidence

- HJE: long-running web, e-commerce, analytics, marketing, content, and
  operations implementation.
- FairRentNYC / Commercial Rent Stabilization: campaign-memory infrastructure,
  source maps, action records, public/private boundaries, and follow-up systems.
- CallNYC: civic-data translation into resident-facing guidance.
- WOWList: public community platform operations and implementation.
- 196 / Sunday Dinner: onboarding, facilitation, hosting, and continuity
  systems.
- Source-Backed Team Memory: decision records, onboarding context, human review,
  and bounded AI-assisted documentation.

## Resume Evidence

Use approved résumé language for role history and selected impact. Keep
contribution language intact for revenue, public funding, collective civic work,
and community systems.

## Suggested Public Language

I build the operating backbone teams need to keep important work focused,
documented, unblocked, and moving toward public launch.
