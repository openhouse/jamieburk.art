# ACLU Product Manager II, Discovery

This role rewards the part of product work that happens before delivery:
listening, framing, evidence, facilitation, governance, and the judgment to
recommend build, buy, pilot, or stop. The public record already supports that
method. The application is not ready until the required launched-product brief
exists and has received factual, privacy, and editorial review.

Reverify the [official posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/aclu/jobs/8482872002)
before treating this as a live opportunity.
