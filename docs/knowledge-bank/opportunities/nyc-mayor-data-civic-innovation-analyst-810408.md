# Data and Civic Innovation Analyst — 810408

[Official City listing](https://cityjobs.nyc.gov/job/data-and-civic-innovation-analyst-in-manhattan-jid-49123) · [Source reading](../sources/jobs-nyc-mayor-data-civic-innovation-analyst-810408.md)

The Innovation Team researches service problems, maps how work gets done, and tests proposed changes with people who use the services. The analyst supports that work with qualitative and quantitative research, visuals, tracking and written summaries. The Efficiency Team helps promising tests move into agency operations; the analyst supports that transition with baseline measures and documented requirements.

The posting establishes public requirements and a route to the application. This record gives no individual eligibility, compensation, placement, submission, or recruitment determination. It makes no change to the visitor-facing résumé or portfolio.
