# Source-Backed Team Memory Opportunity

This is internal alignment context for a bounded paid pilot or bridge-work offer.
It should not become a public product launch page in V1.

## Problem

Knowledge-heavy teams often make decisions faster than their documentation
habits can absorb. Meeting context, source links, onboarding explanations,
corrections, open questions, and rationale become scattered across calendars,
docs, chats, and memory.

## Small First Engagement

A small first engagement should be framed as a source-backed memory sprint, not
as software adoption. The goal is to help a team make selected context easier to
review, correct, transfer, and maintain.

## Deliverables

- source inventory
- decision log
- onboarding reader
- meeting-to-minutes workflow
- open-questions register
- human-reviewed AI summaries
- privacy and correction boundaries
- lightweight evaluation checks for summary quality and retrieval usefulness

## Boundaries

- AI drafts; humans review.
- Sources remain inspectable.
- Corrections remain possible.
- Private material stays private.
- The work is not legal, medical, or financial advice.
- The work is not a production SaaS, autonomous agent, or private archive
  browser.

## Proof Already Visible On Site

- `/lab/source-backed-team-memory`
- Technical Operations proof mapping
- AI Evals for Engineers & PMs completion
- Public-safety, production preflight, and route checks as proof of governance
  posture

## Claims To Avoid

- Do not say this is production SaaS.
- Do not imply client adoption at scale.
- Do not imply AI replaces judgment, consent, facilitation, or direct
  communication.
- Do not publish private transcripts, documents, meeting materials, or customer
  examples.

## Open Approvals

- Exact pilot pricing and scope.
- Whether any sample artifacts can be shown publicly.
- Which client or collaborator names may be used, if any.
