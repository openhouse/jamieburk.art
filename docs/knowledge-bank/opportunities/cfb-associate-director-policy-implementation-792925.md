# NYC Campaign Finance Board - Associate Director of Policy Implementation 792925

This is a strong transition role because its central challenge is not abstract
policy authorship alone. It is building the operating system through which
policy moves from evidence and stakeholder input into decisions,
implementation, evaluation, learning, and maintained guidance. Jamie has
credible evidence in that pattern across public-facing technology, coalition
operations, research, documentation, facilitation, and implementation.

The application should directly address formal staff supervision and should
name elections and campaign finance as a domain to learn, not experience Jamie
already possesses. Sophie Sharps is the likely direct manager by exact official
title match; Allie Swatek is the nearest public senior policy leader. Neither
identification implies awareness, participation, or endorsement. Recheck the
[official posting](../sources/jobs-cfb-policy-implementation-792925.md) before
outward use.
