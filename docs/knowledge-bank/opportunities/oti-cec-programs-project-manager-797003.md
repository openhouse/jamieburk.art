The role turns community participation into coordinated program delivery: shared plans and dependencies, follow-up across teams, staff adoption of tools, partner facilitation and usable reporting. Its program remit spans The People's Money, the TRIE Neighborhood Initiative and voter language access. The supervisory role is Chief Programs Officer. This does not establish who is recruiting or willing to offer a referral.

## Eligibility, distinct from fit

The Community Coordinator minimum routes are a bachelor's degree plus two years of relevant community work; high-school graduation/equivalent plus six years; or an accepted equivalent combination, with at least one year of the specified experience. The City decides how evidence counts. The no-exam designation does not erase these requirements. The posting also states a general NYC residency requirement within 90 days of appointment, with specified exceptions for some existing employees; confirm applicability rather than self-certifying it here.

Preferred evidence should show relevant community partnerships, competing-deadline management, facilitation and training, accessible communication, restorative approaches, digital organizing, data analysis/visualization and advanced office/PM-tool use. Prior participatory-budgeting, sortition or language-access expertise must not be inferred from broad civic interest.
