# New York City Council CouncilStat hackathon

On January 30, 2016, the New York City Council held a constituent-services
hackathon at Civic Hall from 1 to 3 p.m. The bounded public claim is “first
CouncilStat hackathon,” not “first civic-data hackathon.”

The event matters here as context rather than ownership. It provided an early
public-data and service-design setting; Jamie's independently developed
CallNYC.org followed after the fuller CouncilStat dataset became available in
March 2016. See the [corrected CallNYC chronology](../projects/callnyc.md#corrected-chronology).

## What supports the event record

The direct time source is the archived Civic Hall announcement. The Council's
event-day post and promotional graphic corroborate the CouncilStat setting and
Council attribution. A protected Digital District photograph supports only the
visible placard wording and collaborative table context described in the
[photograph boundary](../projects/callnyc.md#digital-district-photograph).
