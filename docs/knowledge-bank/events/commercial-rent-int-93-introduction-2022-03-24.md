# Int. 93 commercial rent stabilization introduction

The Council introduced Int. 93-2022 on 2022-03-24 and referred it to the
Committee on Small Business. Diana I. Ayala was the prime sponsor.

The complete stated-meeting transcript records Ayala at roll call and voting
aye on all. It does **not** record a bill-specific statement from her about
Int. 93. The introduction and sponsor relationship are established by the
official legislation record; a speech is not inferred.
