# Empty Storefronts, High Rents report-release rally

The Action Lab's public event page records a July 29, 2026 rally and press
conference for the release of *Empty Storefronts, High Rents* and advocacy for
the Small Business Rent Stabilization Act.

Protected records establish that Jamie accepted a two-minute speaking role,
prepared a press-release quote, supplied final cultural-space remarks, and
participated in person. The remarks connected predictable commercial rents to
the continuity of jobs, artists, venues, and neighborhoods while keeping the
report's descriptive finding separate from unsupported motive or causal
claims.

## Evidence state

The organizer page and public campaign post establish the event, report, and
legislative context. They do not publish a complete speaker roster or identify
Jamie's delivered words. A named public transcript, organizer caption, press
quotation, or press article quoting Jamie was not recovered in the bounded
search. Participant attestation is therefore retained separately from public
corroboration, and prepared wording is not treated as verbatim delivery.

The live event page also retains a venue-detail discrepancy and “city leaders”
language despite the named bill being state legislation. The page is evidence
of the public event, not an authoritative record of every logistical detail.
