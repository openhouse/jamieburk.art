# Int. 1796 commercial rent stabilization introduction

The Council introduced Int. 1796-2019 on 2019-11-14 and referred it to the
Committee on Small Business. Stephen T. Levin was the prime sponsor.

The complete stated-meeting transcript records Levin at roll call, voting, and
offering remarks about a different bill. It does **not** record a bill-specific
statement from him about Int. 1796. The introduction and sponsor relationship
are established by the official legislation record; a speech is not inferred.
