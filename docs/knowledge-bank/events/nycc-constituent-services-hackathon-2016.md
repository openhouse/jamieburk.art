# New York City Council constituent-services hackathon

## Orientation

On January 30, 2016, the New York City Council held a Civic Hall hackathon
focused on constituent services. The [Civic Hall announcement](../sources/civichall-hackathon-announcement-2016.md)
supports the date, 1-3 p.m. time, Council relationship, and constituent-services
purpose. The [Council's event-day post](../sources/nycc-councilstat-hackathon-post-2016.md)
supports the narrower phrase "first CouncilStat hackathon."

## Source basis

The archived Civic Hall page preserves an embedded social post. It is not a
recovered calendar listing or dedicated event-detail page. The Council post does
not establish the complete agenda, participant roster, winners, or CallNYC as a
formal submission.

## Relationship to CallNYC

[CallNYC](../projects/callnyc.md) followed independently after the fuller
CouncilStat data release. The event provided context; the evidence does not
support Council ownership, commissioning, or formal submission status.
