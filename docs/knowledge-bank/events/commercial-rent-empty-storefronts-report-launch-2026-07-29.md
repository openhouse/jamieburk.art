# *Empty Storefronts, High Rents* report launch

The Action Lab's [public events listing](https://actionlabny.org/events)
records a July 29, 2026 gathering for Small Business United's release of
*Empty Storefronts, High Rents* and a public call for the Small Business Rent
Stabilization Act.

A protected recording and machine transcript establish that Jamie delivered
public remarks. He connected commercial-rent policy to the continuity of
bodegas, cafes, venues, cultural traditions, and the rented rooms where New
York culture is made. He credited the report team and public-data work,
described the report's central relationship carefully as high rents and empty
storefronts going together, explained that stabilization is not a rent freeze,
and made distinct state and city calls to action.

The delivered speech is the important source. It is materially different from
the prepared two-minute statement emailed before the event, and the two must
not be merged. The recording supports the speaking role and broad themes; exact
quotation remains held until the audio is checked against the error-prone
machine transcript.

## Role and credit

Jamie's role was individual public advocacy inside collective campaign work.
The event does not make him author of the report, sole campaign leader, bill
author, legal authority, or cause of any legislative action. A planning email's
run of show is evidence of intended programming, not proof that every listed
official attended or spoke.
