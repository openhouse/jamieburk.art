# Empty Storefronts, High Rents public report launch

The Action Lab and Small Business United held a public report launch on July 29,
2026. A protected authenticated transcript confirms that Jamie delivered a
public statement connecting cultural-space survival, commercial rent
stabilization, public data, and aligned city and state action. State Senator
Julia Salazar and Assembly Member Emily Gallagher, the bill's state sponsors,
also spoke in the same formal program.

This is a strong public-speaking record. Shared appearance in a program does not
establish that another speaker endorses Jamie, that Jamie organized the event,
or that every participant shared authorship of every claim.

## City-office state

Jamie supplied corrected event details to Council Member Christopher Marte's
office and connected the office with the organizers while identifying himself
as a speaker rather than an organizer. Council Member Marte did not speak in the
recovered transcript. Office acknowledgment or presence must not be converted
into Council-member participation, sponsorship, or endorsement.

## External correction state

The public event page still presents a conflicting street number and addresses
“city leaders” even though the bill is state legislation. The public page
corroborates the event and report-release purpose, but not the formal roster,
Jamie's delivered statement, or the corrected logistics.

## Media state

An organizer relayed an urgent request for video from a television outlet. No
resulting broadcast, article, published Jamie quotation, or permission-cleared
media artifact was recovered. A request is a lead, not coverage.
