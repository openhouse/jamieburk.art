# NYC DIY spaces meeting at the Department of Cultural Affairs

## Established event frame

The New York City Department of Cultural Affairs hosted a meeting about DIY and
alternative cultural spaces following the Ghost Ship fire on January 27, 2017.
The recovered event title was **NYC DIY Spaces post Ghost Ship: Dept of Cultural
Affairs Meeting**, and the displayed time was 3-5 p.m.

This is one real-world meeting with more than one surviving platform record.
The DCLA listing and the coalition or Call Script mobilization page are not two
separate events.

## Agency boundary

DCLA hosted the official agency encounter. Call Script, emerging NYC Artist
Coalition participants, and collaborators gathered concerns, developed a
collective response, encouraged venue representation and attendance, and built
continuity after the meeting. Coalition mobilization is not agency hosting, and
the coalition should not be described as the sole organizer of the official
DCLA meeting.

## Participation boundary

Platform response labels are not audited physical attendance, unique people,
endorsement, mandate, conversion, or impact. First-party descriptions of a
packed room remain attributed recollections unless independently corroborated.

## Formation significance

The source sequence shows the work moving from issue gathering, venue outreach,
a provisional name, a collective letter, and meeting mobilization into
follow-up infrastructure, mutual aid, working groups, priority selection, and a
revised government-facing submission.

The February 6 priority process was a top-three approval ballot, not ranked
choice voting. The later submission was a refined collective output, not a
mechanical reproduction of the ballot order.

## Open record

The meeting audio, speaker research, interactive visualization, exact remarks,
complete participant record, and the photographer's identity remain open
research. Non-recovery is not evidence that these records never existed.
