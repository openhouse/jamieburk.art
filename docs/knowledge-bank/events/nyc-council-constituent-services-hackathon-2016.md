# New York City Council constituent-services hackathon

## Orientation

The New York City Council held a constituent-services hackathon at Civic Hall
on January 30, 2016, from 1 to 3 p.m. The Council's event-day post called it its
first CouncilStat hackathon. This narrower description is the supported
superlative.

## Why it matters

The event created the public-sector context from which Jamie later developed
[CallNYC](../projects/callnyc.md) as an independent interpretation of the fuller
CouncilStat constituent-services data.

## Evidence and limits

[Civic Hall's announcement](../sources/civic-hall-hackathon-announcement-2016.md)
supports the date, hours, Council involvement, and constituent-services
purpose. [The Council's post](../sources/nyc-council-councilstat-post-2016.md)
supports the venue, date, CouncilStat context, and the phrase "first CouncilStat
hackathon."

The recovered evidence does not establish a complete formal event title,
agenda, participant roster, winners, or CallNYC as an official submission.
