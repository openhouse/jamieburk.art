# New York City Council Constituent-Services Hackathon

## Orientation

Civic Hall announced the January 30, 2016 gathering for 1–3 p.m. and described
its constituent-services focus. The Council described the gathering that day as
its first CouncilStat hackathon.

## Evidence And Limits

The [Civic Hall announcement](../sources/civic-hall-hackathon-announcement-2016.md)
supports the date, time, and purpose. The
[Council post](../sources/nyc-council-councilstat-hackathon-post-2016.md)
supports the attributed CouncilStat wording. Neither establishes a complete
agenda, roster, formal submission list, or CallNYC as an official Council
product.

## Related Records

Jamie later developed [CallNYC](../projects/callnyc.md) independently after the
fuller CouncilStat records became available.
