# In Terms of Media symposium

The University of Applied Arts Vienna describes this 2024 Weibel Institute
symposium through four themes: data materialism, techno-poetics, atmospheres of
conflict, and planetary interfaces. Jamie's contemporaneous first-person
record says he traveled from Innsbruck to attend while developing Compassion
Software and continued conversations afterward.

## Boundary

The public program establishes the event. Jamie's record supports attendance
and his own working purpose, not a formal invitation, program credit,
institutional affiliation, adoption, or another person's characterization of
him. Private conversation remains private unless every relevant publication
