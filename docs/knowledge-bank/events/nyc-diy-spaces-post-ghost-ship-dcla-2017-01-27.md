# NYC DIY Spaces Meeting at the Department of Cultural Affairs

On January 27, 2017, the New York City Department of Cultural Affairs hosted a
meeting about DIY and alternative cultural spaces following the Ghost Ship
fire. A coalition / Call Script event page mobilized participation around the
official agency encounter. These were two platform records for one meeting.

## Why It Matters

The surviving record places the meeting inside a formation interval for NYC
Artist Coalition. Before and around the meeting, participants gathered issues,
shared a public letter, tested a collective name, recruited venue
representatives and facilitators, and documented resources. A follow-up meeting,
recommendation selection, working groups, and a revised government-facing
letter followed.

This chronology supports continuity and operating structure. It does not make
the meeting a single moment of legal formation, assign sole authorship, or turn
every participant contribution into coalition policy.

## Evidence Boundary

Contemporaneous notes preserve issue categories and follow-up work, not a
transcript or complete roster. Platform response labels are engagement fields,
not audited attendance. A later recollection identifies the standing speaker
in the related photograph, but the image does not preserve her words.
