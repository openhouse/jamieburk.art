# AI Made Me Do It symposium and exhibition

Studio3 at the University of Innsbruck describes this 2024 symposium and
exhibition as an inquiry into artificial intelligence in everyday life,
architecture, spatial practice, data ethics, labor, and environmental impact.
Jamie's contemporaneous first-person record says he traveled to attend while
developing Compassion Software.

## Boundary

his attendance and working purpose. Neither establishes a formal invitation,
presentation credit, institutional affiliation, adoption of his work, or
