# Office Of Nightlife Town Hall At Market Hotel

The October 11, 2017 event titled “Tell NYC's Night Mayor: Save NYC Spaces”
placed the newly established Office of Nightlife in public dialogue with
cultural-space communities at Market Hotel. Public records establish the date,
venue, institutional context, and coalition role.

Protected production records support a broad event system involving public
officials, cultural speakers, questions, projection, sound, seating,
volunteers, documentation, and follow-up. They identify Jamie and Olympia Kazi
as organizers opening the program. The records are not a complete labor ledger
and do not transfer the event's collective authorship to two people.

## Why It Mattered

The town hall made governance encounterable in a cultural venue where
operators, artists, and community members could frame questions from lived
practice. It demonstrates an intermediary function at event scale: convene in
an affected place, produce a shared civic setting, document priorities, and
build routes for follow-up.

## Boundary

Credit Jamie and Olympia only for supported roles while retaining the distinct
labor of cohosts, venue workers, speakers, volunteers, officials, and
participants. A Facebook response is not attendance or unique people. The
event does not prove recommendation adoption or that Jamie created the Office.
Private feedback, contacts, remarks, participant lists, and production
coordinates remain protected.
