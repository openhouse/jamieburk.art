# WOW List X full-population archival-production run

Date: 2026-07-15  
Account: `@wowlist`  
Surface: authenticated replies-inclusive X profile  
Signed-in session verified as: `@urbanhermit`

## Question

What does the complete profile-reported `@wowlist` post population establish about product operation, organizer use, posted sources, stakeholder engagement, mission-relevant patterns, and visible traction?

## Population method

1. Verified the signed-in browser identity and opened `https://x.com/wowlist/with_replies`.
2. Recorded the profile-reported denominator of 38 posts.
3. Scrolled the complete timeline in overlapping increments while deduplicating canonical status IDs.
4. Continued through repeated bottom-of-timeline passes that produced no new IDs.
5. Reconciled 38 distinct recovered IDs to the 38-post profile denominator.
6. Classified authored posts separately from reposts.
7. Opened each project-account reply conversation and recorded recoverable public parent context.
8. Resolved every t.co short URL to its immediate destination, using HTTPS for historical HTTP t.co links when the HTTP endpoint returned a proxy error.
9. Kept visible engagement on reposted originals outside WOW List traction totals.
10. Decomposed the evidence into sources, assertions, bounded claims, anti-claims, research tasks, and selective public projection.

## Results

- Population: 38 of 38 profile-reported items recovered.
- Composition: 22 authored posts and 16 reposts.
- Range: February 12, 2014-January 12, 2017.
- Replies: six authored posts appear as replies; three public parent questions were recovered and one parent was deleted.
- Links: 35 short-URL occurrences in the full population.
- Authored links: 23 occurrences across 19 posts, comprising 12 WOW List destinations, two NYCdiy destinations, and nine external destinations.
- Public support: three recovered threads concern location scope, finding personal lists, and excessive clicks in the add-event workflow.
- Organizer/collaborator use: Punks & Criminals reported adding shows; Richard tagged the account with an event; COTFG linked a WOW List event page.
- Civic/care pattern: eight items linked or amplified demonstrations, vigils, fundraisers, or mutual-aid resources.
- Field-learning pattern: a member-made tutorial, grassroots venue guidance, DIY documentation reporting, Allied Media Conference, and peer DIY funding infrastructure.
- Dated visible engagement: 12 of 22 authored posts showed interaction; totals were two replies, 20 reposts, and 21 likes.

## Editorial decisions

Promote the product-support loop and civic/care use pattern to the WOW List case study because they clarify how the platform operated and what it became useful for.

Keep the engagement totals in the knowledge bank. They are a current platform snapshot, not a durable accomplishment claim.

Treat the Good Times article *Zines 2.0* as a source lead. The account described it as reporting on documenting and connecting DIY communities, but the article body was not recovered in this run.

Attribute all account-level patterns to WOW List or the project team. Do not infer that Jamie authored each post or reply.

## Public-safety boundary

Only public profile, post, and conversation surfaces were inspected. No direct messages, account settings, follower export, private analytics, or session data were captured. The committed raw artifact contains public post text, URLs, dates, and visible interaction labels only.
