## Public-Safety Boundary

- Folder access is not publication permission.
- Filenames, location, ownership, and modification metadata are not authorship.
- Private notes, correspondence, participant data, contact records, strategy,
  completed forms, identifiable media, credentials, and font binaries remain
  outside git.
- Internal activity records cannot independently prove public outcomes.
- Official and independent sources remain authoritative for legislation,
  agency action, public recognition, and press-reported outcomes.
- Collaborator credit, media rights, participant consent, Jamie approval, and
  application judgment remain human gates.
