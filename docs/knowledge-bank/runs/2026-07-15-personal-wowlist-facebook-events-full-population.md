# Personal and WOW List Facebook Events Full-Population Run

## Question

What event population is currently exposed by Jamie Burkart's personal hosted
Facebook events and the WOW List Page, and what public-safe project, source,
response, and stakeholder findings can be integrated into the knowledge bank?

## Method

The review used authenticated task tabs while keeping account credentials,
cookies, session state, and raw captures outside the public repository.

For Jamie's personal account, the Past control was scrolled until eight
consecutive rounds produced no new slots. The traversal was repeated and
returned the same 21-slot population. All 20 public event identities were
reviewed; the one private event received only an identity-free withheld
disposition.

For WOW List, both the Page events view and the Page-hosted Past control were
reviewed while authenticated as the Page. Both currently exposed zero event
records.

Each public event was decomposed into public-safe date, title, setting category,
organizer display, summary, topics, historical response labels, posted resource
routes, and retrieval state. Private addresses, phone numbers, guest identities,
comments, private-event identity, and raw descriptions were excluded.

## Result

- 21 personal hosted-event slots accounted for;
- 20 public-safe event records;
- one private-withheld disposition;
- 19 materialized public detail pages and one unavailable detail;
- public chronology from 2006 through 2019;
- 16 distinct posted resource routes;
- no independent news article recovered from this population;
- current WOW List Page event population of zero on both reviewed surfaces.

The event record documents a long-running practice across participatory art and
technology, waterway work, shared meals, music, collective performance, mutual
care, civic reflection, and venue safety. It also adds dated corroboration to
Sunday Dinner, WOW List, waterway participation, and *NTER CHNG*.

## Response discipline

Nineteen detail pages display nonzero historical response labels; 13 are at
least 10, eight at least 20, and three at least 100. Index and detail values
conflict for several records. No values were converted to attendance or summed
into a unique-people, reach, endorsement, or impact claim.

## Limits

The result is complete current-interface accounting, not a native Meta owner
export or proof that every historical event remains visible. Current WOW List
Page zero does not establish historical absence. Organizer displays do not
assign every planning or production task. No NYC Council-member engagement
record was identified within the reviewed event-page metadata.

Every currently displayed slot has a disposition. Events deleted or made
unavailable before capture remain outside the recovered population and are not
invented from gaps in the interface.

## Disposition

The corpus, manifest, source records, assertions, bounded claims, research
tasks, and project notes were integrated. Public website projection remains
selective and held pending project-level need, role corroboration, and resolution
of the Sunday Dinner milestone conflict.
