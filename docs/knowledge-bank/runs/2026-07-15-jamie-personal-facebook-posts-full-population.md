# Jamie Personal Facebook Posts: Full-Population Archival Production

Date: July 15, 2026

## Method

1. Used Facebook's authenticated Manage Posts surface.
2. Applied **Posted by: You** before population accounting.
3. Traversed 621 cursor pages until Facebook returned no next page.
4. Deduplicated 3,728 returned nodes into 1,243 unique records.
5. Verified zero missing dates and zero owner-absent records.
6. Replayed the filter on July 15 and confirmed the June 2022 newest boundary.
7. Ran deterministic, overlapping mission, source, and stakeholder research routing.
8. Reopened six selected posts individually and confirmed their public audience state.
9. Published only aggregate controls, governed public sources, claims,
   boundaries, anti-claims, research tasks, and one opaque protected-artifact ID.

## Population result

The returned surface runs from December 19, 2006 through June 12, 2022. All
1,243 unique records received a disposition.

For this run, 100 percent means every record returned by the owner-filtered
surface. It is not a native Meta owner export, deletion history, or immutable
lifetime archive.
