# @KCTownHall full-population archival production

Run date: 2026-07-15

Population: 181 of 183 profile-reported account items recovered; two-item gap retained

Conversation context: seven separately rendered public records, excluded from the account-post denominator

## Method

1. Opened the public `@KCTownHall` account in an authenticated browser session as Jamie's `@urbanhermit` account.
2. Traversed the replies-inclusive timeline to exhaustion and deduplicated canonical status IDs.
3. Cross-checked the posts-only timeline, authored search, and a launch-window search.
4. Separated account items from separately rendered public conversation context.
5. Resolved all 31 recovered `t.co` destinations, including normalized visible destinations where an expired short link no longer redirected.
6. Classified authored posts, reposts, top-level replies, media, links, mission patterns, visible incoming stakeholder interactions, and dated visible engagement.
7. Close-read recoverable mission-relevant external sources and retained inaccessible pages as source leads.
8. Recovered internal project routes through the Wayback Machine and distinguished directly recovered pages from root-page cards.
9. Excluded private messages, account settings, private analytics, personal interaction state, credentials, follower exports, browser storage, and session data. Public contact numbers were redacted.

## Stakeholder engagement rule

Incoming engagement requires a visible reply or quote-post connected to a recovered KC Town Hall post. Outbound mentions, tags, and general posts by a stakeholder do not count.

Under that rule, the preserved conversation records show a lower bound of three then-sitting Council-member accounts:

- Melissa Robinson replied in an August 2020 tire-program thread.
- Jolie Justus replied in an April 2019 Leon's Thriftway thread and in an August 2018 service thread.
- Quinton Lucas quote-posted an April 2019 KC Town Hall message about Leon's Thriftway.

KCMO 311 also visibly replied in the August 2018 neighborhood-service thread.

Official or contemporaneous public records corroborate the Council roles. The interaction evidence establishes dialogue, not endorsement, policy causation, complete stakeholder participation, or completed service resolution.

## URLs and source leads

The 181 recovered items contain 133 outgoing-link occurrences. The 155 authored posts contain 130 occurrences across 115 posts: 119 KC Town Hall destinations and eleven external destinations.

Mission-relevant appraised sources include voting resources from KCUR and the Missouri Secretary of State, Northeast News housing-policy coverage, RideKC transit context, two KC Town Hall YouTube records, a KC Star grocery-access lead, a dead GoFundMe lead, and two Facebook leads. Reposted links remain attributed to their original posts.

`Not recovered` remains distinct from `did not exist`. A title or destination alone is not promoted into the article's substantive argument.

## Held observations

Seventy-seven of 155 authored posts displayed at least one visible interaction on the capture date. The visible aggregate was 22 replies, 70 reposts, 174 likes, and one bookmark. These volatile totals remain held from accomplishment messaging.

The account also self-reported 142 tires collected, no tires left on the curb, and $17,768 in avoided costs in August 2020. The archived project page repeats the savings figure, making it corroborated project documentation but not independent verification. Those figures remain held pending independent corroboration. Melissa Robinson's reply in the thread recognizes community work but does not audit the numbers.
