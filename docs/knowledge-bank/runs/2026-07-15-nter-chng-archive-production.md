# NTER CHNG archival-production run

Date: July 15, 2026  
Operator: Codex, public-source review with Jamie Burkart

## Question

What public sources establish Jamie's role in *NTER CHNG*, the installation's
form, and its later inclusion in *America: Now and Here*?

## Method

1. Inventoried deduplicated Wayback captures for `nterchng.com`.
2. Recovered the original landing page and a later capture containing the
   surviving Vimeo project embed.
3. Identified `americanowandhere.org` from contemporaneous public coverage.
4. Inventoried the national domain and its Kansas City subdomain for 2010-2013.
5. Searched URL keys and recovered page bodies for the project title,
   collaborators, and Cocoon Gallery.
6. Cross-checked the resulting record against The Pitch, Vimeo metadata, and
   the Nerman Museum exhibition page.

## Recovered

- The archived project site directly credits Drew Bolton, Jamie Burkart, and
  Garrett Fuselier and identifies the work as an interactive texting
  installation at Arts Incubator Cocoon Gallery.
- A January 2010 *Pitch* listing describes the software-and-architecture form
  and gives a January 8, 2010 event date.
- The surviving 50-second Vimeo project video confirms the collective credit
  and names additional production participants.
- The archived *America: Now and Here Kansas City* site preserves a dedicated
  visual-artists page for Bolton, Burkart, and Fuselier with the *NTER CHNG*
  artist statement.
- The Nerman Museum record confirms the exhibition's Kansas City launch and
  local-plus-national artist structure.

## Not Recovered

- The press-release PDF linked from the original project page was not present
  in the reviewed `nterchng.com` Wayback capture inventory.
- No complete technical architecture, source-code record, audience count,
  message corpus, or role-specific production breakdown was recovered.
- No evidence was recovered that *NTER CHNG* appeared at every national-tour
  stop.

"Not recovered" does not mean the material did not exist or does not survive
elsewhere.
