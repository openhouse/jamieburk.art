# WOW List Facebook post archival-production run

Date: 2026-07-15

## Question

What does the full surviving WOW List Facebook post surface establish about
Jamie Burkart's publishing role, the project's operating practice, posted
sources, mission-relevant patterns, and public traction?

## Population result

The authenticated Lifetime table reached a terminal no-growth state at 54
unique records. Fifty detail pages rendered and four remained table-only. All
50 recovered details displayed Facebook's admin-only **Published by Jamie
Burkart** attribution.

The chronology spans April 25, 2015 through March 22, 2018. This is complete-as-
materialized current-surface accounting, not a Meta owner export, deletion
history, or claim that no other records ever existed.

## Findings

- The record supports Jamie's Facebook publishing stewardship while preserving
  WOW List as shared work and distinguishing publishing from sole authorship.
- The public-safe corpus preserves 42 normalized destinations.
- Overlapping patterns connect calendars and events with cultural-space
  survival, mutual aid, civic action, care, funding, and product feedback.
- One posted Westword article was close-read as source context; the remaining
  destinations stay in the research lifecycle.
- July 15 legacy-row displays total 108 interactions, 512 impressions, and 11
  comments. They remain archival observations, not accomplishment metrics.
- No complete engager-identity population was recovered, so no stakeholder-
  group engagement count is claimed.

## Privacy and public safety

The committed corpus contains record IDs, dates, recovery and publisher states,
normalized public URLs, overlapping classifications, bounded current metrics,
and content fingerprints. It contains no full post bodies, comments, engager
identities, private profile links, direct messages, cookies, tokens, browser
storage, session identifiers, credentials, or private filesystem paths.

## Recursive hill climb

The initial family of passes disagreed over 57 feed renderings and 54 unique
management records. Reconciliation showed that the larger number included
platform wrappers or duplicate renderings. The accepted denominator is 54
stable post identities from the authenticated Lifetime table, with four
table-only details retained rather than silently dropped.

The final eval rejects missing records, duplicate IDs, altered chronology,
publisher inflation, sole-authorship language, summed overlapping themes,
interaction-to-impact inflation, stakeholder-count invention, raw social copy,
authentication leakage, and private paths.

## Projection decision

Project one bounded publishing-stewardship paragraph on `/work/wowlist`. Keep
the archive mechanics, dashboard values, migration gap, and unresolved
stakeholder analysis in the knowledge bank.
