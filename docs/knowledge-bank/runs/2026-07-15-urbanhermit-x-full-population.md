## Method

1. Verified the authenticated profile and its 434-post control.
2. Traversed the replies-inclusive timeline three times through twelve
   consecutive no-growth scroll observations.
3. Recovered 434 primary profile records and separated two conversation-parent
   cards authored by another account.
4. Verified that all three independent passes produced the same 434-status set.
5. Separated account-authored records from external-source native reposts while
   retaining external authorship.
6. Inventoried posted links and applied a checked-in overlapping mission-signal
   manifest to source-post bodies; quoted-card text was excluded.
7. Ran authenticated incoming searches in year windows from 2008 through 2026,
   then directly verified one live mission record omitted by current search.
8. Preserved nine non-mission personal or network results only as an aggregate
   redaction disposition with no public identity, date, URL, or metrics.
9. Stored the 770,372-byte full capture in Jamie's protected archive. The
   public manifest retains a non-locating integrity digest; the archive path
   and private locator remain outside this repository.

## Recursive evaluation return

Independent archive, privacy, and Chad-lens reviews found four defects in the
first candidate: capture assertions were trusted rather than independently
recomputed; protected locator metadata was public; the resume's WOW List entry
conflated recorded activity with individually secured adoption; and the
project-specific visual feedback path was not inspectable.

The revised generator now rejects duplicate primary and incoming status IDs,
incomplete or unequal acquisition passes, overlapping context records, and the
wrong incoming query or year window. Hostile synthetic captures exercise those
failures in the deterministic checker. Public artifacts retain a non-locating
integrity digest without a private path or locator. The resume now says the
platform was used by organizers, with recorded activity across roughly 35 city
ecosystems, and says Jamie supported organizer use. The held claim palette also
generated a bounded [photo-editor brief](../briefs/urbanhermit-photo-editor-brief.md)
whose discoveries must return through intake and inquiry.
