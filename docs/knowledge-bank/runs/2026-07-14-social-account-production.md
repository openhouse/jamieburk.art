# Social-account archival production run

Date: July 14, 2026  
Operator: Codex, using Jamie Burkart's authenticated in-app browser session  
Scope: public X profiles and public posts for recovered project accounts

## Question

What public-safe evidence of project identity, official engagement, collaborator adoption, organizer use, public listening, and mission-relevant traction can the project accounts establish?

## Authentication check

The browser session displayed Jamie's signed-in `@urbanhermit` identity before research began. Searches and timelines were observed through that authenticated session. Authentication improved access but did not make X search or timeline rendering complete.

## Method

1. Identified project handles from public project sites, historical repositories, and authenticated profile pages.
2. Recorded account-level metadata as a dated snapshot.
3. Scrolled each rendered profile timeline and deduplicated canonical status URLs.
4. Searched direct mentions of CallNYC and NYC Artist Coalition.
5. Queried a historical NYC Council handle roster in small groups and inspected every returned post before counting it.
6. Distinguished people serving on the Council at the interaction date from later officeholders.
7. Decomposed significant posts into sources, source assertions, claims, boundaries, anti-claims, research tasks, and selective public projections.

## Count rules

For Council-member engagement, a person counts once per project when a visible dated post was authored by an account attributable to that person while they were serving on the Council and the post directly replied to, quote-posted, retransmitted, named, or linked the project account or site.

Excluded:

- likes and follows;
- private messages or private account data;
- hidden replies and deleted or unavailable posts;
- outbound project-account mentions without an official's response;
- people who became Council members after the interaction;
- posts returned only because another account in the conversation named the project.

## Limitations

- X rendering and search are capped and may omit visible historical posts even in an authenticated session.
- The run did not inspect private messages, credentials, settings, account analytics, or private follower data.
- Current profile counts can change after the observation date.
- Public posts do not identify the author behind a shared account unless the post says so.
- No dedicated Harry J. Epstein or Sunday Dinner / 196 account was recovered. This is not proof that none existed.
