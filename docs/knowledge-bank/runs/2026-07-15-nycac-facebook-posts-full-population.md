# NYC Artist Coalition Facebook posts archival-production run

Date: 2026-07-15

## Question

What does 100% of the available NYC Artist Coalition Facebook post population
establish about project operation, source circulation, traction, stakeholder
interfaces, and Jamie's role?

## Protected inputs

- Authenticated NYC Artist Coalition Page management access.
- Five annual Meta Business Suite Published exports covering 2017-2021.
- Terminal-scroll feed captures and private platform-identity reconciliation.
- Raw post bodies, permalinks, post and Page IDs, and row-level metrics.

The protected inputs are not committed. The public repository contains only
one-way hashes, dates, bounded classifications, public-safe routes, aggregate
findings, and explicit exclusions.

## Population result

- 444 annual Published-export rows.
- 444 unique exported post IDs.
- 445 public-safe terminal-feed ledger rows.
- Owner exports: 185 / 74 / 111 / 69 / 5 rows in 2017 / 2018 / 2019 / 2020 / 2021.
- Feed ledger: 186 / 74 / 111 / 69 / 5 rows in 2017 / 2018 / 2019 / 2020 / 2021.
- January 29, 2017 through September 15, 2021.
- 598 encountered feed render rows, with 153 duplicate or embedded variants
  excluded through protected reconciliation.

This is 100% of all 444 owner-export rows and all 445 distinct feed identities
available on the review date. The populations are complete independently; no
one-to-one row crosswalk is asserted because Meta exposes no reproducible shared
key between the surfaces. This is not proof of every post ever created or
recovery of deleted, hidden, private, unpublished, or no-longer-retained posts.

## Findings

The shared Page operated as collective communications infrastructure across
several advocacy arcs. Its 67 distinct off-Facebook routes include campaign
tools, public meetings, official records, issue reporting, resources, and
intake surfaces. Sixty-five routes are published exactly; two sensitive routes
are represented by hashes and host classes. Nine routes map to governed source
records, while 56 remain a preservation and close-reading queue.

Mission and stakeholder classifications overlap. Sixty-six rows connect to
Council or elected-official subjects, but those rows record Page-authored
references, tags, routes, or issue interfaces. They do not prove incoming
engagement by 66 officials. Incoming engagement remains a separate research
question requiring an identity-complete denominator.

The owner exports preserve capture-date platform values across all 444 rows:
2,589 reactions, 295 comments, 552 shares, 48,044 summed post reach, and 2,190
clicks. Summed post reach is not unique people, and aggregate metrics do not
identify stakeholder groups, attendance, endorsement, conversion, mandate, or
impact.

Jamie remembers predominantly using the Page while believing other
collaborators also used it. Current access confirms present custody; neither the
shared Page nor the exports establish the individual historical publisher of
each post.

## Publication decision

The corpus and claims enter the public knowledge bank. All three projections
remain held from the website because the collective operating record is strong
while individual Facebook stewardship remains unresolved. Existing public
sources already support stronger Jamie-specific coalition claims.

## Recursive criterion

The dedicated evaluator must score 100/100 and reject mutations that drop or
duplicate a record, weaken the 100% boundary, assign the shared feed to Jamie,
convert outgoing references into incoming engagement, convert aggregate
metrics into unique people or impact, infer no sharing from the feed surface,
publish sensitive routes, or leak protected values.
