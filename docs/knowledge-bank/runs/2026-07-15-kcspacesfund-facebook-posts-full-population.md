# KC Spaces Fund Facebook posts archival-production run

Date: July 15, 2026  
Operator: Codex, using Jamie Burkart's authenticated Facebook session  
Scope: the complete surviving public post population exposed by the KC Spaces
Fund Page's 2020 filter

## Question

What does the full surviving KC Spaces Fund Facebook Page population establish
about public operations, circulated sources, traction, stakeholder patterns,
and Jamie's role?

## Authentication and access

The Facebook session was authenticated as Jamie. The public KC Spaces Fund Page
and its 2020 filter were accessible. The Page was not among the assets exposed
through Jamie's current Meta Business Suite management view, so no owner export,
admin analytics, or publisher bylines were available.

Authentication improved access to the surviving public Page. It did not create
owner-level evidence or recover deleted, hidden, private, unpublished, or
no-longer-retained posts.

## Method

1. Opened the public Page and applied its 2020 filter.
2. Scrolled repeatedly to the terminal record and required four consecutive
   stable checks with no visible loading boundary.
3. Reconciled 38 distinct rendered records through protected identifiers.
4. Recovered a publication date for every record and classified overlapping
   mission and stakeholder-reference patterns.
5. Extracted and normalized nine distinct public external routes.
6. Kept outgoing Page references separate from incoming engagement.
7. Stored current reaction and follower displays only with explicit metric
   boundaries.
8. Close-read the public campaign site and fundraiser-print record, and retained
   access-restricted media sources at the level actually recovered.
9. Published a one-way-hash ledger while excluding raw text, post IDs, tokens,
   comments, identities, authenticated routes, and private contact details.

## Population result

The traversal accounts for all 38 distinct records exposed by the authenticated
2020 filter, from April 7 through July 9, 2020. It preserves 33 recovered public
bodies, two unavailable embedded sources, and three unrecovered bodies.

For this run, 100 percent means every record in that surviving public surface.
It does not mean every post ever created or deleted-post recovery.

## Findings

- Twelve records concern grants or grantee documentation.
- Ten route applications or eligibility information.
- Twenty-three concern fundraising or mutual-aid prints.
- Six concern coalition or volunteer participation.
- One circulates published media.
- Nine distinct routes connect the Page to project, application, volunteer,
  fundraiser, recipient-context, and media surfaces.
- Eleven visible named grantee or funded-space highlights corroborate the
  separately preserved project-account record.
- Twenty-eight rows displayed 119 reactions on the capture date; no stakeholder
  identities were recovered.
- The current Page displayed 108 followers, a current snapshot rather than a
  2020 reach measure.
