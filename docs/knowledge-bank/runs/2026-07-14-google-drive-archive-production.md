# Google Drive Shared Drives Archival Production

**Run date:** 2026-07-14
**Scope:** Fonds-level appraisal of 110 accessible Shared Drives, followed by
bounded close reading of selected civic, cultural, research, and collaborator-
handoff records.
**Public-safety rule:** The repo stores source descriptions, atomic assertions,
claim boundaries, dispositions, and research tasks. It does not store Drive
IDs, private locators, raw documents, revision text, contact data, participant
records, credentials, legal or financial material, or unapproved media.

## Appraisal Method

1. Inventory the Shared Drive environment at fonds level.
2. Separate project evidence from personal, administrative, recovery, and
   media-only collections.
3. Close-read high-signal records with direct relevance to existing portfolio
   claims.
4. Compare source contents, revision history, folder structure, and existing
   public sources.
5. Decompose useful records into atomic assertions before promoting a claim.
6. Record duplicates, privacy holds, provenance limits, collective credit, and
   open research instead of silently discarding them.

The 110-drive count describes an appraised records environment. It is not a
claim of 110 projects, accomplishments, or complete collections.
