## Recursive hill climb

knowledge-bank shape. It was recast into the repository's current intake,
source, claim, and inquiry schema rather than replacing the established graph.
The 40-row public corpus moved beside the other governed corpora, gained a
manifest and protected-capture verifier, and was wired into the generated
public citation registry.

The first local evaluator run exposed two evaluator defects: its dimensions
summed to 110 rather than 100, and literal phrase checks did not normalize
Markdown line wrapping. The scoring contract was corrected to 100 points and
whitespace normalization was added without weakening any substantive check.
The evaluator then scored 100/100. An independent archival holdout found that
the immutable corpus digest protected the checked-in file, but the exported
semantic checker still allowed route, aggregate, source-identity, raw-field,
and publisher mutations to escape in memory. The checker was tightened with
strict public schemas, exact route and spotlight controls, aggregate
reconciliation, profile and date controls, full manifest validation, and
broader path/session checks. A second holdout found that the first allowlist did
not yet constrain unexpected fields nested inside public objects. Every nested
corpus and manifest object now has an explicit schema. The suite rejects 22
unsafe corpus mutations and eight unsafe manifest mutations. A final semantic
probe showed that generic lifecycle validation checks links rather than prose;
the KC-specific evaluator now also pins the approved projection and proof
wording, canonical link, review date, guardrails, and anti-claims, rejecting
five role-contract mutations.

The same holdout found that the stronger cross-channel wording had not yet been
propagated through the approval register, human-readable claims, proof summary,

approval for bounded identity-support wording; the registers were synchronized,
the new claim was linked alongside the earlier social record, and the Technical

than hard-coded copy.

A separate Chad-lens holdout then found two website paths the first contract did

The evaluator rejects hard-coding either location, removing the claim resolver,
or bypassing the proof-grid override. The final independent Chad-lens holdout

The first outer repository run correctly reported the citation registry as
