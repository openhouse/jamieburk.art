# CallNYC Workshop Date And Time

On January 30, 2016, the New York City Council held a 1–3 p.m. hackathon at
Civic Hall focused on constituent services.

The [Civic Hall announcement](../sources/civic-hall-hackathon-announcement-2016.md)
directly supports the date, time, Council event, and purpose. The
[Council post](../sources/nyc-council-councilstat-hackathon-post-2016.md)
corroborates the date, venue, and CouncilStat context. A participant photograph
timestamp is not the event start time.
