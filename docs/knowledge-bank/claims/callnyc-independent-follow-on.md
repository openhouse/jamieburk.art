# CallNYC Independent Follow-On Chronology

After the fuller CouncilStat dataset was released, Jamie developed CallNYC.org
as an independent public-facing interpretation of those constituent-services
records.

The [Politico coverage](../sources/politico-callnyc-2016-03-14.md) supports the
sequence and Jamie's role. It does not establish CallNYC as commissioned by the
Council, an official Council product, a formal hackathon submission, or a
documented winner.
