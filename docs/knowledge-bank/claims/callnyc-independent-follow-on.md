# CallNYC independent follow-on claim

## Current account

After the fuller CouncilStat constituent-services data became available, Jamie
independently developed CallNYC.org as a public-facing interpretation of those
records. The project translated public data into issue pathways, district
context, and resident-facing next-step guidance.

Exact approved wording, evidence relationships, and surface-specific
projections remain canonical in `CLM-CALLNYC-INDEPENDENT-FOLLOW-ON`.

## Evidence and limits

[Politico New York's reporting](../sources/politico-callnyc-2016.md) provides the
strongest direct support for the sequence and Jamie's role. The
[Civic Hall announcement](../sources/civic-hall-hackathon-announcement-2016.md)
and [Council event-day post](../sources/nyc-council-councilstat-post-2016.md)
establish the bounded event context.

Do not say Jamie caused the CouncilStat release, that the Council commissioned
CallNYC, or that CallNYC was a winning hackathon submission. See the
[unofficial-product anti-claim](../anti-claims/callnyc-official-product.md).
