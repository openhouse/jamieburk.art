# Jamie completed a bounded prepublication report review

## Bounded proposition

In July 2026, Jamie completed a prepublication review of an Action Lab report,
focusing on factual accuracy, source and methods clarity, current bill
language, and the scope of claims likely to travel through public discussion.
The report team said it incorporated many of his edits.

## Boundary and promotion state

Jamie explicitly did not rerun the analysis. The record does not support
authorship, analytic replication, legal review, or adoption of every
recommendation.

This candidate claim remains off the website until a public report,
acknowledgement, or separately approved organizational statement supplies a
public citation and final wording.
