# Shared NYC Artist Coalition advocacy operating playbook

## Claim

A protected working guide identifies Julia Fredenburg and Jamie as its writers
and makes a repeatable civic operating method explicit:

1. facilitate community meetings and listen for the issues;
2. narrow a long issue list into focused priorities;
3. build a diverse citywide coalition and mutual-support network;
4. translate recommendations into concise materials and concrete Council
   actions;
5. bring situated stories to representatives, public forums, and hearings;
6. sustain follow-through across meetings, public communication, press, and
   calls.

The surviving public event and campaign surfaces corroborate the method's
visible form. The protected guide supplies the shared-authorship and explicit
operating logic.

## Boundaries

Credit Julia and Jamie together for the guide. Keep coalition members, partner
groups, venue hosts, artists, officials, and public institutions inside the
larger field of agency. The guide is method evidence, not an impact evaluation,
attendance record, or causal allocation for legislation.

The website may draw on this finding through an existing, publicly corroborated
participation-system claim. This standalone claim remains held until Jamie and
collaborator review establish a specific audience need.
