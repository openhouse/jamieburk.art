# NYC Artist Coalition Nightlife Recommendation Continuity

Protected records from 2017 through 2019 repeatedly translate cultural-space
experience into concrete agency proposals and, by 2019, prioritize asks by
what the Office of Nightlife could implement.

The sequence supports collective continuity, requirements translation, and an
implementation orientation. It does not establish Jamie's authorship of every
proposal, agency adoption of every recommendation, or sole coalition or
individual causality for an institutional outcome. Public use remains held
pending attribution and editorial review.
