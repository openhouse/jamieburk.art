# NYC Artist Coalition and Fair Rent NYC frontline advocacy, July-August 2026

The bounded source window is July 14 through August 13, 2026. It supports three
accomplishments without collapsing their evidence or authority.

## 1. Credited report review

Jamie completed a detailed prepublication review of *Empty Storefronts, High
Rents*. Protected partner correspondence says many edits were incorporated;
the final public report separately credits his thoughtful and careful review.

This is a meaningful research-quality and editorial contribution. It is not
report authorship, data analysis, research-design ownership, acceptance of
every edit, or sole credit for the report's limits.

## 2. Frontline public speaking

Jamie spoke in the formal July 29 press-conference program as an NYC Artist
Coalition member participating in Fair Rent NYC. The record supports his
cultural-space and public-data speaking lane. It does not yet support a
verbatim delivered quotation, press placement, interview, broadcast, or an
elected official's endorsement.

## 3. Government-relations follow-through

Jamie completed a City Council office event-information handoff. After a later
campaign follow-up, state legislative staff proposed recurring alignment with
Jamie and a campaign organizer and scheduled a first future meeting for August
26.

The first item is completed routing. The second is a scheduled channel, not a
meeting that had already occurred at the cutoff. Neither establishes office
endorsement, a personal commitment by an elected official, sponsorship,
passage, or implementation.

## Why the projection remains held

The report acknowledgment is already public and the three accomplishments are
well supported. The combined portfolio wording still requires Jamie's
editorial approval, original-audio review for any speech quotation, a current
status check after August 26, and a correction-propagation plan across the
federated ecosystem.
