# NYCAC and Fair Rent NYC frontline advocacy, July-August 2026

## Claim

During the July 13-August 12, 2026 review window, Jamie contributed a bounded
prepublication review that the [final report](../sources/empty-storefronts-high-rents-2026-07-28.md)
publicly acknowledged, delivered a
public cultural-space argument for commercial rent stabilization in a formal
program at the [July 29 report launch](../events/sbu-report-launch-2026-07-29.md)
with both state sponsors, completed a city-office event-information
handoff, and helped establish a scheduled future state-office coordination
cadence.

These are four different contributions:

1. **Research translation:** identify factual, source, method, bill-language,
   and claim-scope issues without taking authorship or certifying the analysis.
2. **Public advocacy:** translate a long cultural-space history into a direct
   public argument at the report launch.
3. **City-office coordination:** repair event logistics and connect organizers
   with a Council office without claiming Council-member participation.
4. **State-office coordination:** create a recurring alignment structure with a
   bill sponsor's staff, with the first meeting still in the future at review
   time.

City and state lanes remain aligned but not interchangeable. The city lane in
this record is an event-information handoff and prospective Council work. The
state lane is coordination with the Assembly sponsor's staff around the state
bill. Neither lane establishes enactment, sponsorship caused by Jamie, or sole
campaign leadership.

## Why the claim remains held

The report acknowledgment and public event make a strong public-safe story, but
the exact report lacks an owner-hosted public route and the most precise role
evidence includes a [protected source comparison](../sources/archives/nycac-frontline-advocacy-2026-08.md).
Jamie must
approve the final first-person wording and intended surface. Source owners and
collaborators retain separate quotation, redistribution, attribution, and media
rights. A passing eval does not supply those approvals.
