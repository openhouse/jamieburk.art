# Role-fit and referral map

This page organizes evidence for referral and application decisions. It is held
from automatic website projection and does not claim that an employer has
validated the fit.

## Strongest current role families

### Technical project management

Useful where teams need requirements, workflow maps, implementation plans,
stakeholder coordination, quality assurance, launch support, and handoffs.

### Product operations

Useful where a product or service has outgrown informal memory and needs
decision records, feedback loops, adoption materials, and operating rhythms.

### Implementation and civic delivery

Useful where public-facing systems must translate policy, data, service rules,
or stakeholder knowledge into interfaces and guidance people can use.

### Knowledge systems and responsible AI operations

Useful where teams need source inventories, retrieval and evaluation practice,
human review, corrections, privacy boundaries, and durable operating memory.

### Public-interest web systems

Useful where a small or resource-constrained organization needs maintainable
publishing, e-commerce, campaign, resource-library, or community workflows.

## Conditions for success

The strongest environments value ambiguity reduction, hands-on implementation,
cross-functional translation, documentation, public service, and humane
follow-through. The fit is weaker where the role is narrowly specialized,
expects unsupported executive-scale ownership, or treats documentation and
stakeholder work as peripheral.

## Human gate

Jamie must choose which role, evidence, and public wording fit a particular
application. A deterministic evaluator or simulated reader cannot establish
employer interest, interview performance, hiring validity, or employment
outcomes.
