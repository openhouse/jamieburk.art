# Document bicycle-canoe journeys

## Question

Which distinct bicycle-canoe journeys can be documented by dated, independent
records?

## Counting rule

Count journeys, not photographs. Related frames from one encounter remain one
candidate instance until route and date evidence separates them. First-person
memory may identify leads but cannot alone establish a numeric public claim.

## Publication rule

No journey count enters the portfolio until the evidence set, deduplication
method, lower-bound wording, and human approval are separately reviewed.
