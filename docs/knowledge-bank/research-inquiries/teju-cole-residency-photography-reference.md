# Recover Jamie's Teju Cole residency and photography reference

## Remembered account

Jamie remembers an essay from one of Teju Cole's books describing an overseas
artist residency: Cole proposed to finish writing, arrived, became immersed in
the landscape, and made photography instead. Jamie has long used this story to
tell artists that a residency proposal begins the work but is not a contract.

## What the recovered public record establishes

- The official Zurich profile lists Cole as a writer in residence from June
  through November 2014.
- Swissinfo reports that he traveled and photographed Switzerland during that
  residency while writing a project related to Lagos.
- The Swiss photographs developed into *Fernweh*, a body of work made between
  2014 and 2019.

## What remains unresolved

The exact essay, book, and passage have not been recovered. The present sources
do not establish that Cole stopped writing, that his formal proposal named a
specific manuscript, or that he described the turn exactly as Jamie remembers
it. Do not quote or upgrade the remembered account into verified biography
until the primary passage is located and read in context.
