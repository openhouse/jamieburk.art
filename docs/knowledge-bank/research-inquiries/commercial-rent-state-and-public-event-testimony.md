# State and Public-Event Commercial-Rent Testimony Corpus

This inquiry extends the official NYC Council transcript corpus without
changing its boundaries. It defines a second, governed corpus for Albany bill
records and supportive public-event speeches connected to S8319 and A5568A.

The desired private research artifact is a full-text, source-ordered corpus:
one complete record per attributable supportive speech, with event, speaker,
office or role at the time, timestamps, correction notes, source
representation, and rights status. Public projection remains a separate
editorial decision.

The initial recovered speaker field includes Emily Gallagher, Julia Salazar,
Shahana Hanif, Brad Lander, Eon Huntley, Olympia Kazi, small-business owners,
coalition speakers, and other legislators. This is a starting census, not a
claim of completeness. Huntley's independently published support for
Gallagher's bill corroborates the policy position but does not replace close
listening to his event speech.
