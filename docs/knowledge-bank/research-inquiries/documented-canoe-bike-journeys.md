# Documented bicycle-canoe journeys

The East River occurrence prompted a recollection of a recurring practice. The
next responsible action is not to publish a count. It is to define what counts
as one journey, return to neighboring frames and other sources, cluster likely
instances privately, and preserve uncertainty for human review.

Any future number must be a documented-instance lower bound with a declared
search scope and duplicate rule. No count is authorized by this inquiry.
