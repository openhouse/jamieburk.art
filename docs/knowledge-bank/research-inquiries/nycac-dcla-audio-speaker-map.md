# DCLA Audio, Speaker Spreadsheet, and Discourse-Map Inquiry

Jamie recalls recording the January 2017 meeting, listening closely to it,
researching speakers and affiliations in a spreadsheet, and building an
interactive map of the discussion. Public coverage later reproduced a
coalition-supplied detail image, but the relationship between that image and
the original interactive map remains open.

Recovery would strengthen chronology, speaker identification, and the account
of how listening became documentation. It would not automatically authorize
publication of voices or convert recollection into institutional fact.
