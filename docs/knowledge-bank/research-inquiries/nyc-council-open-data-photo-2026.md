# Corroborate the 2026 NYC Council open-data photograph

## Questions

- What was the exact date and official title of the 250 Broadway session?
- Was it part of NYC School of Data, Open Data Week, or a related Council
  program?
- Which version of the commercial-rent and vacancy proposal did Jamie carry,
  and what records document its discussion or follow-up?
- Which protected source preserves the coalition member sign-in form and its
  participation choices?
- Who made the portrait, and what exact use, credit, crop, and caption do they
  approve?

## Separation rule

This portrait and oral history are not the separate CallNYC interface-in-use
photograph. Evidence about one may create leads for the other, but it may not
fill missing identity, context, rights, consent, or publication fields by
association.

## Publication rule

The portrait remains protected and off public surfaces. Oral history may guide
research, but it does not establish institutional endorsement, proposal
adoption, photographer permission, production approval, deployment, or
indexing.
