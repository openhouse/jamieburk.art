# Outcomes and Adoption

The Wiki must not use "impact" as a container for unlike things. A strong
project account separates what Jamie delivered, what another person used, what
an institution formally adopted, and what changed later.

## Four-part test

| Layer | Evidence needed | Example question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jamie delivered | Artifact, action, role, or completion record | What did Jamie make, operate, decide, or hand off? |
| Collaborators used | Attributable use, reuse, handoff, or workflow evidence | Who used the form, and in what bounded way? |
| Institution adopted | Vote, enactment, policy, release, contract, or owner decision | Which authority adopted what, and when? |
| Conditions changed | Measured result or carefully bounded chronology | What changed afterward, and what causal link is actually supported? |

## Selected project distinctions

| Project | Jamie delivered | Collective or institutional outcome | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Let NYC Dance | Organizing action and public campaign infrastructure | Council repeal of the Cabaret Law | No individual legislative causality |
| Talks Not Raids | Testimony and campaign infrastructure | Local Law 220; later City replacement of MARCH with CURE | Aligned chronology, not sole causality or end of all enforcement |
| Office town hall | Documented organizer and production contribution | A completed public dialogue in a cultural venue | No proof every recommendation was adopted |
| KC Town Hall | Completed Phase One delivery and neighborhood process | Later Phase Two authorization, withdrawal, and unused-fund reappropriation | Authorization was not receipt or completion |
| Fair Rent NYC | Coalition operating design and public-data requirements | Emerging work | No adopted City program or released dataset |
| Harry J. Epstein | Long-term modernization and workflow contribution | Period of 2x revenue growth | Contribution language; private attribution data remains protected |

## Evidence states

- **Delivered:** direct completion or role evidence exists.
- **Used:** another actor's use is documented, with scope.
- **Adopted:** the authorized decision maker took a formal action.
- **Associated:** the work and later result share a chronology or goal, but
  causality is not established.
- **Emerging:** requirements, plans, prototypes, or collaboration are active
  without a completed outcome.
- **Held:** the record is insufficient, protected, or awaiting rights, credit,
  or corroboration.

## Anti-inflation rules

- A plan is not delivery.
- Delivery is not adoption.
- Adoption is not implementation.
- A later institutional change is not proof of campaign causality.
- A public response is not verified participation or impact.
- Revenue growth during Jamie's work is not revenue growth caused solely by
  Jamie.
- A funding appropriation is not receipt or expenditure.

## Advancement record

Every outcome claim should name the current evidence state, the authority that
could establish the next state, and the smallest evidence needed to advance.
The [Claim Maturity Dashboard](claim-maturity-dashboard.md) keeps selected
examples visible without changing their canonical typed records.
