# Source Classes

Use these classes to describe evidence without exposing private records.

## Approved Resume

Language from Jamie's current public resume or resume-derived profile. Good for
role framing, capabilities, and selected impact when it remains within approved
wording.

## Public Source

Material already public on the web, in public records, or in public campaign
materials. Cite the public source in prose when useful; do not import private
copies or raw scraped archives.

## Public-Safe Archive Summary

Aggregate summary derived from Jamie's private records, local repositories, or
project history. This can support a claim, but the raw source should stay out of
the repo.

## Firsthand Collaborator Context

Memory or confirmation from a human collaborator. Use carefully and do not quote
or name people without permission.

## Jamie Review Confirmation

Jamie's direct confirmation that a public-safe claim is accurate enough to use.
This does not override collaborator privacy or collective-work boundaries.

## Collaborator Approval Pending

Potentially useful claim that should not be public until a relevant human
collaborator approves the wording.

## Protected

Evidence exists or may exist, but the public repo should not expose the claim,
source, names, raw records, or surrounding details.
