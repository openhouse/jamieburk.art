# Source Classes

Use source classes rather than private local paths.

Source classes describe the kind of support behind a claim. They do not authorize publication of the underlying evidence.

## Public Source

Already public and safe to link or cite.

Examples:

- public website
- public press citation
- public repository
- public city record
- public campaign page

## Resume-Supported

Supported by Jamie's current approved resume.

Use cautiously. If the resume itself is still approval-gated, mark exact metrics as Open or keep them out of prominent public surfaces.

## Public-Safe Summary

Accurate and defensible summary that intentionally avoids raw records, names, screenshots, contact details, sensitive context, or private source material.

## Private-Source Summary

Supported by private source materials, but the source itself must not be published.

Examples:

- private coalition notes
- private client documentation
- internal dashboards
- raw transcripts
- private correspondence

## Approved Artifact

Artifact Jamie has approved for public use, such as a redacted diagram, public handout, public map, or public-safe representative example.

Approval should include crop, context, caption, and whether collaborator or client review is required.

## Protected

May inform Jamie's confidence but should not be exposed.

Examples:

- raw transcripts
- legal-review materials
- stakeholder lists
- client analytics
- private community records
- unapproved photos or quotes

## Candidate / Needs Approval

The claim may be true or useful, but needs Jamie approval, collaborator approval, source confirmation, screenshot approval, citation confirmation, or metric review before production indexing.

## Rule

Do not add private file paths, private screenshots, raw transcripts, private correspondence, or raw community records to this repo.

When a private source supports a claim, publish the safest summary and keep the protected evidence out of the repo.
