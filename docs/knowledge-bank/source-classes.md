# Source Classes

Source classes describe what kind of support sits behind a claim. They do not
authorize publication of the underlying evidence.

## Classes

### public-artifact

A public-safe PDF, certificate, image, page, report, presentation, or other
artifact Jamie has approved for this repo or site.

### public-website

A public web page that can be linked or summarized without exposing private
context.

### public-record

A government, legislative, court, procurement, archive, or institutional record
that is already public.

### public-safe-summary-of-private-work

An accurate summary of work supported by private or mixed evidence, written so
that protected source material does not appear in the repo or deploy.

### private-source-not-in-repo

Protected evidence can inform Jamie's internal confidence, but it must not be
committed, quoted, linked, or exposed as a public artifact.

### needs-review

The claim, source, citation, screenshot, metric, collaborator name, or surface
needs additional review before public use.

### do-not-publish

The material should not be published or paraphrased because privacy, consent,
client trust, civic sensitivity, legal risk, community safety, or collaborator
approval requires omission.

## Rule

Private source classes may support Jamie's internal confidence, but they do not
become public artifacts. When private evidence supports a claim, publish the
safest accurate summary and keep the protected source outside the repo and
deploy.
