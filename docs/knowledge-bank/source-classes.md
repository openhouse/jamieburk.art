# Source Classes

Use these labels in the knowledge bank instead of exposing raw source files or
private paths.

## Public Source

Public websites, public journalism, public campaign pages, public repositories,
public records, or public project artifacts.

Public sources may be linked when they are stable, relevant, and safe for a
reader to inspect. Do not infer private authorship, private operations, or
private strategy from a public page unless a separate approved source supports
that inference.

## Approved Resume

Claims from a Jamie-approved resume or profile surface.

Resume-backed claims can support public copy, but still require careful wording
when the work was collective, internally measured, or supported by private
records.

## Public-Safe Archive Summary

Aggregate or summarized findings from private records, local files, transcripts,
workbooks, screenshots, or project archives.

The summary may support public claims. The underlying records must not be
published unless separately approved, redacted, and reviewed.

## Jamie Review Confirmation

Claims Jamie has reviewed for professional use, especially role framing,
authorship, or contribution language.

This does not automatically clear collaborator names, screenshots, raw records,
or quotes.

## Collaborator Review Needed

Claims that may affect collaborators, coalition partners, community members,
clients, employers, or named participants.

Do not project these onto production pages until review is complete.

## Protected

Material that must not be published in the repo or website: private emails, raw
transcripts, raw notes, legal-review context, raw community records, contact
lists, private financial or property detail, credentials, private dashboards,
customer/user/vendor data, unapproved photos, private quotes, or sensitive
stakeholder context.

Protected material can inform a public-safe aggregate only when the resulting
claim remains defensible without revealing the source.
