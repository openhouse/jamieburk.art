# Knowledge Bank Public Safety

The knowledge bank is public-safe but repo-internal. It may hold stronger claim
language than the website, but it must not hold private proof artifacts.

## Never Commit

- Private emails, raw transcripts, private coalition notes, private stakeholder
  lists, legal-review material, raw strategy documents, credentials, private
  source files, or local archive paths.
- Health, financial, legal, banking, property, or personal records.
- Client-private materials, internal analytics, dashboards, vendor terms,
  passwords, API keys, or account data.
- Raw community records, guest lists, attendance records, contact lists, private
  stories, private interiors, or unapproved photos.
- Unapproved screenshots, collaborator quotes, named participant lists, or
  documents that reveal strategy or consent-sensitive context.

## Project Boundaries

- **HJE:** public-safe summaries only; no private operations, dashboards,
  revenue detail, customer records, credentials, or vendor terms.
- **NYC Artist Coalition / FairRentNYC / CRS:** collective-work language; no
  private coalition notes, legal-review context, stakeholder lists, private
  emails, raw transcripts, or active strategy details.
- **CallNYC:** archived prototype only; no current-service or official-city
  claim.
- **WOWList:** aggregate summaries only; no user records, organizer contacts,
  account data, raw database records, or unapproved community materials.
- **Sunday Dinner / 196:** summary-only; no guest names, attendance records,
  addresses, contact details, private stories, private interiors, or unapproved
  images.
- **KC Town Hall:** public-record scale/funding facts only; no private
  financial, legal, banking, property, or stakeholder details.
- **Source-Backed Team Memory:** lab/method framing only; no client-private
  source artifacts, private transcripts, or private archive-browser behavior.

## Publishing Rule

When a claim is uncertain, consent-sensitive, or too context-heavy, either
soften it out of production copy or mark it as pending in the approval register.
Do not solve credibility problems by publishing private material.
