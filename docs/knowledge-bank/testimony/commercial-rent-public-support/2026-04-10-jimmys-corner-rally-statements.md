# Jimmy's Corner rally: Small Business Rent Stabilization statements

## Official source

[New York State Senate release](https://www.nysenate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026/julia-salazar/senator-salazar-rallies-jimmys-corner-and-small-business?page=0)

The release contains one attributed statement from each person below. The
public Wiki preserves attribution and bounded paraphrase; the official source
controls complete wording and remains distinct from a rally transcript.

## Julia Salazar

- Role at publication: New York State Senator and lead Senate sponsor
- Public-safe orientation: Connected Jimmy's Corner to the distinctive public
  value of local businesses and argued for rent and lease protections.

## Emily Gallagher

- Role at publication: New York State Assembly Member and lead Assembly sponsor
- Public-safe orientation: Framed local businesses as contributors to safer,
  more distinctive neighborhoods and the bill as a continuity measure.

## Olympia Kazi

- Role at publication: Co-founder, NYC Artist Coalition and Music Workers Alliance
- Public-safe orientation: Used the threatened business as an example of the
  imbalance between commercial tenants and landlords.

## Linda B. Rosenthal

- Role at publication: New York State Assembly Member and Chair, Assembly Committee on Housing
- Public-safe orientation: Supported the proposal as protection for small
  businesses confronting rent pressure and displacement.

## Jo Anne Simon

- Role at publication: New York State Assembly Member
- Public-safe orientation: Connected stable commercial tenancy to neighborhood
  character, community institutions, and economic continuity.
