# State sponsors and advocates introduce the Small Business Survival Act

## Official source

[New York State Senate release](https://www.nysenate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026/julia-salazar/senator-salazar-and-assembly-member-gallagher-introduce?page=4)

The release contains attributed statements from every person or organization
listed below. The public Wiki records attribution and a bounded paraphrase;
the official source controls the complete wording.

## Julia Salazar

- Role at publication: New York State Senator and lead Senate sponsor
- Source unit: one attributed official-release statement
- Public-safe orientation: Presented the proposal as protection against steep
  commercial rent increases, displacement, vacancy, and chain-store pressure.

## Emily Gallagher

- Role at publication: New York State Assembly Member and lead Assembly sponsor
- Source unit: one attributed official-release statement
- Public-safe orientation: Framed small businesses as central to neighborhood
  identity and described the bill's rent and lease protections.

## Olympia Kazi

- Role at publication: Urban activist
- Source unit: one attributed official-release statement
- Public-safe orientation: Placed the legislation in a long history of efforts
  to protect small businesses, nonprofits, and cultural institutions.

## Small Business United

- Role at publication: Small-business organizing coalition
- Source unit: one attributed official-release statement
- Public-safe orientation: Supported the proposal as an affordability and
  continuity measure for locally owned businesses.

These are release statements, not hearing or floor speeches.
