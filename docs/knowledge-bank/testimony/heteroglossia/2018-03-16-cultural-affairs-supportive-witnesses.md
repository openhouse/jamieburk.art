# Cultural Affairs preliminary budget hearing: supportive witness testimony

## Editorial and population note

This governed research view indexes 14 public officials, invited witnesses, and public witnesses whose recorded positions affirmatively supported DCLA funding and implementation of CreateNYC. It includes support stated with amendments or conditions. Presiding remarks, committee questions, opposition, neutral expert analysis, and unresolved stances remain in the official transcript but are outside this supportive-speaker view unless the speaker is the prime sponsor.

The public repository preserves attribution, role, stance, recorded labels, page locators, turn counts, and word counts. It does not republish raw third-party transcript text or incidental personal details. The official transcript controls.

Official source: [New York City Council transcript](https://legistar.council.nyc.gov/View.ashx?M=F&ID=6195706&GUID=7514BB5D-51E7-4B77-92D8-A3919828DBEB)
