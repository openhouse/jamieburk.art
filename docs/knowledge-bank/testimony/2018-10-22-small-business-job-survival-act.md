# Jamie Burkart testimony for the Small Business Job Survival Act

## Editorial note

This readable extract preserves Jamie's complete statement from the official
Council transcript at pages 346-348. Obvious proper-name transcription errors
are corrected in brackets. The official transcript remains the controlling
record.

## Testimony

Hello. I'm Jamie Burkart. I'm a member of the New York City Artist Coalition,
and I live in Fort Greene, Clinton Hill.

It's been great today to see so many Council Members supporting SBJSA and that
we're having this hearing. So thank you very much. And I wanted to say thank you
to the City's first Director of Nightlife, who is here, Ariel [Palitz]. She
spent a couple of hours sitting up in the balcony. So thank you to the
administration for creating that office.

I'm here because New York City's cultural spaces are small businesses, and
cultural spaces close due to rent. My life as an advocate began because of the
loss of another. My friend Nick [Gomez-Hall], who is one of the 36 people killed
in Oakland's tragic Ghost Ship fire.

When a city's residents can't afford appropriate commercial spaces for
culture, our diverse cultures are forced out of our neighborhoods. We're forced
out of business, or we're forced underground into less safe, exploitative
slumlord situations. As a safety advocate, affordability is a life-and-death
issue for culture in New York.

Cherished community spaces can't get a fair lease here and are closing. Silent
Barn closes here due to rent increases. Madiba, a South African restaurant down
the street from me, closed due to rent increases. Bar Sepia closed when the
landlord refused to come to the table for lease negotiations.

Treasured neighborhood institutions must be able to afford to thrive in New
York. When we lose places to come together, we lose New York. Culture needs the
right to renew its lease in New York City.

In Paris, you can still go to the cafe where Sartre and de Beauvoir wrote, but
the spaces in the Bronx and Harlem where hip-hop was born have closed. They're
not open anymore.

We need commercial rent protections. Save the places that make New York New
York with fair leases and fair rent. Pass the Small Business Job Survival Act,
Intro number 737. Thank you.

## Interpretation boundary

This testimony documents Jamie's position and participation. It does not
