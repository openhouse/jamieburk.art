# Application composition briefs

The lifecycle contains four route-less internal briefs. They select from
canonical claims and do not authorize a new resume, cover letter, website
variant, or public statement without exact-surface human review.

## OTI and public-sector delivery

Lead with technical project management, public-data translation,
government-facing implementation, requirements, records, and responsible
public communication. Keep policy outcomes collective and official-source
backed.

## Private-sector implementation

Lead with HJE operating stewardship, durable workflows, technical handoffs,
implementation support, and business context. Keep private records protected
and self-reported outcomes qualified.

## Source-backed team memory

Lead with the bounded sprint, source mapping, inspectable decisions, human
review, and reusable handoffs. Treat proposals, methods, client delivery,
adoption, and outcomes as separate states.

## Civic and cultural infrastructure

Lead with participatory systems, public identities, coalition memory, policy
translation, neighborhood implementation, and shared stewardship. Preserve
collaborators, hosts, and communities as co-authors.

## Composition rule

Opportunity-specific language may narrow and reorder approved facts. It may not
strengthen a claim, widen a reuse permission, expose a protected source, or
convert an internal brief into public approval.
