## Asset publication gate

Editor-field membership, People associations, face detection, filenames,
project-album membership, and embedded dates are retrieval cues, not publication
approval. Keep each image in private editorial review until all of these checks
pass:

- identity;
- project, event, place, and date;
- photographer or creator and publication rights;
- consent or an appropriate documented public-event basis;
- the Knowledge Bank claim ID the image corroborates;
- a factual caption;
- crop and context review, including screens, documents, addresses, minors,
  bystanders, and private interiors;
- Jamie's final approval for the specific image and placement.

Private review remains the default until every check passes.
