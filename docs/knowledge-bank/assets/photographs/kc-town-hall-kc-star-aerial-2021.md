# Kansas City Star aerial view of KC Town Hall

## Published context

The Kansas City Star's December 27, 2021 investigation leads with an elevated
view of the KC Town Hall building and credits the photograph to Tammy
Ljungblad. Jamie identifies it as a drone photograph.

## Evidentiary function

The image supplies an independent visual record of the building and its
neighborhood-scale setting at the moment the Star examined delays in the CCED
process. The article, rather than the photograph alone, supports the
administrative-burden observation.

## Hold

The Star's publication and photo credit do not grant Jamie's portfolio
republication rights. The image URL may remain a retrieval lead, but no copy or
derivative enters the repository or public website without permission and
exact credit terms.
