# August 2026 employment-edition terminal six photographs

These six photographs were selected from the terminal keep of a recursive
photo edit for a specific purpose: make Jamie's product practice legible to a
hiring reader in seconds. The hero shows direct exchange in current civic work.
The supporting sequence shows synthesis, interface, public communication,
maintenance, and situated invention.

The files in the public application are bounded WebP derivatives with metadata
removed and checksums recorded above. The source album and its archive metadata
remain private.

## Public-use boundary

Jamie confirmed publication rights and portfolio permission for the entire
source album on August 12, 2026. Captions stay factual and collective. The
images are context, not endorsements or sole-credit claims. Production and
indexing remain open until Jamie approves the exact edition.
