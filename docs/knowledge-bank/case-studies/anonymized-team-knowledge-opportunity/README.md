# Anonymized Team-Knowledge Opportunity Case Study

This packet is a public-safe derivative of several private conversations about
a possible paid team-knowledge engagement. It preserves the problem, the
relationship, the reasoning, and the conversational character while
withholding the prospective sponsor's identity, employer, product, location,
network, and other details that could reasonably identify them.

## Read the packet

1. [The prospective sponsor's perspective](01-prospective-sponsor-perspective.md)
2. [Jamie's perspective](02-jamie-perspective.md)
3. [The prospective sponsor's voice and reasoning profile](03-prospective-sponsor-voice.md)

## Interpretation and publication limits

- The source material includes automated transcripts and a later corrected
  working transcript. The corrected version is not audio-certified and should
  not be treated as forensic quotation.
- The two perspective documents are careful interpretations, not statements
  written or approved by either participant.
- The voice profile describes observable conversational habits. It is not
  permission to impersonate the person, generate statements in their name, or
  present invented language as theirs.
- The conversations support classifying this as a serious but unresolved
  prospective engagement. They do not establish an offer, budget approval,
  contract, completed pilot, endorsement, or commercial outcome.
- Modeled reader review can help revise this packet. Only the actual source
  participant can confirm that the account feels remarkably accurate from
  their perspective.
