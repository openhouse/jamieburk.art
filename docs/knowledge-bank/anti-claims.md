# Anti-Claims

These are claims, phrasings, or implications the public site should avoid unless
Jamie explicitly approves a stronger position later.

## General

Do not say Jamie single-handedly caused collective outcomes.
Do not imply official government service where the work was civic, volunteer,
prototype, or coalition-adjacent.
Do not imply a prototype is a current official service.
Do not imply Source-Backed Team Memory is a finished SaaS product.
Do not imply AI replaces human judgment.
Do not imply private archives are browsable or available.

Cabaret Law repeal, Office of Nightlife creation, nightlife enforcement
reporting legislation, Commercial Rent Stabilization progress, or storefront
stability advocacy outcomes.

lists, or source documents that were not approved for public use.

Do not say Jamie authored every @NYCArtC post. Credit Jamie for establishing the

identified Council-member interactions, or the recovered three-member minimum

implemented, commissioned by the City, or based on Jamie's access to
confidential RPIE filings. Do not publish the legislative provenance working
