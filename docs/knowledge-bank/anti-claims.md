# Anti-Claims

These claims and implications should not appear publicly.

- Jamie single-handedly led a civic movement.
- Jamie alone repealed the Cabaret Law or created the Office of Nightlife.
- NYC Artist Coalition alone produced policy outcomes that belong to broader
  coalitions and public processes.
- Jamie owned or authored legislation unless that is explicitly approved and
  accurate.
- Source-Backed Team Memory is a finished SaaS product.
- AI replaces judgment, consent, facilitation, direct communication, or human
  review.
- Private coalition materials, client dashboards, raw meeting exports, or raw
  transcripts are publicly available.
- HJE revenue outcomes were solely caused by Jamie.
- CallNYC is an official, current, comprehensive, legal, emergency, or City-run
  service.
- WOWList had official chapters, millions of users, or active-today public
  operations unless separately approved.
- Sunday Dinner / 196 participant details, photos, addresses, contact records,
  or private logistics can be published without consent.
- KC Town Hall funding, redevelopment, property, legal, finance, or partner
  details can be published without approval.

When in doubt, use a lower-claim sentence and leave the stronger version in
`claims.md` until approval catches up.
