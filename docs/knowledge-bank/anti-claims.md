# Anti-Claims

This file records what the site should not say.

If a public sentence risks implying one of these anti-claims, rewrite it or remove it.

## General

Do not say:

- Jamie single-handedly transformed a collective project.
- Jamie owned or led campaigns where the work was collective.
- Jamie authored legal analysis or provided legal advice.
- Jamie caused business growth alone.
- Jamie created an official government service.
- Jamie has a finished AI SaaS product if the work is early method / lab practice.
- Jamie's private archives are browsable or available.
- All artifacts are literal screenshots if many are representative diagrams.
- Exact metrics are approved unless they are marked approved.
- Staging is private.
- The resume PDF is automatically approved because it is present in the repo.

## Commercial Rent Stabilization / FairRentNYC

Do not say:

- Jamie authored the bill.
- Jamie provided legal advice.
- Jamie represented all partners.
- Jamie owned the movement.
- Jamie speaks for every collaborator.
- Private coalition notes or legal-review materials are available.
- A public agency adopted Jamie's data framing unless separately verified and approved.

## NYC Artist Coalition

Do not say:

- Jamie alone repealed the Cabaret Law.
- NYC Artist Coalition alone created the Office of Nightlife.
- Jamie alone led Let NYC Dance, Save NYC Spaces, Talks Not Raids, FairRentNYC, or Commercial Rent Stabilization.
- Public campaign websites imply ownership of every movement, coalition, policy win, partner organization, or piece of site content.

## CallNYC

Do not say:

- CallNYC is current.
- CallNYC is official.
- CallNYC is comprehensive civic guidance.
- CallNYC replaces 311, council offices, legal help, emergency services, or official channels.

## Source-Backed Team Memory

Do not say:

- It is a finished SaaS.
- AI replaces judgment.
- The system can safely browse private archives.
- AI summaries are automatically trustworthy.
- It provides legal, medical, therapeutic, or compliance advice.

## Harry J. Epstein Company

Do not say:

- Jamie caused 2x revenue growth alone.
- Private analytics, customer data, vendor terms, pricing rules, or detailed revenue records are public.

## WOWList

Do not say:

- WOWList is active today unless separately confirmed.
- WOWList had official city chapters.
- WOWList has a complete public archive.
- Raw archive data can be published as a public dataset.

## Rewrite Rule

When a sentence drifts toward an anti-claim:

1. Change inflated verbs to accurate verbs.
2. Add the collective-work qualifier.
3. Name the artifact Jamie made usable.
4. Remove the protected detail.
5. Move the unresolved version to `docs/public-claims-inventory.md` or `docs/knowledge-bank/approval-register.md`.
