# Anti-Claims

Do not say these unless later evidence and explicit approval support them.

- Jamie led the whole movement.
- Jamie owned the legislation.
- Jamie provided legal advice.
- Jamie caused 2x revenue growth.
- Jamie single-handedly doubled revenue.
- CallNYC is an official City Council service.
- CallNYC is current civic guidance, legal guidance, emergency guidance, or a
  comprehensive resident-services source.
- WOWList had official chapters in every city.
- KC Town Hall received, won, or secured public funding if the public record
  only supports a recommendation.
- Source-Backed Team Memory is production SaaS.
- AI produces truth or replaces human judgment.
- Private archives, transcripts, raw notes, contact lists, dashboards, or
  stakeholder records are available publicly.
