# Anti-Claims

This file is as important as the proof bank.

If a public sentence risks implying one of these anti-claims, rewrite it or
remove it.

## Do Not Use

- Jamie single-handedly led collective civic campaigns.
- Jamie alone repealed the Cabaret Law.
- NYC Artist Coalition alone created the Office of Nightlife.
- NYC Artist Coalition alone ended enforcement practices.
- NYC Artist Coalition passed specific laws unless the exact laws, coalition
  role, and public citations are approved.
- Jamie currently leads NYC Artist Coalition unless a current formal title is
  approved.
- Jamie authored official legal analysis for Commercial Rent Stabilization.
- Jamie owns Commercial Rent Stabilization bill text or final coalition
  position.
- Jamie speaks for every Commercial Rent Stabilization collaborator.
- Commercial Rent Stabilization data work proves rent caused closures.
- A public agency adopted Jamie's commercial vacancy or lease-cost proposal.
- Jamie solely caused HJE's revenue growth.
- HJE private dashboards, customer data, vendor terms, or pricing rules can be
  published as proof.
- WOWList is active today, complete, or organized into official city chapters.
- WOWList has millions of users.
- WOWList raw archive data can be published as a public dataset.
- CallNYC is an official or current City service.
- CallNYC gives legal, emergency, or verified current agency guidance.
- Source-Backed Team Memory is production SaaS.
- Source-Backed Team Memory replaces human judgment.
- Source-Backed Team Memory is an omniscient AI layer or broad ingestion system.
- Private records, raw transcripts, private email, screenshots, quotes, or
  photos can be treated as public proof without approval.
- Staging is private.
- The resume PDF is automatically approved because it is present in the repo.
- Phone, collaborator names, screenshots, photos, quotes, or venue-specific
  details are public just because they appear in private source material.

## Rewrite Rule

When a sentence drifts toward an anti-claim:

1. Change inflated verbs to accurate verbs.
2. Add the collective-work qualifier.
3. Name the artifact Jamie made usable.
4. Remove the protected detail.
5. Move the unresolved version to `Pending` in `claims.md`.
