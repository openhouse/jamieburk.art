These are claims, phrasings, or implications the public site should avoid unless
Jamie explicitly approves a stronger position later.

## General

Do not say Jamie single-handedly caused collective outcomes.
Do not imply official government service where the work was civic, volunteer,
prototype, or coalition-adjacent.
Do not imply a prototype is a current official service.
Do not imply Source-Backed Team Memory is a finished SaaS product.
Do not imply AI replaces human judgment.
Do not imply private archives are browsable or available.
