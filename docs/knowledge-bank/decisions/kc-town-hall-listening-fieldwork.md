# KC Town Hall: Listening Within Fieldwork

**Period:** Phase One, completed in 2019. **Question:** How could daily
construction presence also create a bounded route for neighborhood knowledge to
inform a future public place?

## Supported Reconstruction

The record supports Jamie's creation of a neighborhood survey handbill and
backing contact workflow alongside daily site coordination. This joined a
practical field presence to an invitation for residents to describe history,
needs, and possibilities without publishing their person-level responses.

## Unobserved Alternatives

The archive does not establish a formal design-research decision, every method
considered, representative participation, or that all site conversations
entered the survey system.

## Outcome Boundary

The system made contact collection and participatory vision more usable. It
does not establish community consensus, a completed future program, ownership
of neighborhood knowledge, or causal influence on every later municipal
decision.
