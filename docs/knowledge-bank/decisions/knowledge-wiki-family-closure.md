# Close the Knowledge Wiki A-E family into one canonical implementation

The family produced five credible answers to the same product question. Closure
means preserving what each answer discovered while selecting one operating
architecture.

E becomes the foundation. A contributes source-to-story and visual-rights
practice. B contributes identity, embodied learning, and a human-only
authorship gate. C contributes outcome and claim-maturity discipline. D
contributes readable relation semantics and operational distinctions.

The [closure review](../research-runs/knowledge-wiki-family-closure-2026-07-19.md)
and [integration register](../../integration/knowledge-wiki-family-closure.md)
record the evidence and destinations.

## Acceptance boundary

The machine can reject architectural duplication, missing donor destinations,
unreconciled census inflation, broken graph relations, and accidental public
projection. Jamie's editorial acceptance and every external human authority
remain separate.
