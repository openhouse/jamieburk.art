# Open House: Communal Governance Inside Negotiated Boundaries

**Period:** April 2006. **Question:** How could an open gallery experiment make
participation and shared responsibility real while still operating inside an
institution with safety and authority constraints?

## Supported Reconstruction

Public reporting documents Jamie instigating and continuously tending the
space, participants shaping activity, and institutional feedback altering the
conditions. The chosen course was neither sole control nor an unbounded claim
that no rules applied: the project made participation, reflection, and
negotiation part of its form.

## Unobserved Alternatives

The source does not preserve a complete options process, formal decision log,
or every participant's view. It therefore does not claim the group explicitly
considered and rejected a conventional exhibition, closed membership, or any
other reconstructed alternative.

## Outcome Boundary

The record supports a realized ten-day experiment and iterative governance. It
does not establish attendance, consensus, participant impact, sole authorship,
or permission to identify and republish every participant.
