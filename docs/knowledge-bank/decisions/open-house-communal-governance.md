# Open House communal governance inside negotiated boundaries

The strongest source-supported choice is the combination of an open social
form and bounded operation. Jamie established a frame in which participants
could change what happened, rather than presenting participation as a fixed
theme. The project still occupied an institution whose concerns and authority
had to be negotiated.

The alternatives marked “not observed” are not retrospective claims that a
formal team considered and rejected them. They show what this record cannot
infer from the surviving article.
