# Narrow the pre-launch public photo field to fully bound occurrences

Fresh holdout readers found that three public derivatives still carried open
creator, credit, and broader-rights questions. The pre-launch edit responds by
removing those derivatives from public Git and the rendered site. The East
River homepage image remains because its creator, caption, credit, derivative,
placement, and public-use boundary are explicitly bound.

This is editorial restraint, not archival disposal. The private Knowledge Wiki
and photo-fieldwork process may continue researching the removed image
families. A later edition can restore an exact occurrence after the missing
facts and human decisions are recorded.
