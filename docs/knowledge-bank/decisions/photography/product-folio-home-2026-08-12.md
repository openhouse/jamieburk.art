# Select a public-service product folio for the homepage

The sequence moves from conversation to collective synthesis, interface,
public communication, maintenance, and inventive logistics. Each photograph
performs one legible editorial job. The previously selected East River hero
remains in the historical record but is superseded on the homepage.

The group scene with elected officials was intentionally left out. Its prestige
signal would have asked readers to infer affiliation instead of evaluating
Jamie’s product practice.
