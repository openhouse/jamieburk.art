# Use the terminal six to make product practice visible on the homepage

The photo-select output is treated as a useful editorial reduction, not a
substitute for judgment. Its terminal six cover distinct registers of Jamie's
practice and make a stronger hiring argument together than a broad gallery.
The current field exchange earns the hero because it places Jamie inside the
present work, with another person and a working document, rather than turning
the page into a symbolic portrait.

The six-image field record—the other five terminal images plus the retained
East River canary—is intentionally downstream of the ten-second professional
proposition. Visitors do not need to know how the archive was curated in order
to understand the public composition.
