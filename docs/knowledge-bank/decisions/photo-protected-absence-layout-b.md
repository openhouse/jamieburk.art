# Withhold intimate gathering photographs from the Layout B edition

No-photo is a deliberate state. The current edition can describe relational
work without turning intimate scenes into evidentiary texture. A later edition
may revisit the decision through a specific brief, exact image, rights and
consent review, and Jamie's approval.
