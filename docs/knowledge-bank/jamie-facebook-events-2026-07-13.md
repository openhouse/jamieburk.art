# Jamie Burkart Facebook Hosted-Event Archival Production

Date: 2026-07-13

## Result

The authenticated personal events surface exposed 502 distinct public event
associations. That surface is not a portfolio of Jamie's work: 482 cards
displayed another host, while 20 displayed Jamie as host. Association does not
establish authorship, production, attendance, endorsement, or professional
significance.

Detail-page host cards supplied the stronger control: 21 past events hosted by
Jamie. The pass recovered and close-read 20 hosted-event pages and retained one
historical slot as unresolved. The result is therefore complete accounting of
the 21-slot control, not complete recovery of 21 event pages and not a claim
about every event Jamie ever hosted.

## Public-Safety Disposition

The public census is deliberately aggregate-only. It omits event titles, event
IDs, URLs, exact dates, other hosts, addresses, response displays, guest and
relationship context, comments, raw descriptions, and account-administration
data. The 502-record association inventory remains protected and is not
reproduced in the repository. This prevents an archival control from becoming
a new dossier of Jamie's personal and relational life.

Selected event pages may exist as canonical public source records when their
professional relevance and public context are clear. Their inclusion does not
promote every detail or convert the surrounding personal timeline into proof.
