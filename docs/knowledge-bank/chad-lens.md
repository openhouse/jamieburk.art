# Chad Lens

Do not make the reader decode Jamie.

Before shipping public copy, ask:

1. Is Jamie visible as the actor?
2. Does the sentence answer "toward what end?"
3. Does the language say what became usable?
4. Would a hiring, HR, public-sector, civic-tech, implementation, or product-ops reader understand this without decoding?
5. Does the sentence avoid unsupported ownership, official authority, legal analysis, or solo-causality claims?

The aim is courageous precision: neither understatement nor overclaiming.

## Provenance

These criteria are a public-safe synthesis of a protected March 1, 2026 resume
review. The source record is `SRC-JOB-HUNT-CHAD-EDITORIAL-REVIEW-2026`; the
underlying transcript remains outside the repository.

The review specifically praised agency verbs, concrete examples, clarity, and
links to visible work. It recommended a concise role-specific opening and
restraint with unfamiliar acronyms. This is attributed editorial guidance, not
independent verification of any accomplishment claim and not a universal
hiring standard.
