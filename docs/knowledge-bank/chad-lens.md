# Chad Lens

Use this as the public-copy review lens for the portfolio.

Before shipping any public-facing sentence, ask:

- Does the sentence make Jamie visible as the actor?
- Does it answer toward what end?
- Does it say what became usable?
- Does it minimize the reader's burden?
- Does it avoid unexplained acronyms, arrows, insider terms, and clever
  shorthand?
- Does it avoid unsupported ownership claims?
- Does it use contribution language where work was collective?
- Is it legible to HR, government readers, and nontechnical referrers?

## Preferred Verbs

Use concrete verbs:

- clarified;
- structured;
- built;
- co-built;
- stewarded;
- translated;
- coordinated;
- facilitated;
- documented;
- implemented;
- synthesized;
- mapped;
- supported;
- maintained;
- transferred;
- improved.

## Avoid

Avoid unsupported hero language:

- single-handedly built;
- led the movement;
- owned the bill;
- solved the crisis;
- caused 2x growth.

## Public Language

Prefer:

- ambiguous;
- loosely defined;
- complex;
- resource-constrained;
- early-stage;
- stakeholder-heavy.

Avoid making public readers decode terms before they understand the value.
