# Chad Lens

Do not make the reader decode Jamie.

Before shipping public copy, ask:

1. Is Jamie visible as the actor?
2. Does the sentence answer "toward what end?"
3. Does the language say what became usable?
4. Would a hiring, HR, public-sector, civic-tech, implementation, or product-ops reader understand this without decoding?
5. Does the sentence avoid unsupported ownership, official authority, legal analysis, or solo-causality claims?

The aim is courageous precision: neither understatement nor overclaiming.

## Executable gate

`npm run evals:launch` applies this lens to the primary hiring path. The hard
gate requires:

- Jamie's target role, value, and next actions on the homepage;
- an explicit entry condition, coordinating role, and usable team outputs on
  Technical Operations;
- actor-led, contribution-bounded project proof summaries;
- role, selected impact, resume download, and contact paths on Resume;
- canonical proof-bank support for representative claims.

The automated gate reduces repeatable reader-burden failures. It does not
replace an external hiring reader or authorize stronger claims.
