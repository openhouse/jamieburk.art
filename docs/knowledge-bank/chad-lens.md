# Chad Lens

This lens is for making Jamie legible without flattening the record.

Use the strongest accurate claim, not the loudest claim. Do not understate
Jamie's role by hiding him inside passive nouns; do not overstate collective
work by turning shared accomplishments into solo wins.

## Five Questions

1. Is Jamie visible as the actor?
2. Does the sentence answer "toward what end?"
3. Would a hiring manager understand the sentence without decoding internal
   shorthand?
4. Does the verb match the evidence: built, led, co-built, contributed,
   stewarded, supported, translated, mapped, documented?
5. Are collaborators, coalitions, clients, and communities protected by careful
   collective-work language?

## Preferred Pattern

Use this shape:

`Jamie did [concrete operating work] so [team/community/project] could [usable outcome].`

Examples:

- Jamie helped structure Commercial Rent Stabilization campaign memory so
  collaborators could track decisions, open questions, source context, and next
  steps without exposing private notes.
- Jamie co-built WOWList so local organizers could publish, follow, save, sync,
  and distribute events through community vocabulary.
- Jamie helped an 80+ year-old tool business translate catalog knowledge and
  operating routines into maintainable e-commerce workflows.

## Verb Discipline

- Use `built` when Jamie directly built the artifact or system.
- Use `led` when the role and source support leadership over that work.
- Use `co-built` when collaborators materially shared authorship.
- Use `contributed to` for shared business impact or causal outcomes.
- Use `helped`, `supported`, `structured`, or `stewarded` for collective civic,
  coalition, and community work.
- Use `translated` when the core move was making technical, civic, or
  community context usable to a different audience.

## Anti-Patterns

Do not say:

- Jamie single-handedly led collective civic campaigns.
- Jamie alone repealed the Cabaret Law or created the Office of Nightlife.
- Jamie authored official legal analysis for Commercial Rent Stabilization.
- HJE revenue doubled because of Jamie alone.
- WOWList had official city chapters.
- CallNYC is a current or official city service.
- Source-Backed Team Memory is production SaaS.
- Private records, raw transcripts, screenshots, quotes, or photos are public
  proof without approval.
