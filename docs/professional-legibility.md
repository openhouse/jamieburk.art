# Professional Legibility

This is the public-safe Chad lens for the portfolio.

## Core Reader Job

Make Jamie easy to recognize as a Technical Project Manager - Product
Operations & Implementation lead who builds operating structure for emerging
public-facing technical work.

Do not make the reader decode Jamie from project history alone.

## Rules

- Minimize reader burden.
- Make Jamie visible as the actor.
- Say toward what end.
- Use action verbs.
- Translate insider terms.
- Explain what became usable.
- Avoid unsupported ownership claims.
- Use collective-work language where work was collective.
- Do not let tools, archives, or artifacts make Jamie disappear.
- Do not publish private, unapproved, or exact claims without approval.

## Translation Notes

- under-structured -> emerging, early-stage, loosely defined, complex,
  resource-constrained
- tracking -> structured, synthesized, maintained, coordinated, surfaced,
  stewarded
- lightweight -> practical, low-overhead, easy to adopt
- data -> open-data workflows, dataset documentation, source maps, public
  guidance, analytics, data-product requirements
- maintainers -> community platform operators, calendar editors, local stewards
- AI-ready -> human-reviewed AI-assisted workflows, source-backed
  documentation, reviewable AI support

## Positioning

Use `emerging` for public-facing dignity and forward motion. Use `ambiguous`
only where the reader needs a work-condition diagnosis. The site should not
make future collaborators feel ashamed for being in transition.

Preferred public line:

> I build operating structure for emerging public-facing technical work.

Supporting principle:

> Structure grows out of the material you are working with.

The principle should support the professional frame, not replace it.
