# Rights and Permission

Public Git is publication. Draft pull requests and noindex staging pages are
not private review rooms.

Each occurrence tracks these dimensions separately:

- creator attribution;
- use permission or copyright basis;
- archive custody;
- represented-person dignity and privacy;
- visible artwork and third-party rights;
- collective-credit accuracy;
- caption accuracy;
- crop acceptability;
- exact destination;
- public-Git, staging, production, and indexing approval.

A visually strong image cannot compensate for a failed dimension.

## Permission capsules

The public Wiki stores a bounded summary. Raw messages, contact information,
and private evidence remain in a private source vault. A capsule names the
holder, exact scope, credit decision, exclusions, review date, and an opaque
private evidence reference.

## Revocation

Revocation blocks affected active occurrences, opens a correction, removes or
replaces the derivative through an explicit change, preserves history, and may
require cache or CDN purge. The private reason remains private unless its
publication is separately authorized.
