# Photography Workstream

**Status:** planning scaffold

**Branch:** `feature/photos-D`

**Base:** `develop` at `4b6bc3bff48f4fbefdda2f42aec327645f6a357c`

## Purpose

Develop a careful photography practice for the portfolio and Knowledge Wiki
from Jamie's large lifetime photo archive. The work should help readers see the
places, relationships, materials, and lived situations in which Jamie's work
occurred without turning the public site into an archive browser or treating
private photographs as automatically publishable.

The current archive-size estimate is a research lead, not a public metric. Any
quantitative description must be verified before projection.

## Operating Principles

1. The source photo library remains private and outside this public repository.
2. Archive access, visual inspection, evidence value, editorial selection,
   rights clearance, consent, and publication are separate decisions.
3. Public-safe records may use opaque asset IDs, redacted metadata, bounded
   descriptions, and explicit rights and consent states. They must not expose
   private paths, faces, locations, dates, or relationships merely because the
   source library contains them.
4. Every evidentiary image record should state what the image supports and what
   it does not establish. A photograph can document a visible scene without
   proving authorship, causality, representative participation, or consent.
5. People depicted are participants in their own lives, not scenery for an
   individual career narrative. Credit, dignity, context, and permission remain
   human concerns.
6. Curation runs should preserve versioned briefs, candidate manifests,
   selection reasons, exclusions, safety holds, and verification receipts so
   later editors can understand how a field was produced.
7. The Knowledge Wiki may hold a deep visual research layer. The portfolio
   remains a selective rhetorical composition governed by purpose and care.

## Opening Questions

- Which important projects and claims lack useful visual grounding?
- Which photographs show Jamie doing the work rather than merely standing near
  its outcome?
- Which scenes reveal maintenance, hospitality, construction, facilitation,
  translation, and technical operation?
- Which places help explain how Jamie's civic, cultural, artistic, and
  operational practices developed across time?
- Where can photographs clarify collective work while preserving collective
  authorship?
- Which images require collaborator review, photographer credit, depicted-person
  consent, location protection, or a permanent hold?
- What absences in the visual record are meaningful, and which should not be
  filled by inference?

## Initial Stages

1. **Source orientation:** establish authorized, read-only access and verify the
   available population without copying the private library into Git.
2. **Curatorial briefs:** derive project, period, place, practice, and audience
   questions from the Knowledge Wiki and current portfolio.
3. **Candidate fields:** produce versioned, reproducible candidate manifests
   with stable private asset identifiers.
4. **Recursive visual review:** inspect candidates at useful scale, record why
   images advance or leave a field, and seek fresh evidence when a limitation
   causes repeated reuse.
5. **Safety and rights review:** evaluate privacy, consent, credit, location,
   vulnerability, context, and mosaic risk before any public test.
6. **Composition tests:** assess image usefulness, cropping, captions,
   accessibility, responsive behavior, and relationship to supported prose.
7. **Human approval:** Jamie and any relevant collaborators or rights holders
   decide what may be published. Production observation remains a separate
   gate.

## First Deliverables To Design

- a private-source access contract and local configuration pattern;
- a versioned curatorial-brief format;
- an opaque asset and candidate-manifest schema;
- photo-specific evals and adversarial safety fixtures;
- a rights, consent, credit, and editorial-decision register;
- a Knowledge Wiki visual-evidence record that links to claims without exposing
  protected coordinates;
- a portfolio photo-placement and caption review workflow.

## Non-Goals For The First Pass

- committing the Apple Photos library or original private images;
- adding a public photo archive, browser, CMS, database, or search product;
- publishing faces, private locations, or sensitive metadata by default;
- equating technical access with permission;
- selecting photographs solely because they are flattering or visually
  dramatic;
- allowing automated evaluation to clear a human safety, rights, consent, or
  editorial hold.

## Human Gates

The workstream begins only at the level of governed preparation. Jamie's
editorial approval, photographer and rights-holder authority, depicted-person
consent where appropriate, collaborator review, accessibility review, and
production approval remain open.
