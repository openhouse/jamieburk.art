# Recollection and Correction

A staging or public encounter may produce:

- a dated recollection;
- creator or caption correction;
- permission update;
- accessibility finding;
- research inquiry;
- hold or takedown request;
- no action.

Record who supplied the response, what they directly know, what prompted it,
when it was recorded, what it supports, what it does not support, and whether a
page change is proposed.

Knowledge return does not automatically alter the current site. Recollections
remain dated and perspective-bound. Corrections identify affected occurrences
and preserve prior states without exposing private reasons.
