# Recollection and correction

A photograph can prompt memory without proving it.

First-person recollections are dated source records. They preserve the speaker,
scope, uncertainty, and relation to the image or occurrence. They do not become
event metadata, authorship, or impact claims by proximity.

A correction may update creator, date, place, caption assertion, derivative,
permission, or represented-person state. It must:

- preserve superseded statements;
- identify affected occurrences and editions;
- block invalid active uses;
- regenerate impact and placement reports;
- keep private evidence outside Git;
- require human review before public copy changes.
