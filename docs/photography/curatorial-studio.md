# Curatorial Studio

The studio produces proposals, not winners. A session begins with a visitor
problem and an exact page, not a request to find "the best photographs."

## Two passes

### Blind

Look at pixels with minimal technical context. Record attention, structure,
time, agency, atmosphere, ambiguity, sequence desire, and whether the image
opens or closes interpretation.

### Contextual

Then return to neighboring frames, generalized date and place, creator and
rights state, Knowledge Wiki records, recollections, counterevidence, and open
questions. Preserve what changed between the passes.

## Required output

Every session records:

- one lead proposal;
- one meaningful alternative;
- one dissent, concern, or hold;
- one sequence note;
- documentary and rights questions;
- one visitor-experience hypothesis;
- what should remain unexplained;
- a confidence boundary;
- a source-return request when more context could change the decision.

The default simulated lens panel is a project-authored set of public-context
heuristics associated with Ingeborg Gerdes, Margaret Morse, Zora Neale Hurston,
Jonas Mekas, Vivian Gornick, and Deborah Treisman. It is AI-generated role-play,
not those people's participation, private belief, endorsement, or permission.

No vote, aggregate score, or model rank selects an image. Jamie retains final
editorial authority, while rights holders and represented people retain the
authority appropriate to their own gates.
