# Curatorial studio

The studio produces proposals, questions, holds, sequences, crops, and source
returns. It does not publish.

Every simulated panel record must:

- disclose that the named lenses are simulations rather than participants or
  endorsements;
- include documentary questions and dissent;
- treat the full occurrence, not an isolated image, as the evaluative object;
- preserve human gates;
- reject majority vote or aggregate score as an artistic decision.

Agents may inspect authorized private material locally, work in an isolated
branch, and prepare staging candidates. They may not clear rights, consent,
dignity, legal review, production, or indexing.
