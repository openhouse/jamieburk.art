# Curatorial Studio

Begin with a visitor problem, not “find the best photo.” Freeze the page,
audience, current copy, current commit, known rights boundaries, and stop
condition.

Retrieve a bounded private field through three lanes:

- context-rich;
- adjacency and related frames;
- wildcard and underdescribed material.

Run a blind pass before supplying full context. Then return to source metadata,
related frames, rights state, Wiki records, and recollections. Preserve what
changed between the two readings.

Every proposal records a lead, meaningful alternative, dissent or hold,
sequence note, documentary questions, rights questions, no-photo
counterfactual, source-return request, confidence boundary, and what should
remain unexplained.

Named historical or editorial lenses are clearly labeled AI-generated
simulations of public-context reading heuristics. They are never the named
people's participation, private views, endorsements, or permission. Scores and
votes do not select or publish photographs.
