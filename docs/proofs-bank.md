# Proofs Bank

This document lists public-safe proof points that may support the site, resume, cover letters, interviews, referrals, and future claim reviews.

The repo is public. Nothing here should expose private source material, private correspondence, private contact lists, internal dashboards, legal-review materials, raw transcripts, raw community records, unapproved photos, credentials, proprietary font files, or private local paths.

The website is a projection of this bank. The bank keeps claim discipline; the site translates the strongest safe claims for the reader in front of it.

## Claim Format

Each proof should include:

- Claim.
- Safe wording.
- Stronger wording, if approved.
- Support level.
- Source class.
- Public pages where it may appear.
- Approval state.
- Limits / do not say.
- Protected material.
- Last reviewed.

## Support Levels

- `approved`
- `public-source`
- `resume-supported`
- `private-source-summary`
- `practice-supported`
- `needs-review`
- `do-not-publish`

## Approval States

- `approved`
- `needs-jamie-review`
- `needs-citation`
- `protected`
- `do-not-publish`

## Claims To Avoid

- Jamie single-handedly led or owned Commercial Rent Stabilization.
- Jamie single-handedly led NYC Artist Coalition, FairRentNYC, Let NYC Dance, Save NYC Spaces, Talks Not Raids, or any other coalition campaign.
- Jamie alone repealed the Cabaret Law, created the Office of Nightlife, or secured enforcement reforms.
- Jamie provided legal analysis or legal advice.
- CallNYC is a current or official city service.
- KC Town Hall funding was fully disbursed or the redevelopment was completed unless separately verified.
- WOWList was universally adopted or covered every city ecosystem.
- Sunday Dinner records are publishable.
- Source-Backed Team Memory is a finished SaaS or autonomous AI archive system.
- Revenue doubled because of Jamie alone.

## Projection Guidance

- Put Jamie back into the sentence as the actor.
- Preserve collective-work language where the work was collective.
- Use helped, supported, contributed to, co-built, stewarded, translated, mapped, and made usable when those are the actual moves.
- Use exact metrics only when approval state allows them and the phrasing preserves boundaries.
- Move exact citations, unresolved approvals, and sensitive evidence into docs or review notes, not public UI.
- Keep public pages shorter than the bank. The bank exists so the site can be clear without becoming thin.
