# Proofs Bank

This is the public-safe knowledge base for Jamie Burkart's professional claims.
It is not a private archive, evidence locker, transcript browser, or dumping
ground. Everything here should be safe to appear in a newspaper.

The website is a projection of this bank: shorter, more audience-specific, and
more selective. If a claim is not safe enough for this file, it is not safe
enough for the public site.

## Claim Rules

- Use claims that are public-safe, source-aware, and phrased at the right level
  of certainty.
- Separate what is known, what remains open, and what is protected.
- Treat collective work as collective work. Use "helped," "co-built,"
  "contributed to," "supported," "structured," "stewarded," and "translated"
  when that is the accurate role.
- Do not publish private emails, raw meeting records, private strategy context,
  legal-review material, internal analytics, customer data, stakeholder lists,
  guest lists, private correspondence, unapproved quotes, or unapproved photos.
- Do not publish precise impact metrics, adoption counts, revenue detail, or
  collaborator attribution unless the wording and source layer have been
  approved for public use.

## Support Levels

- `public-source`: supported by public pages, public reporting, public artifacts,
  or public repositories.
- `resume-backed`: appears in approved or near-approved resume/profile material,
  but should still be phrased conservatively on the site.
- `archive-backed`: supported by Jamie's private or semi-private archive, but
  only summarized here in public-safe language.
- `collaborator-review-needed`: directionally supported, but naming, quoting,
  screenshots, or stronger attribution need approval.
- `protected`: not for publication.

## Claims Not Ready For Public Use

These categories are not currently site copy:

- exact revenue multipliers or internal financial attribution;
- exact platform adoption counts unless approved for public use;
- exact community-gathering or residency counts unless approved for public use;
- unapproved screenshots, photos, quotes, or collaborator endorsements;
- private legal, strategy, analytics, customer, guest, or stakeholder records.

## Maintenance Workflow

1. Add or revise the claim here first.
2. Assign the support level and boundary.
3. Use the safest repeatable wording in website data or MDX.
4. Keep stronger private evidence out of the repo.
5. Run `npm run check` and the public-safety scan before publication.
