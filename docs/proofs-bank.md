# Proofs Bank

This repo is public. Treat this file as newspaper-safe: every claim here should
be acceptable if quoted without surrounding private context.

The website is a projection of this bank, not the bank itself. Use this file to
keep claims precise, audience-relevant, and public-safe before turning them into
homepage copy, case-study copy, resume language, or reviewer notes.

## Claim Rules

- Use the strongest accurate claim, but keep the verb honest.
- Prefer aggregate numbers, public artifacts, and approved summaries.
- Separate Jamie's role from collective work.
- Do not publish private emails, unedited meeting records, private coalition
  notes, client-private materials, internal analytics, guest lists, stakeholder
  lists, private financial detail, credentials, or unapproved names/photos.
- If a claim depends on private records, use careful wording and keep the
  underlying material out of the repo.
- When unsure, write: `TODO: Jamie approval required.`

## Hold Or Avoid

- "Led the movement."
- "Authored the bill."
- "Jamie leads NYC Artist Coalition" unless a current formal title is approved.
- "NYC Artist Coalition passed three laws" unless the exact laws, coalition role, and public citations are approved.
- "Provided legal analysis."
- "Proved rent caused closures."
- "Comptroller adopted the proposal."
- "Production SaaS" for Source-Backed Team Memory or Noting.us.
- "Official city platform" for CallNYC.
- "35 official city chapters" for WOWList.
- "Complete archive," "all data restored," or "millions of users."
- Named collaborators, participant names, private quotes, photos, screenshots, or raw source artifacts without approval.

## Review Questions

- Is the claim true without private context?
- Is the verb honest about Jamie's role?
- Does the claim help the reader understand role fit?
- Is the underlying source public, approved, aggregated, or safely summarized?
- Would this still feel appropriate if copied into a newspaper profile?
