# Proofs Bank

This is the public-safe knowledge base of Jamie Burkart's professional
accomplishments. It is written for a public repository. Nothing here should
publish or expose private source material, private correspondence, private
contact lists, unapproved photos, internal business records, legal-review
materials, or raw community records.

The website is a projection of this bank. It should select the smallest set of
claims that makes Jamie legible to hiring readers, referrers, collaborators, and
public-interest technology teams. The bank can hold more context than the site,
but it must stay newspaper-safe.

## Publication Model

- **Proofs bank:** Public-safe repository knowledge base. Holds defensible claims,
  source-basis categories, caution notes, and projection guidance.
- **Claim register:** Release-control layer. Records the exact wording currently
  approved for public pages and resume-adjacent surfaces.
- **Website:** Purpose-built projection. Uses the clearest claims for a specific
  reader and omits anything that creates privacy, consent, legal, or overclaiming
  risk.

## Public-Safe Source Basis

Use these source-basis labels instead of publishing private source text:

- Approved public resume PDF.
- Public-safe portfolio briefs.
- Public website or public project artifact.
- Public reporting / public-source notes.
- Aggregate workbook review with no names, contacts, addresses, or private rows.
- Public-safe source bundle reviewed by Jamie.
- Jamie review confirmation.
- Collaborator approval pending.

## Projection Guidance

### Homepage

Use only the most legible proof:

- 14+ years creating operating structure.
- 2x revenue-growth contribution, carefully framed.
- 30+ pages of civic campaign-memory infrastructure.
- Roughly 35 city ecosystems reached through WOWList.
- Hosted Sunday Dinner / 300+ documented gatherings.

The homepage should not explain every source. It should make a trusted referrer
understand Jamie in 30 seconds.

### Work Pages

Each case-study page should answer:

- What was unclear?
- What became usable?
- Toward what end?
- What is known, open, and protected?

Use proof claims as supporting structure, not as a trophy wall.

For the FairRentNYC / NYC Artist Coalition page, name the role plainly:

- co-founding member of NYC Artist Coalition;
- cultural-space fieldwork;
- FairRentNYC / CRS campaign-memory infrastructure;
- public-safe documentation, source mapping, action tracking, and follow-up;
- city/state CRS pathways and public-data framing.

Keep NAC accomplishments collective. The site may mention campaign lineage, but
should not imply Jamie alone repealed the Cabaret Law, created the Office of
Nightlife, led Talks Not Raids, or owns the Commercial Rent Stabilization
movement.

### Technical Operations Page

Lead with operating usefulness:

- requirements;
- workflows;
- status rhythms;
- risk notes;
- action trackers;
- decision records;
- onboarding;
- implementation support;
- launch/adoption support;
- durable handoffs.

Then point to the strongest proof clusters: HJE, CRS, CallNYC, WOWList, Sunday
Dinner, and Source-Backed Team Memory.

### Resume Page

Keep the resume page close to the approved PDF. Use the proof bank only to ensure
that summary bullets match approved wording and do not drift.

## Anti-Claims

Avoid these unless Jamie explicitly approves stronger evidence and wording:

- Jamie single-handedly led or owned Commercial Rent Stabilization.
- Jamie single-handedly led NYC Artist Coalition, FairRentNYC, Let NYC Dance,
  Save NYC Spaces, Talks Not Raids, or any other coalition campaign.
- Jamie alone repealed the Cabaret Law, created the Office of Nightlife, or
  secured M.A.R.C.H. transparency reforms.
- Jamie provided legal analysis or legal advice.
- CallNYC is a current or official city service.
- KC Town Hall received guaranteed funding.
- WOWList was universally adopted or covered every city ecosystem.
- Sunday Dinner public pages can include attendee-level records, addresses, or
  unapproved photos.
- Source-Backed Team Memory is a finished SaaS product, autonomous archive
  system, broad ingestion system, or replacement for human judgment.

## Review Protocol

Before adding a new public claim:

1. Add the claim here with source-basis category and public-use note.
2. If it is intended for the website, add exact wording to
   `docs/claim-register.md`.
3. Confirm the wording uses the right agency: helped, contributed, co-built,
   stewarded, hosted, documented, supported, or led.
4. Confirm no private names, contact details, sensitive records, or unapproved
   images are exposed.
5. Run public-safety and release checks before production indexing.
