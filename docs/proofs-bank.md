# Proofs Bank

This is the public-safe knowledge base for Jamie Burkart's professional
accomplishments. The repo is public. Do not add anything here that would be
unsafe, embarrassing, private, legally sensitive, or context-collapsing if it
appeared in a newspaper.

The website should project selected claims from this bank. It should not expose
the archive itself, raw source files, private transcripts, contact lists,
client-private details, internal analytics, legal-review material, or private
coalition notes.

## Claim Standard

- **Known:** strong enough for public portfolio copy when framed within the
  stated limits.
- **Use with care:** public-safe only with collective-work language, context,
  and visible limits.
- **Open:** plausible or source-backed but not ready for publication without
  Jamie approval, collaborator approval, a public citation, or stronger source
  review.
- **Protected:** intentionally omitted from the website and public repo.

## Projection Rules

- Prefer "built," "stewarded," "translated," "co-built," "contributed to,"
  "supported," and "helped structure" when they accurately describe the role.
- Use collective language for coalition, community, civic, and campaign work.
- Do not imply sole ownership of movements, legislation, legal analysis,
  public agencies, client outcomes, or collective campaigns.
- Publish summaries, approved metrics, public artifacts, representative
  diagrams, and careful source descriptions.
- Omit raw private material even when it would make the claim feel stronger.
