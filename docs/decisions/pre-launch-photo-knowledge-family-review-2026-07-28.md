# Pre-launch decision: closing the photo-knowledge branch family

Date: 2026-07-28

## Decision

`feature/pre-launch-C` begins from the exact frozen
`feature/photo-knowledge-C` candidate at `cf67cfda`. The family remains
preserved for study. Compatible strengths may be reimplemented or selectively
integrated only when they improve the pre-launch candidate without weakening
truth, collective credit, public safety, accessibility, or human authority.

## Strengths by branch

### A: artifacts and source return

A makes implementation tangible. Its strongest move is pairing photographs
with proposal graphics, printed matter, maps, and other working artifacts. Its
source-return records keep first-person recollection, visible observation,
corroboration, and publication permission separate.

### B: authority and operating clarity

B offers the clearest human-authority model. It identifies which named human
may decide creator, rights, consent, credit, crop, caption, represented-person,
editorial, production, deployment, and indexing gates. Its restraint produces
a direct hiring path and a legible person-to-practice-to-consequence sequence.

### C: selective projection and bounded claims

C minimizes reader burden most consistently. It keeps the portfolio composed,
the resume direct, the home page quiet, and the Knowledge Wiki deep. Its
source-return pass also preserves the strongest current KC Town Hall wording
while retaining fuller lifecycle evidence outside the hiring foreground.

### D: occurrence-level governance and collective scenes

D supplies the richest occurrence model: an asset, derivative, route,
component, and placement decision remain distinct. Visually, it most clearly
shows rooms, collaborators, public action, and material work. Its registry is a
strong pattern for preventing a cleared asset from silently inheriting approval
for every future use.

### E: photographic essay and public-practice framing

E has the strongest long-form photographic rhythm. It lets images establish a
world before asking the reader to process a claim, and it narrates the practice
of listening and participation with unusual clarity. Its asset-by-asset review
ledger is an effective public explanation of why working-branch inclusion is
not production clearance.

## Integration rule

The pre-launch branch will borrow:

- A's source-return discipline;
- B's named human-authority registry;
- C's selective public projection and current claims;
- D's occurrence-level placement contract;
- E's photographic sequencing and practice language.

It will not merge whole branch histories, carry forward stale evaluation
receipts, vendor unrelated tool snapshots, or treat automated approval as
publication permission.

## Cost-aware recursive evaluation

Inner-loop evaluation is deterministic and impact-routed. Expensive semantic
panels run once, after all deterministic hard gates pass, against one unchanged
candidate fingerprint. Any later candidate-affecting change invalidates those
receipts. This reduces repeated model use while making the evidentiary contract
more explicit.

## Human launch gates

Passing tests does not authorize production deployment or indexing. Jamie's
final editorial review, exact photo rights and dignity decisions, production
deployment approval, and indexing approval remain separate decisions.
