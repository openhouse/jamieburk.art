# Public Safety

This repo publishes a public-safe selected proof site. It must not become a
private archive, raw transcript store, legal-review folder, client document
browser, private stakeholder list, private font store, or credential repository.

Private source materials must live outside this repo and outside the Docker
context.

Allowed public-safe materials:

- public-safe summaries;
- redacted or representative examples;
- approved artifacts;
- public sources;
- approved resume PDF;
- Knowledge Bank claims reviewed for public use;
- Known / Open / Protected summaries.

## Project Rules

Harry J. Epstein Company: public-safe web, e-commerce, analytics, content,
marketing, operations, and handoff summaries are allowed. Dashboards, customer
data, revenue breakdowns, vendor terms, passwords, and private operations stay
protected.

FairRentNYC / Commercial Rent Stabilization: public-safe campaign-memory,
source-map, public-data framing, and coordination-system summaries are allowed.
Private coalition notes, legal-review materials, stakeholder lists, raw strategy
context, private emails, and unapproved quotes stay protected.

CallNYC: frame as an archived, unofficial civic-data prototype. Do not publish
the Politico citation until the exact public source package is verified.

WOWList: summarize platform model, organizer workflows, keyword communities, and
public-safe aggregate adoption only after wording approval. Private user data,
organizer contact lists, raw account records, and unapproved community records
stay protected.

196 Artists Residency / Sunday Dinner: aggregate participation, onboarding,
facilitation, hosting, and continuity systems may be summarized. Guest lists,
attendance records, addresses, phone numbers, private stories, and unapproved
photos stay protected.

KC Town Hall: use cautious adaptive-reuse and public-benefit documentation
language. Private financial, legal, property, banking, stakeholder, and family
details stay protected.

Source-Backed Team Memory: frame as early method / lab / proof-of-practice. Do
not present it as production SaaS, a surveillance system, an autonomous
publication system, a chatbot, legal/medical advice, or a private archive
browser.
