# Public Safety

This portfolio is a public proof site, not a total archive.

Do not publish:

- Home address or apartment number
- Birth date
- Private therapy, health, medication, insurance, or clinical details
- Private financial, trust, banking, reserve, debt, or tax information
- Private emails, DMs, or raw transcripts
- Unapproved quotes from friends or collaborators
- Private coalition notes
- Legal strategy or legal review
- Internal client analytics
- Passwords, credentials, or admin screenshots
- Raw Sunday Dinner / artist-residency private records
- Raw guest or attendance data
- Private resident details
- Confidential client or company materials
- Private stakeholder lists
- Unapproved photos
- Anything implying an official government service where there was only a civic prototype

Use these phrases when detail is useful but the source material is not public:

- "Selected public-safe materials available on request."
- "Public-safe summary of collective work."
- "Private notes and unapproved internal materials are intentionally omitted."

Collective work should use bounded contribution language: contributed to, helped structure, supported, co-built, stewarded, translated, or maintained.
