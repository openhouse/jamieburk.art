# Public Safety

This portfolio is a public-safe proof surface. It should make selected work
legible without publishing private source material, private correspondence,
unapproved photos, raw transcripts, private coalition notes, credentials,
stakeholder lists, internal analytics, client-private material, or raw community
records.

## Project Rules

- HJE: no dashboards, customer data, private revenue detail, credentials, vendor
  terms, or internal operations.
- FairRentNYC / Commercial Rent Stabilization: no private coalition notes,
  legal-review materials, stakeholder lists, raw strategy context, private
  emails, or unapproved quotes.
- CallNYC: archived prototype only; not an official or current City Council
  service.
- WOWList: no private user data, organizer contact lists, or unapproved
  community records.
- 196 / Sunday Dinner: no guest lists, attendance records, addresses, private
  stories, private interiors, or unapproved photos.
- KC Town Hall: no private financial, legal, property, banking, or stakeholder
  details.
- Source-Backed Team Memory: early method/research/consulting practice, not
  production SaaS, an AI replacement for judgment, legal or medical advice, or a
  private archive browser.

## Release Rule

When a claim, artifact, screenshot, contact value, collaborator name, or metric
is uncertain, either soften it out of production copy or mark it as a launch
blocker outside the public UI.
