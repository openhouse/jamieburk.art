# Launch Checklist

Use this checklist before production indexing. Staging may be reviewable while
items remain open, but production should not become indexable until Jamie has
approved the public content and contact path.

## Required Before Production

- Replace the placeholder resume PDF with the current approved resume.
- Decide whether the resume PDF should be indexable in production, or kept
  noindex while still downloadable from the resume page.
- Confirm the public email address.
- Confirm LinkedIn and GitHub links, or remove those rows from the contact page.
- Confirm screenshots, representative diagrams, and public artifacts.
- Confirm proof metrics and keep contribution language careful:
  - `14+ years building operating structure`
  - `2x revenue growth contribution for legacy e-commerce business`
  - `30+ pages of civic campaign-memory infrastructure`
  - `35 city ecosystems reached through WOWList.org`
  - `300+ hosted gatherings / 20+ resident artists supported`
- Confirm collaborator names, credits, photos, and quotes.
- Confirm production metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap URLs, and OpenGraph image
  all point to `https://jamieburk.art`.
- Confirm production robots settings are intentionally indexable only after
  Jamie approval.

## Public-Safety Review

Do not publish private emails, raw transcripts, private coalition notes,
legal-review materials, health or financial details, private correspondence,
unapproved photos, private fonts, credentials, private stakeholder lists,
internal analytics, client-private materials, raw community records, private
guest or residency details, private company context, or coalition strategy.
