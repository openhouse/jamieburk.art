# Copy Guidelines

This site should make Jamie's work legible without flattening it.

## Rules

1. Put Jamie back into the sentence as the actor.
2. Answer "toward what end?"
3. Translate specialized terms before using them.
4. Prefer stewardship, facilitation, coordination, analysis, implementation, and handoff language over passive "tracking."
5. Use public-safe summaries.
6. Do not overclaim.
7. Do not publish visible TODOs.
8. Move unresolved approvals into launch blockers.
9. Keep the reader's cognitive load low.
10. Use exact numbers only when approved.

## Preferred Patterns

Instead of:
"I tracked meetings and decisions."

Use:
"I stewarded operating memory: synthesizing meetings, preserving decisions, surfacing open questions, coordinating next steps, and making work easier for collaborators to continue."

Instead of:
"I build lightweight systems."

Use:
"I build simple, repeatable, low-overhead systems people can actually use."

Instead of:
"AI-ready documentation."

Use:
"Documentation systems that preserve sources, decisions, open questions, and human review so teams can use AI without losing accountability."

Instead of:
"under-structured environments."

Use:
"early-stage, resource-constrained, stakeholder-heavy environments where requirements, ownership, documentation, and handoffs are not yet clear."
