# Copy Guidelines

This site should make Jamie's work legible without flattening it, overstating it, or exposing protected context.

## Professional Legibility Rules

1. Minimize the reader's burden.
2. Put Jamie back into the sentence as the actor.
3. Translate specialized language for hiring, public-sector, and non-specialist readers.
4. Explain each project's purpose: toward what end?
5. Show what became usable.
6. Avoid unsupported ownership claims.
7. Prefer helped / supported / contributed to / co-built where work was collective.
8. Do not let tool lists make Jamie disappear.
9. Do not publish unapproved contact info, metrics, screenshots, collaborator names, quotes, or private materials.
10. Do not publish uncertainty as public TODOs.

## Preferred Rewrite Examples

Instead of:

`Tracked meetings and decisions.`

Use:

`Stewarded operating memory: synthesized meetings, preserved decisions, surfaced open questions, coordinated next steps, and made the work easier for collaborators to continue.`

Instead of:

`Lightweight systems.`

Use:

`Simple, repeatable, low-overhead systems people can actually use.`

Instead of:

`AI-ready documentation.`

Use:

`Documentation systems that preserve sources, decisions, open questions, and human review so teams can use AI without losing accountability.`

Instead of:

`Under-structured environments.`

Use:

`Early-stage, resource-constrained, stakeholder-heavy environments where requirements, ownership, documentation, and handoffs are not yet clear.`

## Preferred Verbs

- Built.
- Co-built.
- Structured.
- Stewarded.
- Translated.
- Mapped.
- Coordinated.
- Synthesized.
- Made usable.
- Supported.
- Helped.
- Contributed to.

## Avoid

- Vague identity language when role language is clearer.
- Public-first phrases such as private knowledge universe, future living archive, personal Wikipedia, AI philosophy, under-structured, lightweight, maintainers, tracking, or AI-ready.
- Unsupported ownership language such as led, owned, created, secured, or transformed unless the proof bank approves the exact claim.
- Claims that make tools the subject and Jamie disappear.
