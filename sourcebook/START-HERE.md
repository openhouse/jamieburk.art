## Read with care

Each catalog entry distinguishes the words' source, context, stance,
certification, publication basis, credit scope, and intended surface. A
self-authored statement establishes Jamie's position in that context. It does
not establish that the hearing body endorsed him, that each assertion is
independently verified, or that Jamie alone produced a collective or
institutional outcome.

The [scope and gaps](catalog/SCOPE-AND-COVERAGE.md), [rights and consent
report](catalog/RIGHTS-AND-CONSENT.md), [dependency
report](catalog/DEPENDENCIES.md), and [validation
report](catalog/VALIDATION.md) are part of the reading experience, not backroom
administration.
