## Anti-Patterns Verdict

The site passes the AI-slop test because documentary photography, inspectable
artifacts, bounded claims, and its civic-operational subject matter are
specific. Template residue appears below the first viewport through repeated
uppercase labels, pale bordered cards, pills, side-accent callouts, and uniform
evidence schemas.

The deterministic scan of `apps/www/src` reported 15 findings: seven
`side-tab` warnings, seven design-token color advisories around photographic
scrims and white text, and one small-radius advisory. The side-tab findings are
real violations of the established design rule. The scrim colors and citation
radius are intentional design-document drift rather than observed defects.

## Overall Impression

The portfolio opens with human confidence and builds unusual trust through
specific work, real images, collective credit, and source boundaries. The
largest opportunity is compression: let the homepage and first portion of each
case study make the hiring argument, then let the governed record remain
available underneath.

## What Works

- Real photography and inspectable artifacts make the portfolio memorable.
- Collective credit and evidence boundaries communicate mature judgment.
- Responsive structure is stable across the reviewed desktop and mobile routes.

## Priority Issues

### P1: Homepage duplication

The homepage repeats too much of `/work`, producing a roughly 13,000-pixel
mobile page. Keep three lead projects on the homepage and move the full project
field to `/work`.

### P1: Case-study orientation and hierarchy

Case studies ask mobile readers to traverse the narrative before reaching the
at-a-glance summary, and long pages provide no compact route through the
record. Put the summary first on mobile, add a breadcrumb and section index,
and keep evidence available after the hiring argument.

### P1: Resume composition

The desktop resume page leaves an unresolved empty field beside a long impact
panel. Make the introduction full width and present selected impact as a
balanced grid, preserving the PDF as the primary action.

### P2: Repetitive callout grammar

Seven thick left-edge callouts create a recognizable template pattern. Replace
them with full borders, quiet tonal surfaces, and existing radii.

### P2: LCP image hints

The HJE and CallNYC lead artifacts produce development warnings requesting
eager loading. Prioritize the first artifact image where it participates in
the page's largest-contentful paint.

## Persona Red Flags

- A hiring manager may leave before reaching the strongest artifacts.
- A recruiter must download the PDF to obtain conventional chronology.
- A civic collaborator will correctly understand unresolved photo rights,
  credit, and consent as a launch gate.
- A mobile reader sees stable reflow but insufficient prioritization on long
  pages.
