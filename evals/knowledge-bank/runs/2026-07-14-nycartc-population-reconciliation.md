# NYC Artist Coalition Population-Reconciled Social Corpus

Run date: 2026-07-14

## Objective

Disposition the full 5,124-post profile control for `@NYCArtC`, preserve every
public item and posted URL the authenticated carrier exposed, mature significant
sources and claims, keep collective authorship and metric ownership intact, and
leave deeper material available without adding social volume to the public site.

## Population Result

Authenticated profile traversal and bounded date searches recovered 1,026
unique public status records:

- 309 account posts;
- 33 account replies; and
- 684 source-authored statuses observed as native reposts from 216 accounts.

The ledger retains 4,098 unresolved profile-count slots, closing the displayed
population exactly: `1,026 + 4,098 = 5,124`. This is 100 percent population
disposition, not 100 percent item-level recovery, a native export, or a
representative sample. Recovered item dates cover 2017-2018 and 2023-2026; the
2019-2022 and older-repost gaps remain open carrier research, not evidence of
inactivity.

## Findings Retained

- Four campaign signals remain legible: FairRentNYC in 104 records, LetNYCDance
  in 101, SaveNYCSpaces in 98, and TalksNotRaids in 16.
- The recovered amplification field contains 684 source statuses from 216
  accounts. Twenty-five source statuses from 13 outside accounts directly
  mention `@NYCArtC`; this remains separate from the existing official-at-date
  floor of five sitting City Council-member accounts.
- The ledger preserves 536 short-link occurrences and 408 unique short URLs.
  At review time, 384 resolved to 345 unique destinations; 24 did not resolve.
- Ten linked articles were closely read into proposition-level observations
  covering nightlife enforcement, cultural-space displacement, queer
  nightlife, commercial rent, artist-worker economics, and live-music market
  power. The existing Baffler source ID was reused and enriched rather than
  duplicated.
- A bounded July 2026 snapshot of 342 account-authored statuses records 11
  replies, 544 reposts, and 1,111 likes. Metrics on all 684 source-authored
  statuses are excluded from coalition traction.

## Composition Decision

All five new social-corpus claims remain held with no public surfaces. Existing
portfolio claims already use stronger press, official-record, testimony, and
campaign-artifact evidence. The social corpus increases future compositional
range without increasing today's reader burden.
