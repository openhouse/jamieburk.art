# NYC Artist Coalition government-interface hill climb

Date: 2026-07-15

## Objective

Search the recoverable official New York City Council hearing record for Tom
Finkelpearl references to NYC Artist Coalition, preserve the strongest direct
record, and develop bounded interpretations of why the coalition's work was
useful to DCLA, the Council, and Council Member Rafael Espinal.

## Evidence result

One direct coalition reference was recovered in a bounded review of five
indexed official Finkelpearl transcript candidates. In the May 19, 2017 Fiscal
2018 Executive Budget hearing, transcript page 92, Finkelpearl identified NYC
Artist Coalition as having formed after DCLA convened a January meeting for the
DIY arts community. Pages 91-92 place the example inside his account of direct
public feedback, reciprocal public relationships, and organizing around a
common cause.

This is a recovered-reference floor, not a complete native Council transcript
export and not proof that no other reference ever existed.

## Recursive hill climb

### Iteration 1: direct record

The official hearing transcript became a public source with an exact page
locator, a direct observation, and a confirmed-with-boundary claim. The source
records what Finkelpearl said; it does not prove private motive, dependency, or
policy causation.

### Iteration 2: actor-specific interpretation

Three separate inference claims were created rather than one generalized
institutional-value claim. DCLA's civic-intermediary value, the Council's
deliberative value, and Espinal's constituency-and-implementation value each
carry their own evidence, limits, and anti-claims.

### Iteration 3: collective and causal boundaries

All three interpretations remain held off public surfaces. Checks require
collective credit and reject claims that an official depended on Jamie, that
the coalition was a sole constituency, or that Jamie or the coalition alone
caused legislation or agency outcomes.

### Iteration 4: bounded search language

The transcript audit records method, candidate count, recovered-reference
count, corpus limits, and nonexistence boundary. An adversarial test rejects
turning one recovered reference into an exclusivity claim.

### Iteration 5: report and graph integrity

The proof-coverage graph now points to the hearing transcript, DCLA message,
2019 M.A.R.C.H. hearing, and Espinal's public engagement, plus the two research
inquiries. The public-safe report answers the institutional questions while
keeping inference visibly distinct from quoted record.
