# NYC Artist Coalition Facebook posts full-population eval run

Date: 2026-07-15

## Objective

Review every distinct post exposed by the authenticated NYC Artist Coalition
Facebook Page feed, preserve a privacy-safe and replayable census, mature
mission-relevant sources and claims, and prevent references, shared-account
activity, or volatile interface labels from becoming unsupported engagement,
individual authorship, or impact.

## Population reconciliation

The protected capture encountered 598 render rows. Private platform identities
reconciled 153 duplicate variants into 445 distinct dated posts spanning January
29, 2017 through September 15, 2021. All 445 public reconciliation hashes are
unique. Year totals reproduce as 186, 74, 111, 69, and five.

The population crawl produced seven stable terminal checks. A separate
authenticated endpoint recheck again held the January 2017 Cultural Affairs
recommendation record through seven checks with no loading boundary. Small
interface-height changes did not change the endpoint record.

The denominator is the capture-date live Page feed, not a native Meta owner
export or complete lifetime history. Unexposed, deleted, hidden, private,
unpublished, or no-longer-retained records remain possible.

## Public-safe production

The fixture excludes raw post bodies, comment text, follower and interaction
identities, contact details, authenticated URLs, private post identities,
tracking data, session state, and two sensitive exact routes. It retains dates,
one-way reconciliation hashes, route keys, bounded classifications, content
hashes, and displayed counts.

The 445 rows reconcile to the documented mission, stakeholder-reference, and
account-reference totals. Stakeholder classifications are explicitly outgoing
references and issue interfaces, not attributable incoming engagement.

## Sources and interaction signals

The population contains 67 distinct cleaned off-Facebook routes: 65 exact
public-safe routes and two withheld sensitive routes. Nine resolve to governed
source records; 56 remain route-inventory-only. The pass adds Grub Street, FOX
5, and Time Out records and reuses six governed campaign-press sources.

Facebook displayed 2,291 reactions across 371 rows and 212 comments across 128
rows. The highest reaction label was 95 on a Cabaret Law repeal post. Share
counts were absent from this surface. The eval rejects any conversion of these
labels into unique people, reach, attendance, endorsement, mandate, or impact.

## Credit and projection

Jamie remembers predominantly using the Page while believing collaborators also
used it. Current management access establishes present custody only. The shared
Page does not identify historical post authors. The three new claims therefore
remain `hold`, and no website copy changes were made.

## Deterministic hill climb

`evals:nycac-facebook-posts` recomputes population, chronology, mission tags,
stakeholder references, account references, route roles, governed sources, and
displayed interactions. It validates lifecycle closure, current-schema source
references, collective credit, held projections, and public-safety exclusions.

The evaluator rejects:

- dropped, duplicated, reordered, or count-preserving row mutations;
- raw bodies, social-graph identities, contact data, authenticated URLs, or
  session state in the public fixture;
- Page-authored references rewritten as incoming stakeholder engagement;
- posted articles rewritten as coverage, endorsement, partnership, or impact;
- current access rewritten as historical post authorship;
- displayed counts rewritten as reach, attendance, conversion, mandate, or
  impact; and
- held archive claims silently promoted onto website surfaces.

The acceptance criterion is 100 / 100 with no hard failures. The full recursive
check then reruns citation, lifecycle, application, public-safety, corpus, Chad
lens, and all prior archival-production gates.
