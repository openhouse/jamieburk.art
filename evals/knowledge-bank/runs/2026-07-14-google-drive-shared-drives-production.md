## Objective

Develop the public-safe knowledge bank through a bounded close reading of
Jamie's authenticated Google Drive Shared Drives while preserving private
collaborative records, participant data, collective credit, and selective
public composition.

## Archive production

The pass:

- enumerated 110 accessible Shared Drives without retaining private drive IDs,
  URLs, permissions, or complete listings;
- inspected 26 portfolio-relevant drive roots and selected second-level
  folders;
- close-read 7 unique high-signal artifacts after deduplicating copies already
  represented by another archive carrier;
- created 7 dispositioned intake items, 7 protected source records, 10 atomic
  observations, 6 bounded claims, and 5 research inquiries; and
- strengthened one existing public proof while holding the other five mature
  claims for future composition.

This was a relevance-selected sample, not an exhaustive review of all 110
drives. Accessible does not mean owned, authored, consented for publication, or
current. Empty, sparse, unreviewed, or inaccessible folders do not establish
that evidence never existed.

## Selective projection

states that a protected operating ledger records 345 numbered gatherings and

names, contact details, attendance history, and invitation records remain
private.

a citation. The other five new claims remain held with no public surfaces.
