## Recursive hill climb

### Iteration 1: recover and reconcile

Profile routes and yearly searches were exhausted, deduplicated, and reconciled
against the 5,124-post counter. The 2,001-record gap became an explicit owner-
archive inquiry rather than a hidden completeness assumption.

### Iteration 2: classify identity and appearance

Originals, replies, external repost sources, self-repost appearances, and quote-
post corrections were separated. Source-post dates were fenced off from
coalition activity chronology.

### Iteration 3: make classification replayable

Mission rules, classification-input digests, and retained positive-match
evidence were checked in. Link, source-author, incoming-response, and visible-
interaction inventories received distinct boundaries.

The first skeptical holdout found that the selected claim was missing from the
generated public registry and that count-preserving row mutations could bypass
aggregate checks. The registry was regenerated; primary and incoming row
digests and adversarial mutations were added.

Two judges independently found that adding the new claim had invalidated a
suite-wide institutional claim-set lock. The approved digest was updated to the
independently recomputed 21-claim value, and direct citation-resolver and
incoming-row mutation regressions were added.

### Iteration 6: independent acceptance

Two fresh judges reviewed frozen commit `5b68b0c1`. An archival data-integrity
reviewer and a skeptical public-interest hiring editor each scored all 16
criteria 5 out of 5 with no blockers. Earlier rejected runs remain in the ledger.
