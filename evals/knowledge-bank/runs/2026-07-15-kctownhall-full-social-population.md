# KC Town Hall full social-population archival production run

**Run date:** 2026-07-15

**Branch:** `feature/evals-H`

**Scope:** 100 percent of the surviving `@KCTownHall` profile population,
every posted URL, all repost-bearing account statuses, source roles, stakeholder
patterns, public safety, knowledge-bank maturation, and selective portfolio
projection.

## Objective

Account for the full current profile while preserving distinctions that prevent
both overstatement and understatement:

1. reconcile Posts and Replies into one status-ID census;
2. separate account posts, account replies, and external posts reposted by the
   project;
3. inventory every posted short URL and classify its source role;
4. audit every account-authored status displaying reposts;
5. distinguish outreach, project-selected amplification, public repost-list
   appearance, direct outside-authored response, and endorsement;
6. verify public-official roles at the interaction date;
7. protect shared authorship, resident privacy, and the separate municipal
   funding lifecycle; and
8. project only the clearest hiring-relevant finding.

## Recursive hill climb

### Iteration 1: full population and fresh reconciliation

The July 14 census was independently traversed again. Posts and Replies
reconciled to the same exact 183 status IDs with no unresolved slots.

### Iteration 2: complete repost audit

Every one of the 40 repost-bearing account statuses was opened. The public
identity remainder was preserved instead of silently treating 70 displayed
reposts as 70 known people.

### Iteration 3: institutional distinctions

Official city records checked Council service at the date of each public
appearance. Outreach, project-selected reposts, public repost-list appearances,
and direct responses were modeled as separate relationships.

### Iteration 4: source-role and privacy controls

Independent project coverage was separated from sources merely routed by the
account. Raw post bodies, historical phone numbers, street addresses, messages,
credentials, and private analytics remained outside the repository.

### Iteration 5: adversarial evaluation

The hard gate rejects a dropped record, relationship inflation, source-status
metrics assigned to KC Town Hall, raw-body leakage, duplicated reposter
identities, unsupported Council-role evidence, census drift, service-unit
inflation, and misuse of the independent article.

### Iteration 6: citation-order repair

The first generated registry correctly rejected the Kansas City Star source as
unused in the selected page citation order. The article remained in the
knowledge bank and was removed from the case-study footnote sequence.
