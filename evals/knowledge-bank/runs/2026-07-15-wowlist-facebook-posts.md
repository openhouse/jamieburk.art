# WOW List Facebook posts full-population eval run

Date: 2026-07-15

## Objective

Review every distinct post exposed by the authenticated WOW List Facebook page
feed, preserve a public-safe and replayable census, mature mission-relevant
sources and claims, and prevent project-account evidence from becoming
individual authorship, sole credit, or unsupported impact.

## Population reconciliation

Repeated lazy scrolling reached the same terminal post pair across seven
consecutive checks. The ledger contains 57 distinct dated posts from April 25,
2015 through March 22, 2018:

- 22 posts in 2015;
- 27 posts in 2016;
- seven posts in 2017; and
- one post in 2018.

Facebook exposed no native total-post count. The population control is the
stable terminal crawl plus the reconciled 57-row ledger. Meta Business Suite's
Content surface labeled March 31, 2019 through July 14, 2026 as `Lifetime` and
returned no activity, but that window begins after the recovered feed ends. It
cannot account for the 2015-2018 population.

This is complete capture-date live-feed accounting, not a native Meta export or
proof of complete lifetime history. Deleted, hidden, private, unpublished, or
no-longer-retained posts can remain outside the recovered population.

## Public-safe production

The fixture preserves dates, displayed source-card relationships, cleaned URL
routes, bounded mission and stakeholder tags, displayed interaction counts, and
content hashes. It excludes raw post bodies, comment text, interaction and
follower identities, contact details, tracking parameters, authenticated URLs,
cookies, session state, and private analytics.

The 57 rows reconcile to:

- 24 shared-source-card rows and 33 page-originated rows;
- 55 distinct cleaned routes: 30 WOW List and 25 external;
- 17 product-onboarding or use rows;
- 16 cross-city organizer-infrastructure rows;
- three participatory product-governance rows;
- 38 cultural-event-distribution rows;
- 12 venue-safety or survival rows; and
- six mutual-aid or civic-mobilization rows.

## Source and traction findings

Seven routes resolve to governed sources: a Shelby Turner tutorial, Santa
Barbara DIY, DoDIY, Denver Westword, East Bay Express, Willamette Week, and
Meow Wolf. The other 48 routes remain explicitly `not-rechecked-in-this-pass`
and `route-inventory-only`. Issue-context sources establish what WOW List
circulated; they are not coverage of WOW List and do not establish causation,
formal partnership, endorsement, or impact.

At capture time, 41 rows displayed 87 likes, 12 displayed 16 comments, and ten
displayed 49 shares. The October 5, 2015 nine-city introduction had the
population maxima: 13 likes, three comments, and 29 shares. These are volatile
interface counts, not reach, attendance, conversion, endorsement, unique
people, mandate, or impact.

## Credit and projection

Jamie remembers managing WOW List's social presence and currently has
authenticated page-management access. Neither current access nor a page-level
post identifies the historical human author of every post. The project remains
shared work with Richard Caceres, organizers, artists, and other contributors.
Social stewardship therefore remains a research lead pending collaborator
confirmation or native account audit data.

The pass adds one intake, ten observations, eleven sources, three claims, three
research inquiries, a 57-row census, and a 55-route inventory. All three new
projections remain held. No website copy changes were made because the public
case study already carries stronger individual-role evidence, while this
archive adds depth without resolving post-level authorship.

## Deterministic hill climb

The dedicated `evals:wowlist-facebook-posts` criterion recomputes the
population, chronology, relationship, URL, mission, stakeholder, and displayed
interaction totals. It also validates governed-route semantics, lifecycle
closure, source positioning, held projections, collective credit, and privacy
exclusions.

The evaluator rejects:

- dropped, duplicated, or count-preserving row mutations;
- raw post bodies, contact data, authenticated URLs, and session-state fields;
- source cards rewritten as page or human authorship;
- linked issue context rewritten as coverage, partnership, agreement, or
  impact;
- current page access rewritten as historical post authorship;
- displayed interactions rewritten as reach, attendance, endorsement,
  conversion, mandate, or impact;
- an empty bounded Business Suite result rewritten as historical
  nonexistence;
- shared WOW List work rewritten as Jamie's sole project; and
- held archive claims silently promoted to website surfaces.

The acceptance criterion is 100 / 100 with no hard failures. After the
dedicated gate passes, `npm run check` reruns the citation, knowledge lifecycle,
public-safety, application, corpus, Chad-lens, and recursive-protocol gates
across the complete branch.

## Open holdouts

Future independent review should use two lenses:

- archival data integrity, privacy, provenance, route semantics, and population
  reconciliation; and
- skeptical hiring-editor usefulness, collective credit, source positioning,
  and non-inflationary projection.

Neither lens may turn page-level evidence into individual post authorship or
turn displayed interactions into a measure of people, outcomes, or impact.
