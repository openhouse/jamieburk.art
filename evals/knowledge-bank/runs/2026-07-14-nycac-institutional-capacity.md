## Objective

Encode a public-safe answer to why NYC Artist Coalition mattered to DCLA, the
New York City Council, and Rafael Espinal without claiming private motive,
institutional dependency, sole causation, advocate authorship, or advocate
enactment.

## Bounded finding

Public records support treating NYC Artist Coalition as an organized civic
counterpart. DCLA cited coalition formation as an outcome of public convening;
Council and Espinal hearing records document the solicitation and receipt of
situated stakeholder evidence; coalition records and reporting preserve
structured recommendations and public forums connecting grassroots cultural
spaces with officials. These are documented public functions, not evidence of
what an official privately needed or proof that the coalition caused a law.
