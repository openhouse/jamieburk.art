# Personal and WOW List Facebook events archival-production run

Date: 2026-07-15

## Objective

Account for the full event-card population exposed by Jamie Burkart's
authenticated personal Facebook Events index and the authenticated WOW List
page-owner Events surface. Preserve public event pages, source routes, and
research leads that can mature the knowledge bank without turning profile
association, private records, response labels, or an empty current page into
inflated role or impact claims.

## Population reconciliation

Repeated lazy scrolling reached three consecutive stable personal-index counts
of 511 event-card instances. Those instances represent 502 unique parent
events; four recurring parent IDs account for 13 displayed instances. Every
displayed row has one anonymous public-safe disposition:

| Disposition | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Held: profile association only | 398 |

The 511-row public ledger contains only ordinal, year, detail state, displayed
privacy state, recurrence flag, and disposition. It does not expose identities,
titles, descriptions, exact residential addresses, contacts, guest lists, or
authenticated session data for held records.

The current WOW List page-owner surface exposed zero event cards and displayed
"No events to show." That is complete capture-date accounting for the current
owner-visible surface, not evidence that WOW List never created, cohosted,
shared, imported, or linked events.

## Recovered evidence

The 21 selected public event pages span 2006 through 2019 and explicitly name
Jamie as organizer or co-organizer. They preserve a bounded chronology across
participatory art, waterways, community meals, touring and DIY music networks,
cultural-space safety, public discussion, and civic participation. Organizer
display does not establish sole authorship, sole production, attendance, or
project outcomes.

Two records strengthen existing public pages:

- a 2014 page presents the gathering as the 100th Sunday Dinner and names Jamie
  as organizer; and
- a 2016 page presents the 200th Sunday Dinner, names Julia Fredenburg and Jamie
  as co-organizers, and links directly to a WOW List event route.

The numbered titles are contemporaneous milestone evidence, not an independent
audit of every Sunday Dinner or proof of the current 300-plus aggregate claim.
The direct WOW List link is one documented route into the platform, not a
traffic, conversion, or adoption census.

## Source routes and traction limits

Recovered detail bodies contained 77 external-URL occurrences, all unique in
this capture. Nine mission-relevant routes are retained with relationship
labels. Talks Not Raids and Let NYC Dance remain governed by the stronger NYC
Artist Coalition event census; WOW List, River Marvel, Semantic Web,
imagined-community, and Kansas City DIY routes remain attached to selected
public organizer records; Chicago Data Collaborative and Current Museum remain
profile-association-only research leads.

Twenty-three cards overlap the NYC Artist Coalition census. Their stakeholder,
venue, elected-official, agency, and response analysis stays with that
page-level source. A personal-profile card adds no role evidence.

Historical Facebook response labels are retained only for the 21 selected
public pages. They are not verified attendance, unique people, reach,
endorsement, conversion, mandate, or impact, and are not summed.

## Recursive hill climb

The dedicated `evals:personal-wowlist-facebook-events` gate recomputes the
surface counts, unique-parent and recurrence accounting, year distribution,
all five disposition totals, selected public attribution, source-route
relationships, lifecycle closure, website bindings, proof guardrails, and
public-safety exclusions. Its criterion is 100 / 100 with no failed hard check.

The current authenticated replay reached 511 records followed by six no-growth
rounds. Its complete instance-URL set matched the same-day protected 511-row
capture with zero live-only and zero capture-only records. The public fixture
remains hash-locked and contains no private titles, descriptions, contacts,
guest identities, exact residential addresses, or authenticated-session data.

The evaluator rejects:

- missing rows and count-preserving row mutations;
- identifying or private fields added to anonymous held rows;
- profile association rewritten as Jamie's role;
- selected records with Jamie's displayed credit removed;
- response labels rewritten as attendance or reach;
- the empty current WOW List surface rewritten as historical nonexistence;
- research-lead links rewritten as role evidence;
- sole credit for the selected public event set;
- milestone pages rewritten as proof of 300-plus gatherings; and
- mutations that disconnect the projected claims from their page-level citation
  plans or weaken the proof-bank guardrails.

After the dedicated gate passes, `npm run check` reruns the citation, knowledge
lifecycle, public-safety, application, corpus, Chad-lens, and recursive protocol
gates across the complete branch.

## Holdout plan

Future independent review should use two lenses:

- archival data integrity, privacy, provenance, population reconciliation, and
  mutation resistance; and
- skeptical hiring-editor usefulness, collective credit, public clarity, and
  non-inflationary projection.

Neither lens may treat a Facebook response as attendance, a profile card as
Jamie's role, a current empty page as historical nonexistence, or an event-page
organizer display as sole production credit.
