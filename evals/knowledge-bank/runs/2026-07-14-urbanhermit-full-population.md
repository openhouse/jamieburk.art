# Recursive Run: @urbanhermit Full-Population Archival Production

Run date: 2026-07-14

## Objective

Disposition the full 434-slot personal-account population, preserve every
mission-relevant public source lead that can be retained safely, mature bounded
claims, and keep ordinary or sensitive life context out of the public index.

## Hill-Climb Iterations

### Iteration 1: profile traversal

Authenticated traversal recovered 421 unique public statuses and reached the
oldest exposed profile material. This did not meet the criterion because the
profile control was 434 and the Replies carrier failed.

### Iteration 2: bounded date repair

Broad date windows recovered four additional item records. Annual searches
from 2008 through 2024 produced no further unique IDs. The item-level recovery
stabilized at 425.

### Iteration 3: population reconciliation

The criterion was revised from an impossible claim of complete platform export
to complete disposition of the observed population. Nine carrier-limited slots
were retained explicitly: `425 + 9 = 434`. No deletion or inactivity claim was
inferred.

### Iteration 4: personal-account minimization

An early full-detail representation would have over-indexed ordinary life and
third-party context. The durable ledger now exposes exact public records only
for 141 mission-relevant items. It retains 271 context-only and 13 protected-
context items as aggregate-only counts, with no public item-level crosswalk,
identifier, year, metric tuple, digest, link, name, or text fingerprint.

### Iteration 5: authorship and metric ownership

The private working audit separates account-authored records from source-
authored statuses observed as native reposts. The public evidence ledger
contains 81 account-authored and 60 source-authored mission records. Source-
status metrics are `null` in the ledger and excluded from Jamie's aggregate.
Outside-authored recognition is retained only as a three-record floor.

### Iteration 6: source and claim maturation

High-signal records became 15 newly defined sources, one reused canonical
source, 19 atomic observations,
eight held claims, and three open inquiries. Outside evidence corroborates the
Horse Lords video credit and KCUR quotation; self-authored and contextual
sources remain labeled as such. Nothing was added automatically to the public
site.

### Iteration 7: independent safety rejection and unlinkability repair

The first fresh editorial judge rejected the candidate because the 284
withheld items still carried deterministic keys, years, lengths, metric tuples,
and unsalted content digests. Those fields made the supposedly opaque records
linkable. The public ledger now omits all non-evidence item records and retains
only two aggregate disposition buckets. The hard gate adds adversarial checks
against reintroducing an item-level crosswalk or fingerprint.

### Iteration 8: proposition-level media decomposition

The combined media observation asked one record to carry a self-authored UCP
credit, an outside Horse Lords credit, and NPR artifact availability. It is now
three observations so each source establishes one bounded proposition.

### Iteration 9: subtractive-disclosure repair

A second fresh editorial challenge found an exact timestamp for a withheld
item and showed that subtracting published relationship counts from population
relationship counts exposed a singleton withheld reply. Exact recovered-range
timestamps, full-population relationship partitions, and the hidden-source
count were removed. The evaluator now uses exact schema allowlists and rejects
unexpected metadata outside published evidence records.

### Iteration 10: source-specific observation decomposition

Waterways, technical-practice, and outside-recognition observations previously
combined claims from several records under one source ID. They are now split
into source-specific propositions for the river office-hours post, KCUR quote,
Harry J. Epstein website self-report, Let NYC Dance and Talks Not Raids routing,
technical workflow, and Dawnia Darkstone recognition.

### Iteration 11: identity normalization and source binding

Fresh adversarial review found that case variants of two handles inflated the
distinct-account count, three retained sources lacked their own atomic
observation, and one article URL carried stale title and date metadata. Handle
identity is now case-insensitive, Vimeo availability is separated from Jamie's
self-report, the Jamie-authored Horse Lords post and Observer issue article
have bounded observations, and the Observer record matches the page currently
served at its canonical URL.

### Iteration 12: reused-source graph closure

Two fresh judges found that the intake linked the previously canonical Good
Times `Zines 2.0` source but did not give that reused source an @urbanhermit
observation. The graph now separates Jamie's personal-account routing post
from the article's issue context, links both to the maturation inquiry, and
derives coverage from all 16 intake source IDs rather than only the 15 source
records newly defined by this pass.

### Iteration 13: recursive value safety and independent contracts

An adversarial judge showed that exact object keys alone did not prevent nested
personal metadata, false verification values, identifying detail appended to
an unresolved reason, or a simultaneous overclaim in both mirrored semantic
records. The evaluator now validates every method array as strings, pins the
fresh-verification values, requires exact generic unresolved reasons, applies
independent source, claim, and observation contracts, and rejects positive
sole-credit or causation language. Four verified short-link-to-source edges are
also machine-readable, and personal or individual social posts no longer use
institutional metadata.

### Iteration 14: complete metadata and semantic review contracts

Two fresh judges confirmed the Iteration 13 repairs and then found equivalent
ways to move identifying text or an overclaim into fields that were not yet
independently pinned. The evaluator now hashes a normalized projection of the
entire permitted non-record ledger metadata and a separately normalized
projection of every source, observation, claim, projection, and inquiry in the
@urbanhermit graph. Any change to either public surface requires a deliberate
contract update and review. These review digests supplement the arithmetic,
identity, relationship, source-binding, scope, and privacy checks; they are not
treated as evidence or as a substitute for those explanatory validations.

Mutation tests cover nested identifying text in a metric boundary,
contradictory recovery wording, identifying detail moved into the population
definition, and a dual-sided paraphrased observation overclaim.

### Iteration 15: complete ledger and knowledge-subgraph contracts

A fresh judge exhausted 89 non-record string leaves without finding a bypass,
then found that item summaries, coordinated source URL and author changes,
legacy-status dates, unlinked posted URLs, intake rationale, source access time,
observation intake linkage, claim review metadata, and inquiry run time still
sat outside one or both partial projections. The evaluator now binds the entire
public ledger and the entire @urbanhermit knowledge subgraph in addition to its
normalized metadata and semantic projections. The subgraph contract includes
the corpus module and canonical intake, all 16 linked sources, all 19
observations, all eight claims and their projections, and all three inquiries.

This complete-change detection remains paired with proposition-level,
arithmetic, identity, authorship, metric-ownership, scope, link, and privacy
checks. A digest makes an unreviewed change visible; it does not make the
underlying claim true.

### Iteration 16: public-summary and documentation closure

A second fresh judge found three public mirrors outside the complete corpus
contract: the exported population-audit constants, exported corpus findings,
and the personal-account entry in the wider social inventory. The human-
readable intake document was also checked only for selected phrases. A separate
public-surface contract now binds all of those summaries and the full intake
document. Tests mutate every field in both exported summaries and the inventory
entry, alter the documentation, and explicitly cover coordinated changes to a
projection's citation requirement.

## Acceptance Contract

`KB-EVAL-URBANHERMIT-FULL-POPULATION` fails if:

- the 434-slot arithmetic does not reconcile;
- a public evidence item lacks a unique status identifier;
- context-only or protected-context items appear as item-level records or gain
  any identifier, digest, timestamp, length, metric tuple, link, name, or other
  fingerprint;
- raw post text, private paths, contact details, credentials, or session data
  enter the ledger;
- allowed arrays or unresolved reasons gain nested or identifying metadata;
- any permitted non-record metadata changes without a deliberate contract
  update;
- any public evidence record changes without a deliberate contract update;
- fresh-verification values drift from the population control;
- source, claim, and observation semantics violate independent contracts;
- the normalized semantic surface changes without a deliberate contract
  update;
- any intake, source, observation, claim, projection, review field, or inquiry
  in the complete knowledge subgraph changes without a deliberate contract
  update;
- any exported audit summary, corpus finding, personal-account inventory field,
  or intake-document text changes without a deliberate contract update;
- a verified short-link-to-source edge is removed or altered;
- source-status metrics enter Jamie's traction aggregate;
- source, observation, claim, or inquiry counts drift;
- a source loses its scope boundary;
- a held claim gains a public surface; or
- the public registry contains any of the eight held claims.

## Stop Condition

The pass stops only when deterministic knowledge, safety, and application
checks pass and two fresh rubric judges meet every criterion. Fresh-judge
outputs are generated by the existing evaluator workflow and are not treated
as substitutes for the deterministic gates above.
