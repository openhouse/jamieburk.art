# NYC Artist Coalition Facebook events archival-production run

Date: 2026-07-15

## Objective

Review the full event-card population exposed by the authenticated NYC Artist
Coalition Facebook Page, preserve each recovered event as a public-safe civic
plot point, mature defensible claims about the coalition's participation
system, and keep platform, attendance, authorship, and collective-credit
boundaries explicit.

## Population reconciliation

Facebook's Past Events control displayed 34 events. Repeated scrolling reached
a stable terminal state with 33 unique event cards. Earlier authenticated
captures recovered public detail records for all 33. A later authenticated
replay re-found the same 33 event IDs while 28 detail routes remained available
and five returned a temporary unavailable state.

The archive therefore records 100 percent control-slot accounting:

- 33 recovered event records;
- one unresolved, unmaterialized control slot; and
- a later 28 available / 5 temporarily unavailable route state.

This is not a complete native Meta owner export or proof that no historical
event was deleted, withheld, private, absent from the surface, or otherwise
unrecoverable.

## Sources and response signals

Seven published articles appeared as posted source routes in event records:
New York Post, WNYC, Metro, The New Yorker, The Baffler, Curbed, and Gothamist.
Those article identities were linked to bounded source records without treating
their presence on an event page as proof that the publication covered that
specific event.

Thirty-two event pages displayed a historical Facebook response count. The
capture preserves:

- 19 displays at or above 100;
- seven at or above 500; and
- three at or above 1,000.

These are mutable Facebook interface labels. They are not verified attendance,
unique people, reach, participation, endorsement, conversion, mandate, or
impact. A point estimate derived from the displayed strings remains in the
fixture for reproducibility but is deliberately not promoted as a people count.

## Public safety

The public fixture retains event IDs, canonical event URLs, dates, titles,
venues, public organizer displays, response snapshots, topic classifications,
selected mission-relevant public links, and withheld-link categories. Thirteen
outbound link occurrences were withheld by category.

Raw descriptions, comments, attendee identities, emails, phone numbers,
meeting credentials, private working-document links, cookies, authenticated
session state, and protected capture locators remain outside the repository.
