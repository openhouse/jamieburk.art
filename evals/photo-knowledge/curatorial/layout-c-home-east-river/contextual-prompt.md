# Contextual-pass prompt

Review the completed blind result, the public-safe context bundle, and the
attached exact historical mobile and desktop screenshots. Preserve the blind
pass's limits rather than rewriting it as if context had always been known.

Return:

- changed readings;
- preserved blind readings;
- the relation among photograph, literal role copy, next actions, caption,
  credit, typography, and responsive crop;
- one lead proposal;
- one meaningful alternative;
- one dissent or hold;
- one sequence note;
- documentary questions;
- rights or dignity questions;
- a visitor-experience hypothesis;
- what should remain unexplained;
- one source-return request;
- a confidence boundary.

Do not claim visitor research, named-person endorsement, permission, consent,
production approval, or publication authority. The result is an AI-generated
simulated contextual reading.
