# Curatorial brief

Evaluate a lead-image decision for a time-constrained visitor encountering a
professional portfolio. Preserve artistic agency, documentary uncertainty,
rights and dignity boundaries, and the difference between a proposal and a
publication decision.

The blind pass receives only the public-safe candidate pixels and this general
professional-portfolio purpose. It must not receive identity, place, date,
creator, project, career, rights, or narrative context.

The contextual pass receives the completed blind result, the public-safe
context bundle, and the exact historical mobile and desktop screenshots. It
must say what changed, what remained visible, what should remain unexplained,
and which human gates remain open.

Neither pass may claim visitor research, named-person endorsement, permission,
consent, production approval, or authority to select or publish.
