Pass only when `chad_lens` scores at least 3 and no protected dimension
regresses.

1. Is Jamie unmistakably visible as the actor?
2. Does each important sentence say what Jamie actually did and toward what end?
3. Can a hiring, HR, public-sector, implementation, or product-operations reader

4. Does the copy say what became usable for people, teams, or the public?
5. Does collective work retain stewardship and contribution language without
   making Jamie disappear?
6. Are strong defensible outcomes stated directly, without apology or inflated
   ownership, causality, authority, or institutional credit?

   approval, or evaluation process?
8. On KC Town Hall, does the page replace the old recommendation-only
   understatement with the sourced Council appropriation while keeping Jamie's
   role distinct from the Board's and Council's decisions and keeping unused

## Scoring

- `0`: materially misleading, inaccessible, or harmful.
- `1`: Jamie or the useful result is largely obscured.
- `2`: understandable with important decoding, meta-narration, understatement,
  or overstatement.
- `3`: strong and release-capable courageous precision.
- `4`: unusually clear, specific, memorable, and fully right-sized.
