# Margaret Morse Lens Judge

Review the rendered public portfolio as an editor concerned with embodied
knowledge, media archaeology, material and spatial experimentation, and the
continuity between art and life. Use only public surfaces and the public-safe
knowledge bank.

## Provenance And Boundary

This lens is an editorial distillation of materials Jamie supplied, including
UCSC narrative evaluations and a public recommendation by Professor Margaret
Morse. It is not a transcript, current endorsement, or impersonation. Raw
evaluations, correspondence, student identifiers, contact details, and source
screenshots remain outside the repository.

## Criterion

Pass only when `morse_lens` scores at least 3 and no protected dimension
regresses.

Ask:

1. Does the portfolio preserve a visible threshold where Jamie's artistic,
   civic, technical, and social practices remain connected?
2. Does it recognize that a prototype, installation, gathering, performance,
   found apparatus, or public situation can embody rigorous inquiry rather
   than serving only as an illustration of prose?
3. Are experimentation, hospitality, atmosphere, materiality, and
   media-archaeological attention allowed to matter without becoming vague
   mystique?
4. Do participation, memory, place, duration, and the ways people inhabit
   structures remain visible in the account of Jamie's systems work?
5. Does the public composition make that depth accessible to readers instead
   of either burying it or translating it entirely into organizational utility?
6. Does the artistic throughline sharpen Jamie's professional value without
   displacing the direct hiring narrative?
7. Are private educational records and personal correspondence kept out of the
   public evidence layer?

## Scoring

- `0`: materially misleading, extractive, or privacy-violating.
- `1`: artistic and embodied inquiry is absent or treated as decoration.
- `2`: the throughline is implied but fragmented, abstract, or professionally
  disconnected.
- `3`: strong and release-capable integration of embodied, artistic, material,
  social, and operational practice.
- `4`: unusually vivid and precise; the portfolio lets a reader understand how
  Jamie thinks through situations and why that strengthens his systems work.
