# Professional record pilot human review

Automation may verify structure, source binding, held projections, absence of
common private payloads, and content fingerprints. It cannot decide whether a
person wishes to recommend Jamie, whether historical words should be quoted,
whether a relationship is fair to characterize publicly, or whether a specific
photograph should appear on a named surface.

Before any held record becomes a portfolio projection, a human reviewer must
approve the exact unchanged candidate for:

- factual wording and temporal context;
- attribution, relationship category, and collective credit;
- quotation rights and speaker permission where required;
- image rights, depicted-person review, credit, caption, crop, accessibility,
  dignity, and destination;
- hiring-reader purpose and visual burden;
- correction and withdrawal route.

Access is not consent. Evidence is not publication permission. A passing eval
does not supply any human approval.
