# Retrospective intervention-mismatch hill climb

## Objective

Use the minimum necessary meaning from a protected real-world response to
improve the governed evaluation without publishing private wording, identity,
personal circumstances, or unsupported organizational claims.

## Before

The protected response calibration recorded warmth and interest in
reconnecting. It did not explicitly separate the observed retrospective
mismatch from our inference about the historical operating need.

## Failing checks introduced first

- require the calibration to record retrospective intervention mismatch as
  observed and historical operating repair as a supported inference;
- require literal destructive action and personnel action to remain
  not-observed, with present organizational state unknown;
- reject calibration artifacts containing exact private wording or participant
  identity; and
- reject a mutation that promotes figurative language into observed literal
  destructive action.

The focused suite initially failed the new calibration and mutation checks,
confirming that the distinction was not yet enforced.

## Kept change

The calibration, protected source record, and conditional opportunity now
separate observation, supported inference, and explicit non-claims. The public
method remains unchanged; its existing operating-readiness rule already says to
diagnose operating repair, durable memory, or both before proposing a first
engagement.

## Publication boundary

The private message, exact wording, participant and company identities,
personal circumstances, and source locator remain outside repository and
advisory-model artifacts. The update does not claim present need, readership,
proposal evaluation, budget, authorization, hiring, personnel action, or a
literal destructive event.
