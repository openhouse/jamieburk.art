# Knowledge Bank lifecycle run: campaign press corpus

**Date:** 2026-07-13

**Result:** Passed after three recursive rounds

## Scope

The run evaluated all 22 intake records through July 13, including complete
press-section intakes for Let NYC Dance, Talks Not Raids, Save NYC Spaces, and
FairRentNYC. The graph now contains 74 sources, 25 claims, 6 research inquiries,
15 active projections, and 14 held projections.

The campaign corpus contains 46 placements representing 45 distinct articles.
All 45 distinct articles retain a live, moved, or archived public source
location. The campaign-listed Village Voice URL was dead, but a title-level
search recovered the publisher's current canonical page.

## Hill Climb

| Round | Evidence and governance | Editorial and future use | Result |

| 1 | 84 | 86 | Revise |

Round 1 identified four faults:

- placements and distinct articles were being treated as the same denominator;
- the 2021 FairRentNYC page and current reference library were flattened into a
  single simultaneous state;
- a dead Village Voice campaign URL had not yet been searched by title;
- the public language could make press placement sound like publisher
  endorsement or proof of campaign causality.

Round 2 added separate audited denominators, explicit temporal layers, source
preservation states, a recovered Village Voice canonical page, and visible
anti-claims. Round 3 evaluated the unchanged graph. Both judges passed every
hard gate, exceeded the weighted threshold, scored calibration 5, and found no
regression.

## Content Outcome

The Knowledge Bank now retains every campaign press placement as a source
relationship while representing each distinct article once. Four campaign-level
claims remain held for future compositional use. One cross-campaign claim is
active on the FairRentNYC case study because it makes Jamie's direct web and
information-architecture work legible without transferring collective policy
outcomes to him.

The article records are intentionally not treated as fully decomposed evidence.
Each remains a claim-research lead until a future close reading records the
specific propositions it supports and the limits of that support.
