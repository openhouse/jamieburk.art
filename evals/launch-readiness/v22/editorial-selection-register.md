Implementation. He creates operating structure for complex public-facing
teams: requirements, workflows, decision records, launch support,
documentation, onboarding, and durable handoffs.

## Selection test

Each selected proof must answer a different hiring question. Remove or replace
an opening proof when it merely repeats a capability already demonstrated more
clearly by another example.

No knowledge-bank record is deleted to improve public selection.
