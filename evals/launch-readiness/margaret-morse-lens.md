# Margaret Morse Lens

Preserve the threshold where Jamie's artistic, civic, technical, and social
practices remain visibly connected.

## Source Basis

This is an editorial interpretation of historical evidence, not a claim that
Professor Margaret Morse has reviewed or endorsed the current portfolio. Its
public-safe basis is:

- two unofficial UCSC narrative evaluations from 2006, held in Jamie's
  participant archive;
- a 2014 public recommendation screenshot supplied by Jamie; and
- source-backed knowledge-bank records for Jamie's participatory installations,
  media-archaeology practice, river and public-space work, recurring gatherings,
  and later civic and technical systems.

The evaluations describe a student who demonstrated ideas by embodying them in
objects, installations, performances, attention, and participation. They identify
media archaeology, art/life practice, real and recorded time, ambitious execution,
warmth, generosity, and seriousness of purpose. Do not publish raw evaluations,
private correspondence, local archive paths, contact details, student identifiers,
or unapproved images to satisfy this lens.

## Questions

Before shipping public copy, ask:

1. Can a reader see that Jamie's systems practice was shaped by artistic inquiry,
   embodied experimentation, and close attention, rather than by administration
   alone?
2. Does at least one first-pass surface connect that formation to Jamie's current
   professional value without making the reader decode an art-theory argument?
3. Do selected projects show participation, hospitality, atmosphere, place, time,
   or encounter as part of the designed system rather than decorative context?
4. Does the portfolio preserve a meaningful connection among artistic, civic,
   technical, and social work while keeping the current hiring argument clear?
5. Are media archaeology, creative technology, and performance used precisely,
   with inspectable examples or bounded source-backed context?
6. Does the rhetoric avoid converting warmth, generosity, artistic success, or a
   professor's historical praise into unsupported present-day outcomes?

## Score Four

Award 4 only when all of the following are true:

- the homepage or About page gives the artistic-to-operating throughline a clear,
  compact first-pass statement;
- at least two project surfaces show embodied or relational participation as an
  operating method, not merely a project theme;
- one source-backed early or artistic example keeps the throughline specific;
- the reader can understand why this formation improves Jamie's implementation
  practice today; and
- hiring clarity, collective credit, privacy, rights, and factual limits remain
  intact.

Do not award 4 for isolated keywords such as `creative`, `care`, `participation`,
or `artist`. Do not reward romantic opacity, decorative biography, inflated
causality, or the publication of private evidence.
