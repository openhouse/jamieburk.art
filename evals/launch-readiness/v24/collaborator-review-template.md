# Collaborator review template

Status: template only; no approval is implied

## Repository record

Record only:

- project code;
- claim or artifact ID;
- review requested date;
- disposition: `approved`, `corrected`, `declined`, `unreachable`, `held`, or
  `omitted`;
- public-use scope; and
- approval-register reference when applicable.

Do not commit collaborator names, addresses, private replies, contact details,
interpersonal context, or raw correspondence.

## Review message

> I am reviewing how I describe our past work in my resume and portfolio. I
> want the account to be accurate about my contribution and fair to everyone
> involved. Would you be willing to review the short claim below? You can
> approve it, correct it, ask me to omit it, or decline to participate. Silence
> will not be treated as approval. I will not publish your private response or
> quote you without separate permission.

## Questions

1. Is the project context accurate?
2. Is Jamie's contribution described accurately and proportionately?
3. Is collective credit preserved?
4. Is the stated consequence supported by your experience?
5. May this wording or asset be used publicly, and under what conditions?
