# Warren Sack Lens

Make the social structure inside Jamie's systems legible.

## Source Basis

This is an editorial interpretation of historical evidence, not a claim that
Professor Warren Sack has reviewed or endorsed the current portfolio. Its
public-safe basis is:

- unofficial UCSC narrative evaluations from 2004 and 2006, held in Jamie's
  participant archive; and
- source-backed knowledge-bank records for participatory software, physical and
  digital interfaces, WOWList, CallNYC, recurring gatherings, coalition systems,
  and source-backed team memory.

The evaluations document original analysis of physical and online social
networks, recursive group relationships, a structurally informed image-search
prototype, interactive interface design, and a collaboratively designed
installation that translated online photo browsing into movement through physical
space. They also emphasize research, evidence, classroom dialogue, prototyping,
documentation across media, and precise group credit.

Do not publish raw evaluations, local archive paths, student identifiers, private
course records, or unapproved project artifacts to satisfy this lens.

## Questions

Before shipping public copy, ask:

1. Does the portfolio connect an observed social or institutional pattern to a
   concrete mechanism, interface, workflow, or feedback loop?
2. Can a reader see who enters the system, what they can do, what becomes visible
   through participation, and how the structure can change or continue?
3. Are recursive effects made legible where they matter: people creating signals,
   those signals creating new pathways, and those pathways supporting further
   participation?
4. Do prototypes and public systems translate between technical architecture and
   embodied or institutional use rather than presenting technology as an end?
5. Are shared authorship, platform limits, recovered-population limits, and
   institutional context kept distinct from Jamie's own contribution?
6. Does the public argument show why this social-systems intelligence matters to
   implementation, product operations, civic technology, and team memory now?

## Score Four

Award 4 only when all of the following are true:

- a first-pass or role-fit surface names Jamie's social-systems method in plain
  professional language;
- at least two project surfaces connect participation patterns to concrete product,
  interface, workflow, or feedback mechanisms;
- at least one example joins physical, social, and digital structure or shows how
  a system supports recursive participation;
- the mechanism and the human purpose are both understandable without specialist
  decoding; and
- collective authorship, source limits, non-adoption boundaries, and public safety
  remain explicit.

Do not award 4 for generic `community building`, network vocabulary, technology
lists, user counts, or engagement metrics. Do not infer adoption, impact,
leadership, or individual authorship from the existence of a platform or account.
