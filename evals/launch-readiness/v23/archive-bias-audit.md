# Archive-bias audit

Status: prepared for independent semantic review

| Archive condition | Distortion risk | Bounded response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social posts and event records are abundant | Communications volume may look like operational importance or impact. | Keep platform populations as bounded infrastructure evidence; require separate sources for role, use, and consequence. |
| Press is easier to cite than quiet maintenance | Public attention may eclipse recurring coordination, repair, documentation, and handoff labor. | Prefer work products, operating records, and collaborator review when available; keep unsupported memory as a lead. |
| Physical and relational work may live mainly in photographs or memory | Construction, facilitation, hosting, and neighborhood contact may disappear behind text. | Queue rights-reviewed artifacts and collaborator checks; do not publish private photos or infer unrecorded scenes. |
| Failed, deferred, or transitioned work leaves uneven records | The public story may imply uninterrupted success or erase responsible transition. | Preserve bounded status, non-recovery, transition, and anti-claims without publishing private circumstances. |
| Older projects have had more time to accumulate sources | Historical range may overpower recent capability and future fit. | Include current learning and lab work with accurate maturity labels; do not make older projects appear current. |
| Some collaborators and communities produce fewer durable digital traces | Recoverability may reproduce unequal visibility. | Keep collective credit, invite correction, and avoid assigning work by archive volume. |

## Editorial check

Public importance is selected using role relevance, evidence quality,
complementarity, consequence, and reader burden. Source count, post count,
press count, and visible engagement are not ranking scores.

## Open evidence needs

- Rights-reviewed work-product and field-practice artifacts.
- Collaborator review for role division where archival traces remain partial.
- Evidence of maintenance, repair, and handoff beyond launch moments.
- Honest records of deferred or transitioned work where public explanation is
  useful and does not expose private circumstances.
