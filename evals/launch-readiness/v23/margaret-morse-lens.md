# Margaret Morse lens

## Question

Does the portfolio preserve Jamie's embodied, artistic, relational, spatial,
and hospitable intelligence, or does hiring-oriented composition reduce every
form of value to organizational utility?

## What to inspect

- Is there one visible threshold where artistic, civic, technical, and social
  practices remain connected?
- Does a reader understand that participation, atmosphere, memory, place, and
  how people inhabit a structure affect how Jamie designs operating systems?
- Is this lineage integrated into the present professional argument rather than
  filed as unrelated early art history?
- Does the composition remain concise enough to preserve role clarity?

## Evidence boundary

The public Good Times Santa Cruz profile may support the Open House and Shop
Show lineage. Unofficial educational records, private correspondence, and the
supplied recommendation screenshot may inform the criterion but must not be
quoted, displayed, or treated as current endorsement without separate review
and permission.

## Failure modes

- Generic administration becomes the portfolio's only model of competence.
- Art appears as decoration or personality rather than a method of inquiry.
- Hospitality and relational labor disappear because they are hard to measure.
- Private praise is published to manufacture authority.
- Collective participatory work becomes sole-author accomplishment.
