# Civic Match: discovery, connections, and the application route

Reviewed August 30, 2026. This is a **partial discovery review**, not a complete
inventory of the authenticated job board or a declaration that either candidate
meets every qualification. No application, outreach, or account update was made.

## What the source adds

Civic Match is already enrolled in our opportunity-source registry. Its
particular value is a talent network alongside a job board: employers can find
candidate profiles, candidates can receive invitations, and staff can help make
matches. Work for America's [job-seeker overview](https://www.workforamerica.org/job-seekers)
also describes free support and resources. Its
[Civic Match overview](https://www.workforamerica.org/civicmatch) provides the
program context.

That makes Chad's suggestion useful: ask for a relevant connection, not just
another listing. It does **not** establish that Civic Match has an OTI contact
for either opening, that an introduction will happen, or that membership confers
preference. Events and coaching are additional routes when available, not
promised appointments. No upcoming event was verified in this review.

Profile approval unlocks membership features; saving a role expresses interest.
Neither is an employer application, referral, or hiring decision.

## One opportunity, several useful sources

| Source | Use it for | Do not infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NYC Jobs Open Data | Bulk discovery, revision detection, stable Job IDs, preliminary salary/deadline screening | A dataset row proves eligibility or submission |
| Official employer posting / application | Current requirements, pay range, requisition identity, formal submission and confirmation | A maximum salary is the offered salary |
| Civic Match | Profile discovery, matching support, invitations, saved-job organization, connections when available | A saved job, profile approval, or introduction is an application or hiring preference |
| BetaNYC newsletter | Curated leads and context, relevant people and community events | Editorial inclusion is a qualification judgment |

Match employer plus Job ID, or an inspected exact official destination; retain
each source observation and its date. Keep relationship routes without
duplicating applications. The employer's instructions control submission.
Use a legitimate referral route if offered, but do not miss a deadline waiting
for an introduction. Mark applied in Civic Match only after the employer
confirms submission, and only through a human-authorized action.

## Coverage and the next review

The authenticated desktop board currently requires sign-in. This review
inspected the two supplied cards and their plausible official counterparts,
but **did not enumerate Civic Match's full inventory**, inspect those cards'
destinations, or identify every competitive listing. The screenshot's 8:29 is a
clock, not a count of jobs.

After authenticated access is available, record filters, review time,
pagination completion, total listings, and inspected destinations. Review the
full available inventory, screening salary, location, deadlines, and
qualifications first; do not silently narrow the search to OTI or one title.
Retain excluded and uncertain dispositions. Above-threshold means eligible for
further review, not automatically applied or guaranteed competitive.

The initial editorial thresholds are fit ≥85 and competitiveness ≥70, with
Jamie's annual salary floor of $100,000. Scores are heuristics, not probabilities.
Unknown facts require review; known mismatches are excluded. Preserve existing
opportunities and real application states while screening new leads.
