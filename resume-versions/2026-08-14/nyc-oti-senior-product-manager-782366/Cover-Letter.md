# Cover letter — NYC OTI Senior Product Manager 782366

## Letter

Dear Hiring Team,

A resident opens a city website because something in life has already become difficult. The product does not need to impress them. It needs to help them understand what is happening, what they can do next, and whether the institution on the other side is prepared to respond. That is the standard that draws me to Senior Product Manager, Job ID 782366, with New York City OTI’s Public Interest Technology Crew.

I built CallNYC after noticing that constituent-services data could describe government activity without giving a resident a usable route through it. The resulting product translated that administrative record into 61 issue pathways, district context, and concrete next steps. I carried the work from problem framing and information architecture through implementation, public launch, communication, measurement, and stewardship. With Richard Caceres, I also co-built WOWList, a natural-language community calendar whose organizer needs became product requirements, releases, onboarding, support, and production operations across roughly 35 city ecosystems.

My current NYC Artist Coalition / Fair Rent NYC work brings product judgment into a more explicitly institutional setting. I translate stakeholder listening, public testimony, policy research, legal questions, press work, and meetings with elected officials and staff into shared priorities, public-facing systems, action pathways, and more than 30 pages of governed campaign memory. Across these projects, the problem is not only building the interface. It is making research, policy, design, engineering, accessibility, privacy, communication, and decision authority coherent enough that the service can launch—and remain accountable after launch.

I would welcome the opportunity to help a PIT Crew frame a resident problem, choose a responsible version one, ship it with agency partners, learn from its use, and leave stronger public delivery capacity behind. I would be glad to discuss one of these products end to end, including the tradeoffs, measures, and handoffs that shaped it.

Thank you for your consideration,

Jamie Burkart
