## Method

Source-Backed Team Memory is a lab / proof-of-practice exploring how knowledge-heavy teams can preserve useful ideas, decision lineage, open questions, onboarding context, source material, and human-correctable AI workflows without turning private archives into an unsafe browsing surface.

## What It Is

It is an early method and consulting practice for teams that need source-backed operating memory: what happened, who decided what, which source supports a claim, what remains uncertain, and what should stay protected.

The bounded loop is deliberately small: an approved source becomes a structured record; the structured record supports a source-linked draft; the draft separates ideas, decisions, and open questions; a trust and privacy check decides what can become accepted team memory.

## What It Is Not

It is not a finished production SaaS, a replacement for judgment, a legal or medical advice system, an omniscient AI layer, a giant knowledge-management platform, or a private archive browser. The point is not to automate trust. The point is to make context easier to review, correct, and transfer.

AI drafts. Humans review. The shared record remains inspectable and correctable.

## Working Pattern

The pattern is Known / Open / Protected. Known material is public-safe or source-backed. Open material needs review, correction, or more context. Protected material is intentionally withheld because privacy, consent, law, safety, or client trust requires it.

## Role Fit

The work connects technical project management, product operations, documentation architecture, AI-readiness, human review, and source-backed memory. It is visible in V1 as a lab page, not as the lead homepage case study.
