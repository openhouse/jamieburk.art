## Method

Source-Backed Team Memory is a human-reviewed documentation and decision-memory practice for teams that need to stop losing context, decisions, ideas, and onboarding knowledge.

It is a lab / method for helping growing teams preserve decision lineage, meeting context, onboarding knowledge, open questions, and source-backed answers without turning private archives into unsafe surfaces.

## What It Is

It is an early method and consulting practice for teams that need source-backed operating memory: what happened, who decided what, which source supports a claim, what remains uncertain, and what should stay protected.

AI drafts. Humans review. The shared record remains inspectable and correctable.

## What It Is Not

It is not production SaaS. It is not a private archive browser. It is not an AI replacement for judgment. It is not a page for raw notes or transcripts. The point is not to automate trust. The point is to make context easier to review, correct, and transfer.

## Working Pattern

The pattern is Known / Open / Protected. Known material is public-safe or source-backed. Open material needs review, correction, or more context. Protected material is intentionally withheld because privacy, consent, law, safety, or client trust requires it.

## Role Fit

The work connects knowledge management, decision memory, onboarding context, team wiki practices, human-reviewed AI assistance, technical project management, product operations, documentation architecture, and source-backed memory. It is visible in V1 as a bounded lab page, not as the lead homepage case study and not as private company detail.
