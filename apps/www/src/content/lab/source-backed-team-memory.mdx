## Method

Source-Backed Team Memory is a bounded lab method for preserving decision lineage, onboarding context, meeting synthesis, and human-correctable AI workflows without turning private archives into an unsafe browsing surface.

## What It Is

It is an early method and consulting practice for teams that need source-backed operating memory: what happened, which decision was made, which source supports a claim, what remains uncertain, and what should stay protected.

## What It Is Not

It is not a finished production SaaS, a chatbot, a surveillance system, a replacement for judgment, a legal, medical, compliance, or financial advice system, or a private archive browser. The point is not to automate trust. The point is to make context easier to review, correct, and transfer.

## Working Pattern

The pattern is Known / Open / Protected. Known material is public-safe or source-backed. Open material needs review, correction, or more context. Protected material is intentionally withheld because privacy, consent, law, safety, or client trust requires it.

AI can draft. Humans review. The shared record remains inspectable and correctable.

## Small Workflow

Approved source -> Structured record -> Source-linked draft -> Human review -> Accepted team memory

## Possible First Sprint

A first engagement could be a small team-memory / decision-operations sprint. The goal would not be to install a heavy knowledge-management platform. The goal would be to create a repeatable habit: meetings become decisions, decisions become records, and records become onboarding memory.

Possible deliverables:

- knowledge-friction map;
- meeting-memory template;
- decision-record template;
- open-question log;
- source map;
- human-review protocol for AI-assisted drafts;
- onboarding starter page;
- 30-day handoff recommendation.
