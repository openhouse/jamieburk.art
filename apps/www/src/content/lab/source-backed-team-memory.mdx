## Method

Source-Backed Team Memory is a bounded lab method for preserving decision lineage, onboarding context, meeting synthesis, and human-correctable AI workflows without turning private archives into an unsafe browsing surface.

Fast-moving teams lose decisions, rationale, meeting context, and source trails when conversations scatter across calls, chats, documents, and individual memory. Source-Backed Team Memory is a practical method for making meetings cumulative instead of disposable.

## What It Is

It is an early method and consulting practice for teams that need source-backed operating memory: what happened, which decision was made, which source supports a claim, what remains uncertain, and what should stay protected.

## What It Is Not

It is not a finished production SaaS, a chatbot, a surveillance system, a replacement for judgment, a legal, medical, or financial advice system, or a private archive browser. The point is not to automate trust. The point is to make context easier to review, correct, and transfer.

## Working Pattern

The pattern is Known / Open / Protected. Known material is public or source-backed. Open material needs review, correction, or more context. Protected material is intentionally withheld because privacy, consent, law, safety, or client trust requires it.

## What Becomes Usable

- Decision logs
- Meeting-to-memory workflow
- Open-question register
- Source-backed note templates
- Onboarding and context pages
- Human-review checklist
- AI summary review rules

## Pilot Shape

A small pilot can map how knowledge currently moves through a team, identify where decisions and context get lost, and produce a low-overhead decision-memory / onboarding workflow that people can actually use.

## Use Cases

- New-hire onboarding
- Project handoffs
- Leadership updates
- Meeting synthesis
- Decision history
- AI-assisted documentation with human review

The goal is not more documentation for its own sake. The goal is fewer lost decisions, faster onboarding, better handoffs, and more trustworthy team memory.

## Role Fit

The work connects technical project management, product operations, documentation architecture, AI-assisted documentation with human review, and source-backed memory. It is visible in V1 as a lab page, not as the lead homepage case study.
