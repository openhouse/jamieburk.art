## Method

Source-Backed Team Memory is a lab / method for helping knowledge-heavy teams preserve decisions, open questions, source-linked notes, onboarding context, and next steps without turning AI into an unreviewed authority.

## What It Is

It is an early method and consulting practice for teams that need source-backed operating memory: what happened, which decision was made, which source supports a claim, what remains uncertain, and what should stay protected.

## Anti-Claims

- Not a finished SaaS.
- Not a chatbot.
- Not a private archive browser.
- Not surveillance.
- Not a replacement for human judgment.
- Not an autonomous publishing system.

The point is not to automate trust. The point is to make context easier to review, correct, and transfer.

## Working Pattern

The pattern is Known / Open / Protected. Known material is public-safe or source-backed. Open material needs review, correction, or more context. Protected material is intentionally withheld because privacy, consent, law, safety, or client trust requires it.

## Bounded First Sprint

A first sprint can produce:

- source map
- decision log
- open-question register
- meeting-memory template
- onboarding reader
- runbook / handbook outline
- privacy and permissions note
- human-review workflow
- AI-use boundaries

For a small team, this can start as a 2-4 week paid pilot.

## Role Fit

The work connects technical project management, product operations, documentation architecture, AI-readiness, human review, and source-backed memory. It is visible in V1 as a lab page, not as the lead homepage case study.
