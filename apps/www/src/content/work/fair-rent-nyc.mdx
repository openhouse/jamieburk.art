## Context

NYC Artist Coalition, FairRentNYC, and Commercial Rent Stabilization work brings together cultural-space advocacy, storefront stability, public policy, city and state strategy, legal questions, lived experience, and public data. With many collaborators and mixed public/private sources, the operating challenge was to preserve decisions and momentum without exposing strategy, legal-review context, private stakeholder information, or unapproved quotes.

Here, documentation is infrastructure: the shared place where meetings, sources, open questions, decisions, and next steps remain usable over time.
