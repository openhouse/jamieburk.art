## Context

NYC Artist Coalition / FairRentNYC / Commercial Rent Stabilization work sits inside a civic and coalition environment where public policy, cultural-space advocacy, storefront stability, arts communities, city and state strategy, legal questions, lived experience, and public data all meet.

Here, documentation is operating infrastructure. Public materials, private notes, legal and policy questions, meeting follow-ups, and city/state strategy had to become easier to remember and continue without exposing protected coalition context.

## What I did

Jamie built and stewarded 30+ pages of campaign memory: running minutes, decision records, action trackers, source maps, a legislative provenance redline, public-data framing, stakeholder follow-up, and public-safe explanations. The shared layer separated known public material from open questions and protected context so collaborators could orient after meetings and continue the work.

## What became usable

Collaborators could find source context, track next steps, identify unresolved legal or policy questions, and prepare public explanations without confusing publishable material with private coordination.

## Transferable proof

This is technical project management and product-operations proof for civic work: source-backed memory, decision lineage, stakeholder coordination, human review, and public/private boundaries that let a coalition move without relying on memory alone.
