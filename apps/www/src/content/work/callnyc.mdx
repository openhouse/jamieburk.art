## What was unclear

CouncilStat data contained civic signal, but a public dataset was not yet a resident pathway. A person still needed to understand what an issue meant, which office or district context might matter, what next step was reasonable, and where the source data could mislead.

The project also needed a firm boundary. CallNYC could demonstrate a translation pattern without pretending to be official guidance, a live service, or a complete measure of district-office performance.

## What I did

Jamie recognized that the released administrative data still needed a resident-facing layer. He built the independent prototype, organized records into issue-oriented pathways, and designed pages for public discovery and sharing. After conversations with prospective users, he expanded the available contact options.<CitationRef set={callNYCCitationSet} refId="callnyc-work-project" />

He also made source limits operational. CallNYC filtered records without borough data as one method of excluding apparent spam or out-of-city entries, while keeping clear that case totals could not be read as simple rankings of office quality, effectiveness, specialization, or resident need.<CitationRef set={callNYCCitationSet} refId="callnyc-work-data" />

## What became usable

The prototype turned administrative records into issue pages, district context, and possible next steps. It made the translation layer visible: residents did not need to understand the entire dataset before entering through a recognizable concern.

For product and operations teams, the transferable pattern is direct: map a difficult source to the decisions a person needs to make, build a bounded pathway, and keep the source limitations inside the product rather than in a separate disclaimer no one sees.

## Use and iteration

Contemporaneous reporting documents a small, fast feedback loop rather than broad adoption. Jamie optimized pages for search and sharing, then added more contact options after conversations with prospective users. The surviving repository keeps the implementation inspectable.<CitationRef set={callNYCCitationSet} refId="callnyc-use-iteration" />
