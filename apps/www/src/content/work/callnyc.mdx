## What was unclear

The data carried civic signal, but a public dataset was not yet a resident pathway. A person still needed to understand what an issue meant, which office or district context might matter, what next step was reasonable, and where the source data could mislead.

## What I did

Jamie built the independent prototype, organized records into issue-oriented pathways, and translated administrative categories into public-facing guidance patterns. The interface let a person enter through a recognizable concern instead of first understanding the complete dataset.

## What became usable

The prototype organized constituent-services records into issue pages, district context, and possible next steps. It treated data translation as a product and information-architecture problem: residents could enter through a recognizable concern instead of interpreting the whole dataset first.
