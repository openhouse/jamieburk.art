## What was unclear

Redevelopment work carries many layers: active construction, property history, preservation sequencing, resident knowledge, funding processes, public value, stakeholder needs, and private legal or financial context that should not become public portfolio content.

## What became usable

The project produced both a stabilized cold shell and a more usable neighborhood process: coordinated restoration work, a survey-to-proposal input loop, public-benefit documentation, municipal-review materials, and recurring neighborhood service operations.
