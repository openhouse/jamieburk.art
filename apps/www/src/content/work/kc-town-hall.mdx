## What was unclear

Redevelopment work carries many layers: property history, funding processes, public value, stakeholder needs, preservation constraints, and private legal or financial context that should not become public portfolio content.

## What became usable

Jamie helped shape redevelopment planning, public-benefit documentation, stakeholder context, and municipal-review support so collaborators could describe the proposal, explain its intended civic value, and carry it into public funding conversations.

## What I did

The work included planning support, documentation, public-benefit framing, stakeholder context, and materials that made a complex built-environment proposal easier to review and discuss. The public record supports a proposal and funding recommendation, not completed construction or final funds received.
