## What was unclear

Redevelopment work carries many layers: property history, funding processes, public value, stakeholder needs, preservation constraints, and private legal or financial context that should not become public portfolio content.

## What I did

I translated property, funding, preservation, and stakeholder context into materials for review and public discussion. The official record supports the proposal, the Council's acceptance of the Board recommendation, and the project appropriation. It does not establish an executed funding agreement, disbursement, or completed construction.
