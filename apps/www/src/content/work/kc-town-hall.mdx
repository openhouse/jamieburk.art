## Context

KC Town Hall LLC involved adaptive reuse planning for a long-vacant historic building. The work sat at the intersection of built environment, historic preservation, public benefit, funding context, stakeholder documentation, and long-horizon project management.

## What was unclear

Redevelopment work carries many layers: property history, funding processes, public value, stakeholder needs, preservation constraints, and private legal or financial context that should not become public portfolio content.

## What became usable

Jamie established a public project identity and participation surface, then helped shape redevelopment planning, public-benefit documentation, stakeholder context, and municipal-review support. Collaborators could carry that public system forward across resident input, neighborhood operations, civic information, and public exchange.

## What I did

The work included public identity and participation infrastructure, planning support, documentation, public-benefit framing, stakeholder context, and materials that helped make a complex built-environment effort easier to discuss and continue.
