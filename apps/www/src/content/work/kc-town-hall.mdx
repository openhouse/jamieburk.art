## Context

KC Town Hall LLC involved physical restoration and adaptive reuse of a long-vacant historic building. The work sat at the intersection of construction delivery, historic preservation, neighborhood listening, public benefit, funding context, stakeholder documentation, and long-horizon project management.

## What was unclear

Redevelopment work carries many layers: property history, funding processes, public value, stakeholder needs, preservation constraints, and private legal or financial context that should not become public portfolio content.

## What became usable

Jamie and Julia translated the restoration into a phased project, coordinated a multi-disciplinary team, paired physical delivery with neighborhood listening, and built the public-benefit documentation needed for funding and stakeholder conversations.
