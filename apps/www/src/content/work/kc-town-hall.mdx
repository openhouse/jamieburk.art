## Context

Jamie Burkart and Julia Fredenburg co-founded KC Town Hall LLC around the neighborhood-led adaptive reuse of a long-vacant historic building. The work joined construction delivery, historic preservation, neighborhood participation, public benefit, funding context, and long-horizon project management.

## What was unclear

The building needed immediate structural care and coordinated specialist trades, but the future place could not be designed from construction requirements alone. Preservation constraints, daily field decisions, neighborhood histories, desired uses, funding processes, and private legal or financial context all had to remain legible without being collapsed together.

## Transferable proof

This is field-based implementation proof: coordinating specialist teams and schedule-sensitive physical work while building a listening and documentation system that could carry neighborhood knowledge into decisions. It also shows the discipline to separate a completed delivery phase from a later funding and transition lifecycle.
