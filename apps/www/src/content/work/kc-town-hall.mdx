## Context

KC Town Hall LLC involved adaptive reuse planning for a long-vacant historic building. The work sat at the intersection of built environment, historic preservation, public benefit, stakeholder documentation, and long-horizon project management.

## What was unclear

Redevelopment work carries many layers: property history, public value, stakeholder needs, preservation constraints, and private legal or financial context that should not become public portfolio content.

## What became usable

Jamie supported redevelopment planning and public-benefit documentation so collaborators could describe the project, understand its civic value, and support stakeholder conversations.

## What I did

The work included planning support, documentation, public-benefit framing, stakeholder context, and materials that helped make a complex built-environment effort easier to discuss and continue.

## Transferable proof

This is long-horizon implementation proof: ambiguous goals, many stakeholders, public/private boundaries, and documentation that has to be useful beyond one meeting.
