## Context

KC Town Hall LLC involved physical restoration and adaptive reuse of a long-vacant historic building in Kansas City. The work sat at the intersection of construction delivery, historic preservation, neighborhood listening, public benefit, funding context, stakeholder documentation, and long-horizon project management.

## What was unclear

Redevelopment work carries many layers: property history, funding processes, public value, stakeholder needs, preservation constraints, and private legal or financial context that should not become public portfolio content.

## What became usable

The 2019 proposal names Jamie and Julia as founders and project managers, records a completed Phase One cold shell, identifies the project's professional and trade contributors, and documents a partner-based neighborhood survey that shaped its plans.

## What I did

The confirmed record names Jamie and Julia as co-founders and project managers during Phase One delivery. It also preserves the distinct work of professional, trade, neighborhood, and civic partners. Jamie's recalled general-contractor and wider neighborhood-program roles remain held for corroboration rather than presented as settled public accomplishments.
