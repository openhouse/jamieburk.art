## Context

KC Spaces Fund was a collaborator-led 2020 mutual-aid campaign supporting
grassroots arts and culture spaces during COVID-19. The public system needed to
make several actions immediately understandable while keeping one recognizable
identity across the website, application, fundraiser, and social channels.

## What was unclear

Urgency did not remove the need for structure. People needed distinct routes to
donate, join the coalition, apply for support, contact the campaign, and follow
funded-space updates. The campaign team also needed a surface it could maintain
and reuse without turning every public action into a separate technical build.

The implementation used a customized Ghost theme, reusable campaign patterns,
and a fundraising-display component. The visible result is a complete public
surface whose hierarchy moves from campaign purpose to concrete action.

## What became usable

The site connected campaign identity to working public pathways for donation,
coalition sign-up, grant applications, contact, and updates. The reusable theme
and deployment pattern also made this a technical-operations case: design,
content structure, implementation, and ongoing public delivery had to work as
one system.
