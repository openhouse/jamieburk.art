The public campaign page described emergency relief grants for DIY, artist-run, cooperative, and grassroots arts and culture spaces, with Allied Media Projects as fiscal sponsor.

The public GoFundMe page reports $9,590 raised from 107 donations. That fact belongs to the campaign as a whole; it is not used here as a claim that Jamie raised the funds.

Jamie supported the campaign by building and maintaining the web infrastructure around it: a Ghost-based public site, campaign theme work, fundraising display support, and donation/application/sign-up/contact affordances.

That made the campaign easier to understand and act on online while keeping public organizer credit with the people publicly named by the campaign.
