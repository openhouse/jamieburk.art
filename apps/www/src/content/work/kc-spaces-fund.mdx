## Context

KC Spaces Fund was a 2020 Kansas City mutual-aid campaign for grassroots arts and culture spaces during COVID-19 closures and cancellations.

The public GoFundMe page describes a fundraiser organized by Kendell Harbin for Allied Media Projects Inc., with emergency relief grants up to $500 for grassroots arts and culture spaces. The public page shows $9,590 raised of a $9,500 goal from 107 donations.

## What was unclear

The campaign needed a public web surface quickly: a place to explain the fund, route people to donate, route spaces toward application information, collect sign-ups or contact interest, and make the effort legible enough to share.

At the same time, the work had real privacy and credit boundaries. Applicant, grantee, donor, subscriber, payment, credential, billing, private email, private message, and private project records should stay offline. Public organizer credit should remain with the organizers named by the campaign unless those people confirm a broader framing.

## What became usable

The public-safe evidence supports a behind-the-scenes digital infrastructure role: a Ghost-based campaign site, reusable campaign theme work, a GoFundMe widget layer, and web affordances for donation, application, sign-up, contact, and sharing flows.

The technical work made the campaign's digital surface function. It helped turn emergency organizing context into a public-facing system that people could understand and act on.
