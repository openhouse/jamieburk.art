The challenge was how to keep participation open, generous, and repeatable without exposing private community records or turning a living social practice into an archive that violated consent.

## What became usable

I created repeatable hosting, onboarding, facilitation, documentation, and continuity systems across 300+ gatherings and 20+ resident artists.

## What I did

I handled invitations, hosting rhythms, artist support, handoffs, space-making, documentation practices, and the quiet logistics that let recurring community work keep happening.
