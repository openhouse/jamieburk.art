## What I did

The work included invitations, hosting rhythms, artist support, access handoffs, space-making, documentation practices, and the quiet logistics that let recurring community work keep happening.

## Current operating loop

- **Observe:** Artist needs and the practical moments of invitation, arrival, access, hosting, participation, and departure reveal what support is needed.
- **Model:** The work treats those moments as a connected participation sequence rather than isolated acts of hospitality.
- **Interface:** Invitations, hosting rhythms, access handoffs, onboarding materials, and the blank specimen above give the sequence a usable form.
- **Use and notice:** Recurring dinners and residencies surface questions about clarity, trust, access, timing, and what future hosts or guests need to know.
- **Revise and hand off:** Instructions and routines can change with experience, while public derivatives support continuity without turning protected participant records into portfolio proof.

## Transferable proof

The operating evidence remains deliberately aggregate. Guest lists, contact details, invitation and attendance histories, private correspondence, access details, and unapproved images stay outside the public site. The role fit is clear: onboarding, facilitation, trust-building systems, continuity planning, stakeholder care, and operations for complex human environments.
