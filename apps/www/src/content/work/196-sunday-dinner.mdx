## Context

196 Artists Residency / Sunday Dinner was cultural infrastructure: recurring gatherings, artist support, hosting, onboarding, facilitation, and continuity work that depended on trust as much as logistics.

## What was unclear

The challenge was how to keep participation open, generous, and repeatable without exposing private community records or turning a living social practice into an archive that violated consent.

## What I did

The work included invitations, hosting rhythms, artist support, handoffs, space-making, documentation practices, and the quiet logistics that let recurring community work keep happening.

Hosting was part of the system, not support around it. Invitations, repetition, introductions, and handoffs made participation legible enough for people to enter and for the gathering to continue. The records support recurring structure, not individual attendance or participant outcomes.
