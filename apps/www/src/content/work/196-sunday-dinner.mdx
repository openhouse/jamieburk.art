## Context

196 Artists Residency / Sunday Dinner was cultural infrastructure: recurring gatherings, artist support, hosting, onboarding, facilitation, and continuity work that depended on trust as much as logistics.

## What was unclear

The challenge was how to keep participation open, generous, and repeatable without exposing private community records or turning a living social practice into an archive that violated consent.

## What became usable

Jamie built and stewarded repeatable hosting, onboarding, facilitation, documentation, and continuity systems within a participant-made cultural practice spanning 300+ gatherings and 20+ resident artists.

Jamie's work included invitations, hosting rhythms, artist support, proposal review, orientation, access planning, dedicated collaboration workspaces, documentation practices, and the quiet logistics that supported recurring community work.
