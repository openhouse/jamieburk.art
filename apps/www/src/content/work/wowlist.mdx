## Context

WOWList.org was a co-built community-calendar platform organized around followable keyword communities. It used Python / Django and Ember.js to support event publishing, distribution, and discovery across DIY arts, music, and local-interest scenes.

## What was unclear

Organizers needed lightweight ways to share events without depending on one editorial gatekeeper or a single static calendar. Communities were often organized by interest, place, venue, scene, and informal networks rather than clean institutional categories.

## What became usable

The platform turned keywords into followable community surfaces. That gave organizers a way to publish and distribute events around the language their communities already used, with product patterns for event entry, community tagging, email digests, and embeddable calendars.

## What I did

Jamie co-built and supported the product model, event workflows, and public-facing web system. The work connected community organizing patterns to a maintainable technical platform: Django-backed data structures, Ember interface work, organizer publishing flows, distribution patterns, and low-overhead operations.

## Transferable proof

This is product-operations and public-web proof: community vocabulary, user workflows, platform logic, and distribution needs had to become a system that real organizers could use.
