## Context

WOWList.org was a co-built community-calendar platform organized around followable keyword communities. It used Django, PostgreSQL / PostGIS, and Ember.js to support event distribution and discovery across DIY arts, music, and local-interest scenes.

## What was unclear

Organizers needed lightweight ways to share events without depending on one editorial gatekeeper or a single static calendar. Communities were often organized by interest, place, venue, scene, and informal networks rather than clean institutional categories.

## What became usable

The platform turned keywords into followable community surfaces. That gave organizers a way to publish and distribute events around the language their communities already used.

The public-safe scale claim is careful: aggregate historical records support 1,800+ users, 16,000+ posts/events, and adoption across roughly 35 city ecosystems. The site does not publish private user rows, organizer contact lists, geolocation records, or raw community data.
