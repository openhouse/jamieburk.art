## Context

WOWList.org was a co-built community-calendar platform organized around followable keyword communities. It used Django, PostgreSQL / PostGIS, and Ember.js to support event distribution and discovery across DIY arts, music, and local-interest scenes.

## What was unclear

Organizers needed lightweight ways to share events without depending on one editorial gatekeeper or a single static calendar. Communities were often organized by interest, place, venue, scene, and informal networks rather than clean institutional categories.

## What became usable

The platform turned keywords into followable community surfaces. That gave organizers a way to publish and distribute events around the language their communities already used, with approved aggregate records supporting 35+ active city scenes.

## What I did

Jamie co-built and supported the product model, event workflows, data model, and public-facing web system. The work connected community organizing patterns to a maintainable technical platform.

## Outcome / evidence

Approved aggregate records support 1,846 users, 16,142 posts/events, 23,864 lists/tags, 28,837 list follows, 20,927 saved/starred events, and 35+ active city scenes by July 2017.

"35+ active city scenes" means city or region entries with at least 50 geocoded posts/events. The wording should not be stretched into official chapters or broader city adoption.

## Transferable proof

This is product-operations and public-web proof: community vocabulary, user workflows, platform logic, and distribution needs had to become a system that real organizers could use.
