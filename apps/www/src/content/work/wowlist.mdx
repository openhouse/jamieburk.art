## Context

WOW List began with a practical organizing question: how could people share events without waiting for one editor to decide what counted? With collaborators, I co-built a system where keywords could become followable community calendars, using Django / PostGIS and Ember to carry an informal practice into a public product.

## What was unclear

A conventional citywide calendar would have flattened scenes into fixed categories and created another editorial bottleneck. We needed a model that could preserve organizers' language while making events discoverable across overlapping interests, places, and venues.

## What became usable

Keywords became the organizing layer. One event could appear in several followable communities at once, giving organizers distribution without requiring them to submit to a central editor.

Historical product records preserve the scale: 1,800+ users and 16,000+ posts/events across roughly 35 city ecosystems.

## What I did

I helped define the keyword-community model and turn it into publishing, list-selection, digest, and distribution flows. With collaborators, I supported the Django / PostGIS and Ember system, translated organizer feedback into product decisions, and kept the public-facing service usable.

## Transferable proof

This is the kind of implementation work I want to keep doing: listening for the language a community already uses, making the operating model explicit, and carrying it through data, interface, support, and handoff.
