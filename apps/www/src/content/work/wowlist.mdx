## Context

Jamie co-built WOWList.org so DIY arts and music organizers could publish and distribute events through followable keyword communities instead of relying on one editorial gatekeeper. The Django, PostgreSQL / PostGIS, and Ember.js platform turned the language communities already used into repeatable publishing and discovery workflows.

## What was unclear

Organizers needed lightweight ways to share events without depending on one editorial gatekeeper or a single static calendar. Communities were often organized by interest, place, venue, scene, and informal networks rather than clean institutional categories.

## What became usable

The platform turned keywords into followable community surfaces. That gave organizers a way to publish and distribute events around the language their communities already used.

The participation loop was concrete: organizers described events in their own language; those terms became followable community feeds; publishing and sharing created additional discovery routes without waiting for a central editor. The archive establishes the mechanism and observed account activity, not adoption or cultural impact.
