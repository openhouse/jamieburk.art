## Context

WOWList.org was a co-built community-calendar platform organized around followable keyword communities. It used Django, PostgreSQL / PostGIS, and Ember.js to support low-cost event publishing and distribution for local calendar organizers.

## What was unclear

Organizers needed lightweight event publishing and distribution through the language their communities already used.

## What became usable

The platform turned keywords into followable community surfaces. That gave organizers a way to publish and distribute events around the language their communities already used.

The public-safe scale claim is careful: aggregate historical records support 1,800+ users and 16,000+ posts/events. A July 2017 snapshot contains 35 city/region entries with at least 50 geocoded posts/events each. Those entries are not proof of cities reached, chapters, unique users, or organizer adoption. The site does not publish private user rows, organizer contact lists, geolocation records, or raw community data.

## What I did

Jamie co-built and supported the product model, event workflows, and public-facing web system. The work connected community organizing patterns to a maintainable technical platform.
