## One-line summary

196 Artists Residency and Sunday Dinner show Jamie's ability to build community systems that feel human while still having repeatable operating structure.

## Context

The work centered on rooms, artists, shared meals, residency support, cultural memory, and the practical details that let people feel welcomed and oriented.

## What was unclear

Gatherings can disappear into memory unless they have enough structure to repeat, document, and transfer. The challenge was to support continuity without over-formalizing the room.

## What became usable

The work supported 300+ hosted gatherings and 20+ resident artists, with a public-safe frame for describing the operational and cultural system behind it.

## Transferable proof

This is proof for community operations, stakeholder care, facilitation, handoff, and documentation under consent boundaries.
