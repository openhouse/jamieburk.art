## One-line summary

Jamie helped turn civic campaign knowledge around commercial rent stabilization into a more inspectable, source-backed, public-safe memory system.

## Context

The work sat inside a civic and coalition context where public information, advocacy history, policy concepts, and practical organizing materials needed to remain usable without exposing private records.

## What was unclear

Important knowledge lived across public sources, conversations, drafts, decisions, and collective memory. The challenge was to preserve enough structure for people to understand and continue the work without publishing sensitive materials.

## What became usable

The work produced a clearer source layer, public-safe summaries, campaign-memory infrastructure, and documentation patterns that made the work easier to explain, review, and hand off.

## What I did

Jamie helped clarify information architecture, organize source-backed records, structure public-safe summaries, document decisions, and create a more durable memory layer for civic work.

## How people used it

Collaborators could use the structure to locate sources, understand what had been decided, explain the work to others, and separate public materials from protected internal context.

## Outcome / evidence

The public-safe evidence includes 30+ pages of civic campaign-memory infrastructure and a repeatable pattern for source-backed public-interest documentation.

## Transferable proof

This work demonstrates implementation discipline in civic contexts: source hygiene, stakeholder awareness, public-facing communication, and durable handoff under privacy constraints.
