## One-line summary

Source-Backed Team Memory is a lab practice for turning approved source materials into inspectable, human-reviewed shared records.

## Context

Knowledge-heavy collaborations often lose the thread across meetings, documents, decisions, and open questions. AI can draft and organize, but the shared record still needs human review and source visibility.

## What was unclear

Teams needed a way to keep memory useful without treating AI output as authority or mixing public and internal boundaries.

## What became usable

The lab frames a first useful loop: approved source, structured record, source-linked draft, ideas, decisions, open questions, trust check, human review, accepted memory.

## Transferable proof

This work is a bridge between product operations, knowledge systems, documentation infrastructure, and responsible AI-assisted workflows.

## Current status

Early local-first research prototype and lab practice.
