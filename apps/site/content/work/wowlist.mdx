## One-line summary

WOWList.org made scattered cultural ecosystem information easier to discover through a public-facing list structure.

## Context

The project sat between city culture, public web publishing, community discovery, and repeatable information maintenance.

## What was unclear

Useful cultural information existed in many places, but it was hard to scan across local ecosystems.

## What became usable

The public-facing structure reached 35 city ecosystems and created a clearer way to browse, connect, and maintain cultural information.

## Transferable proof

This is proof of practical information architecture, public-facing web systems, and community-aware operations.
