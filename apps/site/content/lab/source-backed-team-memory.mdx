## What this is

A research and prototype practice for source-backed organizational memory.

## What it is not

Not production SaaS. Not an AI oracle. Not broad ingestion of private systems. Not a replacement for human judgment.

## The first useful loop

Approved source -> structured record -> source-linked draft -> ideas / decisions / open questions -> trust + privacy check -> human review -> accepted memory.

## Current proof of practice

Fair Rent NYC / Commercial Rent Stabilization institutional-memory work, described only in public-safe terms.

## What this could help with

Decision lineage, onboarding context, meeting memory, source-linked notes, public / internal boundaries, and human-correctable AI workflows.

## Status

Early local-first research prototype / lab practice.
