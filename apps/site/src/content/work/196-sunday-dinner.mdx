## One-line summary

Repeatable hosting, onboarding, facilitation, documentation, and continuity systems across long-running cultural and community practice.

## Context

The work translated hospitality, participation, and cultural care into systems that people could enter, understand, and continue. The public site should show the operating pattern without exposing private stories or permission-dependent details.

## What was unclear

- How to make recurring gatherings and residency participation legible to new people.
- How to maintain continuity without relying only on memory or personal explanation.
- Which details could be shared publicly and which needed consent.

## What I did

- Created repeatable hosting, onboarding, facilitation, and continuity practices.
- Supported 300+ gatherings and 20+ resident artists.
- Built public-safe framing for the systems work beneath the cultural practice.
- Protected private guest, resident, and attendance details.

## What became usable

- A clearer participation infrastructure for gatherings and residency activity.
- Public-safe proof of onboarding, facilitation, care-forward operations, and continuity systems.
- A deeper-room case study that supports the professional frame without overtaking it.

## Known / Open / Protected

Known: long-running gatherings, residency frame, participation systems, public-safe summary.

Open: approved photos, public links, and specific artifacts Jamie wants to show.

Protected: guest records, attendance records, private resident details, sensitive photos, and personal stories without explicit permission.
