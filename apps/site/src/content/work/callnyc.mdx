## One-line summary
Built a civic-data prototype translating constituent-services open data into resident-facing find help / next steps guidance.

## Context
The project followed a New York City Council civic-data hackathon and explored how public constituent-services data could become clearer resident-facing guidance.

## What was unclear?
Constituent-services data existed, but residents needed a clearer public-facing pathway from issue to relevant civic office or next step.

## What I structured
- Issue pathways.
- District context.
- Resident-facing guidance.
- Public issue pages.
- Open-data translation from civic data toward practical next steps.

## What became usable
An archived civic-tech prototype organized open data into issue pathways, district context, and resident-facing guidance.

## How people used it
The prototype demonstrated how residents could move from a civic issue to a clearer sense of where to ask for help or what next step might apply.

## Outcomes / evidence
- Civic-data prototype built after a New York City Council civic-data hackathon.
- CouncilStat / constituent-services open-data translation.
- Public issue pages and resident-facing guidance.
- Historical civic-tech demo, with press or archive links to be added when verified.

## What this proves
- Civic technology implementation.
- Open-data translation.
- Public-facing product judgment.
- Accessible guidance.
- Prototype delivery.
