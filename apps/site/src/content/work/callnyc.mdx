## What changed

CallNYC.org framed civic contact as a product and information problem. People needed a way to move from a real-life issue to a useful next action without decoding the city from scratch.

The work focused on making public information easier to navigate, not on pretending a prototype was an official city service.

## How I worked

- Clarified resident-facing use cases.
- Connected public information to plain-language guidance.
- Kept prototype boundaries visible.
- Shaped the site around action, source context, and trust.
- Preserved future handoff needs for maintenance and updates.

## Public-safe outcome

The prototype became a clearer civic information surface: practical enough to understand, careful enough not to overclaim, and structured enough for future iteration.
