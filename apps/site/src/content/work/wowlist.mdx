## One-line summary

A community-calendar platform for decentralized DIY arts and music organizers.

## Context

WOWList helped organizers publish, follow, syndicate, and discover events across keyword communities and city ecosystems. The system needed to stay useful to small organizers without becoming expensive or overbuilt.

## What was unclear

- How decentralized organizers could publish and follow events across communities.
- How natural-language event entry, digests, and embeddable calendars could support adoption.
- How to keep infrastructure low-cost and maintainable.

## What I did

- Co-built product and community systems for a Python / Django plus Ember.js platform.
- Supported keyword communities, natural-language event entry, weekly digest emails, and embeddable calendars.
- Helped steward adoption across roughly 35 city ecosystems.
- Supported low-cost Dokku / DigitalOcean deployment practices.

## What became usable

- Organizer-facing tools for publishing and sharing events.
- Community calendars that could be embedded and syndicated.
- Proof of product, platform, and community-system work across multiple local ecosystems.

## Known / Open / Protected

Known: community-calendar platform, archived frame, organizer adoption, city ecosystem reach.

Open: approved screenshots, public archive links, and exact city list.

Protected: private organizer data, unpublished community records, credentials, and unapproved quotes.
