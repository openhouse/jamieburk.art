## One-line summary

Long-running e-commerce and operations modernization work for an 80+ year-old industrial tool business.

## Context

The work sat between a legacy business with a distinct institutional voice and a changing digital environment. The public-facing website, product content, marketing rhythms, analytics, and operational workflows all needed to support the business without flattening what made it trusted.

## What was unclear

- How to preserve the business voice while improving digital workflows.
- Which content, product, and analytics practices would help the team act.
- How to keep ongoing web operations maintainable across years of change.

## What I did

- Supported web, e-commerce, marketing, analytics, and operational workflow improvements.
- Helped translate day-to-day business needs into clearer technical and content systems.
- Maintained public-facing web systems and supported implementation details over time.
- Kept public claims careful and avoided publishing private internal materials.

## What became usable

- A more durable public web and e-commerce operating layer.
- Clearer content and workflow practices for ongoing site maintenance.
- Public-safe proof of long-running client trust and digital operations experience.

## Outcomes / impact

- Contributed to 2x revenue growth through a combination of e-commerce, marketing, analytics, content, and operational improvements.
- Supported a legacy business in adapting to digital workflows while preserving institutional voice.
- Built evidence of practical, long-term implementation support rather than one-time launch work.

## What this proves

Technical project management, product operations, e-commerce implementation, analytics literacy, documentation, stakeholder coordination, and durable handoff work.
