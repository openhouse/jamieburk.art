## What changed

This work treated coalition documentation as civic infrastructure. The goal was to keep public facts, campaign context, and next actions usable while protecting the relationships and sensitive materials behind the work.

The useful system was not a giant archive. It was a boundary-aware memory layer: what is known, what is still open, and what must stay protected.

## How I worked

- Organized public-facing claims and source context.
- Separated public summaries from private organizing materials.
- Helped frame civic information for different stakeholders.
- Preserved decision trails so future collaborators could understand why choices were made.
- Used careful labels for unapproved or sensitive details.

## Public-safe outcome

The work became easier to explain, hand off, and continue without flattening uncertainty or exposing private civic materials.
