## One-line summary
A source-backed team-memory practice for turning meetings, documents, transcripts, and decisions into human-reviewed organizational memory.

## What was unclear?
Teams can accumulate meetings, transcripts, documents, and decisions faster than they can turn them into trusted shared memory.

## What I structured
- Approved source.
- Structured record.
- Source-linked draft.
- Ideas, decisions, and open questions.
- Trust and privacy check.
- Accepted team memory.

## What became usable
The method creates a reviewable path from source material to shared memory while keeping human judgment, correction, privacy, and consent inside the workflow.

## What this proves
- Documentation architecture.
- AI readiness without overclaiming.
- Source-backed memory.
- Human-reviewed systems.
- Public-safe implementation judgment.
