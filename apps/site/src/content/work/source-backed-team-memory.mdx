## One-line summary

A source-backed team-memory practice for turning meetings, documents, transcripts, and decisions into human-reviewed organizational memory.

## Context

Teams lose context when decisions, meetings, source documents, and open questions scatter across tools. This lab method explores one bounded source-to-memory loop that remains inspectable, correctable, and human-reviewed.

## What was unclear

- How to reduce context loss without pretending AI can become the source of truth.
- How to keep claims tied to source material.
- How to make access, privacy, retention, and correction boundaries visible.

## What I did

- Framed a source-backed documentation practice for meetings, documents, transcripts, and decisions.
- Designed the method around human review, correction, and inspectable claims.
- Kept the work positioned as a lab / research method rather than a production SaaS claim.

## What became usable

- A practical language for onboarding, decision lineage, open questions, and knowledge-friction mapping.
- A safer AI-readiness frame: AI drafts. Humans review. The shared record remains inspectable and correctable.
- A bounded way to discuss source-backed team memory without exposing private collaborator details.

## Known / Open / Protected

Known: lab method, source-backed memory frame, human-reviewed workflow, privacy-first positioning.

Open: approved public demos, diagrams, and use cases.

Protected: private Jonathan/company details, transcripts, source files, access rules, and any unapproved client or collaborator materials.
