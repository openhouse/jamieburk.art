## One-line summary

Source-Backed Team Memory is a practice for turning messy team source material into human-reviewed, inspectable organizational memory.

## Role

I designed the method around source trails, review loops, correction, and practical documentation rather than opaque automation.

## What was unclear

Teams often have the raw material of memory: meetings, transcripts, notes, documents, decisions, and chats. The hard part is making that material usable without losing context or publishing what should stay private.

## What became usable

The practice creates a shared record that can be traced, reviewed, corrected, and handed off. AI can help draft, but humans remain responsible for review and trust.

## What this proves

This lab note shows AI-adjacent product operations without turning the portfolio into an AI manifesto. The emphasis stays on source discipline, review, and team usefulness.
