## One-line summary

A lab and proof-of-practice exploring source-backed operating memory, decision lineage, onboarding context, and human-correctable AI workflows for knowledge-heavy teams.

## What was unclear

Teams often need memory that is more useful than scattered documents but safer than a broad private archive interface. The question is how to keep evidence, context, uncertainty, and human review visible.

## What became usable

The work produced a method frame for source-backed operating memory: decisions connected to source material, onboarding paths that show context, and workflows that let people correct or qualify AI-generated summaries.

## What this proves

This project shows a careful AI-adjacent product posture: useful source-backed memory, human review, uncertainty labels, and privacy boundaries before scale.
