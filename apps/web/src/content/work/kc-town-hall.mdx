## One-line summary

Co-led redevelopment planning and public-benefit documentation for adaptive reuse of a long-vacant historic building, including public-funding and stakeholder documentation.

## What was unclear

Adaptive reuse work can involve civic value, real estate constraints, funding requirements, building history, stakeholder hopes, and practical uncertainty. The work needed a coherent public-benefit frame without overexposing private planning context.

## What became usable

The project produced planning materials, public-benefit documentation, stakeholder context, and funding-facing narrative structure that made the effort easier to explain and evaluate.

## What this proves

This project shows documentation and implementation support for civic-scale physical infrastructure: making complex public-benefit work legible to stakeholders, funders, and collaborators.
