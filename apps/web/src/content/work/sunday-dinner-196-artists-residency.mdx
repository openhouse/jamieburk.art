## One-line summary

196 / Sunday Dinner turned hosting, residency, facilitation, and community continuity into a repeatable system of care.

## Role

I created and maintained the rhythms, onboarding, documentation, facilitation, and handoff practices that allowed the work to continue across many gatherings and resident artists.

## What was unclear

Community infrastructure often depends on invisible labor. The challenge was making continuity real without turning private community life into public content.

## What became usable

Hosting systems, onboarding patterns, documentation habits, and continuity practices became stable enough for others to enter the work with more context and less friction.

## What this proves

This page shows operational care: building repeatable systems for human work without flattening the people inside the system.
