## One-line summary

A public-safe summary of participation infrastructure built around recurring gatherings, artist support, onboarding, facilitation, documentation, and continuity.

## What was unclear

The work needed to support real human participation without flattening people into content or exposing private records. The challenge was to make a room easier to enter and maintain while protecting the details that made it intimate.

## What I built / coordinated / documented

I created repeatable hosting, onboarding, facilitation, documentation, and continuity systems across 300+ gatherings and 20+ resident artists. The operating work included invitation rhythms, entry points, participation norms, support structures, and memory practices.

## What became usable

The practice gained durable participation infrastructure: ways for people to arrive, understand the room, contribute, and continue the work without every gathering starting from zero.

## Known / Open / Protected

- **Known:** Public-safe scale language and infrastructure framing.
- **Open:** Which photos or public artifacts are permissioned for V1.
- **Protected:** Raw guest data, attendance records, private resident details, unapproved photos, and private stories.
