## One-line summary

Created repeatable hosting, onboarding, facilitation, documentation, and continuity systems across 300+ gatherings and 20+ resident artists.

## What was unclear

The space needed to remain human, generous, and alive while still giving people enough structure to participate safely. Too little process made the work fragile; too much process would have flattened the culture.

## What became usable

Repeatable hosting patterns, onboarding materials, facilitation rhythms, documentation habits, and continuity practices helped people enter the space, understand expectations, and carry work forward.

## What this proves

This project shows operating structure in a cultural context: designing systems that support trust, continuity, and care without over-institutionalizing the work.
