## One-line summary

Co-built a Python / Django + Ember.js community-calendar platform organized around followable keyword communities, adopted by DIY arts and music organizers across roughly 35 city ecosystems.

## What was unclear

Local arts and music ecosystems often spread through informal networks, inconsistent calendars, and person-to-person trust. A useful tool needed to support discovery without forcing organizers into a generic venue database.

## What became usable

WOWList created a shared calendar model around followable keyword communities. It helped organizers and audiences track scenes, topics, and city ecosystems through a public web system.

## What this proves

This project shows product thinking for community infrastructure: translating informal organizing patterns into a usable platform while respecting that culture does not always fit institutional categories.
