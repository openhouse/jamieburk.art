## One-line summary

WOWList.org was a community-calendar platform for DIY arts and music organizers, built around keyword communities, natural-language event entry, weekly digest emails, and embeddable calendars.

## What was unclear

Organizers needed a way to publish and circulate events without turning every city or scene into a separate manual calendar project. Discovery, tagging, recurring updates, and digest circulation all needed lightweight operating structure.

## What I built / coordinated / documented

As a co-builder, I helped shape product and operations around a Python / Django + Ember.js platform. The work included public-facing event workflows, digest rhythms, organizer adoption, low-cost deployment patterns, and practical maintenance.

## What became usable

Organizers had a reusable platform for sharing events across roughly 35 city ecosystems. The system made place-based calendars, keyword communities, digest emails, and embedded calendars easier to maintain.
