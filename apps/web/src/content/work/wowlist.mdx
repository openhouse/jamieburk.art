## One-line summary

WOWList.org was community calendar infrastructure for DIY arts and music organizers working across many local ecosystems.

## Role

I co-built and helped steward the product and community system around it: platform behavior, organizer needs, adoption patterns, and public-facing clarity.

## What was unclear

Event information lived in scattered channels. Organizers needed shared infrastructure that could carry local specificity without becoming unusable.

## What became usable

The platform gave organizers and audiences a clearer way to publish, find, and coordinate events across many city ecosystems.

## What this proves

This short proof page shows product collaboration, community adoption work, and web-systems implementation in a cultural infrastructure context.
