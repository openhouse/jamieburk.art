## One-line summary

Helped an 80+ year-old legacy industrial business adapt to e-commerce through web, analytics, marketing, content, and operational workflow improvements.

## What was unclear

The business already had customer trust, product knowledge, institutional memory, and a distinctive voice. The challenge was translating that strength into web systems, product pages, campaign rhythms, analytics feedback, and operational workflows that could support online growth without flattening the company into generic e-commerce.

## What became usable

The work produced clearer product presentation, improved public-facing web systems, analytics-informed marketing and content decisions, and practical workflows that helped technical and nontechnical stakeholders see what needed attention next.

## Outcomes / impact

The public-safe claim is contribution, not sole causation: Jamie contributed to 2x revenue growth through long-term web, e-commerce, marketing, analytics, and operational systems work.

## What this proves

This project shows technical project management in a small-business environment: translating institutional knowledge into usable systems, coordinating across technical and nontechnical contexts, improving workflows over time, and protecting private operational material while still making the public proof legible.
