## One-line summary

Helped an 80+ year-old legacy industrial business adapt to e-commerce through web, analytics, marketing, content, and operational workflow improvements.

## What was unclear

A durable business voice and deep product knowledge needed to move into modern e-commerce without flattening the company into generic catalog software.

## What became usable

A maintainable digital operating layer that helped the business sell online, understand demand, preserve its voice, and keep improving public-facing workflows.
