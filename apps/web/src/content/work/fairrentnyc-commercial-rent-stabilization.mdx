## One-line summary

Built shared memory and documentation infrastructure so coalition work around Commercial Rent Stabilization could become easier to understand, continue, and hand off.

## Role

I helped structure civic context into usable documents, source maps, running minutes, action trackers, public-safe summaries, and coordination materials.

## Context

The work involved policy history, storefront stability, public data, legislation, civic partners, campaign memory, and materials that needed careful boundaries between public, collective, and private information.

## What I did

- Helped structure campaign history into shared memory.
- Built source maps and public-data framing.
- Turned meeting context into running minutes and action trackers.
- Supported public-safe communications and handoff materials.
- Kept privacy, legal-review boundaries, and consent-dependent material out of the public layer.

## What became usable

The coalition work had clearer continuity: source trails, action paths, public-safe explainers, and shared documents that helped people orient without requiring every prior conversation.

## Tools and systems

Markdown, shared documents, source maps, public-data review, meeting synthesis, action trackers, policy research, civic communication, and structured handoff practices.

## Outcomes / impact

The work supported coalition coordination and policy communication by making scattered civic context more inspectable and easier to continue. The site does not publish raw private records.

## What this proves

This case shows public-interest implementation work: translating complex, sensitive, stakeholder-heavy context into durable documentation and careful next steps.
