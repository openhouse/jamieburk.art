## One-line summary

Built a civic-data prototype translating constituent-services open data into resident-facing guidance.

## What was unclear

CouncilStat-style constituent-services data was public, but not naturally shaped around a resident asking what to do next.

## What became usable

A resident-facing civic-data prototype that connected issue categories to guidance instead of leaving people with raw data alone.
