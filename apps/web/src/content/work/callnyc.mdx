## One-line summary

Built a civic-data prototype translating constituent-services open data into resident-facing find-help and next-step guidance.

## What was unclear

Residents may encounter city issues through everyday language while government systems often organize information through offices, districts, issue codes, and service categories. The useful product question was how to bridge those models without making official-service claims.

## What became usable

The prototype organized open data into issue pathways, district context, and resident-facing guidance. It made the shape of a possible public tool easier to discuss, test, and critique.

## Visible disclaimer

Archived civic-data prototype. Not an official or current City Council service.

## What this proves

This project shows the ability to translate public data into humane user pathways, prototype quickly, and label civic tools carefully so residents are not misled about authority or currency.
