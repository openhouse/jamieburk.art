A public-safe summary of shared campaign-memory and coordination infrastructure for Commercial Rent Stabilization and storefront-stability advocacy.

- **Main outputs:** Shared campaign memory, stakeholder next steps, public-data framing, public-safe materials
- **Transferable strengths:** Civic documentation, knowledge systems, public-safe synthesis, stakeholder coordination

## Context

Civic and coalition work often moves through meetings, messages, public documents, legal questions, policy windows, and shifting stakeholder needs. Without a shared memory layer, important decisions and public-safe source trails can become hard to find precisely when collaborators need them.

## What was unclear

The work needed clearer separation between public facts, open strategy questions, legal-review questions, and private collaborator context. It also needed a way to keep campaign history useful without turning private notes or sensitive stakeholder material into public content.

## What I built / coordinated / documented

I built and stewarded shared documentation infrastructure: running minutes, decision records, action trackers, source maps, public-data framing, public handouts, QR pathways, and collaborator next steps. The work helped organize city and state strategy lanes and gave collaborators a way to return to decisions, facts, and open questions.

## What became usable

The coalition had a clearer shared record of what had happened, what was known, what still needed review, and what public-facing materials could safely support advocacy. That record made the work easier to coordinate across meetings and easier to hand off when attention shifted.

## How people used it

Collaborators could orient quickly, see the current state of the work, identify next actions, and separate public-safe materials from protected context. The infrastructure made it easier to keep continuity across a long-running civic process.

## Outcomes

- Created 30+ pages of campaign-memory infrastructure.
- Organized running minutes, action trackers, source maps, public handouts, QR pathways, and stakeholder next steps.
- Helped translate public data and policy context into materials collaborators could use.
