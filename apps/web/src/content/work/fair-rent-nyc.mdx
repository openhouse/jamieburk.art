## One-line summary

Built and stewarded shared campaign-memory and coordination infrastructure for Commercial Rent Stabilization and storefront-stability advocacy.

## What was unclear

The work involved public materials, private conversations, coalition coordination, legal and policy questions, meeting rhythms, and multiple stakeholder lanes. Without a shared structure, the work could fragment into scattered notes, repeated context-setting, and unclear next steps.

## What became usable

The system turned scattered civic work into running minutes, action trackers, public-data framing, source maps, stakeholder follow-up notes, legal and policy question logs, and public-safe explanations that collaborators could return to.

## Tools and methods

The work used meeting synthesis, source mapping, careful claim review, stakeholder follow-up tracking, public-data interpretation, and Known / Open / Protected framing to separate what could be said publicly from what needed review or privacy.

## What this proves

This project shows documentation as civic infrastructure: creating shared memory, making strategy and public information easier to navigate, and protecting sensitive material while keeping collective work moving.
