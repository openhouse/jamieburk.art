## Principle

AI drafts. Humans review. The shared record remains inspectable and correctable.

## What the lab studies

- Meetings that produce decisions, open questions, useful ideas, and follow-up work.
- Documents and source materials that should remain connected to the claims they support.
- Handoffs where a future reader needs to know what happened, what is still unknown, and where to verify the record.

## What it is

Research, consulting method, and early prototype practice for source-backed team memory.

## What it is not

- A production SaaS product.
- A replacement for judgment.
- A private archive browser.
- A place to expose raw transcripts, private documents, or unreviewed notes.

## Working shape

The useful record should show what is known, what is open, what is protected, and what source layer supports the summary. The goal is not to automate memory away from people. The goal is to make memory more inspectable for the people responsible for it.
