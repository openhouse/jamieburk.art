# Source-Backed Team Memory

## One-line summary

A source-backed team-memory practice for turning meetings, documents, transcripts, and decisions into human-reviewed organizational memory.

## Required line

AI drafts. Humans review. Sources remain inspectable. Corrections remain possible. Private material stays private.

## What was unclear

Teams often collect meetings, transcripts, notes, documents, and decisions faster than they can turn them into usable memory. The risk is not only forgetting. The risk is acting on summaries that cannot be inspected, corrected, or bounded.

## What became usable

A practical operating stance for source-backed memory: draft from sources, review with humans, preserve traceability, name uncertainty, and protect private material.
