# FairRentNYC / Commercial Rent Stabilization

## One-line summary

Built and stewarded shared campaign-memory and coordination infrastructure for Commercial Rent Stabilization and storefront-stability advocacy.

## Context

Commercial rent stabilization work sat across public policy, storefront stability, legal questions, city/state strategy, and coalition coordination. The work had to remain useful to the people doing it without exposing private context that did not belong in public.

## What was unclear

The work involved many stakeholders, public and private source materials, legal/policy questions, city/state strategy lanes, shifting meetings, and sensitive coalition context that could easily become fragmented or overexposed.
