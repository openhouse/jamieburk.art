# Harry J. Epstein Company

## One-line summary

Helped an 80+ year-old legacy industrial business adapt to e-commerce through web, analytics, marketing, content, and operational workflow improvements.

## Context

The company already had what many digital businesses try to manufacture: customer trust, deep product knowledge, institutional memory, and a recognizable voice. What was harder was translating that strength into web, e-commerce, analytics, and operational habits that could scale.

## What was unclear

A legacy business had customer trust, institutional voice, inventory knowledge, and operational habits that did not automatically translate into modern e-commerce workflows.

The practical questions were not only technical. What should move online? What should stay human? Which product details mattered to customers? Which metrics helped the team make decisions without turning the business into a generic storefront?
