# CallNYC.org

## One-line summary

Built a civic-data prototype translating constituent-services open data into resident-facing find-help and next-step guidance.

## Context

Residents often meet civic systems while trying to solve a specific problem. Public data can describe parts of the system, but people still need language, pathways, and next steps that make the system easier to enter.

## What was unclear

Constituent-services data existed, but residents needed a clearer public-facing pathway from issue to relevant civic office or next step.
