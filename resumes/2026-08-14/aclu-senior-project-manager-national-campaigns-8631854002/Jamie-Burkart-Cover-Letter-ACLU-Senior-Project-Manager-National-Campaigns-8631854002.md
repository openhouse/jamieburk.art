# Cover Letter - ACLU Senior Project Manager, National Campaigns

August 20, 2026

Dear ACLU National Campaigns Team,

A campaign can have urgent facts, committed people, and a clear public purpose and still lose time at the handoffs. I have spent much of my working life building the forms that keep collective work moving: a maintained schedule, a decision record, an accurate source map, a public message, and a next step that another person can actually use. That is what draws me to the Senior Project Manager, National Campaigns role.

With NYC Artist Coalition and FairRentNYC, I help coordinate campaign websites, recurring meetings, public forums, testimony planning, press communication, policy research, and follow-through with elected officials and staff. During a 2026 Commercial Rent Stabilization collaboration, I built and stewarded more than thirty pages of shared campaign memory connecting minutes, decisions, action items, risks, source material, policy questions, and city and state workstreams. The system did not replace the judgment of advocates, researchers, cultural-space operators, or coalition partners. It gave their different forms of knowledge somewhere to meet and remain available.

The problem in complex advocacy is not only whether people care; it is whether care has been given a usable operating structure. Across Thick Arts, WOWList, CallNYC, and KC Town Hall, I have translated ambiguous public-facing work into scope, schedules, risk tracking, communications, technical systems, vendor coordination, and durable handoffs. I am comfortable working inside contested situations while distinguishing private strategy from public information, personal contribution from collective outcome, and advocacy from legislative authority.

I would welcome the chance to help the ACLU's national campaigns hold complexity without allowing it to become confusion. I can bring warm facilitation, candid status communication, technical fluency, and a practiced respect for the people whose agency gives a campaign its meaning. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,

Jamie Burkart
