# Cover Letter - CodePath Senior AI Operations Lead

August 20, 2026

Dear CodePath Operations Team,

A useful AI workflow begins before the prompt, with a person explaining where work repeatedly disappears. A decision lives in one document, its source in another, and the next teammate begins again from zero. I am interested in CodePath's Senior AI Operations Lead role because it treats that problem as an operating question: where can AI reduce repeated work while leaving judgment, correction, and ownership with the people doing the job?

At Thick Arts, I build and maintain a source-backed team-memory system that turns permissioned material into typed observations, evidence-linked claims, public-safe projections, reusable skills, and correction paths. Deterministic checks catch failures that do not require a model; adversarial mutations test whether an evaluator can notice the wrong thing; human holdouts retain decisions that automation cannot honestly make. I use AI-assisted research, coding, documentation, and evaluation across JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, SQL, APIs, GitHub, and Docker-based delivery, but the tool is never the unit of success.

The question is not how many agents a team can deploy. It is whether the resulting system saves time, improves quality, changes behavior, and leaves the team more capable of continuing without its builder. My work with FairRentNYC, WOWList, CallNYC, and a long-running small-business client has taught me to begin with real workflows, build only the structure people can maintain, document failure states, and protect the boundaries around private sources and collective work. I do not yet claim an audited organization-wide time-savings result; I can show the working system, its tests, its limits, and the evidence needed to measure adoption responsibly.

I would welcome the chance to bring that practice into CodePath: listening first, choosing a bounded high-leverage workflow, shipping it with its review system, and leaving behind reusable capability. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,

Jamie Burkart
