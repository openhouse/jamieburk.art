# Cover Letter - NYC OTI Senior Product Manager 782366

August 20, 2026

Dear NYC Office of Technology and Innovation Hiring Team,

CallNYC began with a practical civic question: when a resident recognizes a problem, what information would help that person understand its public context and take a useful next step? I built the independent project after the City Council's first CouncilStat hackathon, translating constituent-services open data into issue pathways and resident-facing guidance. That experience is one reason I am excited by the PIT Crew Senior Product Manager role.

For more than fourteen years, I have taken public-facing systems from problem framing through implementation, launch, operation, and learning. Through Thick Arts, I translate ambiguous stakeholder needs into product direction, information architecture, technical scope, sequenced releases, testing, adoption materials, and handoff documentation. With Richard Caceres, I co-founded and co-built WOWList, a natural-language community calendar shaped by organizer discovery and maintained through onboarding, support, instrumentation, and low-cost production operations. With NYC Artist Coalition and FairRentNYC, I turn stakeholder listening, public testimony, policy questions, and coalition decisions into buildable digital systems while protecting private strategy and collective credit.

Public technology is not complete when the code ships. It becomes useful when residents can understand it, agency partners can maintain it, and the team can learn from what happens after launch. I bring hands-on fluency across product, design, engineering, operations, open data, privacy, accessibility, and public communication. I also understand that founder-wide responsibility is not identical to product authority inside a large public institution; I welcome the discipline of clear decision rights, shared roadmaps, and accountable team practice.

I would welcome the opportunity to help PIT Crew build services with residents rather than merely for them, and to leave City teams with stronger capacity after each release. I welcome OTI's independent review of the applicable civil-service qualifications. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,

Jamie Burkart
