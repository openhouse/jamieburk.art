# Cover Letter - NYC OTI Operations Manager, SPEED 789810

August 20, 2026

Dear NYC Office of Technology and Innovation Hiring Team,

On a complex building project, the roof, masonry, public-benefit record, vendor schedule, and municipal requirements cannot be managed as separate stories. Each handoff changes what the next person is able to do. I learned this while co-leading the adaptive reuse of KC Town Hall, an approximately 6,500-square-foot historic building, and it is why the SPEED Operations Manager role feels so legible to me.

My work has repeatedly been to make emerging work operable. Through Thick Arts, I turn ambiguous technical and public-facing projects into scope, work plans, requirements, risk and dependency records, releases, adoption materials, and durable handoffs. With NYC Artist Coalition and FairRentNYC, I coordinate campaign websites, meetings, policy communication, public-data framing, and city and state workstreams across advocates, cultural-space operators, elected officials and staff, researchers, and technical collaborators. I built more than thirty pages of shared campaign memory so decisions, questions, actions, and source material would accumulate rather than disappear between meetings.

Operations is not only administration. It is the structure through which a multidisciplinary team can see responsibility, surface risk, and act without asking one person to hold the whole roof alone. I bring vendor coordination, municipal compliance, public funding presentation, reporting, technical implementation, facilitation, and candid status communication. My record does not yet establish formal City procurement authority or independent ownership of a large organizational budget; it does establish long-running responsibility for making mixed physical, technical, civic, and relational work cohere.

I would welcome the chance to help SPEED build an operating rhythm that shortens avoidable delay while protecting accountability and team health. I can bring practical systems, warm follow-through, and respect for the different expertise required to make public work move. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,

Jamie Burkart
