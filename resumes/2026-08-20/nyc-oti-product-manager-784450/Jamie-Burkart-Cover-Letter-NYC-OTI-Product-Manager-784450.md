# Cover Letter - NYC OTI Product Manager 784450

August 20, 2026

Dear NYC Office of Technology and Innovation Hiring Team,

CallNYC began with a practical product question: what would help a resident move from recognizing a neighborhood problem to understanding its public context and taking a useful next step? I translated constituent-services open data into issue pathways and resident-facing guidance, then took the independent project through information architecture, implementation, acceptance review, and public release. That combination of civic purpose and end-to-end product work is why I am applying for Product Manager 784450.

For more than fourteen years through Thick Arts, I have turned ambiguous stakeholder needs into product direction, requirements, sequenced releases, testing, adoption, analytics, and maintenance systems. At WOWList, participatory discovery with organizers led Richard Caceres and me to make a consequential product choice: rather than impose a complex event form, we centered one collaboratively editable text field and built discovery, weekly digests, profiles, embeds, and calendar connections around how organizers already wrote. We supported that choice through onboarding, instrumentation, and low-cost operations across 35+ city ecosystems, treating that footprint as a limited activity signal rather than causal proof. With NYC Artist Coalition and FairRentNYC, I translate stakeholder listening, public testimony, policy questions, and coalition decisions into usable campaign systems while preserving private boundaries and collective credit.

The work is not only to make a product function. It is to help users, agency partners, designers, engineers, and program owners agree on what problem version one will solve, how success will be observed, and what the team will change after launch. I bring hands-on fluency across web systems, open data, analytics, privacy, accessibility, facilitation, and public communication. I also bring the humility to distinguish founder responsibility from authority inside a public product team and to make decisions through visible roles and evidence.

I would welcome the opportunity to help OTI build services that are easier for residents to use and easier for City teams to sustain. I can contribute immediately while learning the agency's product practice, constraints, and measures of public value. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,

Jamie Burkart
