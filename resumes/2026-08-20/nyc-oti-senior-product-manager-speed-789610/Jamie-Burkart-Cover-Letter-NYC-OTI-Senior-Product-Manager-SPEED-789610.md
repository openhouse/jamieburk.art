# Cover Letter - NYC OTI Senior Product Manager, SPEED 789610

August 20, 2026

Dear NYC Office of Technology and Innovation Hiring Team,

A permitting process is often encountered one waiting person and one missing handoff at a time. I learned the material consequences of those handoffs while co-leading KC Town Hall, the adaptive reuse of an approximately 6,500-square-foot historic building into four commercial spaces and three homes. Plans, vendors, public-benefit documentation, municipal compliance, financing conditions, and field work formed one public system, even when they arrived through different offices and documents.

That experience sits beside fourteen years of product and technical-project work. Through Thick Arts, I turn ambiguous needs into product direction, information architecture, requirements, sequenced releases, testing, adoption materials, data workflows, and durable handoffs. I built CallNYC to translate public constituent-services data into resident-facing issue pathways. With Richard Caceres, I co-built WOWList using Django, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, Ember.js, natural-language publishing, organizer discovery, instrumentation, and low-cost production operations. With FairRentNYC, I connect stakeholder listening, public testimony, policy research, shared digital infrastructure, and government-facing follow-through.

Speed is not only elapsed time. It is whether a process becomes more legible without moving risk onto the resident, small business, applicant, or public servant least able to absorb it. I bring product discovery, process mapping, user stories, acceptance criteria, web and API fluency, SQL and open-data practice, facilitation, and public implementation. I do not claim enterprise data-platform authority that my record does not establish; I can contribute a grounded bridge among lived process, product delivery, technical constraints, and accountable measurement.

I would welcome the chance to help SPEED identify one costly bottleneck, build a focused version one with the people inside it, and leave the process more transparent and maintainable. I welcome OTI's independent review of the applicable civil-service qualifications. Thank you for considering my application.

Sincerely,

Jamie Burkart
