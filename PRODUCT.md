# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are time-pressed hiring readers and referrers deciding
whether to advance Jamie Burkart for technical project management, product
operations, implementation, civic-technology, and related public-interest
roles. They need to understand Jamie's role fit, retrieve credible evidence,
and identify the next action without learning the internal language of the
portfolio system.

Secondary users include product and operations leaders, civic-technology
teams, implementation teams, collaborators, and trusted reviewers evaluating
the work for employment, referral, focused consulting, or collaboration.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio makes Jamie legible as a Technical Project Manager - Product
Operations & Implementation lead who turns ambiguous, stakeholder-heavy work
into usable systems: requirements, workflows, documentation, decision trails,
launch support, onboarding, and durable handoffs.

Success means a hiring reader with limited time can understand Jamie's role
fit, trust the evidence, move to the relevant case study or resume, and choose
a clear next action such as advancing Jamie to an interview. Success also means
that this clarity is achieved without exposing private or sensitive material.

## Positioning

This is a composed portfolio generated from a governed Knowledge Wiki Graph,
not a manually assembled gallery or an archive browser. Project sources become
atomic observations, supported claims, explicit guardrails, and evidence; that
material is then selected into public portfolio surfaces and role-specific
application materials. Feedback from applications and real-world work returns
to the system so later selections can become more useful without rewriting
history or weakening provenance.

The distinctive mechanism is the maintained relationship among project
materials, evidence and claims, and their selective public projections. The
depth stays available to the system while each reader receives a concise,
purposeful composition.

## Operating Context

Hiring readers encounter the product through the public website, shared links,
social previews, case studies, the role-fit page, the public resume, and contact
routes. Jamie also uses the repository to prepare job-specific resumes, cover
letters, application guides, interview evidence, and follow-up materials.

The operating cycle is:

1. Bring project sources and current activity into governed research records.
2. Separate observation, claim, evidence, credit, consent, and public-use
   status.
3. Select only the material needed for a particular reader and purpose.
4. Evaluate the rendered site and application artifacts with deterministic
   gates before modeled or human hiring-reader review.
5. Incorporate application outcomes and work experience without converting
   access or inference into authority.

The canonical application is `apps/www`. Staging is the review surface and
remains non-indexable. Production publication and indexing are later,
separately authorized actions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public product provides a homepage, work index, project case studies,
  role-fit evidence, an HTML resume route, a downloadable resume, About,
  Contact, Colophon, and a public-safe Knowledge Wiki Graph lab surface.
- Claims shown publicly must resolve to the governed claim and evidence layer.
  Stronger wording cannot be introduced only in page copy.
- Access is not consent. Evidence is not publication permission. Attribution,
  collective credit, privacy, consent, publication, deployment, and indexing
  remain separate gates.
- Collective work stays collectively credited. Advocacy, participation, and
  technical contribution are not converted into sole causation, legislative
  enactment, official government authority, or sole authorship.
- Jamie's human approval is required for production publication and indexing.
  Automated checks, modeled readers, staging access, and repository history do
  not replace that approval.
- Approved public-safe summaries, governed photographs, screenshots of public
  surfaces, representative diagrams, and synthetic examples may be used.
  Private correspondence, raw transcripts, coalition notes, legal-review
  material, health or financial details, credentials, private stakeholder
  lists, internal analytics, client-private material, and raw community
  records may not be published.
- The V1 product remains a focused Next.js portfolio. It does not add a CMS,
  database, authentication system, search system, analytics tracker, private
  archive browser, full photo archive, AI chatbot, heavy animation, or major
  framework change without explicit authorization.

## Brand Commitments

The product uses the name Jamie Burkart and the literal professional role
Technical Project Manager - Product Operations & Implementation. Its voice is
precise, warm, modular, ethically grounded, and confidently human. It avoids
inflation, defensive over-explanation, generic consulting language, and
self-mythologizing.

Documentation is care. The portfolio must minimize the reader's burden while
keeping important nuance, collective credit, and public-safety boundaries
intact. Existing approved identity assets and photography remain governed by
their manifests and occurrence-level permissions. The current East River hero
is a confirmed identity asset and remains in place unless Jamie explicitly
approves a change.

## Evidence on Hand

- `apps/www/src/data/knowledge-bank/records.ts` and
  `docs/knowledge-bank/` hold canonical claims, public-safe source records,
  boundaries, inquiries, and project knowledge.
- `apps/www/src/data/photography.ts` and
  `apps/www/src/data/work-covers.ts` govern approved photographic occurrences
  and project covers.
- `apps/www/src/content/work/` and `apps/www/src/data/work.ts` contain the
  composed case-study and project surfaces.
- `resumes/` contains dated, opportunity-specific application resumes, while
  the public resume route selects the appropriate reader-facing artifact.
- `.agents/evals/`, `evals/`, `scripts/`, and `reports/` contain deterministic
  and modeled evaluation contracts, mutation tests, and generated evidence
  reports.

The repository contains substantive public evidence, approved photographs,
project websites, public records, press references, and case studies. It does
not make every underlying private source publishable, and future work must not
fabricate missing testimonials, metrics, permissions, outcomes, or authority.

## Product Principles

1. Minimize the hiring reader's burden: role, value, proof, and next action
   should become clear before attention runs out.
2. Let evidence carry the argument: show specific projects, artifacts, and
   consequences instead of repeating positioning language.
3. Keep the system deeper than the surface: preserve provenance, corrections,
   open questions, and protected boundaries while composing selectively.
4. Treat access, evidence, consent, collective credit, publication, deployment,
   and indexing as distinct responsibilities.
5. Learn from real outcomes: application and work feedback should improve later
   selections without retroactively overstating earlier work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA or better for text contrast, keyboard navigation, visible
focus, semantic structure, responsive behavior, descriptive alternatives,
minimum command-target size, and reduced-motion support. Verify canonical
routes at the maintained desktop and mobile viewports before accepting a
visual candidate.

Keep language plain and specific. Treat privacy, consent, attribution,
collective credit, public-safe redaction, and the ability to use the site
without privileged context as inclusion requirements rather than
afterthoughts.
