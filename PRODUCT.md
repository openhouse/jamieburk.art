# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, product and operations leaders, civic technology teams,
implementation teams, collaborators, and trusted reviewers who need to quickly
understand Jamie Burkart's professional shape. They arrive with limited time
and need credible, public-safe evidence that Jamie can create operating
structure for complex public-facing work. Jamie and trusted collaborators also
use the Knowledge Wiki to develop, challenge, and govern the evidence from
which future portfolio editions and application materials may be composed.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio makes Jamie legible as a Technical Project Manager - Product
Operations & Implementation lead. It should show how ambiguous,
stakeholder-heavy work becomes usable systems: requirements, workflows,
documentation, decision trails, launch support, onboarding, and durable
handoffs. Success means a reviewer can understand Jamie's role fit, trust the
evidence, feel the human and collective conditions of the work, and know what
to do next without exposing private or sensitive materials.

The Knowledge Wiki is the governed working layer behind that public argument.
It preserves sources, claims, limits, decisions, photographic inquiries,
corrections, and protected absences without requiring every record to appear on
the site.

## Positioning

Jamie is not presented as a generic project manager adjacent to interesting
work. The portfolio shows a recurring operating practice: listening across
technical, civic, cultural, and interpersonal contexts; making emerging needs
legible; building the tools, rituals, documentation, and public surfaces a
group can inhabit; and transferring stewardship without claiming collective
outcomes as individual authorship.

## Operating Context

- Hiring readers commonly arrive from an application, referral, or
  conversation and may have only a few minutes to establish role fit.
- The public site offers a fast path through selected work, resume, about, and
  contact pages, with deeper case studies for readers who continue.
- The Knowledge Wiki and evaluation suites operate inside the repository as a
  public-safe production system. Private archives and source bindings remain
  outside public Git.
- Photography is both a public compositional layer and a research surface.
  Private editor fields may prompt recollection or new inquiry without
  authorizing publication.
- Staging remains non-indexed. Production indexing is a separate explicit
  release decision.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router portfolio with typed project data, local MDX, responsive
  photography, citation endnotes, downloadable resume, and production/staging
  release controls.
- Governed Knowledge Wiki records and graph relations for sources, claims,
  projects, methods, decisions, inquiries, projections, and evaluations.
- Recursive evaluation suites for claims, collective credit, public safety,
  accessibility, photographic governance, responsive layout, and release
  readiness.
- Public evidence may be concise, but it must remain traceable to bounded source
  records and explicit outcome limits.
- Never publish private correspondence, raw transcripts, coalition notes,
  legal-review materials, internal analytics, client-private material, private
  archive coordinates, unapproved photographs, or unapproved personal details.
- Selection, visual strength, automated checks, and a green pull request do not
  confer rights, consent, credit, factual authority, production approval, or
  indexing approval.

## Brand Commitments

The voice is precise, warm, modular, and ethically grounded. It should feel
careful, useful, source-backed, and human without becoming inflated or
self-mythologizing. Jamie's phrases "operational production," "active
accommodation," "documentation is care," and "I believe we are all working on
the same project" may orient the work when used with editorial restraint.

The visual system retains the Human Index palette derived in part from Jamie's
work jacket, documentary photography, strong reading surfaces, modest machine
forms, and material distinctions between observed, asserted, corroborated,
disputed, protected, corrected, and harmful states. It must not become a generic
SaaS landing page, private archive browser, consultant hype funnel, or
decorative AI template.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public-safe project records, claims, source notes, decisions, inquiries, and
  evaluation records under `docs/knowledge-bank`.
- Typed public-site project and photography data under `apps/www/src/data`.
- Approved or bounded public derivatives under `apps/www/public`, with
  occurrence-level caption and credit records.
- Resume and application-facing route content maintained in the portfolio app.
- A private Apple Photos corpus of more than 600,000 photographs, including a
  verified 1,000-image editor field and a governed 24-image website candidate
  edit. Those private fields are not publication clearance and do not belong in
  public Git.
- No fabricated testimonials, universal authorship claims, automatic
  represented-person consent, or unreviewed outcome metrics may be supplied to
  fill an editorial gap.

## Product Principles

1. Help a stranger understand role fit quickly, then reward deeper attention.
2. Let photographs create presence, relation, and inquiry; never use people as
   decorative proof around Jamie.
3. Compose the public portfolio subtractively from a deeper governed knowledge
   layer.
4. Keep observation, memory, interpretation, corroboration, and publication
   authority distinct.
5. Make correction, withdrawal, protected absence, and handoff normal parts of
   the system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA or better for text contrast, keyboard navigation, visible
focus, semantic structure, responsive behavior, and reduced-motion support.
Keep language plain and specific. Treat privacy, consent, attribution, and
public-safe redaction as inclusion requirements, not afterthoughts. Images need
bounded alt text, stable responsive dimensions, readable captions, and
represented-person dignity review. The public citation layer should remain
available without overwhelming the primary reading path.
